Discover the unique effects of each Fever Racket! Some, like the Golden Dash Racket, will offer you a helpful boost, while others, like the shocking Amp Racket, can play tricks on your opponent. You’ll need to master all kinds of Fever Rackets to survive in Trial Towers – if you’re looking for inspiration, why not start there?

Take a moment to learn how and when to use each shot. Is your opponent guarding the back of the court? Try landing the ball just past the net with a drop shot (press B, then quickly press A). Are they close to the net instead? Use a lob (press A, then quickly press B) to shoot far over their head.

To reach the end of the Adventure mode, you’ll need to use a wide variety of shots. Take it for a spin and see how your skills improve!

Try these tricks in your next match, grow stronger, and discover your own favourite tennis tactics!

Mario Tennis Fever is available now – only on Nintendo Switch 2.
