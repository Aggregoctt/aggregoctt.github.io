The Donkey Kong & Pauline amiibo and every amiibo released up to this point are compatible with Donkey Kong Bananza.

Join the unlikely duo of Donkey Kong and Pauline as they explore the diverse worlds of the Underground in this strange subterranean adventure in Donkey Kong Bananza, releasing July 17th on Nintendo Switch 2!

Alongside the game, you can pre-order the new Donkey Kong & Pauline amiibo. Scanning this will unlock Pauline’s diva dress early, making discs twice as likely to appear after defeating an enemy.

You can pre-order the Donkey Kong & Pauline amiibo on its own for £16.99, or in a bundle with a copy of the game - £75.98 for digital or £83.98 for physical.
