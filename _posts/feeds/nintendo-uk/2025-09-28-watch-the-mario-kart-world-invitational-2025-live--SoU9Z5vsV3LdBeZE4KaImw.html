Schedule

Here’s when you can catch the action, live from Japan!

  • Parents and Kids – October 5th, 05:30 am (UK time)
  • General – October 5th, approx. 08:00 am (UK time)

Who’s competing?

Competitors from Europe, Japan, North America, Australia and New Zealand, Hong Kong, Taiwan and South Korea will head to the starting grid in one of two competitions: ‘Parents and Kids’ and ‘General’.

Representing Europe in the Parents and Kids competition:

  • Team Ruffy (Salva and Clara from Spain)
  • Barker Boosters (Casey and Harlow from the United Kingdom)

Representing Europe in the General competition:

  • Thomas from France – Mario Kart 8 Deluxe European Champion 2023
  • starxlx from France – Placed 2nd in the Mario Kart 8 Deluxe European Championships 2023
  • Mars from France – Placed 3rd in the Mario Kart 8 Deluxe European Championships 2023
