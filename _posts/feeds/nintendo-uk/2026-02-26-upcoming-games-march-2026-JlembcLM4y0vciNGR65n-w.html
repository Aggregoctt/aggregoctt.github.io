This March, build a Pokémon paradise, discover a new region of the Flower Kingdom, soar on the back of a Rathalos, and more! Check out this selection of games launching in March, and don’t forget to click on any that catch your eye for pre-orders, purchases or to add them to your Wish List. 

Get ready for new friends, new challenges and lots of surprises in this expanded version of Super Mario Bros. Wonder for Nintendo Switch 2! Enjoy a wide variety of multiplayer attractions in Bellabel Park, or battle the Koopalings on a hunt for the stolen Bellabel Flowers. Plus, you can now play as Rosalina throughout the whole adventure and enjoy co-op play with Co-Star Luma!
