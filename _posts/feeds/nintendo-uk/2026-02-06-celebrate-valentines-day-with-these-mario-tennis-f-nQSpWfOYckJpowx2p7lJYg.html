Game, set, and a perfect match! Valentine’s Day is almost here, and it’s the perfect time to share a token of love with your doubles partner. These cards feature a handful of Mario Tennis Fever’s 38 playable characters – which one most closely matches your true feelings? 

Download your cards here!

After you’ve downloaded the cards, open the file on your computer and then print them onto A4 paper. You’ll need a pair of scissors to cut out the cards, so safety first!
