Fresh weapons

Introducing the Splatlands Collection, featuring new weapons from the brands Barazushi and Emberz. Based on existing weapons, they nevertheless bring fresh new looks and, of course, different sub weapons and specials. There are a total of 30 new kits in the collection, so be sure to test them all out! Take a look at the videos below for all the details!

Series Weapon Power

A new stat has been added for Anarchy Battle (Series). For players ranked S and above, Series Weapon Power will track your effectiveness per weapon based on your win/loss ratio in battles – and will then match you with players of a similar power. So, you can try out new weapons without being disadvantaged, or try to push the limits of your favourites.

Nintendo Switch 2 enhancements

As part of the update, Inklings and Octolings playing on Nintendo Switch 2 can enjoy more detailed visuals, as well as smoother performance in areas such as the Square in Splatsville and the Grand Festival grounds.

There are also some other changes and adjustments to improve the balance of the game. You can check out the full patch notes and learn how to update your game here.
