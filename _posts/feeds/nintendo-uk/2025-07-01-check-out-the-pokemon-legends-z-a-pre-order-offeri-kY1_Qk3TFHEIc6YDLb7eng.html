Pokémon Legends: Z-A releases for Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 October 16th!

An ambitious new adventure set in Lumiose City awaits! Pre-orders are available for Pokémon Legends: Z-A for Nintendo Switch and Pokémon Legends: Z-A – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition. Explore the city, unravel its secrets and for the first time, battle Pokémon in dynamic, real-time battles!

You can now pre-order any version of Pokémon Legends: Z-A on My Nintendo Store to receive a Partner Pokémon figurine as a bonus item with purchase. Pre-order items are limited and strictly subject to availability.

Check out the newly available bundles for Pokémon Legends: Z-A on My Nintendo Store UK and Ireland!

You can pre-order the Mega Evolution Bundle containing a copy of the game, Mega Charizard X and Mega Charizard Y figurines as well as a pin set for an extra £7.00/€9.00.

The Trainer Bundle includes a copy of the game, an umbrella, a mug and a cap for an extra £13.00/€16.00.

For an extra £20.00/€25.00, you can also pre-order the Champion’s Choice Bundle including a copy of the game, Mega Charizard X and Y figurines, a pin set, an umbrella, a mug and a cap.

The Partner Pokémon figurine bonus item is included in all bundles while stocks last.

Prices above based on physical editions of Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2. Prices may vary for other formats.

You can learn more about Pokémon Legends: Z-A on its gamepage.
