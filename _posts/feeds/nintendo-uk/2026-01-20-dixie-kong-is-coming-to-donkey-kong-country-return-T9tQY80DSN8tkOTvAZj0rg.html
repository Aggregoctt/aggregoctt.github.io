A free update for Donkey Kong Country Returns HD is available from January 21st, 2026, with Dixie Kong joining the fun!

Players can use her Helicopter Spin manoeuvre to get a little extra height on jumps, and travel a little further too. It can prove rather useful in some of the trickier sections, helping you safely cross gaps or avoid enemies and obstacles. Be sure to try it out!

If you want to pick up the pace, you can also try out the new Turbo Attack mode! Here, Donkey Kong races through high-speed versions of each level. You’ll need to think – and act – fast if you want to reach the goal in the target time!

Nintendo Switch 2 players can also enjoy DK's platforming adventure with improved resolution and higher definition visuals, as well as getting to the action faster with reduced loading times.

Plus, with GameShare support on Nintendo Switch 2, you can (banana) split the fun with a friend using just one copy of the game.
