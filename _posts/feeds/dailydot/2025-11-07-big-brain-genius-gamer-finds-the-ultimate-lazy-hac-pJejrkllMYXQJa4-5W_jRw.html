TikToker and content creator Max Balegde has found a way to turn his self-proclaimed laziness into motivation.

In a now-viral clip, Balegde revealed that he brings his Nintendo Switch to the gym so he can play games while walking on the treadmill—his only reliable method for getting his steps in.

“At first I was embarrassed about it,” he said in the Oct. 29, 2025, TikTok video. But then he realized, “I''m double, triple multitasking, brev—catching Pokémon, fighting crimes,” and working out all the while. 

The clip started a conversation that revealed Balegde isn’t the only one incentivizing himself to work out by bundling the activity with a more pleasurable one. People replying to the post said in addition to gaming, they watch shows, shop online, read books, or scroll social media while they get their reps in.

Balegde’s habit of playing Nintendo Switch while logging time on a treadmill is a perfect example of what productivity experts call “temptation bundling.”

The term, also known as "activity bundling," describes a strategy for completing tasks and increasing willpower by pairing a harder, “should-do” task with a more pleasurable one.

In fact, a behavioral therapist who replied to the post gave Balegde’s hack the stamp of approval in a comment that read, “pairing a preferred activity with something you hate is a great idea.”

“Get on my level, brev!”

Balegde’s video has over 400,0000 views and 50,000 likes on TikTok. “I’m so unbelievably lazy, that the only way I''ve managed to persuade myself to go on the treadmill at the gym is by bringing my Nintendo Switch,” he said. 

“At first I was embarrassed about it,” Balegde admitted. “But now I just think I''m a big, fat, doubledecker, big brain genius.”
