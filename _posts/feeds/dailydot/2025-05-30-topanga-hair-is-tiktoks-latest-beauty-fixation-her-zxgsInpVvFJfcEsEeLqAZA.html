How to get “Topanga hair”:

Once you’ve got a haircut with long face-framing layers emphasizing volume and movement, it’s all about the ’90s blowout. Here’s a step-by-step:

  1. Start with clean, towel-dried hair. 
  2. Work volumizing mousse or spray into the roots of damp hair.
  3. Blow dry with a round brush. Lift hair at the roots to add volume and use the brush to curl the ends of hair inward for bounce. Pro tip: blow drying upside down can enhance lift (and put less strain on your biceps if you’re arms aren’t strong enough) 
  4. Use hot rollers to add even more volume and defined curls. 
  5. Finish with texturizing spray for body and lightweight hairspray to hold the look in place.
