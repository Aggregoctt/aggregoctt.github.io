As the December Flexible or Health Savings Account (FSA/HSA) eligibility date creeps up, workers are debating how much pre-tax income they should set aside for their healthcare costs. Some say they''re contributing a little bit more to get a great deal on "unhinged" health and wellness purchases.

Last week, TikToker @hrsuitespot asked her viewers to share what purchases make it worth adding an extra $1,000 to her HSA or FSA.

And boy, did the commenters deliver. Here are some of the best responses.
