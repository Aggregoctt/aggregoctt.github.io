Dunkin’ has rolled out a new Valentine’s Day menu featuring returning fan favorites, new seasonal beverages, and limited edition gift tins.

The lineup is available at participating locations from January 28 through February 17 and includes heart-shaped donuts, specialty Refreshers, espresso drinks, matcha options, and protein-based beverages.

The seasonal launch also brings back the Brownie Batter donut, one of the chain’s top-selling limited-time items, alongside new promotions for Dunkin’ Rewards members throughout February.

Dunkin unveils new Valentine’s Day drinks and returning treats

This year’s drink menu includes several new limited-time beverages built around espresso, fruit Refreshers, matcha, and protein milk.

The new options include:

  • Heart Eyes Daydream Refresher with Strawberry Dragonfruit Refresher, blueberry flavor, oatmilk, and Sweet Cold Foam
  • Chocolate Covered Strawberry Daydream Refresher with Strawberry Dragonfruit Refresher, oatmilk, and Chocolate Cold Foam
  • Blush Spritz Refresher with Strawberry Dragonfruit Refresher topped with sparkling water
  • Toxic Ex-Pesso Cloud Latte with espresso, mocha swirl, raspberry flavor, and Chocolate Cold Foam
  • Sweet On You Cloud Latte with espresso, whole milk, caramel swirl, coconut flavor, and Sweet Cold Foam
  • The Perfect Matcha with matcha green tea, whole milk, and Sweet Cold Foam
  • Buff Chai made with chai syrup and Protein Milk, served hot or iced with 15 grams of protein in a medium beverage

Alongside the drinks, Dunkin is bringing back its Valentine-themed donuts, including the Brownie Batter Classic Donut filled with brownie batter flavored buttercreme and topped with chocolate icing and sprinkles, and the Cupid’s Choice Classic Donut filled with Bavarian Kreme and finished with strawberry flavored icing and sprinkles.

Throughout the season, all filled donuts, including Boston Kreme and Jell,y will be made with heart-shaped shells. Sprinkled donuts and Valentine’s Day Sprinkle MUNCHKINS will feature pink, red, and white toppings.

“Brownie Batter is the kind of donut our guests mark their calendar for rich, chocolate-forward and undeniably Dunkin,” said Anthony Epter, vice president of menu innovation at Dunkin. “For Valentine’s Day, we built the rest of the lineup to match that same feel-good indulgence, from bold espresso sips to fruit-forward Refreshers, plus protein milk-based options that still feel like a treat.”

Dunkin is also offering a Valentine’s Day 25-count MUNCHKINS tin featuring one of three collectible designs with the phrases Sweet On U, Just Bcuz, and Ur A Treat.
