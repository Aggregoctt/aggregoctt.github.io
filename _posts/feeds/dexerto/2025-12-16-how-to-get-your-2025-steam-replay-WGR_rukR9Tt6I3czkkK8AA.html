Steam Replay has finally launched for 2025, giving users a chance to check out the games they played all year.

Spotify Wrapped started the trend of platforms telling users what they did throughout the year, and sites like YouTube and Twitch quickly jumped on board, highlighting their 2025 reviews and what content they consumed.

On the gaming side, PlayStation’s yearly wrap-up launched in early December, and now Valve’s Steam Recap has become available. Here’s how to get it.

How to see your Steam Recap in 2025

Steam began rolling out its 2025 Recap on Tuesday, December 16, but it may take some time to become available.

To see if you have access, all you have to do is head over to Valve’s website or click the banner on the Steam app’s storefront.

As usual, Steam’s recap does not include any playtime played in offline mode or when disconnected from the internet. It also excludes games that are unreleased, in preload, in prerelease, or disabled.

Valve also says that it includes all of your playtime between January 1, 2025, and the last second of December 14, 2025.
