Summer is here, and that means one thing – Marvel Rivals‘ highly anticipated swimsuit skins are finally on their way once again.

Alongside the multitude of characters Netease keeps adding, the skins are among the most popular changes being made in each season. After all, what’s the point in destroying your enemy as your favorite superhero if they don’t look awesome?

Now, given we’re officially in summer, things are heating up, both for the game’s storyline and for its heroes. Luckily, they can cool down, thanks to the newly revealed swimsuit skins.

Marvel Rivals swimsuits skins revealed for Thor, Psylocke & more

As was previously revealed, the Marvel Rivals swimsuit skins will be showcased in the Marvel Swimsuit Special: Friends, Foes & Rivals comic before coming to Marvel Rivals.

Well, those designs have just been released, showcased on X by Executive Producer at Marvel Games, Danny Koo.

In the post, he shows off Psylocke, complete with a butterfly top with a turquoise swimsuit underneath and matching sandals. Then, we see Luna Snow, with similar yet icy designs, with a black and white top over a bikini.

We also see The Punisher rocking some Speedos embroidered with the hero’s skull logo, which is a reference to his design in the 1993 Marvel’s Swimsuit Edition comic. He also rocks a towel-style shawl and what seems to be some rather summery guns, too.

Loki and Thor will also be getting a new look. Loki will wear a Speedo with a very fitting green and gold jacket, along with a horned sunhat. Thor boasts trunks and a cute little bag with Loki and (we think) Black Panther toys inside.

Currently, those are the only skins revealed for the Marvel Rivals event. There is no word on whether more will be coming, so keep your eyes peeled.
