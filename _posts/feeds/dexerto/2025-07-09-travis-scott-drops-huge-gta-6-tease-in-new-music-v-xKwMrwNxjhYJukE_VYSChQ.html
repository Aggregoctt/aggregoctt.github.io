GTA 6 fans suspect Travis Scott could have his own radio station after the rapper featured a cheeky VI reference in his latest music video.

Rockstar Games’ long-awaited open-world titan GTA 6 is set to finally arrive in 2026, and while the studio has been relatively mum on info, fans are getting plenty of hints and leaks about the game’s content.

After the second GTA 6 trailer went live, an established leaker made several claims about VI’s gameplay mechanics, the story length, cut features, and more.

Now, just weeks after rapper Jermaine Dupri claimed Drake will have a radio station in GTA 6 that will play unreleased music, Travis Scott dropped a huge tease of his own.

GTA 6 could feature Travis Scott after rapper’s cryptic music video

On July 8, Travis Scott uploaded a music video for his and JackBoys new song, 2000 EXCURSION.

Towards the end of the video, a car could be seen with a Florida license plate that reads “GTA VI 6.”

(The GTA 6 plate is shown at 3:56)

Many fans of already pointed to this as a sign that Cactus Jack Records or Travis Scott could have a radio station in GTA 6. So far, nothing has been confirmed, and it’s always possible that this was just a nod to the game and an Easter Egg for fans.

The GTA series has always made its radio stations special with numerous celebrity appearances. In addition to playing hit music, they’ll also have coverage of events in the game world and provide comedic commentary.

In addition to the Drake and Travis Scott rumors, DJ Khaled’s apparent involvement was rumored in February, and rapper Danny Brown has been trying to get back into the mix, too.
