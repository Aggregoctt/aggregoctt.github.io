What happened in Episode 2?

Episode 2 – titled ‘The Commander and the Grey Lady’ – was something of a bottle episode, largely playing out in and around Henry Muck’s stately home, and focussing on his own mental breakdown.

The action takes place at Henry’s 40th birthday party, which brings back terrible memories for Muck, as he witnessed his father’s suicide on his 40th birthday.

Yasmine has organised a costume party for the ocassion, and invited her husband’s friends, family, a potential business partner offering him a job at Tender, and the Labour MP who recently defeated Henry in a by-election.

But while they are partying hard, Henry is spiralling, snorting every drug in sight, arguing about the proletariat, and considering taking his own life.

He makes a scene at his birthday dinner, before heading off to a nearby pub with an old friend, where he beats a local to a pulp. It’s then revealed that said friend is actually a mirage of his father, which pushes Henry over the edge.

Henry heads to the garage, turns on the engine of his dead dad’s car, and tries to kill himself via carbon monoxide poisoning. But then he hear’s Yasmine’s voice in his head, which inspires Henry to live.

He narrowly escapes, and reunites with Yasmine via a spot of sex on that same car’s bonnet. A seemingly rejuvenated Henry then says he’ll take the job he was offered earlier in the episode, and states that he wants to try for a baby.

But the look on Yasmine’s face in that moment suggests she doesn’t believe all is suddenly well with her husband, and a baby is the last thing they should be bringing into the world.
