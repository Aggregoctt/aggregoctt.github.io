Umamusume now has its own fan-made Roblox game mode, bringing the adrenaline-fueled horse races to the blocky game.

Umamusume: Pretty Derby has rapidly grown in popularity overseas following its global release, with players delving into its unique mix of idol training and tactical horse racing. Now, that international fanbase has sparked a new creation, a fully playable fan-made mode on Roblox.

Titled Umamusume Tokyo Racecourse, the game lets players race around Tokyo’s iconic racetrack. Built entirely within Roblox, the game appears to replicate key mechanics from Cygames’ official title, including stamina management, positional tactics, and burst acceleration.

Umamusume gets its very own Roblox game mode

Footage from the game even shows characters activating skills mid-race, complete with the Umamusume voiceover lines used in the original title, which only add to the urgency of each race.

However, it’s not just Umamusume characters on the track – the comical clip shows the likes of Sega’s Sonic the Hedgehog and Dragon Ball’s Goku turning up to flex their super speed.

While the visual style is simplified, the attention to racing mechanics and timing makes it stand out, especially given that races are shown from a first-person perspective.

As of writing, Umamusume Tokyo Racecourse has over 1k favorites and 201 active users, so you’ll be able to duke it out against other Umamusume enthusiasts if you do choose to compete. Who knows, you may even win the all-important final.
