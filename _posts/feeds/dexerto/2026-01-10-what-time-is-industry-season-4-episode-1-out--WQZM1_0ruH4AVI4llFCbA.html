Acclaimed TV drama Industry is returning for Season 4, so here’s details of when, where, and at what time you can watch Episode 1.

Industry had humble beginnings, but since launching at the tail-end of 2020, the TV show has grown exponentially, both in terms of audience numbers, and critical acclaim.

Playing like a cross between Wall Street and Succession, the series follows the trials and tribulations of several young graduates at a prestigious investment bank in London.

Season 4 promises more intrigue and drama, as well as some superb guest stars, so here’s everything you need to know about the show’s return…

Industry returns to HBO Max on Sunday, January 11, at 6pm PT/9pm ET.

The series is a co-production between HBO and the BBC, so if you are in the UK, the new episode will be likely available on the BBC iPlayer on the morning of Monday, January 12, while it drops on BBC One at 10.40pm that same day.

How many episodes are in Season 4?

As with the previous three seasons, there are eight episodes in Season 4. Here’s how the release schedule looks for US viewers (so just add a day if you are in the UK):

  • Episode 1 – January 11
  • Episode 2 – January 18
  • Episode 3 – January 25
  • Episode 4 – February 1
  • Episode 5 – February 8
  • Episode 6 – February 15
  • Episode 7 – February 22
  • Episode 6 – March 1

What happens in Season 4 of Industry?

Here’s the official synopsis for Industry Season 4: “At the top of their game and living the lives they set out to have as Pierpoint grads, Harper and Yasmin are drawn into a high stakes, globetrotting cat-and-mouse game when a splashy fintech darling bursts onto the London scene.

“As Yasmin navigates her relationship with tech founder Sir Henry Muck and Harper is pulled into the orbit of enigmatic executive Whitney Halberstram, their twisted friendship begins to warp and ignite under the pressure of money, power, and the desire to be on top.”

The likes of Myha’la, Marisa Abela, Kit Harrington, Miriam Petche, Sagar Radia, and Ken Leung all return for Season 4, while new cast members include Max Minghhella, Toheem Jimoh, Kiernan Shipka, Claire Forlani, Kal Penn, and Charlie Heaton.
