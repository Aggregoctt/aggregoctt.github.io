Fallout players rejoice, because we just got a major element from the games added into the show… And the next episode cannot arrive quick enough! So here’s what time Fallout Season 2 Episode 7 is out… Major spoilers ahead.

Let’s not beat around the wasteland here, we just got our first live action Super Mutant voiced by none other than Ron Perlman! That is a huge deal, not only because Super Mutants are some of the most iconic monsters in the franchise, but Perlman’s voice is equally synonymous with the games.

In case you didn’t know, he’s the one who narrates the start of the games and delivers that iconic line: “War never changes.” But in the TV show he voices the Super Mutant who saves the Ghoul from being impaled on the pole in New Vegas, and heals him using a chunk of uranium.

He even warns the Ghoul it’s up to the “abominations” to stop the Enclave from decimating the post-apocalyptic world even further. Woah. Well, let’s hope Fallout Season 2 Episode 7 lives up to expectations after that!

Fallout Season 2 Episode 7 will start streaming on Prime Video at 12 am PT/3pm ET on Wednesday January 28. Here’s what time it streams in other timezones:

  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Summer Time
  • 10am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 1.30pm India
  • 5pm Tokyo
  • 7pm Sydney

Prime Video has continued with the usual Wednesday slot after dropping the premiere early during the holidays. But now we’re into the last few episodes of the season, things are really going to start ramping up.

We even got a reveal that Barbara Howard (Frances Turner) was blackmailed by Dr. Wilzig (Michael Emerson) into delivering the speech at the end of Season 1 about Vault-Tec dropping the bombs themselves. This basically means that the Enclave are the real villains of the whole franchise (but anyone who’s played the games will know this already!)

How many episodes are there in Fallout Season 2?

There are eight episodes in Fallout Season 2, which means there are only two episodes left to watch including Episode 7. So it’s time to make the most of the remaining chapters, because there’s going to be a long wait for Season 3.

For now, the final episode of Fallout Season 2 will be released on February 4, 2026. Thankfully, Season 3 has already been confirmed, and Todd Howard told Dexerto how it will change from the games.

“I don’t wanna spoil anything, but what I would say is the show has an opportunity more so than a single game to pull things because we can jump locations, we can jump timelines,” he explained, before adding “We can do more than we thought we could when we first started, if that makes sense.”
