KFC is turning its signature flavors into candy with a brand new jelly bean lineup.

According to viral food insider page Snackolator, fast food giant Kentucky Fried Chicken is gearing up to launch jelly beans flavored like fried chicken, sweet corn, and gravy.

The product has already surfaced on Redstonefoods, a distributor specializing in novelty snacks and sodas.

The listing says the jelly beans will hit shelves in time for Easter 2026, meaning fans could be popping fried chicken-flavored candy before April.

KFC pulls another wild collab out of the bucket

So far, there’s no word on how much these beans will cost or how close they’ll come to actually mimicking the taste of a bucket of the Colonel’s famous chicken.

The move continues KFC’s streak of bizarre food crossovers in 2025. The chain grabbed attention earlier this year with launches like gravy-flavored gelato and even fried chicken toothpaste.

Meanwhile, KFC Canada went all in on pickles with an entire menu inspired by them, including a Pickle Sandwich, Loaded Pickle Fries, and to wash it all down? A wild Pickle Pepsi with a splash of brine.

With these jelly beans, KFC is doubling down on turning its classic flavors into unexpected formats. Whether they’re a novelty gag gift or a genuine snack, the limited-edition candy is already sparking buzz months before release.
