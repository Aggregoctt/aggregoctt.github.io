How to redeem codes

Before you can redeem your codes in Volleyball Legends, you’ll need to make sure that you’re at least level 15 to redeem codes. Once you’ve hit that level, follow these short steps to claim your freebies:

  1. Tap the orange ‘Shop’ button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. In the menu that pops up, select the purple ‘Codes’ option on the bottom left side of the Shop menu.
  3. Type a working code and press ‘Use Code’.
  4. If successful, a notification will confirm your rewards.
