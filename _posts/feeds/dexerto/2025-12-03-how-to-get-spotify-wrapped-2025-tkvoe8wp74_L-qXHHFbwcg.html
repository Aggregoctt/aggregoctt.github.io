It’s that time of year again, and Spotify Wrapped 2025 has finally landed, showing how many hours (or days) you’ve listened to music, your top artists, and so much more.

Every December, different platforms like Twitch, newly YouTube, and so many more offer fans a year-long recap of everything they’ve consumed over the last year.

Well, Spotify is among the most popular, often calling people out for their impressive listening time or questionable (not judging) music tastes. So, if you want to find out who your top artists are, here’s how to get your Spotify Wrapped for 2025.

How to get your 2025 Spotify Wrapped

Just like previous years, all you need to do is head over to your Spotify app on your phone. From there, you’ll either get a notification pop-up to view your Spotify Wrapped or have an icon you can click to access it.

Click that, and let the slides show your listening time, your top artists, top songs, and a few facts based on your listening.

If it’s not appearing for you yet, then it’s likely still rolling out in your country, so give it a few hours and check back later.
