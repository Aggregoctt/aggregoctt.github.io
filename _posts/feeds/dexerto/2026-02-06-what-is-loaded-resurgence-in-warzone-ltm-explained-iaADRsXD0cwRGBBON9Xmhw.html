Warzone Season 2 is here, bringing new weapons, Ranked Play, and a wintery makeover to Rebirth Island. It’s also brought back the Loaded Resurgence mode.

Although Ranked Play Resurgence takes place on Haven’s Hollow, Rebirth Island’s Winter Refresh brought new POIs, infil gas explosions, and redesigned buildings — and you can explore the new version with the return of the Loaded Resurgence LTM, so here’s how it works.

Warzone Loaded Resurgence explained

Loaded Resurgence plays almost the same as regular Resurgence, but you’ll spawn in with your loadout. Played on Rebirth Island, you’ll still have to keep one squad member alive to keep your team in the fight, but it’s much faster-paced as you don’t need to loot for weapons.

Instead of looting for Perks, you’ll earn more with each kill. Get enough, and you’ll earn the Specialist bonus, granting you every Perk in the game.

The mode was last seen back in 2024’s Modern Warfare 3 Season 3 Reloaded update.

When does Loaded Resurgence leave?

Loaded Resurgence will be available until Thursday, February 19, before being replaced with Loaded Resurgence Casual. This is the same mode but with bots, making it more casual-friendly, and it’ll run until March 5.

Then, the new Iron Gauntlet mode arrives, bringing a new competitive mode to Verdansk with increased health, no free loadout drops, and no Gulag.
