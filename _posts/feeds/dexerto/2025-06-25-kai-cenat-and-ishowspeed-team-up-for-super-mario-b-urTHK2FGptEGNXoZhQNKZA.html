Kai Cenat and IShowSpeed are back with a new streaming challenge, and this one could be their longest yet, as they intend to beat every Super Mario Bros game in one sitting.

On June 25, the duo will begin a Super Mario Bros. marathon, aiming to beat every mainline Mario game – although it’s not exactly clear how far back they’ll be going to start the challenge.

Kai and Speed team up for another gaming marathon

The stream will start at 4pm EST (9pm BST / 1pm PT), on June 25. Which game they start with is uncertain, but fan pages are claiming they will beat every single Super Mario game, which would be 24 in total.

The streaming pair have made a name for themselves with their viral, high-stakes marathons. In December 2024, they went live for a Fortnite challenge, promising not to stop until they secured a Victory Royale. The stream lasted nearly two full days, before they finally won on their 80th match.

One of their most memorable collaborations came in July 2024, when they streamed a full run of Chained Together while literally chained to each other. The stream lasted over 12 hours and saw the two battle frustrating jumps, high-stakes platforming, and each other.

The game was completed with an in-game time of just over 11 hours, and the stream quickly went viral thanks to their chaotic chemistry and constant setbacks.

They’ve also teamed up for a 105-hour Minecraft Hardcore stream, eventually defeating the Ender Dragon after days of trial and error. Their unpredictable chemistry and relentless energy have turned their marathons into major internet events.

This new Super Mario challenge is set to test their endurance all over again. With some titles taking hours to beat on their own, clearing the full lineup could stretch well beyond a day. The event will be streamed live on both Kai Cenat’s and IShowSpeed’s channels, on Twitch and YouTube, respectively.
