Joe Rogan was left furious after mentalist Oz Pearlman managed to guess the pin number for his bank account during his appearance on the JRE Podcast.

Over the last few years, Oz Pearlman has become an internet celebrity. The mentalist has wowed celebrities, sports teams, and others with his ability to make accurate predictions and uncover secrets with a few tricks.

The mentalist has made multiple appearances on national TV too, and is the exact type of interesting personality that Joe Rogan picks up as a regular guest for his Joe Rogan Experience Podcast.

Oz appeared on episode 2332 of the JRE show, and while the podcast host was excited to chat with the mentalist, Rogan was left gobsmacked less than 20 minutes into the show. 

Oz Pearlman guesses Joe Rogan’s pin number

After revealing that he had a difficult start to life as a mentalist, getting hundreds of predictions wrong, Pearlman urged Rogan to let him demonstrate his skills and let him guess his ATM pin. 

“Lie to me, do not tell your real ATM pin code, obviously. Make up a four-digit number,” he told Rogan, who quickly picked out 2020 as his number. After a few questions, the mentalist was able to figure out the podcast host’s pin code. Of course, he didn’t show it off to the camera.

“Yeah. That is weird. I’m skeptical because I got that pin code in the mail, you know,” he said. “Yeah, I don’t like that,” Rogan added before calling it “weird” for a second time. 

Timestamp of 16:34

While Pearlman tried to explain some of the reasoning behind his deduction, Rogan was quick to shut him down with more skepticism. 

Yet, the mentalist noted that it couldn’t be fake because Rogan “didn’t want it to happen” and wasn’t in on the bit. He added that there is “causality” for everything. 

“I can rework your memory, thats a lot of the techniques I do,” Pearlman added. “People think that your memory is infallible, that what you see is some sort of blueprint, and it’s not. It has to do with how you feel in the moment, the same way hypnosis works.”
