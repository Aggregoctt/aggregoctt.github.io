MrBeast has claimed another Guinness World Records milestone after building what officials are calling the largest continuous LED video floor on the planet.

The creator revealed the achievement while sharing a look at the Season 2 set for Beast Games.

Guinness World Records confirmed the new benchmark on November 24, announcing that the circular LED surface spans 5,183.6 square feet, or 481.67 square meters, and surpasses the size of a basketball court.

Guinness confirms record-breaking video floor

The announcement followed MrBeast’s post showing himself standing in the center of the enormous display, writing, “Largest video floor in the world, I’M SO EXCITED FOR SEASON 2 OF BEAST GAMES.”

Guinness noted the floor is “about the length of a blue whale,” validating the record as the largest continuous LED video floor built to date.

Season 2 of Beast Games is currently in production, though MrBeast has not yet shared details on how the display will factor into the competition. The first season of the show recorded dozens of Guinness World Records across its set and challenges.
