Waited years for a follow-up to Bully? Well, Agefield High: Rock the School could fill that void as it just perfectly sums up the mid-2000s. 

Even though 2026 is set to be the year of Grand Theft Auto 6, there are plenty of other games releasing in the next 12 months that are going to have plenty of intrigue around them. That includes Agefield High: Rock the School. 

The new game, which is set to release in early 2026, has become a bit of a viral hit on social media, with plenty of comparisons to Rockstar Games’ Bully title. 

It comes from Refugium Games and sees players play as Sam, a brand-new student at Agefield High. Alongside his friends, Kale and Axel, the trio plan to ‘rock the school’ before leaving. You’ll also be taken back to 2002, seeing as thats where its set.

Agefield High: Rock The School trailer

Just like Bully, the game will have you balancing between school life, making money, and getting into a relationship. You can choose to skip school, mow lawns, and make money to buy new clothes and items, but you will have to avoid being spotted. 

There are also five different classes for you to attend at school, which are: English, Math, German, Geography, and Music. They all appear to have small mini-games, just like Bully did. 

And with it being high school, you’ll have to avoid some of the more popular kids. That includes Seth, who is the game’s antagonist. You’ll have missions where you have to avoid his gaze, otherwise, you’ll end up in a fist fight – and he looks to have a fair bit more support than Sam. 

On top of comparisons to Bully, Agefield High: Rock the School has also drawn comparisons to American Pie too. It’s got so much of that early 2000s nostalgia running through it, especially with awkward teenagers and relationships at the heart of it.

As noted, there isn’t a solid release date yet. The Steam listing just has it down as ‘Q2 2026’ but it has drawn a fair bit of love from social media. 

“American Pie Simulator before GTA6? Hell yeah guys!” one said. “Not bully 2, but close enough,” another added.
