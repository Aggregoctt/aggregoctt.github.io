Unlike the original adventure, Elden Ring Nightreign relies heavily on its multiplayer. Naturally, with servers comes maintenance and downtime.

Elden Ring was extremely popular when it released, and its standalone sequel, Nightreign, is no different, with thousands of players looking to destroy bosses and complete all the quests the adventure has to offer.

However, it’s hard to do that when the game suddenly doesn’t load up, or when you plan to play with friends only to find you can’t get into a lobby. So, if you’re struggling to play or want to plan your battles accordingly, here’s the server status for Elden Ring Nightreign.

Elden Ring Nightreign server status

At the time of writing, the Elden Ring Nightreign servers are currently down for maintenance to deploy Patch 1.01.1, as shared on the Elden Ring X account.

Thankfully, the servers won’t be down for too long, with the game returning three hours after the downtime began.

You can expect to see Elden Ring Nightreigns servers back at the following times:

  • PT: 4 am
  • ET: 9 am
  • BST: 12 pm
  • CEST: 1 pm
  • JST: 8 pm

How to check the Elden Ring Nightreign server status

The best way to keep updated on the Nightreign server status is by bookmarking this article and checking back whenever you can’t get into the game. When the servers are down, we’ll be quick to update this, showcasing when you can expect the servers to be back up and running.

On top of this, keep an eye on the Elden Ring X account, as they regularly share maintenance times and dates.
