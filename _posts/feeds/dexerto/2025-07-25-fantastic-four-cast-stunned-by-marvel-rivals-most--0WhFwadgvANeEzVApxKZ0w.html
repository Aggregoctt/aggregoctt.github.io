The stars of The Fantastic Four: First Steps were blown away when shown one of the most infamous Invisible Woman skins in Marvel Rivals.

Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Joseph Quinn, and Ebon Moss-Bachrach have been doing press in recent weeks for Fantastic Four: First Steps. This is the first movie starring Marvel’s First Family since Disney acquired Fox back in 2019, and the first time the characters have appeared in the MCU.

However, Reed Richards and co. have featured in plenty of Marvel games in recent years, including Marvel Rivals.

As part of the press tour for the film, the cast were all shown their respective characters in-game, and one Sue Storm skin blew them all away.

Sue Storm Malice skin earns thumbs up from Fantastic Four cast

In a clip with Loserfruit that was shared on X, the four were all shown the Malice skin for Invisible Woman, an outfit that sent thirsty fans into meltdown when it was released in January, and has since become one of the most popular in the game.

The cast was all clearly taken aback by the striking look, with Pedro Pascal, in particular, looking very surprised at how his onscreen wife was portrayed.

Meanwhile, Vanessa Kirby, who plays Sue Storm in the movie, was quick to quip about the outfit appearing in the follow-up.

“Thank you. This is my favorite bit of her as well,” joked Kirby, before looking back at her castmates and saying: “Guys, watch out for the sequel, you better watch it.”

“Costume designer, take note,” replied Pascal.

This isn’t the first time that Kirby has mentioned Malice, either. In an episode of the Happy Sad Confused podcast, she explained that she would love to play Sue’s darker half in a future story.

Of course, this was most likely a joke between the actors and doesn’t suggest that Malice will actually appear in a First Steps sequel or Avengers: Doomsday, but even just a small nod would send both comic fans and Marvel Rivals players into a frenzy.
