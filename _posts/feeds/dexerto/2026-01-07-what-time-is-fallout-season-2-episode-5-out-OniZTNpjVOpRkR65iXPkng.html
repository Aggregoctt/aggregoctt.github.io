After waiting over a year, Fallout fans have finally got what they’ve been desperate to see thanks to a monstrous cliffhanger and we’re already desperate to know what time Fallout Season 2 Episode 5 is out. Thankfully, there isn’t too long to wait. Spoilers ahead!

Episode 4 was a huge turning point for Season 2, as civil war explodes within the Brotherhood when Quintus (Michael Cristofer) starts a firefight with Maximus (Aaron Moten) after he confesses to leaving the ghoul children alive and murdering Paladin Harkness (Kumail Nanjiani).

Not only that, but Lucy (Ella Purnell) and the Ghoul (Walton Goggins) finally made it to New Vegas! The Easter eggs and references were flying all over the place. From Lucy getting hooked on Buffout and using Addictol to get rid of it, the Elvis inspired gang — the Kings — who had turned into ghouls, and then of course the behemoth Deathclaw emerging from the Gomorrah Hotel and Casino…

Basically, it was an all-out episode that leaned into what makes the Fallout franchise so great in the first place. So, here’s what time Fallout Season 2 Episode 5 is out.

Fallout Season 2 Episode 5 will start streaming on Prime Video at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Wednesday January 14. Here’s what time it streams in other timezones:

  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Summer Time
  • 10am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 1.30pm India
  • 5pm Tokyo
  • 7pm Sydney

Prime Video has continued with its normal release schedule now after it started by dropped the Fallout Season 2 premiere early for the holidays. Episode five has some big shoes to fill after the craziness of Episode four, especially since the show is starting to move into the back half of the season.

How many episodes are there in Fallout Season 2?

There are eight episodes in Fallout Season 2, the same as the first season. The streaming service decided not to bump up the episode count following the first season’s success. But that means audiences have two whole months of new Fallout episodes to enjoy, instead of just binging them all on release day.

The final episode of Fallout Season 2 will be released on February 4, 2026. Don’t worry, Prime Video has already renewed the series for a third outing and Todd Howard told Dexerto how Season 3 will change from the games.

“I don’t wanna spoil anything, but what I would say is the show has an opportunity more so than a single game to pull things because we can jump locations, we can jump timelines,” he explained, before adding “We can do more than we thought we could when we first started, if that makes sense.”
