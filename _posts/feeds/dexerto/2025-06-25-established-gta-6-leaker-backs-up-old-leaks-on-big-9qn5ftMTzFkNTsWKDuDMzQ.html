A GTA 6 leak has revealed some of the key missions that fans will have to play out as Jason and Lucia, and it backs up claims from 2023.

At the start of May, Rockstar Games broke Grand Theft Auto fans’ hearts by announcing that GTA 6 would be delayed until May 26, 2026. That agonizing delay was, however, followed by the reveal of trailer two and close to 100 new screenshots.

While fans have been digging through the fresh trailer and screenshots for new details, GameRoll dropped a big set of leaks about Vice City, the game’s development, features, and storyline. 

Those leaks downplayed some of the most anticipated things that fans had hoped for, including being able to play music outside of cars. Yet, they also reaffirmed some earlier leaks that had initially been laughed off by GTA fans.

GTA 6 leaks back up claims of key missions with Russians 

That includes parts of the single-player missions. In GameRoll’s leak, missions around a Russian drug lord were mentioned, including one where Jason follows a plane smuggling drugs for Russians.

This mission was mentioned in the 2023 leaks, which claimed that this leads to the Russians becoming a mission giver, as well as a job where drugs are stolen and fall into the ocean. Jason, apparently, goes and recovers them in a later mission. 

As Redditor FrozenFrost2000 highlighted, the leaks also mentioned Jason having to pick Lucia up from prison – something that was highly teased in trailer two – as well as planning heists from a nightclub known as “Vault.”

According to GameRoll, Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to follow Red Dead Redemption 2 in having chapters for each part of the story. The fifth chapter is reportedly in “development hell. 

The leaker hasn’t provided any updates since early June, however.
