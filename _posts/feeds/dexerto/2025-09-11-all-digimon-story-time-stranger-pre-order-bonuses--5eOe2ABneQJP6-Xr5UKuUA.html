Digimon Story: Time Stranger pre-orders are live, giving fans a look at all the bonuses you can get with each edition and how much they cost in your region.

Digimon Story: Time Stranger’s October 2 release is fast approaching, and soon players will be able to embark on a time-traveling adventure with more than 450 Digimon. Building on the success of past entries, the game introduces revamped turn-based combat, streamlined exploration, and deeper party customization to keep fans engaged.

So, if you’ve played the game’s demo and are planning to pick up the RPG at launch and want to know exactly what bonuses and special editions are on offer, our Digimon Story: Time Stranger pre-order and editions guide has you covered.

Pre-order bonuses

If you pre-order any edition of Digimon Story: Time Stranger, you’ll receive a pack with the following exclusive bonuses:

  • Black Agumon and Black Gabumon as recruitable Digimon
  • School uniform costumes for Dan and Kanan Yuki
  • Adventure Item Set
    • Medical Spray DX ×3
    • Multi-Recovery DX ×3
    • Full Revival Spray ×3
  • Attacker Attachment I & Guarder Attachment I equipment

These items will be available on all platforms and added to your account at launch.
