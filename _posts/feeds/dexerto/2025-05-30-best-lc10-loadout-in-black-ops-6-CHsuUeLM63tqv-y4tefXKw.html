The LC10 is back as part of Black Ops 6 Season 4, being a fantastic choice if you’re looking for a versatile SMG for all ranges.

This full-auto submachine gun has “excellent accuracy and range,” meaning it’s capable of outgunning other SMGs and even ARs at longer ranges. Combined with its high mobility, it’s already one of the best guns in the game.

It only gets better with meta attachments, so here’s everything you need to build the best LC10 loadout in Black Ops 6’s multiplayer.

Meta LC10 loadout attachments

  • Muzzle: Suppressor
  • Barrel: Long Barrel
  • Underbarrel: Ranger Foregrip
  • Magazine: Extended Mag I
  • Rear Grip: Commando Grip

For the best Black Ops 6 LC10 loadout, we kick off with the Suppressor to keep your shots hidden from the minimap. Then, the Long Barrel will increase its damage range, ensuring you’re still competitive at further ranges.

The Ranger Foregrip helps keep horizontal recoil under control while boosting your sprint speed, and the Commando Grip ensures you’re always ready for a fight by boosting ADS and sprint-to-fire speed.

Finally, it’s worth using the Extended Mag I so you can take down several enemies before having to reload.

Best Perks & equipment

  • Perk 1: Dexterity
  • Perk 2: Hunter’s Instinct
  • Perk 3: Slipstream
  • Perk Greed: Ninja
  • Wildcard: Perk Greed
  • Lethal: Semtex
  • Tactical: Flashbang
  • Field Upgrade: Assault Pack

We’ve equipped three red Perks so you can get the Enforcer Specialty, temporarily boosting your healing rate and movement speed after each kill.

We start with Dexterity, which keeps your weapon steady when jumping, sliding, and diving, which is extremely useful when using an SMG. Then, Hunter’s Instinct highlights the next closest enemies’ location on your minimap after each kill, ensuring you’re ready for the next fight.

Slipstream removes Tac Sprint and increases your movement speed, meaning you’re never caught in that annoying animation. Finally, you’ll want to equip the Perk Greed Wildcard with Ninja, as it’ll let you stealthily move around the map.

Semtex and Flashbang are a reliable combo that lets you blind enemies and deal quick splash damage, and you can use the Assault Pack to stock up on ammo.

Is the LC10 meta in Black Ops 6?

Yes, the LC10 is meta in Black Ops 6. With its low recoil and great mobility, it’s almost impossible to go wrong with Season 4’s SMG.

It has a fast TTK at range, but you might find it struggles against meta SMGs like the Kompakt 92 and Ladra at close range. However, its low recoil makes it much more versatile.

How to unlock LC10

The LC10 SMG is unlocked on Page 3 of the Season 4 Battle Pass. It’s part of the free pass, so simply play the game enough and you’ll have it unlocked.

If you miss out on unlocking it during Season 4, you can find it in The Armory, where it can be unlocked through an XP challenge.
