Reese’s and Oreo are joining forces for a two-way collaboration that delivers two products: the Reese’s Oreo Cup and the Oreo Reese’s Cookie. Both will roll out nationwide starting September 2025.

The Reese’s Oreo Cup combines a peanut butter cup with milk chocolate and white creme, with Oreo cookie crumbs mixed into the filling. It will be a permanent addition to the Reese’s lineup and sold in King Size, Standard, and Miniature Cups.

The Oreo Reese’s Cookie looks like a classic Oreo but swaps in layers of Reese’s peanut butter creme with Oreo cookie crumbs. It will be available for a limited time this fall, then return as a permanent Oreo offering in January 2026.

Reese’s joins forces with Oreo

Presales begin August 18 on The Hershey’s Store for the Reese’s Oreo Cup, with Oreo’s website opening early-access sign-ups starting July 30. Both products head to retail shelves nationwide in early September.

News of the collab spread across social media, with creators like Snachwithzach sharing it on their channels. Fans flocked to the comments on his video to share their thoughts, and many are excited.
