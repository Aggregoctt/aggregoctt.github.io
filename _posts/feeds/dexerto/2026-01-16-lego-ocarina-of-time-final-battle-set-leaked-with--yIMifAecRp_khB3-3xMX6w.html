A new The Legend of Zelda Lego set has leaked early, giving fans an early look at its new Ocarina of Time-themed model.

Dubbed The Final Battle, the build recreates Ocarina of Time’s climactic finale, featuring a towering brick-built Ganon wielding massive swords as Link and Zelda desperately try to rid Hyrule of the Demon King.

According to details shared by Brick Tap, The Final Battle: Ocarina of Time includes 1003 pieces, launches on March 1, 2026, and is priced at $129.99 / €119.99 / £99.99.

New Zelda Ocarina of Time set leaked

The set is said to include Link, Zelda, and Ganondorf minifigures, along with Navi the Fairy, a larger brick-built Ganon, Megaton Hammer, mini recovery hearts, and the Triforce emblem, which is built into the display.

For context, Lego’s first Zelda release was the Great Deku Tree set, which launched as a premium collector build priced at $299.99 / €299.99 / £259.99. By comparison, this Ocarina of Time set lands at a much lower price point and still packs one of the most iconic moments from Zelda history.

The new Ocarina of Time set isn’t the only Lego news that has caused a stir in January, as its new Pokemon sets triggered massive online queues and rapid sellouts.
