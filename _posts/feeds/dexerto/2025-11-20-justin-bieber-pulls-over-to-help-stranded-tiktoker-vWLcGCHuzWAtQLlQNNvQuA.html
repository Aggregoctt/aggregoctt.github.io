A TikToker is going viral after Justin Bieber pulled over to help him when his car broke down on the side of the road.

The encounter was shared by creator Bukumusic, who posted the footage on TikTok. In the video, he sits beside his stalled car when Bieber approaches and asks if he’s alright. Bukumusic quickly realizes who he’s talking to and asks, “Are you Justin Bieber?” Bieber replies, “Yeah. What’s up, bro?”

The moment hits him hard. He explains on camera that he recently moved to the city, has been struggling, and was feeling overwhelmed when the car broke down. He tells Bieber, “My life is crazy,” before adding that the singer then offered “the best prayer I probably ever had in my life.”

Bukumusic’s video captures their full exchange, including his initial disbelief, repeated “I love you, bro,” reactions, and Bieber offering support before the clip cuts out.

The video has amassed over two million views as viewers react to the unexpected roadside meeting between the creator and one of the world’s biggest artists.

One user commented: “Having the worst day of your life and the universe gives you Justin Bieber.”

“The way you looked at him and asked, ‘Are you Justin Bieber?’ like he was an angel sent to help you,” another said.

A third user joked: “Is Justin Bieber your fairy godmother?”
