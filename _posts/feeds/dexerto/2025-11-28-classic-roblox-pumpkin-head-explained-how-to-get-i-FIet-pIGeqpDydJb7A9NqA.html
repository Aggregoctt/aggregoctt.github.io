It’s been eight years since we last saw the Classic Roblox Pumpkin Head in the Roblox Store, but it’s finally back, and you’ll need to be quick (and have plenty of Robux) to bag it.

Whether you’re diving into YBA, Blox Fruits, Steal a Brainrot, The Forge, or any of the thousands of popular games inside Roblox, it’s always worth looking your best. Just like Fortnite, some players get the chance to show off their rare and unique skins, as some cosmetics are often only available for a short time.

Well, just like the famous Headless Horseman bundle, Roblox has another extremely rare item for you to buy, and it’s not been in the store for eight years. So, here’s all you need to know and how to get hold of the Classic Roblox Pumpkin Head.

What is the Classic Roblox Pumpkin Head?

The Classic Roblox Pumpkin Head is a hat you can buy in the Roblox store that was originally found in the marketplace back in 2007. It remained there every October for ten years, finally being taken off its seasonal appearance back in 2017.

Since then, the item has been impossible to buy, making it extremely rare and pretty highly desired among those looking for rare items to kit their avatars out with.

Originally, it cost 250 Robux, but since it was readded in 2025, it’s had an impressive 10% markup, now costing 2,500 Robux instead.

How to get the classic Pumpkin Head

The Pumpkin Head can be bought on Roblox’s Marketplace for 2,500 Robux, but it’s only available for 24 hours, meaning by the end of November 28, 2025, it will be taken down from the store.

So, be quick to bag this iconic Classic Pumpkin Head before it disappears — and hopefully not for another eight years.
