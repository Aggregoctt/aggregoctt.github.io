Despite being romantic, funny, and never scared of a good conflict, Dispatch doesn’t hold back on heartbreaking scenes, and Chase’s death at the end of Episode 6 is no different.

One of the best things about a good narrative game is how much it can destroy or warm your heart. One minute you’re leading the team and having a lovely housewarming party, the next you’re trying to stop a fight between two superheroes and potentially losing one for good.

Well, that’s exactly what happened in Episode Six of Dispatch, when one hero decided to essentially sacrifice themselves for another. But do they actually die, and most importantly, can you save them from their fate?

Does Chase die in Dispatch Episode 6?

Yes, it seems like Chase dies at the end of Episode 6.

After an argument with Invisigal, Chase leaves the housewarming party, and Vis heads off to try and recover Mecha Man’s power source on her own. Chase eventually comes back to the apartment to apologize to Vis, and he and Robert get a nice, long heart-to-heart about what’s happened and being a hero.

Shortly after, Vis gets into trouble, hunted and toyed with by Shroud. In a last-ditch effort to save her life, Chase sacrifices himself by speeding to her rescue, a move that would age him and therefore kill him.

At the end, we see Chase rescue Vis and drop to the ground shortly after, with Vis unable to wake him, and Robert on the way.

So, technically, we don’t see him die properly, but given the lore that’s been established, that he ages faster when he uses his powers, it stands to reason that this is indeed a sacrifice that costs Chase’s life.

Nevertheless, there are still two more episodes to go, so there’s always a chance someone manages to save his life.

Can you save Chase?

Aside from a few choices and some unlocking of doors, there’s nothing you or Robert can do to save Chase in episode six.

The sacrifice appears to be set in stone, and no one has yet found a way to save his life.

How old is Chase?

Chase is technically 39 years old, but as explained in the game, when he moves faster, he also ages faster, which is why he has the look and body of an elderly man.

This is why, when he runs for the last time to save Invisigal, he ends up dying of old age.
