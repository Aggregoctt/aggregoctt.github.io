Pokemon TCG Pocket continues to shake up the mobile card game scene with fast-paced matches, stunning visuals, and most of all, a growing library of collectible sets. Here are all the expansions coming!

Each Pocket expansion introduces new cards, mechanics, and artwork that keep players coming back – whether you’re chasing powerful ex cards, adorable fan-favorites, or the latest Secret Rares.

To help you stay on top of it all, we’re tracking every mainline Pokemon TCG Pocket set as they’re released. From nostalgic throwbacks to newer regions, here’s what to expect from each drop.

Newest expansion – Wisdom of Sea and Sky (A4)

The upcoming Pokemon TCG Pocket set – Wisdom of Sea and Sky – drops July 30, 2025, and brings a wave of Johto nostalgia with over 200 new cards.

Headliners include Lugia ex, Ho‑Oh ex, the Shiny Red Gyarados, and the iconic starter trio, plus baby Pokemon like Pichu and Cleffa making their Pocket debut.

Alongside the fresh card lineup, the update introduces key trading upgrades. After a long wait, Shinedust will finally replace Trade Tokens, and a new Wishlist system will let players highlight their most-wanted cards. It’s a must-watch release for collectors and casuals alike.
