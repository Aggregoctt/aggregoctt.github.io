K-Pop Demon Hunters is already the most watched animated movie in Netflix history, and now the fantasy film is on course to break a record held by Ryan Reynolds and Dwayne Johnson.

K-Pop Demon Hunters hit Netflix on June 20, 2025, and quickly became a streaming, viral, and musical sensation.

The story of K-Pop superstars Mira, Rumi, and Zooey protecting the world from supernatural threats became an immediate hit, and just nine days later, Netflix announced that Demon Hunters was their most watched animated movie of all-time.

With songs from the movie also flying up the charts, and scenes all over social media, K-Pop Demon Hunters has been a bona fide phenomenon. And now, the film has another records in its sights…

K-Pop Demon Hunters could soon be Netflix’s most watched movie ever

K-Pop Demon Hunters is now the fourth most watched movie of all-time on Netflix, and the film is well on its way to ousting Ryan Reynolds and Dwayne Johnson vehicle Red Notice from the top spot.

The film has received a total of 158.8 million views since debuting, putting the Demon Hunters behind the aforementioned Red Notice (230.9 million views), as well as Carry-On (172.1 million), and Don’t Look Up (171.4 million).

But there’s time for it to overtake that trio, as Netflix decides ‘all-time’ lists using views in the first 91 days on release, meaning K-Pop has seven more weeks to reach Number 1.

Here’s how that Top 10 currently looks, up to the week of July 28-August 3:

  • 1. Red Notice – 230 million views
  • 2. Carry-On – 172 million views
  • 3. Don’t Look Up – 171 million views
  • 4. K-Pop Demon Hunters – 159 million views
  • 5. The Adam Project – 158 million views
  • 6. Bird Box – 157 million views
  • 7. Back in Action – 147 million views
  • 8. Leave the World Behind – 143 million views
  • 9. The Gray Man – 139 million views
  • 10. Damsel – 138 million views
