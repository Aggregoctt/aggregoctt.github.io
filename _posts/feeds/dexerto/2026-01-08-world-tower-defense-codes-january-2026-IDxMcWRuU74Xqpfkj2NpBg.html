Although the game includes a tutorial, you don’t need to complete it to access the codes menu. Simply follow these steps to redeem a code:

What are World Tower Defense codes used for?

Credits, tokens, and trophies are at the core of progression in World Tower Defense, and codes are one of the ways you can earn them.

Credits are used for most purchases, including unlocking units and equipment, while tokens are required for stronger units and late-game content. Trophies act more as progression milestones, unlocking higher-tier units and advanced maps once you reach certain thresholds.

Where to find more codes

World Tower Defense doesn’t have a fixed schedule for releasing codes. When new ones drop, they’re usually tied to updates, milestones, or community announcements shared by the developers on Discord. Checking back here is an easy way to stay updated without having to track multiple sources.
