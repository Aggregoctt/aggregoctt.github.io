NICKMERCS had his game of Arc Raiders interrupted by a worried neighbor who came by to check on the Twitch streamer after hearing him scream for help.

We’ve all had gaming sessions get a bit too heated. Whether it’s yelling for healing, tagging a target, shot calling, or even raging, sometimes noise-cancelling headphones are a bit too good.

That’s exactly what happened when NICKMERCS was competing in Arc Raiders and kept yelling about needing help, not expecting anyone from the outside world to come to his aid.

Sure enough, a neighbor heard Nick’s screams and came by to make sure the Twitch star was okay, creating an awkward situation for everyone involved.

Neighbor worried about NICKMERCS after intense Arc Raiders game

During the game, Nick’s neighbor came to the window to check in, leaving the streamer to explain that he was actually screaming at his Arc Raiders teammates.

“I’m yelling at my stream, I’m sorry. Thank you!”

NICKMERCS explained that his neighbor asked him if he needed help, adding that it was a random who was strolling through the area.

“I said, ‘me and my friend are wounded and we need your help’ and he walked up to the window screen!”

This isn’t the first time a loud game has resulted in a streamer getting IRL help he never asked for.
