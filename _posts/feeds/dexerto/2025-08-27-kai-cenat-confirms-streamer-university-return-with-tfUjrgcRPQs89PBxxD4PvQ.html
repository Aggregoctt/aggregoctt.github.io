Kai Cenat has confirmed that Streamer University will be returning for a second semester, but you might have actually missed it.

Over the last few years, Kai Cenat has been raising the bar for streamers and content creators with his massive events. His Mafiathon marathons have broken Twitch records, he’s produced massively viral clips from his Batam streams, and fans always look forward to him linking up with IShowSpeed.

However, back in May, he launched a new idea – Streamer University. The AMP member brought over 100 content creators together on a college campus to teach them some big lessons about streaming. 

It was a huge success for Kai and everyone involved, with a number of creators skyrocketing in viewership and follower numbers overnight. 

Kai Cenat Streamer University 2026 confirmed

Even though Kai suggested that he might not bring it back, he has since confirmed that a second semester will be happening at some point in 2026.

It came during his announcement for Mafiathon 3 – which he says will be his final Mafiathon stream – but only in the form of a small hint.

A poster saying “Welcome class of 2026” and “Streamer University” popped up for two seconds during a shootout scene. If you missed it, the poster features again for a second just after that brief shot, but the text isn’t clear. 

“YESSSSIR, seen that. Easter egg,” one excited fan commented. “That was a great easter egg,” another said.

As noted, Kai hinted at not redoing Streamer University at one point, but changed his mind on that. “No matter which direction I go, no matter which way I try to make sure things is good, I always get the bad end of the stick,” he said at the time.
