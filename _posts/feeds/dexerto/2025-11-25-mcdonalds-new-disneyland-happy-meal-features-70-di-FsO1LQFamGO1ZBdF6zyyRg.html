McDonald’s is rolling out a massive Disneyland Happy Meal collection to mark Disney’s 70th anniversary, offering 70 different toys themed around classic characters and park attractions.

Each Happy Meal will include two toys, giving fans a chance to build out the full set during the limited-time promotion.

“McDonald’s will launch on Dec. 2 a collector’s edition Happy Meal featuring 70 toys from Disney’s iconic worlds, including Star Wars, Pixar, Marvel, and more,” said Guillaume Huin, McDonald’s Senior Marketing Director.

All Disneyland Happy Meal toys

The collection spans characters, vehicles, and attraction pieces from across Disneyland’s history. McDonald’s says toy distribution will rotate throughout the promotion, and availability may vary by location.
