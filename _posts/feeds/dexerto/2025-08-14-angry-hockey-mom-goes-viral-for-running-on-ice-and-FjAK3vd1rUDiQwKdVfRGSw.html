A youth hockey game in Prague descended into chaos after a furious mother stormed the ice and confronted a referee over penalties against her son’s team, earning herself a viral moment in the process.

The incident took place earlier in August during a matchup between the “United Selects” and the “Ice Mammoths.” Viral clips posted to Reddit, YouTube, and other platforms show the woman charging onto the ice in protest after a string of calls went against her son’s squad, who were already trailing 9-0 late in the second period.

In the footage, she can be seen arguing with officials before shoving one of them, prompting another to push her toward the bench. Since spectators can’t be put in the penalty box, she was removed from the ice, but the confrontation didn’t end there.

The woman continued hurling insults and flipping the bird at the refs before storming out again. Even a man wearing a Canada hat, who appeared to try to defuse the situation, failed to stop her from making a second trip onto the ice and appearing to throw something at an official. She was once again escorted away before the video cuts off.

Enraged mom among other wild hockey incidents

According to the uploader, her frustration stemmed from multiple penalties called against her son’s team in quick succession.

This left them shorthanded in an already lopsided game, with just over a minute left in the second frame.

It’s not the first time an irate hockey mom has made headlines for clashing with an official. Earlier this year, a Pittsburgh mother was charged with assault after allegedly attacking a referee who ejected her son for blindsiding an opposing player.
