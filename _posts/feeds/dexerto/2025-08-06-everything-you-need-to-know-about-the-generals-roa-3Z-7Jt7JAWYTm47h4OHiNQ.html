The General’s Road Test Royale map puts players behind the wheel in a driving challenge unlike anything else in Fortnite. Designed in partnership with the Twitch Brand Partnership Studio (a specialized group within Amazon Ads), this map drops the battle royale chaos in favor of precision driving across three custom city environments. 

The goal is simple: drive smart, stay safe, and don’t crash. If you’re looking for a new way to test your skills, here’s how to play Road Test Royale, plus the island code you’ll need.

What is Road Test Royale in Fortnite?

Road Test Royale is a custom Fortnite island developed in partnership with The General and the Twitch Brand Partnership Studio, designed to challenge players with precision and safe driving rather than high-speed racing. 

Instead of a traditional race format, players will need to navigate tight city streets, avoid hazards, and complete driving challenges across three unique locations. Along the way, you’ll collect coins which can be spent on faster cars and new maps.

Crashing into the many obstacles or leaving the track will result in a penalty, so it’s best to take it slow and steady if you want the highest score. 

Created by the Twitch Brand Partnership Studio in collaboration with the developers at Look North World and the educators at National Driver Training, the mode brings an entertaining, yet informative twist to Fortnite’s usual chaos. It’s all about smart driving decisions, not speed, rewarding players who stay in control and make it through tricky cityscapes without crashing.

How to play Road Test Royale in Fortnite

To jump into Road Test Royale, follow these steps:

  1. Launch Fortnite and head to the Creative or Island Code menu.
  2. Enter the island code: 9524-2023-3031.
  3. Once loaded in, head to the first course.
  4. You’ll spawn and the start line and the race will begin. 

All Road Test Royale maps

Here’s a breakdown of what to expect from each area of the map:

San Francisco

Steep hills and sloped streets define San Francisco. Momentum plays a bigger role here, and it’s easy to roll if you take a corner too quickly. 

Miami

Palm trees and beachside roads give Miami a smoother layout, but it still packs in tight turns and visual distractions. Staying on course is tougher than it looks, and players will need to react quickly to sudden obstacles.

New York City

NYC is built for chaos. Narrow roads, dense layouts, and a drive through Central Park make this the most difficult area to navigate.

Each city offers a different challenge, and mastering all three will require a balance of patience and fast reflexes.
