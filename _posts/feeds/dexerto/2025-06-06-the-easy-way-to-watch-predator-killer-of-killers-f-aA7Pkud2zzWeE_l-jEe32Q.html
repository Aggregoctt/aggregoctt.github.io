Sci-fi horror franchise Predator has been given an animated update in Killer of Killers, and there’s an easy way to watch the new movie for free – just in time for the weekend. 

Filmmaker Dan Trachtenberg has been taking the Predator saga in a bold new direction, kicking off with 2022’s Prey, which stripped things back to a grounded, character-driven survival story set in 18th-century America.

The next live-action entry will arrive later this year with Predator: Badlands, marking another first for the franchise. By following an outcast member of the Yautja species, it’s the first time a Predator becomes the prey. 

Before then, we’ve got Trachtenberg’s Killer of Killers, an animated anthology feature that has already become a hit with the critics, earning a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score at the time of writing – and you can watch it on streaming right now. 

How to watch Predator: Killer of Killers for free

Predator: Killer of Killers is available to stream on Hulu now, where new and eligible returning subscribers can sign up for a free trial. It really is that simple!

The length of your free trial period depends on the subscription that you’re signing up for. For instance, they’re not available for certain offerings such as the Hulu no-ads plan or a bundle subscription.

However, if you sign up for the streaming service’s ad-supported subscription, you’ll get a whopping 30 days free. All you have to do is cancel before it auto-renews and you won’t be charged.
