One Battle After Another is the most nominated movie at this year’s Golden Globes with nine nods, while The White Lotus takes the TV crown with six.

One Battle After another is an early 2026 Golden Globes favorite, picking up nominations for Best Motion Picture (comedy or musical), and Best Director (Paul Thomas Anderson) as well as acting nods for Leonardo DiCaprio, Chase Infiniti, Sean Penn, Teyana Taylor, and Benicio Del Toro.

As for TV, The White Lotus also scored multiple acting nominations – as it does every year – while Adolescence received 5 nods, and Only Murders in the Building and Severance four apiece.

Below you’ll find all the nominations for this year’s Golden Globes, which are handed out on January 11, 2026.
