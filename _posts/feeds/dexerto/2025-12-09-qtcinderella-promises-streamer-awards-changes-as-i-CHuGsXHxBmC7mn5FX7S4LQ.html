QTCinderella has revealed that she is doing a “very hard pivot” with The Streamer Awards for 2026, promising to make some big changes.

The Streamer Awards has become one event that Twitch stars circle on their calendars as they get to celebrate the previous year of streaming. 

This year, Kai Cenat’s Mafiathon 3 took home four awards, while IShowSpeed was named Streamer of the Year. The event also had its fair share of drama, with the VTubing community being split over TheBurntPeanut winning VTuber of the Year and India Love having to apologize for taking to the stage after Breakout Streamer of the Year was awarded. 

QTCinderella, the host of the show, has confirmed that a 2026 instalment is already in the works with changes to make it “much better” than before.

QTCinderella “learned so much” from 2025 Streamer Awards

“I learned so much this year, genuinely. Every year I learn so, so much. I have so many good ideas for next year. I’m doing a very hard pivot, I’m changing a lot of things next year,” QT said during her December 8 stream.

“I promise you, with my whole heart, that next year is going to be better. I really, really know that I’ve figured it out for next year. So, thank you for trusting me. If I let some of you down, next year, I promise, I’ve got it figured out. 

“I had this epiphany and I was like ‘momma didn’t raise no quitter’ and next year is going to be a movie, I promise.’

QT added that a lot of the criticism she’s received for this year is “really valid,” but she is “excited” to make the 2026 show “so much better” than this year’s. 

She also noted that the show is “much bigger than it used to be” and that she has to “adjust for that,” with some of the jokes and bits that are conducted throughout the show.

QT included her joke about Rakai’s recent ban in that, claiming everyone in the room understood it, but not everyone at home would have. 

It remains to be seen what changes she’ll make moving forward.
