Grow a Garden’s Beanstalk event has players offering different Plants to Jack. The bigger the beanstalk, the more rewards and points. However, he doesn’t tell you what these plants are. Leaving requests like Woody Plants a little confusing.

It may be a simple, cozy, gardening adventure, but Roblox’s Grow a Garden doesn’t like to hand you easy events. Just like the cooking event, players need to uncover what Jack means when he asks for certain plants.

For some, like Vegetables or Berries, it’s a little easier, as you can just plant real-life veggies or berries. However, for others, like Woody Plants, it’s a bit trickier. So here’s all you need to know about Woody Plants in Grow a Garden.

All Woody Plants & how to get them

Currently, we’ve uncovered 27 different Woody Plants in Grow a Garden. As we uncover more, we’ll be adding them to the table below, so be sure to check back soon.

Some plants are currently unobtainable, but will still work if you have them in your inventory.

What are Woody Plants?

Woody Plants in Roblox’s Grow a Garden are essentially plants that grow off trees (wood) or ones with a more solid texture. For example, Apple, Mango, and Pear all grow on trees. While Rhubarb grows out of the ground, it’s tougher in texture, almost wooden.

It’s not as easy to decipher as the likes of Vegetable, Berries, or even Prickly Plants, but there is often an overlap, so check the table above and you’ll be fine.

When you find them, offer them to Jack. As we highlighted in the table above, the rarer the item, the more points you get. The player who gets the most points will get an exclusive reward when the event ends on August 23.
