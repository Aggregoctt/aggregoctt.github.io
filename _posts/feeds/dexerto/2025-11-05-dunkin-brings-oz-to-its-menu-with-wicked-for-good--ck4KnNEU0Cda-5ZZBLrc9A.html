Dunkin’ has launched a nationwide collaboration with Universal Pictures’ Wicked: For Good as the second installment of the two-part film adaptation arrives in theaters on November 21.

The tie-in includes themed menu items based on the movie’s leading duo, Elphaba and Glinda, and features green and pink branding across drinks, donut treats, and exclusive merch.

The collab arrives as Wicked: For Good closes out the musical’s cinematic story following the first film’s 2024 debut. Dunkin’ is supporting the release with an in-store campaign and a promotional video featuring Wicked stars Cynthia Erivo and Jonathan Bailey imagining what Oz would look like if it ran on Dunkin’.

Dunkin’ x Wicked menu and merch

Here is everything that Dunkin’ has released in its collab with Wicked:

  • Wicked Green Matcha: Iced matcha latte with brown sugar and toasted almond flavor
  • Wicked Pink Refresher: Sparkling drink with strawberry, dragonfruit, and blueberry flavors
  • Wicked MUNCHKINS: Old Fashioned Glazed donut holes with pink and green glaze

The collaboration also includes limited-edition merchandise themed after Wicked. Items include a 10-count MUNCHKINS tin for $9.99 with purchase, a Dunkin’ x Wicked tumbler with reusable pink and green straws, a straw topper set featuring Elphaba and Glinda icons, and character-themed cup sleeves.
