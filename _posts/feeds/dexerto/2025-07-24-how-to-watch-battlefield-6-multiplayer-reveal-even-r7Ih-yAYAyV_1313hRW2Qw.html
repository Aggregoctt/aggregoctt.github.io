Battlefield 6 is officially on the way, and we’ve now got a taste of what to expect following the official reveal trailer. We haven’t seen any multiplayer yet, but that’ll soon change with the multiplayer reveal event confirmed.

In the first trailer, we saw that NATO is engaged in a bloody conflict with private military company, Pax Armata, who have launched a devastating attack on New York. The campaign looks cinematic and action-packed, but it’s Battlefield’s signature large-scale multiplayer that keeps players coming back.

Luckily, we don’t have long to wait to see what DICE have been cooking, so here’s everything you need to know about the BF6 multiplayer reveal event.

The Battlefield 6 multiplayer reveal event takes place on Thursday, July 31, at 11.30 AM PT / 2.30 PM ET / 7.30 PM BST.

What to expect

Of course, we can expect to see the Battlefield 6 multiplayer gameplay, but we’ll also be able to see it live as content creators have been invited out to the event to play the game early.

Here, we’ll be able to see a handful of the new maps, weapons, and vehicles we’ll see in the full game, as well as what the latest version of Battlefield’s signature destruction looks like.

“Lock & load for the ultimate all-out warfare experience,” says the official description. “Fight in high-intensity infantry combat. Rip through the skies in aerial dogfights. Demolish your environment for a strategic advantage. In a war of tanks, fighter jets, and massive combat arsenals, your squad is the deadliest weapon.”

An Open Beta has also been confirmed, so perhaps we’ll get to play that shortly after the reveal, but that’s just wishful thinking.
