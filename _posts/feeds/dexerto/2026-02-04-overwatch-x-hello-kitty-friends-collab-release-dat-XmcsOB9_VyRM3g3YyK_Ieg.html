Overwatch has just revealed an upcoming crossover with Hello Kitty & Friends. Here’s everything you need to know about it.

Remember when the official Overwatch account changed its profile picture to the Talon logo? Well, it turns out that teaser was pointing toward a new era for the game.

As seen from the Spotlight event, we know that alongside new heroes, story content, events, and updates, players can also expect fresh cosmetic additions. 

And speaking of the latter, things are about to get more chaotic, though in the cutest way possible, thanks to an upcoming collaboration with Sanrio.

The Overwatch x Hello Kitty & Friends collaboration is set to be released on February 10, 2026, and the skins will be available in the shop until February 23, 2026.

Crossovers in the game tend to be limited-time events, as seen with past collaborations such as Persona, My Hero Academia, LE SSERAFIM, and others.

All Overwatch x Hello Kitty & Friends collab skins

A total of six heroes will be included in this crossover, with each of them getting outfits inspired by Sanrio characters. Below, we’ve listed all of them and their counterpart:

  • Juno as Hello Kitty
  • Kiriko as Cinnamoroll
  • Mercy as Pompompurin
  • D.Va as My Melody
  • Widowmaker as Kuromi
  • Lucio as Keroppi

With this lineup, it’s safe to say this crossover is shaping up to be one of the biggest in the game. It also marks the first time ever that our support hero Lucio, is getting a collab skin. And even a fitting one too, with the Keroppi theme.

The exact pricing for the full bundle and individual skins has yet to be revealed. However, Blizzard typically shares this information closer to release, and we’ll be sure to update this section once more details are available.

As with other collabs, there’s also a chance we may be getting a limited-time event. Though you’ll want to take this with a grain of salt for now until the update is live.
