How to redeem codes

Once codes become available, redeeming them is a quick process. Here’s how it can be done once you’ve loaded into the game:

  1. From the main menu, find and select the “Redeem Code” option in the menu.
  2. Type in your code exactly as shown.
  3. Confirm your entry and check your in-game mailbox for rewards.
