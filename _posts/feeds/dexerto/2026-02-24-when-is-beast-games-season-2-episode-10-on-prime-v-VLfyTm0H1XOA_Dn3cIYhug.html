What happened in Season 2 Episode 9?

Beast Games Season 2 Episode 9 ended with, you guessed it, a cliffhanger. With nothing but phones, bribes, and strategy, the players voted for each other to decide the six finalists, but we won’t find out the sixth until the finale. 

After the burial challenge, which saw each player take a cut of $1 million, the 10 remaining contestants were flown to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, for the penultimate challenge. They arrived at a futuristic arena filled with individual glass pods.

Inside each pod is a red phone, with another placed in the center of the room. MrBeast announced that they would go through six 30-minute rounds of voting to determine who will advance to the final game. 

To complicate matters, the $1 million they had just divided was brought back into play, giving contestants the opportunity to bribe one another for votes. The five who are definitely making it through include: 

  • Player 167 (Tyler)
  • Player 167 (Tyler)
  • Player 58 (Jack)
  • Player 172 (August)
  • Player 191 (Cory)

But before revealing the sixth, the episode ends. This cliffhanger frustrated viewers, so much so that MrBeast has promised to rectify its overuse in Beast Games Season 3. 

In Season 2 Episode 10, we’ll first find out who that sixth player is, before the final game will unfold to decide the ultimate winner.
