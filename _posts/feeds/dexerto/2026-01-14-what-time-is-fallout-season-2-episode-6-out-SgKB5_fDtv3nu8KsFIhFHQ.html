Okie dokey, how’s that for a cliffhanger? If you’re desperate to see what happens to Lucy (Ella Purnell) after that fight, we’ve got you covered with all the information on when Fallout Season 2 Episode 6 is out. Major spoilers ahead!

What an episode… It kicked off by showing us Lucy and the Ghoul (Walton Goggins) trying to escape the Deathclaw family living in New Vegas, and it surpassed all expectations. Prime Video really heard all the fans crying out to see live action Deathclaws and decided to give us a handful of dinosaur-lizard-monsters. What a time to be alive.

But the real meat of the episode tied into the overarching story of Fallout Season 2 as Hank (Kyle MacLachlan) finally figures out how to make the mind control device work, and the Snake Oil Salesman (Jon Daly) becomes the latest Vault-Tec employee.

Hank uses the salesman to reveal that he’s holding the Ghoul’s wife and daughter hostage, and he’ll kill them if the gunslinger doesn’t return Lucy to him, it erupts into a fight between the two traveling companions… And Lucy punches the Ghoul out of a window using a Power Fist. Woah. So with that jaw-dropping cliffhanger out of the way, here’s when you can watch Fallout Season 2 Episode 6.

Fallout Season 2 Episode 6 will start streaming on Prime Video at 12 am PT/3pm ET on Wednesday January 21. Here’s what time it streams in other timezones:

  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Summer Time
  • 10am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 1.30pm India
  • 5pm Tokyo
  • 7pm Sydney

Prime Video has continued with its normal release schedule now after it started by dropped the Fallout Season 2 premiere early for the holidays. Considering Episode 5 had Deathclaws, a Power Fist, and a reference to the Forced Evolutionary Virus on a Vault-Tec terminal (hello Super Mutants!), Episode 6 has a lot to live up to.

How many episodes are there in Fallout Season 2?

There are eight episodes in Fallout Season 2, just like the first season. Prime Video didn’t bump up the episode count following the success of Season 1. But that means audiences get to enjoy the ssecond season for 2 months, with all the fun of speculating about what’s going to happen instead of just binging them all on release day. There’s even a mysterious countdown that could imply the long-rumored Fallout 3 remaster might shadowdrop with the finale…

The last episode of Fallout Season 2 will stream on February 4, 2026. But don’t panic! The third season has already been greenlit by Prime Video, and Todd Howard told Dexerto how Season 3 will change from the games.

“I don’t wanna spoil anything, but what I would say is the show has an opportunity more so than a single game to pull things because we can jump locations, we can jump timelines,” he explained, before adding “We can do more than we thought we could when we first started, if that makes sense.”
