Roblox’s The Forge may have plenty to do, after all, a blacksmith’s job is never done. That doesn’t stop it from requiring a little bit of grinding to succeed — but that’s what codes are there for.

Roblox is famous for its codes, granting players free items for Grow a Garden, Uma Racing, Blox Fruits, and so much more. Naturally, The Forge is no different, offering free rerolls to players looking to keep those enemies at bay and thrive as a blacksmith.

So, if you’re working on crafting new weapons and are in the middle of perfecting that rather tricky crafting mini-game, then you’ll want to grab the currently active Forge codes before they expire.

Active codes in The Forge

There are currently two active codes to redeem in The Forge as of November 26, providing a total of three rerolls.

How to redeem codes

To actually redeem those codes, you’ll want to follow the below steps:

  • Launch The Forge in Roblox.
  • Head to the cog on the top left of your screen.
  • Scroll down in the settings until you see codes.
  • Paste the code into the box and press claim.

Once you’ve redeemed it successfully, the reward will appear without you needing to open any mail or notifications.

What are The Forge codes used for?

Forge codes offer rerolls, which are used to change your race and other stats. For example, at the beginning of the game, you’re given a race. You can choose to reroll this to get a rarer one, like Dragonborn, Dwarf, Angel, or demon.

So, it’s useful to always have a few rerolls in your pocked before diving into The Forge, just in case you’re not happy with the selection.
