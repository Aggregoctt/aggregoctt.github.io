Streaming star Felix ‘xQc’ Lengyel responded to Machine Gun Kelly’s claim that the streamer owes him $10,000, saying no one ever told him he owed money. 

As streaming has become increasingly more mainstream, it isn’t a shock to see content creators and celebrities mingle on and off-stream. 

The on-screen appearances vary from sponsorship and advertising commitments to genuine friendships. xQc has appeared on-stream with a number of big names, including Will Smith. Yet, his appearance alongside Adin Ross and MGK in 2024 has become a topic of conversation again. 

At the time, the trio were joined by other streamers, talking about Kick, music, and other topics as they hung out. However, when MGK rejoined Adin on his June 3 stream, the Canadian streamer became a topic of conversation. 

MGK calls out xQc for $10k debt

The Cleveland-born artist claimed that xQc owed him $10,000 from their previous meeting. “No. xQc owes me money,” he said after Adin asked if they were all good. 

“10 bands. One, two, three, four, 10, I need those. Bring ’em my way. You got it?”

xQc responds to MGK over $10k debt claim

That got onto xQc’s radar, and he wasn’t fussed at all by the amount, but more that he hadn’t been told that he owed the money. 

“I don’t like owing anybody any money ever. Okay. If that’s true, I’ll give it to him right now. But I think, I remember at the time, so I remember when I left the warehouse, I told Tav and Adin, ‘Yo, pay him back because we split a jet way back,’ I’m pretty sure, and I had paid, I overpaid…there’s a discrepency at the time, I told him about that,” the streamer said.

 “I said, ‘Yo, remember the money you owe me,’ or whatever. I think Jordan has it. I said, ‘Yo! Just take it from that.’

“You guys know for a fact that when I was leaving, I said, ‘Yo, you can pay him whenever and take it from the money that he owes me.’ I remember saying something like along those lines. Like, I’m not stupid. I mean, nobody ever told me that I owe that money. Like, you guys think I give a f**k about $10k?”

The former Overwatch star said that the situation was a “nothing burger” and he’d only worry about it if it became a “something burger.”
