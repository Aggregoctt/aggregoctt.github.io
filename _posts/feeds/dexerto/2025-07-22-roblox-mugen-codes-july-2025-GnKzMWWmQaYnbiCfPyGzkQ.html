Roblox’s Mugen is by no means an easy game, pitting you and your Style against other extremely skilled players. However, you can always get a little better with some free codes.

In this arena-style brawler, players get the chance to pick their favorite anime fighter and battle anyone they see. It’s brutal, can be extremely deadly, and isn’t a game for the faint-hearted, as it’s pretty unforgiving.

Luckily, while we can’t help with the skill, codes can. They’ll offer you free rewards, and maybe they’ll let you pull your dream character.

Active Mugen codes to redeem

There are currently four codes available to redeem in Mugen as of July 22. We’ve checked these all in-game and can confirm their validity.

Codes are regularly added in Mugen, but they also expire pretty quickly, so you’ll want to redeem them quickly to grab those free spins.

When a code expires, it’ll appear once you attempt to redeem it. Look below the box and you’ll see red text explaining the code has expired. If it’s been redeemed, it’ll be in green.

How to redeem codes

Thankfully, redeeming codes in Mugen is pretty simple. You don’t need to complete any tutorials or join any groups. All you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Open Mugen in Roblox.
  • Look on the left-hand side of the main menu and click Codes.
  • Copy and paste the codes listed above into the box.
  • Press Redeem.

With that, your code will be redeemed and your rewards will be instantly added to your game. It’s worth noting, all the codes for Mugen are case sensitive, so be sure to copy and paste them in.

What are Mugen codes used for?

Mugen codes will offer one of two things: Yen or Lucky Spins.

Yen, as many might have guessed, is the game’s key currency, allowing you to purchase various abilities and items.

However, Lucky Spins is the most sought-after feature, as it allows you to pull on the game’s gacha to unlock different Styles, each of which ranges in power and use during the game.
