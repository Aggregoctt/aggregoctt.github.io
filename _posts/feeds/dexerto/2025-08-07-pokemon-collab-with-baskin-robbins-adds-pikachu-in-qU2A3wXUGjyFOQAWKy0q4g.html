Pokemon is bringing various ice cream flavors inspired by its characters, as well as other items, to Baskin-Robbins as part of a new collaboration.

As one of the most popular franchises out there, Pokemon has done plenty of collabs in the past to introduce products and reel new fans in. 

While the franchise is famous for its TCG releases, video games, and anime, it certainly doesn’t shy away from collaborating with food brands, with Hershey’s chocolate being just one recent example.

As first spotted by Sora News, now fans can enjoy the all-new 31 Poké Summer! Campaign, which allows them to try out limited-time Pokémon-inspired ice cream flavors in Baskin-Robbins Japan until September 3, 2025.

Pokemon partners with Baskin Baskin-Robbins again for new flavors and items

One of the main highlights of this campaign with Baskin-Robbins is the various Pokemon-inspired flavors available, one of which is the Pikachu Tropical Soda that’s designed to evoke the taste of a refreshing ice cream. This combines the taste of vanilla and sorbet, as well as Pikachu-shaped candies as a sweet addition. 

There’s also a Pikachu and Fuecoco Fruit Ice Cream flavor, which rocks a yellow and red look to mimic these mons’ appearances and combines seven different fruits for a “Summertime Fullness” theme and even a Pikachu Ice Cream cake, among others.

As for the cups, as seen from the official blog, there are three variations available. All of them feature Pikachu hanging out with other characters, including starters from Scarlet and Violet, such as Sprigatito, Quaxly, and Fuecoco.

What’s more, fans who order a double scoop or cup can also bring home a random sticker from 20 different options. Of course, an ice cream cone wouldn’t be complete without a container bag to keep it cool on their way home.

This crossover with Baskin-Robbins also features a “Pokemon Special Set” that includes nine ice cream flavors of your choice in a two-way insulated bag. 

Though this one is on the pricier end, with the small size set costing 3,500 yen ($23) and the Regular size one priced at 4,140 yen ($28).
