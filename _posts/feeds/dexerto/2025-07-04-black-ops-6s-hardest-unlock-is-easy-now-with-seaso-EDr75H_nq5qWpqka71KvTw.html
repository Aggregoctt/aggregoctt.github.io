Black Ops 6’s new Party Ops mode makes it pretty easy to unlock one of the hardest items in the game, so don’t miss out. 

Over the last few years, Call of Duty has increasingly had some difficult items to unlock. The rewards might only be Calling Cards, badges, or even a weapon skin, but there is also bragging rights to go along with that. 

Sure, Black Ops 6 made things a little easier with The Armory, but there are still some big challenges that you’ve got to navigate as well. 

The toughest of which centres around the Hard Breakup Calling Card. To unlock it, you’ve got to perform seven finishing moves in one match. However, it has been made a little easier now.

Easiest way to unlock Hard Breakup in Black Ops 6

With the start of Season 4 Reloaded, Treyarch have released a new playlist – Party Ops. As Redditor cambofresh1 points out, the new mode has a mode that requires you to only kill your opponent with finishing moves. 

“Getting 7 finishing moves in one game is a lot easier with this event. Get it before it’s over,” they noted. 

It is a limited-time mode, and each game is randomized, so you may have to play a number of Party Ops matches to get the one you need. 

Regardless of that, it is something players have made a note of. “Damn Didn’t Know this. thank you,” one said. “Logged on just for this and got it, thank you,” another added.

“Where was this event a week ago when I was actually going insane in the membrane trying to get it,” another commented.

As noted, the Calling Card has been one of the toughest unlocks in Black Ops 6 to this point. It hasn’t been impossible, but there is a select group that have it in their inventory. That changes now.
