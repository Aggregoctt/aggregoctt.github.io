Popeyes has just released three new snack wraps alongside a lineup of blueberry-flavored beverages.

Over the last few years, snack wraps have been all the rage at fast-food restaurants that offer chicken tenders. They’re budget-friendly and simple to make, as they often feature pre-existing ingredients like ranch, cheese, and lettuce.

Arby’s, Wendy’s, and plenty of other restaurants have introduced their takes on the popular treat, and on Monday, June 2, Popeyes has joined the market with three new wraps.

Here’s everything we know about Popeyes Chicken Wraps, including flavors, price, and more.

Popeyes reveals new chicken wraps

Announced in a blog post, the Popeye’s Chicken Wraps are only available for a limited time, although they haven’t revealed how long they’re around.

There are three flavors: classic, spicy, and honey mustard. Each one comes with one of its hand-breaded chicken tenders, crispy lettuce, shredded cheese, crunchy pickles, and topped with whichever spread you wish.

You can get them from your Popeye’s for $3.99 each, and the company is also releasing a whole new beverage lineup that consists of blueberry lemonade, cane sweet tea, and unsweet tea.

The company shared the news in a post on X as well. It reads: “Road trip vibes are elite with Popeyes Chicken Wraps.”

This comes as McDonald’s plans the return of its popular snack wraps that were discontinued back in 2016. Back in December, McDonald’s revealed that fans requesting the snack wrap’s return over the years is what led to the decision to bring it back in the United States.
