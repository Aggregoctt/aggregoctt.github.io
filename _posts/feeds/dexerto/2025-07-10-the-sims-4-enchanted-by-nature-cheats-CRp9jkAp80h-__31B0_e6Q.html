How to enter cheats

You’ll need to turn on the ‘testingcheats’ cheat before you can use many of the cheats above, so I’d always suggest you make sure you turn that on first. The method of doing that is slightly different depending on whether you’re playing on PC and Mac or on consoles, so here’s what to do:

  • PC/Mac: CTRL/CMD + Shift + C
  • Console: Press all four shoulder buttons

Once that’s done, a white box will appear in the top-left of your screen, as shown in the image below:

  • Next, type in ‘testingcheats on’ and press ‘Enter’, or the X or A button on console.
    • You’ll get a ‘Cheats are enabled’ message in the top-left.
  • From there, you can press CTRL/CMD + Shift + C or all four shoulder buttons again to close the window, or enter another cheat from the the lists above.
