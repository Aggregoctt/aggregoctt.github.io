Fortnite is lining up another blockbuster crossover for Chapter 7, with Kill Bill’s The Bride, Gogo Yubari, and Yuki Yubari set to join the game in the new season.

Epic confirmed The Bride’s arrival during the Chapter 6 Finale Live Event teaser trailer, which showed her riding a yellow motorcycle through the map as Kong and Godzilla clash in the background.

However, some new promos have revealed that Gogo Yubari and her sister Yuki Yubari will also be available when Chapter 7 goes live on November 29, giving fans an early look at the upcoming cosmetics.

Fortnite x Kill Bill collab skins revealed

First up is The Bride, who appears in her classic yellow-and-black jumpsuit, riding a motorcycle, and has her iconic katana strapped to her back. Meanwhile, the leaked Gogo skins carry over her school uniform look from Kill Bill Vol. 1, complete with a blazer, bow, and her signature stern expression.

Fortnite leaker Shiina also revealed that Epic is teaming up with US-based movie ticket distributor Fandango, which will allow players to claim Gogo’s skin when they buy a ticket for the opening weekend of Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair. The promotion will run until November 29, with the skin being added in Chapter 7.

The Bride and Gogo aren’t the only Kill Bill cosmetics arriving in the new update, as Epic shared new key art titled “Yuki’s Revenge,” revealing that Yuki Yubari will join her sister in Chapter 7. The teaser shows Yuki sitting in a bright pink sports car holding an Uzi, while The Bride stands behind her with a pistol.

More details are expected to be revealed once Epic begins rolling out full-season marketing, including exact release dates and how all three will be unlocked.
