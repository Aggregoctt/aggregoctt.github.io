Jump Stars pits players against one another to gather ten gems without being taken down. It requires skill, a well-timed dodge, and a powerful character. While we can’t help with the skill, codes are guaranteed to get you a powerful fighter to win with.

Codes are one of Roblox’s primary features. Tons of its games allow players the chance to pick up free rewards in their favorite adventures, and Jump Stars is no different. After all, it’s by no means an easy game to play or succeed in, especially if you have to grind through Gems to get a half-decent character to use.

Luckily, that’s exactly what codes are there for: offering you a much easier time in your battles, all without spending any money. So, here are all the currently active codes in Jump Stars as well as how to redeem them.

Active Jump Stars codes

There are currently three active codes available for Jump Stars, all of which have been checked and confirmed as of July 17, 2025.

Codes are pretty regular in Jump Stars, with many being added as part of events or as a celebration for a certain number of likes or members. As such, players can expect to see plenty more codes arriving for the game. However, plenty have also expired, so be sure to redeem them quickly before they disappear.

How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in Jump Stars is a little more complex than other Roblox adventures. Follow the steps below to redeem your codes:

  • Join the Jump Corp game group on Roblox.
  • Launch Jump Stars.
  • Go through the tutorial (if you’re new to the game).
  • When in the game’s lobby, look to the right and click Codes.
  • Paste your code into the box.
  • Hit Redeem.

It’s worth noting, the codes are case sensitive, and they won’t reward you unless you’re part of the Jump Corp game group, so be sure to join that before redeeming.

List of expired codes

In Jump Stars, codes regularly expire. Once they do, we’ll move them here to save you redeeming something that won’t offer a reward.

What are Jump Stars codes used for?

Currently, Jump Stars codes offer only one type of reward: Gems. These Gems can be used for summoning stronger characters. They’re essentially the game’s premium currency and are, therefore, extremely useful to have on your account, especially since the codes won’t cost money.

Players can get ten summons by using 500 Gems; these will offer a variety of characters, with the focused summon getting higher rates.
