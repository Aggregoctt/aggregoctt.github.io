A video game collector has completed what they described as a complete North American Xbox 360 game library, 20 years after receiving their first game and the console itself.

Sharing the achievement on Reddit, the collector explained that a complete Xbox 360 set is widely considered to require 1,256 games. Their collection now totals 1,353 titles, a number that includes different variants alongside standard editions.

Their journey began on Christmas Day 2006, when the user received an Xbox 360 and a copy of GUN. Over the next 20 years, they tracked down every required release, culminating in the final missing title, Pro Evolution Soccer 18, which was gifted to them by their son.

Playing every game is the next challenge

While the collection itself is now complete, the 360 fan explained in their post that the next goal is to actually play through the library. So far, they’ve earned 1,000 Gamerscore in around 175 games, with plans to continue working through the rest over time.

Elsewhere in the thread, they loosely estimated that, on average, 360 games were around $15 on the secondary market, putting the total value of their green and white-colored library at around $20,000.

Among the entire set, the most valuable title is F1 2013, which the collector estimates is currently worth around $250 due to its rarity on the platform, particularly in the U.S.
