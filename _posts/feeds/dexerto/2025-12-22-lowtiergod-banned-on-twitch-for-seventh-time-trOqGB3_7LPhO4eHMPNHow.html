Twitch streamer LowTierGod has been banned from the platform, with his channel going offline on December 21 and displaying Twitch’s standard message indicating a Terms of Service or Community Guidelines violation.

The 41-year-old, a long-running fighting game streamer, has repeatedly faced enforcement action on Twitch over the years.

As of December 21, his channel is inaccessible, and Twitch has not publicly provided a reason for the ban, in line with its usual policy.

Subsequent Kick stream

Following the ban, LowTierGod went live on his Kick channel on December 22 in a broadcast labelled “Suspended but not banned,” in which he criticized people celebrating his ban.

“I can stream on Kick, still be doing better than you or your parents, and you’re still a bum. So what is the point in rejoicing over some sh*t that’s temporary? I’m not even permed,” he said.

Per StreamerBans, this marks the seventh time LowTierGod has been banned from Twitch. He has previously served multiple temporary bans between 2023 and 2025, ranging from short lockouts to multi-week suspensions.

As standard, Twitch does not comment on individual enforcement actions, meaning any claims about the cause remain speculative unless confirmed by Twitch itself.

It is currently unclear how long the suspension will last, or whether it will escalate into a longer-term removal given his history of repeat enforcement actions.
