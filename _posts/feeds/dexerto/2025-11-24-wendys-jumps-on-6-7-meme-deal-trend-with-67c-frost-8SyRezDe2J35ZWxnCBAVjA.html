Wendy’s is tapping into one of 2025’s biggest internet trends with a Black Friday offer that prices Small Frosty Classic treats at 67 cents.

The deal runs on November 28 only, and brings the chain directly into the ongoing wave of meme-driven fast-food promos centered on the viral 6-7 phrase.

The chain confirmed that customers can grab the discounted Frosty while supplies last at participating locations. The offer applies to the classic Small Frosty and is positioned as a simple numeric tie-in built around the meme’s surge across TikTok and X.

It’s not the first 6-7 meal deal to go viral

The 6-7 trend took off earlier this year after creators began posting edits, captions, and sound clips built around the number. The meme’s popularity spread quickly into mainstream feeds, prompting major brands to experiment with short-run promos tied to the digits.

Pizza Hut launched a two-day 6-7 Menu on November 6 and 7 that priced boneless wings at 67 cents each with a dedicated code.

Before that, Domino’s offered a $6.70 large one-topping pizza deal available through its website and app. Wendy’s is the latest chain to join the trend, using the 67 cent Frosty offer as its Black Friday special for 2025.
