The best Zhaohui build in GFL2 can drastically raise your damage, giving you everything you need to crush your enemies on the battlefield.

Zhaohui is an SSR Vanguard Doll who uses her fantastic mobility to rush down her foes, unleashing Hydro attacks and debuffs with her SMG. While there aren’t many options when it comes to building a Hydro team in the global version of GFL2, Zhaohui does help towards making your Hydro homie team a reality.

So, if you’ve unlocked Zhaohui on the current GLF2 banner or just want to know the best build, weapon, team comp, and more, we have you covered.

Best Zhaohui build

To get the best Zhaohui build, we recommend using the following Keys, attachments, weapons, and team:

  • Weapon: Juggernaut
  • Team: Zhaohui, Tololo, Qiongjiu, Suomi

Keys

  • Weakness Exposed: Increases damage dealt against targets with Hydro debuffs by 10%.
  • Detest Evil: When the active skill Shadow Snare hits only one target, it deals one extra instance of fixed damage equal to 10% of attack and applies Damp for two turns.
  • Light of Justice: When using the active skill Wanderer’s Acumen, Zhaohui gains one stack of Quick Barrier and knocks the target back by two tiles.

Best attachments

  • Phase Strike: Deals 15% increased damage to targets with Phase Element debuffs. This set is great for players who want to avoid grinding and have a set that can be used on multiple characters.
  • Hydro Boost: When dealing Hydro damage, the damage is increased by 20%.

Zhaohui was released on June 12, 2025, and her banner will be available until July 2, 2025.

This means players have plenty of time to pull on the limited banner. She’s also available alongside Groza and Colphne, two SR units that have a rate-up boost.

Best weapon

The best Zhaohui weapon is Juggernaut. This weapon is tailor-made for Zhaohui, as it gives her Movement Up 2 when she moves five tiles or more for one turn. Additionally, when Zhaohui gains 10% increased damage when dealing Hydro damage to Hydro weak enemies, while Critical damage against targets affected by Hydro debuffs is increased by 10%.

As Yoohee’s abilities are tailored around mobility and unleashing Hydro damage, the Vanguard will constantly be able to capitalize on the added DPS and mobility from Juggernaut.

Best Zhaohui team

The current best Zhaohui team comp in the global version of GFL2 is the following:

  • Zhaohui (Debuff)
  • Tololo (DPS)
  • Qiongjiu (DPS)
  • Suomi (Support/Healer)

Zhaohui currently lacks a strong team comp due to the GFL2 global server not having many Hydro options. So, the main synergy here is Zhaohui + Tololo, as both have amazing mobility.

I recommend using Zhaohui to weaken enemies, while Tololo can run in for the kill and use her extra action to obliterate her foes. The added support attacks from Zhaohui’s passive also open up some nice DPS options, especially when you add QJ to the mix, who will also be capitalising off Tololo’s attacks.

Suomi is your Support option here, as her tanky shields and lifesaving heals will keep all your units alive during the toughest fights. Suomi’s shields are particularly useful for Zhaohui and Tololo, especially since they will be both rushing into the frontline.
