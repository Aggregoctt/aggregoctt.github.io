Persona 5: The Phantom X tasks you with naming your protagonist pretty much as soon as you get into the game. If you’re a Persona fan, then you’ll know there are official ‘canon’ names for these characters, so it’s only natural to want to use that.

While Persona usually lets you adjust how you play and really get into the shoes of the protagonist, these characters still have a pretty distinct personality, despite usually being silent. Complimenting their personality is their name, which is a key focus for many Persona fans.

So, if you’re looking for the Persona 5: The Phantom X protagonist’s canon name or just want to learn everything about him, then we’ve got what you need. Oh, and if you’ve already chosen a name but feel like his OG one is better, don’t worry, you can change it.

Persona 5: The Phantom X protagonists canon name

The canon name for the Persona 5: The Phantom X protagonist is Nagisa Kamisiro.

However, if you’re playing the game in Japan, then his name is officially Nagisa Kamishiro. This change is purely down to the fact that his name doesn’t fully fit in the slot for the English version of the game, so it had to be condensed down.

Who is Nagisa Kamisiro?

Nagisa Kamisiro is a young man with short red hair and matching red eyes. He wears a white undershirt and white hoodie underneath his uniform, as well as red sneakers. When he’s wondering through the Metaverse, he wears a gray tailcoat over a navy bodysuit with black gloves, red fingers, boots, and a red mask.

He has the following stats:

  • Codename: Wonder
  • Persona: Jánošík, Wild Card
  • Age: 16-17
  • School: Kokatsu Academy
  • Occupation: Student
  • Affiliation: Phantom Thieves
  • Melee Weapon: Knives
  • Ranged Weapon: Handgun
  • Voice Actors: Koki Uchiyama (JPN), ShangQing Su (CHN)

As highlighted above, Nagisa Kamisiro is a second-year student from Kokatsu Academy in Shimokitazawa Ward living alone in Zoshigaya, Tokyo. His parents left to travel, though he is in contact with his mother and relies on his neighbors if needed, though he doesn’t speak to them much.

While Nagisa Kamisiro is a silent protagonist, we know he lacks a considerable amount of desire, showing no care in filling out his worksheets at school, he’s mostly apathetic and seems distant from most of his classmates and neighbors.

Naturally, just like other protagonists in the franchise, he’s got a mischievous streak, imitating Haru’s “Beauty Thief” entrance pose during Persona 5’s collaboration CrossFate story.

On top of this, he has the ability to see visions that seem to be of the future, or at least possible futures, which we see more of in Persona 5: The Phantom X. Complementing this, he’s serious, focused, has a strong will, and a strong sense of justice, willing to rescue anyone in need.

He’s rather emotional and often worries or gets stressed in disastrous situations, which makes sense given his age and the things he goes through.

Can you change your name?

Yes, you can change your name. The first time is free, the second time will cost you 200 Meta Jewels, the premium currency.

It’s worth noting, while Nagisa Kamisiro is a great name to choose, other players will pick the same, which can make finding your pals pretty tricky. As such, we recommend making your name a little more unique, unless you’re not fussed about being found, which is a pretty Nagisa Kamisiro thing to do.

To change your name, follow the steps below:

  • Open your phone (in-game).
  • Click the profile icon at the top of the screen.
  • Choose the ellipses above your set icon.
  • Change your name.
