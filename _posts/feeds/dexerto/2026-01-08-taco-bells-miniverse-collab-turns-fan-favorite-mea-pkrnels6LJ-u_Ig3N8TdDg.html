Taco Bell is teaming up with MGA’s Miniverse for a new collectible line that turns its most recognizable menu items into miniature collectibles.

The collaboration, officially titled the MGA’s Miniverse Make It Mini Taco Bell Collection, was announced on January 7 and features hyper-realistic DIY mini versions of popular Taco Bell foods and drinks. The line allows fans to assemble, set, and display miniature recreations of items straight from the Taco Bell menu.

The eight-piece collection includes mini builds inspired by items such as the Crunchwrap Supreme, Bean and Cheese Burrito, Mexican Pizza, and Spicy Potato Soft Taco. Each blind-packaged capsule comes with miniature ingredients, accessories, and a recipe card, with the capsule itself doubling as a display case.

The MGA’s Miniverse Make It Mini Taco Bell Collection launches nationwide on March 1, 2026, with prices ranging from $10.99 to $12.99 per capsule. The collectibles will be sold at major retailers, including Target, Amazon, Walmart, and the MGA Shop.

Ahead of the full release, limited quantities will be made available through TikTok Live Drops hosted by the Miniverse page on January 13 and February 3. Taco Bell Rewards members will also have a chance to win one of 500 collectibles during a Tuesday Drop inside the Taco Bell app on January 13.

The collection also includes miniature sauce packets, drinks like Agua Refrescas and Churro Chillers, along with branded trays, utensils, and packaging. The line is designed for ages eight and up and targets both younger fans and adult collectors.
