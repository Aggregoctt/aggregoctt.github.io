Grand Theft Auto fans are convinced they’ve found a big detail in the GTA 6 story after creating ‘trailer 3’ with Rockstar’s teaser clips.

At the start of May, Rockstar Games delivered the news that no Grand Theft Auto fan wanted as they delayed GTA 6’s release date to May 26, 2026. 

While fans were upset about that news, the GTA developers finally released a second trailer and dropped some big information about characters. They’ve revealed Jason and Lucia’s full names – Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos – as well as Boobie Ike, Cal Hampton, Raul Bautista, and others. There are also 100 new screenshots to dig through. 

With the new trailer and screenshots, fans have been combing through them to try and find some secrets and hints about what’s coming in the story. And, it looks like there might be a big choice to make based on the ‘trailer 3’ that fans have made. 

GTA 6 ‘trailer 3’ suggests big twist in the story

That’s right, fans have made a third trailer using some of the additional teasers and character profiles on Rockstar’s website. 

In this, there is a big focus on the small interactions between Jason and Lucia, with fans pointing out that it looks like a start menu with a way to pick between the characters in more than one point. As a result, they believe that the GTA 6 story will begin with a choice between Jason and Lucia before they eventually meet later down the road. 

“It’s like a new mini trailer. But the one with Jason and Lucia looks like an important choice. Maybe heist job, important mission with consequences,” one said. 

“Whoever said that little camera move between Jason and Lucia is like a character selection thing, I can’t get it out of my head. Feels like a ‘choose your start’ screen,” another added. 

As noted, fans have noted some other details that they’ve spotted, including a belief that the main characters will betray one another.

“Lucia hugging Jason while also holding a gun behind his back is a sign that she”ll betray him. I’m pretty sure of it,” another pointed out.
