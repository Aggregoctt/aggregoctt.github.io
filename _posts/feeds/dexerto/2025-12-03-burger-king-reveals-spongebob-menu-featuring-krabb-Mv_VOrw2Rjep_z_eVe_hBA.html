Burger King has launched a full SpongeBob SquarePants-inspired menu on Tuesday, December 2, ahead of The SpongeBob Movie: Search for SquarePants, which hits theaters on December 19.

In November, early images of the lineup started circulating after a food account shared alleged photos of the themed items, prompting Burger King to begin teasing the launch of the items.

It wasn’t until Monday, December 1, that the Whopper creators finally revealed the Krabby Patty formula with the launch of the SpongeBob Squarepants meal.

SpongeBob Squarepants menu lands at Burger King

The limited-time lineup includes four themed items:

  • SpongeBob’s Krabby Whopper
    A quarter-pound beef patty with American cheese, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles, onions, mayonnaise, and ketchup, served on a yellow square sesame bun modeled after the Krabby Patty.
  • Mr. Krabs’ Cheesy Bacon Tots
    Potato bites filled with cheese and bacon bits, packaged in a treasure chest-style carton.
  • Patrick’s Star-berry Shortcake Pie
    A strawberry and vanilla cream dessert with a cookie crust, topped with shortcake crumbles and star-shaped sprinkles.
  • Pirate’s Frozen Pineapple Float
    A frozen pineapple drink topped with tropical-flavored cold foam.

Items can be ordered individually or as part of the Bikini Bottom Bundle and King Jr. Meal. The bundle arrives in a box shaped like SpongeBob’s pineapple house, and King Jr. Meals include one of six collectible SpongeBob toys and a paper crown.
