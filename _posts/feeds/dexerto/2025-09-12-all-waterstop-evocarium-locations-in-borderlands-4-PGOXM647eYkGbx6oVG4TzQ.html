One of the first real roadblocks for collectors in Borderlands 4 happens in Waterstop. This area is packed with loot and enemies, but it also hides three Evocarium orbs you’ll want to grab.

These orbs expand your SDUs, making them essential if you want to carry more ammo and loot as the game ramps up. The problem is that while two are in plain sight, the third looks unreachable and leaves many players scratching their heads.

Don’t worry, you don’t need to glitch through walls or overthink it. Here’s exactly where each Evocarium is in Waterstop and how to grab them without wasting thirty minutes running in circles.

How to find every Orb in Waterstop Evocarium

Orb #1

This is the one that confuses most players. Just follow these steps to reach it:

  1. From the main Waterstop platform, look around for a large pipe running along the side.
  2. Jump over the pipe and you’ll spot a ladder tucked away.
  3. Climb up, drop down into the small chamber, and the first Evocarium will be right there.

It’s easy to miss, but once you know the route it takes less than a minute.

Orb #2

After you grab the first orb, head back out toward the main walkway and stand in front of the Evocarium console. From here, climb up onto the rooftop to your right and you’ll see it glowing over a water tank.

This one is straightforward, just make sure you hit the button before climbing so the path actually opens.

Orb #3

The last Evocarium is also high up, but you’ll need to make use of your gliding pack to reach it.

Look across from the previous rooftop and you’ll see another platform with the orb hovering above it. Line up your jump, deploy your glider at the peak, and drift across to snag the collectible.

It’s a short flight, so even a basic gliding pack will get you there safely.
