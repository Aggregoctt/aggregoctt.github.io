Gameloft

Disney Dreamlight Valley is diving into summer with an update packed with new characters, story content, events, and long-awaited quality-of-life improvements.

Following their recent Developer Update, the team previewed a rich slate of features coming to both the base game and the Storybook Vale Expansion.

Here’s a detailed look at what’s included in the latest free patch.

New content: Skull Rock, Peter Pan, and The Forgotten

  • New story content continues the tale of the Forgotten, sending players deep into Skull Rock to uncover new secrets
  • Peter Pan joins the Valley as a new Villager, with friendship quests, unique rewards, and a questline to rebuild his Dreamlight Lost Boys crew
  • The Forgotten becomes a full Villager with hangout options, custom naming, and new friendship items
    Seasonal events and gameplay
  • Adventures in Never Land Star Path brings pirate and mermaid-inspired outfits, decor, and Dream Styles for Goofy and Minnie
  • Troves of Treasure event lets players hunt for maps, solve riddles, and earn unique treasure-themed rewards
  • Summer Sizzle returns with marshmallows, new treats, and seasonal cosmetics
  • Weekly DreamSnaps challenges themed around the Skull Rock update

Premium shop and new bundles

  • River Kit bundle lets players design their own waterways
  • Max from The Little Mermaid arrives as a new companion, able to help dig at nearby treasure spots
  • Max Bundle includes nautical furniture and clothing items
  • New seasonal Moonstone packs and rotating cosmetics added
    Note: Arcade Edition players do not have access to Star Paths or Premium Shop features

Companion friendship system

  • Companions now have friendship levels, unlocking rewards and perks as you spend time with them
  • Different species collect specific resource types, including:
  • Squirrels for fruits
  • Rabbits for vegetables
  • Turtles for seafood
  • Sunbirds for flowers
  • Foxes and crocodiles for fish
  • Raccoons and whimsical creatures for gems
  • Ravens for the soil
  • Capybaras, snakes, and monkeys for timebending parts
  • Owls, baby dragons, and pegasi for snippets
  • Dogs for sticks
  • Some named Disney companions offer special rewards at max level
  • Players can nickname unnamed companions
  • A new Collections Menu tab helps track all your critters
  • Path tiles can now be placed diagonally with an easy toggle between straight and diagonal modes
  • The decorating grid has been expanded, offering more freedom to place items
  • Player house styles can now be placed once in each expansion area, including on a floating island

Additional improvements

  • Photo Mode adds seated poses and more retouching tools
  • Memory shard drops are now guaranteed with each orb, unless that memory is complete
  • More memory orb drops when watering dried flowers
  • Premium Shop now shows six recommended bundles
  • Uncrafting Station costs adjusted to make reclaiming materials easier
  • Cross-save support arrives for PlayStation

Top changes and bug fixes

  • Fixed multiple quest progression blockers across quests like Furniture Fluster, Mannequin Madness, Monkey Business, and Teeny, Tiny Problems
  • Resolved issues with disappearing or unreachable quest items
  • Corrected visuals, clipping, and UI issues across various assets, including the Spirit Water Curtain and Alice’s house on iOS
  • Fixed crafting, memory, and inventory glitches
  • Cheshire Cat and companion animation bugs fixed
  • Various audio improvements and minor bug fixes throughout the game
