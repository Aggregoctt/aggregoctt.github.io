A TikToker has responded after her confrontation and punching of streamer JayRelly went viral on social media. 

IRL (in-real-life) streaming is fraught with danger for a lot of streamers. Some big-name creators have employed bodyguards and security teams to stop the public from ruining their broadcasts, but even then, mishaps happen.

Twitch streamer JayRelly isn’t one of those big names – he has just under 1000 followers on the Amazon-owned platform – but regularly streams out in public.

During his June 22 broadcast, the Twitch streamer had a drink thrown at him by a woman, TikToker ClassyyM, as the pair got into a confrontation. The TikToker eventually punched the streamer too, with him being totally taken aback by what had gone on. 

TikToker ClassyyM addresses punching Jayrelly

The TikToker, who was accused of being rude by numerous social media accounts, responded to the incident after it had spread like wildfire across X (formerly Twitter.)

“I’m not sitting here justifying my opinions on anybody,” she said, claiming that Jayrelly had been trying to start a fight near her. “I’ve already got a little attitude because the dude was ready to fight. So when I walked past him, I’m like ‘excuse you’.

“Throwing the water, I ain’t gonna lie, a little too much. I ain’t got no excuse, drunk as f*ck. I tried around to go walk away, and you was aggravating me too with a bright a*s light, with drinking and s*it, that s*it is too much. I heard ‘bitch’, thats all I heard. At the end of the day, I send my apologies, but what’s done is done.”

As of writing, the TikToker’s initial response has gotten around 600,000 views. The original video of the interaction has way above 8 million views. 

She has published two other TikToks about it too, including reusing an old video where she urged herself to take her own advice about ignoring the outside noise and goings on.
