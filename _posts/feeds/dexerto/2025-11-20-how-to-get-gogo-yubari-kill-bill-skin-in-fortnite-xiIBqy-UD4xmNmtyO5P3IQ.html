Fortnite’s Kill Bill crossover is shaping up to be one of Chapter 7’s biggest draws, and players can already unlock Gogo Yubari via a special promotion event.

The Kill Bill collab was first confirmed in the Chapter 6 Finale teaser, which showed The Bride speeding across the map on a yellow motorcycle while Kong and Godzilla battle overhead.

However, Epic has now confirmed that Gogo Yubari and her sister Yuki Yubari will also feature heavily once Chapter 7 goes live on November 29.

The new collab comes hot on the heels of the theatrical release of Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair, and Epic is giving fans the chance to claim Gogo’s skin before it appears in the item shop.

How to unlock Gogo Yubari in Fortnite

Fortnite players in the United States can unlock the Gogo Yubari skin by following the simple steps outlined below:

  1. Log in or make a Fandango account between November 20 at 12am ET and November 29 at 11:59pm PT.
  2. Purchase at least one ticket to Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair for an opening-weekend showtime between December 4 and December 7.
  3. Wait for November 30 and check your email for a Fortnite redemption code for the Gogo Yubari outfit.
  4. Redeem the code at fortnite.com/redeem before December 31 at 4pm PT.

Once you have redeemed the Gogo Yubari skin code, the cosmetic will appear in your account as soon as it becomes available in-game.

It’s important to note that the code is one-time use only, and players are limited to one Code per Fandango/Fortnite account.
