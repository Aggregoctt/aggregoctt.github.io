Hollow Knight Silksong is comprised of three major acts, but getting to the final one isn’t easy, unless you know how to unlock it.

Very little about Silksong is actually easy. Its combat is arguably harder than its predecessor, prompting Team Cherry to step in with a handy patch. On top of that, it’s filled with hidden collectables, wishes, upgrades, and more for you to discover, all while nearly being decimated by enemies.

Well, this extends to the main story, too. Specifically, Act 3, which you’ll have to actively discover and unlock if you want to complete the game. So, if you’re close to the end of Act 2, here’s how to finally get those credits rolling.

How to unlock Act 3

All requirements

To unlock Act 3 in Hollow Knight Silksong, you’ll need to unlock and address every single wish in the game. These are as follows:

  • Pharloom’s Crown
  • Savage Beastfly
  • The Hidden Hunter
  • The Terrible Tyrant
  • The Wailing Mother
  • Cloaks of the Choir
  • Crawbug Clearing
  • Final Audience
  • Fine Pins
  • Flexible Spines
  • Garb of the Pilgrims
  • Roach Guts
  • Volatile Flintbeetles
  • Architect’s Melody
  • Bugs of Pharloom
  • Conductor’s Melody
  • Vaultkeeper’s Melody
  • Broodfest
  • Grand Gate
  • Liquid Lacquer
  • Queen’s Egg
  • Bone Bottom Repairs
  • Bellhart’s Glory
  • An Icon Of Hope
  • A Lifesaving Bridge
  • Building Up Songclave
  • Restoration of Bellhart
  • Strengthening Songclave
  • Alchemist’s Assistant
  • Great Taste Of Pharloom
  • Berry Picking
  • Rite of the Pollip
  • Silver Bells
  • The Threadspun Town
  • Silent Halls
  • The Great Citadel
  • Fastest in Pharloom
  • Balm for the Wounded
  • Infestation Operation
  • My Missing Brother
  • My Missing Courier
  • Pinmaster’s Oil
  • Rite of Rebirth
  • The Lost Fleas
  • The Lost Merchant
  • The Wandering Merchant
  • Trail’s End

These are found throughout the map and will offer you various items and upgrades for the battles ahead. Once you’ve completed all the above Wishes, you’ll be able to unlock Silk and Soul, the final quest to unlock Act 3.

Complete Silk and Soul

Collect items for the trap

Once you’ve collected all the Wishes, head to Pavo in Bellhart. Doing so will give you a Bellhome. Once you’ve done this, collect 25 fleas and head to Fleamaster Mooshka. You’ll need to move them up to three times to place them in the Putrefied Ducts.

Next, head to Sonclave and speak to the Caretaker, who will share his plan and enact the Silk and Soul quest.

With that, you’ll need to collect four trap components:

  • Maiden’s Soul: Sit on the Bench in Bone Bottom, speak to the Maiden, and beat the second Skull Tyrant.
  • Hermit’s Soul: Destroy the fake wall on the right of Bellhard Bellway and locate the Bell Hermit.
  • Seeker’s Soul: Located at the abandoned bellhome on top of Bilewater.
  • Snare Setter: Grab the item from the table above the bench in Weavenest.

Once you’ve got those, head back to the Caretake and prepare the trap.

Fight Grand Mother Silk

With that, head over to The Cradle and begin to prepare for your battle. The Caretaker will let you know the trap is set.

Once you’ve fought Grand Mother Silk, it’s important that you choose the option to play the Needolin. This will unlock Act 3 instead of ending the story. With that, you’re in the next act, but no spoilers here.

Fastest way to unlock Act 3

All of that is certainly complicated (that’s the point), but there’s an easier way to get to Act 3 if you prefer not to go through all that.

A mod called Start Act 3 Abyss and Snared Silk Achievement will let you trigger Act 3 instantly. Sure, it’s not really the most morally correct way to do it, but it’s great if you missed Act 3 and ran straight to the credits without knowing.
