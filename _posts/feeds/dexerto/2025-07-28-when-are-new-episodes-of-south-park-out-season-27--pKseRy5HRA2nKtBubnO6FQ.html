Season 27 release schedule ---

South Park is so back. Trey Parker and Matt Stone delivered a truly wild episode with the Season 27 premiere, so be sure to check out the full release schedule so you don’t miss a thing.

The long-running animated series was on shaky ground amid the ongoing bidding war between Paramount and HBO Max, sparking fears South Park Season 27 would get delayed as a result.

However, mere hours before the new chapter’s debut, Parker and Stone inked a $1.5 billion licensing deal with Paramount Plus, meaning all 26 seasons are now on the streaming service, and new episodes will head there after airing on Comedy Central.

With Season 27 Episode 1, ‘Sermon on the ‘Mount’, out now, here’s when to expect new episodes, as well as the release schedule.

Episode 2 of South Park Season 27 airs on Wednesday, July 30, at 10pm ET/PT on Comedy Central. It will be available for streaming on Paramount Plus the following day, July 31, at 6am ET/3am PT.

All new episodes of Season 27 will follow this release date and time schedule, with single episodes dropping weekly. It was supposed to kick off proceedings with a two-episode premiere, but this was later changed to one.

  • 7:30am India Standard Time (July 31)
  • 12pm Australia (July 31)
  • 2pm New Zealand (July 31)

Similarly, here are different time zones for when the new episodes land on Paramount Plus:

  • 7am Brazil

  • 3:30pm India Standard Time
  • 8pm Australia

How many episodes are there?

There are 10 episodes in South Park Season 27, which will no doubt be music to the ears of fans.

In recent years, South Park seasons have been noticeably shorter, with some chapters only running for six episodes or focusing on standalone specials.

This shift came as Parker and Stone balanced their massive Paramount deal alongside an ongoing tug-of-war between HBO Max over streaming rights.

But with Season 27, the show is back to a traditional format, with the recent five-year $1.5 billion licensing agreement including 50 new episodes across five seasons.

Speaking about the deal, Stone said (via Deadline), “Trey and I and the whole South Park crew are grateful for this extension and this deal.

“We want to thank Chris McCarthy and Keyes Hill-Edgar for years of great partnership and are looking forward to continuing to make South Park for the next five years.”

Parker added, “We are grateful for this opportunity and deeply honored by the trust placed in us. This is about more than a contract – it’s about our commitment to this organization, our teammates, and our fans.”

How to watch South Park

If you have cable TV or a platform that offers Comedy Central, you can watch new episodes of South Park as they air live. But if you can wait a few hours, the best way to watch Season 27 is via Paramount Plus.

There are a couple of ways to enjoy new episodes for free. Paramount Plus offers new users a seven-day free trial, which you can sign up for directly via the streaming service. Alternatively, Walmart Plus subscribers can get a 30-day free trial.
