YouTuber SteveWillDoIt has addressed the situation around his appearance on imPaulsive with Logan Paul after his episode ended abruptly. 

Back on November 25, SteveWillDoIt, one of the founding members of the NELK Boys, appeared on imPaulsive with Logan Paul and Mike Majlak. The content creator had been speaking about his recent unbanning from YouTube and plans for the future. 

However, things got contentious as he spoke about Logan, Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson, and made a number of jokes. Just before the 40-minute mark, Steve called time on the podcast, saying he was done and ready to leave. He tried to make a return, but was quickly moved on by Logan. 

In the aftermath, he called Majlak a “fake friend” and a “bad person” for what had gone down. The imPaulsive co-host said he had “protected” Steve and that a good portion of the episode had been cut. 

SteveWillDoIt responds to ImPaulsive drama again

Steve had posted a number of social media posts regarding the drama from the podcast, but also addressed it during his appearance on One Night with Steiny. 

“I ended up not remembering the interview and watched it back. In the beginning, I was sober, and I was drinking tequila a lot. But with Logan Paul, he was asking, ‘how do you make your money?’ He was really interested in how I make my money,” he said.

“I don’t think Logan is a low-IQ individual. If he’s genuinely curious about my bread and an ally of mine, he would have started, as a business owner and smart person, where I would have started. He never asked me about Happy Dad. 

“He’s seen me for seven years, Happy Dad is everywhere. You’re either really stupid, which I don’t think so, or you’re just trying to catch me slip. There’s nothing to slip to, but the internet can change anything.”

Steve agreed with Steiny that the pair had “good intentions,” but just couldn’t believe that Happy Dad wasn’t brought up.

“If you’re going to talk about my finances, talk Happy Dad. It’s literally everywhere,” he added. “If I don’t bring up Prime, I’m either the lowest IQ dumb motherf**ker in the world, or I’m trying to f*ck with him. That’s my opinion, maybe I’m wrong, but if I was him and I was doing that, that’s how I would be doing that.”
