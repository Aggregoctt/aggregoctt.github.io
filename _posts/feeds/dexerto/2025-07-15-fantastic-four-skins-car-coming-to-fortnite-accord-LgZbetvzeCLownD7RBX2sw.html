A brand new Fortnite update is on its way, and multiple leaks have suggested that Marvel’s first family could be coming to the game.

Move over, Superman, it’s now well and truly the month of the Fantastic Four. Thanks to their upcoming movie, all eyes are on the First Family, whether that’s through teaser trailers or their leaked appearances in extremely popular games.

Now, Fortnite has its 36.20 update, which already promises to bring tons of exciting features, with one potentially being centered around the Fantastic Four.

Fortnite getting Fantastic Four skins according to leak

According to renowned leaker, HYPEX, the Fantastic Four will be coming to Fortnite in this update (July 15). They didn’t reveal exactly how and in what capacity, but other renowned leakers were quick to verify these claims and add more details.

Thanks to another leaker, Shiina, we know that players will get access to “Reed Richards, Susan Storm, Johnny Storm” as confirmed skins. Each skin will offer different animated styles and fighting techniques, including Fantastic Fists, Psionic Orbs, Fantastic Calculations, and Torch’s Flame when equipped to their respective outfits.

Interestingly, Fantastic Fists seems to be for Reed Richards, instead of Ben Grimm (The Thing). Currently, there’s no word as to whether we’ll be getting The Thing in Fortnite yet, as only the above three have been leaked.

That’s not all, it’s also rumored that players will get hold of a “free collaboration car,” which is assumed to be the Fantasticar.

It’s important to note, the above are all leaks, albeit by pretty reputable sources, so it’s subject to change. The update drops on July 15, so if these details are confirmed, we’ll be updating this article, so be sure to check back soon to see if Marvel’s First Family is finally coming to Fortnite in its latest update.
