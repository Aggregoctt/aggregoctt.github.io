When Nintendo released the Wii U in 2012, it was one of the most misunderstood consoles of its time — but also one of the most quietly creative. Built around the GamePad, the Wii U experimented with second-screen play, asymmetric multiplayer, and off-TV gaming years before those ideas became common elsewhere. It never reached the audience Nintendo hoped for, yet the players who did invest in it found a library packed with imagination and ambition.

This was the system that introduced Splatoon to the world, let players build and share levels in Super Mario Maker, and delivered definitive editions like Mario Kart 8 and Wind Waker HD. It also became the home of cult favorites and bold experiments, from Bayonetta 2 and The Wonderful 101 to Xenoblade Chronicles X and Affordable Space Adventures. Even Breath of the Wild began its journey here, closing the Wii U era with one of the most influential games ever made.

Commercially overlooked but creatively fearless, the Wii U earned a legacy far stronger than its sales suggest.

With that in mind, here’s our ranking of the best Wii U games.
