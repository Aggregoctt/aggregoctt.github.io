xQc put his honesty to the test in a chaotic lie detector stream, as MrBeast and Adin Ross put him on the spot about his net worth, relationships, opinions on other streamers and the Kick vs Twitch debate.

Kicking off with a bang, the first question from Adin was, “Have you ever had feelings for Pokimane?” He initially said “No” before the test flagged it as inconclusive. After trying again, he passed and laughed it off.

On his current standing in streaming, he claimed he is “less” relevant than two years ago, saying, “The sea has more fish. Therefore, losing relevancy is not a reflection on me. It’s a reflection on the ocean.”

xQc’s net worth revealed and more

Money questions drew some of the biggest reactions. He denied having more than $100 million in his bank account but admitted “Yes” when the question switched to his crypto holdings. “It’s true to both,” the operator confirmed.

On the topic of future earnings, he said “No” when asked if he would make over $15 million this year, explaining that while his investments might return that amount, that’s not what he was thinking about. “When I think about making money, it’s like going to work and getting a paycheck. That’s how my brain processes that.”

He gave straight answers on relationships and rivals. “I like Kick more than Twitch,” he said, passing the test. He confirmed he has “only dated streamers” and admitted “Yes” to being cheated on. Asked if there was any streamer he “actually hates,” he replied, “No” and was truthful.

There were plenty of lighter moments. He admitted “Yes” to peeing sitting down because “it’s cleaner,” said “No” to dating Madison Beer or Sabrina Carpenter even if single, and refused to call America anything but “the greatest country on earth.” When asked to choose between sacrificing Adin Ross or losing a finger, xQc quickly picked Adin.

The most blunt moment came at the end. When asked “Have you ever cheated?” xQc answered “Yes,” passing the test. Wrapping up, he told viewers, “I answered truthfully all of them… I didn’t try to lie, at least.”
