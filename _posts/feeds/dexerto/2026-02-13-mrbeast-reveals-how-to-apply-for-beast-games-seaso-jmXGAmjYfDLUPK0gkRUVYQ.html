Beast Games Season 2 isn’t over just yet, but if you want to be in with a chance of winning $5 million, MrBeast has revealed how to apply for Season 3 of his Prime Video series.

Jimmy Donaldson – aka MrBeast – has basically built a career on massive giveaways, and so it’s no surprise that the same can be said for his competition series. Alongside the final $5 million prize, he shells out another $5 million in bribes and prizes. 

In Season 2 so far, Player 148, Ian, won an island worth $1.8 million, Player 126, JT, took home a $1 million bribe, and Player 152, Monika, just bagged herself a cool $500,000. 

Not only has Prime Video given Season 3 the go-ahead, but the show has sent out a casting call with some unique specifications – here’s how to apply. 

How to apply for Beast Games Season 3

If you want to be in with a chance, head to this page and fill out your details. There are some criteria that are worth making note of, the first being that you must be at least 18 years old to apply. 

You’ll need to submit a one-minute video, in which you introduce yourself, give your name, what makes you unique, what drives you, and what you’d do with $5 million. The team also wants to see recent photos of yourself.

Notably, each season so far has had a unique angle, and in this case, it’s about seeking different nationalities. As MrBeast said in a recent video, “Season 2, we’re still releasing, but I gotta start working on Season 3. 

“We’re looking for people from every country on the planet, and if you think you have what it takes to represent your country on the big stage for one of the largest cash prizes in history, arguably the largest cash prize… then apply right here to be on Beast Games Season 3.”

Meanwhile, the application page states, “Show your international pride! Represent your country in whatever way feels right to you – traditional clothing, flags, symbols, or anything that showcases your heritage.”

Perhaps Beast Games will be following on from Physical: Asia, which saw huge success and involved eight countries competing to be titled the strongest nation. 

Alongside the info to include in your application, there are a few rules to make note of, including that you need to: 

  • Be 18 or older
  • Have a valid passport that lasts to January 2027
  • Be able to legally travel and reenter the US, UK, Italy, France, Monaco, South Africa, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Panama, Fiji, UAE, Japan, Mexico, and Canada
  • Have the required visa or waiver to allow you to compete in Beast Games in the US or aforementioned countries
  • Be able to receive the prize winnings in your country
  • Be free of any obligations for six consecutive weeks during April to July 2026
  • Not be a current candidate for public office or intend to run for public office from July 2026 to the following year
  • Not be employed or closely connected to anyone working on Beast Games or the MrBeast YouTube channel
