Palworld is officially making the jump from survival sandbox to tabletop, with a new Trading Card Game launching on July 30, 2026.

According to details shared on the game’s official website, the Palworld TCG is designed as a two-player competitive card game built around the same core ideas that made the creature-collecting survival game so popular.

Players will deploy different Pals, use their unique traits, gather resources, and build bases as they battle toward victory.

Palworld TCG launches July 2026

Rather than being a simple reskin, the card game is structured to mirror Palworld’s mix of creature collecting, base management, and tactical combat. Each Pal brings its own abilities to the table, allowing players to create different strategies depending on which companions they put into play.

The press release states that players will “fight alongside their Pal companions” while facing off against opponents, with matches focused on strategic and tactical battles that reward smart deployment and synergy between cards.

“Team up with your trusty Pals and experience the thrill of overcoming formidable enemies through this exciting trading card game,” reads the official description for the game.

While no pricing, card lists, or starter deck details have been revealed yet, the Palworld card game will release on July 30, 2026, so fans won’t have to wait too much longer before they can battle it out with their favorite monsters.
