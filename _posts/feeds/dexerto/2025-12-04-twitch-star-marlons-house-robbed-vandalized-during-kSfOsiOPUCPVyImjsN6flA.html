Twitch star Marlon has revealed that his new home was broken into and vandalized while he was away on his 40-day streaming tour. 

Streaming tours are becoming increasingly more popular for big stars. IShowSpeed has led the way, travelling across the globe to meet fans on almost every continent, and others have been following his lead. 

Twitch star Marlon has been following suit, travelling on a massive streaming tour. However, as his most recent tour across the United States came to a close, he returned home to find that his home had been broken into. 

“Longest month of my life, and I come back to my house getting broken into and robbed,” the streamer said on a set of Snapchat stories.

Marlon’s home robbed during streaming tour

“Y’all know I just got my house a few months ago and I haven’t been home, like I said, and now I find out that my house has been broken into. Smashed my entire back door, smashed the entire window down, and clothes all over the floor,” he continued. 

“I’ve been on a flight, I didn’t know until I just got here. Took my PS5, stole all my s*it, clothes everywhere. Yeah, fun bro.” 

Marlon also showed off video of the police taking a look at the home invasion, documenting some of the damage, and suggesting that the intruders were also searching for batteries. 

“Been gone for about 40 days from home, just came back and walked into this,” he also said, posting on X (formerly Twitter). “S*it is just a joke at this point.”

Marlon hasn’t been live since the incident, and has not announced when he’ll next be in front of the camera. It remains to be seen as to when he’ll next do so.
