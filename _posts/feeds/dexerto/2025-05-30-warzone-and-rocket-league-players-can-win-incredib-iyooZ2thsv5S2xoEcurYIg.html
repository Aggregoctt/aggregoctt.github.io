Warzone and Rocket League fans have the chance to put their skills on display and win a VIP trip to the Esports World Cup in Riyadh. Here’s what you need to know.

The Esports World Cup, which is the largest esports tournament in the world, has been around since 2024. Held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, gamers have been able to compete in some of the biggest titles like Call of Duty, Counter-Strike, League of Legends, and Valorant for some life-changing cash. Oh, and some sweet trophies.

Only the best players in the world have been able to make history in Riyadh, putting their skills up against the rest in a bid to secure their spot.

Well, you can actually join them if you’re a skilled Warzone or Rocket League player, thanks to the Esports World Cup and Repeat.

What prizes are up for grabs? 

That’s right, The Esports World is offering five players – two from Rocket League, three from Call of Duty – the chance to attend EWC with a VIP Hospitality package.

The package includes flights, hotel, visa costs, local experiences, backstage tour of the Esports World Cup, and express passes for this summer’s event. Each winner will also get a guest spot, so they’ll be able to share the experience with someone else.

You’ll be able to win one through Repeat’s Road to EWC Challenge, so long as you come out on top in a stacked field.

How to enter

For your chance to win, you’ll have to register on Repeat for either Warzone or Rocket League. The Road to EWC Challenge will be available across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Latin America.

They get underway on May 27 with the first stage. That will last until June 22, with everyone being eligible. After that, the Warzone event will be whittled down to the top 140 competitors, with the Rocket League portion consisting of the top eight.

That second stage will see Warzone become a best-of-three with points for kills and placement. Rocket League will be a best-of-three double elimination bracket before a best-of-five grand final.

The only thing standing between you and a VIP EWC experience? Signing up. So, get a move on and head over to Repeat.
