Rematch is a fast-paced 5v5 soccer game from indie studio Sloclap, the same developer behind 2022 beat ’em up, Sifu.

Likened to Rocket League for soccer fans, players take control of a single kicker on the pitch from a third-person perspective rather than an entire team like in traditional sports games like EA Sports FC.

Throughout 2025, Sloclap has held betas for the arcade title, the most recent taking place between May 28 and May 31. Per SteamDB, player counts for the beta test peaked at 159,865 players during the 72 hours, indicating substantial interest.

Here’s everything we know about Rematch, including when you’ll be able to play it.

Rematch releases officially on June 19. It’s had a number of beta phases that have allowed players to try it early, which is where you may have seen early gameplay. However, the full version of the game won’t be out and playable until that June 19 date. At least you won’t have to wait long.

Platforms

Rematch will be available on PC (via Steam), PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. On Steam, Rematch is available to pre-purchase for £20.99 (appox. $30). It’s not known if console versions of Rematch will cost the same as on PC, but you can likely expect a similar, if not identical price tag.

If you want to get in on the action sooner, a Pro and Elite edition of Rematch on Steam grants three-day early access.

Rematch gameplay details & features

Sloclap describes Rematch as providing “A new perspective on football,” with the following features:

  • Third-person camera – Tackle, dribble, aim, and shoot; dive into the heart of the action from an immersive viewpoint.
  • One player control – Observe the action, position yourself, execute perfectly; every decision matters, and relying on your teammates is essential to prevail.
  • Skill-based gameplay – In a level playing field, with no player stats, victory is only about player skill and tactical coordination.
  • Team-based online gameplay – Designed from the ground up as an online multiplayer experiece, Rematch offers split-second gameplay response that always feels fair.
  • Competitive Modes – Challenge yourself and your mates to reach the top leagues.
  • Seasonal Content – There’s always something new around the corner. Every season will feature new game modes and add more cosmetic content.
