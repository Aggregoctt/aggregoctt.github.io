Fans of the whacky nuclear wasteland can pay to visit the set of Fallout Season 3 when production kicks off later this year… But it’s not as straightforward as buying a ticket.

Fallout Season 2 ended with a bang as Maximus (Aaron Moten) brawled with Deathclaws on the streets of New Vegas, while the Ghoul (Walton Goggins) searched for his family, and Lucy (Ella Purnell) put a stop to her father’s nefarious plans. That’s all without mentioning Caesar’s Legion marching on the city as the Brotherhood of Steel descends into civil war.

The season also moved the Enclave into prime place as the overarching big bad of the show (which players of the games already anticipated) and the second season leaves things open for a bigger conflict with the all-powerful faction.

Now, fans have the opportunity to head to the Fallout Season 3 set for themselves, but we hope you’ve got some deep pockets.

Fans can visit the Fallout Season 3 set if they win a charity auction

While the next season of the Prime Video show is a mystery, one lucky fan (and a guest) can get a glimpse of Fallout Season 3 if they win an auction for the Make-a-Wish foundation. The only problem is the minimum auction bid is $19,000.

Sure, the winner will get their travel and accommodation sorted for them, but that is a steep price to pay for the privilege of stepping into the nuclear wasteland. Luckily, Bethesda will also chuck in a handful of Fallout goodies.

According to the auction listing, the winner will also get an “exclusive collection of Fallout fan gear: a Fallout S2 poster, Fallout jacket, Fallout beanie, Fallout playing cards with leather case, Nuka Cola bottle cap magnet, an action figure of Cooper, and The Ghoul bobblehead.”

So, if you’ve got $19,000 or more and you want to visit the Fallout Season 3 set while donating to charity, then you’re in luck. Of course, you’ll likely be rubbing shoulders with the cast, just make sure you’re monitoring rads on your Pip-Boy. The auction goes live on February 20 at 3pm PT/6pm ET.
