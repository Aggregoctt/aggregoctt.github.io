The Esports Awards returns in 2025 with its most ambitious ceremony yet, celebrating a full decade of greatness. For the first time, the awards will honor the best players, teams, creators, and moments from 2015 to 2025.

Voting is open now and runs daily until August 11. Winners will be revealed during the live ceremony in Riyadh at the Esports World Cup on August 13.

How to Vote

Fans can vote once per day via the official Esports Awards site.

Some categories are 100% public vote, while others use a 25/75 split between public and panel voting. A few are decided by the panel alone.
