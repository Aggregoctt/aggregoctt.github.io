YouTuber Majd has already crossed 1M views on a video and has received over 100k subs just days after appearing in MrBeast’s latest video.

On June 21, MrBeast uploaded a video where he challenged his friend, Majd, to stay inside of an area until he lost 100 pounds. The deal was originally set for $250,000, but the YouTube star ended up paying his friend over $400,000 after the goal was reached.

Just days after the video went live, Majd uploaded his reaction on a YouTube channel of his own, which was the first video shared to that channel. Shortly after it first went live, he had just over a thousand subscribers.

Fast forward just three days and the man has already skyrocketed in popularity across YouTube and other social media sites.

Majd gets 100k subs in just days after MrBeast video

At the time of writing, Majd’s first video has amassed over 1.4M views. It was dedicated to his fitness Coach Tyler Wall, who died while filming the video.

Within three days of the video being posted, his YouTube channel absolutely skyrocketed in popularity as the man quickly reached 119,000 subscribers on the platform.

That’s not the only place Majd has gained a plethora of followers, either. On Instagram, his first post was made in early June, and since then, his account has grown to over 260,000 followers.

The man has over 170,000 followers on TikTok as well, and even had to make a post addressing “fake accounts” on the platform.

Meanwhile, MrBeast’s video has taken off in popularity, too. It’s already among the best-performing uploads he’s made in 2025, with over 130M views in just five days according to Viewstats.
