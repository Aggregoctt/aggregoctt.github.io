Lara Croft’s future is now set in stone as two brand-new Tomb Raider games were just revealed at The Game Awards 2025.

Everyone’s favorite gaming archaeologist is back (sorry, Nathan Drake). Lara Croft’s next adventures were just unveiled as the Tomb Raider series took center stage during the 2025 Game Awards event.

Not only is there a remake of the very first game, known as Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, but there’s also another brand new entry in the franchise on the way, Tomb Raider: Catalyst.

While we’ve known about a live-action TV series in the works for a number of years now, these mark the first new video games since Shadow of the Tomb Raider in 2018 – a seven-year gap.
