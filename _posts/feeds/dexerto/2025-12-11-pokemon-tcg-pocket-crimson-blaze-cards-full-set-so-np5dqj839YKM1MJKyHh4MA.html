Pokemon TCG Pocket is turning up the heat with the Crimson Blaze expansion, a fiery new set centered around explosive attackers, stunning artwork, and a refreshed focus on aggressive playstyles.

Expect the meta to shift quickly as Mega Charizard Y ex, Mega Venusaur ex, and Mega Blastoise ex headline with high-pressure cards and new insane combos. Fire decks get major upgrades, while slower builds may struggle to keep up.

Crimson Blaze also rolls out fresh collection features, holiday events, and exclusive login rewards, making this one of Pocket’s most energetic expansions yet.
