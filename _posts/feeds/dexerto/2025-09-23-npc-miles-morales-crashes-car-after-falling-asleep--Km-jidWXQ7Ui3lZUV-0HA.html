NPC Miles Morales, real name Malik, says he crashed his car after dozing off behind the wheel following a late night of streaming for Kai Cenat’s Mafiathon 3.

Malik, who also streams under the Twitch alias leaksworld_, told viewers he had just been in an accident while going live the morning after another all-night shift reading donations and subscriptions in character. He reassured fans that he wasn’t hurt, but admitted to feeling “dumb” after hitting another driver.

“I’m good, bro. I basically just got into an accident on the way here. I feel dumb as f***, bro,” Malik said on stream. “IRL is so hard. I didn’t get hurt or anything, but I fell asleep and I hit somebody.”

Kai Cenat calls for medics, donates 200 subs

The accident quickly spread across both Malik’s and Cenat’s streams, with Cenat donating 200 subs to Malik and asking Mafiathon medics to check on him. “Medic, can y’all go check on Malik, please. Miles. Please make sure he’s good,” Cenat said while live.

Mafiathon 3 cast members had spent the night in a moving U-Haul instead of the mansion’s beds, with Malik staying up in character to read alerts while the others slept. Despite the accident, he returned the following night ready for another all-nighter. The crew briefly check on Malik, asking if he was good after the incident but Malik refused to break character. He spent the rest of the night switching between characters and continuing his usual antics for Cenat’s marathon event.
