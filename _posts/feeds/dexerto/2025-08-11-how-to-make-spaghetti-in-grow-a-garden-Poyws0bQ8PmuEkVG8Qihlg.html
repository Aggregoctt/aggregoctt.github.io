Grow a Garden‘s cooking event has added tons of new recipes to the game, including a delicious plate of Spaghetti. Unfortunately, making it isn’t that easy, unless you have the ingredients.

While the Roblox game is all about growing vegetables, fruit, and plants, Grow a Garden occasionally hosts events, allowing players to complete new challenges and grab exclusive rewards. Well, now Chris P. Bacon is finally here, offering just that.

So, if you’re taking part in the event and Chris has asked for Spaghetti, then here’s all you need to know about how to make it, including all the ingredients and rarities so you can bag better rewards.

All Grow a Garden Spaghetti recipes

There are seven different Spaghetti recipes we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden, each offering a different rarity. Thankfully, you can make them with different ingredients, guaranteeing the rarity.

No matter the rarity of your dish, the cook time will be 5 minutes and 50 seconds.

How to make Spaghetti

Once you have all the ingredients listed above, you can make your Spaghetti; all you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Go to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat.
  • Head over to the steel pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them inside the pot.
  • Press the green Cook button.
  • Wait the allotted time and grab your dish.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and satisfied Chris’s craving, he’ll offer you a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth giving Chris the best meals you can.

You can check out all the available rewards in our guide.

You can cook Spaghetti at any time, but it’s best to save it for when Chris is craving it, so you can get those rewards.
