Rockstar Games has launched the Cfx Marketplace, a digital storefront for verified modders to make real money for their GTA Online creations.

In 2023, Rockstar acquired Cfx.re, the creative team behind some of the most popular GTA Online roleplay servers, including FiveM and RedM. Three years on, the partnership has led to the creation of a premium marketplace for approved game add-ons and variations.

Rockstar announced the Cfx Marketplace on January 13, 2026. Described as a “curated digital storefront,” it’s a place where established modders in the community can share their work in an authenticated way, and earn money for doing so.

If you’re looking at hosting your own GTA Online server, for instance, you can now flesh it out with purchasable goods like maps, vehicles, activities, and more, without having to code and design it all yourself.

Rockstar launches marketplace for paid GTA Online mods

As the “official Rockstar modding UGC marketplace,” the service allows server hosts to buy a range of digital goods for their custom experiences.

Let’s take functional ATMs as one example. A modder in the community has already put in the man hours to create this system. Players can hack ATMs, use drills to break into them, blow them up, or even attach a hook and drive off with one.

For $37.99 USD, you can purchase all of the relevant coding and integrate it into your server. The creator of that particular mod gets an undisclosed chunk of revenue from each sale.

Some items are available for free while others are a bit more expensive, ranging up to a $428.99 USD activities bundle, packing in the likes of bowling, bungee jumping, and more.

A number of goods currently available also have recurring fees. For example, a “lore-friendly” emergency services package with vehicles and uniforms will run you $32.99 a month.

It’s unclear at this stage how such a marketplace may impact the future of GTA Online when GTA 6 is in focus. It’s currently described as an “initial launch”, with evident plans to continue expanding the platform moving forward.
