If you want to give yourself the best possible chances of winning fights in Warzone Season 1 Reloaded, then you need to run the meta loadout with the best TTK to cover you at any distance.

After integrating with Black Ops 7, there are well over 200 different weapons in Warzone, all with their own strengths and weaknesses. This can make choosing the right combination tricky, as every category has plenty of appealing options.

However, as always, there are a handful of guns that stand above the rest when it comes to time-to-kill, meaning that, if you hit your shots, you’ve got the edge in every fight.

Warzone loadout with best TTK makes winning fights a breeze

As highlighted by CoD expert WhosImmortal in an upload to his YouTube channel, when it comes to long-range battles, the MK. 78 LMG has one of the fastest TTKs in the entire game. It drops fully-plated enemies in just 657ms up to 50 meters, outshining Assault Rifles like the AK-27 and M15 Mod 0.

Its rivals overtake it slightly between 50 and 60 meters, but it retakes the top spot from 60 meters and onwards, with a 764ms that only Sniper Rifles can compete with.

When it comes to short-range, the meta is much closer, but the Sturmwolf 45 just edges out the competition. It kills in just 558ms up to the 13 meters, with WhosImmortal calling it “insanely good” in close quarters combat.

Not only that, but it’s also the “easiest SMG in the entire game” thanks to its low recoil, meaning you’ll be able to land your shots consistently to get the best out of it.

This combination absolutely “dominates” in Season 1 Reloaded, as long as you’re using each weapon in the right circumstances. Check out the best attachments to run for the MK. 78 and Sturmwolf 45 for maximum impact:

MK.78

  • Magazine: MFS Heatshield Belt Fed
  • Underbarrel: Bowen Sentry Foregrip
  • Optic: Greaves Accuspot 3X
  • Barrel: 22″ Impulse HB-762 Barrel
  • Muzzle: RL-7.62 Compensator

Sturmwolf 45

  • Magazine: Loxus Extended Mag
  • Underbarrel: Envoy Foregrip
  • Stock: Itinerant Light Stock
  • Barrel: 14.8″ Perigee Barrel
  • Rear Grip: Selene Rover Grip

If you want to clear a building quickly with no fuss, then make sure you have the SMG equipped. But if you plan on hunkering down and deleting enemies before they know what hit them, then use the LMG to take them out quickly.
