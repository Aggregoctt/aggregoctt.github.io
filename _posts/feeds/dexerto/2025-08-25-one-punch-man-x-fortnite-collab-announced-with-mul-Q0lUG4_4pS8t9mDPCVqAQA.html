One Punch Man is finally coming to Fortnite in a new major collaboration, after being teased on the game’s X account.

When Fortnite posted “100 push-ups, 100 sit-ups, 100 squats, and a 10km run! Do it every single day!!!” on X, fans went wild. After all, the epic yet rather stoic superhero is the protagonist of one of the biggest animes out there.

Well, now he’s about to be in Fortnite, alongside a variety of other leaked additions that any fan of Saitama will absolutely adore.

When is One Punch Man coming to Fortnite?

One Punch Man will be coming to Fortnite on August 27, 2025, as announced on the Fortnite X page, featuring a first look at how the new skin will look in-game.

What’s coming in the One Punch Man x Fortnite collab?

Currently, the only fully confirmed additions are the Saitama, Tatsumaki, and Genos skins, as seen in the post above. However, various leaks have revealed we’ll also be getting a few other weapons, instruments, and more.

These include, thanks to renowned leaker Shiina:

  • Saitama: Skin, Back Bling, 2x Emotes, Pickaxe, Glider, Drums, Wrap
  • Tatsumaki: Skin, Back Bling, Emote, Pickaxe, Microphone
  • Genos: Skin (Glow Variant), Back Bling, Emote, Pickaxe, Keytar

As mentioned before, Saitama is best known as One Punch Man, and is the protagonist of the anime and manga. Tatsumaki is another incredible hero in One Punch Man who often puts Saitama through his paces. Lastly, Genos is the apprentice and best friend of Saitama.

Aside from the release date, a view of the skins, and predicted bundles, nothing more has been announced for the collaboration. However, if Fortnite adds more information or releases new teasers, we’ll be sure to update this article.
