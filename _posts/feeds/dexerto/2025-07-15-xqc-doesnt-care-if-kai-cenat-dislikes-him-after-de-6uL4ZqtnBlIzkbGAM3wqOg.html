Twitch and Kick star Felix ‘xQc’ Lengyel admitted that he “doesn’t care” that Kai Cenat won’t interact with him after calling Kai’s treatment of Adin Ross “awful.”

When Adin Ross was unbanned from Twitch back in early 2025, he spoke about a number of big-name creators congratulating him. However, there seemed to be some shade thrown Kai Cenat’s way.

The pair regularly collaborated before Adin’s ban, but have seemingly fallen out since then. Though Adin has praised Kai’s big streams. Being a pal of Adin’s, xQc has regularly weighed in on things, taking his friend’s side in any suspected drama.

He has also taken a few swipes at Kai, most notably during some viewbotting drama with the AMP’s stars’ friends, and calling his treatment of Adin “awful.”

xQc ‘doesn’t care’ about collabs with Kai Cenat & others

Some fans have stated that Kai doesn’t bother with xQc as a result of his friendship with Adin, but he doesn’t care. “I don’t give a f*ck though,” the Canadian said, responding to one fan’s post on X (formerly Twitter). 

“Like, I know he doesn’t f*ck with me, does that make sense? If somebody didn’t tell me why they don’t f*ck with me, and they were kind of playing this game online and trying to play both sides, then I’d be annoyed by it.

“I’d be annoyed, but I know he doesn’t f*ck with me, so it’s not that big of a deal. I don’t give a f*ck. People act like it’s something that limits me or whatever. Bro, I’ve never cared for collab s*it. I don’t mind it, but I don’t need it! I don’t play my cards in a way that I need this, I don’t give a f*ck about it.”

The former Overwatch star also questioned how Ross deals with some of the shade thrown his way.

“That’s just awful, man. Adin, don’t know how you deal with that s*it, I’m not gonna lie. I wouldn’t be able to deal with that,” he said. 

Kai and xQc have mingled in the past at The Sidemen’s charity matches in London, but they’re clearly not on the same page.
