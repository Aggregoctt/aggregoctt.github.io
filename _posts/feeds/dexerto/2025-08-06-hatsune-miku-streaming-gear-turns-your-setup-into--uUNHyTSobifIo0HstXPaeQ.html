Hatsune Miku is joining the streaming world in style thanks to a new collaboration with Elgato, with themed gear based on the virtual idol.  

Revealed ahead of Hatsune Miku’s Magical Mirai 2025 concert in Osaka, the collab includes a Miku-themed Stream Deck MK.2, Wave DX microphone, Wave XLR audio interface, and Mic Arm LP. 

Each product is decked out in Miku’s signature turquoise color palette, with pixel-style graphics so that you can stream in style. 

According to Elgato, the new product line will debut in person at Magical Mirai’s Osaka event from August 9 to 11, before being sold later at the Tokyo venue and through official online channels. 

Hastune Miku x Elgato streaming collab

The Elgato x Miku streaming setup also comes bundled with exclusive digital extras, including icon packs, audio-reactive screensavers, and animated backgrounds. The collab marks Elgato’s first official partnership with a virtual artist, and who better to lead the charge than the legendary Vocaloid – Hatsune Miku. 

While no international pricing and shipping have been revealed yet, given the huge push for Hatsune Miku collabs and her global popularity, it will only be a matter of time before you can get your hands on this idol-themed equipment. 

While Elgato’s collab purely focuses on streaming, ASUS also announced its own Hatsune Miku collaboration, unveiling a full line of themed gaming hardware. The ASUS ROG x Miku collection includes a custom motherboard, RTX 5080 GPU, 260Hz gaming monitor, AIO cooler, and more. 

From microphones to motherboards, Hatsune Miku is making her presence known across gaming, PC hardware, and now streaming setups.
