The next expansion coming to Pokemon TCG Pocket – Wisdom of Sea and Sky – dives into the Johto region and its mighty Legendaries, so check all the cards you’ll be able to pull with the new boosters.

The Pokemon TCG Pocket meta changes every time a new set is released, so players should get ready for new cards and decks popping in Ranked matches, as they will probably be filled with Gen 2 creatures now that the Wisdom of Sea and Sky is coming.

This A4 set brings back fan-favorites like Lugia ex, Ho‑Oh ex, and Red Gyarados, alongside the classic starters Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile, Baby Pokemon, and more. Plus, Shinedust will finally replace the Trade Tokens in the trading mechanic, and a Wishlist feature will be added.

Here are all of the cards that will release on July 30, 2025, with the new expansion.
