Insurance company The General is crossing over with Fortnite this August with a tournament featuring 16 top Twitch streamers, including Grammy Award-winning artist and popular creator T-Pain, competing on a custom-made map.

The competition is based on The General Road Test Royale, a custom map created in Fortnite’s Creative mode that swaps high-speed racing for smart, controlled driving.

Players must navigate digital recreations of cities like Miami, New York, and San Francisco while avoiding obstacles and maintaining precision to progress through the course. The map was developed in partnership with Twitch, Look North World, and National Driver Training to ensure that the mode was both fun to play and accurate when promoting road safety.

T-Pain, who is streaming the map throughout July, will return in August to compete in the official tournament.

Viewers tuning into the stream on Twitch will be able to enter two giveaways. One winner will take home a 2025 Honda Civic, while others can win prizes such as a PlayStation 5, a pair of Beyerdynamic headphones, and Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer glasses.

The draw is completely free to enter, with full terms set to be announced ahead of the broadcast.

How to play The General Road Test Royale in Fortnite

If you want to try the mode for yourself, The General Road Test Royale is now available in Fortnite under Island Code 9524-2023-3031.

Simply head to the main menu, go to the search bar, and type in the code to bring it up right away. Alternatively, just search for the name of the mode.

The full tournament, including the schedule and stream links, is expected to be shared closer to the event in August.
