Fortnite has had some wild crossovers, but few hit as hard as Street Fighter.

These skins were part fan service and part tribute to an iconic franchise. But after August 2022, they vanished for nearly three years and these skins ghosted the Item Shop. That silence turned them into digital gold. Chun-Li especially became a unicorn of Fortnite fashion and every emote queen’s dream.

People begged, memes were made, and now… the wait is over. Here’s everything you need to know about the Street Fighter bundle return in Fortnite

When will the Street Fighter skins return to Fortnite?

The Street Fighter bundle will be available in the Item Shop starting June 20, 2025. Keep in mind that the shop refreshes every night at 0:00 UTC. Unforunately, Epic Games hasn’t confirmed when they’ll be removed again.

This was announced on Fortnite’s official X account with a short teaser.

What’s in the Street Fighter set?

Each character comes loaded with themed gear. Expect flashy throwbacks and punchy flair. Here’s who’s included:

  • Ryu
  • Chun-Li
  • Cammy
  • Guile
  • Sakura
  • Blanka

Each bundle also includes back blings, pickaxes, emotes, gliders, and loading screens. In total, the OG Street Fighter set has 26 cosmetics.

How much do Street Fighter cosmetics cost?

In the past, each individual Street Fighter skin has cost 1,600 V-Bucks while the bundles have set you back 2,200 V-Bucks, each including two characters, two emotes, a loading screen, and a backpack.

Epic has not made the prices for this return official, but we’d expect something along those lines.
