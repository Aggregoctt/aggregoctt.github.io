Asmongold has cleaned his room, with the streamer sharing the results during a January 3 livestream on Kick and giving viewers a breakdown of how long it took and what was uncovered.

Discussing his cleaning spree on stream, Asmon said the clean-up took him three hours and provided a substantial workout.

“We got the entire thing cleaned up. This took me three hours. I’m gonna be honest, my legs hurt. My legs hurt, my arms hurt, my back hurts,” he told viewers.

This isn’t the first time the streamer has shared anecdotes about his cleaning and home in general. In 2024, he shared before-and-after photos of his home after giving it a deep clean, and once found a dead rat in his house.

Asmon reveals what he found while cleaning

That being the case, one viewer naturally asked how many dead rodents Asmon had discovered while cleaning, to which he responded, “surprisingly zero.”

What he did discover instead was old food that had gone unnoticed. “I didn’t realize how many rotten burritos were in my room,” he said, adding, “There’s like five. I had no idea. I thought there’d be, at most, two, right? At most, two burritos, but no.”
