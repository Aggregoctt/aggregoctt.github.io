If you want to watch the Dexter: Resurrection finale early, you’re in luck… except not really, because a Russian dub of Episode 10 has leaked, and spoilers are already being shared on social media.

The ninth episode of the franchise’s second revival – a sequel to New Blood that follows Dexter on his son’s trail to New York, where he encounters a society of serial killers – has just aired, and it features one of the show’s biggest shocks yet.

However, amid upset over the penultimate installment and excitement for next week’s ending, the finale has already leaked online via a Russian streaming site.

It has a Russian dub, so in terms of the dialogue, English viewers are safe – alas, given the show revolves around people killing each other, it wouldn’t be hard to watch it and figure out what happens.

Dexter: Resurrection fans warned after finale leaks online

Images, clips, and plot details from Dexter: Resurrection Episode 10 have already emerged online since the Russian dub was leaked. Take this advice: mute everything on X/Twitter, do your best to stay off Reddit, and hope you don’t see any spoilers before it officially airs.

“To those of you sharing the #DexterResurrection Leaks: PLEASE STOP!!! One would think people would have enough love for the show to only share what @paramountplus puts out!! We don’t need to kill the ratings ESPECIALLY after Original Sin just got cancelled,” one user complained.

“Logging off Twitter and won’t be reading my YouTube comments for the next week because Dexter: Resurrection finale episode leaked and I want to avoid spoilers. Stay safe out there, y’all,” another wrote.

Fans have asked Paramount Plus to drop the finale early, so they don’t need to evade spoilers for a whole week. “We need early access or it’s over. The #DexterResurrection Episode 10/10 leaks are real. It’s online. We won’t survive another full week without spoilers,” one fan posted.

“I’m praying to God that they release the final episode early or it’s gonna be hell avoiding spoilers,” another wrote. “Debating whether I watch it now even though I can’t understand sh*t or just wait another week,” a third posted.

Unfortunately, it’s highly unlikely that Paramount will release it early. Finales have leaked before: the finale episode of The Last of Us Season 2 leaked, as did the House of the Dragon Season 1 finale, and neither show’s release schedule changed.
