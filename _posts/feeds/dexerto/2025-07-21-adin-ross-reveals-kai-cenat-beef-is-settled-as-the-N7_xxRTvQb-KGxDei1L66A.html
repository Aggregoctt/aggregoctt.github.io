Adin Ross revealed that he and Kai Cenat have settled their beef, stating that there is “no bad blood” between the two streaming stars. 

Prior to Adin Ross’ ban from Twitch, he and Kai Ceant had streamed together a few times. The pair regularly showed love to each other on stream and bigged each other up to their own fans. 

When Adin got banned from Twitch, that became harder. Kai vowed to help get his friend unbanned, but when he did, it seemed like there was a whole load of beef between them. Ross shaded streamers who ‘abandoned’ him amid the ban, with many claiming that involved Kai. 

The AMP star had not mentioned Adin on stream for quite some time, which only added to those claims. And even though Adin stated they didn’t have any beef, he would seemingly shade Kai at times. 

Adin Ross lifts lid on call with Kai Cenat over beef

It boiled over during Kai’s July 18 stream, as he addressed a few things, including beef with xQc, and his relationship with Adin. In that, the AMP star called out Adin’s manager, Taav, but claimed that the streamer is ultimately his “brother” and they were all good. 

The Kick co-owner also addressed the situation in his July 19 stream, revealing they’d spoken things over. 

“Me and Kai spoke on the phone. Kai and I had a great conversation, just in general about a lot of stuff. It’s not Adin vs Kai, we don’t want that,” he said. “There’s no bad blood between us. It’s a very mutual respect that him and I have for one another. That’s where it stands.”

The Kick star stated that’s the way all drama should be handled, over the phone instead of on-stream, but didn’t add anything further. 

Whether or not the pair will, finally, stream together remains to be seen, though.
