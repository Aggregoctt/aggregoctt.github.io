Sony’s State of Play returns on June, 2025, with a fresh livestream event packed with updates on the hottest games coming to PS5.

With highly anticipated games like Death Stranding 2: On the Beach and Ghost of Yotei likely taking center stage, along with exciting indie and third-party announcements, PlayStation fans have plenty to look forward to.

So, whether you want to catch the full 40+ minute show live or watch highlights later, here’s everything you need to know to tune in.

PlayStation June Showcase time & schedule

The PlayStation Showcase (State of Play) will stream live on June 4 at 2 PM PT / 5 PM ET / 11 PM CEST (June 5, 6 AM JST) on both YouTube and Twitch.

Here’s a handy timezone converter if you want to check what time the event starts wherever you are in the world.

When the time comes, visit the official PlayStation YouTube channel or Twitch channel to watch the event live. The broadcast will be in English and last around 40 minutes.

If you miss the livestream, VODs may be available, but note that licensing restrictions could limit re-streams or archived videos, especially regarding licensed music content.

Many creators and co-streamers will also be watching live, so checking your favorite gaming influencers’ channels might be a great way to experience the event with commentary.

What to expect at the PlayStation Showcase

Sony’s event is expected to highlight:

  • Death Stranding 2: On the Beach: new trailers, gameplay, and launch details for the June 26 release.
  • Ghost of Yotei: major first-party exclusive updates and possible release info.
  • Potential surprise reveals, such as Marvel’s Wolverine or new PlayStation Studios projects.
  • More info about Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet, Neil Druckmann’s upcoming video game project after The Last of Us.
  • Updates on PlayStation Plus and platform services.
