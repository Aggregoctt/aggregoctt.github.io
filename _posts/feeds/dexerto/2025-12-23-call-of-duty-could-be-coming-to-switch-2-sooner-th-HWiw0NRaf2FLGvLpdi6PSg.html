Call of Duty is coming to the Nintendo Switch, after being announced for the platform back in 2023, and it’s coming much sooner than you think.

CoD isn’t exactly a small or forgiving game for a portable platform, which is why it’s been released on Xbox, PlayStation, and PC for so many years, with a few smaller releases on mobile for those wanting to dive into Warzone.

Well, now the Nintendo Switch 2 is here, bringing higher specs and hardware than can handle the fast-paced action Call of Duty brings. However, there’s never been a release date for the game’s arrival on the Switch 2. That is, until it was revealed to be coming very soon.

Call of Duty coming to Switch 2 “in a few months”

According to Jez Corden, the executive editor at Windows Central, “The first CoD Switch version is nearly done and launching in a few months.”

This was revealed after fans began wondering where the promised port was, to which Corden explained that they “Need dev kits to port. They didn’t have them.”

Thankfully, it seems they’ve resolved their issue and are nearly complete with the Switch 2 version of Call of Duty.

Of course, no release date has been confirmed by Activision or Nintendo, but it certainly looks like we’ll be getting a portable CoD very soon in 2026.
