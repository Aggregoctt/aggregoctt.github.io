The Call of Duty League 2025 season wraps up with the Championship Weekend hosted by Toronto Ultra, so here’s everything you need to know, including the stream, schedule, and teams.

Black Ops 6’s 2025 season has been a two-horse race between Atlanta FaZe and LA Thieves for the most part, with each winning two of the four majors.

For the first time, Atlanta FaZe aren’t the top seed going into Champs and are instead second behind LA Thieves, who are coming off wins at Majors 3 and 4.

Still, any team could take it; despite a shaky 2025 season, OpTic Texas are looking to make history by going back-to-back after their dominant win in 2024’s event, and we can’t really count any of the other teams out.

CDL Champs 2025 runs from Thursday, June 26, until Sunday, June 29, and will be streamed on Call of Duty’s YouTube channel. The stream isn’t live yet, but we’ll embed it here when it is.

Matches will kick off at 12 PM PT / 3 PM ET / 8 PM BST each day, with a bracket system whittling down the teams until Championship Sunday, in which the final two rosters play for the ring.

While the main broadcast will be on Call of Duty’s YouTube channel, you can also tune in to watch parties from creators such as The Flank or Scump to hear your favorite personalities’ analysis and see their live reactions.
