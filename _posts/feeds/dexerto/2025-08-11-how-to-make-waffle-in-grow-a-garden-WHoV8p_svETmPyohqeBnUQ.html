Grow a Garden’s cooking event focuses on preparing various dishes to earn special rewards, including a breakfast staple: the waffle. However, you need to ensure you have the necessary ingredients to make one.

While the popular Roblox game is all about growing veggies, fruit, and limited-edition plants, Grow a Garden hosts weekly events that allow players to complete new challenges for special rewards.

The latest event focuses on cooking, with Chef Chris P Bacon sitting in the middle of the server asking you to create his current craving. If you’re playing the game and Chris asked for a waffle, here’s everything you need to know about making it.

All Grow a Garden Waffle recipes

There are five different Waffle recipes we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden, each offering a different rarity. Thankfully, you can make each one with different ingredients that guarantee the rarity.

Each rarity will take six minutes to cook with regular-sized fruit, but it will take longer if you use bigger ingredients.

How to make a Waffle

Once you have all the ingredients listed above, you can make your Waffle by following these steps:

  • Go to Chris P in the middle of the server.
  • Head over to the cooking pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them inside the pot.
  • Press the green Cook button.
  • Wait the allotted time and grab your dish.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and satisfied Chris’s craving, he’ll offer you a reward that changes depending on which rarity of the dish you created. You can check out all of the available rewards in our guide.
