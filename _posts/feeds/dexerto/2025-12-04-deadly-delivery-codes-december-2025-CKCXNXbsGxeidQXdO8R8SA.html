How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in Deadly Delivery is pretty straightforward, but before you can redeem them, you’ll need to complete the game’s tutorial, which only takes a few minutes. Once you’ve done that, follow the steps below and you’ll be able to claim rewards from any code:

  • Click on the cogwheel icon at the top-left corner of your screen, as shown in the image below:
  • A new window will pop up. At the bottom, you’ll see a rectangular box where you need to enter the code.
  • Click on ‘Redeem’ to claim the corresponding reward.

After redeeming a code, the rewards will go directly to your active inventory, unless your inventory is already full. In this case, the rewards will be sent to your backpack instead.
