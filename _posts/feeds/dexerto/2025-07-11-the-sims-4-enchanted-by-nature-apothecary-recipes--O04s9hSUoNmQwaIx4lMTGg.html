Sims 4’s Enchanted by Nature expansion brings players a brand new skill to master, Apothecary. Using this, you can craft potions, cure ailments, and forage for ingredients, but you’ll need to know the recipes to do it.

There’s so much to do in Sims 4’s Enchanted by Nature expansion, from becoming a Fairy Sim to exploring all the new features, and of course, trying out the brand new skills. Apothecary will have you crafting natural potions, cures, gases, and drinks to make you wet yourself.

But before you start drinking potions that’ll make your Sim feel pretty unwell, you’ll need to know exactly what you can make, how to get the ingredients, and of course, how to level up your Apothecary skill. So, here’s all you need to know.

How to get Apothecary ingredients

If you’re looking to create a potion but don’t have the ingredients, then you’ll want to set your Sims off to forage.

Explore the world around you and click on the floor. Then, select “Forage here” or “Forage for Apothecary Ingredients”, the latter of which you’ll need to be level 2 in the skill to be able to do. Doing so will let you grab some of the more common ingredients. It’s worth noting that a few are only found in specific locations, so we recommend you keep exploring and Foraging.

If you don’t fancy exploring, you can always head to your Apothecary Table and purchase the ingredients you need instead — though that’ll cost you a few Simoleons.

How to build your Apothecary skill

If you’re looking to increase your Apothecary skill fast, there are a few easy ways to level up, and they don’t break the bank.

Create cures and elixirs

Perhaps one of the most rewarding ways to level up your Apothecary skill is to create cures, potions, and elixirs.

Just like it is learning to cook or play guitar in Sims 4, you need to practice in order to level up. Head over to your Apothecary Table, craft the level one potions, and keep going until you’re at the level you’re looking for. Plus, you’ll get some useful potions as a reward.

Use the computer

While it’s not as quick, researching Apothecary at your Sims’ computer is an easy way to increase your Apothecary skill.

Head over to your nearest PC, research the Apothecary skill, and you’ll see it going up. If you’re patient, this will level you up as much as you need.

Read skill books

Alternatively, if your Sim prefers paperback research, reading Apothecary skill books is another surefire way to increase your skill from the comfort of your own home.

Purchase the book from your computer and let your Sim give it a read, just like Vampirism, you’ll learn tons about the new way of life.

Mentor other Sims

Once you reach level five, you’ll be able to mentor other Sims in the Apothecary. Again, this isn’t the quickest, but it’s a great way to both increase your Sims’ Apothecary level and add the skill to another family member or friend.
