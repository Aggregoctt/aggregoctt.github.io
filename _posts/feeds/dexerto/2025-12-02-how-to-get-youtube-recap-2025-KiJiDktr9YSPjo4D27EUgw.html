YouTube has launched its first-ever end-of-the-year recap to show off what videos you watched throughout the year. Here’s how to get it.

Every December, platforms like Spotify and Twitch begin to roll out features that give users a full recap of the content they consumed over the past year.

YouTube offered one for Music and Gaming content back in 2024, but has expanded that to the whole platform for 2025.

Here’s how to get your YouTube recap for 2025, and what songs and creators topped the site for the last year.

How to get 2025 YouTube recap

YouTube recap launches on December 2, 2025, and all you have to do is go to the website. Once you are logged in, your recap will be available to view.

Marques Brownlee shared screenshots of what the recap looks like on his X account, revealing that it shows your top interests as well as a list of creators you watched throughout the year.
