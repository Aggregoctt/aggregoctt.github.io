The Warzone Season 1 Reloaded update introduced win streak rewards, allowing you to earn rare camos for winning games back-to-back.

What’s the only thing better than winning a match of Warzone and being the last team standing? Winning another one, of course. Although for many players coming out on top in a single match is a monumental feat, the best want to do it time and time again.

Previously, the reward for earning another victory was nothing more than pride. But after the mid-season update, there are now exclusive rewards to hunt down.

Here’s a breakdown of all the Warzone win streak rewards and how you can unlock them.

Win streak challenges & rewards

Battle royale

Resurgence

It’s worth keeping in mind that Casual modes don’t count towards win streaks, so you’ll need to earn these camos by playing regular Battle Royale and Resurgence without bots.

These challenges might seem tricky for the average player, but the devs explained in the Season 1 Reloaded patch notes that this was an intentional decision.

“We recognize that these win streak rewards may feel out of reach for many players, but we’re excited to offer a meaningful challenge and exclusive recognition for those who put in the time, coordination, and effort to reach these levels of performance,” they said.

Has anyone unlocked them?

Just a few hours after the update went live, Raven Software posted that streamers JoeWoe, HusKerrs, Twitty, and Bbreadman had already earned the Neon Pulse camo by winning 10 BR matches in a row. They beat another team by around 10 minutes.

But as far as we know, the other three camos haven’t been unlocked yet. So, if you lock in, you could be the first to equip them.
