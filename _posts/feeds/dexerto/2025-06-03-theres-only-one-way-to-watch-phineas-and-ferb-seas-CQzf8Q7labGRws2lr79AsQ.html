Stick with Disney this week, because Phineas and Ferb are gonna do it all (again) with Season 5, the highly anticipated revival of the classic show. And, if you’re lucky, there’s a way to watch it for free.

It’s been nearly 10 years since Phineas and Ferb’s 104-day summer vacation came to an end. The chaotic, ever-inventing pair returned in 2020 for Disney Plus’ Candace Against the Universe, but soon, they’ll make the comeback we’ve all been waiting for.

This isn’t a small revival, either. Disney has given the green light to a whopping 40 episodes over two seasons, and Ashley Tisdale is even reprising her role as Candace. Everyone’s back, including Perry the Platypus and Dr. Heinz Doofenshmirtz.

Whether you’re a grown-up fan or a parent looking for something your kids can watch this week, Phineas and Ferb is a must-watch – and you may be able to watch it without spending a dime.

How to watch Phineas and Ferb for free

If you want to watch Phineas and Ferb Season 5 for free, you’ll need an eligible Unlimited plan with Verizon.

As its website explains, “for a limited time, if you have an eligible Unlimited plan, you can get Disney+ Premium (No Ads), the on-demand streaming service, included for six months.”

That means you need to have one of these plans:

  • 5G Do More
  • 5G Start
  • 5G Get More
  • 5G Play More
  • Welcome Unlimited
  • One Unlimited for iPhone
  • Get More Unlimited
  • Do More Unlimited
  • Play More Unlimited
  • Verizon Plan Unlimited
  • Go Unlimited
  • Beyond Unlimited
  • Above Unlimited

However, there’s an extra condition: the Disney Plus perk is no longer available to new customers, so you need to be an existing subscriber to one of these plans (and you can’t have already redeemed your six-month trial). “Plans that are eligible for this offer are no longer available to add to your account,” the website says.

You have until November 30, 2025, at midnight to enroll in the plan if you fit this criteria. You can check Verizon’s website for more information.

When is the new Phineas and Ferb out?

Phineas and Ferb Season 5 premieres on the Disney Channel on Thursday, June 5, at 5pm PT/8pm PT.

New episodes will be available to stream on Disney Plus from 12am the next day.
