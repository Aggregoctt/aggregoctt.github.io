The Battlefield 6 Open Beta has already peaked at nearly 10,000 players on Steam, even though no one can actually play it yet.

So far, it’s safe to say that Battlefield 6 is making all the right moves. The gameplay footage shown off has fans convinced it could be a return to form for the series, and the devs have already committed to realistic skins without goofy crossovers.

The Open Beta is set to be the first opportunity for many players to get hands-on time with the shooter. Interest is clearly high too, as it’s already got thousands of players just sitting there waiting for it to start.

Thousands of players are ready & waiting for BF6 beta to open

According to SteamDB, the BF6 Open Beta has already peaked at 9,463 players on Steam, after preloads went live on August 4. This is extra impressive, given that the servers don’t actually go live until August 7 for early access players, or August 9 for the rest of the world.

In fact, it has just over 3,700 people playing at the time of writing, meaning they have the app open but aren’t playing games. These figures only take Steam into account as well, meaning there are likely even more across other PC platforms and on consoles.

But what exactly are all these players doing? Well, although matches are currently offline, you can still jump into the app and customize your settings, so players are likely making sure that their gameplay, mouse and keyboard, and audio settings are up to scratch before the first game.

Aside from that, it appears that many eager fans are just ready and waiting for August 7 to roll around so they can test out BF6 the second the beta opens.
