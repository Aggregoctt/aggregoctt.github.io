Asmongold was left in awe after a viewer perfectly recreated his notoriously messy room in World of Warcraft down to the last roach.

When it comes to streaming rooms, Asmongold’s may be the most famous of all, for all the wrong reasons.

It’s been a meme in streaming culture for ages because of how much of a mess it is, with fast-food boxes piled up, empty soda cans all over, bugs, and even dead animals in the walls.

In fact, the last time he cleaned it, it took him a whopping 3 hours, and he was exhausted afterwards.

On January 24, a viewer recreated his room in WoW and days later, the streamer reacted to it during a live broadcast, and was surprisingly impressed at how accurate it was.

Asmongold blown away after fan makes his room in World of Warcraft

The recreation was uploaded to YouTube by ‘Zanziabar,’ who said he intended for the build to be funny.

“In this video I showcase my funny build of ‘The Iconic Streamer Room’, specifically Asmongold’s room from many years ago,” they said. “I had a lot of fun making the build and video to be as goofy (but recognizable!) as possible.”

The room included a dead rat on the floor, roaches on the walls, his gaming chair, flags, and food that had gone bad.

Asmon thought it was spot on. “I think it’s pretty good. I’m gonna be honest, I think this is pretty damn good. Holy sh*t!”

The streamer honed in on the roaches and his desk, which was crowded with food.

“Oh, is that old Wendy’s chilli? That’s gotta be old Wendy’s chill right there!” he exclaimed. “Honestly man, it’s about right.”

While recreating Asmon’s room in WoW was impressive, it’s not the first time fans have wanted it added to a video game.
