American Dad skins have been leaked for Call of Duty: Black Ops 6, and yes, you will be able to play as Stan Smith. 

Call of Duty’s crossovers with pop culture have split the fanbase, to say the least. Some fans are happy to have their favorite celebrities and characters in-game, while others want things to be a little more realistic. 

With the start of Season 4 Reloaded, a crossover with Beavis and Butt-Head was launched, giving fans the chance to play as the iconic ’90s television characters. However, it hasn’t taken leakers long to find the next one. 

If you’ve ever wanted to run around Nuketown as Stan or Roger from American Dad, well, you’re in luck.

American Dad skins coming to Black Ops 6

That’s right, a new crossover between Black Ops 6 and American Dad has been leaked by RealityUK_, with multiple skins for Stan and Roger appearing on social media. 

Just like the Beavis and Butt-Head skins, these cosmetics are cell-shaded to maintain the look from the cartoon. There is no attempt at making Roger the Alien a real person. 

While there is just the one skin for Stan right now – as well as a handful of weapon blueprints and other cosmetics – Roger will have at least three in-game skins, including a look based on his Jeannie Gold persona. 

CODToons event leaked for Season 4 Reloaded

The Stan pack is will cost you 2400 CoD points, while the Roger set is a little more expensive at 2800 CoD points. 

They will be a part of the CODToons event that is set to come later in Season 4 Reloaded. Unlike other events, you won’t be required to collect coins or tokens to unlock the new items, but rather complete specific challenges. 

There is also no date mentioned for when it will start, but leakers have confirmed it will be part of the current season.

So, keep an eye out for more.
