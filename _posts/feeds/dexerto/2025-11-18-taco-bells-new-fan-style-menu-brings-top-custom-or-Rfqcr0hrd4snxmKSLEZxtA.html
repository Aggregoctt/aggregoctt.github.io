Taco Bell is bringing three fan-made items to restaurants across the country as the chain expands its Fan Style program into a full nationwide menu.

The feature first launched in July as an in-app tool that lets users build, name, and share custom orders. When other fans claim those creations, the original creator earns points in the rewards program.

The new lineup, which comes from more than 40,000 submissions, spotlights three top community picks: Brock’s California Crunchwrap, Sandra’s Cantina Craze, and Kajol’s Burrito Bliss. All three will be available for a limited time starting November 20.

Fan Style picks hit the menu

Brock’s California Crunchwrap remixes the classic with steak, seasoned fries, and guacamole wrapped in a grilled Crunchwrap shell. Kajol’s Burrito Bliss offers a vegetarian option built from the Cheesy Bean and Rice Burrito with Pico de Gallo, Fiesta Strips, and Avocado Ranch, then grilled.

The third item, Sandra’s Cantina Craze, puts a twist on the Cantina Chicken Crispy Taco with Creamy Jalapeño Sauce, lettuce, tomatoes,s and Reduced-Fat Sour Cream.

Taco Bell says the Fan Style Menu is designed to highlight the community’s most popular builds as the brand continues to integrate in-app customization into its broader menu strategy.
