Nick Nayersina gifted rapper GloRilla a Chanel “love bag” for her birthday in a new YouTube video, almost bringing the star to tears.

The YouTuber, who first built a following through high‑energy vlogs and celebrity collaborations after working with the Nelk Boys, was invited to GloRilla’s birthday performance in Memphis by her team.

Heading to the party, he explained, “My mom always said, ‘Never show up to someone’s birthday without a gift.’”

So, Nick revealed the present was a Chanel purse, telling her, “I feel like this might be the best way to start this relationship out. I hope I can see you again. Got something special for you.”

GloRilla reacted with surprise, saying, “Oh my god. Thank you. I appreciate it. Oh, I love this.

“There’s a heart on there. This is made with love,” Nick explained.

Nayersina told her the bag originated in Paris and joked that it meant she had to take a trip to Paris, to which GloRilla replied, “Okay. I’m going to take a trip to Paris.”

The moment came just before she took the stage, with Nayersina later telling viewers, “That was a moment. I think there’s going to be a part two.”
