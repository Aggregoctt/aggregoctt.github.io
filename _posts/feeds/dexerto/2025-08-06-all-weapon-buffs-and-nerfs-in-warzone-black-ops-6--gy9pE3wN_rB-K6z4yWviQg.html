Activision

The Black Ops 6 and Warzone Season 5 update brings plenty of weapon buffs and nerfs that are set to shake up the meta across both multiplayer and battle royale.

Although we’re heading towards Black Ops 7, there’s still plenty of life left in the BO6 era. Season 5 is yet another big content drop, with a host of new weapons to try out, a 90s Action Hero event confirmed, and the long-awaited opening of the Verdansk Stadium roof.

On top of all that, it also mixes things up with some weapon balancing changes to keep the meta fresh. So, if you want to know which guns are going to dominate and which have been knocked down a peg or two, here are all the buffs and nerfs in the update.
