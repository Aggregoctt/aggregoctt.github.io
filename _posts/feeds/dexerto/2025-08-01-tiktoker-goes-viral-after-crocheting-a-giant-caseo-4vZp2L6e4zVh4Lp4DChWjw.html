TikToker TheCrochetGinger created a smaller-than-life mini CaseOh. It took her 5 days to make and weighs almost 5 pounds, pretty heavy for a crocheted plushie.

After several hours of work, she was able to come up with a design that bears a striking resemblance to CaseOh despite using some pretty simple materials.

Though there are some spots where may have included arguably a bit too much detail, people are in love with mini CaseOh nonetheless.

And, so that people who are passionate about crocheting can make their own, she’s even put the CaseOh plushie specs up for sale if people want to buy them and make their own.

TheCrochetGinger goes viral for making mini giant CaseOh

While CaseOh’s got a long-running joke about banning anyone who jokes about his size, this crocheting TikToker may just get a pass.

She created the ultimate CaseOh plushie, one that’s pretty accurate to his appearance when put side-by-side with his face.

And yes, when turned around, he does have some high-fidelity detail on his backside. She showed off the full CaseOh in her full TikTok detailing what it took to make the jumbo version of him come to life.

It’s the last part of a full, 5 episode series she made detailing the construction of the jumbo CaseOh. and she also thanked some of her fans who tested her design and helped her build the spec so fans could have their own.

Additionally, she’ll be sending the regular size CaseOh to the man himself.
