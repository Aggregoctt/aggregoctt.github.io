Fortnite’s next big crossover isn’t another galaxy far, far away – it’s down Main Street, U.S.A.

The Disneyland x Fortnite event, launching November 6, 2025, marks the first time Disney has turned its real-world theme park into a playable island. It’s called Disneyland Game Rush, and it’s bringing fireworks, nostalgia, and a few golden Mickey ears to Epic’s battle royale.

Disney’s first theme park collaboration in Fortnite

The crossover arrives under Disney’s 1.5 billion dollar investment in Epic Games, first announced in February 2024. It follows the Star Wars Galactic Battle island and the recent Simpsons mini-season, only this time, players are stepping into the happiest place on Earth.

Using the code 4617-4819-8826, players can explore a stylized Disneyland Park and California Adventure, complete with Sleeping Beauty’s Castle, the Pixar Pier Ferris wheel, and a 70th-anniversary statue at the center. The park acts as a hub for seven mini-games, each based on a Disneyland attraction.

All Disneyland Game Rush mini-games

  • Star Wars: Stormtrooper Showdown – battle First Order troops at Galaxy’s Edge
  • WEB-Slingers: Spider-Bot Blasters – fend off runaway spider-bots
  • Space Mountain: Rocket Race – race through a cosmic obstacle course
  • Guardians of the Galaxy: Mission Sneak Out! – break out of The Collector’s fortress
  • Haunted Mansion: Scavenger Haunt – solve eerie puzzles through ghostly halls
  • Indiana Jones: Tomb Runner – escape traps inside ancient ruins
  • Matterhorn: Slip and Climb – race to the peak through icy chaos

You can trade earned tokens for cosmetics like Mickey Ears, a Spider-Bot Hat, or an R2-D2 Head, with golden anniversary versions hidden behind collectible park keys.
