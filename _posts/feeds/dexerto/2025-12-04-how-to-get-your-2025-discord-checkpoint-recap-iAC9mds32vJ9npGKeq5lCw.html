Discord has begun rolling out its first-ever recap feature for 2025, taking notes from the likes of Spotify and YouTube to offer fans an insight into what they did throughout the year.

As the year comes to a close, entertainment platforms around the internet begin to roll out features that give users a full recap of how they used it over the previous year.

Spotify Wrapped was one of the first, but now YouTube, Twitch, and even Discord offer the chance to check out your stats.

How to get 2025 Discord Recap

Discord Checkpoint began rolling out to users on Thursday, December 4, but the company says it’ll take a few days to become available to everyone.

Here’s how to get your Discord Checkpoint on mobile and desktop:

  • Make sure your app is fully up to date
  • On Desktop, look for the icon at the top right of the screen
  • On Mobile, tap on your profile picture on the bottom right and then tap the checkpoint banner

If you don’t see a Checkpoint, you may not have had enough activity on Discord to summarize your 2025. Or, you have toggled off “Use data to personalize my Discord experience.”
