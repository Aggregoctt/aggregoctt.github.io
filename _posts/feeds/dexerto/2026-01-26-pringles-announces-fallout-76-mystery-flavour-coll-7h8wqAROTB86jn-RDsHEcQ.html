Pringles has announced a new collaboration with Xbox and Bethesda Softworks to produce a limited-edition Fallout 76 Mystery Flavour.

A press release announcing the crossover confirmed that the new Mystery Flavor, which customers can try and guess for the chance to win a prize, is part of a wider collaboration between the three parties.

Tubes of chips adorned with Fallout 76 branding are available in the UK supermarket chain Morrisons from January 28, with a wider rollout to follow. The promotion is currently available only in the UK and EU.

What we know about the Pringles Fallout 76 Mystery Flavour

Fans who believe they have identified the flavour can submit their guess online between January 6 and May 7, 2026, by scanning a QR code on each tube’s packaging for a chance to win a five-night trip for two to California.

Other prizes include codes to unlock exclusive in-game rewards for other games, including World of Warcraft: Midnight, Sea of Thieves, and The Outer Worlds 2, although the rewards themselves aren’t specified.

These codes can be found in specially marked tubes of existing Pringles flavors with “limited-edition Xbox-themed packaging.”

Pringles says the Mystery Flavour concept is designed to encourage discussion among fans and spark debate, with the guessing window remaining open for five months.
