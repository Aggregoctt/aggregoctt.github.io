How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in Rogue Piece is straightforward and only takes a moment once you know where the menu is located.

  • Click on the cogwheel (settings) icon on the left side of the screen.
  • Enter the code in the ‘…’ field in the new window that appears.
  • Click ‘Enter’ to claim the corresponding reward for the code.

What are Rogue Piece codes used for?

Rogue Piece codes are primarily used to unlock rerolls that support progression, allowing you to refine their build or adjust their character path without spending in-game resources. These include things like race rerolls, fruit rerolls, and stat resets.

Apart from reroll shards, you will usually get Gems from codes, which is the in-game currency. Whatever purchases or upgrades you make, you’ll need to spend Gems.
