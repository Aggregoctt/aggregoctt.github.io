A tech-oriented Chinese YouTuber has built an all-in-one gaming console, merging together a PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, and an Xbox Series X under one roof.

While they’re fewer and farther between these days, console-exclusive games indeed still exist. You can’t just boot up your Xbox and start playing The Last of Us, for instance, nor will Mario Kart show up on the PlayStation store.

To play absolutely everything, you need to own absolutely everything, but even then, it can be a bit of a headache. To alleviate the issue, a Chinese tech YouTuber who goes by 小宁子 XNZ on the platform, built their own all-in-one device.

In the one ‘console,’ she’s crammed in the technology of the leading gaming hardware brands, meaning she can seamlessly swap from PS5 to Xbox Series X, then to Switch 2, on a dime.

PS5, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox Series X merged into a single console

The YouTuber revealed their creation back in a November, 2025 video that’s only now making the rounds in the Western world.

Growing tired of swapping inputs, or worse yet, having to stand up and change some wiring around to get a different console working, they engineered a unique solution. They called it the “Ningtendo PXBOX 5.”

In short, the YouTuber stripped all three consoles down to their basic parts while discarding any cooling systems and power supplies, as they’d be running on central components. For example, a single fan at the bottom of the new device cools all three platforms at once.

A 250W power supply proved to be enough to do the job, so long as only one game is ever running at a time. And the only major quirk came when figuring out how to implement the Switch 2’s dock, which required a custom 3D printed case for the motherboard, allowing for handheld functionality at any given time without ruining the build.

The magic touch – a button was added at the top of the triangular build. When pressed, this button changes between the three consoles in a rapid process that only takes about three seconds.

Clear examples in the video show the YouTuber jumping from Donkey Kong Bananza on the Nintendo Switch 2 over to Ghost of Yotei on the PS5 in mere moments.

The only real caveat is that both the PS5 and Xbox Series X are relegated to digital-only devices, as there’s simply no room in this one-off build for any disc drives.

But that’s certainly not a hindrance to this YouTuber who can now play any game across any platform in just a few button presses. Console exclusivity be damned.
