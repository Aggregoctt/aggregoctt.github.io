McDonald’s has quietly launched a new limited-time Secret Menu in the UK, featuring items that are already catching attention online, including a Chicken Cheeseburger that is not available in the US.

The Secret Menu lineup appears to remix existing McDonald’s ingredients into new combinations, with several items confirmed through official promotional imagery shared by the brand. Availability is limited to McDonald’s UK locations.

What’s on McDonald’s UK Secret Menu

The Chicken Cheeseburger features a breaded chicken patty topped with a slice of cheese and sauce, served on a standard McDonald’s bun. Unlike McDonald’s chicken sandwiches in the US, this item combines the structure of a classic cheeseburger, but with an added chicken patty.

The Apple Pie Mini McFlurry blends McDonald’s soft-serve ice cream with pieces of apple pie mixed throughout. The dessert is served in a small cup, with visible pastry chunks and apple filling.

The Chicken Big Mac replaces the traditional beef patties with breaded chicken while keeping the Big Mac’s layered bun and toppings. The Surf N’ Turf combines a beef patty with a breaded fish filet in a single sandwich.

The Chicken Cheeseburger stands out in particular, as there is currently no equivalent item on the US McDonald’s menu. While US locations regularly test new chicken sandwiches and variations, this specific cheeseburger-style chicken build remains exclusive to the UK Secret Menu rollout.
