Stranger Things Season 5 will bring Netflix‘s flagship series to a close later this year, but one lucky buyer has the chance to hold on to a piece of Hawkins forever – the Wheeler family home is officially up for sale.

The Netflix show has been a cultural phenomenon ever since it first debuted (and made us obsessed with Eggo waffles) in 2016. But the Duffer Brothers always intended for Stranger Things to be a five-season arc, and the main cast is growing up and focusing on new projects.

As they say, all good things must come to an end. Even though we’ll be saying goodbye to the show in 2025, there are plenty of spinoffs to get excited about, including animated series Tales from ’85 and an untitled live-action show. 

Adding to the hype, real-life news has emerged from the Upside Down: a listing for the very house where Mike, Nancy, Holly, and their parents Karen and Ted Wheeler lived throughout the show’s run.

Stranger Things’ Wheeler family home selling for $350,000

The five-bedroom, three-bath home is situated at 2530 Piney Wood Lane in Atlanta, Georgia, and is listed at $350,000 – just under the city’s $374,900 average home price. Plus, as the ad states, you’ll get to “own a piece of television history.” 

“Sitting at the end of the Piney Wood Lane cul-de-sac on two acres, this quintessential ’80s time capsule offers plenty of room to roam, and an incredible restoration opportunity to turn some upside down elements, right side up again,” it says. 

There’s plenty of parking room for your station wagon (or, a Mike Wheeler-style bike), the main living area has retro wood panel walls, there’s cassette-tape listening space thanks to some built-in bookshelves, and plenty of kitchen room for a waffle breakfast. 

The second living space or the famed dining room are two of many spots where you could recreate the Stranger Things gang’s Dungeons & Dragons game nights. On the second floor, there are three bedrooms alongside the main room with its own bath and fireplace. 

The listing concludes, “2530 Piney Wood Lane is looking for its new owner, and whether you plan to restore the retro lore, bring the home into the 2000s, or create an experiential host home, we cannot wait to see what happens next season.”

Only one lucky buyer will get their hands on the Wheeler family home, but here’s hoping it goes to a Stranger Things fan – it’s what Mike would have wanted. 

Stranger Things Season 5 will be released in three parts – Volume 1 on November 26, 2025, Volume 2 on Christmas Day 2025, and Volume 3 on New Year’s Eve 2025.
