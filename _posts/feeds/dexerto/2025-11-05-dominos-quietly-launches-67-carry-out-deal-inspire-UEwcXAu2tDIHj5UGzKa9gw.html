Domino’s customers can get a large 1-topping pizza for $6.70 by using the promo code 67. The offer is available for carry-out orders and has been spotted at various franchise locations, though it hasn’t been promoted nationally by Domino’s corporate.

The deal has been circulated through Facebook posts from local stores and franchise groups, but it has not been confirmed as a nationwide offer. Availability may vary, and customers are encouraged to check with their local stores to see if the code is valid.

The promo is active at least through mid-November, based on franchise posts, with some operators listing an end date of November 16.

The TikTok 6-7 meme connection

The “6-7” meme has been trending on TikTok and other social platforms throughout 2025. The phrase originally came from the 2024 track Doot Doot (6 7) by rapper Skrilla, but went viral after edits began featuring NBA player LaMelo Ball, who stands 6’7”.

The audio then spread rapidly through absurdist memes, soundbites, and random usage across youth culture, becoming a catch-all phrase for humor without a set meaning. That virality has now crossed over into marketing, with some Domino’s customers linking the $6.70 pizza deal to the ongoing “6-7” trend.

Customers must enter the code 67 during online checkout or in the app, select carry-out, and choose one topping to get the discounted price. The code may be limited by location, so participation will differ by franchise.
