Kick streamer ShakoMako was walking down a street in the Philippines when she was suddenly wounded by a stray dog in her path, prompting a visit to the emergency room.

IRL streaming can take many forms, but one of the most popular types of IRL broadcasts are travel streams, where influencers take their viewers along for trips abroad.

ShakoMako is one such travel streamer, who was broadcasting her experiences in the Philippines when an accident occurred.

Mako was speaking with a local while walking down a street at night when a dog suddenly whined loudly, out of view of the camera. After screaming in shock, Mako adjusted the view so chatters could see a medium-sized dog trot away across the street.

“Oh my god, I didn’t see it!” the streamer explained, having seemingly walked into the dog while she was adjusting her camera. “Did he scratch me? …I did not see it. I’m fine, but do you think I need to go to the hospital?”

Mako panned the camera down to show her knee, where she had presumably been bitten or scratched by the animal.

Kick streamer gets rabies shot after stray dog accident

Eventually, Mako decided to protect her health and went to a clinic to receive a rabies shot. She streamed the entire ordeal, where she was very clearly distressed about the giant needle being driven right into the spot where she was bitten.

Luckily, the streamer is otherwise unharmed — but things could have been much worse. Rabies is a significant issue in the Philippines, and the country ranks sixth in the world for rabies cases.

In fact, there was a significant uptick in the amount of rabies cases in the Philippines in 2023, all of which proved fatal. That year, 71% of all rabies cases in the country came from dogs, meaning that Mako was smart to take swift action and get the vaccine.
