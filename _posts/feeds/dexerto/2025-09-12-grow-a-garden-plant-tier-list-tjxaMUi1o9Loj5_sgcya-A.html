Grow a Garden is all about growing the best plants to make the highest profit so you can unlock more seeds, pets, garden items, and more. But you’ll need the best plants to make the most money.

Sure, on the surface, Roblox’s Grow a Garden is a chill farming game where you can grow crops without really doing much. However, it’s so much more than that. It’s filled with techniques that have you growing the right crops for the highest profit.

So, if you want to thrive in Grow a Garden, you’ll need the best plants in the game, and we’ve got a tier list to help you with that right here.

Best plants in Grow a Garden

To find a specific plant, type the crop into the filter section under Plant.

Our tiers explained

  • S: Plant these if you can afford or find the crop.
  • A: A great second option and will give you good returns.
  • B: Good, but not worth prioritizing.
  • C: They’ll work if they’re cheap, but they’re not really worth it.
  • D: Avoid planting these.

As new plants are added to the game, we’ll be adding them to this tier list and explaining any plants that have moved here, so check back soon for more information on every new event or update.

S-Tier plants

There are currently 60 plants sitting in S-Tier, each offering a high profit and proving pretty quick in their growth time. These are the plants you should be planting if you can.

A-Tier plants

There are currently 35 plants sitting in A-Tier, each of which offers a great profit and is quick in its growth time. These are the plants you should be planting if you can’t get hold of S-tier or have extra space.
