The developers of Wuchang: Fallen Feathers have addressed mounting player frustration following the game’s troubled PC launch.

Wuchang: Fallen Feathers launched with major technical issues on PC, prompting a flood of negative feedback from players. The game currently holds a Mostly Negative rating on Steam, with over 37,000 user reviews criticizing poor performance, bugs, and broken pre-order rewards.

In response, developer Leenzee and publisher 505 Games have issued a statement via the game’s official Steam page, acknowledging the complaints and promising fixes.

Wuchang devs promise performance fixes

“We are confident in the game’s performance within our recommended spec, but we’re working on optimization,” the team wrote. “We are looking to issue a patch as soon as we can.”

Players experiencing issues have been encouraged to try adjusting in-game settings using the official FAQ or submit support tickets to help the team identify problems more efficiently.

Alongside performance concerns, the devs also acknowledged a bug in the reward distribution system that prevented some players from receiving the correct pre-order items for both the Standard and Deluxe editions.

As compensation, all players who purchase the game on Steam will receive a free digital copy of the original soundtrack, while refunds will be issued to anyone who has already purchased the soundtrack separately.

The team also announced plans to add a brand-new exclusive costume for Deluxe Edition owners in a future update, though they noted this may take some time to implement.

“We are fully committed to investigating and resolving these issues as quickly as possible,” the developers said. “Our team is prioritizing these problems at the highest level… We sincerely ask for your patience, understanding, and support during this process.”
