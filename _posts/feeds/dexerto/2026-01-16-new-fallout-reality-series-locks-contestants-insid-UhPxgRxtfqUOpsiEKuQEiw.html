Amazon has announced a new reality series based on Fallout, following the success of its live-action adaptation of Bethesda’s iconic franchise.

The unscripted show, titled Fallout Shelter after the mobile game, drops a group of contestants into a Vault-Tec vault, where they will live together and be faced with “escalating challenges, strategic dilemmas, and moral crossroads.” As part of the 10-episode run, the participants will have the chance to win “huge” cash prizes.

In a nod to the games, this includes a series of games that test the S.P.E.C.I.A.L attributes that are a core part of developing your characters: strength, perception, endurance, charisma, intelligence, agility, and luck.

In the announcement post, the creators made it clear that they want to keep as much of the series’ DNA intact, despite this being a completely new spin on the universe.

“The new series will blend large-scale challenges with deeply social, psychological, and narrative-driven gameplay, staying true to the tone, world-building, and choice-driven ethos that have defined Fallout for over 25 years,” it reads.

If you’re a Fallout fanatic and you want to see how you’d fare stuck below the surface, casting is now open.

How to apply to be in Fallout Shelter

  1. Head over to the official Fallout Shelter casting website.
  2. Fill out the application form with your details.
  3. Upload a short video that tells them about you.
  4. Hit ‘Submit.’

Applications close on February 15, 2026, so you’ll have to act fast if you want to apply. Of course, filling out the form doesn’t guarantee your spot, as only a handful of lucky fans will be selected to take part.

There is currently no word on when filming for Fallout Shelter will begin, or when it will finally air on Amazon Prime.
