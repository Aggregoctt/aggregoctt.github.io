Noel Miller has officially closed the book on the Tiny Meat Gang Podcast, announcing in a November 28 upload that the long-running show has come to an end.

The update arrives more than a year after Cody Ko stepped away from TMG Studios following resurfaced allegations from Tana Mongeau that reshaped the network and shifted its future.

Ko exited the network in 2024 after Mongeau alleged on her podcast that the two had “hooked up” when she was 17. The claims resurfaced online months later, leading to Cody Ko losing hundreds of thousands of subscribers.

Noel Miller explains why TMG podcast is ending

In an upload on November 28, Miller revealed that it would be the last episode of the TMG Podcast. He explained that the team kept the show running through 2024 in part because listeners continued to support it, including paid subscribers, and because the studio was under a major advertising contract that had to be completed.

With that contract fulfilled, Miller said the team was now “free” to decide the show’s future. He thanked viewers whose support allowed TMG Studios to maintain staff during a turbulent period after Ko’s exit, noting that the stability helped the network navigate an uncertain chapter.

Miller also acknowledged that the show couldn’t be reinvented following its “fundamental change.” He said the team always understood that the podcast’s identity had shifted and that “there would inevitably come a time that this chapter would have to close.”
