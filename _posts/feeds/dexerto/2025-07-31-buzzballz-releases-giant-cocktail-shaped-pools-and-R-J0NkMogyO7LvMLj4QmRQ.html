BuzzBallz just dropped the ultimate summer flex: a giant, inflatable pool shaped exactly like one of their signature cocktails, and fans snatched it up instantly.

Launched on July 31, the limited-edition pool mimics the round, brightly colored container of the Berry Cherry Limeade BuzzBallz drink, standing over 5 feet tall and 6 feet wide.

The absurdly accurate replica even includes a built-in pull-tab lid and comes with a ladder and a pool alarm for safety, with the accessories shipping separately.

First batch of BuzzBallz pools sell out instantly

BuzzBallz says the idea came straight from fan comments over the years.

“We kept hearing, ‘I want to drink this in a pool,’ or, ‘Let me swim in a BuzzBallz,’” a rep said. “We just thought: why not make the pool the drink?”

While you (unfortunately) won’t be floating in real cocktails, the brand made good on a viral dream, turning a meme-worthy concept into a real product.

The pool retailed for just $20, though it’s reportedly valued at $3,000. Unsurprisingly, it sold out almost immediately.

If you missed it, there’s a planned stock in August. But if you aren’t quick enough, it may be time to start cozying up to someone who managed to snag one.
