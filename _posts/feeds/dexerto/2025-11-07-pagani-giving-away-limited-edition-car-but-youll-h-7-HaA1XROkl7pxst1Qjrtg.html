Super car company Pagani will give you a ‘free’ limited-edition car if you splash out $30 million on their new luxurious apartments in Miami, Florida. 

Anyone who digs deep into their pocket for a really expensive luxury car typically gets a few freebies. This might be an invitation to go on a factory tour, or a free meal, or even a free umbrella. But, it’s a little token of appreciation for you spending such a large amount of money with them.

Well, Pagani are now raising the bar by giving away a ‘free’ car, but you’ll have to spend almost $30 million on a brand-new luxury apartment in Miami first. 

The Italian supercar maker is throwing in the ‘Miami Edition’ of their Utopia Roadster with a purchase of one of their two penthouses in a new residential tower. 

$30m Pagani Residences comes with ‘free’ car

As per Motor1, the Utopia Roadster has been personally designed by Horacio Pagani himself, and is uniquely blue colored throughout. It can also hit a top speed of 217 miles per hour. 

The Miami Edition isn’t the only bonus you’ll get from splashing out, either. Buyers are given a trip to Modena, Italy, for a personal meeting with Horacio. You’ll also be given a private tour of the Horacio Pagani Museo and a private Atelier experience. 

On top of all that, you’ll be able to customize your new apartment to your hearts content. Though, personalization of the Miami Edition Pagani is not mentioned. You likely wouldn’t be able to sell the car, at least for a short while too, given thats how exclusive purchases work. 

If you can’t shoot for the expensive penthouses, the basic apartments in the Pagani Residences start at around $4 million. You won’t get a car with those, but just a luxurious spot to live in. 

The Utopia Roadster starts at around $3 million too, but Pagani are only producing 130 of them worldwide. So, the $30 million penthouse is the best way to skip the queue – unless your surname is, of course, Pagani.
