Fortnite is set to add Playboi Carti to the game soon as the newest Icon Series skin, and here are all the details you need to know about it.

From creators like Clix and Kai Cenat to artists such as Sabrina Carpenter, Lady Gaga, and even Metallica, Fortnite has pulled in some massive real-world collabs over the years. And with a brand-new chapter out, Epic clearly isn’t slowing down.

Next up to join the Icon Series is Playboi Carti, the rapper behind hits like EVIL J0RDAN, Sky, and Magnolia.

Epic just dropped a teaser showing off his upcoming skin, and if you’re thinking about adding Carti to your locker, here’s everything we know so far.

The Playboi Carti skin is set to hit the Item Shop on December 11, 2025. As for the exact time, it’ll most likely be around 7 PM ET when the shop resets.

That’s also the same day the v39.10 update goes live, bringing other exciting additions to the game.

How to get the Playboi Carti skin in Fortnite

The skin will be available in the Item Shop once the next update drops. You’ll be able to get it by heading to the Item Shop tab and purchasing the skin in the Playboi Carti tab once it’s available.

Epic hasn’t shared the full breakdown of the cosmetics yet, but dataminers have already spotted a few things coming with the collab:

The first one is the Playboi Carti Skin, which is expected to cost 1,500 V-Bucks by itself, or 2,500 V-Bucks for the full bundle. Based on the teaser, it looks like it’ll come with more than one style.

Additionally, there’ll also be an “EVIL J0RDAN” Jam Track, priced at 500 V-Bucks.

Like most Icon Series releases, we can probably expect a back bling, pickaxe, emote, or wrap to show up too. We’ll update the details once everything becomes official.
