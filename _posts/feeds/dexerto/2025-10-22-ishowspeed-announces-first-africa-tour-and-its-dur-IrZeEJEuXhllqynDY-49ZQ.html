IShowSpeed has announced plans for a major Africa tour in December, coinciding with the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) tournament.

The streamer revealed the news during his stream, but didn’t specify the exact date the tour would start, only that it would be in December.

As with previous tours, expect Speed to travel across several African countries for live shows, meet-and-greets, and football-related appearances. The tour will mark his first visit to the continent and will overlap with AFCON 2025, which runs from December 21 to January 18 in Morocco.

Speed added that he will be spending New Year in Africa too. The announcement follows his long-running passion for football content, having previously attended major matches across Europe and the United States, and collaborated with players such as Cristiano Ronaldo and Achraf Hakimi.

AFCON 2025 always draws global attention as one of the biggest sporting events of the year, with Speed’s tour potentially aligning with key fixtures and fan celebrations during the tournament.

More details on IShowSpeed’s Africa tour are expected to be revealed in the coming weeks.
