And just like that, The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 Episode 11 is nearly here. If you want to see how Belly, Jeremiah, and Conrad’s love triangle concludes, here’s the finale’s release date, time, and more so you don’t miss a thing. 

The Prime Video series has kept us on tenterhooks ever since the very beginning, with the prevailing question always being: who is Belly’s endgame? Anyone who’s read the books the show is based on may know the answer.

However, author and showrunner Jenny Han has teased there will be some surprises in the TV adaptation’s conclusion, meaning we’re going to have to wait until the finale to see whether Belly chooses Conrad, Jere, or someone else entirely. 

Right now, Belly is pulling an Emily in Paris, but thoughts of the Fisher brothers are never far away. Following the Season 3 Episode 10 cliffhanger ending, you’ll no doubt be itching to find out what’s next. 

The Season 3 finale lands on Prime Video on Wednesday, September 17, 2025, at 12am PT / 3am ET. 

If you’re not based in the US, you can find different time zones below so you know when to tune in: 

  • 4am Brazil
  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Summer Time
  • 12:30pm India Standard Time
  • 5pm Australia
  • 7pm New Zealand

The third chapter of the hit TV series was originally supposed to air much earlier, but due to the Hollywood strikes, it was delayed from 2024 to summer 2025. On the plus side, we’ve been treated to an extra episode. 

How many episodes are there?

The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 consists of 11 episodes, rather than the usual 10. 

Part of the reason was due to the Season 3 time jump, which sees the characters dealing with more adult issues, including family ties, studying, careers, travelling, and marriage. 

When it comes to the finale, Han told The Wrap that it will be a “very satisfying ending … as far as a TV show goes.”

“For me as a storyteller, I have to feel good about what I’m making, and I have to feel proud of it, and I have to be excited by it,” she added. “I just write what I think is the best story, and that’s what I’ll stand by.”

What to expect in the finale

POTENTIAL SPOILERS AHEAD: The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 Episode 10 jumps forward in time, with Belly settled into her new life in France with new boyfriend Benito. But on the way to a conference in Brussels, Conrad decides to make a pitstop in Paris – he’s going to see Belly. 

What happens next is the subject of the finale. Book readers will know that Conrad is Belly’s endgame but, as said, Han has promised there will be surprises. 

In fact, we’ve already had plenty of changes already. The third and final book, We’ll Always Have Summer, ends with Jeremiah calling off the wedding, with an epilogue revealing “a couple years later” Belly is studying in Spain before receiving Conrad’s letters.

Speaking with Entertainment Weekly about the TV adaptation, Han explained, “There is so much that is going to surprise fans about how much we’re showing of Belly in Paris and the whole ending. It’s not just an epilogue.

“I always planned to shoot the end in Paris. I mean, the last book is named after Casablanca, so it always felt right. 

“I’ve been putting in Paris Easter eggs since the very beginning of the show too, like how Belly was reading The Hunger Games in French, so I always was planning this – whether we shot there or not.”

As for the ending, Han previously told Elite Daily, “I always knew how I wanted to end the books. But with the show, I went into it with an open mind. I wanted to approach it with fresh eyes and just see what sort of magic happened on screen.”

Is this a misdirect or will Belly and Conrad end up together? Thankfully, there’s not too long to wait to find out. 

Will Season 3 be the last?

Here’s the deal: The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 is based on the third and final book in the series, and there’s no news on a Season 4 right now. 

At the time of writing, we can expect the flagship series to end here. However, Han has teased she’d “never say never” to continuing the story in the future – either as a sequel series or picking up with Belly and co. years later. 

“Never say never – I never do. If the story is there, then I’m there. As a storyteller, you just get hit with inspiration all the time,” she told Entertainment Weekly. 

“If I had a really great idea that I was excited by, that I wanted to keep telling the story, and if [the stars] were excited to do it, then I would be so pumped. It really depends on if I feel like there is more story to tell.”
