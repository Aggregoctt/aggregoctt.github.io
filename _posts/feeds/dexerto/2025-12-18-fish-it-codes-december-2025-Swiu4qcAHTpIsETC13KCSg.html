How to redeem codes

While the ability to redeem codes is available right from the start, you’ll need to reach level 10 before you can actually claim them. Once you hit that level, you can start claiming rewards from them using the steps below:

  • Click the shopping basket icon at the top of the screen.
  • Enter a working code into the text box at the bottom of the menu that appears.

What are Fish It codes used for?

In Fish It, potions play a big role in shaping your fishing runs, and codes are one of the ways you can get access to things like luck and mutation effects that influence what you catch.
