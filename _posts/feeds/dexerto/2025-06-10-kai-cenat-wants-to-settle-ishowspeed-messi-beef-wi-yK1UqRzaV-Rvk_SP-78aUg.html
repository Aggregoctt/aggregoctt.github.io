Kai Cenat wants to make IShowSpeed meet Lionel Messi after receiving a big invite, despite Speed’s love of Cristiano Ronaldo. 

Over the last few years, as IShowSpeed has risen up in the streaming scene, he has made it clear that he adores one football star above the rest – Cristiano Ronaldo. 

The American streamer has a Ronaldo-themed car, his streaming setup is basically a shrine to the Portuguese star, and he has a whole host of rare CR7 merchandise to boot. That naturally makes him anti-Messi. 

Speed has gotten Ronaldo’s name printed on Argentina shirts, just so he doesn’t have to have Messi on there, and regularly downplays the Inter Miami forward’s accolades. However, Kai Cenat has a cunning plan to try to change that. 

Kai Cenat has chance to meet Lionel Messi 

The AMP star revealed that he has several invites to football-related events, including a chance to attend the Ballon d’Or ceremony in France. He also has the chance to head to Miami for a “behind the scenes” stream with Inter Miami themselves. 

“I don’t watch football. Chat, look, I got invited by the Miami football team that Messi is on. I got invited by them to go to one of their practices or something, behind the scenes,” Kai told his viewers on June 9.

“But, chat, I’m not into football. I just might go to the Miami thing and surprise Speed with Messi. That’s what I should do! Now we’re talking!”

Whenever Kai says he and Speed are going to do something, it usually happens, but getting him face-to-face with Messi would certainly be a new level.

It would give Speed the chance to squash his beef and switch sides from pro-Ronaldo to pro-Messi, but that is unlikely to happen. It would be fun to see either way.
