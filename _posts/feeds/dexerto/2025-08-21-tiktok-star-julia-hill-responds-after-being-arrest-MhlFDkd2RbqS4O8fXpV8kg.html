TikTok star Julia Hill has responded after being arrested for running on the field during a  Los Angeles Angels baseball game.

Running on the field or court of a sports game can lead to some pretty serious consequences. You’ll be banned from the stadium in question and probably every other stadium in that league, as well as being given a trespassing charge by the police.

Everyone still has the intrusive thoughts, though, of what it would be like to experience a few moments on that field. Could you outrun the security guards and get away scott free? Or would you ended up falling over after 10 seconds?

Well, TikToker Julia Hill – Mysteriousjulia to others – decided to let those intrusive thoughts get the better of her as she decided to run on the field during a Los Angeles Angels game. 

TikToker Julia Hill rushed field at MLB game

The TikToker, who has almost 2 million followers on the video platform, was captured by plenty of fans hopping over the wall and down onto the field. 

However, on August 20, she uploaded her own point of view. The video, complete with the ‘nothing beats a Jet2 holiday’ sound, shows he avoiding security for a few moments before eventually being apprehended. It has racked up almost 8 million views.

“Intrusive thoughts got the best of me,” she captioned the video. 

As noted, being arrested for rushing the field can lead to the legal trouble. However, Julia seemed unbothered by that as she posted a video of herself dancing outside a police station.

She had apparently been held for six hours prior to the release. “Good story ig,” Julia also responded to one who asked if the arrest was worth it. 

Some viewers found the whole situation funny, but others questioned why she would risk doing it. “Nothing like being banned for life for 2 minutes of thrill,” one said. 

“Girl, real life consequences. Don’t let people peer pressure you into this stuff. You’re a role model for girls on here,” another added.
