Will Georgia be able to get out of prison after being arrested for murder during her wedding? Ginny and Georgia Season 3 holds the answer, and you can watch it for free.

It’s been two years since we last saw Netflix’s favorite mother-daughter duo on screen, so let’s refresh your memory. Tom has been murdered and we’ve already seen the arrest take place, which happened during Georgia and Paul’s first dance. We also know it’s Austin who holds key information and is yet to tell anyone what he saw the night of Tom’s death.

While this is going on, Gil has been blackmailing Georgia, although she’s neglected to tell Paul about it. As the final moments of Season 2 came to a close, flashbacks showed Gil throwing Georgia to the ground and Georgia threatening Gil with a gun. Gil then wrestles it out of her hands… and that’s all we saw.

Now, Ginny & Georgia Season 3 is back and (hopefully) going to give us some answers. Even better, there’s a way to watch the binge-worthy TV show without paying a dime.

How to watch Ginny & Georgia Season 3 for free

Eligible T-Mobile customers are entitled to a free Netflix subscription, meaning they could stream Ginny & Georgia Season 3 (and anything else on the platform) for free.

There are a few conditions, though. Firstly, this is only on offer to customers in the US, so international fans are out of luck. Secondly, if you are eligible, you can only get access to Netflix with ads.

Thirdly, as per the T-Mobile website, “you need to have two or more lines of any type of Go5G or Magenta plan, or you need to have at least one line of Go5G Next, Go5G Plus or Magenta MAX plan to be eligible for the Netflix ON US.”

“Discounts may be applied if you’d like to upgrade to another Netflix subscription, such as Netflix Standard or Netflix Premium,” it adds.

If this all applies to you, log into your T-Mobile account and simply add it to your add-ons. If you don’t already have a Netflix account, “you’ll be prompted to create a Netflix account if you do not have one. This links to your T-Mobile account. T-Mobile will then pay your Netflix Standard with ads subscription.”

Sadly, Netflix doesn’t offer free trials, so this is your only option.
