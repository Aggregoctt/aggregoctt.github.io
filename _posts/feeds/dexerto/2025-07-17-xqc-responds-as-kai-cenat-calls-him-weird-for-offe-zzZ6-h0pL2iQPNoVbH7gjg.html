Twitch star Kai Cenat called xQc “weird” for trying to help him out during his stream, as the pair continue to not see eye to eye on a few things. 

Over the last few months, xQc and Kai Cenat haven’t agreed with each other on a few things. The Canadian streamer claimed that Kai’s friends were viewbotting their way to success back in April, which the AMP star didn’t appreciate, calling xQc “weird” for it. 

xQc also suggested that Kai had treated Adin Ross ‘awfully’ with their friendship seemingly coming to an end. “That’s just awful, man. Adin, don’t know how you deal with that s*it, I’m not gonna lie. I wouldn’t be able to deal with that,” he said after some apparent shade from Kai. 

The Canadian has admitted he ‘doesn’t care’ if Kai likes him or not, but made an attempt to help him during his July 16 stream.

Kai Cenat calls out xQc over stream advice

Kai, who is in the middle of his AMP Summer marathon, had some tech issues early on in his stream. xQc, who was watching along, tried to alert Kai to a stream add-on “bugging” out. 

“Why this typing in my chat, boy?” Kai said after seeing the message, claiming that xQc was on some “weird s*it” with the comment. “I’m not on that today. I’m on that today, keep all energy over there, bro. Please, bro! You a funny individual.

“I’m telling y’all, bro, be knowing what they doing, and been trying to be, you know?”

xQc responded, noting that “wasn’t the energy at all,” but had “no problem” with Kai’s response. 

“I went to Kai’s chat, and then he started lagging, and I’ve been having some lag. So, I try to give him advice on the lag so that the IRL toolkit is fixed,” he said. “I think that was fine. I don’t take it back. That was fine. No problem, though. It’s whatever.”

xQc also disagreed with fans suggesting that he should send gifted subs to Kai in order to get his attention. 

Reacting to each other is probably the closest that fans will get to a collaboration, seeing as xQc isn’t bothered by that idea. 

“Bro, I’ve never cared for collab s*it. I don’t mind it, but I don’t need it!” he said on July 14.
