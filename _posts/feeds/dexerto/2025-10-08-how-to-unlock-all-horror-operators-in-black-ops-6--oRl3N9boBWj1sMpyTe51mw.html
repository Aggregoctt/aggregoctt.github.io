The Haunting returns in Black Ops 6 and Warzone Season 6, bringing another bunch of terrifying crossovers to both games.

This annual event has become a fan favorite ever since it was introduced back in Black Ops Cold War, as it always brings plenty of iconic Halloween characters to the series, with past versions bringing the likes of Ghostface and Art the Clown.

2025 is no different, with long-requested characters Chucky, Jason Voorhees, and even Predator making their way to Call of Duty. So, here’s how to unlock each one.

How to unlock Predator in BO6 & Warzone

Predator is available by simply buying the Season 6 Battle Pass, which will set you back 1,100 CoD Points. The legendary hunter’s “Jungle” look from the original 1987 movie starring Arnold Schwarzenegger is an instant unlock, so you don’t even need to play a match to unlock it.

However, if you manage to complete the entire Battle Pass over the course of Season 6, you’ll be rewarded with the Feral Predator skin from the 2022 movie Prey.

How to unlock Chucky in Warzone & BO6

The murderous doll Chucky and his bride Tiffany are also coming during The Haunting, but they’re part of the special Chucky event. There’s currently no release date, but once it goes live, you’ll be able to unlock a ton of themed rewards.

There will be plenty of free items to unlock, but both Chucky and Tiffany will be part of the Premium Track, which you’ll only have access to if you buy the Event Pass. In the past, these have cost around 1,100 CoD Points, but we’ll have to wait and see if the same is true this year.

How to unlock Jason Voorhees in Black Ops 6 & Warzone

Iconic slasher Jason Voorhees from Friday the 13th will be part of a store bundle, which will set you back 2,400 CoD Points. Again, there is currently no release date, but the devs have said it will arrive “in-season.”

The bundle contains two different looks for Jason, as well as a host of other cosmetics based on one of the most infamous characters in horror movie history. Here’s what’s included:

  • Jason (Operator skin)
  • Tactical Jason (Operator skin)
  • Jason’s Gun (ASG-89 Shotgun)
  • Jason’s Machete (Cleaver Blueprint)
  • Lucky 13 (KSV Blueprint)
  • Jason’s Revenge (Finishing Move)
  • Head Games (Emote)
  • Jason 13 Mask (Weapon Charm)
  • Out for Blood (Spray)
  • Jason at Every Turn (Emblem)
  • Crystal Lake Killer (Calling Card)

We’ll be sure to update this page with a full breakdown of how to unlock the Chucky Operator and all the unannounced release dates as soon as we know more.
