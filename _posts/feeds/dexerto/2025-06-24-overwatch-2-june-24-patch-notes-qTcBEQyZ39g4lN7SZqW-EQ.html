Blizzard

Blizzard is going all out for the June 24 Overwatch 2 update. From dozens of hero balance adjustments to all-new maps, modes, and systems, there’s a lot to be excited about.

At long last, we finally have the full details on Overwatch 2’s Season 17 update and it’s a doozy. Not only does the new Flashpoint map, Aatlis, arrive to shake things up, but Map Voting is now in effect to let teams pick and choose their battles.

Reporting and Endorsements have been improved all while a new game mode arrives as well. Hacks & Hijinx basically randomizes effects with each round, providing insane buffs like HP packs providing Ult charge, making every matchup feel fresh.

There’s a lot to unpack, so be sure to brush up with the full Overwatch 2 June 24 patch notes below.

Map Voting

  • Map voting is a new feature that gives players more agency about what map they would prefer to play on.
  • At the start of every Quick Play or Competitive Match, three randomly selected maps will appear, and each player will cast a single vote on the map they will want to play.
  • During the Map Voting phase, all text and voice channels to both teammates and opponents are disabled.
  • Votes for each map from all players are then are displayed and the game will then “roll” to see which vote is selected.
  • Maps with more votes are more likely to be selected but maps with less votes can still be selected if the roll lands on one of the votes for the map with less votes.
  • If players vote overwhelmingly for a single map, that map is automatically selected.
  • After Map Voting in Competitive Play, the Hero Ban phase will begin.

Report Tool

  • A new report category called Emergency is now added to the report tool and can be access in all areas you can report a player except for the quick-select report button in the Scoreboard.
  • You can then report instances of social communication that implies a threat of extreme harm to another person or a group of persons.
  • False reporting other players using these categories may result in actions issued on the reporting player’s account. Please remember to abide by the Blizzard In-Game Code of Conduct including when reporting other players.

Player Endorsements

  • Endorsement scoring has been updated to be more dynamic and to improve the accuracy of the system, with the goal of making Endorsement level feel more earned. Endorsement level should feel like a better measure of positivity, sportsmanship, and teamwork once the system has had some time to adjust to the new scoring.
  • The scoring system now considers a variety of circumstances when players endorse each other, such as giving bonus score in situations players rarely endorse, like endorsing an enemy on a loss.
  • Players that never Endorse others will cause much less decay for their allies. We’d like players that want to ignore the system to also be ignored by the system.
  • These scoring updates may cause endorsement levels to be more volatile in the short term, so we’ll be watching these changes carefully and tuning the dynamic scoring as needed.

Streamer Protect

  • Hide My Name now rotates to a different fake BattleTag after every match.
  • A new option in Streamer Protect advanced options will now block licensed music from playing in order to prevent the risk of copyright claims on player’s personal social and streaming channels. When enabled, the default Overwatch 2 theme will now play in any lobby where music we flag is considered to be copyrighted by a third party.

  • Base health regeneration timer increased from 5 to 6 seconds 

Developer Comments: These changes are intended to broadly increase the impact of Damage-role heroes and slightly reduce the amount of healing sustain felt in moment-to-moment gameplay.

Self-Care

  • Redesigned to: When you use Healing Ofuda, each Ofuda thrown heals you for 3% of max life.

Developer Comments: Soothing Step was designed to be a core part of Kiriko’s ability power-focused build, enabling her to serve as a highly mobile healer. This update brings it more in line with other build options while reinforcing her role as a capable burst healer in fast-paced fights. Additionally, Self-Care has been redesigned—both to address a bug and to make its healing more accurately reflect Kiriko’s healing output.
