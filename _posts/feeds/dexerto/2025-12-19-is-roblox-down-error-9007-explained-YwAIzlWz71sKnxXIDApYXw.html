Roblox may have tons of games to enjoy, but it’s hard to play them when Roblox itself is down, or if you’re being met with an odd 9007 error.

Whether you’re trying to dive into Steal a Brainrot, Blox Fruit, The Forge, Pokemon Bronze Forever, or your favorite game, it’s always a little tricky to do if you’re constantly being met with an error. Well, that’s an issue many players are currently facing, leading them to wonder if Roblox is down or if it’s an error with their own game.

So, here’s all you need to know, as well as what the error 9007 and Service Instances Unavailable notification mean.

Is Roblox down?

Currently, Roblox is down for many people across the world.

There have been various reports on social media of players attempting to play their favorite game and being met with an Error 9007, stopping them from joining or logging into the service in the first place. This does appear to be a global issue, affecting various regions.

What is Error 9007?

When you log into Roblox or attempt to enter the website and individual game pages, you may be met with the following error: “code”:9007, “message”: “Service Instances Unavailable.”

This issue explains that the backend services are currently down and features like DataStores aren’t working correctly, meaning there’s a lack of saving, loading, and retrieving player data. As such, it isn’t able to log you in, can have issues with saving your game, and fails to load games or account data, which is why you can’t get into Roblox at the moment.

As of the time of writing, Roblox hasn’t put out any word regarding a fix or acknowledgment of the outage, but when they do, or when it goes back online, we’ll update this article, so be sure to check back soon.
