We now know the length of next MCU movie The Fantastic Four: First Steps, so here’s how it compares with the runtime of every other entry in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Since Iron Man launched the Marvel Cinematic Universe in 2008, the series has become the most lucrative franchise in movie history.

Indeed, 11 of the Top 50 grossing movies of all-time are Marvel, with Avengers: Endgame, Avengers: Infinity War, and Spider-Man: No Way Home all making the Top 10.

Those three movies also have lengthy runtimes, and with the upcoming Fantastic Four: First Steps clocking in at 2 hours 10 minutes, here’s where that sits in the all-time list…
