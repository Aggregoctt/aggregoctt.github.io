Looking to cook up a delicious cake for Chris P. Bacon in Grow a Garden? Well, for that, you’ll need to know all the ingredients.

Roblox’s Grow a Garden may be all about gardening and growing vegetables, but what’s the point in growing the biggest carrot or brightest tomato if you have nothing to do with it. Well, that’s where the cooking event comes in, bringing tons of exciting dishes and rewards to Grow a Garden.

However, despite Chris P. Bacon craving a different meal each hour, the game doesn’t tell you the ingredients. So, if Chris is craving a cake, here’s how to make it in all the different rarities.

All Grow a Garden cake recipes

There are six different cake recipes we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden, each offering a different rarity. Thankfully, you can make them with different ingredients, guaranteeing the rarity.

Note: No matter the rarity of your dish, the cook time will be six minutes.

How to make a cake

Once you have all the ingredients, you can make your cake; all you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Go to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat.
  • Head over to the steel pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them inside the pot.
  • Press the big green Cook button, as shown in the image above.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and satisfied Chris’s craving, he’ll offer you a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth using the best meals you can.

You can check out all the available rewards in our guide.

It’s worth noting, you can cook a cake at any time in Grow a Garden, but save it for when Chris is craving it before you offer the dish so you can get those rewards.
