Black Ops Cold War’s LC10 has arrived in Warzone Season 4, offering more accuracy and range than you’d typically expect from an SMG.

While the LC10 was pretty strong when it was last in Call of Duty, the Black Ops 6 version is a different beast. With rapid TTK, almost no recoil, and super fast mobility, it’s immediately become one of the best guns in the game.

Here’s everything you need to build the best LC10 loadout in Warzone, including all the meta attachments, Perks, and equipment.

Meta LC10 loadout attachments

  • Muzzle: Suppressor
  • Barrel: Reinforced Barrel
  • Underbarrel: Ranger Foregrip
  • Magazine: Extended Mag II
  • Rear Grip: Commando Grip

The best LC10 loadout is all about improving its mobility and magazine size, letting you finesse and clear out entire squads.

The Suppressor is a solid Muzzle option as it hides your shots from the minimap. But if you’re not too worried about that, you can equip the Infiltrator Stock for extra aim walking movement speed.

The Reinforced Barrel increases both bullet velocity and damage range, helping you out when enemies are beyond extreme close range. Then, you should equip the Ranger Foregrip for extra sprint speed and horizontal recoil control.

The Commando Grip improves mobility in all the most important ways, increasing ADS and sprint to fire speed, and the Extended Mag II gives you 55 rounds in the mag, which is enough to take down a trio without having to reload.

Best Perks & equipment

  • Perk 1: Survivor
  • Perk 2: Tracker
  • Perk 3: Ghost
  • Wildcard: Overkill
  • Lethal: Semtex
  • Tactical: Smoke Grenade

For the best Perks and equipment to use with the LC10, we’d recommend using the Survivor Perk in Slot 1. It allows you to heal faster and get revived quicker, allowing you to immediately get back into the fight.

Tracker is an excellent choice for Perk 2 as it highlights any enemy you shoot, making it easier to track them at range and hit them through smoke. Although less useful, being able to see their footprints is a nice bonus.

For Perk 3, it’s hard to go wrong with Ghost. There’s nothing more frustrating than getting caught by a UAV or Heartbeat Sensor, so this will keep you invisible.

Since the LC10 is an SMG, you’ll want to equip the Overkill Wildcard and equip the Krig C Assault Rifle so you can be effective at all ranges.

Finally, the Semtex is always a strong Lethal as it deals quick splash damage to enemies, and the Smoke Grenade will let you cross open areas and get out of tough situations.

Is the LC10 meta in Warzone?

Yes, the LC10 is meta in Season 4, being one of the best guns in Warzone. The most important stats for an SMG are time to kill and mobility, and the LC10 has the perfect amount.

Not quite as important, but a huge bonus, is low recoil, and this is where it excels. You should prioritize unlocking and leveling up this new SMG as quickly as you can.

Pros and cons

How to unlock

The LC10 is unlocked on Page 3 of the Season 4 Battle Pass. It’s completely free, so play Warzone enough and you’ll soon have it unlocked.

But if you miss out during Season 4, you’ll be able to get it through The Armory by completing an XP challenge.

Best alternatives

If you haven’t unlocked the LC10 yet or are looking for an alternative, the new FFAR 1 is a solid option, but there are also plenty of meta SMGs like the C9 and Ladra.
