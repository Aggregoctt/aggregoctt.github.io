Many suspected she’d be coming to the game, and it’s finally been confirmed, with Aurora now added to Disney Dreamlight Valley through the Storybook Vale Unwritten Realm update, but you’ll need to unlock her.

Tons of characters are being regularly added to Disney Dreamlight Valley thanks to its updates. Recently, we’ve had Peter Pan, Alice, and the Cheshire Cat. This time it’s Maleficent and Aurora, otherwise known as Sleeping Beauty.

So, if you have the expansion and are looking to unlock Aurora, here’s all you need to know.

How to unlock Aurora

Complete the Storybook Vale main quests

The main storyline of Storybook Vale has you attempting to get Hades and Maleficent to see the error of their ways. Hades is pretty easy to help, but Maleficent requires you to reach her castle in order to speak with her. It’s then that she’ll allow you to uncover Aurora.

This is only done through the main quest. If you’ve already completed this in the first part of the storyline, then skip to the next step. If not, check out our guide for all the main quests in Storybook Vale.

Unlock Maleficent

After completing the main story quests, your task is far from complete. You’ll need to unlock Maleficent before you get the quests to find Aurora.

You can find out how to unlock Maleficent in our guide.

The Missing Princess

You’ll have to have reached at least friendship level seven with Flynn Rider before you can go that far with The Missing Princess.

Once you have, speak to him and he’ll offer help in finding where she is. Unfortunately, since the curse he’s not able to remember much. Which is why you need to reach friendship level seven. Shortly after, you’ll be able to speak to him and he’ll instruct you to dig up and open three pages of Aurora’s Diary.

These are in the following locations:

  • Near a waterfall on the west side of the Bind.
  • Near a beanstalk root by Maleficent’s castle.
  • By some water in the Elysian Fields.

You’ll need to dig up the pages and interact with them. When you do, speak to Flynn who will explain that Aurora wanted to speak to the Lorekeeper, but never appeared after that.
