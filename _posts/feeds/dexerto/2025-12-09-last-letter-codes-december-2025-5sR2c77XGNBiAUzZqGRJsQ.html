What are Last Letter codes used for?

Since the game’s objective is linear, there isn’t any resource farming required. The only rewards handed out from codes are Spins, which is your only pathway to obtaining different Styles, which function as unique traits you can equip in the game.

Each Style comes with its own set of attributes, and the rarer they are, the more influence they tend to have in a match. While you can spend in-game money to buy spins and try for better Styles, codes give you extra spins for free, making the hunt for stronger options feel far more accessible early on.
