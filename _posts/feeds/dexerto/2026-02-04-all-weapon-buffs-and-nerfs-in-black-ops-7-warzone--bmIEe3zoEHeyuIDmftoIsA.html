Activision

Black Ops 7 and Warzone Season 2 brings another big set of weapon buffs and nerfs that look set to completely change the meta going forward.

Every new Call of Duty season brings plenty of fresh content, and the Season 2 update is no exception. Not only does it bring the usual new guns and Battle Pass, but it also marks the return of Ranked Play in multiplayer, with Warzone’s version coming a little later.

However, if you’re planning to drop into either competitive mode, you might have to rethink your loadouts, as the devs have also rolled plenty of weapon balancing changes to keep things fresh.

Here’s a breakdown of every buff and nerf in Warzone and Black Ops 7 Season 2.
