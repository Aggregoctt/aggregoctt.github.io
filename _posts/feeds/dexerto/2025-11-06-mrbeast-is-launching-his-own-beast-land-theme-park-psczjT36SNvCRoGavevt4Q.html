Legendary YouTuber MrBeast has revealed his own theme park is set to open up in November, but fans in North America will have to travel across the globe to get there.

On November 6, Jimmy ‘MrBeast‘ Donaldson, the world’s most subscribed YouTuber announced that ‘Beast Land’ will be opening in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

The park will feature custom rides inspired by his plethora of YouTube videos, none of which are found at other amusement parks.

Donaldson pointed to Tower Siege as one example. Visitors load balls into real catapults and try to fire them into massive 60-foot tubes for points. Another game, Drop Zone, places six players on trap doors. Whoever hits their button last gets dropped, with the final player standing earning the most points.

Alongside the competitive minigames, Beast Land will also have traditional rollercoasters and rides. Donaldson says the park includes the “world’s largest prize wall,” allowing fans to win high-value rewards throughout the day.

There will also be numerous rollercoasters and rides, as expected from an amusement park.

The YouTuber noted that he didn’t want this to be a “typical theme park” experience and created games that he himself would want to play.

MrBeast explains why he chose to open a theme park in Saudi Arabia

When pressed about why he decided to select Saudi Arabia as the location for the park and not somewhere in North America or Europe, Donaldson explained that he wanted to appeal to fans in the Middle East.

“Middle of the world because a majority of my audience is outside America and we have a big Middle Eastern fan base,” he said. “Wanted to give them a chance to participate!”

It’s not clear if the Saudi government paid MrBeast for the rights to the park. Saudi Arabia has been making big moves in an attempt to become a major tourist destination such as launching the Esports World Cup and hosting WWE PLEs such as WrestleMania.

How much will it cost to enter Beast Land?

The park will have a few different ticket types. The least expensive general admission ticket will run you just under $7 USD, but won’t include rides, activations, experiences, or games.

‘Beast Mode’ will cost around $25 and will let you partake in challenges, experiences, games, plus three rides.

The most expensive tier, ‘Beast Mode+’ will let you go on every ride, but will cost roughly $66.

Of course, this isn’t counting other factors such as travel arrangements, but as far as theme parks go, entry is certainly on the cheaper side.

Beast Land is set to open on November 13, 2025.
