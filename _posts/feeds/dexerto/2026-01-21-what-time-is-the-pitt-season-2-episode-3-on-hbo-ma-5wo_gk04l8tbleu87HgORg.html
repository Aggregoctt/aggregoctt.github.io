What happened in Season 2 Episode 2?

The Pitt Season 2 Episode 2 focused on 8am to 9am during a busy July 4 holiday, and sees Dr. Robby and Dr. Baran Al-Hashimi continuing to disagree over training methods, and the use of AI in the department.

Ongoing stories concern the mystery deepening around the baby left in hospital, while a social worker arrives to speak to the father of the young girl with injuries all over her body.

The nun with the weeping eye tests positive for gonorrhoea, while doctors crack open the cast on the arm of the homeless man, and find it filled with maggots.

In terms of new patients, there’s a man whose arm is smashed by a roof tile, a woman who glued her eye closed with super-glue, a patient whose arm is burned by grease from a griddle, and a guy with broccoli trapped in his gullet. Oh, and an erect penis that won’t go down.

As for the doctors, King has an action-packed hour, worrying about her forthcoming deposition, then being flirted with by a patient, who suddenly knocks her over when the police arrive looking for him, resulting in King banging her head.

Between his clashes with Baran Al-Hashimi, it’s revealed that Robby is having a secret romance with the hospital’s case manager, Noelle Hastings, a story that will doubtless develop over the course of the season.
