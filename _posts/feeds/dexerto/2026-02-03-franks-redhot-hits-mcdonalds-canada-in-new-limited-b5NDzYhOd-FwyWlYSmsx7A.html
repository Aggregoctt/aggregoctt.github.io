McDonald’s Canada has announced a new limited-time collaboration with Frank’s RedHot, bringing a spicy twist to several menu items nationwide.

The McDonald’s x Frank’s RedHot lineup launches across Canada on February 3, marking the first time the fast food chain has worked with the hot sauce brand in the Canadian market, adding heat to breakfast sandwiches, chicken items, and dipping sauces.

The menu includes several new and modified items featuring Frank’s RedHot flavors.

What’s on the McDonald’s x Frank’s RedHot menu

Frank’s RedHot Original McCrispy is made with 100% Canadian-raised seasoned chicken, shredded lettuce, mayo-style sauce, and Frank’s RedHot Original Thick Sauce on a potato bun.

McDonald’s Original McVeggie features a breaded vegetable patty topped with shredded lettuce, mayo-style sauce, and Frank’s RedHot Original Thick Sauce on a toasted sesame bun.

Frank’s RedHot Hot Honey Dipping Sauce blends honey sweetness with heat and is designed for McNuggets.

Breakfast also gets multiple additions with the Frank’s RedHot Creamy Garlic McMuffin lineup, which includes Creamy Garlic Bacon ‘N Egg McMuffin, Creamy Garlic Sausage ‘N Egg McMuffin, Creamy Garlic Egg McMuffin, and Creamy Garlic Chicken McMuffin.

Francesca Cardarelli, Chief Marketing Officer at McDonald’s Canada, said in a statement that the collaboration was built to span multiple meal occasions.

“Our fans love to turn up the flavour, and collaborating with Frank’s RedHot let us do that, McDonald’s style,” she said. “We didn’t just add heat; we are celebrating this collaboration across breakfast, dunk-worthy dips, and delicious sandwiches. They say it goes on everything, but our iconic taste makes everything something special.”
