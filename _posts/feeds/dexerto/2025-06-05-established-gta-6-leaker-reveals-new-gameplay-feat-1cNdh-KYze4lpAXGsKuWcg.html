A credible GTA 6 leaker who knew Jason and Lucia’s last names before the second trailer was released has revealed a slew of new details about the game.

Weeks before Rockstar released the second GTA 6 trailer and character bios, an X user known as ‘GameRoll’ confirmed that the two protagonists would be named Lucia Caminos and Jason Duvall.

GameRoll has since revealed a bunch of new information about how GTA 6 will play, including some content that has been cut from the infamous leaked footage from 2022.

The information, screengrabbed from a Discord server, will come as good news for fans of the series, especially considering the pressure Rockstar has to meet expectations.

GTA 6 will reportedly let you freeroam with Jason and Lucia

In GTA 5, players had the option to switch between Michael, Trevor and Frankin and complete missions with them.

While some missions involved one, two, or even three members of the group, players couldn’t walk around Los Santos with the three protagonists together.

That’s going to change in GTA 6, as GameRoll claims players will be able to roam with Jason by himself, Lucia by herself, or the two at the same time.

“Basically they’re companions, two of them at the same time you can switch between,” he said.

This will make exploring Leonida much more intriguing and could create a bunch of gameplay situations that will be new to the series.

GTA 6 will have over 700 “stores” and malls to rob

One of the big rumors about GTA 6 is that it will include more enterable buildings than ever before and it seems like that information has some truth to it.

According to GameRoll, while house burglaries won’t be an option, there are over 700 stores, including malls. And they’ll be robbable.

In the Gaming Leaks and Rumors subreddit, GameRoll added more information.

“By stores I’m not sure what the source is referring to fyi. I know malls are enterable but like in the leaks there are clips of being able to purchase things from gumball machines,” he remarked.

“Wouldn’t surprise me if everything that you can purchase from is defined as a ‘store’ which yeah would make it more believable.”

That’s not all – he also claimed that there will be elevators in certain skyscrapers, so players will be able to venture around the interior of buildings.

As far as other buildings, the leaker didn’t reveal much, but he did note that VI will feature male strippers, suggesting that strip clubs won’t be limited to just female dancers anymore.

Furthermore, he claimed that “warehouse robots” – something players spotted in the 2022 leaks – had been cut back in 2023.

GTA 6 cover system and weapon inventory leaks

GameRoll claims that the The Last of Us Part 2-style dynamic cover system was scrapped after it first appeared in the 2022 leaks.

“[It was] complete ass,” he said. “So it’s good they cut it and returned back to a button. It’s a lot better apparently.”

He also revealed that the trunk of cars will function similarly to horse inventory from RDR2 with players being able to get weapons out of their vehicles.

However, the rumored option to dual-wield certain guns isn’t actually happening. So, anyone expecting Max Payne-like shootouts may be a bit disappointed by this decision.

GTA 6 will reportedly have 5 chapters

Finally, the leaker claims that his source told GTA 6 will be comprised of 5 chapters. It’s unclear if this would include a prologue or epilogue.

If RDR2’s chapter length is any indication, this could make GTA 6 a bit shorter, considering the previous game had six chapters plus a sizable epilogue.

The main RDR2 story takes around 50 hours to beat, so this might make GTA 6 close to 40 by comparison, but VI’s chapters may be significantly longer.

That’s also not counting activities that aren’t part of the main campaign, which will likely take up a huge chunk of time.
