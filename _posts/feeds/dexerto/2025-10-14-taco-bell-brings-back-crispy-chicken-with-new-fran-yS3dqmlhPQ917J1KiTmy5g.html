Taco Bell is bringing back its popular Crispy Chicken, this time paired with a new Frank’s RedHot Diablo sauce that combines Buffalo flavor, Taco Bell’s Diablo kick, and creamy ranch. The new menu items launch nationwide on October 16.

The Frank’s RedHot Diablo lineup adds the brand’s signature heat to Taco Bell’s Crispy Chicken Burritos, Soft Tacos, Strips, and Nacho Fries.

Crispy Chicken, made from all-white meat marinated in jalapeno buttermilk and coated in a crunchy blend of breadcrumbs and tortilla chips, has been a recurring fan favorite. Taco Bell Global Chief Food Innovation Officer Liz Matthews said the chain’s sauces are a “core part of the Taco Bell experience,” adding that the latest Crispy Chicken collaboration continues its streak of flavor experimentation.

Frank’s RedHot Diablo lineup

Taco Bell’s new menu lineup gives fans multiple ways to try the returning Crispy Chicken and Frank’s RedHot Diablo combo:

  • Frank’s RedHot Diablo Crispy Chicken Burrito – Crispy chicken strips, lettuce, cabbage, pico de gallo, and cheddar cheese, grilled and topped with Frank’s RedHot Diablo or Avocado Ranch, priced at $5.49.
  • Frank’s RedHot Diablo Crispy Chicken Soft Taco – A single Crispy Chicken strip with cabbage, lettuce, pico de gallo, and cheese, available with either Frank’s RedHot Diablo or Avocado Ranch for $2.99.
  • Frank’s RedHot Diablo Chicken Nacho Fries – Seasoned Nacho Fries topped with chicken, nacho cheese, pico de gallo, a three-cheese blend, and a drizzle of Frank’s RedHot Diablo, priced at $4.99.
  • Crispy Chicken Strips with Spicy Ranch – Four Crispy Chicken strips served with Spicy Ranch or Frank’s RedHot Diablo for $6.99.

McCormick & Company Vice President of Marketing Valda Coryat said the collaboration highlights Frank’s RedHot’s balance of flavor and heat while reimagining Taco Bell classics.
