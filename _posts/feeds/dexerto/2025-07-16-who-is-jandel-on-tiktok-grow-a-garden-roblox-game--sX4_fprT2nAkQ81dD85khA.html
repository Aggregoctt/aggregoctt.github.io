Grow a Garden Roblox game creator goes viral ---

Roblox developer Jandel is making waves on TikTok after his game Grow a Garden shattered player count records, sending fans into a frenzy across social media.

Grow a Garden has quickly become one of the most-played games on the Roblox platform, breaking player count records in the process. The farming sim’s success has catapulted Jandel into the spotlight, with his TikTok account amassing over 7.5M followers and hundreds of millions of views in just weeks.

Recent TikToks from Jandel show behind-the-scenes footage of the game’s development, fan shoutouts, and updates that tease new in-game items and features.

Jandel shows off Grow a Garden development in viral TikTok’s

In a post on Tuesday, July 15, Jandel shared a video showing off a new pet – a nine-tailed fox – and asked fans what they think about the ability he had in mind for it.

The dev revealed that he wants to make the new pet go to other people’s gardens, mutate it, and bring home a cloned version of that fruit.

“So both people win! Let me know what you guys think,” he said.
