GTA Online’s new Mansions update has seemingly added a number of new cars from GTA 6, and it also includes a hint about a key character returning. 

Over the last few years, Grand Theft Auto fans have gotten a number of unexpected hints about GTA 6 in GTA Online. Rockstar Games has seemingly backported a number of cars and features, and even included Vice City in several vehicles’ liveries, too. 

With the brand-new Safehouse in the Hills update, GTA Online players can now own their own mansions at three different locations. The update isn’t just limited to the swanky new houses either. 

There are several new cars included in the update, including the Buffalo STX Pursuit. The police vehicle appeared in the GTA 6 screenshots that were released shortly after the initial release date delay announcement back in May. 

Several new GTA 6 cars appear in GTA Online

On top of that, there are two other cars that have already featured in the GTA 6 trailers. 

That includes another police vehicle, the Bravado Buffalo Police cruiser and a pickup truck – the Shitzu Keitora. 

The Keitora’s appearance in the trailer is a little less obvious than the two police cars, as it appears during a dark segment. However, fans have already pointed out the very obvious similarities between the two.

GTA 5’s Jimmy hinted at for GTA 6

As noted, there is also a hint in the new GTA Online update about Jimmy De Santa, Michael’s son, possibly making an appearance in GTA 6. 

After meeting his parents, there is a trigger for people who own the Arcade upgrade in their mansions to receive a message from Jimmy. Though, as per Rockstar insider TezFun, it appears to have been bugged or cut out of the game. 

“Did you meet my parents?? WTF. I tell them I’m moving out of state and they immediately go looking for a replacement kid? Ugh whatever. Welcome to the family. I guess?” he says. 

Jimmy has long been suggested as one of the carryover characters from GTA 5 to GTA 6, similar to how Johnny Klebitz popped up as a part of Trevor’s chapter. 

It remains to be seen if he will, indeed, play a role in Grand Theft Auto 6, but fan theories have only run wilder after this.
