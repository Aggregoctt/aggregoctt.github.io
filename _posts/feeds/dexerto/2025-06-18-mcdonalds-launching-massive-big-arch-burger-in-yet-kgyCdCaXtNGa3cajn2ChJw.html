McDonald’s has launched the Big Arch burger in yet another country, the UK, as fans in the United States continue to wait for the massive sandwich.

Advertised as McDonald’s biggest burger yet, the Big Arch was released back in the Summer of 2024 in Canada and Portugal.

The massive sandwich features two beef patties, lettuce, pickles, onion, cheese, and, of course, the ‘Big Arch Sauce.’

Since its release, fans all around the world have patiently waited for it to come to their country, and now McDonald’s has news for the United Kingdom.

Big Arch launches in the UK

“The new Big Arch for Big McDonald’s Hunger!” said McDonald’s UK in a trailer they uploaded to YouTube.

The burger is available now until September 9, 2025, as long as your store has the ingredients in stock.

This comes just months after McDonald’s CEO Chris Kempczinski revealed in an earnings call back in February that they have plans to expand the burger to more markets around the world in 2025 and “nearly all markets” by the end of 2026.

In the meantime, the company is preparing for the long-awaited return of its Snack Wraps on July 10. The highly requested treat will feature two flavors, ranch and spicy, much like the existing McCrispy sandwiches that McDonald’s offers.
