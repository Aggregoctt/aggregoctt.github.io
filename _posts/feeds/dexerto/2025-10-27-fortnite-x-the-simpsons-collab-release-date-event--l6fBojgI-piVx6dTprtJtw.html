There are so many supposed leaks around the Fortnite x Simpsons crossover that it’s hard to tell what’s real anymore.

Springfield maps, Bart skins, and “confirmed” key art, yet Epic hasn’t actually announced anything official. Still, the sheer amount of imagery shared by big leakers like HYPEX and ShiinaBR makes this one of Fortnite’s wildest rumor cycles yet.

Here’s everything that’s been spotted, shared, or suspected so far.

The new season, with the Fortnite x Simpsons collab, will start on November 1, 2025, immediately after the current Fortnitemares event concludes.

All leaked Simpsons skins

If even half the leaks are true, Springfield is about to invade the island. Characters “confirmed” by various HYPEX posts include:

  • Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, Moe, Ned Flanders, Krusty, and even Scratchy
  • Parody versions of Peely and Fishstick in Simpsons’ cel-shaded style
  • Kang and Kodos as an Item Shop cosmetic have been leaked

None of these have been shown in Epic’s official channels, and many are fan-made mockups, so for now they remain unconfirmed.

Leaked Springfield map

Several images and teasers show a new island resembling Springfield, complete with a Nuclear Power Plant POI, treehouse, and TV-static teaser screens. By the looks of it, the next season’s map will be fully cel-shaded like the Rick and Morty crossover, giving the whole island a cartoon look.

Event and teasers

The collab’s supposed event has taken over social feeds and the current Fortnite map.

Teasers feature graffiti tagged “El Barto,” a Fortnitemares logo, and even a “tune-in” message for November 1 at 11AM ET.

On the current Fortnite map, you can also spot two iconic items from The Simpsons: the family’s treehouse and TV.
