Date Everything may have you romancing your furniture, but you can also bring them to life through Realizing.

In order to actually complete Date Everything, you need to Realize the game’s 100 characters. This will allow you to get rid of the Dateviators, stopping your boss from descending on you. Oh, it’ll also let you leave the house, if that’s your kind of thing.

However, Realizing isn’t that simple; not only do you need the right SPECS, but you also need to meet a fair few other requirements. So, here’s how to Realize in Date Everything, and all the SPECS you need to acquire.

How to Realize

To Realize, you need to complete the storyline of each character. Once you’ve completed the individual’s quests, speak to them again and explain that you want to Realize them, and they’ll usually accept.

If you have the right S.P.E.C.S. then you’ll be able to Realize the dateable, making them real. Of course, for this, you’ll need to increase those stats, which can be done by following our guide.

It’s worth noting, once you do Realize, most of the dateables will then leave the house, so you won’t be able to interact with them anymore. So choose carefully.
