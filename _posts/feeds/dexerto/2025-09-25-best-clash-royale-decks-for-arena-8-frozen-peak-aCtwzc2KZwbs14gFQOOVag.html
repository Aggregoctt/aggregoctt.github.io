Clash Royale’s Arena 8, Frozen Peak, can be tricky to beat. However, by using the best decks, you’ll be able to breeze through this level easily.

One of the best ways to unlock cards in Clash Royale is by progressing through the Arenas. By the time you reach Frozen Peak, you’ll be introduced to a number of ice-themed cards. These include Freeze, Ice Spirit, and many more.

While these can add powerful utility to your deck, it can be challenging to get past this Arena solely because you’ll be up against players who are more experienced.

So, if you’re struggling to win, we’ve compiled the best Arena 8 decks you can use below.

Best Arena 8 decks

The best thing about reaching Arena 8 is that you’ll already have some strong cards in your collection by this time. Still, building the right deck can be tricky since you haven’t unlocked everything yet.

With new cards becoming available here, you can start experimenting more with different builds and strategies. Below, you’ll find three solid options tailored to different playstyles.

Giant Skeleton-Hog Rider-Freeze deck

Deck composition:

  • Giant Skeleton
  • Hog Rider 
  • Freeze 
  • Fireball 
  • Baby Dragon 
  • Musketeer 
  • Skeletons  
  • Cannon

With an average cost of 3.8 Elixir, this deck balances offense, defense, and spell control to help you progress through Arena 8.

It’s built around the Giant Skeleton + Freeze combo: the Giant Skeleton soaks up damage and clears defenses with its death bomb, while Freeze locks down enemy troops or towers to secure damage.

The Hog Rider serves as your secondary win condition, letting you pressure lanes and chip towers if the Giant Skeleton gets defeated. For support, you can rely on Baby Dragon and Musketeer to provide splash and ranged DPS, letting you deal with swarms and air threats easily.

On defense, Cannon stalls tanks, Skeletons provide cheap cycles and distractions, and Fireball cleans up mid-health troops while chipping away at towers.

Hog Rider-Ice Golem-Musketeer deck

Deck composition:

  • Hog Rider
  • Ice Golem
  • Musketeer
  • Cannon
  • Skeletons
  • Ice Spirit
  • Vines
  • Fireball

If you’re looking for a fast Hog cycle deck, this one is a solid choice, with an average Elixir cost of just 2.9. While it doesn’t have a heavy tank, the tradeoff is you get to deal steady chip damage and the ability to stall enemy pushes.

On offense, send Hog Rider with Ice Golem to tank and pressure towers. If you’re low on Elixir, the Hog + Ice Spirit combo works as a cheap alternative, with the Ice Spirit freezing defenders for extra hits.

Against tanks and defensive troops, use Vines to trap them and buy time for your Musketeer to deal damage. Musketeer also pairs well with Cannon on defense, handling both air and ground threats.

Don’t forget to use Fireball to clear glass cannons (like Musketeer, Wizard, or Witch) that block your Hog. Lastly, Skeletons are best used to distract heavy hitters like P.E.K.K.A., so your defenses can finish them off.

The key to this deck is to out-cycle your opponent with cheap cards and keep the Hog pressure going until their tower falls.

What is the best deck in Arena 8 in Clash Royale?

Deck composition:

  • Balloon
  • Ice Golem
  • Musketeer
  • Minions
  • Skeleton Army
  • Tombstone
  • Fireball
  • Zap

This fast-cycle Balloon deck, with an average cost of just 3.3 Elixir, lets you apply constant pressure while defending efficiently. Compared to other decks, it’s safer, more forgiving, and handles common Arena 8 threats like Giant, Hog, Witch, and P.E.K.K.A. very well.

With this deck, you’ll want to use the Ice Golem as a tank, followed by the Balloon, and then spells like Zap or Fireball to help protect your push against swarms.

When it comes to defense, deploy the Skeleton Army to stop enemy tanks like Giant or Hog, and use Tombstone to stall and distract them. You also have Musketeer and Minions that can deal with enemies in the air and on the ground. 

If the enemies are all clumped together, use Fireball to sweep them off. This is also a great spell to use against glass cannons.

One way to use this deck effectively is by playing patiently. Defend first, then counterpush with Balloon behind surviving troops, cycling cheap cards to keep up steady pressure until you wear your opponent down.
