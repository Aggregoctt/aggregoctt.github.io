The Summer I Turned Pretty Season 3 is approaching its halfway point, so make sure you know exactly when Episode 5 drops and the final season’s entire release schedule.

Based on Jenny Han’s beloved YA trilogy of novels, the Prime Video series charts the story of Isabel “Belly” Conklin (Lola Tung) and the love triangle that develops between her, Conrad (Christopher Briney), and his brother Jeremiah (Gavin Casalegno).

The first season was a hit, rocketing the sales of the original novels on Amazon’s bestsellers list, and Season 2 was one of the streamer’s most popular shows, even listed in its top three for driving global acquisition. Sadly, all good things must come to an end, and the third season will bring the story to a close.

It premiered on July 16, with Belly and Jeremiah navigating the turmoil caused by their new engagement in the early episodes. The drama will continue in Season 3 Episode 5, so let’s get details of the show’s release schedule straightened out.
