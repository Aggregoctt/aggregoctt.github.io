Pokemon TCG Pocket is doubling down on nostalgia and power with the Mega Rising expansion, a set that introduces Mega Evolutions into the mobile realm.

Expect the meta to get shaken with Mega Gyarados, Mega Blaziken, and Mega Altaria ready to dominate with their boosters packed with new cards. New strategies will emerge with Mega Evolutions, while older ones will have to adapt fast or disappear.

But the hype doesn’t stop there! Mega Rising also brings updated systems for sharing and trading cards among friends, and celebrates the game’s first anniversary with bonus events and free packs.

This is the fifth major expansion – following Wisdom of Sea and Sky – and the first set of the B Series. Here are all the cards that will be released on October 30, 2025.
