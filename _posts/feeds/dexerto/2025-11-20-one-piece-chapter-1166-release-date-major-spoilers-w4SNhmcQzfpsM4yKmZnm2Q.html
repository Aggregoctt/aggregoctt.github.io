One Piece Chapter 1166 sees the end of the infamous Rocks D. Xebec, so here’s everything you need to know about what’s coming.

History knows him as the ruthless pirate and the captain of the notorious Rocks Pirates, but the truth is something quite different. The Elbaf Arc reveals what really occurred during the God Valley Incident, which marked the end of Rocks’ era.

After Imu took over Saturn’s body, they used Domi Reversi on Rocks and turned him into a demon. In his demonic form, he was forced to attack his wife and child, who were fortunately saved by Kuma just in time.

Roger and Garp joined forces in order to defeat him in Chapter 1165. What happened to the trio after that is revealed in One Piece Chapter 1166.

One Piece Chapter 1166 major spoilers

Roger and Garp finally defeat Rocks by combining their Conqueror’s Haki through their strongest moves. One Piece Chapter 1166 picks up right after Rocks falls. The cover page depicts the Straw Hats enjoying hot sweet potatoes together.

After Rocks is defeated, Garling suddenly appears and lands the fatal blow on him. The island finally sinks into the sea, and everything about its truth is hidden from the world. However, people all over the world are shocked to learn about the end of the Rocks Pirates.

In the aftermath, Loki remembers how Rocks once shared his dream with him – to be the king of the world. Elsewhere, Roger finds baby Shanks in a treasure chest. Dragon, however, has some consequences to face after he went against the Navy to save the common people.

Dragon is imprisoned for his actions. But Garp visits the prison and breaks him free. One Piece Chapter 1166 ends with Harald learning about Rocks’ death and breaking his horns in mourning.
