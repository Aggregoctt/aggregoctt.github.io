Logan Paul has shared a video showing the moment he gave his one-year-old daughter, Esme, her first Pokemon card, which turned out to be a high-value collectible.

In the clip, posted to Instagram, Paul sits with Esme as he presents her with a PSA 10 First Edition Shining Magikarp. The card is widely regarded as one of the most iconic rare Pokemon cards from the Neo Revelation set and is valued at around $9,000 based on recent market sales.

The video shows Paul guiding Esme as she holds the card and looks at it for the first time, with the moment quickly gaining traction across social media platforms.

Logan Paul gifts daughter rare PSA 10 Shining Magikarp

“Since I know you chose Magikarp as your starter Pokemon, I got you a first edition Shiny Magikarp,” he said.”

Paul, who has long been involved in the Pokemon trading card scene, has previously showcased his collection publicly and has spoken about the value of graded cards.

This comes just days after Logan revealed plans to auction off his super-rare PSA graded Pikachu Illustrator card that he paid $5.3M for back in 2022.
