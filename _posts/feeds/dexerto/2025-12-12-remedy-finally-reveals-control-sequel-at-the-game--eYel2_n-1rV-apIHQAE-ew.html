Not Jesse Faden, but her brother Dylan, is back, and the Remedy-verse continues. A follow-up to Control has been unveiled at The Game Awards 2025, as Control: Resonant is now in focus.

Two years on from Remedy’s last brain-melting experience, Alan Wake 2 (sorry, FBC Firebreak), the Finnish studio’s next game has now stepped into the light. A Faden is back in full force, but it’s not Jesse.

Six years after the original, Control: Resonant continues the complex, interwoven narrative of Remedy’s surreal games. The sequel is set to launch in 2026.
