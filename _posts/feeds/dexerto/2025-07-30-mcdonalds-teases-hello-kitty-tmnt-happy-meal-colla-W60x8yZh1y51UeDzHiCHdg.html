McDonald’s has teased the next Happy Meal toy release, and fans are certain it’s a collab between Sanrio’s Hello Kitty and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Every month or so, McDonald’s releases a new set of Happy Meal toys for kids in the United States to enjoy.

July’s release included a set of miniature McDonald’s restaurant items, including a Happy Meal box, an ordering kiosk, and even the building itself.

On Wednesday, July 30, McDonald’s Senior Marketing Director Guillaume Huin posted a picture to his X account teasing the next toys, and fans are excited.

McDonald’s teases Hello Kitty x Ninja Turtles collab

In the picture, you can see a hair bow and turtle emoji underneath a picture of the Happy Meal box with the date August 12, 2025. Beneath those, you’ll see what appears to be the top of a green Hello Kitty head.

Fans began speculating what the teaser could be in the comments, with most settling on it being a Hello Kitty collab with the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Back in July, McDonald’s revealed the collab in Malaysia. It’s possible the US launch will receive different toys, however, so we’ll have to wait to see what is available when the fast food giant reveals it fully.
