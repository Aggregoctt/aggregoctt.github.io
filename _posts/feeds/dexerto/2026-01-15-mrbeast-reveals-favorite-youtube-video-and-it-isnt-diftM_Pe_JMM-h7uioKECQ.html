Social media star Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson has revealed his favorite YouTube video, and it’s not actually something that he uploaded. 

YouTube is home to tens of millions of videos, but everyone is able to pick out a favorite. Or, at least, pick out a few that they love to reminisce about. Some of these videos might be good life advice, a funny meme, or something that just instantly takes them back to better days. 

When it comes to MrBeast, he has dominated YouTube over the last few years, uploading thousands of videos across his different channels. He’s spent millions on massive sets and prizes too. 

Basically, Donaldson has got plenty of his own videos to choose from when it comes to picking a favorite. However, his go-to video is actually something that wasn’t uploaded to his own channel, but rather Technoblade’s. 

MrBeast’s favorite YouTube video is from Technoblade

It came up during MrBeast’s chat with WIRED for their signature ‘Answers The Web’s Most Searched Questions’ series. 

“Favorite YouTube video? This might seem like a weird one, I bet most people listening to this will have no idea what I’m saying but there’s this YouTuber back in the day called Technoblade,” Jimmy said.

“He had this series where he’d try and farm the most potatoes on a server. People who don’t play Minecraft are going to think I’m crazy as a grown man saying this is my favorite video. But, Technoblade was very, very funny and charismatic, and that video is just so freaking funny.”

Timestamp of 8:30

The video in question in Technoblade’s ‘Skyblock: The Great Potato War’ which has racked up close to 56 million views in six years. 

Plenty of new viewers have taken to the video to say that MrBeast directed them to it as well. 

MrBeast added that he makes time to watch the video at least “once a year” and that it is “always inspirational” to him.
