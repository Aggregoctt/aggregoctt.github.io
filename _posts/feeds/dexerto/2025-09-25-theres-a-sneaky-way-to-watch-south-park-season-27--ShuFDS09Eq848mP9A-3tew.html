Why everyone’s talking about ‘Conflict of Interest’

The new South Park episode, ‘Conflict of Interest’, was one of the most anticipated in recent memory, and it does not hold back. 

Season 27 Episode 5 ruthlessly mocks Donald Trump Jr. and online betting, while the POTUS spends much of the runtime in a cartoonish quest to get rid of his and Satan’s “butt baby”. 

Eventually, this leads him to ​attempting to give Satan toxoplasmosis – an infection caused by a common parasite found in cat feces and undercooked meat, which is especially dangerous for pregnant women as it can harm the baby.

In South Park, FCC head Brendan Carr becomes infected, with a doctor warning he may “lose his freedom of speech.”

Elsewhere, the entire town is betting on Kyle’s mom Sheila bombing a Palestinian hospital. She voices her frustration at the conflict, pointing out that “it’s not Jews vs Palestine, it’s Israel vs Palestine.”

At her wits’ end, she goes straight to Israel to give Benjamin Netanyahu a piece of her mind. Oh, and JD Vance unveils his secret plan to be next in line as the president.

When is Season 27 Episode 6 out?

If you agree, there’s some bad news: the next episode won’t be out for another three weeks. 

South Park Season 27 was initially meant to be released weekly, but from the beginning there has been a two-week gap between each episode due to the fast turnaround of the series. 

Parker, Stone, and their team write, animate, and edit new episodes in a matter of days, meaning delays aren’t unusual. The Season 27 release schedule officially switched to bi-weekly, but with Episode 5 needing more time, this has switched to tri-weekly. 

The plan is to switch back to once every two weeks after Season 27 Episode 6, which will drop on October 15, 2025.
