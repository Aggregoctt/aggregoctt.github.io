GTA 6 could end up being a multi-state game after a player made a major discovery and a nice bit of cash.

Back in May 2025, the second trailer for GTA 6 was revealed, along with screenshots. In those images, fans noticed license plates for states other than just Leonida, with one being ‘Gloriana’ – a clear parody of Georgia.

While this suggested the existence of a special location on the map that hasn’t been announced yet, this could very well have just been Rockstar trying to expand the world of Grand Theft Auto.

Well, on January 15, a new wrinkle emerged that could further point to Gloriana being an actual location players will be able to travel to.

GTA 6 website twist suggests a whole other state will be playable

According to the user ‘rainyaltaccount’ on Reddit, in May, they decided to register the website visitgloriana.com and made it redirect to Rockstar’s GTA 6 site.

However, this decision, originally done for trolling purposes, apparently came with a big payday.

“Fast forward to the end of 2025, I was contacted by a middleman saying someone was interested in buying the domain for around $600. I initially turned it down because I thought it was just someone joking.”

From there, the buyer made a more aggressive offer, and after some negotiations, the Redditor agreed to sell the domain for around $2000.

Here’s where it gets interesting. The new registrar of Visit Gloriana, Markmonitor, manages the domains for a bunch of websites, including ones owned by Rockstar, with some even seemingly related to activities or features in GTA 6.

Of course, it’s best to take all this info with a grain of salt as it’s possible this isn’t related to GTA VI at all, but it does raise some eyebrows and questions about even more locations.

Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA 5 both had secret areas not part of the main map, so that’s yet another possibility for Gloriana.

In other recent leaks, court proceedings, and comments from Rockstar have confirmed an assortment of gameplay details, including over 700 stores, the return of the 6-star wanted level and the story’s length.

GTA 6 is set to release on November 19, 2026.
