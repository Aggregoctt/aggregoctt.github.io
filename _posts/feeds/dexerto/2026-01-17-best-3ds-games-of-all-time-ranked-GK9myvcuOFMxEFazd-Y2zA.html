When Nintendo released the 3DS in 2011, it quietly became one of the most beloved handhelds in the company’s history. It wasn’t just a successor to the DS, it was a celebration of everything Nintendo fans love: charming worlds, inventive mechanics, and franchises that feel like home.

The 3DS carried legendary series forward in confident new ways. The Legend of Zelda returned with A Link Between Worlds and definitive 3D versions of Ocarina of Time and Majora’s Mask. Pokémon evolved into full 3D with X and Y, before expanding through Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. Fire Emblem Awakening revived a series and built a new generation of strategy fans. Meanwhile, Animal Crossing: New Leaf became a daily ritual for millions, and Super Mario 3D Land proved Mario still ruled handheld platforming.

With all that in mind, here’s our ranking of the best Nintendo 3DS games.
