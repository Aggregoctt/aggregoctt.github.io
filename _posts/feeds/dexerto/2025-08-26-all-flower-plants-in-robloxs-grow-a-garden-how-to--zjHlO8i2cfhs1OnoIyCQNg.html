Grow a Garden’s Beanstalk event has players collecting various Plants to give to Jack. Once they do, they’ll get exclusive rewards. However, he doesn’t tell you the exact plants you need to find, which makes requests like Flower Plants a little tricky.

Alongside its huge player count, Roblox’s Grow a Garden loves handing its players challenging events. Previously, it was the cooking event, where a pig asked you for certain dishes. Now, it’s the Beanstalk event, in which Jack asks for certain plants.

Well, if he’s asked for Flower Plants, then we’ve got all you need to know, including the best ones to give him, and of course, how to get them.

All Flower Plants & how to get them

We’ve uncovered 52 different Flower Plants in Grow a Garden, all of which work when given to Jack. As we find more, we’ll be updating this table, so check back soon.

Some plants are currently unobtainable, but will still work if you have them in your inventory.

What are Flower Plants?

Grow a Garden’s Flower Plants are really what they say on the tin, they’re flowers. Any flower you can find or grow will count as a Flower Plant when given to Jack.

For example, Lavender or Sunflowers are both flowers, so they’ll work when Jack asks for them. They’re much easier to decipher than the likes of Woody Plants of Prickly Plants.

When you find them, offer them to Jack. The rarer the item, the more points you get. The player who gets the most points will get an exclusive reward when the event ends on August 23.
