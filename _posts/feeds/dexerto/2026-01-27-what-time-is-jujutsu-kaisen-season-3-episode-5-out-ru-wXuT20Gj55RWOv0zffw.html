Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 is giving us an animated version of ‘Culling Game Arc,’ and here’s details of when and where to watch Episode 5, as well as a refresher on what happened in Episode 4.

Jujutsu Kaisen fans got an early peak at Season 3 of the manga adaptation at the tail-end of 2025, when Episodes 1 and 2 were packaged, combined, and released in cinemas.

Titled Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution, the film served as a bridge between the Shonen Jump’s Shubuya Incident and Culling Game, and grossed more than $40 million worldwide.

Now fans of Greg Akutami’s manga can see what happens next, with episodes 1-4 out now, and Episode 5 dropping this week.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 5 drops on Crunchyroll on January 29, 2026, at 9am PT / 12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (January 30)
  • 6am New Zealand (January 30)

The new episode is titled: ‘Fever.’

What happened in Episode 4?

Episode 4 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 saw Maki encountering her mother, experiencing tragedy with her sister, and endeavouring to wipe out the Zen’in clan.

Proceedings commence with Maki entering her family’s compound, receiving abuse from Maoya, and being being told by her mother to stay out of the vault – an instruction she ignores.

Once inside, she sees her twin sister Mai gravely injured by their father Ogi, while she herself is struck by dad, who traps his daughters in a chamber filled with cursed spirits.

Mai then sacrifices herself by gifting Maki a cursed sword and empowering her with all their cursed energy so she can reach her potential and fulfil her destiny by destroying the clan. Maki proceeds to kill their father.

Now, it’s on, as Maki massacres every member of the Kukuru guard. In response, powerful sorcerers are sent, but she wipes them out too, leading to a major confrontation with Naoya.

He punches her over and over, at great speed, but Maki is even faster, and when he endeavours to punch through her head, she counters by smashing through his skull.

Maki finds her mother and cuts her throat, but before she bleeds out, mom stabs Naoya to death, then dies herself. Maki carries Mai’s now lifeless body out of the compound, and continues her mission to destroy the Zen’in clan.
