Bobbi Althoff told Drake that him publicly blocking her after their first podcast together ended up losing her a ton of guests that she had lined up.

Back in 2023, Drake joined Bobbi Althoff’s ‘The Really Good Podcast’ for a rare conversation. Naturally, the podcast gained plenty of attention online. However, it was removed from YouTube shortly after going live. 

The pair appeared to have a falling out over it, unfollowing each other on social media, sparking plenty of theories from fans. Yet, they have patched things up now.

Drake appeared on Althoff’s new podcast, Not This Again, on September 2. Bobbi explained that their falling out came after she’d jokingly sent the Canadian rapper a message while he was on stage in LA, prompting him to block her. 

Drake fallout cost Bobbi Althoff big guests

That caused her to lose quite a few interviews that she had lined up for the now-defunct ‘The Really Good Podcast’ as well. 

“I didn’t take you off,” Drake said. “That’s how people saw it. I had so many interviews lined up where afterwards, once you unfollowed me and blocked me, everything it was just pulled,” Althoff explained. 

“There were ones that I had that were scheduled back in the day. They pulled right after. People were like ‘why would I? If he doesn’t f*ck with her, I’m not going to’ and it was like pretty hard after that.”

The podcast host noted that people assumed that she and Drake had a “bigger” problem given the public falling out, which didn’t help her either. 

“People thought it was bigger too than it was. People thought what happened between us was something like… I don’t know,” she added. 

The two of them have clearly patched things up, but who knows if Bobbi will get her guests back.
