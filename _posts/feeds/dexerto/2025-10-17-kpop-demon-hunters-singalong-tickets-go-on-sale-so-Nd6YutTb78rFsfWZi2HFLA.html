KPop Demon Hunters is returning to the big screen again with the singalong version of the animated movie, but you’ll have to be quick to get tickets.

For anyone living under a rock, KPop Demon Hunters follows a hit girlband, Huntr/x, who moonlight as demon slayers when they’re not performing onstage, and use their music to strengthen the barrier that protects our world from the demon realm.

KPop Demon Hunters has already smashed through several Netflix viewing records, and the streamer is already weighing up plans for sequels and a live-action remake.

In fact, Netflix is partnering with several theater chains to release a singalong version of the film in theaters around the world in October, which makes it a perfect Halloween movie, so here’s how you can get tickets.

How to get tickets for KPop Demon Hunters singalong screenings

Netflix is partnering with AMC, Regal, and Cinemark in the US from October 31 to November 2 to host K-Pop Demon Hunters singalong screenings. Tickets go on sale on on October 17 from 6am PT, so you’ll have to be quick!

You can find your nearest screening here to book tickets, but be quick because these screenings will sell out. The previous singalong screenings in August sold out quickly as fans flocked to theaters to relive the adventure all over again.

There will also be showings in select theaters in the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain, Germany, Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Korea, Australia, and New Zealand, but you’ll need to check your local theater times.

All showings will have lyrics onscreen so you can join in with Mira, Rumi, and Zoey as they fight the Saja Boys.

If you really want to get involved, Netflix wants to see fans in their best cosplay outfits (it is Halloween, after all!) and they’ve partnered with Spirit Halloween to create official costumes.
