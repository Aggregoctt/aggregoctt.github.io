Kai Cenat has revealed that LeBron James will be the one to cut his hair during Mafiathon 3 if he achieves his massive goal of 1 million Twitch subscribers.

Celebrities have been increasingly getting more involved with the world of Twitch, with Kai Cenat being right at the front of that.

The AMP star has linked up with the likes of John Cena, Kevin Hart, Nicki Minaj, Michael B Jordan, and others at times. He’s even tried to have Drake on stream as well, but that has been shelved for a bit. 

To kick off his third and final Mafiathon, his signature Twitch subathon, Kai had Kim Kardashian appear as a guest. However, while other big names might be planned, his ultimate guest could appear if he hits 1 million subscribers.

Kai Cenat reveals Lebron could join Mafiathon 3

That’s right, not only will Kai finally cut his hair if he reaches the astounding milestone, but he’ll also linked up with LeBron James live on Twitch. 

“Once we hit a million, the person that will be shaving my head will be LeBron James! LeBron James! When we hit a million, LeBron James will come in on day 30,” Kai told viewers after he started to zero in on the 100,000 subscriber mark. 

“Not only will we have a full stream with LeBron James, but LeBron James will be the one to cut my entire hair.”

Kai also announced that if he breaks his previous record of 728,535 subscribers, he will also go skydiving on stream. 

“If, when I break it, that world record, you guys will be locking in skydiving. I will skydive on stream,” he told viewers. “I’m skydiving on stream. Okay? I’m cooked! I told myself in the first Mafiathon that I was not going to do this, but here we are. Okay? I will skydive on my stream if we get it.”

There is a long way to go for Kai to reach either of those goals, but if there’s anyone who can do it, its him.
