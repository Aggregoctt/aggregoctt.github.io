Can you believe the Single’s Inferno Season 5 finale is just around the corner? It’s hard to predict who the final choices will be, so be sure to tune in when Episodes 11 and 12 drop on Netflix. 

This year saw some changes to the format of the Netflix dating show, including the ‘truth or drink’ game, which eliminated the option to drink instead of answering a question. The Season 5 cast is also the biggest yet, meaning even more connections (and plenty of drama).

That has also meant more love triangles (or, in some cases, squares), one of the most talked about being between Choi Mina Sue, Song Seung-il, and Kim Min-ji. However, this all changed in Season 5 Part 3 when a new round of Paradise dates led to some unexpected choices.

Soon enough, we’ll find out who chooses who for their final decisions and which couples will be leaving Inferno hand-in-hand. So, here’s the exact release date and time of Single’s Inferno Season 5 Part 4. 

Following its four-episode debut, Single’s Inferno Season 5 Part 4 drops on Netflix on Tuesday, February 10, 2026 at 12am PT/3am ET. Part 4 consists of Episodes 11-12.

We’ve listed several timezones below so you know exactly when to tune in: 

  • 5am Brazil
  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Time
  • 1:30pm India Standard Time
  • 7pm Australia
  • 9pm New Zealand

Unlike scripted Netflix originals, the streaming service tends to drop reality shows in batches to ensure maximum engagement over several weeks rather than one short binge window. 

By splitting seasons into parts, Netflix can build anticipation around cliffhangers and twists, while encouraging discourse on social media (of which there’s always a lot).

How many episodes are there?

We now know that Single’s Inferno Season 5 will have 12 episodes in total. 

Even though Season 1 had just eight episodes, this number steadily increased as the show gained popularity. Season 2 had 10, there were 11 in Season 3, and Season 4 bumped this up to 12. 

Full release schedule

It feels like just yesterday that Season 5 premiered, but now it’s coming to a close, with the remaining release schedule as follows:

  • Episode 11 – February 10, 2026
  • Episode 12 – February 10, 2026

What happened in Single’s Inferno Season 5 Part 3?

A lot happened in Single’s Inferno Season 5 Episodes 8-10, starting off with the latest round of Paradise dates. Choi Mina Sue picked Song Seung-il, but through their time together, they figured out that they weren’t compatible. 

Seung-il grew frustrated with Mina Sue’s indecisiveness, as she repeatedly expressed interest in other guys. This helped him to make his mind up and realize Kim Min-ji is the one for him. 

Throughout the course of the episodes, we found out Min-ji was hurt with Seung-il’s disregard for her feelings. They finally cleared the air and let each other know that they’re each other’s top choice.

As such, when Min-ji won the next challenge and got first choice for who to go to Paradise with, it seemed obvious she’d pick Seung-il… except she didn’t. In a totally unexpected twist, she chose Lim Su-been (after asking Park Hee-sun first). 

Even though it was just a lighthearted decision, the pair got on surprisingly well during their date, although they both still have feelings for their respective top choices. 

Elsewhere, Kim Go-eun went to Paradise with Shin Hyeon-woo, but he came on a little strong, pushing her more towards Woo Sung-min and game changer Jo I-geon. 

Although she has interest in both, it seemed by the end of Part 3 that she’s leaning more towards I-geon. Things continued to go in the right direction when I-geon won the next challenge, choosing Go-eun as his Paradise date. 

Since Hyeon-woo came second in the challenge and Go-eun is the only one he’s interested in, he pulled an unprecedented move and decided to not go to Paradise. 

In the earlier set of Paradise dates, Lee Joo-young chose Kim Jae-jin and the pair had a great time together, so much so that the Single’s Inferno hosts couldn’t stop smiling while watching their interactions. 

As for Mina Sue, it seemed she’d switched from Seung-il to Su-been, but when realizing Hee-sun is his top pick, she decided she’s interested in the one guy who has had her on his mind the entire time: Lee Sung-hun.

But it may have been too late, as Hee-sun ended up choosing Sung-hun for her Paradise date and they discovered they have far more in common than they realized. 

In the preview for Single’s Inferno Season 5 Part 4, we see Seung-il seemingly telling Hee-sun that Min-ji and Su-been shared a bed in Paradise (which we know is true, but only because he fell asleep there while they were chatting). 

The next clip shows Sung-hun flirting with Hee-sun, before cutting to Mina Sue asking Sung-hun what he spoke to Hee-sun about during Paradise. 

Then, Go-eun asks Hyeon-woo why he didn’t pick anyone to leave Inferno with, to which he says he’s not interested in anyone but her. Go-eun has a difficult decision to make, as Sung-min and I-geon let their feelings for her known too. 

Finally, we see all the contestants together, just before making their final decision.
