Oreo just went full Thanksgiving, and yes, that means turkey-flavored cookies are real now.

The cookie giant has unveiled a special holiday tin packed with seven bizarre, dinner table-inspired flavors, pushing the brand’s experimental streak to a whole new level. Fans who thought Coca-Cola Oreos were wild are in for a shock.

Oreo’s Thanksgiving Dinner Tin includes two cookies each of: Cranberry Sauce, Pumpkin Pie, Turkey & Stuffing, Creamed Corn, Sweet Potato and Caramel Apple Pie.

The pies make sense. Cranberry sauce? Sure. But turkey and creamed corn? That one has the internet squinting.

“Keep everything else I just want the can,” one fan remarked.

“Who asked for this? Straight to jail,” another blasted.

Oreo calls its Thanksgiving cookies a “flavor experiment”

As weird as the cookies may sound, Oreo is taking this as an opportunity to try new things and knows that these flavors aren’t exactly what people think of when it comes to cookies.

“This flavor experiment is not your typical Oreo indulgence, it’s a chance to try something unforgettable and help shape the future of flavor innovation,” the company said, calling the lineup a “bold taste journey.”

In other words, brace yourself, not just for the flavors, but the cost, too. This tin will set you back $19.99, and that’s not including shipping.
