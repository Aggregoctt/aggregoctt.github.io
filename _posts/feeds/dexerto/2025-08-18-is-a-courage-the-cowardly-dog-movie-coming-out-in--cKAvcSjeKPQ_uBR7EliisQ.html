Hullabaloo has emerged from a small corner of social media: according to a post, there’s a new Courage the Cowardly Dog movie coming out in 2025. But is it true?

While he was only on TV for three years, Courage the Cowardly Dog was an iconic staple of Cartoon Network’s programming, firmly in the echelon alongside the likes of Foster’s Home for Imaginary Friends, The Powerpuff Girls, and Ben 10.

The original cartoon revolved around the titular dog and his life in a farmhouse in Kansas (in the middle of Knowhere), with his elderly owners, Muriel and Eustace. It is one of the best TV shows the network has ever produced – and, crucially, it was really scary and emotional when it wanted to be.

A possible revival has been discussed for years, so ‘news’ of a Courage the Cowardly Dog movie wouldn’t be hard to believe.

Will there be a Courage the Cowardly Dog movie?

No, a Courage the Cowardly Dog movie isn’t coming out in 2025, nor is any such project in development.

You’ve been duped by AI-generated artwork in a fake news post on Facebook, and one page is to blame: Cine History.

Alongside two images, one showing an elderly couple (one of whom is seemingly Liam Neeson, even though they look nothing alike) and another of a scared dog with a flashlight, it links out to a concept trailer uploaded by Fandom Topics, which has been viewed over 14,000 times.

“Courage the Cowardly Dog is reimagined as a live-action horror-fantasy, turning the surreal comedy of the original into a dark, atmospheric nightmare,” the post reads.

“Blending unsettling visuals, suspenseful storytelling, and moments of bittersweet heart, this imagined adaptation transforms the cult cartoon into a tense, cinematic battle between fear and courage.”

Sorry to break it to 46,000 people who’ve reacted to it, the 1,400 users who’ve commented on it, and the 8,000 people who shared the post, but it’s completely made up.

It’s possible the notion of a revival has been brought on by the interest in Good Boy, a new horror movie that takes place in a haunted house and unfolds from the perspective of a dog.

In 2018, the show’s creator John R. Dilworth revealed he was in negotiations with Boomerang to develop a prequel, but it never came to pass, with Cartoon Network focusing on other projects. In February 2024, he uploaded an animatic version of the pilot to YouTube, titled Goblins of Litter.

It’s not all bad news, though. In 2021, the Mystery Incorporated gang had a crossover with Courage in Straight Outta Nowhere: Scooby-Doo! Meets Courage the Cowardly Dog, an animated movie. It’s available to stream on HBO Max now.
