One Piece Chapter 1167 reveals the aftermath of the God Valley Incident, so here’s what to expect from the upcoming installment.

The infamous incident came to an end in Chapter 1166 after Garling delivered the final blow to Rocks following his defeat by Roger and Garp. This made a huge impact on Loki and Harald. Wracked by grief, Harald decided to become the World Government’s weapon so that Elbaf could coexist peacefully with the rest of the world.

After recovering from God Valley’s battle, Roger found an infant Shanks on his ship. Elsewhere, Garp decided to stay in the Marines to protect the weak. He visited Dragon in prison, who, in turn, declared that he hated his father and would leave the Marines. Garp gave his son the key to escape.

The consequences of Harald succumbing to the World Government and Shanks’ inclusion in the Roger Pirates are revealed in One Piece Chapter 1167.

One Piece Chapter 1167 major spoilers

Titled ‘Ida’s Sons,’ One Piece Chapter 1167 picks up from the last chapter. Harald has officially become a weapon for the World Government. He now destroys pirate crews for them and comes to be known as the Monster in the Mist. Harald’s new rank for the World Government is God’s Blade, similar to Gunko. It’s the lowest of the three ranking titles.  

Chapter 1167 then shows a flashback from 15 years ago. It’s revealed that Shanks was looking for more information on the Mary Geoise and the World Government. He also freed Fisher Tiger from the Celestial Dragons’ clutches. He later met Harald and helped the giant king patch his wounded left eye. 

Back in Elbaf, Ida gets poisoned by someone from Estrid Town. Despite his apparent hatred for the woman, Prince Loki is enraged. He takes revenge on the town and declares: “Ida is my only mother!”
