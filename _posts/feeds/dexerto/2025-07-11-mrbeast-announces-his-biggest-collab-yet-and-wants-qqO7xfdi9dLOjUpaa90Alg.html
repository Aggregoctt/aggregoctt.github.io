MrBeast, the world’s most-subscribed YouTuber, has announced a massive collab and wants “every creator ever” to join in.

Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson is preparing for yet another huge creator collab, almost one year after his massive $1 million challenge featuring 50 of YouTube’s the top creators.

On July 11, the YouTuber tweeted out that he would be hosting the “biggest creator collab” on August 1 and urged anyone who wants to take part to DM him.

However, when users began to comment that they were unable to message him, he followed this up by opening the requests to the replies, further noting that he wants “every creator ever on board.”

Needless to say, the floodgates were opened, and seemingly everyone was left trying their luck at securing an invite.

The internet scrambles to join MrBeast’s “biggest creator collab” yet

Just over an hour after announcing the event, Twitch streamers, YouTubers, TikTokers, and top X accounts spammed the comments to try to get an invitation.

“Yo,” Twitch’s Nmp commented.

“Hi Mr. Beast,” Call of Duty streamer LuckyChamu replied, trying to get Donaldson’s attention.

Even accounts like Creepy.org made their intentions clear: “Just replying in case you need someone to scream in the background.”

As of posting, MrBeast has only replied publicly to FaZe Ronaldo, YouTuber isaacwhy, and Kick star Tectone, confirming he messaged him, but is still looking at the replies.

“Sent over 1,000 DMs so far today,” MrBeast said in response to a Dexerto tweet, adding that he wants every streamer, TikToker, and YouTuber in the world involved in the collab.

Given how big MrBeast’s past videos have been with the likes of IShowSpeed, Logan Paul, Ludwig, MoistCritikal, Pokimane, Kai Cenat, and more, we can only assume that he has something special planned for this one.

Wanting “every creator ever” is a tall order, so it’ll be interesting to see if legends such as PewDiePie, VSauce, and early YouTube pioneers like The Angry Video Game Nerd join in.

Luckily, we won’t have long to wait, as August 1 is only a few short weeks away at the time of writing.
