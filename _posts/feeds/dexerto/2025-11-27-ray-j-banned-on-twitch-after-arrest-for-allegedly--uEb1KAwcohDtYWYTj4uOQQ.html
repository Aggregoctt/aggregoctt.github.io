Ray J has been banned from Twitch after a chaotic late-night stream allegedly ended with him pulling a gun on Princess Love before being arrested by police.

The incident unfolded on November 27 during a Twitch broadcast where Ray J vented about having “the worst Thanksgiving in the f***ing world,” claiming someone was trying to take his kids and that he was being threatened.

As the stream escalated, Ray J appeared to grab a handgun from the table and load it, warning that he was “going to blow this f**king sh*t away” if anyone came toward his door. He then stormed off-camera, yelling at someone off-screen and accusing them of trespassing.

Princess Love soon entered the frame holding their daughter, Melody. The actress repeatedly claimed Ray J had pointed a gun at them, saying, “You pointed a gun at us,” while Ray accused Love and her cousin of being drunk and about to drive while intoxicated.

Ray and Love were married in 2016 and have been in an off-again-on-again relationship for years, with the duo filing for divorce four times, the latest coming in 2024.

Ray J arrested after allegedly pulling firearm live on Twitch

Moments later, a woman and a man entered the home. The man confronted Ray J, sparking another explosion with Ray shouting, “I’ll shoot the f*** out of you,” and warning he’d kill him if he didn’t leave the house.

Police sirens could be heard approaching in the background shortly after the chaos.

According to TMZ, law enforcement responded around 4 AM to a residence in Porter Ranch following reports of a domestic violence incident involving threats. Officers cleared the home and took Ray J into custody. He was booked for making a criminal threat, and his bail was set at $50,000.

Ray J’s Twitch channel now displays a standard message typically used for permabans: “This channel is currently unavailable due to a violation of Twitch’s Community Guidelines or Terms of Service.”

Twitch does not comment on individual bans, so it’s unknown if he’ll ever be allowed back on the platform.

Ray J remains in custody at a Los Angeles-area jail as the investigation continues.
