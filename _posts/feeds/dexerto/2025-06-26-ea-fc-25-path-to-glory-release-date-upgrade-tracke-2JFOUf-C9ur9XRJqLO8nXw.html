Path to Glory cards are returning in EA FC 25 as part of the Women’s Euro 2025 tournament, bringing a new set of special players that upgrade over time.

We’re heading towards the end of EA SPORTS FC 25‘s lifecycle, with FC 26 on the horizon, but there are still plenty of promos coming thick and fast. The Shapeshifters event brought cards with weird and wonderful position modifiers, and the fan-favorite Futties is set to bring the Ultimate Team season to a close.

But with the Women’s Euros due to kick off on July 2, the popular Path to Glory cards are also making a comeback. Here’s everything you need to know about them, including all of the players that have leaked so far.

The Path to Glory team is set to arrive on Friday, June 27, 2025, the same day that Shapeshifters Team 3. Both sets of cards will be available in packs from 10 AM PT / 1 PM ET / 6 PM BST.

It hasn’t been confirmed how long the event will last, so it remains to be seen if a second squad will arrive the following week on July 4.

Upgrades explained

Path to Glory items receive special upgrades based on the nation of the player depicted on the card perform in the real-life Women’s Euro 2025 competition. Check out how it works below:

  • Nation Scores 3 goals in Group Stage: +1 OVR
  • Nation Qualifies for Quarter-Finals: 5*/5* & +1 Alt Position w/ all Roles++
  • Nation Qualifies for the Semi-Finals: +1 PS+
  • Nation Qualifies for the Final: +1 OVR
  • Nation Wins the Final: 99 OVR & Icon Chemistry
