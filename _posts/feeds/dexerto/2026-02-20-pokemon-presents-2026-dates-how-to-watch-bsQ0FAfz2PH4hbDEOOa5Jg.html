Pokemon Presents 2026 is finally on its way after being announced by the company alongside the reveal of a highly anticipated re-release for the Switch consoles.

Throughout the year Pokemon hosts a stream to showcase what they’ve got coming up, often dropping new features for Pokemon Go, Pokemon TCG, and of course, any upcoming games on the Horizon.

Well, this time Pokemon Presents lands on Pokemon Day, meaning there’s going to be plenty to celebrate and, of course, tons of announcements for the franchise. So, to ensure you don’t miss a thing, here’s when the next Pokemon Presents is, how to watch it, and what we already know.

Pokemon Presents will take place on February 27, 2026, at 2 pm UTC, so 2 pm GMT / 6 AM PST / 9 am EST / 3 pm CET.

February 27 is also Pokemon Day, with the company celebrating its 30th anniversary, likely making the stream a pretty big celebration of the franchise.

How to watch

You can watch Pokemon Presents on YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, or TikTok when the stream goes live.

Currently, those streams aren’t live just yet. But when they are, we’ll update this article, so check back soon to tune in.

What’s been announced

Pokemon FireRed and Pokemon LeafGreen for Switch

Pokemon has already announced some upcoming content before the Pokemon Presents, with the upcoming release of Pokemon FireRed and Pokemon LeafGreen versions coming to the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2.

Naturally, this will release on February 27, the day of Pokemon Presents across both platforms.
