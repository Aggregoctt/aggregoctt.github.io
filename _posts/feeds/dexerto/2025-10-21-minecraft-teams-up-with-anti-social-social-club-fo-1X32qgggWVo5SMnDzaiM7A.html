Minecraft and the Anti Social Social Club have officially teamed up for a new clothing collection that blends the world’s most iconic block builder with one of streetwear’s most unpredictable and beloved brands.

Anti Social Social Club, known for its minimal logo, self-aware streetwear style, and tendency to collab with plenty of different games and brands (like Hatsune Miku), is bringing its signature look to Minecraft’s pixelated universe, and the result is a full collection that turns in-game nostalgia into some highly sought-after street-ready pieces, as well as a variety of wild items.

Minecract x Anti Social Social Club reveal brand new merch

The lineup includes a mix of classic and unexpected designs. There are t-shirts featuring the iconic Creeper face as well as a cherry blossom design. Then you can also purchase some adorable cherry blossom pants inspired by one of Minecraft’s newer biomes.

Naturally, as is custom with Anti Social Social Club, there’s plenty of hoodies too, only this time themed with blocky textures and classic Minecraft colors. There are even pixelated camo cargo pants for blending seamlessly into the overworld.

It doesn’t stop at apparel, however. The collection also drops a range of lifestyle items, including mugs, caps, blankets, neck wallets, and a camo backpack. There’s even a steel folding shovel branded with both logos, a tongue-in-cheek nod to Minecraft’s survival mode and digging gameplay.
