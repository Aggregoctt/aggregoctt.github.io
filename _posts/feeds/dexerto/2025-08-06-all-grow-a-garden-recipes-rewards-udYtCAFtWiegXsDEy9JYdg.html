Grow a Garden’s cooking event has been a huge success, allowing players to dish up their favorite meals for the brilliantly named Chris P Bacon. However, you’ll need to know the ingredients before you get the rewards.

Taking place from August 2 to August 16, Grow a Garden players have been given the opportunity to earn some fantastic rewards by cooking up a dish for the resident chef, Chris P Bacon (yes, he is a pig, how did you know?).

However, it’s not that easy, as you’ll need to know all the ingredients, and each recipe has multiple rarities, granting you a higher reward. So, here are all the recipes in Grow a Garden and how to make them.

All recipes

How to cook

Cooking in Grow a Garden is pretty simple once you get the hang of it, just simply follow the steps below:

  • Head to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat in the middle of the world.
  • He’ll tell you what meal he wants you to cook.
  • Then, gather the ingredients above and put them into the cauldron next to Chris.
  • Press the big green Cook button.

It’s worth noting, while he’ll be craving a certain dish you can still make other foods during this time.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and give him his craving, then you’ll get a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth using the best meals you can.
