Marvel Rivals’ Season 3 will be bringing a Summer Party Event as well as new swimsuit skins that fit the occasion.

As we get closer to the release of Season 3, devs have just unveiled a plethora of content coming to the game in a roadmap to keep up the hype. And so far, things are already looking very packed with tons to look forward to.

The new season isn’t just going to bring Phoenix at launch and Blade at a later date, a new map, alongside other exciting additions, but there will also be a limited-time Summer Party event players can take part in.

Below, we’ve compiled all the details you need to know about this event.

The Summer Party Event is set to arrive on July 18, 2025, just one week after the launch of Season 3, as confirmed in the roadmap. Though at the time of writing, it’s currently unknown how long this will be around.

We’ll be sure to keep this section updated once there’s more information.

Summer Party Event skins & details

If the Summer event is anything like previous events, it’s safe to expect that there’ll be a plethora of new quests players can participate in to get XP and rewards. There’s also a chance there could be a limited-time game mode.

Speaking of which, what’s a Summer event without swimsuit skins? It was previously revealed that the swimsuit designs featured in the Marvel Swimsuit Special: Friends, Foes & Rivals comic will make their way to Marvel Rivals.

In a post on X, Executive Producer Danny Koo himself has shared the characters that’ll be getting skins from this new line, which include:

  • Psylocke
  • Luna Snow
  • Thor
  • Loki
