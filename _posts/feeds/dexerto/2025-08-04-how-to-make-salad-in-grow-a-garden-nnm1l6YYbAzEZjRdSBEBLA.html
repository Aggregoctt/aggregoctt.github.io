Grow a Garden has finally introduced cooking as a brand-new feature for players to enjoy, thanks to its current event, allowing you the chance to cook various meals, including a Salad.

When you think of growing plants in a game like Grow a Garden, it’s hard not to think about fixing up a delicious salad. After all, all of the ingredients are easy to plant, and it’s kind of the meal that’s synonymous with gardens.

So, if you’re exploring the new cooking event in Roblox’s Grow a Garden and want to cook up a salad but don’t know where to start, we’ve got all you need to know right here.

How to make Salad in Grow a Garden

Making a Salad in Grow a Garden isn’t as simple as grabbing your cooking utensils and cooking. Instead, you need to follow the steps below:

  • Head to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat in the middle of the world.
  • He’ll tell you what meal he wants you to cook.
  • Then, gather the ingredients above and put them into the cauldron next to Chris.
  • Press the big green Cook button.

If you’ve used the right ingredients, then you’ll get a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth using the best meals you can.
