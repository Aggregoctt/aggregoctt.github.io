The latest set of South Park Quests in Fortnite are given out by Kenny, but all of the descriptions are hidden, leaving players who can’t understand “mph mmph mhh mmph” confused.

Fortnite‘s South Park crossover is in full swing, bringing plenty of skins based on the show’s most popular characters, as well as the Stick of Truth Mythic weapon. On top of all that, there’s also been a steady stream of Quests to complete, rewarding players with Memberberries to unlock the Imaginationland Airship Glider.

Up until now, the challenges have been clear and easy to understand, but Kenny’s makes life much more difficult. Just like in the show, it’s impossible to understand what he’s asking, as his signature orange jacket muffles his words.

So, if you’re wondering how to complete the “mph mmph mhh mmph” Quests in Fortnite, here’s a breakdown of what they all mean.

Fortnite Kenny “mph mmph mhh mmph” Quests explained

Even if you decipher what each of the Kenny clues says, you’re still left with a fairly vague riddle to solve. But they’re actually quite straightforward challenges once you know what to do.

Check them all out below:

I’m an angel keeping watch over the city at night

Visit the tall pylon in Battlewood Boulevard during in any match

All you have to do for this Quest is visit the pylon in the middle of Battlewood Boulevard. We’ve marked it on the map below.

In Loving Memory, Kenny McCormick.

Get eliminated

No matter how strong a player you are, chances are you’ll get eliminated at some point. Luckily, that’s all you have to do to tick off this objective, earning 400 Cred in the process.

I’m being serious. I really, really can’t die

Get revived three different times after being knocked

Kenny is notoriously difficult to kill off permanently, and his third Quest challenges you to be the same. You’ll need to be picked up three times after being knocked.

If you play Duos, Trios, or Squads, this should be fairly easy, as it’s common to be revived by your allies. If you play Solos, you’ll have to use a Self Revive Kit to bring yourself back from the Brink.

Use your ninja star!

Hit 10 headshots using any weapons

This is a challenge you’ll likely complete naturally by simply playing Fortnite and getting kills. However, if you want to maximize your chances, you can’t go wrong with an Assault Rifle or Sniper Rifle to land those precise hits.

Wee! He can fly! He can fly!

Travel 200 meters while in the air

You can only complete this task using the Wingsuit or Jump Pad items. These will launch you high into the air to get around the map quickly.

If you want to do it in one go, try to find a high vantage point, such as a tall hill or one of the floating islands scattered across the map, to jump from.

My followers shall help see me through until I am accepted as a princess by all
