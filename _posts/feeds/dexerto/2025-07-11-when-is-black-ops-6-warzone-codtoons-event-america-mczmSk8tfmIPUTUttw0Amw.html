American Dad rewards leaked ---

The CODtoons event is coming to Black Ops 6 and Warzone, and all of the animated rewards have leaked ahead of time, including a number of American Dad-themed cosmetics.

Call of Duty has been ramping up the number of crossovers in recent weeks. First, Ana de Armas’ Ballerina arrived during Season 4, before Beavis and Butt-Head took over the game with their own event and free skins.

There’s no sign of slowing down, though, as the CODtoons event has been confirmed, which is set to be part of the American Dad collab.

Here’s everything we know about the event so far, including all of the rewards that have been revealed by leaks.

When is CODtoons event in Black Ops 6 & Warzone?

There is currently no official release date for the CODtoons event, although it’s likely to arrive on Thursday, July 17, 2025.

This is when the Beavis and Butt-Head and King of the Dead events are scheduled to end, so we’re expecting it to be replaced immediately. However, it’s possible that there could be a slight break, meaning it might begin the following week on July 24.

Either way, it’s confirmed to arrive during Season 4 Reloaded, and with Season 5 dropping on August 7, we should see it very soon.

CODtoons event explained

CODtoons will bring a series of challenges that must be completed to earn rewards, with specific objectives unlocking a signal prize. This is vastly different from many of the most recent events, which require players to pick up items from defeated enemies or simply earn XP.

Each reward will actually have three different challenges tied to it, relating to multiplayer, Warzone, and Zombies. The good news is that you only need to tick off one to claim the prize, and you can swap between modes.

There is also a Mastery reward, which is only unlocked after you receive every other reward, so it’s well worth trying to grind them all.

Only a handful of rewards have been confirmed so far, such as the TR2 CQB Auto Conversion. Luckily, though, leaker ‘realityuk_’ was able to find the full slate of prizes and their challenges in the game files once Season 4 Reloaded went live.
