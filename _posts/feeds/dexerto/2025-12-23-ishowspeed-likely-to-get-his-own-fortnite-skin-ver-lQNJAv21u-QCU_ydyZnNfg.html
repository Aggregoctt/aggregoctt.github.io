Popular streamer iShowSpeed looks set to get his own Fortnite skin, after he posted a subtle tease to his fans.

Over the last few years, iShowSpeed has emerged as one of the biggest content creators around. He’s showing no signs of slowing down either, as he was crowned the most-watched YouTube Gaming streamer in 2025, with a total of fans glued to his videos for 64.1M hours.

With so much buzz around him, it’s a surprise that he hasn’t joined the likes of Ninja, Kai Cenat, and Mr Beast in getting his own skin in Fortnite. It’s an honor that more and more internet personalities have been blessed with, as Epic looks to attract even more players to the game.

Well, it sounds like this wrong is about to be righted, as Speed has strongly suggested that he’ll be joining the Icon Series line-up.

iShowSpeed fans think he’s teasing a Fortnite skin

After revealing that he would be streaming on December 24 (Christmas Eve), a fan asked what it was going to be about. Speed replied: “Go back to my first stream ever.”

It turns out that his first-ever stream was actually a Fortnite stream, which has fuelled speculation that a skin is on the way. After this post, many trusted leakers latched on, claiming it was “likely” that a collab would arrive, or at least be announced, on Christmas Eve.

HYPEX even went on to say that, if the crossover does happen, Speed will have two Icon skins, as two unknown cosmetics have been found in the files.

This isn’t the first time that iShowSpeed has hinted about coming to Fortnite, either. Back in October, he offered a coy little wink to his fans while on stream, which already had many convinced that some kind of collaboration was in the works.

As for what the Speed skin looks like, we’ll have to wait for December 24 to see if an announcement comes. But if it does come, this could be one of the most popular Fortnite skins to date, given the size of his audience.
