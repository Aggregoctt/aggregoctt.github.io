Roblox’s Grow a Garden has another brand-new event after seeing tons of success from its cooking adventure. This time, you’ll need to give Jack the right plants to grow his beanstalk. But what will you need when he asks for Vegetable Plants?

Grow a Garden may have you planting various flowers and food so you can sell them, make tons of money, and buy even more (often rarer) seeds. But, on occasion, it also features a few exciting events, offering unique rewards and challenges to spice up your gardening.

The latest is the Beanstalk event, which places Jack (fitting, really), who asks players for a certain type of plant in order to grow the beanstalk in the middle of the server. However, what those plants actually are is a mystery. So, here’s all you need to know about Vegetable Plants in Grow a Garden.

All Vegetable Plants in Grow a Garden

There are currently 29 different Vegetable Plants we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden. Each will be accepted by Jack. As we find more, we’ll add to the table below, so check back soon.

What are Vegetable Plants?

Grow a Garden’s Vegetable Plants are exactly what you think they are, they’re vegetables you would normally eat in the real world (though Tomato is technically a fruit irl).

Any vegetables you come across in Grow a Garden will be part of the Vegetable Plant Jack is asking for. However, they all have different rarities and points.

The aim is to get the most points, as that will make the beanstalk grow and give you an exclusive reward when the event ends on August 23. So, it’s always worth working out if you want to grow many common or a few legendary.

How to get Vegetable Plants in Grow a Garden

Luckily, getting hold of Vegetable Plants in Grow a Garden is exactly the same as if you were playing without the event. Make sure you have some money, head over to your Seeds stall, and buy any listed in the table above.

Some will be offered as part of the event seller, like the Romanesco Seed or the Mandrake Seed. For that, head over to the event seller and purchase the seeds, just like you would normally.
