Fortnite has never shied away from big-name collabs, but the Daft Punk Fortnite collab might be its most stylish yet.

For the game’s birthday celebrations, Epic is handing players an interstellar light show headlined by the French duo who made helmets cooler than any superhero mask. Sure, Daft Punk split in 2021, but in Fortnite nothing truly retires.

Here’s what’s in the cosmetic bundle, how much it costs, and what to expect when the robots take over Battle Royale.

Fortnite Daft Punk skins and bundle price

The Fortnite Daft Punk bundle goes live September 25, 2025, priced at 3,200 V-Bucks. Here’s the full breakdown:

  • 2 Skins (1,500 V-Bucks each)
  • 2 Back Blings (300 V-Bucks each)
  • 2 Emotes (Around the World & Get Lucky) – 500 V-Bucks each
  • 2 Instruments (800 V-Bucks)
  • 2 Wraps (500 V-Bucks)

The set nails the duo’s retro-futuristic vibe, with glittering suits, chrome accents, and glowing details that stand out even in Fortnite’s wildest lobbies.

The Stage of an Unforgettable Era event

The real highlight is the Fortnite x Daft Punk Experience, a one-off event launching September 27, 2025 at 2 PM ET / 6 PM UTC.

It’s an interactive concert through Daft Punk’s career. We can expect dazzling neon grids, pyramids of light, and the duo’s iconic silhouettes guiding you through a curated soundtrack of their biggest hits.

Early trailers show players moving through vast digital arenas filled with glowing tunnels and kaleidoscopic dance floors, all synced to the music.

This ties directly into the Fortnite birthday event, which easily makes it one of the biggest anniversary celebrations Epic has staged yet.

That’s everything you need to know about the Daft Punk Fortnite collab.
