The Streamer Awards 2025 has revealed the full list of nominees across 35 categories, celebrating creators, orgs, and events ahead of the live show at The Wiltern in Los Angeles on December 6, 2025, hosted by QTCinderella.

Nominations were opened in October and officially announced on QTCinderella’s Twitch stream on November 16, with categories covering everything from specific game genres to IRL, VTubers, and major honors like Gamer of the Year and Streamer of the Year.

Streamer Awards 2025 livestream

The stream will take place on QTCinderella’s Twitch channel, but you can also watch co-streams on numerous other channels.

The event will begin on December 6 at 3pm PT / 6pm ET / 11pm GMT, with red carpet interviews and live performances, before the ceremony begins.

Tickets for the event are also still available at the time of writing, for those wishing to attend in person.

Streamer Awards 2025 nominees and voting

Here is every category and nominee for The Streamer Awards 2025.

You can vote for each category on the official website. Fan submissions contribute towards the eventual winner. If you are unsure on any categories, you can skip them. Voting will close on Saturday, November 29.
