Fishing games have grown into a dedicated niche of their own. So much so that even mainstream giants like Pokémon, Stardew Valley, and Animal Crossing now treat fishing as a staple activity. But for this list, we’ve focused on games where fishing is the main event.

That doesn’t mean every entry is a hardcore simulation. We’ve considered arcade classics, modern indie hits, deep fishing sims, and console favorites played across PC, handheld, and home systems. While casual players may decide to just keep catching Magikarp, and that’s fine, every game on our list is built primarily around fishing each in their own way.

Here’s our ranking of the best fishing games. With five very different styles at the top, there’s something here for everyone!

Whether playing solo or with a friend in drop-in-drop-out local co-op, you’ll need to cook your catch of the day, then sell a variety of recipes to upgrade your shop, gear, and fishing boat. Combining a playful voxel art style with a sumptuous soundtrack, your journey is split into several distinct story arcs featuring a fully-inhabited town to traverse and the potential for epic encounters with the rumoured Mystical Monsters of Moonglow. With your journal and rod in hand, explore the furthest reaches of the ocean, from frigid glaciers to boiling geysers, and learn to cast nets, set traps and fish through ice, documenting over 100 varied aquatic species while uncovering the secrets of Moonglow Bay.30
