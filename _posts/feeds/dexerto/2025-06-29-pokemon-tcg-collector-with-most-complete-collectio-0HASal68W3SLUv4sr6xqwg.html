A Pokemon TCG collector claiming to have the “most complete” English collection wants $9 million for some of the game’s rarest cards.

David Persin, a well-known collector in the Pokémon TCG community, has bundled together 67 one-of-a-kind cards and listed them on eBay for the eight-figure sum.

Each card included in the listing was originally printed specifically for winners and runners-up of various Pokemon World Championships competitions from 2004 to 2024.

How did Persin come to be the sole owner of so many cardboard trophies? “My wife and I attended every World Championships event from 2006 to 2019, approaching the winners of the events and more often than not purchasing their cards at the event venue itself,” he explained in the eBay listing’s description.

Why sell now?

“Unfortunately, at 64 years of age, my health is not what it once was, and I am committed to refocusing more on my growing gamily,” Persin continued, explaining why he had decided to sell.

As of writing, the listing is still live, with over 30 people having added the collection to their basket. Whether any of these prospective buyers will actually pull the trigger on the $9 million investment remains to be seen.

For genuinely interested buyers, Persin indicates that he’s open to haggling – “Please reach out for my best price and with any questions.” Should anyone go through with the purchase, they’ll need to book a plane ticket to Las Vegas to pickup the cards, as Persin is “obviously not comfortable shipping something of this value.”
