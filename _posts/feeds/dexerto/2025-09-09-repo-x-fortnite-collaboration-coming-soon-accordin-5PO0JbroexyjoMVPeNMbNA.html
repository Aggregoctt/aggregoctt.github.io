Fortnite has yet another big crossover on the way, and this one could bring pure chaos to the island after a new leak suggests the indie hit R.E.P.O. will be arriving in an upcoming update.

For those unaware, R.E.P.O. is a frantic multiplayer horror hit that exploded in popularity thanks to its over-the-top gameplay and rather unique and colorful cast of characters. Games throw players into chaotic horror environments with the sole purpose of locating valuable and likely cursed items to make money.

The blend of chaotic action and quirky designs quickly turned it into a cult favorite, with highlight clips spreading fast across social platforms and many highlighting the similarities between this and the similarly popular Lethal Company. Well, now it’s coming to a new game, Fortnite.

R.E.P.O. is heading to Fortnite says leak

According to renowned Fortnite leakers ShiinaBR and FNBRintel, who shared the news on X, the Fortnite x R.E.P.O. crossover is “coming very soon,” all but been confirming it to be in the pipeline.

Naturally, details are still under wraps as Epic Games hasn’t yet confirmed or denied the upcoming crossover, but Fortnite collaborations usually mean character skins, themed items, and possibly even limited-time modes. After all, R.E.P.O.’s lineup of bold, distinctive designs makes it a natural fit for Fortnite’s locker, so fans can expect at least a few of the most iconic looks to arrive.

With leaks pointing to imminent updates, it seems only a matter of time before the crazy gameplay and horror of R.E.P.O. storm into Fortnite. While there’s no date yet, if the leaks are to be believed, players should keep an eye on the next few patches for official news or the collab itself.
