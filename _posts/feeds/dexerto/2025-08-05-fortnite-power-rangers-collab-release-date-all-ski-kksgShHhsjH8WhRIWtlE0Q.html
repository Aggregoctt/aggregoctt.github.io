The Power Rangers have officially morphed into Fortnite, and it’s looking like one of the most nostalgic crossovers in years.

After weeks of teasing, Epic Games dropped the trailer on August 5, 2025, confirming the collab just in time for Chapter 6 Season 4. From iconic skins to potential themed weapons, this event taps into classic 90s hype with a modern Fortnite twist.

It’s finally time to shout “It’s morphin’ time” before a Victory Royale. Here’s everything we know about Fortnite’s Power Rangers event.

The Power Rangers crossover officially kicks off with the launch of Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 4 on August 7, 2025.

The trailer revealed on August 5 confirmed this as the event’s start date, and the Battle Pass will roll out from day one.

The collaboration is expected to run for the full length of the season, but the The Mighty Morphin Bundle hits the Item Shop mid-August

Power Rangers Fortnite skins

The headline skin is Tommy Oliver (White Ranger) as the Tier 100 Battle Pass reward, with a sleek alternate style based on his Black Dino Thunder look.

There’s also a secret Megazord skin, tied to Battle Pass progression, which pays tribute to the original Mighty Morphin Power Rangers’ Zord.

Additional skins, including the Red, Pink, and Blue Rangers, are coming to the Item Shop, either as standalone outfits or as part of the The Mighty Morphin Bundle. These are all based on the classic 1993 MMPR lineup, with more Rangers possibly being added later if the collab is a hit.

Power Rangers Fortnite cosmetics

While no Power Ranger-themed cosmetics or emotes have been confirmed, leaks suggest we’ll see Power Coin back bling, a Blade Blaster-inspired pickaxe, and emotes that mimic transformation sequences.

There’s also speculation of a Megazord glider or deployable cosmetic tied to event challenges later in the season.
