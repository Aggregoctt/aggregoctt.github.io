Streaming star Amouranth has announced that she will be making a return to Twitch after a few years making bank on Kick.

Kaitlyn ‘Amouranth’ Siragusa joined Kick back in 2023, inking a deal shortly after xQc made history by signing a massive $100M contract.

While the details on her deal aren’t known, she did say in an interview with Dexerto that the lucrative deal “doubled” her income.

Now, on June 19, almost two years to the day that she signed with Kick, Siragusa is departing the green platform and rejoining Twitch.

Amouranth back on Twitch after lucrative Kick deal

In a video posted to X, Amouranth was filmed refueling her car at a 7-Eleven gas station before taking a cell on her cellphone.

“I’m on my way after I refuel a bit. Be there soon,” she says before hopping into her green car with a “Kick Com” license plate.

Moments later, a Spongebob “$38 million later” screen pops up and the green car turns purple, revealing she is rejoining Twitch.

This would mean that she was making $16M per year from Kick, which would line up with her comments about the deal doubling her income.

Amouranth’s return is currently set for June 20. It’s not clear if the streamer will be multistreaming or if she will be streaming exclusively on the Amazon-owned platform.

It’s also not clear yet if Amouranth’s Kick deal has expired or if she’s leaving early. Kick, like many platforms, has moved away from signing creators to contracts and has instead urged them to multistream.

Notably, Asmongold made a ton of money in his first Kick stream, easily eclipsing what he was making on Twitch.
