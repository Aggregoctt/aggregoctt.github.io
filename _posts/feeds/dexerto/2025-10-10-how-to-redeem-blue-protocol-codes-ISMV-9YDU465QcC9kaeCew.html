How to redeem codes

To find the redemption screen in-game, follow these steps after you’ve logged into the game and have created your character:

  • Press the Esc button to bring up the menu, and select the Settings option/cog wheel from the menu on the right.

  • On the next screen that appears, press the Redeem Code button on the right-hand side of the screen.
