Teen Twitch streamer RaKai broadcasted a phone call with his mother, where she demanded that he move back home due to comments he made about fellow streamer QTCinderella.

QTCinderella’s Streamer Awards took place on December 6 to mixed reviews. Many viewers and attendees took issue with her jokes about certain broadcasters during the show, which included remarks poking fun at Twitch star Caseoh’s weight and a quip about RaKai stealing.

“Question: What’s the coolest thing you’ve ever stolen?” she asked him, referencing a moment where he gifted a woman flowers he hadn’t paid for, essentially ‘tricking’ her into walking off with stolen merchandise. The incident caused Twitch to temporarily ban him from their platform, and it’s as-yet unclear how long the ban will last.

On December 8, RaKai reacted to a clip of her question toward him during his own stream, where he lashed out at QT and insulted her over the incident.

“You wanna know what I was gonna say? I was gonna say, ‘Listen, b**ch. What’s the coolest d**k you’ve ever sucked?’ …I was gonna say that sh*t to her face, in front of everybody.”

RaKai’s mother punishes son over remarks toward QTCinderella

RaKai’s remarks didn’t go unnoticed. In fact, his rant got all the way to his own mother, who angrily phoned him during a live stream on December 9 to rip him a new one in front of his viewers.

“Did you really tell that woman that?” she asked during the call, referencing QTCinderella. “Are you losing your mind? …That is not how you carry yourself.

“That is not how you talk to a woman, first of all. I raised you way better than that. So guess what? Your a** is coming home, since you think you’re grown. I’m booking your flight tomorrow.”

Commenters couldn’t help but poke fun at his mom’s lecture, and many agreed with her chosen form of punishment. Even Twitch streamer Silky commented on the situation, saying that RaKai needs guidance from more mature colleagues in the space to keep him grounded.

“I’m not saying he’s right, at all. One, you should not be calling no girl that you know a b**ch. He’s literally a child. He’s an immature child. There’s no maturity in his bones at all,” Silky remarked.
