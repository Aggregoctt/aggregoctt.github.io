After taking over your life, the biggest K-drama-style action film of the summer – KPop Demon Hunters – is stepping straight into Fortnite with a unique limited-time takeover!

It’s not just another cosmetic drop, though. From full character skins to a reworked Horde Rush mode and even creator-made minigames, this collab is bringing the full stage show to Epic Game’s battle royale.

You can expect to see Rumi, Mira, and Zoey front and center – and yes – their moves, looks, and weapons are all here. If you’ve sung the songs or watched the fights, Fortnite now gives you a way to play them.

The collaboration goes live on October 2, 2025, and will run through November 1, 2025.

All skins, emotes & cosmetics

Players can pick up Rumi, Mira, and Zoey outfits, each paired with themed accessories.

The HUNTR/X bundle drops on October 2 in the Fortnite Shop, alongside emotes inspired by the film’s most recognizable moments.

Battle Royale and Blitz quests also unlock exclusive rewards like Rumi’s Sword and Mira’s X-tra Spicy Ramyeon.

Demon Rush mode

Horde Rush returns, but this time reimagined as Demon Rush. Squad up with friends, recruit Rumi, Mira, or Zoey, and fight wave after wave of faceless demons.

Between survival phases, load up on unique KPop Demon Hunters perks. The mode will be live from October 2 until November 1.

Creator-made KPop Demon Hunters games

For the first time, creators can immediately publish collab-themed islands using Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN) and Creative.

Assets include film-inspired backdrops, demon enemies, and the HUNTR/X idols as NPCs. Whether you want to build melee arenas with Rumi’s sword or stage your own neon-lit street showdowns, the tools are live from October 2.
