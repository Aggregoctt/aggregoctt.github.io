Several Pokemon Legends Z-A players are cashing in on their shiny Pokemon, listing them online for sales worth up to $300 USD.

You’ll never forget your first Shiny catch. There’s just something special about snagging a rare Pokemon for your collection and watching it shine in battle. Sure, they’re not always easy to find, but that’s part of the fun.

Pokemon Legends Z-A is packed with Shinies, with different tricks you can use to track them down.

Even though you can get them for free if you put in the effort, Legends Z-A Shinies have started popping up on eBay at staggering prices, as spotted by TheGamer.

Pokemon Legends Z-A Shinies are already being listed on eBay

One listing on eBay is offering a Pokemon Legends Z-A Shiny collection at a whopping $299.00 USD. According to the image’s description, that’s including over 230 Pokemon with perfect IVs/EVs and custom names.

Aside from this one, there are also other listings that sell single Pokémon or smaller bundles, with prices varying depending on rarity. Starters shinies, for example, can be spotted to cost around $8. 

Big favorites like Gardevoir or Garchomp can go for a bit more, while bundles with fully trained stats can reach $100.

Sellers are offering entire accounts or collections of Pokemon once payment is made. 

This isn’t new in the Pokemon community, as people have been doing the same thing with Pokemon GO for years, but it shows just how quickly some players try to cash in.

While some try to shortcut their shiny collection by buying them online, the game makes it totally possible to catch Shinies yourself without risking your account.

Shinies show up randomly, but players have noticed that moving in and out of an area can “reset” spawns and give you more chances.

No shiny disappears permanently. If you don’t spot one, you’ll just have to be patient and keep trying.
