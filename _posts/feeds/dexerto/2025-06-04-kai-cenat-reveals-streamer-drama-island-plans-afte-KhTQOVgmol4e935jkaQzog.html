Kai Cenat has revealed his plans for follow-ups to Streamer University, as he wants to host a Streamer Hunger Games and a 24/7 drama island. However, it is proving tricky. 

At the start of 2025, Kai Cenat revealed that he had plans to help out 150 other content creators with a Streamer University. He made it a reality in May, bringing in some big names to the University of Akron campus to help creators out. It was a massive success for all parties. 

Naturally, fans have been asking Kai about a follow-up. The AMP star was initially hesitant to do a second class for Streamer University due to the “hate” he’d received from picking the creators. He owns the name, though, so no one else can do it.

However, it isn’t Streamer University part two that Kai is currently planning. Instead, he’s got ideas for a Streamer Hunger Games and 24/7 drama island. 

Kai wants to do a Total Drama Island with streamers

“One thing that I will confirm, that I will be doing in the future is a Total Drama Island live with streamers,” the Twitch star said during his June 3 stream.

“Now, we don’t know when this is going to be, but we do know that I can confirm that, you feel me?”

Additionally, the Hunger Games idea is “the most difficult” event that Kai has tried to make a reality. 

“Hunger Games is my most difficult task. Out of everything that I’ve done, Hunger Games is my most difficult task,” he said. “Y’all know I already announced Hunger Games like a year or two ago.”

The ideas aren’t massively new to the streaming world, but Total Drama Island would be a new twist – especially if Kai followed the animated series in using a run-down camp as the setting.

With Kai’s backing, his spin on Hunger Games would likely outdo anything that has come before it. Though, as he notes, he has been working on the idea for a while and it is still yet to come together.
