Pringles is rolling out another wave of its Once You Pop Mystery Boxes for the holiday shopping rush, which features a mystery flavor and one of six possible collectibles.

The release follows a surge of interest from collectors who have already started reselling past boxes on StockX and trading them across Facebook Marketplace.

Each box comes with a Pringles Mystery Flavor and one of six Pringamabobs, a line of crisp-themed characters that have quickly become the centerpiece of the promotion. Pringles says this latest wave includes the biggest drop yet of the Snaxolotl, which has become the most sought-after figure in the set.

The Once You Pop Mystery Box includes two cans. One can holds the limited-run Mystery Flavor crisps. The second can holds a Pringamabob collectible, with six possible characters in rotation. Fans can collect the full lineup or try their luck at pulling the rare Snaxolotl.

Pringamabobs range from the Crispybara to the Crunchback Whale, each tied to a themed personality. Fans have been clipping them to bags, keys, and holiday decorations as part of the promotion’s growing collector scene.

All Pringamabob collectibles

Here’s the full character list featured in the run:

  • Snaxolotl: The rarest pull in the lineup.
  • Crispybara: An explorer character with an adventure theme.
  • Puptato: A dog-themed companion figure.
  • Duckalips: A character known for its oversized duck bill.
  • Crunchback Whale: A laid-back, whale-themed collectible.
  • Pringypus: A playful character designed around party energy.
