Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 7 will deal with the fallout of the ‘Earth X’ reveal, and it could be hiding a shocking cameo, if fans’ theories are correct.

In Episode 6, Peacemaker discovered the horrifying truth behind his seemingly perfect alternate dimension: it’s a world where the Nazis won World War II, and there are no people of color on the streets (it’s still unclear exactly where they are, if anywhere).

So, what does this mean for Adebayo, who was last seen being chased down by an angry white mob? What about Judomaster, who followed Chris into the QUC before it closed?

Whatever happens, James Gunn has assured (and warned) fans that they should expect the unexpected, so you’d better make sure you tune in for Episode 7 before you see any spoilers.

Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 7 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Thursday, October 2.

Unfortunately, if you’re not based in or around the US, you’ll probably have to stay up late or watch it as soon as you wake up (if not, you’ll need to mute everything on your social media platforms). You can find out what time it’s airing in other time zones below:

  • 4am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 6:30am India
  • 11am Australia
  • 1pm New Zealand

How many episodes of Peacemaker Season 2 are there?

There are eight episodes in Peacemaker Season 2.

One episode is being released per week, with Gunn telling his social media followers that “the anticipation and conversation around dropping an episode week to week is part of the fun.”

What happened in Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 6?

Episode 6 was the most important chapter of Peacemaker Season 2 so far, and not just because of the Earth X reveal.

Harcourt, Adebayo, Economos, and Vigilante figured out how to get into the QUC and found the alternate dimension. While Harcourt pretended to be her other self in an awkward car ride with Keith, Vigilante found the other Adrian. They were exactly the same, with one key difference: in Earth X, Peacemaker is Vigilante’s arch-enemy.

When Harcourt arrived at A.R.G.U.S., she was covered in cocaine (Adrian stashed drugs in his basement that blew everywhere when they opened the QUC, no big deal). She was eventually cleared, but this allowed Peacemaker to have another one-to-one with her in the interrogation room.

He confessed that he’s in love with her, which she already knew. She didn’t say it back, but she tearfully asked him to come back with her.

However, everything went haywire when Adebayo decided to go for a walk. Keith caught sight of her, grinding his car to a halt and screaming, “One got out… a Black!”

Meanwhile, Harcourt pointed out to Chris that she hadn’t seen any people of color. He picked up a small American flag and unfurled it, discovering a swastika where the stars should be.

That’s right, it’s a Man in the High Castle situation, which could mean we’ll get David Corenswet as its version of Superman: Overman. Episode 7 is also set to feature another cameo from Superman, though not one you may be expecting.
