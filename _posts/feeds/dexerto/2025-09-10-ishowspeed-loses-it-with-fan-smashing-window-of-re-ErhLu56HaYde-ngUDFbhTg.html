IShowSpeed lost it with one fan who headbutted and broke a window of the restaurant where the streaming star was eating. 

Over the last few years, IShowSpeed has gone on some massive streaming tours. He’s ticked off pretty much all of Europe, Australia, South East Asia, and South America. Oh, and he still has plans for Africa and Antarctica. 

At times, these streaming tours have gotten a little wild. Speed has needed police intervention, his fans have caused tens of thousands of dollars worth of damage to rental cars, and his own personal security has had to physically move viewers away from Speed. 

His tour of the United States has, at times, been pretty wholesome – Speed gifted a holiday to a 71-year-old fan in Detroit. Though, those lively moments have still come across as well.

IShowSpeed fan smashes restaurant window as he’s eating

One such incident happened during his return to Ohio when, as Speed tried to enjoy a meal at a restaurant, fans pretty much surrounded it outside. While the streamer asked for calm, one fan headbutted a window repeatedly, causing it to smash.

Speed wasn’t having that and quickly set about demanding an apology. “Get his ass! Get him right now, thats a damn shame,” the YouTuber shouted as he and security chased after the fan. 

“That’s a damn shame, get him right now! Ain’t no way he just did that. Come on bro. Stop playing man. Where’s he at bro? He got to apologize right now. Where he at bro?”

The YouTube and Twitch streamer was annoyed that the fan had ran off, but returned to the restaurant to pay for the damages.

“I want to pay for the window. I’m going to pay for the window,” he told staff. However, that didn’t prevent fans from further banging on the window to try and get Speed’s attention before leaving.
