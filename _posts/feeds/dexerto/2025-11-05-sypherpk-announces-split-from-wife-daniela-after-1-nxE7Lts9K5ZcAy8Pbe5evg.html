Fortnite streamer SypherPK has announced that he and his wife Daniela are separating after ten years together. Both shared statements on social media confirming the split.

SypherPK, real name Ali Hassan, said the decision came after months of private discussions. “Daniela and I want to share that after 10 years of partnership, love, and life together, we’re moving forward on different paths,” his post read.

“After spending several months navigating this change privately, we felt it was time to share it with those who’ve followed our journey over the years. This was a very difficult decision to make. I’m deeply grateful for the life we built, the memories we created, and the dreams we achieved.”

He added that the two would “continue working together on our shared ventures with respect and care as we move through this transition,” asking fans for “privacy and understanding” as they move forward.

Daniela shared a separate statement of her own, writing, “After almost a decade of partnership in both life and business, Ali and I are moving forward on separate paths.”

She continued, “This was not a change I expected, but I’ve come to meet it with deep gratitude for the love I shared, the lessons I’ve learned, and the dreams we built that shaped so much of who I am today.”

She also confirmed they remain partners in their shared companies, which includes Oni studios, which the couple founded.

“We remain co-owners of our shared ventures for the time being, and we’ll continue to run them with care as we navigate this transition for our companies, our team, and the legacy we’ve created together.”

Daniela closed by thanking supporters and requesting privacy: “Though our paths now diverge, I carry profound appreciation for what was and immense pride in what we accomplished. That chapter gave me strength, clarity, and an even brighter sense of who I am becoming. I kindly ask for privacy as we continue to untangle our lives and move forward with care.”
