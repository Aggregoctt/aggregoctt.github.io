Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson responded to a fan who wants him to pay out money for the number of days that they’ve been a subscriber to his YouTube channel.

Over the years, MrBeast’s lavish spending and generosity has seen him scale the YouTube summit, becoming the biggest YouTuber around.

In the early days, Jimmy only gave out money to his friends and other creators, but has since expanded that to viewers and celebrities too. Many of his newer videos centre on putting viewers to the test in some way, including locking them in a room with their former partners. 

His generosity has also seen Beast Industries, his company, need to create a ‘Jimmy Fund’ for whenever he goes off script and donates money that they haven’t budgeted for.

That was likely needed when a woman on TikTok revealed that she only followed him in the hopes of getting free money. Donaldson was made aware of that and told her to check her messages for $10,000.

It has spawned spin-offs too, with one fan taking to X (formerly Twitter) to ask Jimmy to pay them the monetary value of the number of days they’d been subscribed to his YouTube channel. 

“Hey MrBeast, I been subscribed for 2,843 days! Can I get $2,843?” they asked. “Sure,” the answer came back.

Other fans have also tried their luck with the same formula. “2,537 days for me 😀 Am I getting something?” one asked. “I’ve been subscribed since Bitcoin first came out, can I get 1 Bitcoin?” another commented.

Fans have used it as a way to flex their loyal to MrBeast and his YouTube channels, but he isn’t going to start giving money away to everyone who subscribes. That would be pretty costly.
