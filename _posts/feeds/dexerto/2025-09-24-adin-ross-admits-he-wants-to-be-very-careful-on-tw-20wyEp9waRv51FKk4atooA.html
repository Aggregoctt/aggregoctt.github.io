Streaming star Adin Ross has admitted that he plans to be “very careful” with his return to Twitch and will keep “sketchy” content to Kick.

After two years of waiting, Adin Ross was unbanned by Twitch back in March. His unban followed Twitch making changes on indefinite bans with no end date, giving him the option to split time between Kick and the Amazon-owned platform.

He briefly reappeared on Twitch on March 31, but the Kick co-owner has stuck with the Stake-backed platform otherwise. However, on September 23, he returned to the Amazon-owned platform again for a few hours.

The Twitch stream followed his usual style, but without any gambling, seeing as that content is banned on the purple platform. And, as a result, he is aiming to be “very careful” with what he broadcasts. 

Adin Ross will keep “sketchy” content on Kick

Speaking to fans on Kick, Adin noted that there was a “different energy” on Twitch compared to his old streams. 

“I’ll probably multi-stream or I’ll do some type of Twitch streams with certain streams,” he said. “If there’s streams that I know that I’ll get TOS for somebody incoming on my stream, where I know, for sure… if it’s going to be sketchy, I’m going to make sure we’re on Kick. 

“I don’t want anything happening. I just want to be very careful with who I collab with, with what’s going to be said, because on Twitch you’ve got to walk on tippy toes and that’s not bad.” 

It is something that Adin has said previously, claiming Twitch would be the home for his big collaborations. 

“You’re going to see a different Adin because of what I can do now, I’m not on a leash,” he said back in March. 

It remains to be seen if his Twitch streams will become more regular or if they’ll just be special events, but he did rack up a peak of 31,000 viewers for the return.
