Oreo is launching a new Marvel-themed cookie collaboration featuring 32 unique superhero and villain designs, complete with a color-changing creme.

The MARVEL OREO Stuf of Legends Cookies are set to debut at retailers nationwide on February 2, with presales beginning January 26. The limited-time release spans characters from the Avengers, Spider-Man, X-Men, and Fantastic Four.

According to Oreo, the collaboration includes four special-edition packs, custom illustrations by Marvel Comics artist Todd Nauck, and a record-breaking number of cookie embossments across the lineup.

Every Marvel character featured on the Oreo cookies

In addition to the designs, the cookies introduce a first-of-its-kind color-changing creme that shifts from gray to blue during consumption.

The Stuf of Legends cookies feature 32 distinct embossments showcasing the following characters and logos:

Only three of the four cookie pack variations will initially appear on store shelves, with the fourth pack tied to a fan-driven interactive promotion. Beginning February 2, consumers can scan a QR code on participating packages or visit their website to take part in a digital experience centered around guiding delivery trucks across the U.S.
