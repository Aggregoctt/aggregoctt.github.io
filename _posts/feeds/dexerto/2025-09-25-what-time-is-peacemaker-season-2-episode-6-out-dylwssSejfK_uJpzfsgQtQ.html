Peacemaker Season 2 is about to enter its most secretive stretch, with Episode 6 set to deliver big answers that have been held under lock and key.

In the opening minutes of Season 2, Peacemaker discovered an incredible alternate dimension in the Quantum Unfolding Chamber. There, his father is nice, his brother is alive, and he has a romantic connection with Harcourt. Also, he didn’t murder Rick Flag Jr in that timeline.

Whenever he spends any time there, everything seems perfect; he’s worshipped as a superhero, and everyone seems to love him. After he accidentally killed his alternate self, why wouldn’t he just switch lives?

Well, if the ‘Earth X’ theories are correct, Chris is in for a disturbing awakening in Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 6.

Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 6 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Thursday, September 26.

Unfortunately, if you’re not based in or around the US, you’ll probably have to stay up late or watch it as soon as you wake up (if not, you’ll need to mute everything on your social media platforms). You can find out what time it’s airing in other time zones below:

How many episodes of Peacemaker Season 2 are there?

There are eight episodes in Peacemaker Season 2.

One episode is being released per week, with Gunn telling his social media followers that “the anticipation and conversation around dropping an episode week to week is part of the fun.”

What happened in Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 5?

By the end of Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 5, Chris abandoned his world and moved to the alternate dimension, parting ways with Harcourt, Adebayo, and co. to live out his dreams as a superhero.

This came after he had a heartbreaking conversation with Harcourt, where she made it clear she doesn’t have any other feelings for him (something tells me she’s not telling the truth, and probably resents her own romantic feelings given that Chris murdered her friend, Rick Flag Jr).

He was apprehended by A.R.G.U.S., beaten to a pulp by Rick Flag Sr (remember that knee to the face), and let go after Economos booked him. Feeling like he had nothing else to contribute to his own world (he wrote in a letter that he didn’t believe redemption was possible), he closed the door to the QUC… not realizing that Judomaster followed him in.

However, a plan took shape in the closing seconds, with Harcourt vowing to go and bring him back. The 11th Street Kids are back together, and they may be about to uncover the horrifying truth of the alternate dimension.
