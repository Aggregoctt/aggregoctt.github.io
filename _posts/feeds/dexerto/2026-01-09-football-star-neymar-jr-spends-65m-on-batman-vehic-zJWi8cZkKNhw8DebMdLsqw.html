Brazilian football icon Neymar Jr has gone full Bruce Wayne, splashing roughly $65 million on a fleet of Batman-themed vehicles that includes the iconic Tumbler Batmobile.

The 33-year-old shared photos and videos of the armored Tumbler parked alongside his helicopter and private jet, captioning the post: “Dreams can come true.”

Neymar’s Batman obsession is well known, but this latest spree takes it to another level. Alongside the Batmobile replica, he also picked up a Batman-themed private jet and helicopter, turning his hangar into something straight out of Gotham City.

Neymar Jr becomes real-life Bruce Wayne with $65M Bat vehicles

According to SupercarBlondie, the helicopter is an Airbus H145 valued at around $13 million, while the private jet, a Dassault Falcon 900LX, reportedly cost $50 million. The high-end Tumbler replica itself set Neymar back about $1.5 million.

As reported by MotorBiscuit, the Tumbler is far from a static display piece. It packs a 6.2L LS3 V8 producing 525 horsepower and 486 lb-ft of torque, paired with a GM 4L85E transmission. Built from Kevlar, carbon fiber, fiberglass, and sheet metal, the vehicle weighs roughly 5,500 pounds.

Considering Neymar made a fortune playing in Saudi Arabia, this likely isn’t the end of his Batman collection.

And if the next DC film featuring the Caped Crusader introduces a new show-stealing ride, don’t be surprised if the Brazilian star adds yet another piece of Gotham to his garage.
