Grow a Garden’s next event is here, with Fall Market bringing a little bit of seasonal celebration to our crops, pets, and…well, gardens.

Roblox’s Grow a Garden is no stranger to great events, whether it’s growing beanstalks, cooking up delicious meals, or grabbing rewards in the wishing well. Each event offers something completely different for the game’s massive player count to get stuck into and grant some handy exclusive rewards.

Well, a brand new one has landed, this time focusing on the fall season, so expect orange leaves, spooky vibes, and more in the adorable Fall Market event. Here’s all you need to know about it so you can make the most of the adventure.

The Grow a Garden Fall Market event will be taking place from Saturday, September 13, 2025, at 3 pm BST / 7 am PT until Saturday, September 20, 2025, at 11 am BST / 3 am PT.

Currently, as announced by the game, the event will be running for a week, but usually these events are extended by another week, so we expect it to last until September 27. But we’ll update this article if that’s confirmed.

Features

As announced by Grow a Garden on X, we have a taste of the event’s key features. We know that the Fall Market will bring in “tons of new seeds, pets, gear, weather, and much more throughout the event,” so expect to see plenty of Fall-inspired seeds (maybe Jack’O’Lanterns), spooky pets, and so much more.

As seen in many other events, we expect it to be like the Fairy Event or the Bee event, in which players will be able to offer their crops to a certain NPC who, once an hour, will activate a weather event, mutating your plants.

However, nothing has been announced outside of the tweet yet, so check back soon for a full rundown on how the event will work.

What’s more, the X account also detailed: “We will be adding and revising some features too,” so there’s likely to be changes to the base game on top of the new additions.
