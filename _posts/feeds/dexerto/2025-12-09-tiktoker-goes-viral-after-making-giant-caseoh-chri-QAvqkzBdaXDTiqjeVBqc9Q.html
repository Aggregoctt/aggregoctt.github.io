TikToker ilimadee has gone viral on the app for making a giant Christmas ornament shaped like CaseOh, and she even made a talking one for his mom.

The crafter posted the first video showing off her custom ornament on November 8, 2025, and it quickly gained attention from other fans of the streamer.

In a follow-up, she thanked viewers for “all the love and support” on her first video and revealed that she decided to make the ornament because he “genuinely helped me get through so much this year.”

The TikToker also made it possible for people to order one of their own, but they quickly sold out as she only made a few available.

Even CaseOh’s mom wants streamer ornament

As an avid supporter of her son, CaseOh’s mom was quick to leave a comment requesting one of her own.

Ilimadee replied: “Omg, of course! dm me”

But the TikToker didn’t want to send his mom just a regular ornament… so she made one even bigger that says some of CaseOh’s most popular sayings. To go with it, she also made the streamers mom a tiny version of the ornament.

Ilimadee isn’t the first TikToker to go viral after making a CaseOh-themed craft, either. Back in August, TheCrochetGinger gained the streamer’s attention after designing and crocheting a giant CaseOh plushie that weighs five pounds.
