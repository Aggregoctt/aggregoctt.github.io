David Dobirk has revealed that his home was robbed while he and some of his friends were there, even showing off security camera footage of the intruders. 

No matter if you’re a regular Joe or an online celebrity, making sure your home is secure is a pretty key thing. There are, of course, plenty of ways to make things as safe as you can, but if thieves want to get in, then they probably will. 

David Dobrik has plenty of security around his Los Angeles home, including cameras that record everything and guards, but he suffered a break-in while he and some of his friends were there. 

In his August 12 vlog, the YouTube star revealed some of the security footage, showing at least three thieves inside his home. “Security is already on the phone with you guys,” Dobrik said to police as he showed a clip of the robbers running away. 

David Dobrik shows off aftermath of home intrusion

The trio of thieves could be seen running through his backyard as two LAPD squad cars arrived at the YouTuber’s home. He also had a police helicopter involved. 

Dobrik told his friends that the robbers had been into his home and showed off some of the aftermath as they had ransacked his closet and drawers. “They f**king trashed the place,” one of his friends said. 

The YouTuber added that he has 24/7 security out front of his home, but the three intruders snuck in through the backyard – which was the spot that they eventually exited from too. 

Another of his roommates assumed that it was a “bit” from Dobrik, and didn’t think the intruder situation was actually real. Of course, he was mistaken. 

The YouTuber has yet to reveal if the thieves made off with anything valuable during their hurried escape. He may, however, need to shell out to up his home security.
