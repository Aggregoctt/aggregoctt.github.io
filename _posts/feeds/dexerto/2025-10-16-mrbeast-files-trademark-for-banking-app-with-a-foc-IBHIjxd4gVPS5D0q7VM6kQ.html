MrBeast may be expanding his empire into finance. The YouTube creator has filed a trademark application for “MrBeast Financial,” covering a wide range of money-related services including banking, investing, lending, and cryptocurrency.

According to filings submitted to the United States Patent and Trademark Office on October 13, 2025, the application covers “downloadable software in the nature of a mobile application for banking services,” as well as features such as short-term cash advances, cryptocurrency exchange services, investment management, and financial education.

The trademark also includes “online banking services, cash advance services, microfinance lending, cryptocurrency payment processing, investment advisory services, and issuing of credit and debit cards,” among other financial tools.

Application still pending

The filing does not confirm that MrBeast is officially launching a financial product, only that his team has applied for the name. However, the application is currently awaiting approval, and the YouTuber hasn’t revealed anything.

MrBeast, whose real name is Jimmy Donaldson, is the world’s most-watched YouTuber, with more than 445 million subscribers.

Known for large-scale challenge videos and philanthropy, he has built a multimillion-dollar brand that spans food, merchandise, and entertainment ventures like Feastables, MrBeast Burger, and Beast Games.

Season one of Beast Games was a massive success for the YouTuber, and Amazon Prime quickly signed him for a second season of the show.
