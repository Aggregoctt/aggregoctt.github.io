A brand new Lofi Girl Cafe appears to have just landed in Paris, inviting fans to sign up and come visit “A cozy place in the heart of Paris to take a break, work and relax.”

Ever sat listening or watching Lofi Girl on YouTube and wished you could be sitting in a cafe with a hot drink, studying, working, or just reading a good book? Well, you’re not alone. There’s something cozy about listening to Lofi while completing your tasks, and doing so with others makes it all the better.

Well, those dreams can finally come true after an adorable Lofi Girl cafe in Paris has been spotted opening soon.

How to find and sign up for Paris’ Lofi Cafe

As spotted by various fans, a Lofi Cafe is opening in Paris, specifically located at 60 Rue Saint-Denis, 75001 Paris, France. This is pretty much bang in the middle of Paris, just across the river from Notre Dame.

While it’s not currently open, if you do find the location, it will have the classic Lofi Girl backdrop with a QR code allowing you to sign up and be notified when it’s open, showing it likely doesn’t have too long left until you can order a hot drink and listen to some beats (hopefully the ginger cat will be there too).

Thanks to the website and an official reply from Lofi Girl on X acknowledging the new Paris Cafe, it’s confirmed to be an official collaboration by the team of Lofi Girl.
