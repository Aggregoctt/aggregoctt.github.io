The first-ever Pokemon and Lego collaboration was announced back in September 2025, and fans went wild for the upcoming sets. Well, now you can finally preorder them, and it’s already broken the internet.

It’s no secret that Pokemon and Lego fans have been calling for a collab for years now. Being able to build their favorite Pokemon or something from the popular anime and video games was top on many builders’ wishlists. So, naturally, when the announcement came, many were thrilled, anticipating what the sets would be.

Well, now we finally know, and three of them have come up for preorder. You just have to be fast if you want to bag them.

New collab leaves Pokemon & Lego sites in major queues

Due to the preorders being so popular, as soon as they dropped on the official Lego and Pokemon site, queues ran rampant. Fans can jump onto the website but will have to wait for around 5 minutes until they’re allowed to enter, likely due to immense popularity.

When you’re in, you’re able to preorder the following:

  • Pikachu and Poké Ball: £179.99
  • Eevee: £54.99
  • Venusaur, Charizard, and Blastoise: £579.99

Each is up for preorder, but the largest set (a whopping 6,838 pieces) is the Venusaur, Charizard, and Blastoise build, and it comes with an exclusive additional gift if you preorder it. This comes in the form of a Lego Pokemon Kanto Region Badge Collection, which is 312 pieces and is currently unavailable to buy anywhere else.

Whether the set becomes available to buy on its own after the preorder date is yet to be seen, but given the queues and popularity, you’ll have to be quick if you want to buy ’em all.
