Police in New York responded to reports of a “masked suspect” roaming a neighborhood, only to discover the suspect was actually a deer with its head stuck inside a plastic bag.

The unusual call came in on December 28, 2025, prompting officers to investigate what was initially believed to be a person wearing a mask and moving through the area.

According to the Ramapo Police Department, officers arrived on the scene to find a deer lying in the snow with an oversized plastic bag covering its head. The department later shared a video of the encounter on Facebook, documenting the moment the animal was safely freed.

Police realize the “masked suspect” was a deer

In the clip, officers cautiously approach the deer before one officer gently removes the bag. Once freed, the deer immediately jumps to its feet and runs off into a nearby wooded area.

The police department joked about the mix-up in the video’s caption, saying the subject “turned out to be a deer disguising itself as a bag.” Officers added that they “sprang into deer-tective mode,” removed the disguise, and released the suspect back into the wild “on its own hoof.”
