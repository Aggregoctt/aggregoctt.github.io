Roblox YouTuber KreekCraft says he has been swatted and received death threats over a game he claims has not even been officially announced.

The creator shared the claims in a post on February 18 after a Roblox news account circulated a screenshot suggesting he had released a new game called Sail For Brainrots. KreekCraft responded by saying the project was not fully released and had not yet been revealed by him publicly.

The post included a screenshot showing a Roblox game page for Sail For Brainrots, listed under the creator name Tommy Tides, which is a community owned by KreekCraft.

“I appreciate the discussion around the game, but it’s not even fully released yet (or announced lol), and I’ve already been,” he wrote before listing what he says happened next: being swatted, receiving death threats, and having people attempt to dox his family.

In his post, KreekCraft said the attention around Sail For Brainrots escalated before he had the chance to introduce the game properly.

“I knew Twitter was crazy, but at least let me announce it first,” he added.

Who is KreekCraft?

KreekCraft is a popular Roblox-focused YouTuber and livestreamer known for covering Roblox updates, events, and gameplay content. He has built a large audience through daily streams and commentary around the platform’s biggest trends, often spotlighting new games and creator projects.

His name regularly appears in Roblox community discussions, with fans closely tracking new projects or collaborations tied to him.
