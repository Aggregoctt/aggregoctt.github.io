Popular Twitch streamer shroud left the internet disgusted after showing off his nasty gaming mouse.

Former CSGO pro Michael ‘shroud’ Grzesiek is one of the top FPS streamers, frequently dominating almost any game you put in front of him with his pinpoint accuracy, smart tactics, and fast reflexes.

With his ultra-competitive mindset, it’s no surprise that his hands would get as sweaty as his gameplay and he showed off the effects of that during a broadcast.

While taking a little break, he picked up his mouse, examined it, and grossed out viewers by unveiling the state of his device with some convinced a “living organism” was growing on it.

Twitch star shroud’s mouse goes viral after disgusting the internet

Showing his mouse to the camera, the Polish-Canadian asked his viewers, “okay, be honest. Is this gross or is this normal? I just don’t know.”

The sticky, dirty mouse was covered in a nasty substance, leading fans watching to demand he clean it.

“I was playing WoW earlier and my mouse pad got stuck on something and this part fell off while I was playing. All the grime that was in the back was on the front and I rubbed it into my mousepad. All of it. Just so gross,” he explained. “Now my mousepad has streaks in it.”

Footage of the stream instantly went viral across X with fans voicing their displeasure with the gaming hardware.

“Shroud’s mouse is basically growing a living organism, is this normal?” asked popular account Modern Warzone.

“It’s just gunk from your hands and happens with controllers too but THAT AMOUNT OF GUNK is f**king crazy,” another chimed in.

“This is not getting enough hate. I streamed full time for 3+ years using the same mouse and never had to clean it like that,” streamer RLaw commented. “Did longer subathon than shroud has done as well. I can’t think of a single excuse for this other than lack of hygiene.”

Shroud is hardly the first streamer to trigger viewers’ gag reflexes. Asmongold has routinely left the internet baffled by going months without showering and had dead animals in his house at one point.

Grzesiek’s mouse is probably a good indicator for anyone playing a lot of PC games, if it gets dirty like that, you should consider giving it a good cleaning. Otherwise, it may impact your gameplay, and no one wants that.
