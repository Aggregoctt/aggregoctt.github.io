Popeyes has launched a new set of Streamer Bundles in collaboration with seven popular creators, offering limited-time meals that can only be ordered online.

The bundles are available for one week only through the Popeyes app and Popeyes.com for delivery or in-restaurant pickup. They do not appear on in-store menus. The launch also includes a giveaway of 400 Twitch subscriptions per streamer.

Alongside the creator meals, Popeyes is promoting its Build Your Own Bundle option, which lets customers choose three proteins and two sides for $20. The BYOB deal is always available through Popeyes’ digital channels, while the Streamer Bundles are exclusive to this limited run.

What are the Popeyes Streamer Bundles?

The Streamer Bundles are tied to seven creators, each with a custom meal featuring Popeyes chicken, sides, and sandwiches.

  • The Cinna Bundle includes two Chicken Sandwiches, six boneless wings, Homestyle Mac & Cheese, and Cajun Fries.
  • The Extra Emily Bundle features a three-piece tenders order, a Spicy Chicken Sandwich, two pieces of Signature Chicken, Homestyle Mac & Cheese, and Cajun Fries.
  • The Agent Bundle comes with three pieces of Spicy Signature Chicken, three Classic Tenders, a Spicy Chicken Sandwich, and two regular Cajun Fries.
  • The Lacy Bundle includes three pieces of Signature Chicken, six bone-in wings, a Chicken Sandwich, regular Mashed Potatoes, and Homestyle Mac & Cheese.
  • The RaKai Bundle consists of three pieces of Classic Signature Chicken white meat, six Classic Boneless Wings, three Classic Tenders, Homestyle Mac & Cheese, and Cajun Fries.
  • The Tori Bundle offers six bone-in Lemon Pepper Rub Wings, three Spicy Tenders, a Chicken Sandwich, Homestyle Mac & Cheese, and Cajun Fries.
  • The CookingWithKya Bundle includes three Classic Tenders, a Spicy Chicken Sandwich, three pieces of Spicy Signature Chicken, Homestyle Mac & Cheese, and Cajun Fries.

Each bundle is available for a limited time and can only be ordered digitally, with Popeyes encouraging fans to order through the app or website before the promotion ends.
