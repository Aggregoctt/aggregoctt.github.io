The Assassin is one of the strongest damage classes in Solo Leveling Arise Overdrive, but he only reaches that level when you build him around back attacks, crit stacking, and perfect repositioning. With the right weapons and skill path, he turns every fight into a constant cycle of overdrive bursts that melt bosses before they even land a hit.

Best Assassin build

This build focuses on maximizing mobility, abusing extreme evasion, and loading overdrive as fast as possible. Once everything lines up, this class stops being fragile and becomes a relentless back-attacking machine.

Here’s what defines the best Assassin build:

  • Weapons: Razan’s Blade IV and Knight Killer IV
  • Class skills: Shadow Dash, Vertical Arts, Ambush chain, Magnifying Slashes
  • Stat priority: Agility, perception, vitality, strength
  • Blessing Stones: Sharp Perception, Mana Rampage, Kasaka’s Steel Scales
  • Strengths: Huge crit windows, unmatched mobility, fast overdrive generation
  • Weaknesses: Weak early game, heavily timing dependent

How to play Assassin

Assassin is all about staying behind the target, loading overdrive, and unloading it at the right moment. You use extreme evasion and shadow step to reposition, trigger cooldown resets, and keep your damage high. Vertical Arts, Shadow Dash, and Kasaka’s teleport all exist to put you behind the boss faster so you can stack crits, refresh internal wound, and chain-smash without losing momentum.

Always attack from behind, dash often, and switch weapons to trigger chain smash whenever possible. Your entire kit revolves around crit windows and back-attack bursts, so keep agility and perception high, let your blessings handle crit reliability and damage, and use defensive penetrations and vitality when content gets harder. Once everything lines up, you stop feeling fragile and start looping overdrive almost nonstop.
