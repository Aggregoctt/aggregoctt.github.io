Taco Bell is adding a new twist to one of its most popular menu staples with the launch of the Steak & Poblano Rolled Quesadilla, rolling out nationwide for a limited time starting November 20, 2025.

The new item marks the national debut of poblano peppers on Taco Bell’s menu, combining fire-roasted poblano flavor, marinated steak, melted cheese, and Spicy Ranch sauce — all wrapped in a warm, portable rolled quesadilla format.

The brand says the new offering “reimagines comfort food” with a bolder, on-the-go design inspired by fan-favorite quesadillas.

Poblano flavor takes the spotlight

Taco Bell’s Chief Marketing Officer, Luis Restrepo said the debut celebrates the distinct flavor of the poblano pepper in a fresh way. “With the debut of the Steak & Poblano Rolled Quesadilla, we’re celebrating the unique flavor profile of the poblano pepper in a new form,” Restrepo said.

Fans can also grab the new item as part of the $9 Discovery Luxe Cravings Box, which includes:

  • One Steak & Poblano Rolled Quesadilla
  • One Doritos Locos Tacos
  • One Crunchy Taco
  • Chips and Nacho Cheese Sauce
  • A medium fountain drink

The promotion arrives alongside a limited-edition merch drop. Taco Bell will release 300 exclusive hand-loomed “Poblano Sweaters” on December 9 through its Taco Bell Rewards app. Members can enter the Tuesday Drops page from 2–3 p.m. PT for a chance to win.

The Steak & Poblano Rolled Quesadilla and Discovery Luxe Cravings Box will be available nationwide for a limited time at participating locations starting November 20.
