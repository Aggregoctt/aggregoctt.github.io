Twitch has released its recap for 2025, offering insights to viewers, streamers, and the community as a whole.

As the new year gets closer, websites and media platforms begin to offer a recap that shows how you used the site over the previous year.

Spotify Wrapped started it off, but now the likes of YouTube and Apple Music have joined the end-of-the-year trend.

Twitch’s recap for 2025 has been released for users to check out. Here’s how to get it.

How to see your Twitch Recap for 2025

In order to get your 2025 Twitch Recap, get ready to do the following:

  • Go to the Twitch Recap Website
  • Sign in to your account
  • Choose between the Viewer, Creator, or Community recap

Twitch recap requirements for viewers and creators

While everyone will be able to access Twitch’s community recap for 2025, people who do not use the website enough will not receive a Viewer or Creator recap.

Twitch says you must have watched streams for 10 hours or streamed the same amount between October 31, 2024, and October 31, 2025.

According to the Amazon-owned company, there were over 45 billion chats sent throughout 2025, and Just Chatting was the top category of the year.
