A Beavis & Butt-Head event is coming to Black Ops 6 and Warzone, bringing a ton of rewards inspired by the classic MTV cartoon.

There’s been no shortage of Call of Duty events to get stuck into recently, from the Ballerina collab to the Rivals event. Things are showing no signs of slowing down either, as we’ve got King of the Dead to look forward to in Season 4 Reloaded.

But in one of the most surprising crossovers to date, it’s also been revealed that a Beavis and Butt-Head event is on the way, complete with plenty of weapons, attachments, and skins to unlock. Here’s everything you need to know, including the rewards confirmed so far.

When is Beavis & Butt-Head event?

There is currently no official date for the event, but we know it’s coming during Season 4 Reloaded, which gets underway on July 2.

It hasn’t been confirmed if it will go live at the same time as the update or if it will come at a later date. King of the Dead kicks off in the update, too, but it’s a Zombies-only event, so they may run alongside each other.

Beavis & Butt-Head event explained

The Beavis and Butt-Head event brings two tracks of rewards, which are levelled up by earning XP from matches. First is a free track that’s available to all players as soon as the event goes live, and the second is a premium track, which is unlocked by spending 1,100 CoD Points ($10/£8.39).

These are both levelled up at the same time, and if you decide to purchase the paid version later, you’ll receive all of the rewards you would have unlocked right away. There are also mastery rewards for completing both tracks.

All you have to do is play matches as normal in either Warzone, BO6 multiplayer, or Zombies, and you’ll level up the event pass based on how many wins and kills you rack up.

All rewards so far

Although the full slate of rewards and the milestones needed haven’t been revealed, plenty of the prizes on offer were mentioned in the Season 4 Reloaded blog. The biggest include skins for Beavis and Butt-Head characters, as well as new weapons like the Olympia Shotgun.

Check out all of the rewards we know so far below:

Free

  • Pickaxe (Melee Weapon)
  • Todd (Operator)
  • Coach Buzzcut (Operator)
  • SVD Full Auto Mod (Attachment)
  • Olympia Special (Shotgun) – Mastery

Premium

  • Beavis (Operator)
  • Dumbass (Finishing Move)
  • Hot Cheese (Saug Blueprint)
  • Rockin’ the Couch (Calling Couch)
  • Cornholio (Gun Screen)
  • Butt-Head (Operator) – Mastery

We’ll be sure to update this page with a full breakdown of all the rewards as soon as they’re revealed.
