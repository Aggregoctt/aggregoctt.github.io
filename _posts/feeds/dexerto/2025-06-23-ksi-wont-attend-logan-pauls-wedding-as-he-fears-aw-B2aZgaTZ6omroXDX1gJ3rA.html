KSI has revealed that he won’t be attending Logan Paul’s wedding as he fears “something” might happen between him and Jake.

If you rewind a few years, KSI and Logan Paul were rivals both on YouTube and in the boxing ring. However, after settling their differences, the pair have become business partners on PRIME Hydration drinks. KSI even had Logan’s back in the WWE ring.

The British YouTube star has been a regular on his imPaulsive podcast as well, where Logan has suggested he try and squash his beef with Jake Paul at some point. Though that is yet to happen.

With Logan getting married in the near future, many fans were looking forward to seeing KSI there and if anything would happen with him and Jake being in such close proximity. However, he isn’t going.

Logan Paul jokes about KSI not attending wedding

KSI’s non-attendance of the wedding was something Logan brought up as the pair reunited at Fanatics Fest in New York.

“Busy. Chilling now. Planning for this wedding that you’re not coming to. I get it. I get it,” Logan said after being asked by JJ about what he’d been up to.

“Nah, don’t do that. You know why! Bro, imagine I go to your wedding, Jake’s there, I am there, it’s awkward, bro. There’s that tension between us,” KSI replied. Everyone is going to be like, ‘Something’s about to happen, someone’s going down.’ I got my bodyguards. He’s got his bodyguards.”

A fight between Jake and KSI has been in the offing for years, with the two rivals agreeing to a winner-take-all clash at Wembley back in 2022. However, that didn’t happen. 

Logan even floated the idea of them fighting at his wedding in late 2024, urging KSI to “just settle it” at some point. 

It would undoubtedly draw big interest to the Paul American reality series, but it isn’t going to happen.
