A TikToker, who calls himself Uncle CCTV, keeps going viral after listening in on conversations and spying on a Tinder first date.

When out in public on their own, a lot of people just want to keep themselves. They want to eat and drink in peace, usually using headphones to block out unwanted distractions. However, there are more nosy people amongst them too.

Some will use their solo trips as a way to spy on others, listening in on random conversations for some tea that they can spill to their friends or post online. Sometimes, this gets back to the people involved as well.

However, a TikToker has taken this up a notch. He is called Uncle CCTV and routinely films himself listening in to conversations in public, giving his viewers some details about what he’s heard.

Uncle CCTV becomes TikTok hit

The TikToker has gone viral a few times, but most recently caught the attention of the internet for spying on a first date between a Tinder match. 

“It’s a couple, they kissed before he went to get a drink. They kissed as soon as he got the drink,” he revealed in his video. “I moved closer, they met on Tinder. It’s a first date.”

The minute-long video has gotten almost 2 million views, with viewers dying for an update from Uncle CCTV.

“Ok did they leave together afterwards? Do you think he is gonna drop her home? I want details, uncle, details!!!!!” one commented. “You need some meta glasses. My type of nosy,” another said. 

Some viewers suggested that they want to “hire” him to do their snooping for them too. “Uncle I am living vicariously through you,” another chimed in. 

Sadly, he has not yet provided an update on the first date, so there’s no idea if it was a successful match. Uncle CCTV has previously dropped multiple videos when snooping on some situations, and viewers are clearly desperate for that to happen again.
