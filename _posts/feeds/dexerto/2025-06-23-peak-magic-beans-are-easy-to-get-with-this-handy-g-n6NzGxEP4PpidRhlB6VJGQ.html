Peak has taken over the indie gaming scene, reaching a huge player count and supporting tons of adventurers looking for a little chaos with their friends, or those just making fun of its name.

However, aside from great communication and a little skill, you’ll need to fully understand all this game has to offer if you want to explore the island and climb to the top of the mountain to be rescued. After all, your climbing skills can only get you so far without any of the game’s items.

One such item is the Magic Bean, a powerful item that can get you much higher up the mountain, or really anywhere. So, here’s how to get it, and most importantly, what it does.

What does the Magic Bean do?

Before going into where you can find it, you’ll need to know exactly what the Magic Bean does. After all, if it’s not for you, then there’s no need to find it.

The Magic Bean is a bean that can be thrown onto any surface to create a tall beanstalk, taller than most of the palm trees you’ll find on your adventure.

As such, it’ll act as a sort of climbable pole once you throw it down on the ground. When you do, it’ll grow and allow you to either get higher or explore unreachable locations. Get creative with it, and you’ll thrive.

How to get the Magic Bean in Peak

So, if it turns out that you do want the Magic Bean for you and your pals, then you’ll need to look on any Shore on the island.

They’re purely found on the beaches of the Shore. Just keep an eye out for a small bean marked with the Magic Bean lettering when you hover over it. Then, simply pick it up and only use it on an area where you can’t climb. These aren’t the most common items in the game, so use them sparingly.
