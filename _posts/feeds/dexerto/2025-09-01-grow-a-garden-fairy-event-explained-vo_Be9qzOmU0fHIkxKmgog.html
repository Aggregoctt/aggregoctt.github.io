Grow a Garden’s new event is here, following on from the extremely successful Cooking event and Beanstalk adventure. Well, now it’s all about Fairy’s, and you can get some fantastic rewards if you know what you’re doing.

When it comes to Roblox, Grow a Garden is one of the biggest games out there, bringing in millions of players almost consistently. While some of that is down to the addictive gameplay, plenty is owed to the game’s constant events.

Whether you’re cooking up burgers, making honey, or climbing beanstalks, there’s always something new to do in Grow a Garden. Well, now it’s time for the Fairy event. Here’s all you need to know.

Grow a Garden’s Fairy Event began on August 30 and will end on September 6, 2025.

However, Grow A Garden likes to extend events with a second part, so, unless the admins change it up, the Fairy Event will go on until Saturday, September 13.

This means players will have at least one full week to grab all the rewards and make the most out of the event.

Grow a Garden’s Fairy event explained

The Grow a Garden Fairy event is all about offering the Wishing Well as many Glimmering crops as you can. Once you do, you’ll end up earning the rewards given by the Fountain in the middle of the map.

Think of it like the Bizzy Bee event back in May and June, where you have to pollinate your crops to a certain mutation and offer it to the event hub in the middle. Except this time, you need to get the Glimmering Mutation

Glimmering Mutation explained

You’ll only be able to hand in crops with the Glimmering Mutation to the Fountain. So, you’ll need to know how to get it on your plants.

Just like the Bizzy Bee event, every hour, at the start of the hour, the sky will turn purple and glitter will fill the air. Then, for ten minutes, you’ll see Fairies fly from the event hub and into your garden. They’ll transform random crops into Glimmering Mutations.

Then, all you need to do is pick them and offer them to the Wishing Well or the Fairy Favors.

As such, we recommend filling your garden with as many crops as you can, to maximise the number of Glimmering Mutations you get.

How to use the Wishing Well & Fairy Favors

Once you have the Glimmering Mutations, you’ll need to use the Wishing Well. This structure will ask for various specific crops called Fairy Favors.

If you offer the right crops, you’ll be given a Wish (three different rewards for you to choose from) and some Fairy Points.

As you offer more crops to the Wishing Well, you’ll move up in tiers. The higher the tier, the better the rewards, so it’s worth submitting as many as you can.

On top of this, you can submit any Glimmering Mutation crop; it just won’t offer you the Wish, but instead just the Fairy Points. Though once you fill the Fountain Power Meter, you’ll also gain access to a Wish as well.

How to earn Fairy Points

As mentioned above, you can earn Fairy Points which can either be used to fill the Fountain Power Meter for a wish or to purchase extra questlines or upgrades.

To get them, you’ll need to either complete Fairy Favors at the Wishing Well or submit rarer crops to the same location.

Completing the Fairy Favors consists of offering the right crops to the Wishing Well, but if you submit your rare crops then you can bag yourself more points. The rarer the crop, the higher the points:

  • Common – 1
  • Uncommon – 2
  • Rare – 3
  • Legendary – 4
  • Mythical – 5
  • Divine – 6
  • Prismatic – 7
  • Transcendent – 8
  • For Silver Variants – +1
  • For Gold Variants – +2
  • For Rainbow Variants – +3

Naturally, this is before the multiplier is added, depending on the crop.
