Lego has officially revealed five upcoming Lego Star Wars sets based on The Mandalorian and Grogu, expanding its lineup with new ships, a walker-focused build, and a BrickHeadz multipack.

Since The Mandalorian and his adorable companion took over the internet when the TV show released, Lego fans have been eager to build the various ships, characters, and more from the series. Well, now that’s possible.

The newly announced lineup includes the New Republic X-Wing Starfighter, a new Razor Crest, the Anzellan Starship, an AT-RT Attack set, and a BrickHeadz release titled The Mandalorian and Grogu: Allies & Villains. And they’re set to release very soon.

Every Mandalorian Lego set confirmed so far

The New Republic X-Wing Starfighter includes 558 pieces and is priced at $69.99, with Lego officially confirming a release date of April 1, 2026, making it the only set in the wave with a specific launch day.

Next is the Razor Crest, which contains 930 pieces and is priced at the slightly higher $159.99. This is currently listed to release later in April, with Lego stating it will ship during the month rather than giving a fixed date.

The Anzellan Starship is much in the same vein, releasing in April 2026. However, this one includes 701 pieces and carries a price tag of $79.99.

Then we have the iconic AT-RT Attack design, which features the smallest set in the group with 297 pieces and is priced at $39.99 (though it does come with Grogu, driver, and The Mandalorian). Lego has listed it as part of the same April 2026 release window.

Rounding out the wave is the BrickHeadz set titled The Mandalorian and Grogu: Allies & Villains. The set includes 661 pieces and comes with Anzellan, an Imperian Remnant Snowtrooper, The Mandalorian, Grogu, and Colonel Ward. It will retail for $39.99 and is listed for an April 2026 release, too.
