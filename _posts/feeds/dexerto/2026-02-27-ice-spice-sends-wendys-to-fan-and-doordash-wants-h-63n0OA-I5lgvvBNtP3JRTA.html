Ice Spice’s casual post about craving Wendy’s turned into a viral exchange after a fan asked her to send them food, eventually pulling DoorDash into the conversation as the delivery platform responded publicly.

The rapper posted on X on February 25 saying she was “rich asf craving a spicy chicken sandwich from Wendy’s,” which quickly sparked replies from fans. One user asked if she could DoorDash them one too, prompting Ice Spice to reply: “addy?”

The fan replied that they would send the details through DMs and explained they had recently experienced doxxing, so they did not want to post personal information publicly.

Soon after, the fan posted a photo of a Wendy’s bag at their door. They thanked Ice Spice and confirmed she had sent them a chicken salad.

DoorDash responds to the viral exchange

The interaction gained more attention after DoorDash’s official X account joined the conversation, replying “QUEEN” under the thread.

Later, the brand followed up with another post directed at Ice Spice: “hey @icespice should we team up to get more people what they’re craving?”

DoorDash’s response helped push the exchange further across social media, with users sharing screenshots of the back-and-forth and praising the unexpected interaction between the rapper, the fan, and the delivery platform.
