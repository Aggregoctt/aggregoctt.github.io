Helldivers 2 is seeing a huge resurgence, hitting a major player count milestone just days after it arrived on the Xbox.

It’s been well over a year since Helldivers 2 released. When it did, it took the industry by storm with its chaotic combat, democratic danger, and, of course, plenty of pesky bugs and robots to destroy, if you don’t accidentally destroy yourself or a teammate first. During that year, there was one highly demanded change players called for – its arrival on Xbox.

Well, now it’s finally here, bringing it to all major platforms (apart from the Switch 2, sorry). However, once you combine that with the new free Halo DLC, we have a brand new player count record, with Helldivers reaching the highest number of players it’s ever had.

Helldivers 2 smashes player count record after releasing to Xbox

As shared by the CEO of Arrowhead, the developers of Helldivers 2, it was revealed that the total player count for the game hit 500,490 players.

For context, the peak player count for Helldivers 2 on PC has never hit more than 458,000, and that was back in February 2024 when it released.

Since then, in the last 30 days, the average player count on PC, according to Steam Charts, was 33,956, meaning this is a huge increase from the game’s standard turnout.

Of course, the game’s out on PlayStation too, so it’s likely the player count has been higher than 33,000 in recent months, but to hit a record of over half a million players over a year into the game is wildly impressive.
