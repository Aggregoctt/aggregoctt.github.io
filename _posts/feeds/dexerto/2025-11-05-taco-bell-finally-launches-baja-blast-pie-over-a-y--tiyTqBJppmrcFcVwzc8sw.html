Taco Bell is finally launching the Mountain Dew Baja Blast Pie after it was announced over a year ago. The key lime-style dessert, made with Baja Blast’s signature tropical lime flavor, will be available starting November 6 for a limited time.

The pie was first teased at the Live Más LIVE 2024 event and later spotted at the 20th Bajaversary celebration, where it sparked fan memes and calls for a wider release.

Now it’s arriving in-store across the U.S. while supplies last. The pie will only be sold whole, however, meaning fans won’t be able to buy just slices. The whole pie will cost customers $19.99.

Baja Blast Pie finally launches alongside drink version

A frozen drink version of the dessert called the Mountain Dew Baja Blast Pie Chiller will also launch exclusively at select Live Más Cafe locations in California and Texas.

The drink blends chunks of the pie with a vanilla ice cream base and tops it with whipped cream and churro crunch. Prices start at $4.19 for a 16oz.
