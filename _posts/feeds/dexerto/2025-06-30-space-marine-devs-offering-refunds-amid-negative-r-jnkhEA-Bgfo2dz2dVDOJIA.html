After a rocky launch and sitting with Mostly Negative reviews, Warhammer 40k Space Marine remastered developers are offering players to get a full refund and even a discounted upgrade.

While fans were rather excited for the Space Marine remaster prior to its release, things took a completely different turn when it finally launched on June 10, 2025.

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine—Master Crafted Edition’s description mentioned that it intended to bring a range of quality-of-life and graphical improvements. However, many players have shared their frustrations since its release, saying this hasn’t been the case and that the title doesn’t justify its price tag due to bugs, and that it only looks “slightly better.”

With “Mostly Negative” reviews piling up since then, devs have now announced in a new post on Steam that they’re offering players a full refund for those who regret their purchase.

Warhammer 40k Space Marine devs are offering a full refund as well as a big discount

According to the developers, anyone who has purchased the game is eligible for a full refund until 6PM BST | 10PM PST on July 10, 2025, the same time the Summer Sale ends. 

This refund policy bypasses the players’ playtime, so even if you’ve clocked more than the usual two hours on Steam, you can still request a refund to get your money back.

In addition to a full refund, devs have also offered a 50% discounted price for those who want to purchase the Master Crafted edition. 

While those who already own the Anniversary Edition are automatically eligible for the discount, players who purchased the Master Crafted Edition will need to request a refund manually first and then re-purchase the game to get it at a discounted price.

For players who choose to stick with it, the developers have laid out some plans that they’ll implement in the future.

As a way to improve the experience, a plethora of fixes and improvements will be rolled out in patches in the upcoming weeks. These include “improved localisation, controller options, and key terms,” along with UI/UX enchantments, platform & controller support, and others.

“We’ll continue listening, improving, and communicating as these changes go live. Thanks for sticking with us,” devs wrote.
