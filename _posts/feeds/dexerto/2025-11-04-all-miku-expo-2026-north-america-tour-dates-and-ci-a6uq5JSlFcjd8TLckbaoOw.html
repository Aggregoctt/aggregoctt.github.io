Hatsune Miku is heading to North America in 2026, where the virtual idol will perform on stage across Chicago, Los Angeles, and more.

Crypton Future Media has confirmed the Miku Expo tour will run from April 12 to May 19, marking the virtual idol’s return for a full North America run. The shows will pair a live band with updated visuals and performance content.

The tour spans major stops including Chicago, Los Angeles, Seattle, Vancouver, Dallas, Boston, and Mexico City, with several cities hosting the virtual idol for two nights. Miku Expo will also spotlight community-made music, where the winning track will be performed live during the tour.

So, if you’re a diehard Miku fan and want to know where she’ll be playing, then we have everything you need to know about the upcoming concert tour.

How to get tickets

Tickets for Miku Expo 2026 are available now through the official tour site, which links directly to approved ticket partners for each venue. Availability varies by city, so be sure to purchase tickets as soon as you can to avoid disappointment.

VIP perks and glowstick rules

Miku Expo 2026 offers a VIP ticket tier that includes early venue entry, an exclusive tour pass, and special merchandise.

The official Miku Expo glowsticks come in seven colors, but won’t be part of the VIP package. Instead, they’ll be sold in limited quantities online and on-site.

However, if you don’t wish to purchase an official glowstick, you can bring your own as long as it’s under 30cm and not modified.
