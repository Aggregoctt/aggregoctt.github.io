IShowSpeed is getting his own Doritos flavor in 2026, according to a new post from Instagram foodie Snackolator.

Back in mid-December 2025, Doritos posted a video on its social media pages showing IShowSpeed sitting in a car as he grabs a bag of their iconic Nacho Cheese-flavored chips.

When he pulls a chip out, however, it appears to be a regular wavy-style potato chip. In the caption, they said: “IShowSpeed is up to something,” with the date of February 2026 beside it.

Thanks to a new post from Instagram foodie Snackolator, we now know what that collab is going to be.

IShowSpeed’s face is going on a Doritos bag

On Wednesday, December 31, Snackolator shared Lay’s upcoming ‘Flavor Swap’ chips that bring the flavor from one brand over to another.

IShowSpeed’s collab features the flavor from Cheddar and Sour Cream Ruffles chips, but on a triangle-shaped Doritos chip for the first time.

Two other collaborations include Madison Beer’s Southern Sweet Heat Barbecue-flavored Cheetos and Dude Perfect’s Cool Ranch Ruffles.
