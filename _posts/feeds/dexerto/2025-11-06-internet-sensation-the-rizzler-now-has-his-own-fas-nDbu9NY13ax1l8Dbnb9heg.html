The Rizzler has teamed up with American fast food chain Hardee’s to launch his very own ‘Rizzwich Combo,’ delivering the grilled cheese he’s always wanted.

Everyone loves The Rizzler. How could you not? The iconic ‘rizz face’ has carried the nine-year-old to global stardom through social media. Be it appearances at blockbuster sporting events, modelling for fashion lines, or getting in the ring with AEW, there’s seemingly nothing he can’t do.

Now, in another step toward world domination, The Rizzler has taken his talents to the kitchen. Partnering up with Hardee’s, the influencer now has his very own combo meal available to order.

The Rizzwich Combo combines everything he wants to see in a grilled cheese sandwich, and you can try it for yourself starting today.

What’s in the Rizzwich Combo meal at Hardee’s?

This limited-edition meal ‘curated’ by The Rizzler is available exclusively through the Hardee’s app. Even if you’re in-store, it has to be ordered online through the mobile app.

What you get is a sandwich containing a beef patty, American and Swiss cheese, along with some bacon – multiple strips, as The Rizzler personally ensured. It comes with some fries and your choice of milkshake too.

The partnership kicked off on November 5, 2025, and while it’s set to be available for a limited time, there’s no quite telling when it may disappear.

“The Rizzwich Meal is the first taste of where Hardee’s marketing is headed,” Vice President of brand Sarah Breymaier said in an accompanying press release. If successful, perhaps this is just the first of many Rizztastic menu items we’ll see at the fast food chain.
