A couple of football fans are going viral after spending 27 hours in a bathroom at Munich’s Allianz Arena to sneak into the Champions League Final without tickets.

The Champions League Final between PSG and Inter Milan ended with a dominating 5-0 win for the French squad and a couple of fans were able to catch all the action without paying.

Getting tickets to the year’s hottest football match can be a big challenge for two reasons: price and demand. But, with a bit of luck and some extreme patience, two TikTokers found a workaround.

In a video going viral, Belgian football fans Neal Remmerie and Senne Haverbeke showed how they managed to get into the arena and watch the final in-person: By spending 27 hours in a cramped bathroom stall.

The day before the match, Neal and Senne gained access to the arena by wearing vests and casually walking in.

From there, they found a public bathroom and taped “out of order” signs on two stalls before playing the long waiting game and trying to remain silent to not alert security.

“We had a backpack with snacks and we played around on our phones to kill time,” Remmerie explained. “The lights were on all the time and the sitting position was uncomfortable, so sleeping was almost impossible. That made it physically and mentally difficult.”

Eventually, over a day later, the two could hear spectators inside the arena so they exited their stalls and snuck into the main stands without ever buying tickets.

The goal was simple: find a security guard paying the least amount of attention and stroll by casually while on their phones and with food in their hands. Once through, the pair entered the stands with a batch of PSG supporters and watched the match unfold.

Since being uploaded, the duo’s video has been viewed nearly 1M times in one day. So far, neither UEFA nor Allianz Arena have responded to the incident and it’s unclear if the two will face any consequences.
