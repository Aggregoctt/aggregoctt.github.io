What are Sorcerer Ascent codes used for?

Since Sorcerer Ascent is still fairly new, codes currently focus on rewarding Soul Points, the game’s core currency tied to character progression. Soul Points are mainly used to reroll your Clan and Innate Cursed Technique, which plays a major role in shaping how strong and flexible your build becomes.

Because higher-tier techniques are significantly rarer, rerolling often requires a large number of Soul Points, making codes especially valuable if you’re chasing better outcomes without excessive grinding. On rare occasions, codes may also grant rerolls directly, giving you another chance at landing a stronger setup.
