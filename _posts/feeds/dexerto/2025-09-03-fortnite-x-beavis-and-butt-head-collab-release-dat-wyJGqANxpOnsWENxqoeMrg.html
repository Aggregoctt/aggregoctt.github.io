The Fortnite x Beavis and Butt-Head collab is happening, and it’s looking to be one of the strangest (and funniest) crossovers yet.

Epic has turned Fortnite into a pop culture playground. You can’t get through a single battle royale match without seeing at least one of the rarest skins in the game. Still, seeing Beavis yelling about TP for his bunghole while Butt-Head snickers at your loadout screen is a new level of surreal.

Some details are confirmed by Epic, while others come from trusted Fortnite leakers. Either way, fans of ’90s cartoon absurdity are in for a treat.

Confirmed: Beavis and Butt-Head are arriving in Fortnite during the first week of September 2025.

Leaked: Reliable insiders point to a drop window between September 3 and September 9, with Thursday or Friday at 8 PM ET being the most likely time. This would sync perfectly with the premiere of Season 3 of the Beavis and Butt-Head show.

Price

Not officially confirmed, but based on Epic’s pricing history, the individual skins could cost 1,500 V-Bucks each and the bundles are expected to cost between 2,500 and 3,000 V-Bucks, including extras like wraps or emotes.

Skins & emotes

Epic has confirmed 2 skins for Beavis and Butt-Head, with at least one alternate style. Each will have a matching back bling.

Based on Fortnite’s history with collabs, we can also expect two pickaxes, a wrap, and three emotes. Leakers have uncovered two out of the three emotes: The Great Cornholio and a Metallica TV-watching gag.
