Pokemon TCG Pocket is putting a stop to adding new expansions to the game and is using its latest set, Deluxe Pack ex, to relaunch old cards with new, stunning illustrations.

The meta in Pokemon TCG Pocket can rest for once, as the Deluxe Pack ex expansion will not add new cards to the game, but will instead rework the art on previously released ones.

Each Deluxe Pack ex booster contains four cards, with at least one guaranteed ♦♦♦♦ rarity or higher from past sets like Genetic Apex and Wisdom of Sea and Sky. The packs also feature parallel foils with a unique shine and reprints sporting new artwork.

The Deluxe Pack ex set will only be available between September 30 and October 30, 2025. Here are the cards confirmed for the A4b expansion.
