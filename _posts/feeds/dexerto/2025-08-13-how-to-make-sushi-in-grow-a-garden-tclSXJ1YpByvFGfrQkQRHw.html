Sushi is tricky to make at the best of times, and when it comes to Grow a Garden’s cooking event, it’s no different. Luckily, it’s made much easier when you know the ingredients.

Grow a Garden‘s cooking event has been a huge success for the game, adding tons of new recipes for players to grab as they grow vegetables, fruit, and plants so they can cook their way to Chris’ exclusive rewards.

However, this Roblox adventure isn’t giving you all the answers, as it keeps the ingredients hidden. Well, to save you wasting your crops, we’ve got all the Grow a Garden Sushi recipes and ingredients right here.

All Grow a Garden Sushi recipes & ingredients

There are 10 different Sushi recipes we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden, each offering a different rarity. Thankfully, you can make them with different ingredients to guarantee your rarity.

No matter the rarity of your dish, the cook time will be 6 minutes. Though it will be longer if you use bigger ingredients.

How to make Sushi

Once you have all the ingredients listed above, you can make your Sushi; all you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Go to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat in the middle of the map.
  • Head over to the steel pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them inside the pot.
  • Press the green Cook button.
  • Wait the allotted time and get the Sushi.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and satisfied Chris’s craving, he’ll offer you a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth giving Chris the best meals possible.

You can check out all the available rewards in our guide.

You can cook Sushi at any time, but it’s best to save it for when Chris is craving it.
