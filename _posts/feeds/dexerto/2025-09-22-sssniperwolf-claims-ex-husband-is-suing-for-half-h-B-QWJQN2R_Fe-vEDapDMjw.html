YouTube star Alia ‘SSSniperwolf’ Shelesh says her ex-husband, Evan ‘Sausage’ Young, is suing her for control of her social media accounts and future earnings, after apparently already claimed half of her net worth in divorce proceedings.

She revealed the details of the complex split during a guest appearance on Kai Cenat’s Mafiathon 3 stream.

When Cenat asked her directly about Sausage, he said: “Wait, what happened to you and Mr. Sausage? … So, he’s suing you? Why? So, he owns half of that?”

SSSniperwolf responded that the case has been ongoing for years and involves both her current and future income.

“He wants all my money. He has taken half of everything I own, and he still wants half of everything in the future. Like, all the future earnings, and everything. And he wants all my social media accounts, and he wants my new channel I just made,” she explained.

Shelesh, who has over 35 million YouTube subscribers, said the legal battle has been running for three years. She added that she is hopeful it could finally be resolved next year, though she did not reveal exact details on court dates.

SSSniperwolf and Sausage were in a relationship for years, with Young frequently appearing in her early videos. The pair later married but separated after what she described as an unstable partnership.

Although their on-and-off relationship has often been discussed publicly, this is the first time she has claimed in detail that her ex-husband is seeking ownership of her platforms and revenue through the courts.
