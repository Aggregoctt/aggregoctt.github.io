Destiny 2‘s annual summer-themed Solstice event returns in The Edge of Fate, bringing with it new weapons to chase in standard and Holofoil forms.

Like Arms week, Guardians will be able to spend an event-exclusive currency to purchase random rolls of this year’s exclusive weapons as well as earn and upgrade a returning armor set from previous years.

For everything you need to know about the event, including which rolls of newcomers Yeartide Apex and Festival Flight you’ll want to chase, see below.

When does Solstice 2025 start?

Destiny 2’s 2025 Solstice celebration begins with weekly reset on August 5.

As standard, the limited-time event runs for two weeks, ending with weekly reset on August 19.

Weapons & perks

Solstice 2025 introduces two new weapons to the sandbox: Festival Flight and Yeartide Apex, an Area Denial grenade launcher and Lightweight SMG, respectively.

Both come packing some irresistible perk pools that deserve some analysis, but first, check out the full list for both in the table below.

Meta Analysis

For both PvE and PvP, Yeartide Apex is an excellent gun bursting with potential. For the former, Heal Clip and Incandescent are a match made in heaven for Solar builds, while Lone Wolf and Target Lock should prove to be a potent and sought-after combo for Crucible.

Similar to VS Velocity Baton and Lost Signal, Festival Flight has the potential to be an Orb-generating machine for Strand builds. Even though Attrition Orbs is no longer as potent as before following nerfs, paired with Envious Arsenal, it should still prove to be a solid add-clear and Super-generating special weapon.

Armor

Two sets of armor are available during Solstice 2025. One of these, sporting a summary vacation theme, will be available through Destiny 2’s Eververse store (pictured left) as armor ornaments.

The second (pictured right) will be earnable in-game as actual armor and require Guardians to complete various tasks and objectives to unlock a visual glow on each piece.
