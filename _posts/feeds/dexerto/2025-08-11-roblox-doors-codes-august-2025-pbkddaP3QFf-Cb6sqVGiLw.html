What are Doors codes used for?

Doors isn’t an easy game to complete. Luckily, codes will help you survive and thrive in this horror adventure.

They’ll grant you Knobs, Revives, and the occasional Boost. Knobs are the game’s version of currency, so the more you have, the more items in the Pre-Run Shop you’ll be able to buy, like a Flashlight, Lockpick, Lighter, and Vitamins.

Revives do what they say on the tin, they’ll revive a fallen player, keeping you in the game for longer. Similarly, boosts will do the same, offering small bonuses to keep you in the game and alive.

Doors codes are extremely useful, and given how easy they are to redeem, they’re well worth grabbing when you can.
