Beast Games Season 2 Part 1 ended on an almighty cliffhanger, with one player taking a $1 million bribe. If you’re itching to see Episode 4, we’ve laid out all of the release date and time details so you know exactly when to tune in. 

MrBeast pumped even more cash into the sophomore season of his Prime Video competition series, building an entire city he’s dubbed (you guessed it) “Beast City”, where the competitors live and take part in huge challenges. 

There are some changes to the format this time round. Firstly, there are 200 players split into two: 100 strong vs 100 smart. But we’re also shown more of the competitors’ interactions in Season 2, including a romance blossoming between Players 152 and 173. 

In the first three episodes, the 200 count was whittled down to just 25, and right at the end, the Captain Bribe made a reappearance. We’ll have to wait for Episode 4 to find out who takes that $1 million prize. 

Beast Games Season 2 Episode 4 drops on Prime Video on January 14, 2026, at 12am PT / 3am ET.

What happened in Episode 3?

Beast Games Season 2 Episode 3 ended with MrBeast announcing that someone took the $1 million bribe – but we’ll have to wait for Episode 4 to find out who. 

The first three episodes saw the 200 players take part in a series of games and challenges, with the third – a huge obstacle course – seeing this number drop to just 25 finalists. 

Then came the twist: the Captain Bribe. Similarly to Season 1, MrBeast announces that the teams must each select a captain to represent them. 

The five captains stand in front of a button while a huge screen shows a figure of cash, which steadily rises. At any point, the captain can press their button and will instantly win all of the money for themselves. 

If they take the bribe, they’re not eliminated – but their teammates are. Initially, no one pushes the button, and the prize pot climbs all the way to $1 million. 

Right at the end, MrBeast announces, “Someone took the prize!” But the episode ends there.

As said, next week’s episode also looks set to be the Survivor crossover – a hybrid of Beast Games’ classic challenges alongside Survivor’s format, with host Jeff Probst even making an appearance. 

Where to watch Beast Games

Beast Games is exclusively available to stream via Prime Video. 

Since it’s an exclusive to Amazon MGM Studios, this is the only streaming service where you can watch the show. Even MrBeast’s own YouTube channel does not air it. 

If you want to watch the first batch of episodes for free, Amazon offers a 30-day free trial for eligible customers.
