It’s nearly time for Hollywood’s biggest red carpet event, as the Academy Awards ceremony is just around the corner. For now, the 2026 Oscar nominations are in. 

Look back at the best movies of 2025 and you’ll see there’s a lot to choose from. Notably, Sinners reinvented the vampire genre and proved to be one of the most impeccable horror films ever made. 

Then you had Paul Thomas Anderson’s latest masterpiece, One Battle After Another, Chloé Zhao’s critically-acclaimed Hamnet, and Yorgos Lanthimos’ latest hit Bugonia – and that’s before we even get to the best performances of the year.
