The Umamusume: Cinderella Gray anime is back, and to celebrate, Umamusume: Pretty Derby players can get 3,000 free Carats.

Running from October 6 through December 29, 2025, the promotion spans episodes 14 to 23 of the Umamusume anime. Each week, Umamusume: Pretty Derby players can earn Carats by simply logging in during the campaign period.

In total, 3,000 Carats are up for grabs, making this one of the most generous anime tie-ins to date. With login bonuses arriving in step with the show’s weekly schedule, it gives players the chance to pull on the new Fuji Kiseki banner and save for upcoming characters.

How to get the Umamusume anime login bonus

The campaign officially began on October 6, 2025, at 12:00 p.m. UTC with the airing of Episode 14. From there, every new episode unlocks a bonus window worth 300 Carats.

Logging in once during each period is enough to secure the reward, though missed weeks cannot be claimed retroactively. For Episode 14 specifically, players will need to re-enter from the title screen after the campaign has started.
