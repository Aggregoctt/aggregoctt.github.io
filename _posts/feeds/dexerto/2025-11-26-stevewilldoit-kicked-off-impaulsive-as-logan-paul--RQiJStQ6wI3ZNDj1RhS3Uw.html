SteveWillDoIt’s episode of the imPaulsive podcast with Logan Paul and Mike Majlak ended abruptly as the YouTuber walked off the show less than 40 minutes into it. 

On November 18, SteveWillDoIt, one of the founding members of the NELK Boys, announced that he had finally been unbanned from YouTube and would be making his long-awaited return in December. 

Naturally, he has been hyping up that return, and was invited onto the imPaulsive podcast to talk about it. However, less than 40 minutes into it, Steve called time on the podcast, moving his mic away from his mouth, shaking hands with Logan and Mike, and eventually walking away. 

“The show’s not over,” Maljak said after Steve returned briefly. “Nah, I’m done with it,” the NELK founder replied. 

SteveWillDoIt leaves imPaulsive early

He then challenged the hosts to a game of padel, with a $40,000 watch being on the line for the winners, before claiming he was “good” and saying a few things that were censored by the podcast. 

“Steve. I definitely have a line, I think you’ve reached the line, and I am going to ask you to chill a little bit,” Logan said. “Not even just for my sake, I can handle the jokes, right? Just careful, because I told you I hit my line.” 

He then stood up and left again. “He just got a little carried away,” Majlak added before apologizing for inviting Steve on as a guest after he took some swipes at MrBeast. “The statements he was making weren’t really making sense, and they were blanket statements,” he continued.

“I can’t believe he hasn’t gotten hit,” Logan added. “I’m just shocked no one has beat the s*it out of him. Dude, if we weren’t on this podcast, I’d slap him up the second he said something but I’m being friendly because he’s the guest, we invited him here, right?”

Viewers hit out at the podcast hosts for “belittling” their guest, with some saying they had “publicly humiliated” him with some of the topics that were brought up.

“Ngl this is sad to watch. Steve is just looking for a genuine friend. Feel bad for him. We’ve all been there,” one added.
