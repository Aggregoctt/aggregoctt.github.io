After months of playtests and teasers, Battlefield 6 has finally been confirmed, and the official gameplay trailer is coming soon.

This is the first Battlefield game since 2021’s 2042, which launched to a mixed reception, to say the least. We don’t have many official details yet, but the devs have been running playtests through Battlefield LABS, and, from what we’ve seen, it’s a semi-futuristic setting with all the signature large-team modes and destruction you’d expect.

Now, we know that the next entry is called Battlefield 6, and here’s how you’ll be able to watch the reveal trailer live.

How to watch Battlefield 6 reveal trailer

The Battlefield 6 reveal trailer debuts on YouTube on Thursday, July 24 at 8 AM PT / 11 AM ET / 4 PM BST. We’ve embedded the trailer below:

We don’t know what this trailer will involve, but the description reads: “Prepare for the ultimate all-out warfare experience in Battlefield 6.”

We expect some cinematic footage showing off the setting and destruction, and perhaps even the release date reveal. All we know at the moment is that it’ll release before March 2026, but now, a 2025 release date is looking likely.

DICE have also confirmed that an open beta is on the way, so there’s a slim chance we could find out more about that, but this will most likely just show some gameplay footage.
