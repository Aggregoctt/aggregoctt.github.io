Competitors attending the 300th Yu-Gi-Oh! Championship Series can win one of the most legendary items from the franchise: the Millennium Ring.

As revealed on the official Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG X account, the top eight duelists competing at the YCS stops in Richmond (USA), Guadalajara (Mexico), and Dortmund (Germany) will receive an official Millennium Ring necklace as a prize.

The Millennium Ring is one of the seven Millennium Items from the original Yu-Gi-Oh! anime, an ancient Egyptian artifact tied to supernatural power. In the anime, it’s most closely associated with Ryo Bakura and serves as a compass capable of locating other Millennium Items.

While the Millennium Ring Necklace won’t let fans hunt down ancient artifacts or find rare cards, it does make for a standout prize.

Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG fans can win IRL Millennium Ring

The Yu-Gi-Oh! x King Ice Millennium Ring Necklace retails for around $120. It features a large triangular centerpiece engraved with the franchise’s signature eye symbol. The pendant is finished in gold plating over brass to mirror the look of the original artifact from the anime.

Each pendant comes with a 22-inch, 5mm Miami Cuban link chain secured by a lobster clasp and weighs about 107 grams.

King Ice has also released other Yu-Gi-Oh!-inspired jewelry items, including the Blue-Eyes White Dragon necklace, Dark Magician necklace, and Yami Yugi necklace, giving fans more ways to rep their favorite series in style.

This isn’t the only Yu-Gi-Oh! themed collaboration that has gone viral, as Lego has asked fans to make sets based on Battle City or Duelist Kingdom tournament arcs from the Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Monsters anime.

The 300th Yu-Gi-Oh! Championship Series begins on February 14, with Richmond kicking things off before the competition moves to Guadalajara and Dortmund.
