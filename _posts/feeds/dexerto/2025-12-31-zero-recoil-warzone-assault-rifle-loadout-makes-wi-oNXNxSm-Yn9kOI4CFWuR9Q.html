Controlling recoil is a major part of winning gunfights in Warzone, but what if you didn’t have to? This Assault Rifle loadout has no recoil at all, making landing shots a breeze.

Low-recoil powerhouses have always dominated Warzone’s meta, and thanks to these attachments, the AK-27 Assault Rifle has no recoil at all. Simply aim at the target, pull the trigger, and let the weapon do all the work.

No-recoil Warzone AK-27 loadout

  • Muzzle: EMT3 Compensator
  • Barrel: 17″ Bystro Speed Barrel
  • Magazine: Epitaph Extended Magazine
  • Rear Grip: Czar Control Grip
  • Fire Mods: Buffer Spring
  • Build Code: A02-FTRU1-951

Combining all of these attachments makes it so that the AK-27 doesn’t move at all — it’s as simple as pointing and shooting. It moves side to side a little, but there’s absolutely no vertical recoil to worry about.

Since this build doesn’t use a scope, I’d recommend using it in Resurgence instead of Verdansk, as you’ll get a lot more close-medium gunfights in Haven’s Hollow and Rebirth Island.

If you do want to use it on Verdansk, it’s best used as a sniper support weapon when paired with a sniper such as the VS Recon, so you can be effective at all ranges.

While the Compensator is the best Muzzle attachment, if you’d rather use a Suppressor, the Redwell Shade-X Suppressor allows it to keep its zero-recoil status, but it harms its damage range and bullet velocity, so it’ll be less effective over distance.
