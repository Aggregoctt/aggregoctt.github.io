A first look at Lego brick Crocs has surfaced online, revealing one of the strangest footwear collaborations Crocs has attempted to date.

According to a report from Bloomberg, the shoes are part of a formal partnership between Crocs and Lego, with the collaboration launching through brick-inspired clogs.

The clogs are molded to resemble oversized Lego bricks, complete with raised studs across the upper instead of Crocs’ signature perforated design.

Bloomberg says the shoes are expected to retail for around $150, with the unique footwear being available to purchase later this year.

Crocs x Lego collab reveals Lego clogs

“Lego has a very broad consumer base, very similar to Crocs,” said Anne Mehlman, president of the Crocs brand, via Bloomberg. “They have very engaged kids and very engaged adults — a lot of adults have full Lego rooms dedicated, and we do too.”

While neither company has published a standalone announcement on their own channels yet, Jay’s Brick Blog notes that the Lego brick clogs are scheduled for release on February 16, 2026.

The website also mentions the collection will be sold directly through Crocs and Lego’s official websites, and will also include Lego-themed Jibbitz charms.
