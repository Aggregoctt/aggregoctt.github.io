Jake Paul has claimed that he is “banned” from WWE and thats why he hasn’t made the switch to join his brother, Logan, just yet. However, that could change.

When Logan first started getting seriously involved with WWE, fans believed that it would surely be a matter of time before his brother, Jake, followed him into the ring.

The ‘Problem Child’, who has been more focused on boxing than anything else, did help Logan out during his brother’s match at Crown Jewel in 2022. However, he’s been absent since then, with the likes of KSI and IShowSpeed being drafted in instead of him as Logan’s backup.

Him and Logan teaming up to go after the tag team titles like The Hardy Boyz is something that has been mentioned in the past. However, he’s revealed why it hasn’t happened just yet. 

Jake Paul wants to join Logan in WWE

According to Jake, he is “banned” from the WWE. “I’m actually banned from the WWE,” he said in a Q&A video with Logan on July 9.

“That’s not true! No, it’s not,” Logan responded. “You’ve upset some of the people involved in the company because you’ve made an enemy out of Dana White… you have a diss track on him buddy!

“Dana is now part of the TKO group because the TKO group now owns the UFC and WWE. I think there is a little bit of static, but its nothing we can’t fix.”

Timestamp of 10:45

Jake said he’d be “down” to join his brother in the WWE tag team division, if he was able to do so. 

While Dana doesn’t hold any creative control over WWE, they would likely need his sign-off before Jake gets involved, especially given the cross-promotion between that and the UFC. 

Jake very rarely backs down in feuds, so he’s hardly going to apologize to Dana without there being any reward for doing so.
