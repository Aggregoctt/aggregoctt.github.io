When the Xbox One launched in 2013, Microsoft entered another fiercely competitive console generation. Facing off against Sony’s PlayStation 4 and Nintendo’s Switch later in the cycle, Xbox shifted its strategy toward building a connected ecosystem — one that tried to blend console, PC, and online services into a single platform.

The Xbox One era delivered some of Microsoft’s strongest first-party efforts. Forza Horizon evolved into a flagship racing series, Halo: The Master Chief Collection preserved a legacy, and Gears 5 refined cover-based action for a new age. At the same time, massive third-party and cross-platform hits like Elden Ring, Red Dead Redemption 2, The Witcher 3, GTA V, and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare became shared landmarks of the generation, defining how players explored open worlds and competed online.

It was also a console where services and communities took center stage. Minecraft, Fortnite, Sea of Thieves, and Destiny 2 turned shared worlds into long-running social spaces, while Xbox Game Pass reshaped how players discovered and accessed games.

From blockbuster adventures to endlessly replayable live-service staples, the Xbox One library reflects a generation where games became platforms of their own.

With that in mind, here’s our ranking of the best Xbox One games.

What it's about: What Remains of Edith Finch is a collection of short stories about a cursed family in Washington State. Each story offers a chance to experience the life of a different family member with stories ranging from the early 1900s to the present day. Yakuza 0

What it's about: Yakuza 0 is an action-adventure game developed by Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio and published by Sega. Serving as a prequel to the Yakuza series, it is set in 1988 Japan during the country's economic bubble era. The game follows two protagonists: Kazuma Kiryu in Tokyo's Kamurocho district and Goro Majima in Osaka's Sotenbori district. Players alternate between their stories as they navigate the criminal underworld, engaging in street fights, managing businesses, and participating in various side activities. The gameplay combines beat 'em up combat with open-world exploration, offering numerous mini-games and side quests. Yakuza 0 was initially released for PlayStation 3 and PlayStation 4 in Japan in March 2015, followed by a worldwide release on PlayStation 4 in January 2017. It later became available on Windows via Steam in August 2018 and on Xbox One in February 2020. A Director's Cut version is scheduled for release on Nintendo Switch 2 on June 5, 2025.41
