A Pokemon TCG shop in Japan was robbed by a man who allegedly turned out to be the manager of another TCG shop located just a few blocks away.

Over the years, we’ve seen plenty of robberies revolving around Pokemon cards, given that some of these items have insane market value and are worth a ton of money among collectors in the community. 

While it’s always been a fun card-collecting hobby, there’s no denying it also has a dangerous side, with fans occasionally suffering physical injuries due to others going out of their way to get their hands on the rarest goods.

Recently, there was another robbery that took place in a shop that sells Pokemon cards. The twist? The culprit supposedly wasn’t just some random person, but it was actually someone working in another TCG shop nearby.

Pokemon TCG shop owner robbed and threatened with a knife by staff from another shop

As first spotted by Automaton, Japanese police arrested a 35-year-old man, who was reportedly on suspicion of robbery on July 9, 2025.

The police didn’t reveal further details at the time, but that hasn’t stopped the shop owner from taking it a step further, allegedly showing the thief’s identity on social media. 

As it turns out, the recently arrested suspect was the manager of another TCG shop located in the same neighborhood, just a few blocks away. This other shop also specializes in selling Pokemon cards, along with One Piece.

The manager of the said shop wasn’t just accused of stealing 123 items, which included cash and Pokemon cards, approximately worth $94,000, but also of threatening the trading card shop owner with a knife. 

According to reports, he wasn’t exactly alone either, as the man was the third person related to the case who got arrested. Two of his accomplices had already been caught by authorities.

In a post that has since gone viral on X, the owner also mentioned that there are other individuals involved in the robbery who have yet to be caught.

“Those still involved should surrender immediately. Thinking you won’t get caught is impossible. We will absolutely catch everyone. We welcome any information, no matter how small,” they wrote.
