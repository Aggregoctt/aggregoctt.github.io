Peacemaker Season 2 has been peak superhero TV, but now the series is drawing to a close, so here’s when you can watch the climactic Episode 8.

Season 2 of Peacemaker has been a rollercoaster of emotions for the title character, with Christopher Smith discovering an alternate Earth X where his father and brother are still alive, then learning that the Nazis won WWII in that world, meaning America is a supremacist state.

During Episode 7, the 11th Street Kids also crossed over to rescue him, and the gang then heads back to our world, only to be confronted by A.R.G.U.S., to whom Peacemaker hands over the portal device, and gives himself up for arrest. Though the episode nearly had a very different ending.

Showrunner James Gunn has assured fans that no one will expect what’s coming in the season finale, so here’s details of when and where you can watch the big ending.

Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 8 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Thursday, October 9.

Unfortunately, if you’re not based in or around the US, you’ll probably have to stay up late or watch it as soon as you wake up (if not, you’ll need to mute everything on your social media platforms). You can find out what time it’s airing in other time zones below:

  • 10pm Brazil
  • 2am UK
  • 3am Central European Summer Time
  • 4am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 6:30am India
  • 11am Australia
  • 1pm New Zealand

How many episodes of Peacemaker Season 2 are there?

There are eight episodes in Peacemaker Season 2.

One episode is being released per week, with Gunn telling his social media followers that “the anticipation and conversation around dropping an episode week to week is part of the fun.”

What happened in Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 7?

Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 7 saw the 11th Street Kids get to grips with life in the Nazi dimension, with Judomaster saving Adebayo from a bunch of racists, and Auggie and Keith capturing Chris and Harcourt and taking them back to their mansion.

Meanwhile in our world, Rick Flag Sr. charges Lex Luthor’s tech genius Sydney Happersen with the task of locating the dimensional rift so they can find and take the portal device.

A fight follows on Earth X in the Smith mansion; one that leaves Auggie dead and sees Keith nearly going the same way, before Chris puts a stop to the violence, heads back through the portal, and gives everything up to A.R.G.U.S., paving the way for the finale.
