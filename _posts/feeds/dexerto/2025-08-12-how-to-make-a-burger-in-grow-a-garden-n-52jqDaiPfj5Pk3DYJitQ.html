Grow a Garden‘s cooking event introduced a long list of recipes for players to discover and create their favorite dishes, including Burgers. Many of these aren’t easy to make unless you already have the ingredients at hand, however.

Nurturing vegetables, fruits, and other foliage is the main goal of Roblox’s Grow a Garden game, but that doesn’t mean you can’t throw together some delicious fast food with the help of Chris P. Bacon.

If you’re taking part in the event and Chris is hungry for a juicy Burger, here’s everything you need to know about how to make them, the ingredients needed, and all available rarities.

All Grow a Garden Burger recipes

As of writing, there are five different Burger recipes available in Grow a Garden. These are all of varying rarities requiring different ingredients, all of which are listed below.

Note that all Burgers, regardless of rarity or ingredients used, take 7 minutes to cook.

How to make a Burger

With any of the ingredients listed above, head on over to Chris P. Bacon’s cooking station to get started on cooking your Burger.

  • Go to pig chef Chris P. Bacon in the central portion of the map.
  • Interact with the steel cooking pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them in the cooking pot.
  • Press the green Cook button.
  • Wait the allotted cooking time and retrieve your Burger.

As with any recipe in Grow a Garden, you can cook whatever you wish at any time. However, we’d recommend saving all your hard-earned ingredients and using them to satisfy Chris P. Bacon’s craving, displayed above his head in a chat bubble. Doing this will unlock exclusive rewards.
