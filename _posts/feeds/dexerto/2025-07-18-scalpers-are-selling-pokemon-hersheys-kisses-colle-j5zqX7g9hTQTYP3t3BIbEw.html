Rare Hershey Kisses Pokemon collector’s cases are being resold on eBay for as much as $350, following a limited-time raffle hosted by Hershey earlier this year.

The exclusive case was part of a promotional giveaway tied to the Hershey Kisses collaboration with Pokemon. While the Kisses themselves could be purchased in stores, the collector’s case was never available for direct sale.

Winners of the sweepstakes received the box as a special storage case designed to hold the collaboration-themed chocolates. It stores them upside down to show the pictures of Pokemon that are printed on the wrapper.

The case features artwork of fan-favorite Pokéemon like Pikachu, Jigglypuff, and Snorlax, with compartments inside tailored to fit the specially wrapped Hershey Kisses.

Only a limited number were made available, and the item has now become a hot commodity among both Pokemon and Hershey collectors.

Scalpers sell Pokemon Hershey’s collector’s cases for hundreds

Listings on eBay show the case reaching prices up to $350, with most of the top listings selling the boxes without any chocolate included.

There are a fair number of cheaper sold auctions, however, with some going for $200-$250 on the lower end of the spectrum.

For those looking to just buy the Kisses in search of completing your own chocolate Pokedex, you can find them in bags for six bucks or nearly complete sets for $250.

This isn’t the first time a Nintendo-related product has made its way to eBay in 2025. Shortly after the reveal of the Switch 2 back in April, pre-orders went live for fans worldwide.
