The Wire actor Isiah Whitlock Jr. has died at the age of 71, and if you want to watch his most popular and acclaimed role, here’s where you can stream The Wire.

Whitlock’s manager Brian Liebman shared the news on Instagram, writing: “It is with tremendous sadness that I share the passing of my dear friend and client Isiah Whitlock Jr.

“If you knew him – you loved him. A brilliant actor and even better person. May his memory forever be a blessing. Our hearts are so broken. He will be very, very missed.”

Whitlock Jr. appeared in multiple Spike Lee movies, while he also delivered a memorable turn as the US Secretary of Defense in Veep. But the actor is best know for his work on a modern TV classic…

Where to watch Isiah Whitlock Jr. as Clay Davis in The Wire

The Wire is available to stream on HBO, having broadcast on the network between 2002 and 2008.

Whitlock plays corrupt state senator Clay Davis in the series, a character who became best-known for saying “sheeeee-it.” Though the actor first elongated the curse word in Spike Lee movie She Hate Me, before The Wire popularized it.

If you don’t have a subscription to HBO, you can see Clay swearing on individual episodes of the series that are available to rent or buy on the likes of Prime Video, Apple TV, and Fandango at Home.

Spike Lee pays tribute to longtime collaborator

As well as appearing in She Hate Me, Whitlock acted in Spike Lee joints 25th Hour, Red Hook Summer, Chi-Raq, BlacKkKlansman, and Da 5 Bloods.
