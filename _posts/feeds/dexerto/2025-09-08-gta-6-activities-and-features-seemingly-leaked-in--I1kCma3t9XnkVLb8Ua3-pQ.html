A series of possible in-game GTA 6 websites have been revealed, pointing to some new activities and features that haven’t been confirmed just yet.

GTA VI is still slated to release on May 26, 2026 and so far, Rockstar has kept details about the game to a minimum, only dropping a couple of trailers and some screenshots.

While fans have meticulously dug through the 2022 gameplay leaks and have a good idea of what the map for the state of Leonida will look like, they’ve been yearning for some more news from Rockstar for ages.

On September 8, insider Tez2 shared that Rockstar’s parent company, Take-Two Interactive, registered a series of domains that all appear to be related to GTA VI and they give some good insight into what types of new gameplay features will be included once the game finally launches.

GTA 6 leak reveals parodies of Uber, WhatsApp and mature websites

In a post on GTA Forums, Tez2 explained that all the domains he found appear to be related to GTA 6.

“I came across some domains all registered on May 27 under Take-Two’s nameservers that seem to be VI-related. These domains could be in-game sites that R* may redirect to VI’s page later on, like what we’ve seen with IV and V,” he said. “Or some could be completely unrelated instead.”

One of the sites, ‘what-up.app’ is a clear parody of WhatsApp and was already known in previous leaks. Another, ‘rydeme.app’ is likely a ride-share service like Uber or Lyft.

Having a ride-share app in GTA 6 would make sense, especially as the series has included taxis that let players fast travel across the map.

Another app, ‘buckme.app’ could be some sort of parody of Cash App or PayPal, but some users on the forum suspect it might be referencing Patreon or a crowdfunding type platform. Others are going a completely different direction and think it could be a dating app such as Tinder.

The website ‘brianandbradley’ is likely tied to some sort of luxury clothing store. A different site, ‘h**kers-galore’ is pretty obvious, especially considering encountering ladies of the night is a series staple.

It’s also possible that during a story mission, Lucia, one of the game’s playable characters, may need to lure unsuspecting clients using the site and rob them.

The final two are a bit more mysterious, titled ‘wipeoutcornskin’ and ‘myboyhasacreepycorndog.’ GTA is known for its wild, random humor, and these could be tied to side quests or special mysteries.
