CiCi’s Pizza has partnered with popular Twitch streamer CaseOh to launch a pizza named after him, rebranding one of its existing fan-favorite menu items.

The collaboration introduces the Mac & CaseOh Pizza, which is CiCi’s longtime mac and cheese pizza renamed in honor of CaseOh and his favorite flavors.

According to the company, the Mac & CaseOh Pizza combines macaroni and cheese with CiCi’s garlic butter brushed crust, topped with additional cheese. The pizza is now available at participating CiCi’s buffet locations.

Mac & CaseOh Pizza launches nationwide at CiCi’s

The popular pizza chain confirmed that the Mac & CaseOh Pizza is available as part of its buffet lineup, allowing guests to grab individual slices. The brand noted that the pizza is not available in deep-dish or stuffed-crust options.

CaseOh, a viral Twitch streamer known for his large online following, is featured prominently in the collaboration branding, with CiCi’s positioning the rename as a tribute rather than a brand-new recipe.

The Mac & CaseOh Pizza is currently listed on its website and in-store promotional materials, with availability varying by location. CiCi’s has not announced an end date for the collaboration.
