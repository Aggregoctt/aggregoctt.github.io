Grand Theft Auto Online players have a final chance to unlock one of the rarest masks in the game’s history, as it has returned after an 11-year absence. 

With Grand Theft Auto 6 on the horizon, GTA Online players have started trying to tick off a few things that they may have missed over the years. Some fans are trying to collect rare cars, others are just trying to run up their bank balance, while others just want chances to get rare items. 

There are some rare items that just won’t make a return at any point, including the Rockstar Crosswalk T-shirt. However, Rockstar are bringing some of them back in the 2025 Halloween update.

GTA Online’s October 2 weekly update unveiled the Vinewood Undead Collection, which is four pieces of spooky season clothing that have been missing for years. The most interesting of the lot is the PLEASE STOP ME Mask. The mask hasn’t been since in GTA Online since May 2014. 

How to get PLEASE STOP ME mask in GTA Online (Halloween 2025)

That’s right, after more than 11 years, GTA Online players finally have the chance to get their hands on the yellow PLEASE STOP ME Mask once again. 

To get it, you have to complete the first three weeks of Weekly Challenges in GTA Online for October. 

The first challenge is to survive two Waves of a Survival without dying. This will also net you the Brown Vintage Zombie Mask and $200,000 to spend in-game. Further challenges will be released each Thursday. 

The PLEASE STOP ME Mask was originally only available through crate drops and games of Last Team Standing. 

While the mask is the rarest of the returning items, the Vinewood Zombie t-shirt hasn’t been around since 2017, so it’s rarely seen, too. The Slasher Clown Outfit, however, is brand-new. 

Rockstar hasn’t yet announced what will happen to Online, but many expect it to be shuttered and replaced when GTA 6 drops.
