The Simpsons is coming to Fortnite, and a new leak has revealed a stacked Battle Pass full of iconic characters, as well as the entire Springfield map.

Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 4 is set to be a crossover with The Simpsons, the long-running cartoon that’s conjured up countless memorable quotes and episodes over the years. Not only is the game going completely cell-shaded as part of the mini-season, but fans will also have a fully explorable version of Springfield.

Although the update isn’t due to arrive until November 1, leaks have already begun trickling out. Now, thanks to a number of dataminers, we can see both the map layout and the familiar faces who make up the Battle Pass.

Fortnite x The Simpsons Springfield map leaked

‘Jorge_Most’ was able to post the map in its entirety, giving us a good look at the layout, although it’s tough to make out exactly what each POI is.

In the top right-hand corner is clearly the power plant where Homer works, next to what is likely Lake Springfield and Kamp Krusty. On the left, there is also a series of houses, where you’ll find the homes of The Simpsons and Flanders.

The others are slightly tougher to make out, but the center looks a lot like Springfield Square, where the courthouse is located. There’s also a sandy area, which will probably be Springfield Gorge.

However, it’s worth remembering that this is just a leak right now, so nothing is confirmed. Epic Games partner ‘EDMIRE‘ also mentioned that while this is a good representation of the size, it’s an “unfinished” version of the map, so it could look different when it finally arrives.

The Simpsons Fortnite skins leaked

Leaks have also revealed a number of characters who will appear in the Battle Pass as skins. According to ‘blortzen’ and FNBRintel,’ the new BP will contain Homer Simpson, Marge Simpson, and Ned Flanders.

As always, there will also be some original Fortnite characters who have been given a makeover; this time Peely and Fishsticks, who can both be seen in the leaked key art.

This artwork also shows a Bart skin where he is piloting a mech, much like the Morty skin from back in 2021, as well as Krusty the Clown and bartender Moe Szyslak, who are expected to arrive later.

If you’ve been a fan of The Simpsons since it first aired in the 1980s, this crossover is a dream come true. There isn’t much longer to wait, either, as the season goes live on November 1.
