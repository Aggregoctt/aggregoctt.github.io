Pizza Hut has introduced a limited-time “6-7 Menu” that turns Gen Alpha’s latest slang obsession into a food deal. The 2-day promotion runs from November 6 through November 7 and offers boneless wings for 67 cents each.

The breaded boneless wings are available in nine flavors, including sauces like Garlic Parmesan, Honey BBQ, Spicy Garlic, and Buffalo Burnin’ Hot, as well as dry rubs like Lemon Pepper and Cajun-Style.

The deal applies to carryout and delivery orders placed through Pizza Hut’s website or app. All customers have to do is use use promo code “SIXSEVEN” at checkout.

Pizza Hut isn’t the only chain going quiet-mode with 6-7-themed deals. On November 5, some Domino’s franchise locations began offering large carryout pizzas for $6.70 with the promo code “67.”

What is the 6-7 meme?

The 6-7 phrase, pronounced “six seven,” is part of a viral slang trend with unclear meaning. The phrase appears in the song “Doot Doot (6 7)” by Skrilla and is also linked to NBA star LaMelo Ball, who stands 6-foot-7.

It’s used humorously online, often with a shrug or palm-up hand motion. Some interpret it as “so-so,” while others take it more literally to refer to someone tall.
