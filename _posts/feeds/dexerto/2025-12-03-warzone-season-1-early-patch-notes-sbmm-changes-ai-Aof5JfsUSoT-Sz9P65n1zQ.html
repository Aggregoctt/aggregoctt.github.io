Activision

Warzone integrates with Black Ops 7 as part of the Season 1 update, but it also brings a host of new changes.

Every year, the mainline Call of Duty title integrates with Warzone in the Season 1 update. This resets your player level while adding a ton of new weapons, Operators, and gameplay changes.

The devs have revealed some of the changes you’ll see in the update, so here are the early patch notes.

Warzone Season 1 arrives on Thursday, December 4, at 9am PT / 12pm ET / 5pm GMT.

The update can be preloaded already, so make sure you check the Call of Duty app for a (large) update.

Haven’s Hollow Resurgence map

The Haven’s Hollow Resurgence map arrives with the following POIs:

  • Mansion
  • Pond
  • Train Station
  • Barn
  • Research Center
  • Main Street
  • River Boat
  • Coal Depot
  • Lumbermill

Verdansk map changes

The following POIs will be added to Verdansk:

  • Signal Station
  • Factory

Black Ops 7 weapons & Operators

  • All of Black Ops 7’s weapons added (including S1 additions)
  • Weapon Build Sharing enabled for Black Ops 7 weapons
  • All of Black Ops 7’s Operators added (including S1 additions)

Perks & Wildcards

  • Gunfighter Wildcard removed
  • Overkill Perk removed and will now be enabled by default
  • Adaptive Perk added
  • Sprinter Perk added, giving Tactical Sprint
  • Reactive Armor Perk triggers an EMP pulse when armor breaks
  • Survivor Perk provides one-hit kill protection and moved to Perk 3
  • Bomb Squad Perk reduces damage from killstreaks
  • Mountaineer Perk has combat roll ability, further reduces fall damage, and improves ladder climb speed

Equipment

  • Pinpoint Grenade Tactical added (Ground Loot Only)
    • Provides sustained reconnaissance for a short duration. When active, it deploys tracking darts at enemies entering its radius, marking their positions for you and your squad. Each pulse can tag multiple targets over time, but enemies can remove the dart.
  • Psych Grenade Tactical added
    • Releases a disorienting red gas that can spawn phantom enemies, creating visual, audio, and minimap distortion within its radius.
  • Phantom Signal Tactical added
    • When deployed, it forces up to three nearby foes to emit distorted audio cues while briefly revealing their locations on the compass.
  • Needle Drone Lethal added (Ground Loot Only)
    • A compact, throwable mini-drone that detonates on impact. It can be remotely steered and manually triggered within a brief six-second control window.
  • Combat Axe Lethal added with improved throw speed
  • Throwing Knives removed
  • Echo Unit Field Upgrade added
    • Creates a lifelike decoy that mirrors your Operator’s appearance and stance.
  • C4 no longer requires the remote detonator
  • Flash Grenades can be cooked for up to 3 additional flashes
  • Stim Shot can be used immediately after being hit by a Flash or Concussion grenade to remove their effects entirely
  • Smoke Grenade can douse lingering flames left behind by a Molotov Cocktail or even a Napalm Killstreak

Movement

  • Tac Sprint is now part of the Sprinter Perk, with base movement speed increased to compensate.
  • Wall Jump and the Grapple Hook Tactical will be tested in Limited-Time Modes.

Skill-based matchmaking

  • Open-matchmaking added
    • Same matchmaking as Black Ops 7, where “skill consideration is drastically reduced.”

Aim assist

  • Aim assist nerfed to match Black Ops 7
    • Your right stick movement must be tracking an enemy target for Rotational Aim Assist to activate at full strength
