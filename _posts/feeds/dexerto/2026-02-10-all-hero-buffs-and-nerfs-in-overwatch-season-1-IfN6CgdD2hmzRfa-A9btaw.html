The Overwatch Season 1 update brings a ton of hero buffs and nerfs that look set to completely change the meta going forward.

Season 1 marks the beginning of a whole new chapter for Overwatch, one that puts the focus on storytelling. After dropping the two from its name, the game will now serve up a yearly story before resetting after 12 months.

As part of this new shift, there are five new characters being added to the roster. But that doesn’t mean that the existing heroes are being ignored, as the huge batch brings plenty of balancing changes designed to level the playing field.

Here are all of the buffs and nerfs in the Overwatch Season 1 update. A
