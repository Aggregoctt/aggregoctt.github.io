How to redeem codes

You’ll need to join the game’s official community group before redeeming any codes. After that’s done, follow these steps to enter a code and claim your rewards:

  • Press M on your keyboard to open the main menu.
  • Click on ‘Shop.’
  • Click on Miscellaneous from the new menu that pops up.
  • Enter the code into the text box.
  • Click on ‘Redeem Code’ to claim the rewards.
