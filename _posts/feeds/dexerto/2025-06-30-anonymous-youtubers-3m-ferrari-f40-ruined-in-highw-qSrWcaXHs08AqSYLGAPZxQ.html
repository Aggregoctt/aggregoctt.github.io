Anonymous car YouTuber Supercar Ron is offering a reward after his $3M Ferrari F40 was hit by a semi driving down the highway.

Despite having just under 100k subscribers on YouTube, Supercar Ron has gained a sizable following thanks to the fact that he’s remained completely anonymous in his videos.

Instead of showing his true identity, Ron wears what he describes as “silly beards” and mustaches because he wants to make videos focused on the rare cars he owns — not himself.

While he remains in videos on YouTube, his TikTok features Tace Anderson, a man who was hired to drive around in Ron’s cars and make short-form video content about them.

On Sunday, June 29, however, Tace’s drive went horribly wrong after a semi truck totalled one of the YouTuber’s rarest cars.

$3 million Ferrari F40 totalled by “long lug nuts”

“Alright if you wanna know how your night goes bad really quick, it’s when a semi truck side swipes you in an F40,” said Tace in a viral video on TikTok.

“So, this just happened. I was driving, and a semi truck with those big lug nuts got a little bit too close. He hit the side of the car, side-swiped me all the way across, and got back in his lane. I cannot believe this. Holy crap.”

Ron also made a statement on Instagram offering a reward for anyone who could help find the semi that hit his car.

“We need help tracking this person down,” he said in the post.

“Hit and run drivers need to be held responsible. If you know who this could be or were in the area southbound I-15 around 10 pm near north Orem or Lindon and have dash cam footage, please let me know if you have any video. Or any other surrounding business that have a view of South bound I-15 if you have security footage, please let us know. Reward offered if we can find who did this.”

Supercar Ron purchased the car back in November 2024 and gave fans a tour of the $3M Ferrari in a video on YouTube.

Since then, both he and Tace have made several videos on it, including one uploaded to TikTok just three days before the accident.
