Streamer Caseoh has shared a terrifying story about thinking his father had died after a four-wheeler fell on top of him, before confirming his dad survived with serious but non-life-threatening injuries.

During a November 7 stream, Caseoh explained that he and his father were riding their four-wheelers on a new hiking trail when the accident happened. His dad attempted to drive down a steep 20-foot slope, but lost control and rolled down the hill. Caseoh said he thought his father had been killed instantly when the vehicle landed on him. The clip quickly spread across social media as fans expressed concern for the streamer and his family.

Caseoh later posted an update on X to let viewers know his dad was alive, writing: “My dad is okay, 3 broken ribs and a collapsed lung, but no other internal injuries. He’s in pain but in a good mood. I’d hope I could survive flipping a 4 wheeler off a 20 foot cliff at 58 years old like him.”

The streamer reassured fans that his father is expected to make a full recovery despite the extent of his injuries. Many in Caseoh’s community responded with relief and support, praising the 58-year-old’s toughness and sending well-wishes during his recovery.

Caseoh, best known for his viral TikTok clips and high-energy Twitch streams, has become one of the platform’s most watched creators in 2024–25. His openness about family moments, from lighthearted anecdotes to serious incidents like this, has continued to draw his audience closer.
