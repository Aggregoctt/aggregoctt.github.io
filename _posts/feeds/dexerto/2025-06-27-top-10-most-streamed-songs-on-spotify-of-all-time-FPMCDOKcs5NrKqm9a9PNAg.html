Ever since Spotify was launched in 2008, it’s been the home of some of the most-streamed songs released in nearly 20 years.

Some of the biggest names in the industry top the list, including the likes of Post Malone, Drake, Ed Sheeran, The Weeknd, and more.

On June 26, 2025, it was reported that Post Malone and Swae Lee’s ‘Sunflower’ became the most-streamed song in the United States with 3.96 billion plays on the app. It was released back in 2018 as a song on the soundtrack for the film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse.

But Posty’s most popular song still has a way to go before becoming the most-streamed song globally.

Top 10 most-streamed songs on Spotify of all time

Here are the 10 most-streamed songs on all of Spotify:

  1. Blinding Lights – The Weeknd – 4.89 billion
  2. Shape of You – Ed Sheeran – 4.4 billion
  3. Sunflower – Post Malone & Swae Lee – 3.96 billion
  4. Starboy – The Weeknd & Daft Punk – 3.95 billion
  5. Someone You Loved – Lewis Capaldi – 3.94 billion
  6. As It Was – Harry Styles – 3.93 billion
  7. Sweater Weather – The Neighbourhood – 3.85 billion
  8. One Dance – Drake, Wizkid, Kyla – 3.68 billion
  9. Stay – The Kid Laroi & Justin Bieber – 3.58 billion
  10. Perfect – Ed Sheeran – 3.49 billion

Throughout the rest of the Top 100 most-streamed songs, you’ll find some of the biggest names in music, including Dua Lipa, Billie Eilish and Kendrick Lamar, who all have multiple songs on the list.

Another popular Spotify artist is TikToker and Hype House co-founder Alex Warren, who released his wedding hit Ordinary in early 2024 and almost instantly skyrocketed in popularity.
