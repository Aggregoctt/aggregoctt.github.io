James Carter Cathcart, a beloved voice actor best known for his work on the Pokemon anime, has died at the age of 71.

Cathcart was a key figure in the English-language adaptation of Pokemon, lending his voice to several of the franchise’s most recognizable characters. He played Gary Oak, Ash Ketchum’s rival, as well as Professor Oak, the wise mentor guiding young trainers. Cathcart was also the voice behind two of Team Rocket’s most iconic members: James and his friend, Meowth.

For decades, his performances helped shape the childhoods of millions of fans around the world. His range, humor, and heart brought these characters to life and became an essential part of the show’s global success.

Fellow Pokemon voice actor Erica Schroeder shared a heartfelt tribute on social media, honoring Cathcart’s life and legacy.

“Rest in peace dear sweet man. I will miss you. The community will miss you. The world will miss you,” Schroeder wrote. “One of the most joyful, exuberant, kind-hearted and talented souls no longer walks with us. James Carter Cathcart you were one of a kind, a gentle, beautiful, playful genius and I was happy to call you my friend.”

She described him as a master of many talents—“the pen, piano, and microphone”—and remembered him as a loving father, husband, and friend to many.

Cathcart had stepped away from voice acting in recent years following a cancer diagnosis, with his final work on Pokemon wrapping in 2023 after nearly 25 years with the series.
