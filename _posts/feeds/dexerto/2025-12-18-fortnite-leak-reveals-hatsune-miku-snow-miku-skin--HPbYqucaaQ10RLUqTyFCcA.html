The long-awaited Snow Miku skin themed around virtual idol Hatsune Miku has leaked ahead of this year’s Fortnite Winterfest update.

The festive variant surfaced via datamines on December 18, with leaked images showcasing the full bundle ahead of Epic’s official reveal.

Winterfest has kicked off, bringing snow back to the battle royale island alongside a packed holiday lineup. This year’s event is expected to include a customizable Harry Potter-style outfit that lets players pick a Hogwarts house, a Christmas-themed Sabrina Carpenter skin, and a Yule Trooper boss encounter.

As with previous years, Fortnite‘s Winterfest will also rotate daily gifts and limited-time cosmetics through the Lodge. However, it’s the virtual idol that has many fans excited, and we now have our first look at the long-awaited Snow Miku skin.

Snow Miku bundle

While pricing has not been confirmed, leakers have revealed that the Snow Miku bundle will hit the Item Shop on December 19.

Leaked images show the bundle includes multiple themed cosmetics, with her Rabbit Yukine as Back Bling, an icicle-style guitar, a Miku-themed Jam Track, and a new Snow Mix emote.

Snow Miku herself also appears in her iconic winter outfit, complete with white-and-blue color accents and snowflake detailing, adapting the long-running seasonal design with added Fortnite flair.

Just like the previous Hatsune Miku skin release, the Snow Miku bundle could mark one of Fortnite’s most high-profile virtual idol crossovers yet, so expect to see many a Miku running around this Holiday season.
