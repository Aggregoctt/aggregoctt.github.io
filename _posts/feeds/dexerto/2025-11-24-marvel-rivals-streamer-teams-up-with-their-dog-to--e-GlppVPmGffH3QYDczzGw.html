A dog named Pea has won hearts on social media by teaming up with her owner to play Marvel Rivals.

Marvel Rivals can be fun on its own, but Twitch streamer poopernoodle took it to a whole new level by teaming up with her dog, Pea, who quickly became a social media sensation.

In a viral clip, her sausage dog, Pea, is seen helping them play the competitive shooter. Using a special button setup, Pea was actually able to heal teammates while playing as Jeff the Land Shark.

The wholesome moment has taken off, with the clip already surpassing 1M views on X as commenters flood in with praise.

Twitch streamer plays Marvel Rivals with their dog

By teaming up, the streamer and their dog essentially controlled the same character. The streamer controlled movement and most abilities, while Pea handled dropping Jeff’s healing bubbles for teammates to pick up. 

To make it work, poopernoodle set up a separate macro key bound to Jeff’s Ability 1, which Pea could hit with her paw.

Before heading into a real match, the streamer spent some time in the practice range showing Pea what to do. She picked it up quickly, and, of course, earned plenty of treats along the way.

When it came time for actual match, the duo worked surprisingly well together. Pea sat right behind the streamer, keeping a close eye on the action and dropping healing bubbles during team fights or when she was asked to.

Viewers on social media couldn’t get enough of her antics, calling her “adorable” and “cute.” 

“1000% better than any Jeff I’ve ever played with,” one person wrote. 

Another added: “Look, I have never played Marvel Rivals, but if it’s what it takes to get healed by Pea, I WILL start playing Marvel Rivals.”
