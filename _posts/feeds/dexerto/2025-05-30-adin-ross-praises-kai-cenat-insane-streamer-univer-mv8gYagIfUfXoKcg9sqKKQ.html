Adin Ross showed some love to Kai Cenat and his Streamer University despite the supposed beef between the two big-name creators. 

At one point in time, Kai Cenat and Adin Ross were pretty tight. The pair would regularly talk on stream and appeared in each other’s content, but that slowed down following Adin’s ban from Twitch. 

Since being unbanned by the Amazon-owned platform, Adin has denied that there is any ongoing drama between them. Though he has sent shots Kai’s way by saying big streamers have an “ego” and that he was one of the creators to not contact him following the suspension being lifted. 

When Kai hosted his Streamer University, the Kick co-owner was slightly busy and didn’t appear on his own stream to weigh in. Now that the dust has settled, he has shown some love to the AMP star. 

Adin Ross calls Streamer University “insane”

“I wasn’t streaming at that time, but if you guys just flat out didn’t f*ck with it or didn’t see it, you’re just hating. What Kai did with Streamer University was f**king insane,” Adin said during his May 28 stream.

“He elevated and changed the game again. Anyone who is just talking s*it is just hating. The Streamer University was so f**king big. Amazing for streaming. It was absolutely f**king great.”

Adin was also pleased that Kai got to link up with NBA superstar LeBron James, calling that a “huge” moment as well.

Streamer University was an undeniable success for Kai and Twitch. It had over 27 million hours of watch time and Kai had over 600,000 viewers for his introductory stream.

A number of the 150 creators that attended have had big growth too, with a few being showered in gifted subs from other big streamers.
