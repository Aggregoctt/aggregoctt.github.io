In Elden Ring Nightreign, changing your character’s outfit, or “skin,” is a popular way to personalize your look and stand out in the world.

But unlike most RPGs, this customization option isn’t immediately available. Many players reach the early stages of the game without knowing how to access or equip new skins. The option to change appearance is tied to specific progress milestones, and until you meet them, the feature remains completely hidden.

If you’re wondering why your newly acquired outfit isn’t showing up or how to make your character look different, this guide will help. We’ll walk you through unlocking the dressing room, using the Fitting Mirror, and equipping your favorite looks step by step.

How to change skins in Elden Ring

You can change your skin by unlocking the Fitting Mirror in Roundtable Hold and interacting with it in the east wing, next to the fireplace.

How to unlock the Fitting Mirror

Before you can change outfits, you need to unlock the dressing room feature:

  1. Complete two expeditions and defeat two Nightlords.
  2. After these objectives are complete, return to Roundtable Hold.
  3. The NPC will appear and notify you that the dressing room is now available.

This unlocks the ability to change skins through a special interface.

Where is the Fitting Mirror

The Fitting Mirror is found in the easternmost room of the Roundtable Hold.

  1. Interact with the mirror to open the outfit menu.
  2. Here, you can browse, select, and equip available skins.

Unlocking more skins

Here’s how you can buy new skins:

  1. Skins are purchased using Murk, which is earned by completing expeditions or selling relics.
  2. Visit the Small Jar Merchant in Roundtable Hold to purchase new skins.
  3. Once purchased, use the Fitting Mirror to equip them.
  4. Skins are class-specific, so available outfits depend on your chosen class.

You can also access the dressing room directly from the map menu by selecting “Change Garb” for quick outfit swaps.
