When Nintendo launched the GameCube in 2001, it doubled down on what it has always done best: precision, polish, and games that simply felt great to play. The compact purple cube traded DVD movies and massive hard drives for lightning-fast load times, a controller that became an instant classic, and a first-party lineup tuned for responsiveness and fluid movement. Rather than chasing multimedia features or raw specs, the GameCube was built around gameplay-first design.

Its library is packed with cult classics and enduring icons. Super Smash Bros. Melee became a competitive staple that still thrives today. The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker and Twilight Princess offered bold artistic and epic adventure in equal measure. Metroid Prime reimagined first-person exploration, while Resident Evil 4 set a new standard for action-horror. Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, Super Mario Sunshine, and Luigi’s Mansion kept Nintendo’s signature charm at the forefront, and deeper cuts like Eternal Darkness, Tales of Symphonia, and Skies of Arcadia broadened the console’s appeal.

Though it finished behind its rivals commercially, the GameCube earned a legacy built on quality, creativity, and games that aged beautifully.

With that in mind, here’s our ranking of the best GameCube games.
