Political commentary streamer Hasan Piker has had his Twitch account banned once again, marking his first strike of 2026, but far from his first ban overall.

Hasan’s Twitch account was taken offline at roughly 7PM PT on January 29, 2026. This came midway through a broadcast wherein he was discussing a recent murder in America.

It’s unconfirmed, but it appears certain comments pertaining to the murder resulted in his ban, as Hasan himself addressed the situation on X (formerly Twitter) moments after, pointing to his own coverage of the divisive situation.

At the time of writing, it’s currently unclear how long the Twitch ban may last. Notably, the majority of Hasan’s previous bans on the streaming platform have lasted a single day.

Hasan’s Twitch account banned again

Currently, Hasan’s Twitch account is disabled, meaning you can’t access the channel whatsoever. There’s no indication when it might be restored, but history could give us an idea.

Far from his first ban on the purple streaming giant, he was also recently struck offline for 24 hours back in March, 2025. That ban stemmed from heated comments that Hasan himself admitted were over the line. “Ok, so obviously I shouldn’t have used hyperbolic language, and there’s definitely some ownership I must take,” he said at the time.

A dedicated tracking site lists six bans total against Hasan’s account. Two of them lasted a week each, while the rest were for just one day or less.

We’ll be sure to update you here when further details emerge and Hasan finds his way back to streaming in front of tens of thousands of viewers once again.
