How to redeem codes

You don’t need to go through any tutorial to redeem codes, but your account must be at least one month old before you can start. Once that’s met, you can follow the steps below:

  • Click the ‘Menu‘ option in the top-left corner of the screen.
  • Select the shopping cart icon from the pop-up menu.
  • Enter the code into the text box that appears in the menu.
  • Click on ‘REDEEM‘ to claim the reward.

What are Onikami codes used for?

Onikami codes are especially valuable because they can reward Sun Essence, one of the game’s most valuable resources. Sun Essence allows Demon players to gain immunity to sunlight, removing a big survival restriction, and is also required to unlock Sun Breathing, one of the strongest techniques available. Since this item is normally locked behind high-level bosses or rare drops, getting it through codes can save a huge amount of time.
