Twitch is finally releasing a new feature that allows creators to stream in both vertical and horizontal formats, making it easier for mobile viewers to watch their broadcasts.

Vertical livestreams started gaining popularity on apps like TikTok and Instagram, where the entire app is designed around that video format.

Back in February 2024, YouTube added vertical livestreams to its Shorts feed to accompany regular horizontal broadcasts, which allows creators on its platform to stream in both formats at the same time.

Twitch is now following in YouTube’s footsteps and has revealed the ability to stream vertically for mobile users to watch their broadcast even easier.

Twitch reveals vertical streaming

The feature was announced at TwitchCon Europe and shared in a blog post on Twitch’s website. It reads: “When viewers come to your channel, we want to make sure they’re getting the best possible viewing experience, no matter what screen they’re watching on.”

Once the feature is available, users viewing on desktop will only be able to see landscape streams. On mobile, however, viewers will see your stream vertically if they hold the phone upright and landscape if they hold their phone horizontally.

“The new vertical layout makes it easier for viewers to participate in events like Hype Trains and support you by subbing, gifting, and cheering while watching your stream in full screen. They’ll also be able to customize chat to have more control over how much chat they see on screen,” it reads.

As for availability, Twitch says it will roll out to a small number of channels this summer before slowly expanding to more users later this year.

That’s not the only feature they’re releasing in the coming months, either, as they’re expanding beta access to 2K resolution streaming for Partners and Affiliates.
