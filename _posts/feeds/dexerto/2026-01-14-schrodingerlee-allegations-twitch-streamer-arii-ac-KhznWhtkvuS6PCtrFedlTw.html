Twitch streamer Arii has published a lengthy document accusing fellow streamer and YouTuber schrodingerLee of non-consensual filming, repeated dishonesty, and making false allegations of blackmail.

The document, released publicly on January 12, includes screenshots, chat logs, call records, and timestamps from Twitch VODs. Arii alleges that schrodingerLee filmed sexual encounters without consent on multiple occasions during their relationship, which she claims he later admitted to.

According to the document, schrodingerLee initially went live to address the situation, framing the disclosure as an attempt to “take accountability.”

Arii claims she advised against making the issue public at that time. Following backlash, she alleges his explanations shifted repeatedly, with new claims emerging around harassment, swatting attempts, and threats against his family.

The document states that schrodingerLee later accused her of blackmail, claiming she demanded money under threat of going public or legal action. Arii denies this, saying the money discussed was framed as compensation and financial support after the alleged non-consensual filming, and that recordings of their conversations show no threat of punishment.

Transcripts included in the document reference a recorded conversation in which the streamer allegedly discussed paying Arii approximately $18,000–$20,000 to help cover housing costs after the incident. She says she later rejected a remaining payment and acknowledges in the document that asking for compensation was a mistake made while distressed.

Arii further alleges that schrodingerLee privately admitted fault multiple times while publicly suggesting she pressured or manipulated him. Screenshots included show messages in which he apparently says the consequences were “his fault,” followed later by claims that he was coerced.

The document also challenges schrodingerLee’s public statements about being in immediate physical danger. It includes claims from third parties, including a former roommate, disputing past swatting stories and saying similar incidents never occurred. The statement alleges these danger claims were exaggerated or fabricated to gain sympathy and deflect criticism.

Additional allegations include emotional cheating, followed by claims of physical cheating at TwitchCon 2024. In an update dated January 14, the accuser says another individual contacted her to confirm that physical cheating did occur, separate from a previously known situation. This claim came after the document was initially published.

schrodingerLee is also accused of forging private messages to support his version of events. Screenshots appear to show an apology message in which he admits to fabricating Discord messages and lying about their origin. Arii claims these were used to accuse her and others of attempting to have him jailed.

At the time of writing, schrodingerLee has not released a formal response addressing the allegations. He also has not streamed since January 3.
