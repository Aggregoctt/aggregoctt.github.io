Hot on the heels of Geoff Keighley’s Summer Game Fest showcase on June 6, Microsoft has taken to the stage to showcase all the games coming to Xbox and other platforms throughout 2025 and beyond.

You can find out when and where to watch the show for yourself with our handy guide. Otherwise, we’re updating this article in real time with every new game announcement so you can keep up to date on every one of Microsoft’s big reveals.

Once the show has wrapped, be sure to check back for trailers and all the details on every reveal.

Outer Wilds 2

Microsoft kicked off its 2025 showcase with a sizzle reel for Outer Wilds 2. Obsidian’s sequel is getting its own dedicated showcase following the conclusion of its main showcase but this trailer provided a taste of the first-person RPG.

High on Life 2

High on Life 2 is the sequel to Squanch Games’ original, released in 2022. Like the first game, High on Life 2 retains its comedic themes and first-person perspective, bursting with colour and fast, frenetic action.

Resonance

ROG Xbox Ally

Microsoft unveiled a first official look at the ROG Xbox Ally during its Games Showcase. The console, coming in late 2025, will be available in two versions, the Ally, and Ally X. Both will operate on Windows 11 and have been created in partnership with ASUS.
