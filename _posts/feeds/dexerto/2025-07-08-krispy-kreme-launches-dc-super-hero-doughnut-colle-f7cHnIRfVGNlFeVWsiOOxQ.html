Krispy Kreme has teamed up with Warner Bros. Discovery to release a limited-edition line of doughnuts inspired by DC Super Heroes.

The “Hungry for Heroes” collection features three doughnuts themed around Superman, Batman, and Wonder Woman, offering fans a sweet way to celebrate their favorite heroes.

Each doughnut is designed with signature colors, flavors, and decorations representing the iconic characters.

Superman, Batman, and Wonder Woman doughnuts arrive

The donuts are available at Krispy Kreme stores for a limited time and are available in three different variants.

The Superman doughnut is an unglazed shell filled with white Kreme, dipped in light blue icing, and topped with Superman buttercream-flavored clouds, an image of the hero, and sprinkles.

The Batman treat is filled with cookies & Kreme, dipped in black chocolate icing, and topped with the Bat emblem. The Wonder Woman variant features an Original Glazed dipped in red raspberry-flavored icing, finished with white stars, blue sprinkles, and a Wonder Woman logo and belt.

The doughnuts are available individually or in a themed box, in shops, for pickup or delivery through Krispy Kreme’s app and website, and in select retailers as part of a six-pack.

Krispy Kreme is also offering a promotion from July 24 through July 27. Guests who show appreciation for their favorite DC Super Hero while visiting participating locations can receive a free Hungry for Heroes doughnut with any purchase, while supplies last.
