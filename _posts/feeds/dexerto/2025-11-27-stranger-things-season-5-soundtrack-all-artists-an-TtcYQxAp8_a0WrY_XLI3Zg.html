Stranger Things is back with a bang, and Episodes 1-4 feature banging soundtracks, with every song and the corresponding artist listed below.

Stranger Things has given the world a cast of amazing characters to root for, as well as terrifying monsters to spice up our collective nightmares.

With a story that’s set in the 1980s, the sci-fi series also features hits from one of the great music decades, with the teens of Hawkins listening to a combination of pop, soul, and Eddie Munson’s favorite, rock.

Season 4 gave us an iconic sequence that was underscored by Kate Bush classic ‘Running Up That Hill,’ so to find out if Stranger Things has managed to equal or top that in Season 5, scroll down. Just be warned of SONG SPOILERS ahead…

Every song on the Stranger Things 5 soundtrack

From Diana Ross and Michael Jackson to ABBA, this is every artist and song on that plays during Season 5 of Stranger Things, listed in order of appearance.

Episode 1 – ‘The Crawl’

Thanks to Hawkins’ newest radio station, Chapter 1 gives us some disco and a Psychedelic Furs classic, while we also get a Kate Bush reprise.

Episode 2 – ‘The Vanishing of…’

Chapter 2 features some unexpected ABBA, a beautiful Chordettes song, and Kate Bush running up that hill, again.

Episode 3 – ‘The Turnbow Trap’

Chapter 3 gives us some Freddy Martin (and his orchestra), Tiffany’s shopping mall classic, and a Yello song that featured in multiple ’80s movies.

Episode 4 – Sorcerer

Kate Bush is back again in Chapter 4, as well as a beloved tune from The Great Escape, and some Chords.
