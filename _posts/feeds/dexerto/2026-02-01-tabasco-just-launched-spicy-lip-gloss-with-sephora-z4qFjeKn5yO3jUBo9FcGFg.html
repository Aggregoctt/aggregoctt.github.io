The Sephora Collection has teamed up with the TABASCO Brand for an unexpected limited-edition lip gloss collaboration that brings the heat of hot sauce into the beauty aisle.

Launched in January 2026, the Sephora Collection x TABASCO collaboration centers on a special run of Outrageous Plump Volume Effect Lip Glosses inspired by the iconic sauces. The line blends Sephora’s existing plumping formula with chili pepper extract, using capsaicin to create a warming and tingling sensation designed to mimic the heat associated with TABASCO products.

The collection includes four shades, each tied to a different sauce profile and heat level. Jalapeno is a clear gloss with a sheer green tint and a mild tingle, while Sriracha offers a warm translucent brown meant to deliver moderate spice.

Chili pepper extract makes lips plump

TABASCO Red is a bright red gloss with a high-shine finish, positioned as the boldest color in the lineup. Extreme Heat rounds out the collection with a clear black gloss featuring plum undertones and the strongest plumping sensation.

To offset the heat-focused concept, Sephora says the formula includes hyaluronic acid to provide up to eight hours of hydration. The glosses are also packaged in collectible silicone travel cases modeled after the TABASCO bottle label, complete with a key ring and chili charm.

The collaboration debuted online through Sephora’s website on January 6, 2026, before rolling out to physical Sephora stores worldwide starting January 12. Individual glosses are priced at around €15.99 or $16, with regional variations, and a limited-edition set featuring all four shades is also available.
