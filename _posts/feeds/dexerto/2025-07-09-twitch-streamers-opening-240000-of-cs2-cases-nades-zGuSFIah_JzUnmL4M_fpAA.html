A $240,000 CS2 case opening is going down on Friday, July 11, hosted by Twitch streamer ohnePixel alongside Nadeshot, TimTheTatman, and Arrow.

The grand unboxing, one of the largest ever taking place on stream, is largely thanks to a single high-tier collector, Megalodon888, who supplied the thousands of rare cases being opened.

The stream begins at 2pm ET (11am pt / 7pm BST) on Twitch, with all four creators expected to go live during the event.

The cases include some of the most valuable and sought-after in CS history, such as Cobblestone Souvenir packages, Bravo Cases, and CS:GO Weapon Case 1, which contain skins like the AWP Dragon Lore and AK-47 Fire Serpent and Case Hardened. These skins can be worth tens of thousands of dollars on the market, but the streamers will need to get lucky to pull one of these.

The entire case haul comes directly from Megalodon888, who is known for holding one of the largest and rarest skin collections in the CS2 community.

You can watch the streams on their respective Twitch channels: ohnePixel, Nadeshot, TimTheTatman, and Arrow.

This is expected to be the biggest CS2 case opening stream ever, with fans hoping for some big pulls, but statistically speaking, the $240,000 value of the cases is likely to be largely turned to dust, unless luck is really on their side.

While ohnePixel and Arrow are seasoned CS skin collectors, who have done big openings in the past, Nadeshot and TimTheTatman are relative newcomers to case openings. Nadeshot has already spent upwards of $50,000 buying cases, although did manage to pull a pair of gloves worth approximately $20,000, recouping some of the cost.
