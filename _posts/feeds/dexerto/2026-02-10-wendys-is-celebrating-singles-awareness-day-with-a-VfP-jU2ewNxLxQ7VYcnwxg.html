If you’re an unlucky soul who couldn’t get a date for Valentine’s Day, Wendy’s has your back with a $1 Dave’s Single cheeseburger for Singles Awareness Day.

Being single can be tough if you’re someone who craves companionship and doesn’t have $173K to spend on a Chinese AI robot. That’s where Wendy’s comes into play.

The burger chain has announced that on February 15, it’ll give singles something to celebrate in the form of a special cheeseburger deal.

The best part? Unlike a fancy Valentine’s date night where you’d likely spend hundreds of dollars, Wendy’s offer just costs you a single $1.

Wendy’s reveals $1 cheeseburger for hungry singles after Valentine’s Day

According to Wendy’s, the special deal is only available on February 15 and must be accompanied by a purchase.

The special deal for a $1 Dave’s Single will appear in the Wendy’s app.

“Forget the drama, the ghosting, the ‘left on read’ texts. At Wendy’s, we’ve got something that actually shows up: a Dave’s Single, reliable since 1969 because it never disappoints,” the chain explained.

While Wendy’s is celebrating singles, other chains are going all in on couples with their own unique offers.

McDonald’s announced a limited-time Valentine’s Day stunt that pairs its Chicken McNuggets with high-end caviar through kits sent to customers online.

Meanwhile, Denny’s is offering couples in Las Vegas the chance to get married for free at its Fremont Street restaurant.
