Better Late Than Single has been the latest dating show to obsess over, but its debut chapter is soon set to end with the Season 1 finale. To catch up on who ends up with who, be sure to check out when Episodes 9-10 land on Netflix. 

Netflix is home to some of the best new reality TV shows out there, from Love is Blind and Too Hot to Handle to The Devil’s Plan and Single’s Inferno.

If the latter title is more your vibe, no doubt you’ve been enjoying Better Late Than Single, a new dating series that sees contestants who’ve never had a relationship undergo a makeover before heading to a shared resort, all in the hope of finding love. 

Up until now, there have been plenty of ups and downs as the cast went on dates and explored their feelings, but in the two-episode finale, we’ll finally see which couples come together in the end.

The final two episodes of Better Late Than Single Season 1 land on Netflix on July 29 at 12am PT / 3am ET. If you’re outside the US, we’ve listed other time zones for you below:

  • 4am Brazil
  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Summer Time
  • 12:30pm India Standard Time
  • 4pm South Korea
  • 5pm Australia
  • 7pm New Zealand

How many episodes are there?

Better Late Than Single’s debut chapter consists of 10 episodes in total. Much like its other dating shows, the streaming service has released new episodes in batches as follows: 

  • Episode 1: July 8
  • Episode 2: July 8
  • Episode 3: July 8
  • Episode 4: July 15
  • Episode 5: July 15
  • Episode 6: July 15
  • Episode 7: July 22
  • Episode 8: July 22
  • Episode 9: July 29
  • Episode 10: July 29

Episodes 9-10 are expected to have the same runtime as the rest of the TV show: approximately 60-75 minutes each. 

Will there be a Season 2?

As of July 2025, Netflix hasn’t given the greenlight on Better Late Than Single Season 2. But that’s not to say it won’t ever. 

While the new TV show might not be on the Top 10 in the US, it’s currently in third place on the Netflix charts in South Korea, fifth place in Taiwan, and seventh place in Singapore. It also made it to Netflix’s Top 10 performing non-English shows last week. 

Single’s Inferno was a similar hit, and it was renewed for Season 5 earlier this year – the day after the Season 4 dropped. As such, we may even hear news of a Better Late Than Single Season 2 this week.
