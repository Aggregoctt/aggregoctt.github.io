Geoff Keighley’s gaming spectacle is back for another year, as The Game Awards 2025 is now underway. While we already have some idea of what to expect, there’s sure to be plenty of surprises throughout the show.

From the future of the Tomb Raider franchise to a mysterious statue in the desert, there’s been plenty to keep fans guessing in the lead-up, but today, we finally get some answers.

Alongside awards for the very best games of 2025, the show is set to feature blockbuster announcements shaping the next year. Could we finally see Half-Life 3? Is Persona 6 ready to steal our hearts? What about the next God of War?

You’re in the right place, as we’ll keep you updated right here with all the big announcements as the show rolls on.

The Game Awards 2025 announcements

The Free Shepherd

Kicking off the pre-show was this new indie game from Frame Interactive Studios.

Decrepit

A spooky first-person soulslike experience.

Audiomech

A flashy rhythm-based bullet hell where “your music is your partner.”

Not only did Pragmata get a release date, set for April 24, 2026, but a demo is also live now on Steam.

Bradley The Badger

A free-form platformer from a brand new indie studio, Day 4 Night, founded by a lead dev on the Red Dead Redemption team. It also stars actor Evan Peters.
