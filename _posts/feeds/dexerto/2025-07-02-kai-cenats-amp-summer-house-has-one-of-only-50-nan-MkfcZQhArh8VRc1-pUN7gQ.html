The house that Kai Cenat and AMP are using for their 30 days of summer streaming marathon actually has one of only 50 Nando’s in the whole United States.

Kai Cenat and the AMP crew have raising the bar when it comes to big Twitch streams. Kai, himself, has dominated Twitch with his Streamer University, Batman Arkham, and Mafiathon broadcasts, but things ramp up again when AMP get involved.

The creator collective has hosted a Summer of AMP marathon for the last few years, and this year, they’re based in Texas for the whole of July. 

While they had a $30 million penthouse last year in New York, they’ve gone up another level this year. They’re using NBA legend Tony Parker’s mansion as their home base, which has one of only 50 Nando’s restaurants in the whole United States inside. Seriously.

AMP Summer House has a Nando’s inside

Kai and AMP kicked off their AMP Summer streams on July 1, with the former San Antonio Spurs point guard joining them on a tour of the house. 

As well as showing off his NBA championships and accolades, the group also revealed that they need to “eat good” at a 24/7 Nando’s that is at Parker’s home. “I don’t think I’ve ever seen this in my life,” Kai said. 

The South African chicken restaurant is pretty popular in the UK and Europe, but in the United States, there are fewer than 50 spots. So, to have one inside the house is quite something.  

Timestamp of 36:10 

Given that they quickly quipped it was ‘Any Mains Possible’ on the menu, it’s likely a sponsorship between AMP and Nando’s. Yet, it is still impressive. 

The NBA Hall of Famer also confirmed that the AMP boys can “do whatever you want,” and that he is familiar with what they’ve been doing. 

“Don’t f*ck up my house now,” he told Kai. “I trust you guys. I know the concept, I know you did before, so it’s cool.”
