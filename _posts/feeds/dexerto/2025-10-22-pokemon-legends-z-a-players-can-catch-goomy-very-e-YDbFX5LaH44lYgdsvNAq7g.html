Setting up a good team early in Pokemon Legends Z-A can be a difficult task due to limited areas and low-level Pokemon. One Pokemon many have been eager to add to their teams is Goomy, the small Dragon-type with a powerful set of attacks. Thankfully, using this trick, players can grab Goomy as soon as the tutorial is over.

How to Get Goomy early in Pokemon Legends Z-A

To get a Goomy early in Pokemon Legends Z-A, players must complete the main tutorials and unlock bench time skipping. This is going to be a key factor in catching a Goomy early.

Typically, Goomy is only available in the Lumiose Sewers, a late-game location offering hard-to-catch Pokemon at high levels. However, by using the bench trick in a specific area near Wild Zone 1, players can encounter one as soon as it starts raining.

To do this trick, head to the Vert Sector 9 waterway and break the stones to get inside. Here, there will be a water canal with a small bridge, and some benches up above in a courtyard.

If it is already raining, players will be in luck, as a Level 30+ Goomy will spawn on the bridge. If it isn’t raining, use the benches above the canal to change the time of day until it starts to rain.

Once it is raining, head down to the bridge below to encounter Goomy. It does have the chance of spawning as an Alpha in this location, so if the player is a low level at this point, they will want to be very careful when engaging it.

Getting a Goomy using this trick will put players in a good place as they come up against difficult battles in upcoming Main Missions, like Mega Evolution battles. With few weaknesses and multiple resistances, Goomy is a great option for a strong team in Pokemon Legends Z-A.
