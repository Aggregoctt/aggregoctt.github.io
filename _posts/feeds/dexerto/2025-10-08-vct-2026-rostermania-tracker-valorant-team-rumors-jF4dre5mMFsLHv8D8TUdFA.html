The VCT Rostermania for 2026 is underway in Valorant following Valorant Champions Paris, and there have already been some interesting moves going down. 

Valorant Champions Paris marked the end of the 2025 season for VCT, with NRG bringing home the crown for the Americas. 

They only dropped two maps on the way to the final against Fnatic, almost blowing a 2-0 lead as their EMEA counterparts leveled the best-of-five at 2-2, before NRG ended a thrilling finale on Sunset to take home the $1 million jackpot. 

The conclusion of the end-of-season event has already triggered a massive Rostermania period, too. This has seen the likes of Evil Geniuses and Team Vitality trying to bolster their rosters after missing out on qualification for Paris, while T1 and MIBR are also making changes.

Which Valorant rosters are changing for VCT 2026? 

Focusing on the latter part of that, a number of teams have already stated their intentions to rebuild. 100 Thieves are undergoing a “full rebuild,” with Asuna, Zander, and HC Zikz, considering joining new teams. Cryo is also exploring his options away from the North American org. 

Gentle Mates, the French org backed by content creators Squeezie, Gotaga & Brawks have joined VCT EMEA for the upcoming season. It is a busy off-season for them, seeing as their Call of Duty roster has also been reshaped. 

Leviatán have also allowed okeanos and c0m to explore their options. Though, the Argentine organization has, according to Sheep Esport’s Alejandro Gomis, reached a verbal agreement with spikeziN. 

Despite making it to Paris, MIBR are also shaking up their roster. According to The Spike’s Bruno Povoleri, artzin, cortezia, and xenom are also looking to join other teams. Povoleri also reportedly that FURIA are looking to sign artzin and cortezia. 

Additional rumors about singular players and coaches moving have also surfaced on social media, and we’ll be updating our tracker below with those.
