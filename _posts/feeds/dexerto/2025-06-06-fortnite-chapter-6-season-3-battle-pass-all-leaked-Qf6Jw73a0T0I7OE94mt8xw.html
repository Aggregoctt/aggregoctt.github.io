Chapter 6 Season 3 is set to bring a superhero theme to Fortnite, which means a Battle Pass full of comic book-inspired skins, including some familiar faces from DC.

Every Fortnite season comes with its own distinct theme, which influences the weapons, map, and overall vibe. In Chapter 6 alone, we’ve seen updates inspired by Japanese culture, heists, and even a full Star Wars takeover.

Next up is Season 3, which brings superheroes back to the island, only this time it’s DC instead of Marvel. Luckily, leaks have revealed many of the skins coming to the Battle Pass, so here’s everything we know so far.

All skins in Chapter 6 Season 3 Battle Pass

Although the new Battle Pass hasn’t been officially revealed, leakers like ‘Hypex’ have managed to uncover many of the skins that will be included. The two standouts are this season’s crossover characters, DC’s Superman and Robin.

Although Superman has already appeared in Fortnite, based on the images, this version seems to be based on David Corenswet’s portrayal in James Gunn’s upcoming movie. Not only is the suit the same, but it also uses the actor’s likeness.

Meanwhile, there’s also a Robin outfit, marking the first time that Batman’s sidekick has appeared in the game. Although not confirmed, many have speculated that this will be the Tim Drake version of the hero, rather than Dick Grayson or Jason Todd.

The rest of the Battle Pass will be made up of original designs from Epic Games, and three have been leaked so far.

The first is a female character with red and black hair and a gothic outfit, which seems to take a few cues from Harley Quinn. The second is a male character with a neon green and black supersuit and a large pistol on his hip.

Then, the third is another female hero, this time with blonde hair and a white and pink supersuit.

Hypex also shared an image of five other characters with matching black uniforms and different power sets. It’s unclear at the moment if these are different heroes set to appear in the Battle Pass, or styles for the same skin.

We’ll get more details about who these new faces are and their powers once the season is fully revealed, so be sure to check back here for more details.
