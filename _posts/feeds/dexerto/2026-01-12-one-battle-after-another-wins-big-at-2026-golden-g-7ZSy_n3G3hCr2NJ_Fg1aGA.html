One Battle After Another and Hamnet were the big winners at this year’s Golden Globes, while in the TV categories, Adolescence took home four awards.

One Battle After Another is tipped to sweep the Academy Awards, and last night the movie landed Golden Globes for Best Picture (Comedy or Musical), Best Screenplay and Best Director (both Paul Thomas Anderson), and Best Supporting Actress (Teyana Taylor).

Hamnet won Best Picture in the ‘Comedy or Musical’ category, while Jessie Buckley was named Best Actress, alongside other acting winners Wagner Moura (The Secret Agent), Timothée Chalamet (Marty Supreme), and Rose Byrne (If I Had Legs I’d Kick You).

Elsewhere, the KPop Demon Hunters took home the prize for Best Animated Movie, as well as Best Song, for smash-hit ‘Golden.’ Scroll down for the full list of winners…

Golden Globes movie awards

Best Motion Picture (Drama): Hamnet
Best Motion Picture (Musical or Comedy): One Battle After Another
Best Director: Paul Thomas Anderson – One Battle After Another
Best Screenplay: Paul Thomas Anderson – One Battle After Another
Best Actor – Motion Picture (Drama): Wagner Moura – The Secret Agent
Best Actress – Motion Picture (Drama): Jessie Buckley – Hamnet
Best Actor – Motion Picture (Musical or Comedy): Timothée Chalamet – Marty Supreme
Best Actress – Motion Picture (Musical or Comedy): Rose Byrne – If I Had Legs I’d Kick You
Best Supporting Actor – Motion Picture: Stellan Skarsgard – Sentimental Value
Best Supporting Actress – Motion Picture: Teyana Taylor – One Battle After Another
Best Original Score: Ludwig Göransson – Sinners
Best Original Song: ‘Golden’ – KPop Demon Hunters
Best Animated Film: KPop Demon Hunters
Best Non-English Language Film: The Secret Agent
Cinematic and Box Office achievement: Sinners

Golden Globe television awards

Best Television Series (Drama:) The Pitt
Best Television Series (Musical or Comedy): The Studio
Best Limited Series: Adolescence
Best Actor – TV Series (Drama): Noah Wyle – The Pitt
Best Actress – TV Series (Drama): Rhea Seehorn – Pluribus
Best Actor – TV Series (Musical or Comedy): Seth Rogen – The Studio
Best Actress – TV Series (Musical or Comedy): Jean Smart – Hacks
Best Actor (Limited Series): Stephen Graham – Adolescence
Best Actress (Limited Series): Michelle Williams – Dying for Sex
Best Supporting Actor: Owen Cooper – Adolescence
Best Supporting Actress: Erin Doherty – Adolescence

Best Performance in Stand-Up Comedy: Ricky Gervais – Mortality
Best Podcast: Good Hang with Amy Poehler
