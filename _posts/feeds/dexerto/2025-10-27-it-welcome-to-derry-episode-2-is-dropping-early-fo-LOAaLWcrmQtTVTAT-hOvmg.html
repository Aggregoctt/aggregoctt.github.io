It: Welcome to Derry kicked things off with a brutal, bloody episode that proves no one is safe. Thankfully, Episode 2 is dropping soon for Halloween. Warning: spoilers ahead!

The series takes place in 1962, 27 years before the events of Andy Muschietti’s first IT movie during Pennywise the Clown’s (Bill Skarsgard) previous hunting cycle in the infamous town.

The first episode follows a group of schoolkids, including Mikkal Karim-Fidler as Teddy Uris, Jack Molloy Legault as Phil Malkin, and Matilda Legault as Susie Malkin… But the entity that often disguises itself as Pennywise butchers all three of them at the end. It’s a shocking twist, and means that the story will examine the aftermath of the mini massacre.

It’s a strong start for the horror TV series, as now fans know that almost anything can happen. For those desperate to see more, It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2 will drop on Halloween for the perfect spooky watch.

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2 will air on Halloween on HBO Max

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Friday, October 31. HBO confirmed that the show is slightly messing with the release schedule so that fans celebrating Halloween will have something new to watch.

Audiences not in the US will either have to stay up late to start It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2, or catch up the next day and try to avoid spoilers. Clearly, Muschietti and HBO have some surprises in store for this new chapter of Pennywise’s story, so it’s going to be important not get the reveals ruined on social media.

  • 4am Eastern European Summer Time

UK audiences will be able to stream It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2 on Sky Max and NowTV live.

How many episodes are in It: Welcome to Derry?

There are eight episodes in It: Welcome to Derry, and the story is only going to get even more twisted. Muschietti addressed the brutal twist ahead of It: Welcome to Derry Episode 2, saying that it was a conscious decision to keep fans on their toes.

“This is strategically a devastating event to set the audience into that sense of ‘nothing is safe in this world,'” he told Entertainment Weekly. “We kind of trick the audience into thinking that these are the new Losers. Well, guess what? I guess they’re all dead.”

The violence is just a taste of what to come, and we wrote in our review that the show “widens the story of the titular town (and clown), alongside the brutal uncompromising violence it inflicts on the entire cast.”
