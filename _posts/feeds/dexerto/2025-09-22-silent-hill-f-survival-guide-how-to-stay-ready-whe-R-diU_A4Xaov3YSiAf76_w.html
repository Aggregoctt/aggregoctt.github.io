Konami’s Silent Hill f is set to take the legendary horror series in a bold new direction. Set in 1960s Japan, the game moves away from the familiar American small‑town backdrop and into the fictional town of Ebisugaoka.

Players step into the shoes of Hinako Shimizu, a high school student who finds her world torn apart when a strange fog engulfs her hometown, unleashing unspeakable horrors.

We’ve teamed up with Credit One Bank to help you get ready for the unknown. With new horrors in an all-new setting to discover, Credit One Bank is here to help you prepare for what’s ahead, just as players will need to stay a step ahead to survive Ebisugaoka’s dangers.

Reviews have made it clear this isn’t a traditional Silent Hill entry. The new setting leans heavily on Japanese folklore and history, and that cultural backdrop is key to survival. The town’s shrines, rural streets, and decaying homes hide more than collectibles – they hold the clues players will need to survive.

Be ready for Silent Hill f

Resource management will be essential. Unlike some past entries, Silent Hill f focuses on close‑quarters combat and scarcity. Supplies are hard to come by, meaning every encounter has to be carefully considered. Choosing when to fight and when to run will often mean the difference between life and death.

Puzzles will also play a major role, reflecting the psychological focus brought in by writer Ryukishi07, best known for the When They Cry series. Solving them requires more than just logic – they’re designed to confront trauma and choice, with decisions that could shape Hinako’s fate by the time the credits roll.

That kind of anticipation is what Silent Hill f will reward. Listening carefully to audio cues, exploring every corner of the fog‑shrouded streets, and conserving resources whenever possible are the keys to making it through in one piece.

Silent Hill f launches on September 25, 2025, for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. With a creative team led by horror veteran Ryukishi07, concept artist Kera, and producer Motoi Okamoto, and a soundtrack from series legend Akira Yamaoka, the stage is set for one of the most atmospheric entries the franchise has ever seen. Players who go in prepared – and know how to look ahead – will have the best chance of surviving the nightmares waiting in Ebisugaoka.
