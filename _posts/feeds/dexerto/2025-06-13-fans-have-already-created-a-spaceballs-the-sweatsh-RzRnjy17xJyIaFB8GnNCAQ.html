Spaceballs 2 is officially happening, and you can already buy Mel Brooks’ Spaceballs: The Sweatshirt thanks to fans.

If you’ve never seen the 1987 sci-fi movie, Spaceballs follows Mercenary Lone Starr and his alien sidekick as they set out to rescue Princess Vespa. She’s being held by an evil force commanded by the Dark Helmet – as you can guess, it’s a total parody.

Spaceballs 2 is now set to arrive in 2027, and original actors like Brooks, Bill Pullman, and Rick Moranis will all be returning. New faces Keke Palmer and Lewis Pullman (yes, the Pullmans will be teaming up this time) are set to join, though details around the film’s plot are being kept under wraps.

Sources describe the existing script as “a Non-Prequel Non-Reboot Sequel Part Two but with Reboot Elements Franchise Expansion Film,” but if you noticed the Spaceballs Sweatshirt in the new teaser trailer, you can already buy it.

How to get the Spaceballs: The Sweatshirt fan merch

A quick Google will tell you multiple variations of Spaceballs: The Sweatshirt are already available to buy (or Spaceballs: The Hoodie if that’s more your vibe), in stock on sites including Etsy and Redbubble.

On top of this, you can find a range of other wearable merch referencing different scenes and quotes from the first movie. Of course, the actual one Brooks is seen wearing will likely be released by Amazon MGM in line with Spaceballs 2’s release.

Brooks is set to reprise his role as Yogurt, despite turning 99 years old at the end of the month (meaning he’d be 101 by the time Spaceballs 2 actually came out). “After 40 years, we asked ‘What do the fans want?’ But instead, we’re making this movie,” he says at the end of the teaser.

Josh Gad, Benji Samit, and Dan Hernandez are our team of writers this time, with Gad describing the script as “the funniest and best thing” he’s ever written in a press statement.

An exact release date for Spaceballs 2 hasn’t been given yet, so keep your eyes peeled.
