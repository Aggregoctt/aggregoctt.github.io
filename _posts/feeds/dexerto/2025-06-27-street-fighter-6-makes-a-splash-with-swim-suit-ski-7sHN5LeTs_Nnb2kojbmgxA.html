Things are heating up in Street Fighter 6 as Capcom is gearing up for summer by releasing a wave of swimsuit skins for Chun-Li, Cammy, Jamie, and more. 

With summer on the horizon and temperatures gradually rising, Street Fighter 6 devs are getting seven of their battle-hardened brawlers ready to soak up the sun. Instead of taking a break from throwing punches, players will soon be able to kick butt with some beach-ready outfits.

During the Capcom Spotlight livestream on June 26, the devs revealed that Sagat would be joining the ever-expanding roster on August 5, but it was the reveal of Outfit 4 DLC that has fans turning their heads. 

Street Fighter 6 swimsuit outfits revealed

As pointed out in a post on X by Street Fighter director and designer Takayuki Nakayama, Cammy and Chun-Li’s swimsuits are very similar to the outfits shown in Street Fighter II’s original artwork. 

The only difference here is that Chun-Li’s gold jewelry has been replaced with a matching blue color scheme that complements her blue one-piece swimsuit. 

Meanwhile, Cammy has a white belt and what appears to be a phone strap around her leg. We just hope she remembers to remove it before diving into the pool; otherwise, she could be in for an upgrade sooner rather than later. 

Meanwhile, Luke has unleashed his inner Baywatch (move over, David Hasselhoff) by sporting some vibrant orange swimming shorts and cool shades. 

His rival, Jaimie, on the other hand, has opted for a more traditional look, incorporating a striking dragon design paired with a gold gourd (after all, it’s always important to stay hydrated).
