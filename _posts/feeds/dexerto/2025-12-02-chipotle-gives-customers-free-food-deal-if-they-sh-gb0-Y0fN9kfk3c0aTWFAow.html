Chipotle is rolling out a month of holiday deals built around its new Unwrap Extra promotion, including an in-restaurant BOGO free offer tied to an ugly sweater event later in December.

Chipotle announced that customers can get buy-one-get-one dinners on the first three Saturdays of December from 4 p.m. to close. The promotion includes specific themed events each week and surprise rewards for members throughout the month.

Chipotle Ugly Sweater BOGO

The chain is using National Ugly Sweater Day as a springboard for its own “Extra Sweater Day” on Saturday, December 20.

Anyone who visits a Chipotle restaurant wearing an extra or festive sweater and buys any meal can get a second one for free.

Here is the full Unwrap Extra Saturday schedule:

  • December 6: Tacos BOGO. Buy a three-taco entrée, get a free entrée.
  • December 13: Burrito BOGO. Buy a burrito, get a free entrée.
  • December 20: Extra Sweater Day BOGO. Wear an extra sweater in-restaurant and buy any meal, get a free meal.

Chipotle is also giving Rewards Members additional perks in December. Free guac, double protein, chips, or drinks will be sent directly to member wallets as part of two surprise drops. Customers can join the program through the Chipotle Rewards page.

The company is also continuing its partnership with No Kid Hungry. From December 3 through December 22, customers ordering through the Chipotle app or website can round up their total to donate. Chipotle says it has raised more than $825,000 for the organization since the start of 2024.
