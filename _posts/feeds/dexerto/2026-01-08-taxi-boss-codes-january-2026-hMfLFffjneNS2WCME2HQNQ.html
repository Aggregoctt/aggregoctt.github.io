What are Taxi Boss codes used for?

Taxi Boss codes mostly give cash, and cash sits at the center of almost every upgrade you make. It’s used to buy better cars that attract higher-paying passengers, improve your company’s star rating, and boost earnings per trip. Cash also goes into building and upgrading your company office, hiring AI drivers, and improving their stats to increase passive income.

Extra money helps with car modifications and covering upgrade costs without needing to grind every run, making codes a useful boost when expansion starts getting expensive.
