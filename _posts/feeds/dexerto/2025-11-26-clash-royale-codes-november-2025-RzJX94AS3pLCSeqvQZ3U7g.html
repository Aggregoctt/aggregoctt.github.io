You can grab emotes, banners, and other rewards completely for free through Clash Royale codes, all of which we’ve listed below.

Clash Royale might be a competitive game where you’re constantly facing players with all kinds of crazy decks, but that doesn’t mean you can’t make your experience even more fun with cosmetics.

Many of these are sold in the shop, but every now and then the devs drop free rewards you can claim without spending a thing.

To help you grab them before they expire, we’ve put together a list of active Clash Royale codes for November 2025, including any available QR codes.

Active Clash Royale codes

As of November 26, 2025, there are currently two active Clash Royale codes available to redeem in-game, allowing you to grab an emote and banner for free.

Devs have yet to specify when these codes will expire. Though looking at the previous ones, it’s safe to assume they won’t stick around for long.

New rewards regularly get added, and we’ll be sure to keep this list updated once they pop up. So, make sure to keep checking back if you don’t want to miss out.

Active QR codes 

In addition, there are six active QR codes you can use to claim even more freebies. Unlike regular codes, all you have to do is point your camera at the image, and you’ll be taken straight into the game to grab the rewards.

How to redeem codes

As long as you’ve completed the in-game tutorial, which only takes a few minutes, you’re pretty much set to follow the steps below:

  1. Launch Clash Royale.
  2. Make sure you’re logged in with your account.
  3. Open the Supercell Store website.
  4. Login with the same ID in-game.
  5. Scroll down and click ‘Redeem a Store Code’.
  6. Paste your code into the box.
  7. Hit Submit.
  8. Open the game and claim your rewards!
