Winterfest has taken over Fortnite, bringing plenty of Quests for players to complete in order to earn stacks of XP.

After getting underway on December 18, Winterfest 2025 has Fortnite players getting into the festive spirit. The cabin is back and full of presents to open, while wintery skins for Sabrina Carpenter and Hatsune Miku are on the way.

The map has also been given a makeover, and there are plenty of Quests arriving regularly that encourage you to explore it. So, here are all the Fortnite Winterfest Quests so far and what’s up for grabs.

All Winterfest Quests so far

New Winterfest missions are set to arrive every two days, so here are the ones we know about so far:

Gift to the Stars

  • Collect a Perfect Gift from a chest – 10,000 XP 
    • Find a Perfect Gift inside a chest
  • Bring a Perfect Gift to any character – 10,000 XP 
    • Once you have a Perfect Gift in your inventory, find any NPC and deliver it within three minutes.
  • Damage Yule Trooper or any other boss (500) – 10,000 XP 
    • Find any boss and deal 500 damage. Bosses spawn randomly, so we recommend doing this immediately after landing.

Passion for Presents (December 20)

  • Bring a Perfect Gift to The Bride, Carter Wu, or Kingston – 10,000 XP
  • Travel distance with Icy Feet – 10,000 XP
  • Search chests at Clawsy Lodge or Tiptop Terrace – 10,000 XP

Step three (December 22)

  • Bring a Perfect Gift to Marty McFly, Heroic Hope, or Carina – 10,000 XP
  • Maintain a speed of 88mph in a Whiplash Time Machine – 10,000 XP
  • Damage opponents with the Snowball Launcher or Blizzard Grenade – 10,000 XP

Step four (December 24)

  • Find Lt. Winter and bring him a Perfect Gift – 10,000 XP 

Squad Up Quests

There are also Squad up Quests to complete, which task you with collecting Shiny Llamas while playing with friends. These can be picked up from eliminated players.
