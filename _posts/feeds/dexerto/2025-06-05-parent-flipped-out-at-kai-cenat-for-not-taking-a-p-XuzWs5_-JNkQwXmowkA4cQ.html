Kai Cenat has revealed that he had an interaction with an angry fan who cursed him out because he was unable to stop to take a photo.

Streamers and content creators have become celebrities for a new generation, but with the majority of them broadcasting from home, it can be difficult to have interactions with them in the outside world. 

Kai Cenat has a history of wild interactions with fans. He was charged with inciting a riot in New York when he tried to give away a PS5 back in 2023, he had a flight disrupted by a flight attendant who kept waking him up for selfies, and had to warn fans not to try and sneak into his Streamer University. 

While the Twitch star does try to make time for anyone he meets in person, timing conflicts can throw a spanner in the works, requiring him to make a swift exit.

Kai Cenat reveals fan’s parent flipped him off

During his June 3 stream, the AMP star revealed that the parents of an angry fan “cursed” him out and flipped him off, all because he couldn’t stop for a photo.

“I was in a rush to go to my flight bro and I was running late and I couldn’t take a picture with somebody’s child, right? And me having to be in a hurry, I said ‘my bad, I appreciate you but I got to hurry because I’m going to miss my plane’, the parent of the child flipped me off,” Kai said.

“They cursed me out because I couldn’t stop. I couldn’t stop for the photo and they flipped me off and cursed me out, bro, because I was in a rush for my flight.”

Kai did noted that he usually “loves” taking flights because it allows him to interact with fans, but that was a different experience.
