MrBeast’s Feastables is teaming up with the upcoming Super Mario Galaxy movie for a limited-time candy collab that’s already popping up in stores.

Photos of the crossover first surfaced on February 18 after a shopper claimed they found one of the bars at a 7-Eleven and shared it on Reddit. The post quickly spread to X, where images of the products began circulating widely.

The collaboration includes three themed items inspired by the Mario universe.

Feastables x Super Mario Galaxy movie collab

According to images shared online, the limited-time lineup features a Galaxy Cocoa Crunch Bar, White Chocolate Peanut Butter Yoshi Eggs, and Sour Boost Gummies.

Packaging shows Super Mario Galaxy branding alongside the Feastables logo, with characters like Yoshi featured on select products.

Feastables was launched by MrBeast back in 2022 and has since continued to grow the brand with collabs and even a whole new formula.

The Super Mario Galaxy movie crossover is the latest in a growing list of pop culture collaborations tied to the chocolate brand.

At the time of writing, neither MrBeast nor Feastables’ official social channels have shared details about the Super Mario Galaxy lineup. Availability, pricing, and a nationwide release date have not yet been confirmed.
