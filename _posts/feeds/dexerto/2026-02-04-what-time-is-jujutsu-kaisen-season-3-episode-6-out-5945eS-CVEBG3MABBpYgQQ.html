Details of when and where to watch Episode 6 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3, as well as a refresher on what happened in Episode 5.

Season 3 of Jujutsu Kaisen is providing fans with an animated adaptation of the Culling Arc.

The season was initially packaged as a movie called Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution, which combined Episodes 1 and 2, and released in cinemas.

While now fans of Greg Akutami’s manga are seeing what happens next, with Episodes 1-5 already available, and Episode 6 dropping this week.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 6 drops on Crunchyroll on February 5, 2026, at 9am PT / 12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (January 30)
  • 6am New Zealand (January 30)

The new episode is titled: ‘Cog.’

What happened in Episode 5?

Episode 5 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 saw Principal Yaga giving away his cursed secret before dying, and Yuji competing in a sorcerer’s fight club.

The episode kicks off with the revelation that Yaga – the principal of Tokyo’s Jujutsu High School – is able to create cursed corpses that are self-sustaining. A secret he refuses to share.

He’s then fatally wounded by Gakuganji, and with his dying breath, reveals how the cursed corpses are made, before telling his killer that it’s now his curse. Panda – who was just freed by sorcerer Atsuya Kusakabe – finds him dead, and becomes distraught.

Meanwhile Yuji and Megumi find an illegal fight club for sorcerers where there are two rules – no cursed techniques, and no running away.

Yuji secures himself a fight, his opponent ends up being Panda, and the friends pretend to trade blows that they succeed in making look real.

Yuji is then invited to become part of the operation by fight club boss Kinji Hakari, but when he claims not to know who Gojo is, Hakari smells a rat, and swings for him.
