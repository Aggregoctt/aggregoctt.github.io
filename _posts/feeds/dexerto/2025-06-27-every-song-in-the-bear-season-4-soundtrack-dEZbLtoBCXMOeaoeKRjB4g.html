Order up! The Bear Season 4 is finally here, and as expected, its soundtrack is incredible – so, we’ve rounded up all of the songs so you can add them to your playlist.

The fourth season picks up immediately after Season 3, with Carmy and co. dealing with the aftermath of the restaurant review in the newspaper – slight spoiler, but it got an “unsavory” write-up.

If there’s been one constant in the chaos of The Bear, it’s the music. Each season has an eclectic mix of relaxing and pulse-racing tracks (including the score from Michael Mann’s Thief, an inspired choice).

With Season 4 streaming on Hulu and Disney Plus in its entirety now, here’s a full rundown of every song in the soundtrack.

The Bear soundtrack: Season 4 songs
