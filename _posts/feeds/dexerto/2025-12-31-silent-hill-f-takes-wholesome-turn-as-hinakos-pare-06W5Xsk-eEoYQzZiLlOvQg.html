The Silent Hill f streaming trend has taken its most unexpected turn yet, with Hinako Shimizu’s parents now streaming their playthrough of Konami’s latest survival horror.

The actors behind Hinako’s mother and father have begun streaming Silent Hill f on PS5, giving fans another look at the famous faces tied to some of its most uncomfortable family moments.

Their first stream is already proving popular and went live on December 29, where it drew in 124,996 views as fans eagerly tuned in for the dynamic duo’s playthrough.

Hinako’s parents are playing Silent Hill f

Hinako’s father, Kanta Shimizu, is portrayed by Takeshi Masago, while her mother, Kimie Shimizu, is played by Tamami Hiraoka. Both are experienced screen actors, but far from seasoned gamers, which has quickly become part of the appeal.

According to the official YouTube description, it’s been decades since Hinako’s father last touched a video game, while this marks her mother’s very first time playing on PS5. Both actors have asked viewers to watch their playthroughs with “warm eyes” as they learn the controls live.

The parents’ streams build on earlier buzz around Konatsu Kato, who has been streaming Silent Hill f on her official YouTube channel, revisiting the nightmare she helped bring to life. Since starting her playthrough on October 24, Kato’s broadcasts have regularly pulled in thousands of viewers and taken off across social media, with fans cheering her on as she pushes through the fog-filled town of Ebisugaoka.

Hinako’s parents’ next stream will go live on January 4, 2026, where both actors will be kicking off the New Year with a scare.
