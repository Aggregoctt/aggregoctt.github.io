The Night Agent is returning for a third season of action, drama, and intrigue, so here’s when you can watch the further adventures of FBI agent Peter Sutherland.

The Night Agent debuted with one of the most successful first seasons in Netflix history, attracting 98.2 million views during its first 91 days.

The action thriller stars Gabriel Basso as a government agent investigating conspiracies in high places while protecting the US from escalating crises.

You can read Dexerto’s Night Agent Season 2 review, where we wrote that “it flies as a perfectly serviceable tale of government moles and secret agents.” And scroll down for all Season 3 details.

The Night Agent Season 3 drops on Netflix on February 19, 2026, at the usual time of 12am PT / 3am ET.

How many episodes are in Season 3?

There are 10 episodes in Season 3, the same amount as in Season 1 and 2, bringing the show’s grand total to 30 episodes.

As The Night Agent is streaming on Netflix, all 10 are dropping at once, meaning you binge Season 3 in a single sitting should you have the time and stamina.

There are also plans afoot for Season 4, though much depends on the success of these new installments.

What happened in The Night Agent Season 2?

In The Night Agent Season 2 finale, the team manages to stop a chemical weapon from going off in New York, while Peter gets his next mission.

Once the team secures the KX canisters at the UN, they follow Markus – and the one remaining canister – to an apartment building where they also prevent that from being triggered, before Peter shoots Markus dead.

Peter is then placed in custody by Catherine for appearing to help Solomon and Jacob. In response – and sometime later – he confesses all into a tape, much like his father before him.

Jacob meets with Governor Hagen, who is now running for President, and gives him a UN case file that incriminates his opponent Patrick Knox. Knox then drops out of the race, giving Hagen a free run at the White House.

Catherine tells Peter that his father was selling government intel but had his reasons, and now it’s his turn. Catherine explains that Peter has an opportunity to “expose” and “destroy” Jacob, and if he’s successful, she will wipe his slate clean.
