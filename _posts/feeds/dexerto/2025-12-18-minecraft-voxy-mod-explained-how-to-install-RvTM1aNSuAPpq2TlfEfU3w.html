Minecraft may have tons of features to enjoy, like crafting your favorite weapon, taming the animals, making armor for dogs, or just exploring, but sometimes it’s made a little more personal with a mod, like Voxy.

Sure, Minecraft can be pretty immersive as you explore the creepy Pale Garden or build an underwater home, but sometimes that render distance can really take you out of the world, especially if your PC isn’t powerful enough.

Well, luckily, there’s a mod for everything in Minecraft, and render distance is no exception. So, here’s all you need to know about the Minecraft Voxy mod and how to install it.

What is the Voxy mod in Minecraft?

The Voxy mod is essentially a way to massively increase your render distance in Minecraft, allowing you to see further when playing.

For example, in the base game, you can load a maximum of 32 chunks around you; well, the Voxy mod will expand that to as far as your PC can handle.

The mod also helps with performance, as the 32 chunk render distance is designed to keep the game’s FPS manageable. The Voxy mod will increase the render distance and keep the FPS high, allowing you to see more without any stutters.

How to install Voxy

To install the Voxy mod, you’ll want to follow the steps below:

  • Install Fabric for Minecraft 1.21.10 or older.
  • Install Curseforge (great for other Minecraft mods too).
  • Find the Voxy mod on Curseforge.
  • Download the mod file.
  • Copy and paste the mod files into the mods folder in the Minecraft directory (it should be under AppData)
  • Open the launcher and click on the Fabric modded version.
  • Play Minecraft.
