How to redeem codes

There’s no tutorial or milestone required to redeem codes in Knockout. Follow the steps below to get started:

  • Click on the gear icon in the bottom-right corner to open the Settings menu.
  • Enter a working code into the text box at the top of the pop-up that appears.

What are Knockout codes used for?

Knockout codes are used to claim free in-game rewards that help you unlock cosmetics and enhance your character’s appearance.

Most codes reward Ice, the primary currency used to purchase skins directly in the shop, and Spins, which can be used on the wheel to roll for random skins. These rewards don’t give you a gameplay advantage in fights, but they let you customize your penguin and enjoy more variety in matches.
