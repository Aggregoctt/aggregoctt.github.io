Dune: Awakening, a survival game based on Frank Herbert’s classic sci-fi novel, which had a hit adaptation starring Timothée Chalamet, has arrived, and there are freebies to redeem with codes.

While the new Dune game is all about surviving Arrakis’s heat, avoiding sandworms, and fighting Sardaukar soldiers, it’s an MMO game after all, so customizing your character’s appearance and gear is a big part of it. Luckily, the developer is already offering a free skin through a redeemable code.

The devs haven’t said whether the code will expire, but it’s better to redeem it as soon as possible − just in case.

How to redeem codes

  1. Head to the Dune Awakening account page.
  2. Sign in to Steam to link your accounts.
  3. Click ‘Redeem Key.’
  4. Paste the key from the table above into the box.
  5. Hit ‘Continue,’ then ‘Confirm.’

Make sure that you’re copying and pasting the code as-is when entering it, because Dune: Awakening codes are case-sensitive.

Lastly, you’ll need to have purchased the game to be able to fully connect your accounts and redeem cosmetics.

Where to find cosmetics in-game

To find your cosmetics in-game, open up the menu, then scroll over to the Customization tab. To find the Frameblade skin specifically, head to Melee Weapons and then find the Kindjal, and you can apply the skin.
