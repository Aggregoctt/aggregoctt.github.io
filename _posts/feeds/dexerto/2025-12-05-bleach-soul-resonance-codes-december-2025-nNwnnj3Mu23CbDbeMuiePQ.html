How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in BLEACH: Soul Resonance is straightforward, but you usually need to complete the early tutorial and story missions up to Stage 1–6 (including the school rooftop mission) before the redemption menu is unlocked. With that done, follow these steps:

  • On the main screen, or from in-game, click on the ‘Event’ icon in the top-right corner. It looks like a box with a star inside, as shown in the image below:
  • In the bottom-left corner of the new screen that opens, click on ‘Treasure Redeem’.
  • A new box will appear on the right side where you need to enter the code. Once done, click on ‘Redeem’ to claim the corresponding reward.

After redeeming a code, the reward will be sent to your in-game mail, and you can collect it from there.

What are BLEACH: Soul Resonance codes used for?

While resources can generally be obtained by simply playing and progressing through the game, codes hand out some sweet rewards that can be of excellent use to anyone. When it comes to crafting, upgrade materials like Tamahagane and different Insights are valuable resources.

For free-to-play players, Spiritual Jade is of immense use, as it is the game’s currency required for pulling different characters or weapons. It goes without saying that Spiritual Jade is hard to accumulate unless you are willing to spend real money.

Occasionally, you will also be able to net different boost items like Omamori, which will help you enhance your performance in the game.
