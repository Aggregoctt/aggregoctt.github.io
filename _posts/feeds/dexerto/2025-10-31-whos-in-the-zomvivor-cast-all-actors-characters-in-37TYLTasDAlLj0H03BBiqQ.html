Can’t wait for All of Us Are Dead Season 2? Netflix has another school-set zombie series to binge-watch in the meantime, so here are all the cast and characters in Zomvivor.

It’s officially Halloween, which means it’s time to close the curtains, shut off the lights, grab some snacks, and spend all night watching creepy content. All streaming services have something to offer, whether that be classic horror movies or new scary shows. 

That includes Netflix, which has just dropped the new seven-episode TV show, Zomvivor. Similarly to All of Us Are Dead, the series centers on a group of students who take refuge in a school when a zombie apocalypse hits. 

Zomvivor wastes no time, kicking off right in the thick of the undead mayhem. As such, there’s not a lot of time to get to know the characters pre-outbreak. 

Zomivor cast & characters

Don’t fret, as we’ve put together a rundown of the lead Zomvivor cast, as well as the characters they’re playing.  

Ning: Janis Janistar Phomphadungcheep

Janis Janistar Phomphadungcheep plays Ning, a student whose mother is sick. She plans to give up her scholarship to look after her mom, until professor Wiroj tells her about a potential experimental cure. Of course, that all falls by the wayside when a zombie apocalypse hits. 

Phomphadungcheep is an actress best known for titles including Rerk Sanghan, Khemjira, and Sea of Greed. She has more than 2.1 million followers on Instagram – you can follow her here. 

Non: NuNew Chawarin Perdpiriyawong

Taking on the role of Non is Chawarin Perdpiriyawong. As Ning’s brother, Non is struggling with the same issues as she is. Both siblings strive to protect each other when the undead infiltrate their world. 

Perdpiriyawong, better known as NuNew, is an actor, singer, and host who made his acting debut in the 2022 series Cutie Pie, earning him the nickname “Son of the nation”. NuNew has more than 4.1 million followers on Instagram – follow him here. 

San: Zee Pruk Panich

San is played by Zee Pruk Panich. His character has slight anger issues, and is close with both Ning and Non. He joins the gang as one of the survivors who must fight against the undead. 

Zomvivor isn’t the first time Zee has worked with NuNew – the pair were also in the Cutie Pie cast together. A number of Zee’s other acting credits include Why R U?, You Never Eat Alone, and The Next Prince. He has nearly five million Instagram followers – you can follow him here. 

Dr. Wiroj: Weir Sukollawat Kanarot 

Weir Sukollawat Kanarot plays Dr. Wiroj, a researcher who has dedicated his life to medicine – as is teased at the start, he may have a connection to the pandemic. He’s Ning’s professor, and offers her mom to undergo an experimental procedure. 

Kanarot, nicknamed Weir, is a famous actor and model, with a number of his credits including 4 Tigers, Once Upon a Star, Sweet Prison, and Pachara Montra. You can follow him on Instagram here, where he shares adorable updates on his life and family. 

Jean: Tommy Sittichok Pueakpoolpol 

Tommy Sittichok Pueakpoolpol joins the Zomvivor cast as Jean, Ning’s friend. He reveals early on that he hasn’t had the easiest start in life, having grown up without parents, which is why he tries to convince Ning to say yes to the treatment for her mom. 

Pueakpoolpol, nicknamed Tommy, is an actor, model, and singer under Domundi TV. He’s appeared in titles such as Remember 15, Peach of Time, Why R U?, and Pee Nak. You can follow him on Instagram here, where he has nearly one million followers. 

Muk: Dream Apichaya Phanichtrakool

Dream Apichaya Phanichtrakool plays Muk, a friend of Ning and Jean’s, and one of many survivors who are thrust into a zombie apocalypse. 

Phanichtrakool, who goes by Dream, is best known as a YouTuber, appearing as one of three best friends who host the channel GoyNattyDream. She’s also enjoyed supporting roles in Spice and Spell and Doctor Climax. You can follow Dream on Instagram here.

Thi: Yim Pharinyakorn Khansawa

Yim Pharinyakorn Khansawa takes on the role of Thi, a student and wannabe filmmaker who, ironically, is making a zombie movie before the outbreak. 

Earlier this year, Yim was announced to be a member of DMD Music’s new T-pop group DEXX. He’s also appeared in Cutie Pie alongside other Zomvivor cast members. You can follow Yim on Instagram here.

Pao: Jimmy Karn Kritsanaphan

Jimmy Karn Kritsanaphan takes on the role of Pao, Jean’s friend who’s going through a tough time financially. He’s another survivor who’s faced with a terrifying future when the zombies arrive. 

Kritsanaphan, who goes by Jimmy, is an actor under DomundiTV. A number of his credits include The Next Prince, Why R U?, and Peach of Time. You can follow him on Instagram here, where he has more than 1.1 million followers. 

Phu: Boss Chaikamon Sermsongwittaya

Chaikamon Sermsongwittaya, aka Boss, plays Phu in the Zomvivor cast. In the opening scene, his character is established as a confident leader, taking Ning’s phone to instruct San’s crew. 

Boss is an actor best known for Love in the Air, The Boy Next World, and Wedding Plan. You can follow him on Instagram here, where he has 2.5 million followers. 

Thorn: Max Kornthas Rujeerattanavorapan

Max Kornthas Rujeerattanavorapan takes on the role of Thorn, Thi’s brother and one of many students who take refuge in the school to survive the zombie apocalypse. 

Rujeerattanavorapan, aka Max, is a model and actor, and like many of the cast members, has appeared in Cutie Pie. His other credits include Two Worlds and Naughty Babe. You can follow him on Instagram here. 

Prao: Praew Narupornkamol Chaisang

Praew Narupornkamol Chaisang joins the Zomvivor cast as Prao, a brave and straight-to-the-point character who helps lead her fellow survivors amid the outbreak. 

Chaisang, nicknamed Praew, is an actress and model whose accolades include Home for Rent, The Bride, and Long Khong. To keep tabs on her activities, follow Praew on Instagram here.
