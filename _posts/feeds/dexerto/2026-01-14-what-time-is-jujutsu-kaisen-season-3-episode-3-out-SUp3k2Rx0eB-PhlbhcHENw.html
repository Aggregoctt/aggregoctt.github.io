Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 launched last week with Episodes 1 and 2, so here’s details of when and where to watch Episode 3, as well as what to expect from the ongoing ‘Culling Game Arc.’

Jujutsu Kaisen fans got an early sneak peak at Season 3 of the manga adaptation last November when Episodes 1 and 2 were packaged together and released in cinemas.

Titled Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution, the movie provided a bridge between the the Shonen Jump’s Shubuya Incident and Culling Game, and grossed more than $40 million in theaters.

Now fans of Greg Akutami’s manga can see what happens next, with Episode 3 dropping this week.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 3 drops on Crunchyroll tomorrow – January 15, 2026 – at 9am PT/12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (January 9)
  • 6am New Zealand (January 9)

The episode is titled ‘About the Culling Game.’

What happened in Episodes 1 and 2?

Episodes 1 and 2 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 saw evil sorcerer Noritoshi setting the deadly Culling Game in motion; a battle royale of supreme jujutsu sorcerers who were awakened by the Shibuya Incident.

Here’s how Crunchyroll described the ‘Execution’ episodes via official press release: “A veil abruptly descends over the busy Shibuya area amid the bustling Halloween crowds, trapping countless civilians inside. Satoru Gojo, the strongest jujutsu sorcerer, steps into the chaos. But lying in wait are curse users and spirits scheming to seal him away.

“Yuji Itadori, accompanied by his classmates and other top-tier jujutsu sorcerers, enters the fray in an unprecedented clash of curses – the Shibuya Incident.

“In the aftermath, 10 colonies across Japan are transformed into dens of curses in a plan orchestrated by Noritoshi Kamo, the most wicked sorcerer in history. As the deadly Culling Game starts, Special Grade sorcerer Yuta Okkotsu is assigned to carry out Yuji’s execution for his perceived crimes.”
