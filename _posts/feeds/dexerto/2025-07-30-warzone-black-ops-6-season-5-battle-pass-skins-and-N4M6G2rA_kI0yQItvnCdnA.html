Black Ops 6 and Warzone Season 5 arrives on August 7, bringing a Battle Pass full of new weapons, Operators, cosmetics, and more.

This new season brings the classic WMD map to multiplayer, the final Zombies map, and opens Verdansk’s Stadium. But, it wouldn’t be a new season without new weapons and Operators, and a handful have been revealed ahead of the major update.

Here’s everything we know about Warzone and Black Ops 6 Season 5’s Battle Pass so far, including the price and rewards.

BO6 & Warzone Season 5 Battle Pass Price

The Warzone and Black Ops 6 Season 5 Battle Pass is set to cost 1,100 CoD Points ($9.99 / £8.39).

This includes all of the rewards, but be careful, you’ll need to finish the Battle Pass to get them all – anything not unlocked by the end of the season will be gone forever. You can also pay extra to get a head start or buy Tokens throughout the season.

The BlackCell pass costs $30 / £25.99, granting the entire Battle Pass and a page of exclusive rewards, which offers an extra 1,100 CoD Points.

All weapons, Operators & skins so far

The Battle Pass will go live on August 7 at 9 AM PT / 12 PM ET / 5 PM BST, and here are the rewards so far:

Instant rewards

  • 10% Battle Pass XP Boost
  • “Stogie” Sims Operator and “Stogie BlackCell” Sims Operator Skin (BlackCell only)
  • “Infernous” Legendary Blueprint for the Kilo 141 Assault Rifle
  • “Free Fire” Emote
  • “Venom Eye” Weapon Charm

Instant BlackCell rewards

  • “Solace” BlackCell Operator
  • 10% XP Boost
  • 1,100 COD Points
  • “Griffin Nabbed” Finishing Move
  • “Herald” Mastercraft Blueprint for the Krig C Assault Rifle with Golden Aura Tracers and Golden Spirit Death FX
  • “Iris Rise” Animated Weapon Charm
  • “BlackCell” Clan Tag (showcased in Lobbies and Winner’s Circles)

New weapons & attachments

  • PML 5.56 LMG (Page 3)
  • ABR A1 Assault Rifle (Page 6)
  • 9mm PM Skull Splitter (Page 7)

Skins

  • Sims “Stogie” Operator skin & Battle Pass variant (Instant Unlock)
  • Sims “Envenomate” & Battle Pass variant (Completion Page)

And that’s everything we know about the Season 5 Battle Pass so far! The devs usually drop another blog post going more in-depth with the Battle Pass before launch, so we’ll be sure to update you with more details as soon as we learn more.
