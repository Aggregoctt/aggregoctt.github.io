McDonald’s has officially partnered with Drake’s OVO brand for a new limited-time menu collaboration titled the ‘Afters Meal.’

The collaboration began appearing in the McDonald’s app on February 17, 2026, featuring OVO’s signature owl logo alongside the company’s name and the tagline “Where Night Owls Land.”

The Afters Meal appears to target late-night customers, aligning with OVO’s owl branding and Drake’s longtime association with nighttime culture.

OVO’s collab with McDonald’s is only available in Canada.

What’s included in the McDonald’s OVO Afters Meal

The featured item is the McDouble Afters Meal, which features the regular McDonald’s McDouble cheeseburger.

A Junior Chicken Afters Meal is also listed, featuring a Jr McChicken sandwich instead of the burger. Separately, a blue colored Nite Sprite is available for drink. Some have described it as Blue Raspberry flavor, but McDonald’s hasn’t confirmed anything as of writing.

Both meals feature McDonald’s poutine, which has become a staple at the Canada-based Golden Arches.

The OVO owl logo appears on promotional materials for the collaboration, with black-and-gold posters displaying both McDonald’s and OVO branding.
