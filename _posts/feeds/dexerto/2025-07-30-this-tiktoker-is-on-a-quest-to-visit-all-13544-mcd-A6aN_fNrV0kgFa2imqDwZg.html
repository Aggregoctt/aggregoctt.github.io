TikToker Donny Boys is on a mission unlike any other: visiting every single McDonald’s in the United States, all 13,544 of them.

Since kicking off the journey on July 17, 2024, donnyboys10 has already hit 275 locations across the country, documenting each stop with reviews, ratings, and bathroom checks along the way.

“I’ve always dreamed of visiting every McDonald’s in the United States, tasting each unique item on the menu and experiencing the local vibes,” his GoFundMe page reads.

“This journey isn’t just about the food; it’s about connecting with people and cultures across the country.”

This McJourney could take years

From interiors and drive-thrus to menu quirks and washrooms, nothing escapes Donny’s scrutiny. He even gives each restaurant a score out of 10.

Donny claims he’s averaging 60 to 90 McDonald’s a month, but even at that pace, it could take well over a decade to finish — especially if more golden arches open up in the meantime.

For context, hitting one new location a day would take nearly 37 years.

His quirky fast food quest has earned him 127,000 TikTok followers so far, with several videos cracking over a million views.

It’s unclear if he’ll ever complete his mission, but one thing’s for sure: He’s absolutely lovin’ it.
