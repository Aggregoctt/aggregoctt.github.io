Tic Tac is teaming up with Dr Pepper for a new limited-time flavor that brings the soda’s signature taste into mint form.

The collaboration introduces Tic Tac Dr Pepper Mints, combining the soft drink’s well-known 23-flavor blend with Tic Tac’s pocket-sized format. The launch marks the latest crossover between major snack and beverage brands.

According to the company, each mint delivers the recognizable Dr Pepper flavor profile in the same small container that Tic Tac has used for decades.

Tic Tac launches limited-time Dr Pepper mints

In a statement, Endri Shtylla, Marketing Director at Ferrero, said: “Tic Tac Dr Pepper Mints bring together two powerhouse brands in an unexpected way. We’ve brought Dr Pepper’s original 23-flavor blend into the convenient mint format Tic Tac does best—giving fans a whole new way to enjoy the flavors they love.”

Fans on Tic Tac’s Instagram shared their thoughts about the release, with many sharing their excitement for the soda-flavored mints.;

“Why am I obsessed with the color of these,” one user asked.

Another said: “My favorite!”

Tic Tac Dr Pepper Mints launched at select retailers on February 12, 2026, and are expected to roll out to additional stores nationwide in the coming months. The brand encouraged fans to follow its social media channels for updates on availability and future product announcements.
