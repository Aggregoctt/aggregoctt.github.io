Fish Hunters has enough deadly weapons that give CoD a run for its money, and if you’ve ever wanted to quickscope a sea bass, then your prayers have just been answered.

Whether it’s running a Japanese supermarket in inKONBINI or making your ideal island in Fantasy Life i, cozy games have become incredibly popular for players looking to retreat from the stresses of the real world. 

However, Fish Hunters: The Most Ridiculous Fishing Simulator (yes, that is its real name) takes aim at what is otherwise a relaxing pastime, and gives it a deadly twist. As the name suggests, Fish Hunters is all about reeling in prized catches before shooting them to pieces with CoD-style guns. 

Fishing game lets you quickscope cod

Fish Hunters is an upcoming cozy game (can we call it that?) that features over 140 species of fish, which can be collected using a variety of unlocable rods and baits. Well, we say collect, you’ll be obliterating them with your arsenal of deadly firearms. 

Yes, everything from double-barreled shotguns, assault rifles, pistols, and SMGs can be used to annihilate your fishy foes. Hell, the gameplay trailer even shows one player quickscoping a fish – now we truly have seen it all!

“Hunt fish solo or in co-op. Haul them in and take them down with crazy guns. Try out different baits and rods, explore unique locations, and complete your fish collection in the atlas. Or just kick back by the campfire with friends in this wild webfishing adventure,” reads the game’s description. 

Judging from the absolutely bonkers trailer, players will gain points for both the number of fish they gun down and the overall rarity of their unlucky catch. These points can then be spent to unlock, you guessed it, even more guns.
