Starbucks has added a new beverage to its secret menu after the success of their Cannon Ball Refresher drink, created in partnership with MrBeast for Beast Games Season 2.

Starbucks worked closely with YouTube icon MrBeast to provide the contestants on Beast Games Season 2 with constant refreshments, day or night.

The massive city that was built to house these participants included a Starbucks location that was open 24/7, as well as a special drink themed after one of the challenges: the Cannon Ball Refresher.

The special beverage was available for a limited time starting January 14, 2026… and it was so successful that Starbucks is bringing it back for their springtime menu, this time with a twist.

Starbucks adds 2 new Cannon Ball Drinks to its regular menu

Starting March 3, customers can grab a brand-new Pink Cannon Ball Drink as part of the chain’s spring menu.

The creamy pink drink is made with coconut milk, Mango Dragonfruit and Strawberry Açaí Refreshers, shaken with ice and topped with strawberry and dragonfruit inclusions for a smooth, creamy flavor.

Unlike the original Cannon Ball Drink, this Pink version will remain on the menu past the spring season.

That’s not all; Starbucks is also making a coffee version of the Beast Games-inspired beverage, which will be available to purchase on its not-so-secret secret menu.

Customers can order the Cold foam Cannon Ball beginning Monday, February 23, exclusively through the Starbucks app in the Offers tab.

To order, simply select a Grande Cannon Ball Refresher and add Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Foam.
