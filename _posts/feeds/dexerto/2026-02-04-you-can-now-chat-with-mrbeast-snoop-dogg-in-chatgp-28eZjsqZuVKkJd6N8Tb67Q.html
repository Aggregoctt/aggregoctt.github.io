Text-to-speech software Speechify has launched an app for ChatGPT that gives the AI a voice donated by famous pop culture figures like rapper Snoop Dogg and YouTube star MrBeast.

ChatGPT already has its own built-in voice mode, allowing users to ‘chat’ with the AI for hands-free conversations. But thanks to Speechify, you can now talk to actual celebrities… Well, sort of.

Speechify is an AI-powered text-to-speech software that turns the text from any website, PDF, or other document into natural-sounding speech. This is especially useful for those with blindness, dyslexia, or other impairments, or even busy folks who want to multitask.

Speechify is now available directly in ChatGPT, bringing its technology to the chatbot in an app designed specifically for it, along with some big star power.

ChatGPT gets MrBeast voice with Speechify app integration

As of February 3, 2026, users can add the Speechify app directly into ChatGPT using the ‘apps’ option in the AI’s sidebar. Simply add ‘@speechify’ after the response you’d like to have read aloud, and the program will do it for you.

What’s more, a few high-profile celebrities have lent their voice to Speechify’s library. These stars include rapper Snoop Dogg, A-List actress Gwyneth Paltrow, and even YouTube icon Jimmy ‘MrBeast‘ Donaldson.

“Speechify is built to make information easier to consume through voice,” Speechify’s founder and CEO, Cliff Weitzman, said in a press release.

“By integrating our premium voice AI into ChatGPT, we’re extending that experience to the most widely used AI interfaces. This launch allows people to instantly turn answers into audio using the best voices available, making AI more accessible and more human through Speechify.”

This isn’t the first time MrBeast has lent his image to an AI; in 2023, the YouTuber also partnered with Meta to create a custom chatbot of himself, along with TikTok star Charli D’Amelio, Kendall Jenner, Tom Brady, Naomi Osaka, and even Paris Hilton, among several others.
