Totally Legit Wheeler Seller puts you in charge of a questionable used-car lot where the goal is simple: buy beat-up cars for pennies on the dollar, fix them up, and flip them for profit.

Developed by Sombrero Labs and published by Simplicity Games, Totally Legit Wheeler Seller drops players into a world of sketchy deals, suspicious bargains, and barely roadworthy classics.

You’ll hunt for cars, patch them together (sometimes literally), negotiate for the best deals around, and build your own dealership from the ground up.

Here’s everything we know so far about the indie second-hand car sales sim.

Release window

Totally Legit Wheeler Seller does not currently have a confirmed release date. The game is listed as “coming soon” on Steam, with players able to wishlist it ahead of launch to stay updated on future announcements.

What platforms will Totally Legit Wheeler Seller be on?

So far, Totally Legit Wheeler Seller has only been announced for PC via Steam. There’s been no mention of console versions.

Trailers

Simplicity Games released a trailer on September 24, 2025, that provides an overview of the game. Check it out below.

What’s it about?

Totally Legit Wheeler Seller casts you as an entrepreneur looking to turn rust buckets into quick cash.

You’ll scour junkyards and back-alley sellers for cheap finds, fix them just enough to be sellable, and use smooth talk and “carefully edited” photos to convince buyers you’re running a reputable operation.

Gameplay details

Your daily grind revolves around hunting for cars, giving them quick cosmetic touch-ups, and mastering the art of negotiation. Players make use of haggling dialogue, price sliders, and well-timed charm to seal deals with customers.

A built-in photo-editing tool lets you retouch shots of your cars to make them look far better than they are, while upgrading your lot unlocks new services like car washes and tuning shops.

That’s everything we know about Totally Legit Wheeler Seller so far, but we’ll update this page as soon as the developer share more.
