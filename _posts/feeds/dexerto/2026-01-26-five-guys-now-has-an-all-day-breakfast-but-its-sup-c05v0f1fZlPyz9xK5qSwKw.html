Five Guys has debuted an all-day breakfast menu at one of its locations, and you’ll have to be in the United States to try it. 

Over the last few years, fast food restaurants have been experimenting with different options that are outside of their norm. Whethers thats new limited-time new menu items, fresh drink options, or a full-blown spin-off restaurant, there has been plenty of innovation at items. 

Five Guys is the next to follow the likes of McDonald’s and KFC with a bit of innovation. While the burger joint does have a breakfast menu, unlike McDonald’s, they don’t run it all day across the United States.

However, that is changing – at least in one location – as Five Guys is going to run an all-day breakfast menu at all. 

Five Guys has all-day breakfast in Las Vegas

That’s right, one of Five Guys’ restaurants in Las Vegas, the newly opened spot inside the Grand Canal Shoppes at The Venetian casino, will run an all-day breakfast, according to The Street. 

Yet, that’s not the only interesting twist. Additionally, the new Las Vegas store will also offer a full menu of alcoholic beverages. That means a full bar serving beer, wine, frozen cocktails, and specialty alcoholic milkshakes. 

Alcohol is nothing new to Five Guys, seeing as their UK stores already stock different Budweiser brands, as well as collaborating with Hawkstone Lager in recent months. 

The all-day breakfast menu in Vegas is a trial run to see if its worth extending the offering to other outlets across the United States. Vegas’ unique positioning as a party city means that all-day offerings such as this will likely be a hit with tourists. 

Five Guys’ store within Grand Canal Shoppes at The Venetian casino has been open since the end of 2025, and has it’s own uniqueness compared to other stores. It even has a way for customers to watch as their fries are prepared.
