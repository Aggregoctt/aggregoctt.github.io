How to enter cheats

To utilize the above cheats, you’ll first need to turn on the ‘testingcheats’ code by opening the game’s console menu, which can be done by pressing CTRL/CMD + Shift + C on PC or Mac, or by pressing all four shoulder buttons on console.

  • After doing so, a box will appear at the top-left of your screen
    • Type ‘testingcheats on’ (without the apostrophes) and press Enter, X, or X depending on your platform.
  • A ‘Cheats are enabled’ message will appear in the bar below it.
  • With the cheat now enabled, you can copy any of the codes from the list above, paste them into the bar, and tweak them where relevant before submitting them.
