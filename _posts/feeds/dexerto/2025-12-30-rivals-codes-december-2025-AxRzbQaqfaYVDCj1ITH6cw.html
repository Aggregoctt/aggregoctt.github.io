How to redeem codes

To redeem codes in Rivals, you’ll need to take care of a few requirements first. This includes following the developers on X (@NosniyRBLX and @Sensei_RBX), joining the Nosniy Games Roblox group, and following SenseiWarrior’s Roblox profile. After completing these, you can redeem codes normally by following these steps:

  • Click on the ‘Shop‘ option at the bottom of the screen.
  • Click on ‘Rewards’ from the new menu that appears.
  • Scroll down to the bottom and enter your Roblox ID into the text box next to Verify.
  • After verifying your ID, enter a code into the text box below.
  • Click on ‘Redeem‘ to claim the rewards.
