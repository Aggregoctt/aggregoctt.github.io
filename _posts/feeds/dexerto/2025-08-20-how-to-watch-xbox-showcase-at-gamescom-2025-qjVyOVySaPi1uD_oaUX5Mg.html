Xbox is kicking off the first proper day of Gamescom with a showcase that’s set to be packed with exciting reveals.

After an Opening Night show that brought extensive looks at Black Ops 7, Resident Evil Requiem, and even the surprise announcement of Black Myth: Zhong Kui from Game Science, it’s Microsoft’s turn to take center stage.

Here’s everything you need to know about how to watch both Xbox Gamescom broadcasts, as well as what we’re expecting to see.

The first Xbox Gamescom show gets underway on Wednesday, August 20, 2025, at 6 AM PT / 9 AM EDT / 2 PM BST / 3 PM CEST. It hasn’t been confirmed how long it will run for, but we’re expecting it to be between one and two hours.

Then, the second show begins on Thursday, August 21, at the same time.

Where to watch Xbox Gamescom showcases

You can watch the events live by tuning into any of Xbox’s official channels, which we’ve linked below:

  • YouTube
  • Twitch
  • TikTok
  • Facebook

Alternatively, many popular content creators will be streaming and reacting live, so head over to your favorite channel when the time comes to watch along with them.
