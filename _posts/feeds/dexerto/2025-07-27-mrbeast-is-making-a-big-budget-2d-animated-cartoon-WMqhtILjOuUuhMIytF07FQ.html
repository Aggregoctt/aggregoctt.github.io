MrBeast has rapidly risen to superstar status, with his YouTube channel being the biggest on the platform. He’s more famous than most Hollywood celebrities at this point, and now he’s making an animated series starring himself.

Though MrBeast is mainly known as a YouTuber, he’s no stranger to traditional TV. The Amazon Prime exclusive Beast Games was a huge hit, topping viewership on the streaming platform every time a new episode came out. And, with him considering the purchase of an American Football club, it’s clear that the YouTuber is looking into more and more business ventures as his channel grows.

Now, as part of a cross-promotional effort to push his line of MrBeast Lab toys, he’s making an entire animated series to flesh out the storyline.

He claims to have been working on this series for a long time, and you’ll be able to watch it in October 2025.

MrBeast announces MrBeast Lab animated series

MrBeast Lab has that sort of late 90s/early 2000s Saturday morning cartoon look that’s largely missing from modern kids programming. It’s a throwback in a lot of ways, though it is directed toward mainly children.

The series will see MrBeast travelling through his 100-story lab, with each layer containing all sorts of different experiments. The story is centered on his pursuit to create the strongest monsters in order to fight off evil shadow monsters and assumedly save humanity from the menace.

MrBeast didn’t have any superpowers in the trailer. Rather, his creations are doing the heavy lifting, with all sorts of bizarre creatures showing up. The main protagonist aside from him was a creature sharing the same look as MrBeast’s iconic logo, and the action shots featuring him had some real production value behind them. This show’s quality is no joke if the trailer’s anything to go off of.

MrBeast Lab’s toys may not be on your radar if you’re an adult, but they’re incredibly popular amongst children and have a number of TV ads that are often pushed separate from his videos.

They’re collectible little monsters that have some limited-edition and very rare pulls, making some creatures more valuable than others. Regardless of how much money he expects to make off the show, it may not matter if he can use it as a way to raise awareness of his toy line and create revenue there.

When is MrBeast Lab coming out?

Many of the details about the show are still veiled in mystery, however. For instance, it isn’t yet known if this is a series we can expect on YouTube or if it’ll be on a streaming platform. It also isn’t clear how many episodes there will be, nor how long we can expect these episodes to run.

However, with a release window of October 2025, it won’t be long until we know more about the show.
