If you’re playing Fortnite Reload and spotted the wild, colorful chaos of Squid Grounds, you might be tempted to jump right in.

Epic Games has teamed up with Squid Game for one of the most stylish and surreal map crossovers yet, packed with familiar sights, reimagined challenges, and plenty of loot.

Maybe you’re curious about where to land, looking for nostalgic nods to the show, or just want to survive longer than your last match. Either way, this guide breaks down every POI you’ll find in the Squid Grounds map.

Fortnite Reload Squid Grounds map

Here is every POI confirmed to be coming to Reload during the Squid Grounds collab.

  • Affluent Arrivals
  • Costly Condos
  • Designer Docks
  • Hectic Hedges
  • Manager’s Meals
  • Phoney Crossroads
  • Player’s Power
  • Red Greens
  • Square Meals
  • The Labyrinth

The Labyrinth

This POI is a cool nod to the Squid Game Labyrinth. The colors are super bright: pinks, yellows, reds just like in the show. The layout is that same trippy maze of staircases going in every direction.

It feels chaotic on purpose, with doors and paths that don’t make sense, which is exactly how the Labyrinth was meant to mess with your head. They’ve even got the circular windows and weird geometry.

Red Greens

Red Greens is Squid Game’s “Red Light, Green Light” arena, but totally reimagined in Fortnite style.

You’ve got this peaceful-looking farm with barns, crops, silos, and white fences, but right in the center is that iconic field from the game, complete with hay bales and the creepy playground vibe.

Square Meals

Square Meals is a clever remix of the Squid Game dorms where the players slept and stressed out.

But here, it’s way brighter and blockier, almost like a pastel city made out of toy blocks. You’ve got these colorful square buildings stacked with those familiar pink and teal doors, triangle/star/square symbols on the walls, and even cafeteria-style benches outside.
