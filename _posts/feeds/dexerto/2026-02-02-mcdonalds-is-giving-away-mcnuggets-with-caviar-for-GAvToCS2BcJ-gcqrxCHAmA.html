McDonald’s has announced a limited-time Valentine’s Day stunt that pairs its Chicken McNuggets with high-end caviar, and is giving away exclusive kits online to fans for free.

The fast food giant announced it has partnered with Paramount Caviar to create McNugget Caviar kits that will drop online at 11 a.m. ET on Tuesday, February 10. The release is online only, and supplies are limited.

What comes in the McNugget Caviar kit

Each kit includes a one-ounce tin of McNugget Caviar made with Baerii sturgeon roe, a $25 Arch Card to purchase Chicken McNuggets, plus crème fraîche and a Mother of Pearl caviar spoon.

McDonald’s says the kits will be available exclusively through the McNugget Caviar website and will not be sold in restaurants.

According to the company, the idea was inspired by fans who already enjoy pairing McNuggets with caviar. The brand said the contrast between the crispy nuggets and the salty roe helped drive the collaboration.

The kits are being offered at no cost, with McDonald’s covering the price of the premium product. Fans will need to act quickly once the drop goes live since quantities are limited.
