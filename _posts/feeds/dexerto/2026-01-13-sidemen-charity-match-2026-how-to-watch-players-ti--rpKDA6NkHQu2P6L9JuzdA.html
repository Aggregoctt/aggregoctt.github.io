The Sidemen have confirmed the first details for the Sidemen Charity Match 2026, with the creator football event returning to Wembley Stadium on Saturday, April 18.

The annual match will once again see Sidemen FC face YouTube Allstars, bringing together some of the biggest creators on the internet for a globally streamed charity fixture. The 2026 edition will raise money for Bright Side and BBC Children in Need.

When is the Sidemen Charity Match 2026?

The Sidemen Charity Match 2026 takes place on Saturday, April 18, at Wembley Stadium in London. Kick-off time and full matchday timings are yet to be confirmed.

As with past editions, the focus will be on a global online audience, with fans able to watch the entire match live via official Sidemen platforms.

How to watch Sidemen Charity Match 2026

Broadcast details for the Sidemen Charity Match 2026 have not yet been fully confirmed. But expect it to be streamed free on YouTube on the Sidemen YouTube channel, just as in previous years.

As it’s all for charity, donation links will be available ahead of the match and throughout the broadcast to support the chosen charities.

Tickets

Tickets for the Sidemen Charity Match 2026 go on sale on Monday, February 2, from 9 am. Fans can sign up in advance to gain early access to the ticket queue via the Wembley Stadium website.

Ticket prices are still to be announced, as well as the prices for hospitality packages and VIP boxes. For reference, 2025 prices ranged between £12 and £29 for standard tickets.

Sidemen FC Players

The match will feature Sidemen FC versus YouTube Allstars. Player announcements are expected closer to the event, with previous charity matches featuring creators, streamers, musicians, and athletes from across the internet.

The players for each team have been drip-fed in various announcements in past years, with the likes of KSI, MrBeast, IShowSpeed, ChrisMD, and others regularly taking part.

However, this year, the Sidemen have “split up” to be spread across both teams and won’t just all be on the Sidemen FC squad.

“The teams have not been decided yet, but players will be announced over the next few weeks,” Miniminter revealed on January 12. “We’ve got people that have been confirmed, but they haven’t been publicly confirmed.”
