MrBeast’s Feastables is going to be available in drink form as part of a new collab with Liquid Death to introduce peanut butter cup sparkling water.

Liquid Death has made headlines over the years with its wild collabs, proving that flavored water doesn’t have to just taste like fruit.

Earlier this year, the company joined forces with Fruity Pebbles for cereal milk-flavored water – a creation that had this author extremely impressed.

Before that, they released Hot Fudge Sundae water in a partnership with Van Leeuwena, a surprisingly solid hit that tickled tastebuds.

Now, Liquid Death is reportedly set to work with MrBeast’s chocolate company, Feastables, in what could be its craziest creation yet, but we’re going to be waiting quite a bit to try it.

MrBeast and Liquid Death join forces for absurd new water flavor

According to food blogs Sodaseekers and Sporked, the Liquid Death and Feastables peanut butter cup water is set to release for Halloween 2026.

That means fans are going to be stuck in limbo for a full year before this item is available on shelves.

Sodaseekers reports that the collab was revealed at The NACS Show, a convenience store tradeshow.

It will be interesting to see if this product has any tie-in to MrBeast’s Team Water, the YouTuber’s global campaign to provide clean water to 2 million people worldwide.

The peanut butter cup water comes as absurd food flavors are becoming increasingly common across the industry.

Earlier this year, two ice cream companies went viral after they revealed breast milk-flavored ice cream and another that tasted like sunscreen.
