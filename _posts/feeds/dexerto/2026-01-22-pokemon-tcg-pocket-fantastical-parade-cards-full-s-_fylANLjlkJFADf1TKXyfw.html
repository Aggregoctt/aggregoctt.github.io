The next expansion for Pokemon TCG Pocket – Fantastical Parade – is almost here, and fans are buzzing about its vibrant festival theme, new gameplay elements, and powerful new cards such as Mega Gardevoir ex.

This new set not only continues the B-series’ tradition of introducing Mega Evolution ex cards, but also marks the debut of Stadium Trainer cards and gives a boost to Psychic-type creatures and decks.

Fantastical Parade also rolls out new events and features, including messages between players and random solo battles where you don’t know the CPU’s deck until the battle begins.
