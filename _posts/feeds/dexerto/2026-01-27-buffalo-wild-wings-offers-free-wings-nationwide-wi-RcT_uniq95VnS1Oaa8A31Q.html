Buffalo Wild Wings is bringing back its annual Free Wings for America promotion around the NFL Super Bowl in California, giving customers two different ways to score six wings at no cost.

The restaurant chain confirmed the deal will run nationwide for the eighth straight year, tying the giveaway to either a qualifying pre-order ahead of game day or the matchup going into overtime.

This year’s Super Bowl is set for February 8, with Buffalo Wild Wings outlining specific dates and redemption windows for each option, including limits on bundles and reward expirations.

How Buffalo Wild Wings free wings will work

Fans who choose the pre-order route must log into or create a Blazin’ Rewards account, then place a minimum $25 order between February 2 and February 7 for pickup on February 8. Those customers will receive a reward for six free boneless or traditional wings that can be redeemed on a future visit from February 10 through March 10.

The second option depends on the game itself. If the matchup in Santa Clara, California, is tied at the end of regulation and goes to overtime, Buffalo Wild Wings will offer six free wings to all guests at participating locations on February 23 from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. The overtime deal is valid for dine-in and in-person takeout only, with one free order per customer while supplies last.

“Even though last year’s matchup didn’t go into overtime, the thrill of the game is why we keep coming back,” said Tristan Meline, Brand President at Buffalo Wild Wings. “Whether fans are gathering at B Dubs or cheering from home, we can’t wait to give everyone a chance this year to score big with six free wings – no matter what team they’re rooting for.”
