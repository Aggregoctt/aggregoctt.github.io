The worlds of the NFL and Umamusume have unexpectedly collided after the Seattle Seahawks shared anime-style fan art inspired by Umamusume: Pretty Derby on their official social media.

Following their win against the San Francisco 49ers on January 17, 2026, the Seahawks posted an illustration featuring Umamusume characters Agnes Tachyon and Gold Ship.

In the artwork, Agnes Tachyon is dressed in Seahawks-style colours and is shown overtaking Gold Ship to claim the win. Meanwhile, Gold Ship is wearing a hat emblazoned with a San Francisco 49ers logo, looking shocked as her rival overtakes her.

Umamusume gets unofficial collab with NFL

The post has quickly gone viral, where it’s racked up more than 88,000 likes and caught the attention of the Umamusume community.

“No way, I can’t believe I’m in the timeline where umamusume x NFL is a thing,” wrote one fan while another joked, “Damn, I didn’t have Uma x NFL tweet on my bingo card.”

Others leaned fully into the absurdity, adding: “At this rate, the Seahawks are gonna win the Super Bowl and then we’re gonna get an official Umamusume themed Seahawks Super Bowl victory poster despite the two having nothing in common, and I’m totally down for it.”
