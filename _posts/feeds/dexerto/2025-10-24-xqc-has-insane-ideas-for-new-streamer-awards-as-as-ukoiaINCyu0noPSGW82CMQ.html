Streaming star Felix ‘xQc’ Lengyel has revealed he has “insane ideas” for Asmongold and Adin Ross’ streamer awards show, and Asmongold wants him involved.

For quite some time, streamers and content creators were not recognised at end-of-year awards shows. Some events did tack on streamer of the year awards, but that encompassed both Twitch and YouTube, causing frustration and controversy. 

That sparked QTCinderella to launch her show – The Streamer Awards. The annual event has grown and grown over the years, and is a night that the streaming world looks forward to with plenty of excitement. 

QT has even been supportive of Adin Ross and Asmongold’s plans to start their own, rival awards show, saying it would be “incredibly entertaining” to watch as they would have categories that she doesn’t give out awards for. 

Asmongold wants xQc on rival streamer awards

Twitch and Kick star xQc was used as the connect to get Adin and Asmon to link up, and Asmon wants him to be involved in the planning and judging. 

“I don’t know, really, how much he wants to get involved with it at all, thats going to be up to him. It would be great to have him do it,” Asmon said, noting he hasn’t spoken to xQc about things.

The Canadian, though, is up for it. “I told Adin I would get involved,” he said, reacting to Asmon’s clip. “I don’t wanna leak all my ideas because people always steal them, but I had these ideas that are insane.”

The new awards show hasn’t nailed down a date or venue yet, but they are looking at “end of December” to host it. They also aren’t looking to do it from Adin Ross’ compound, but rather somewhere like Las Vegas. 

With all three being heavily involved with Kick, it has been suggested that things would be slanted towards the Stake-backed platform. However, they all retain a presence on Twitch too, so it could very well be pretty balanced.
