Kai Cenat gave Nicki Minaj a bit of advice on joining Twitch as the rapper has an interest in streaming and has also held talks with Adin Ross about joining Kick. 

Over the years, plenty of celebrities have gotten involved with the streaming world. Some have gone all in, launching their own channel so that they can make music with fans or show off their gaming skills. Others, though, just stick to guest appearances. 

Kai Cenat has become of the streamers that attracts plenty of celebrity collaborations, having the likes of John Cena, Kevin Hart, and Nicki Minaj on stream with him.

Minaj is the closest of those to getting involved full-time. The ‘Starships’ artist has expressed an interest in streaming, and Adin Ross has attempted to get her over on Kick.

Nicki Minaj speaks to Kai Cenat about joining Twitch

However, she has sought advice from Kai about the two platforms, and he has given her some big positives about joining Twitch.

“Twitch is just easier discoverability, and they’ll be able to find you easier. Kick is pretty good too, but it’s just fairly new, you know,” he told her during a phone call on his July 9 stream. 

“They have,” Kai added when asked about being offered a contract from Kick. “They do do deals and stuff like that, yeah.”

The artist has also been pitched by Adin, who said she wouldn’t have to worry about censoring herself on the Stake-back platform. 

“You could just do that, be you and not have to worry about bans or any of that,” the Kick co-owner told Minaj.

It remains to be seen if she will go all in and start streaming, and just where she’ll do that, too.
