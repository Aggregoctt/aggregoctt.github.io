Baby Groot is finally joining Fortnite as a sidekick, and players are already confused about where he is.

Epic confirmed the companion on December 24, but he is still nowhere to be found. That gap between announcement and availability has left a lot of players refreshing the shop, wondering if they missed something. The short answer is no: Baby Groot simply is not live yet.

This guide breaks down when he releases, where to find him, and what to expect once he arrives.

The Baby Groot sidekick will release on December 27, while other announced cosmetics are rolling out on different dates, with iShowSpeed’s Icon Series skin arriving earlier and the Pulp Fiction bundle following on December 30.

Epic has not announced a limited window, so once Baby Groot lands, he should remain available like other sidekicks.

How to get Baby Groot in Fortnite

Baby Groot will be available directly through the Item Shop in Fortnite. He is not tied to quests, challenges, or Battle Pass progression.

Pricing has not been confirmed, but previous sidekicks give a solid estimate. The SpongeBob sidekick launched as a standalone cosmetic priced around 800 to 1,200 V-Bucks, with bundle options pushing higher, so Baby Groot is expected to fall in a similar range.

Once purchased, Baby Groot can be equipped as a sidekick through the Locker, just like any other cosmetic.

This Baby Groot is separate from the older Baby Groot back bling introduced in Chapter 2 Season 4. That version was quest-based, while this is a brand-new companion.
