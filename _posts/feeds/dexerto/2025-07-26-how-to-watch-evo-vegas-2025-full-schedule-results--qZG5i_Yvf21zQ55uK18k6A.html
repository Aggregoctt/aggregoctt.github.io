Evo has long been the biggest fighting game tournament in the world, with 2024’s event breaking the record for biggest esports tournament in history. They’re looking to run it back in 2025 and put on a tournament with thousands upon thousands of competitors across several games.

The Evo name is big enough at this point that there are three tournaments for it, with the main Evo tourney for the year still being hosted in Vegas all these years later.

However, there are so many games being streamed at once that keeping track of all of them is incredibly difficult. There are multiple Twitch channels devoted to keeping track of different brackets through the competition, all the way from entry-level pools matches to the final top 8s.

So, to help you traverse Evo and keep track of every game, here’s the full schedule for every game along with what Twitch channels you’ll need to keep an eye on to catch the action.

Evo 2025 schedule for every main game

There are a total of 8 main games at Evo this year, with over a dozen of smaller side tournaments having their brackets run independently. For the sake of keeping this schedule somewhat readable, we’ll be keeping it to the main 8 games.
