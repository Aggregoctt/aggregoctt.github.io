The Forge isn’t an easy game to succeed in, but it’s made a lot easier thanks to your race. That is, if you’re lucky enough to pull a powerful race. If not, you can always reroll and grab the perfect one for you; you just need to know how.

Whether you’re looking to destroy enemies, explore the world, or mine for ores to make weapons, there’s so much to do in Roblox’s The Forge. However, a lot of it is dictated by your race, and the game can be considerably harder or easier if you pick the perfect race for you.

Well, it’s not always as easy as picking what one works for your playstyle, as races are randomly rolled. Luckily, you can always reroll. So, here’s how to reroll your race in The Forge, and all you need to know about what you’re likely to land on.

How to reroll Race

When you first start playing The Forge, you get the chance to roll for your initial race, which is found with Sensei Moro in the first quest.

After that, you’ll need to find The Wizard, who can be found by looking for a tall tower behind Greedy Cey. To get there, follow these steps:

  1. Head to the left of The Cave.
  2. Follow up the hill past Greedy Cey.
  3. Follow the path until you see a Tall Tower behind a building.
  1. Enter the building and speak to The Wizard.
  2. Click “I want to change my race.“

It’s worth noting, the main quest will naturally take you here eventually, but you can go there at any time. All you need to do is reroll if you have enough rolls.

How to reroll races in the Shop

Alternatively, if you don’t fancy heading to the Wizard, then you can head to the reroll race menu in the shop.

Head into the shop by pressing T and navigating to the Shop tab. Then click the Race Menu option and click Reroll.

How to get more rolls

To get more rolls in The Forge, you can try the following:

  • Complete quests: These often offer free rerolls as you play.
  • Redeem codes: There are always a few free codes to offer rerolls.
  • Buy rerolls: You can purchase rerolls from the shop by spending Robux.

How to unlock Race Slots

If you’re struggling to choose one of the above races, then you can always stack up to three of them with enough Race Slots.

To do this, you’ll need to reach level 30 to unlock the second slot (the first is unlocked by default) and reach level 100 for the third slot.

You can use Robux to unlock it if you don’t want to wait, and will need to pay 500 Robux to get both slots.
