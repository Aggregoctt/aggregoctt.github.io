How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in Jujutsu Shenanigans is quick and doesn’t require completing a tutorial. You can claim rewards immediately once you spawn into the game by following these steps:

  • Click on the shop icon in the top-left corner of the screen.
  • Go to the ‘Rewards‘ tab in the new menu that appears.

  • Click on ‘Redeem‘ to claim the reward from the code.
