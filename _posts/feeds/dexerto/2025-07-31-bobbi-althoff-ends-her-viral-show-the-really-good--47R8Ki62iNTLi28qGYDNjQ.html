Bobbi Althoff has officially ended her viral interview series, The Really Good Podcast, after more than two years of episodes that frequently went viral on social media. She announced the decision in a final episode published on July 31.

Bobbi Althoff, who rose to prominence with her awkward, deadpan interviews featuring celebrities like Drake, Offset, and Lil Yachty, opened the farewell with a blunt admission. She told viewers, “I thought it was going to last longer. I didn’t know I’d fall off as quickly as I did.”

The podcast first launched in April 2023 and quickly drew attention for her deadpan interview style and outtakes from the show. Episodes featuring Shaquille O’Neal and Nick Cannon racked up millions of views and helped cement Althoff’s brand of intentionally uncomfortable comedy.

Bobbi Althoff ends podcast after two years

Her final episode reflected the same tone that made the series popular. Wearing black sunglasses and seated in a dimly lit room, Althoff confirmed that the show would not continue. She did not share specific plans for what comes next.

Althoff’s exit leaves open questions about whether she will pivot to acting, hosting, or other creator projects, as she closes the chapter on one of the internet’s most recognizable interview shows.
