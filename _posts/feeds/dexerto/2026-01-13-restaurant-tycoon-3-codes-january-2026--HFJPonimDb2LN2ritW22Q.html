What are Restaurant Tycoon 3 codes used for?

Restaurant Tycoon 3 codes are mainly used to give cash and diamonds, both of which play different roles in running and upgrading your restaurant. Cash supports day-to-day growth like expanding your layout and improving operations, while diamonds (gems) are a premium currency used for higher-end features.

Diamonds let you level chefs beyond Level 3, buy furniture from the EKEA shop, unlock worker hats and accessories, and purchase music discs that improve your restaurant’s rating and atmosphere.
