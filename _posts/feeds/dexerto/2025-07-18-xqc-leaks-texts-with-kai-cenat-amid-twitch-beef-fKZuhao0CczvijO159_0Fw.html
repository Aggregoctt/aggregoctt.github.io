Streaming star Felix ‘xQc’ Lengyel leaked a text between himself and Kai Cenat as the pair have been beefing.

Over the last few months, xQc and Kai Cenat haven’t seen eye to eye on a few things. The Canadian claimed Kai’s friends were viewbotting for their Twitch success, which Kai said was “weird” to say, and xQc also took issue with how Adin Ross had seemingly been treated by the AMP member. 

The former Overwatch star also said he ‘didn’t care’ if Kai disliked him because he isn’t after any collaborations with the Twitch superstar. 

Well, Kai cleared things up on his July 17 stream, saying he has no bad blood with any streamers bar xQc. “I don’t want anything to do with him,” the AMP star said, noting he has messaged him in the past when problems have arisen.

xQc responds to Kai Cenat calling him out

In that, Kai said that xQc regularly sends him “paragraphs” trying to solve any issues. Well, xQc leaked an example of that from past December. 

“Thought I’d touch base and pipe down without all the internet noise to tell you I care about you and I’m not a snake in the grass,” the Canadian said. 

xQc added that he was “genuinely sorry” for throwing shade at Kai and would have been happy if he ‘doubled down’ to throw some shade back his way. “I have enough money/views brother, I’m not gonna be a rat for either you know that,” he concluded. 

The Twitch and Kick streamer said the message was an “olive branch” as he didn’t want “problems” with Kai over an out-of-context clip. 

“When I apologize for s*it, I don’t have to be prompted. I’ll do it ahead of time if I feel like I need to apologize. I don’t care about the internet,” he added.

xQc, however, did not reveal if Kai had responded to his messages at the time.
