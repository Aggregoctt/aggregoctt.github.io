A Japanese content creator received a one-of-a-kind Yu-Gi-Oh! card from Konami, created to celebrate his wedding.

When you picture a wedding gift, it’s easy to think of kitchen utensils, gadgets, or even homewares, among other goodies.

For well-known content creator Hajime Syacho, who has also voiced President Goha in Yu-Gi-Oh! Sevens, he didn’t get just any old gift. For him and his bride, they got a Yu-Gi-Oh! card. Not just any card you can buy in store, though.

Konami made a personalized one-of-a-kind card with an illustration of him and his wife as a way to celebrate his recent wedding.

Content creator gets the one and only Yu-Gi-Oh! Card in the world

Hajime Syacho took the news to his X account on December 7, 2025, saying he’s extremely thankful for the “wonderful gift” from Konami. 

He even uploaded a video unboxing the card, and as you can see, it’s absolutely massive compared to the regular ones.

The custom card in question is essentially an alternate or redesigned version of Ritual Sanctuary. While the original version of it features an unknown bride and groom, Hajime Syacho’s card features a reference to his wedding photo, the same exact one in which the bride held a bouquet covering her face.

You can also see Raye and Roze, drawn in chibi style, holding the gown in the image, which gives a nice touch.

And that’s not all. Beyond the custom image, there are also details regarding his marriage printed on the card. 

For example, the date August 30, 2025, on which he announced his marriage, is also featured on the card. It’s also common for TCGs to include a set code number on a card, and in this case, the HJMN-JP001 is a subtle nod to him.

Naturally, it didn’t take too long for people to notice his post, flocking to the comments section to share their thoughts.

“Even their wedding photo turned into a legendary drop,” one user commented.

Meanwhile, another user said, “This card guarantees a summon of love that lasts a lifetime.”
