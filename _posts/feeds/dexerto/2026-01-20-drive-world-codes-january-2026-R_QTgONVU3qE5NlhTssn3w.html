How to redeem codes

After selecting your first car, you’ll be able to redeem any code by following these steps:

  • Click on the gear icon on the top-left side of the screen to open the Settings menu.
  • Click on the ‘Open‘ option next to Promo Codes.
  • Enter the code in the text box.
  • Click on ‘Redeem‘ to claim the rewards.
