Professional boxer Anthony Joshua has been involved in a car crash in Nigeria that resulted in two deaths, with the former world champion sustaining only minor injuries.

The incident took place on December 29 in Ogun State, Nigeria, while Joshua was traveling along the Lagos-Ibadan expressway. Reports state that the Jeep carrying the boxer collided with a stationary truck in the Makun area, to which Joshua was a passenger in the vehicle at the time.

Local reports have confirmed that two people have also died in the incident, but authorities have not released further details regarding their identities. Joshua was treated for minor injuries and is understood to be in stable condition.

Anthony Joshua sustains minor injuries in car crash

The 36-year-old boxer had recently traveled to Nigeria following his latest fight appearance, where he took part in a high-profile bout against Jake Paul in Miami over the Holidays. After the fight, Joshua celebrated in Nigeria, sharing moments from his visit, including time spent with family.

Joshua is one of the most recognizable figures in boxing, having held multiple heavyweight world titles during his career. Since turning professional in 2013, he has headlined events across the UK, the US, and internationally, becoming a major global name in the sport.

At this time, there is no indication that the injuries he sustained will impact his immediate plans. No official statement has been released by Joshua or his representatives beyond confirmation that he is safe.
