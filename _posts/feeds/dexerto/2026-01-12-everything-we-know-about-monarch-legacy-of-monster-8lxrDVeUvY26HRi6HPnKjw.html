Monarch: Legacy of Monsters is returning with a story that sees the fate of the world hanging in the balance, so here’s everything we know about Season 2.

Spinning off from Legendary’s ‘Monsterverse‘ movies – which included multiple King Kong and Godzilla films – Monarch: Legend of Monsters is a period piece that played out in multiple decades.

The central Season 1 storyline concerned a pair of siblings investigating their late father’s connection to the secretive Monarch organisation, which led them to army officer Lee Shaw, played by Wyatt Russell in the 1950s timeline, and his father Kurt Russell in scenes five decades later.

Season 2 picks up on Apple TV where Season 1 left off, so here’s everything we know in terms of release date, cast, trailer, and plot.

Monarch: Legacy of Monsters returns on February 27, 2025, when Episode 1 drops on Apple TV.

Monarch: Legacy of Monsters Season 2 plot

Here’s the official Legacy of Monsters synopsis: “Season 2 will pick up with the fate of Monarch – and the world – hanging in the balance. The dramatic saga reveals buried secrets that reunite our heroes (and villains) on Kong’s Skull Island, and a new, mysterious village where a mythical Titan rises from the sea.

“The ripple effects of the past make waves in the present day, blurring the bonds between family, friend and foe – all with the threat of a Titan event on the horizon.”

That’s it in terms of specifics right now, though we do know that Amber Midthunder’s character Isabel – who is new to the ensemble – will be “an intelligent and powerful businesswoman,” and “heavily recurring” in Season 2, as per Variety.
