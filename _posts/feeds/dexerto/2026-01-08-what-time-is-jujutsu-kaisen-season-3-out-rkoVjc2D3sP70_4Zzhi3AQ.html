Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 hits streaming today, so here’s details of when and where to watch, as well as what to expect from the ‘Culling Game Arc.’

Jujutsu Kaisen fans got an early sneak peak at Season 3 of the manga adaptation last November.

Episodes 1 and 2 were released in cinemas as Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution, providing a bridge between the the Shonen Jump’s Shubuya Incident and Culling Game, and grossing more than $40 million in the process.

Now fans of Greg Akutami’s manga can see where the new animated arc is heading from the comfort of their homes, via the arrival of Season 3 on streaming.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 drops on Crunchyroll today – January 8, 2026 – at 9am PT/12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (January 9)
  • 6am New Zealand (January 9)

The first two episodes arrive today, with more to follow weekly, though the exact episode count and release schedule is yet to be revealed.

What is Season 3 of Jujutsu Kaisen about?

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 follows the further adventures of Yuji Itadori, the boy who became a curse to exorcise a curse, and now finds himself doing battle with evil sorcerer Noritoshi Kamo via the Culling Game.

Here’s how Crunchyroll describes the start of the new arc via official press release: “A veil abruptly descends over the busy Shibuya area amid the bustling Halloween crowds, trapping countless civilians inside. Satoru Gojo, the strongest jujutsu sorcerer, steps into the chaos. But lying in wait are curse users and spirits scheming to seal him away.

“Yuji Itadori, accompanied by his classmates and other top-tier jujutsu sorcerers, enters the fray in an unprecedented clash of curses – the Shibuya Incident.

“In the aftermath, 10 colonies across Japan are transformed into dens of curses in a plan orchestrated by Noritoshi Kamo, the most wicked sorcerer in history. As the deadly Culling Game starts, Special Grade sorcerer Yuta Okkotsu is assigned to carry out Yuji’s execution for his perceived crimes.”
