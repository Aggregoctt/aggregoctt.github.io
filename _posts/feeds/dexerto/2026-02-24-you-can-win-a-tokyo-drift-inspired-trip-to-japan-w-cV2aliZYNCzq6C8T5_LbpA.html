Xbox has launched a Forza Horizon 6 Japan sweepstakes with Sung Kang, best known as Han in The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift, giving fans the chance to win a real-world trip inspired by the game.

With Forza Horizon 6 set in Japan, Xbox has leaned into the Tokyo Drift connection by partnering with Kang, whose character became one of the most recognizable faces in JDM culture on screen.

The Horizon Passport Sweepstakes will send winners to Japan for a multi-day car-focused experience curated by Kang and inspired by the themes of the upcoming title.

The itinerary includes cruising in a JDM car at night, a drifting masterclass with established drift drivers, and a tuning and livery design session at Liberty Walk. Winners will also take part in a Tokyo Drift filming location tour in cars featured in the movie, alongside an auto retail and museum visit.

It also includes a walk and snack tour with Kang, a sumo challenge, and an advance screening of his upcoming film Drifter.

In the press release, Kang said: “As a lifelong car enthusiast, storyteller, and someone who has spent countless hours working in garages and chasing that perfect drive, I’m beyond excited to share something truly special with the Forza and car community.”

He added: “This trip was created for Forza Horizon and car fans, and is our way of saying thank you and celebrating the community.”

How to enter

To enter, fans must wishlist Forza Horizon 6 and submit an entry form through the official sweepstakes site. The competition runs until March 23, 2026. No purchase is necessary, and entrants must be 21 or older.

With Forza Horizon 6 taking players to Japan, Xbox is tying the game directly to one of the most iconic street racing movies ever made.
