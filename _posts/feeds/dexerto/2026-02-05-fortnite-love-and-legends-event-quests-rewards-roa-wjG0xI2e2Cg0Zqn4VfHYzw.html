Fortnite‘s Valentine’s Day events usually lean into hearts and chaos, and Love and Legends does both. This event is full of daily freebies, map scavenging, and thematic quests, all wrapped around familiar faces like The Seven and HUNTR/X.

Fortnite Love and Legends event roadmap

The Love and Legends event runs throughout February 2026, with rewards and events rolling out on specific dates and some freebies tied to limited windows.

  • February 6: Love and Legends begins, daily login rewards go live, and HUNTR/X cosmetics hit the Item Shop.
  • February 7: Battle Royale Power Hour
  • February 13: Pink Storm Shield arrives, Nitemare Island returns, plus unvaults and extra freebies
  • February 14: Another Battle Royale Power Hour
  • February 15: OG Ice Storm Event featuring the Ice King
  • February 19: DeLorean returns and Lantern Fest Reload goes live
  • February 21: Battle Royale Power Hour
  • February 23: The Seven return, with signs pointing to The Foundation and a new item appearing

Daily login rewards are available until February 19 at 4 am ET, while gameplay-based rewards run slightly longer, ending on February 23 at 9 am ET.

Fortnite Love and Legends quests and rewards

Most of the free cosmetics in Love and Legends come from two simple sources: logging in and picking things up while playing with friends. There’s no single questline to grind through, and no mode lock beyond playing in team formats.
