Black Ops 6 Season 4 Reloaded is here, and that means weapon buffs and nerfs. So, here is everything that’s changing in the new update for both multiplayer and Warzone.

Whenever a new season rolls around in Call of Duty, buffs and nerfs are soon to follow. Sure, players might be more focused on new maps, changes to POIs in Warzone, new perks, or fresh game modes, but the weapon changes are what can really shift the gameplay.

Season 4 shook things up in a big way with the introduction of the LC10 SMG and FFAR 1 Assault Rifle, but fan-favorites like the CR-56 Amax, Ames 85, and Jackal PDW were all adjusted too.

As we’ve now hit the midway point of Season 4, that means Season 4 Reloaded is set to hit Black Ops 6 and really shake things up, too.

The new update will bring two new weapons to the mix – Pickaxe and Olympia Shotgun. Both are secondary weapons, though, and likely won’t see as much love as some other others.

There are also new attachments, including the G-Grip, a Stryder .22 3-round burst change, a full auto mod for the SVD, and a CQB full auto conversion kit for the TR2. 

As ever, some of the buffs and nerfs are specific to one mode – some changes you see in Multiplayer won’t make it over to Warzone, and vice versa. So, be sure to give everything a keen once-over.
