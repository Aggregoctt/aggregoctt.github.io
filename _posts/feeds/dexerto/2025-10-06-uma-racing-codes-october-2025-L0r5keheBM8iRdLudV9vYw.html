What are Uma Racing codes used for?

Uma Racing codes offer cash for you to spend in the game’s shop. In the shop, you can roll for a random Uma Style, Emote, MVP, or Name Tag.

Uma Racing offers you a random horse to use in a race. Each has special abilities, boosts, perks, and more, so you’ll want to ensure you settle on the best. Emotes and MVPs are purely ways to express yourself, as well as nametags.
