Good Mythical Morning’s Chief Creative Officer Stevie Wynne Levine has confirmed she is departing the company, sharing the news both on TikTok and in the show’s February 13 YouTube episode.

Levine, a longtime leader behind the Good Mythical Morning brand who often appeared in videos and provided narration, spoke directly to fans about her decision during a segment in the latest episode, addressing her future outside the company.

Levine said the final episode featuring her involvement as executive producer would air on March 13, 2026, the channel’s 3000th episode.

Stevie Wynne Levine announces departure

In a pre-recorded segment included in Good Mythical Morning’s February 13 video, Levine told viewers she was leaving her role at the company.

Reflecting on both personal and professional reasons behind the decision, Levine said she began reassessing her professional life when co-host Rhett McLaughlin experienced health issues in 2025.

“As many of you know, Rhett started experiencing some heart issues in the summer of last year, and he underwent surgery in the fall, and the gravity of those circumstances led to some important moments of reflection,” she said.

Segment begins at 17:41

Levine went on to look back at her 13-year tenure helping build Mythical Entertainment alongside Rhett and Link. “For the past 13 years, I’ve had the immeasurable privilege of building Mythical alongside Rhett and Link…along the way cultivating this extraordinary community that’s come to mean so much to me,” she said.

However, she explained that her priorities had shifted over time. “Like most creative people, I’ve always been motivated by the prospect of making things. My goal was never to build and oversee a large company,” Levine said, noting that as Mythical expanded, she found herself spending less time on the creative work she was “most passionate about.”

After in-depth conversations with hosts Rhett and Link, Levine said she informed the team in 2025 that she would be stepping away. “I’ve come to realize that I need to start putting myself first. I never thought I’d be saying this, I’m leaving Mythical,” she said.

The segment concluded with Rhett, Link, and Levine sharing a hug on set. Addressing her directly, Link said, “We understand the decision, we support you, and we’re also happy that this represents you branching out on your next chapter. It’s sadness and happiness because we know what all went into it.”
