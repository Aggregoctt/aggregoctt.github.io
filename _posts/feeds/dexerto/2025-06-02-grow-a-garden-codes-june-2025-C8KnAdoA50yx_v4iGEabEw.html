Roblox’s Grow a Garden has skyrocketed in popularity since it released back in March 2025, shattering multiple all-time player count records. Luckily, if you’re one of those players, you can grab some free rewards using codes.

One of the best things about any Roblox game is its free codes. Like Pokemon Bronze Forever, Funky Friday, or even Bee Swarm Simulator, Grow a Garden has codes to help grant free items, cosmetics, and the occasional pet.

So, if you’re looking to grab some free items without spending Robux, then here’s all you need to know about Grow a Garden codes.

Are there any active Grow a Garden codes?

There are currently no active codes available in Grow a Garden, following the expiration of LUNARGLOW10.

Nevertheless, it’s likely we’ll see more codes soon, as is typical with plenty of Roblox games, so be sure to check back regularly.

How to redeem codes

To redeem your codes in Grow a Garden you’ll need to complete the following steps:

  • Load up Grow a Garden.
  • Click on the small cog in the top left of your screen.
  • Copy and paste your code into the Redeem Codes section.
  • Press Claim.

With that, your reward will be claimed. If it’s a physical item, it’ll land in your inventory, which you can open by clicking on the backpack on the top left of the screen, next to the cog. Then, simply add it to your hotbar and use as you please.

Expired codes

As previously mentioned, one code has expired in Grow a Garden. Whenever a code expires after our checks, it’ll be moved here, so you know which ones aren’t worth redeeming.

What are Grow a Garden codes used for?

Grow a Garden codes are extremely useful if you want a little boost for your land or just an adorable pet to complement your adventure.

Seed Packs provide free crops for your garden, which not only saves you plenty of money, but can also help you grow crops you’ve not unlocked yet if you’re lucky.

Thanks to some handy codes, you’ll be able to minimize the grinding to get rich quick, and who knows, you may unlock some pretty rare seeds.
