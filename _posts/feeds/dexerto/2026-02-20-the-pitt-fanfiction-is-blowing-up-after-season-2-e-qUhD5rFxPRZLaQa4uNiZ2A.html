Robby (Noah Wyle) did a double take when he saw Mohan (Supriya Ganesh) tending to Abbot’s (Shawn Hatosy) wound, but if you wanted to see more than what The Pitt Season 2 Episode 7 delivered, there’s a solution: fan fiction.

There was a lot going down in the Pittsburgh Trauma Medical Center in the latest episode, one of the most prominent threads being Dana’s (Katherine LaNasa) lead on a sexual assault victim. 

But the very nature of The Pitt means we’re never sat with one case for too long, with the HBO Max show juggling multiple doctors, nurses, students, and critical patients for every hour of the shift. 

Amid the chaos, The Pitt Season 2 just served up a big moment for Mohabbot shippers, with Dr. Mohan giving Dr. Abbot off the books treatment for a bullet graze he got while working for the Pittsburgh SWAT team.

The Pitt Season 2 Episode 7 sparks fresh wave of Mohan & Abbot fan fiction

For those seeking more about this story, your best bet is to head to Archive of Our Own, aka AO3. Dozens of new fan fiction stories have been released or updated over the past day, including multiple tales involving romance between Dr. Samira Mohan and Dr. Jack Abbot.

Just one example comes from user msculper titled ‘Perfect Mix of Saturday Night and the Rest of Your Life’, and it’s already received 1,229 hits since being published on February 19. 

The story depicts sexual scenes between Samira and Jack, but it also expands on the characters outside of the emergency room, weaving in elements of the show, including the aftermath of the PittFest shooting in Season 1. 

But the growing catalogue isn’t limited to Mohabbot ships – fan fiction is used to explore all manner of relationships or other ‘what if’ scenarios that aren’t included in the show. 

Another popular subgenre is Dr. Langdon (Patrick Ball) and Dr. Mel (Taylor Dearden). Their relationship on The Pitt is purely platonic, but there’s plenty of fanfic imagining a world where it’s more than that. 

A recent example from user Cemeterycigs is titled ‘You’ve got a nine to five (so I’ll take the night shift)’, with the description stating, “Langdon starts thinking of Mel like the sun, not only because she’s bright, but because everything else feels colder without her.”

Although it was published last month, it was updated just after the release of The Pitt Season 2 Episode 7 – and the story has proven so popular, it’s received a whopping 24,983 hits at the time of writing. 

Some fanfic uses entirely different framing. For instance, ‘Crush’ by user BlueberryLily18 reimagines the narrative as if you, the reader, are on your first day of residency at the Pittsburgh Trauma Medical Centre. 

In short, there’s a whole world of stories beyond the confines of the ED.

The Season 2 Episode 7 moment hasn’t just sparked activity on AO3, either. Across Reddit, viewers are zeroing in on the brief exchange between Mohan and Abbot – particularly Robby’s double take when he walks past. 

“I laughed so hard at this shot,” wrote one on Reddit, to which another replied, “Just saw this scene and refreshed the sub. Might be the first time the show made me pause because of laughing too hard.”

Fanfic did become a talking point, with one viewer commenting, “Me, to my mother: ‘This scene is going to fuel a lot of fan fic’.” 

“The fanfic writers need to include this moment,” said another, to which a third replied, “This scene is basically a prompt for fanfiction. You can go anywhere from here.”
