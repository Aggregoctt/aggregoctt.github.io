Rainbow Six Siege X Twitch Drops and TikTok Game Rewards enable players to get free SUISUI Dokkaebi bundle and free Alpha Packs, so here’s how you can unlock them. 

Ubisoft is celebrating the release of Rainbow Six Siege X by giving every player the chance to claim some free in-game goodies. The highlight here is the SUISUI Uniform and SUISUI Mk 14 EBR Weapon Skin, which gives Dokkaebi an anime-style weapon skin and a cat-eared outfit. 

There are also some free Alpha Packs up for grabs, which will help kit out your Operators with cosmetics like outfits, weapon charms, and weapon skins. So, if you’re looking to try out Rainbow Six Siege X or are a returning player, then here’s how you can get Twitch Drops and TikTok Game Rewards. 

Twitch drop event duration 

The Rainbow Six Siege X Twitch Drops event began on June 10, 2025, and will run until 9 AM Pacific / 6 PM CEST on June 16. 

How to get Rainbow Six Siege X Twitch Drops

To claim Rainbow Six Siege X Twitch Drops, you need to watch select Siege X partner streams on Twitch. Once you have done this, you’ll be rewarded with in-game items. 

You can find a detailed step-by-step guide below:

  • Link your Twitch account and Ubisoft account.
  • Head over to the official Twitch website.
  • Watch eligible streams for 60 minutes or more. 
  • Claim your rewards from your Twitch Inventory. 
  • Open up Rainbow Six Siege X to get your rewards. 

Rewards

There are a total of four rewards that you can get in the Rainbow Six Siege X Twitch drops campaign. We’ve listed each item and the requirements below: 

  1. Watch 30 Minutes: SUISUI Weapon Charm
  2. Watch 2 Hours: SUISUI Mk 14 EBR Weapon Skin
  3. Watch 4 Hours: SUISUI Uniform
  4. Watch 6 Hours: SUISUI Headgear

It’s important to note that these rewards will be available until 9 AM Pacific / 6 PM CEST on June 16, after which you will no longer be able to claim them. 

TikTok Game Rewards

The Rainbow Six Siege X TikTok Game Rewards event kicks off at 9 AM Pacific / 6 PM CEST on June 10, which allows players to earn five Alpha Packs by watching live gameplay from select creators streaming Siege X.

TikTok Game Rewards will be available until July 9, 2025.

How to get TikTok Game Rewards

To get TikTok Game Rewards for Rainbow Six Siege X, you must do the following: 

  1. Watch a participating TikTok creator livestreaming Siege X gameplay (participating channels will display a “Game Rewards” tag).
  2. After three minutes of watch time, click the Share arrow (lower-right_ and select the Game Rewards icon to view and claim the reward.
  3. Enter the code on the official Rainbow Six Siege redeem website. 
  4. After redeeming the code to your Ubisoft account, the items can be found in your in-game inventory.
