Crochet TikToker TheCrochetGinger is making another massive CaseOh plushie, and this time it’s to celebrate his involvement with Waterpark Simulator.

The TikToker, whose real name is Jasmin, went viral back in August for making a mini version of the popular Twitch streamer. She designed the CaseOh plushie from scratch and documented the process in almost-daily videos on her account.

After it was done, Jasmin sent the amigurumi plushie off to the streamer, who opened up the gift on stream with excitement.

She’s now making another massive stuffed CaseOh, this time a life-sized version, thanks to a commission from the studio behind the game Waterpark Simulator.

TheCrochetGinger is making another massive CaseOh plushie

“The time has finally come, I’m officially making a life-sized CaseOh plushie,” she said in her first video showing the progress. “It’s not just any, though; it’s actually Waterpark Simulator-themed CaseOh.”

She went on to explain that Travis, the CEO of the studio that makes the game, commissioned her to make the “beast” of a project.

Just like the previous version, Jasmin is designing and creating the plushie from scratch. But it shouldn’t take too long, as she had the head finished by the end of the second day.

It’s absolutely massive, too. Before spending an hour adding in the stuffing, she put the crocheted piece over her body, and it covered most of it.

Jasmin created the eyes for CaseOh’s head in the third video and revealed that it alone weighs 16 pounds.

CaseOh built a massive following around his Waterpark Simulator streams, where he constructed and managed his park, Goob Lagoon, while interacting with his audience. Each stream focuses on expanding its in-game operations, introducing new attractions, and refining park layouts to handle the growing virtual crowds.
