Logan Paul has revealed that he picked up a grizzly injury following his WWE match with John Cena after the move went viral across social media.

Back in 2021, Logan Paul first got involved with WWE, but it took almost a year for him to take part in an actual match. Since that debut contest in 2022, ‘The Maverick’ has gone from strength to strength, winning titles, competing in massive matches, and winning awards for his skills.

Even though professional wrestling is pre-determined and for entertainment, the injuries are real. Logan has pulled off some insane moves in the last three years, regularly going viral with them too.

He has, to this point, avoided many serious injuries – aside from a knee injury. The ImPaulsive podcast has picked up plenty of bumps and bruises along the way, and that includes another grizzly following his tag team match with John Cena. 

Logan Paul injured in WWE match with John Cena

The Maverick teamed up with the Greatest of All Time at WWE’s ‘Money in the Bank’ event, and pulled off a moonsault from the ring onto the announce table. That is where the injury occurred. 

“Yeah, my shin is all fucked up,” Logan said in his post-match vlog, showing off the injury. “Little swollen,” he added before consulting WWE’s doctor about the cut across his leg.

“This is getting big,” Logan added, showing off the difference in the bruise after the doctor determined he had a pocket of blood building up. “Oh boy, this is going to get big.”

Timestamp of 12:10

Logan didn’t offer an update on the grizzly injury in the days after it happened, but revealed that the moment had been immortalized in a trading card collection.

As noted, the YouTuber-turned-wrestler has done well to avoid many serious injuries, but his high-risk moveset does leave him open to some big bumps and bruises. 

He isn’t scheduled to get back into the ring just yet, but Logan doesn’t stay away for long.
