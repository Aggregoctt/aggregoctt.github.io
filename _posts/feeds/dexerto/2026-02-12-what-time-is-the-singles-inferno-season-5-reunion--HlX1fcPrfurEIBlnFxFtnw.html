Want to know more about what the Single’s Inferno Season 5 cast are up to now? Then you’ll be pleased to know that a reunion special is on the cards, and it’s landing on Netflix very soon. 

The latest chapter of the hit Netflix dating show was packed full of drama and romance, with five couples forming in the Season 5 finale.

As is the case with past editions of Single’s Inferno, the contestants are keeping their private life, well, private, and so there’s no news on whether any of the couples continued dating after the show. 

However, for the first time, the official Season 5 reunion will be streaming on Netflix, with a new trailer teasing that the “story didn’t end in Inferno.”

The upcoming Single’s Inferno Season 5 reunion special will aptly drop on Valentine’s Day, February 14, 2026. Since it’s airing on Netflix, we can expect it to land at the usual time of 12am PT / 3am ET. 

We’ve listed several timezones below so you know exactly when to tune in: 

  • 5am Brazil
  • 8am UK
  • 9am Central European Time
  • 1:30pm India Standard Time
  • 7pm Australia
  • 9pm New Zealand

Usually, Single’s Inferno shares post-filming interviews with cast members on its various YouTube channels. But the team is shaking it up this year with our first official reunion special. 

How many episodes are there?

Unfortunately, Netflix hasn’t confirmed this just yet, but it’s expected that the reunion will follow the pattern of other dating shows like Love is Blind and consist of just one bumper episode. 

There’s also no news on the runtime, but judging by the trailer, a lot will be going down. 

What to expect in Season 5 reunion

We’re set to see the Single’s Inferno Season 5 cast reacting to a pre-release screening, a reconciliation between Kim Min-ji and Choi Mina Sue, and the entire cast heading back to Paradise for a winter reunion. 

The special is also set to reveal the dating status of the final couples, with a romantic moment showing Lim Su-been telling Park Hee-sun that he wants to continue getting to know her. 

When they head to Paradise with the rest of their peers, they get a moment to themselves, where they’re seen holding hands. 

Another clip shows Jo I-geon and Kim Go-eun expressing regret that they didn’t get to know each other better, suggesting that Go-eun and Woo Sung-min didn’t continue dating after the show.

Of course, all of this is speculation right now – the real answers will be in the reunion. For now, fans are responding to the news with excitement. 

“I’m actually excited to watch! It looks so wholesome,” wrote one on Reddit, while another quipped, “I won’t believe anyone that tells me that Su-been and Hee-sun are not together.”

Will there be a Season 6?

Yes! As well as the upcoming reunion, Single’s Inferno Season 6 is officially going ahead at Netflix.

Producers Kim Jae-won, Kim Jung-hyun, and Park Su-ji said (via The Chosun Daily), “Thanks to the fans who have consistently loved Single’s Inferno, we are able to start a new season. We will listen to our fans’ opinions and strive to make Single’s Inferno even hotter.”

With this news, Single’s Inferno has made history as the longest-running Korean variety show on Netflix. 

There’s plenty of excitement about the sixth chapter, too, with one fan writing, “I hope it’s as entertaining as this season was! There were so many different connections.” Another joked, “We’re still getting over Season 5. I can’t handle this.”

Some fans are even pitching ideas for the next run. “Please bring Sung-bin from Physical: 100,” said one. 

A second suggested “Single Inferno: All Stars” like Love Island’s All Stars, whereby they bring former contestants back to have another go at finding love. Only time will tell if any of these ideas come to fruition.
