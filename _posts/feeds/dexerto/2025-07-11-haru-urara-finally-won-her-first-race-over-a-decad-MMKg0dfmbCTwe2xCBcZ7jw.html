Umamusume’s rendition of the real-life race horse Haru Urara has made the mare go viral all over again, and her trainers opted to enter her into a race for retired horses. At the age of 23, 15 years after her retirement, she finally won.

To say Umamusume’s Western release has made a splash would be an understatement. The biggest streamers on Twitch are all trying it out, memes are everywhere on Twitter, and people are genuinely enjoying the game despite having lukewarm expectations going in.

However, with every character in the game being based on an actual racehorse, its popularity has resulted in the stories of these real horses reaching levels of popularity thought to be previously impossible. In a lot of ways, it’s revived the sport and packaged it in a way modern audiences are eating up.

Haru Urara is one of the biggest real racehorses to gain popularity from this, with her record of never winning a race being made manifest in the actual game. However, that isn’t quite true anymore. Umamusume’s popularity prompted Haru Urara’s owners to enter her in a race made for retired racehorses, and she finally got her win.

Umamusume’s most beloved underdog finally won a real life race

Though the real life Haru Urara was infamous for losing, players have found ways to train her in-game in order to be an absolute beast on the track. Despite her stats being generally poor due to the campaign purposely putting her in races for which she has a low aptitude, players have found ways to make it work.

However, this isn’t a luxury the poor mare had, as she lost so many races that it made her famous. People admired her spirit to compete, despite losing everything.

But, with Umamusume bringing Urara’s story back to popularity, her trainer decided to enter her in a race meant for retired horses. And, on May 18, 2019, she won her first race. The race was almost completely unknown before it was brought to light several years later by some passionate Japanese fans.

So, while this may have been a race that very few people knew about and was purely for fun, the fact that she finally crossed the finish line 15 years after retirement truly embodies the spirit of never giving up – something that players in-game have felt by experiencing her character arc.

What’s more, even back in 2019, Urara’s popularity was surging based on Umamusume. Though the game just came out in the west, it’s been around in Japan since 2018 with a full anime adaptation that included Urara in the supporting cast.
