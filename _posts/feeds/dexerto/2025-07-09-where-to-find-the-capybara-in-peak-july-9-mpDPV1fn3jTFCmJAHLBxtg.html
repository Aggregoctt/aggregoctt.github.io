Looking to grab the coveted Animal Serenading Badge achievement in Peak, or do you just want to meet the adorable Capybaras? Well, you’ll need to find them first.

Peak may have you and your friends climbing up large mountains in cooperative chaos, but there’s so much more to it than just finding the next climbable object. After all, you can uncover Cursed Skulls, Magic Beans, and so much more on your adventure.

One such example is the adorable Capybaras that wander around Peak’s world. If you find them, you can grab an achievement, but they move every day. So, here’s where to find the Capybara in Peak on July 9.

Peak Capybara location: July 9

You’ll find the Capybaras on the bottom left side of the map when facing the Alpine Mountain. Keep an eye out for steam rising from the hot spring; this will indicate their location.

It’s worth noting that while the Capybaras will move around the map, they’ll always be on the Alpine Mountain, so if you’re looking for them, this is the map to explore.

How to get the Animal Serenading Badge achievement

If you’re searching for the Animal Serenading Badge achievement, then all you need to do is head to the above location and play your bugle.

You’ll find a bugle by searching in Chests or Statues. It’s not a guaranteed find, and given that it’s a legendary item, it’s pretty tough to get sometimes. However, if you do manage to find one, head straight to those adorable animals and play music for them.

Just be aware that each bugle does something different, so your Capybara party may be cut short by the Scoutmaster throwing you.
