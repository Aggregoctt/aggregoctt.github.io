Arby’s has launched a new nationwide value meal that bundles a full entree, sides, dessert, and a drink into one box for a single price.

The fast food chain’s new Meat & 3 Value Box is priced at $7.99 and is available starting January 6 at participating locations across the US. The deal includes a choice of sandwich, three sides, and a drink, marking one of Arby’s most expansive value offerings to date.

The Meat & 3 Value Box also introduces a new dessert item to Arby’s menu, the Peach Cobbler Roll.

What comes in Arby’s Meat & 3 Value Box

The Meat & 3 Value Box includes five items total, with customers choosing between two sandwich options.

The full box includes:

  • Choice of Classic Roast Beef or Crispy Chicken Sandwich
  • Small Curly Fries
  • 2-piece Mozzarella Sticks with marinara
  • Peach Cobbler Roll
  • Small drink

Arby’s says the meal is designed to deliver more items than comparable value meals at the same price point.

“At Arby’s, our goal is to always give people more for their money,” said Jeff Baker, Chief Marketing Officer at Arby’s. “The new Meat & 3 Meal gives our guests five delicious items, two more than those other value meals, for just $7.99.”

The Peach Cobbler Roll is a crispy, golden pastry filled with diced peaches, graham cracker, and a bourbon vanilla filling. It is battered in cinnamon and seasoned with nutmeg and lemon juice. The dessert is also available as a 2-piece à la carte option at participating locations.
