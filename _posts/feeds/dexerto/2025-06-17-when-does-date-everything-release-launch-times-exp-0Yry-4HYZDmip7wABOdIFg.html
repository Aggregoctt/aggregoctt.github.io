Launch times explained ---

Date Everything is finally here, bringing all the attractive furniture to your screens, and likely your dreams. However, it doesn’t drop at the same time for everyone.

If you’re looking to romance your door, floor, or walls, or want to hear the sweet sounds of your favorite voice actor as you attempt to romance them in furniture form, then Date Everything is the game for you. We loved it in both our preview and review, and plenty are flocking to try out the wacky adventure.

However, it’s not instantly available on all platforms. Date Everything will release at certain times and on certain days. So, to ensure you don’t miss a second, here are the Date Everything release times and dates.

Date Everything release times in all regions

If you’re looking to play Date Everything on your Xbox or PlayStation, you’re in luck, as this means the game’s already out for you.

However, if you’re diving in on PC, then the game will release on June 17 or 18, depending on your location. It’ll be released at the following times:

  • Toronto: 12 pm ET
  • Los Angeles: 9 am PT
  • New York: 12 pm ET
  • Mexico City: 11 am CT
  • Sao Paulo: 1 pm BRT
  • London: 5 pm BST
  • Helsinki: 6 pm EET
  • Paris: 6 pm CET

June 18

  • Seoul: 1am KST
  • Tokyo: 1am JST
  • Auckland: 5am NZDT

Download file size

Date Everything is 4.67 GB on the Nintendo Switch, and 7.29 GB on PC and console.

As such, while there’s no preloading or preinstalling, it shouldn’t take you too long to install the adventure when it’s out.
