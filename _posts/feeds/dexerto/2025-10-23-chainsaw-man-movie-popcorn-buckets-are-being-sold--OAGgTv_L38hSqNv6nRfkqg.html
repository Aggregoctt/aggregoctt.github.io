Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc is set to drop in US theaters soon, meaning of course there are limited edition popcorn buckets. But you’ll need to be quick if you want to get one – or you’ll have to spend a lot of money.  

Anime has been dominating the box office in 2025. First, Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle broke records in Japan before doing the same internationally, with the film officially becoming the highest-grossing non-English international movie of all time in North America. 

Soon it’ll be the turn of the Chainsaw Man movie. It’s produced by MAPPA and a direct sequel to the debut season of the anime, which itself adapts Tatsuki Fujimoto’s manga of the same name.

According to Box Office Mojo, the Reze Arc movie has already made $68.3 million at the box office, and it’s yet to release in the US. The film has also received a perfect 100% Rotten Tomatoes score from the critics. Amid the hype, some fans are selling limited edition popcorn buckets online. 

Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc popcorn buckets selling for $$$

The limited edition Pochita and Chainsaw Man head popcorn buckets are both being sold on eBay and other online stores for significantly higher than their original prices. 

T-Joy’s Chainsaw Man-themed bucket was made available in Japan when the Reze Arc movie dropped in theaters in September 2025. The limited edition product, which is shaped like a replica of the titular character’s head, cost approximately ¥3,900 (around $26). 

However, over on eBay, they’re selling for around $150 or more – one listing is even flogging it for more than $330, or two for $450.

As for the adorable Pochita bucket, you can find them for slightly less, ranging from around $70-$115, depending on the seller. However, this one’s the most wide-ranging, with one listing at a whopping $511.

This is the collectible you’ll most likely be seeing on your feeds right now. It’s cute as hell, looking just like the fan-fave Devil dog Pochita. But before you go spending your hard-earned cash on touters’ listings, just know that this popcorn bucket is now available in US theaters (at normal prices). 

How to get the Pochita popcorn buckets 

As of October 23, 2025, AMC Theatres and Cinemark are selling the Pochita popcorn bucket, where it will cost you less than $40. 

For instance, at Cinemark, you can bag one for $34.95, which includes the collectible, an XL serving of popcorn, and a free same-day refill. It’s unclear how much the AMC version costs, but you can expect it to be roughly the same price. 

There’s a slight caveat, however: as was the case with the Demon Slayer popcorn buckets, you’re going to have to be quick if you want to get your hands on one. Given they’re now on sale, they’ll be highly sought after, so be sure to check if your local cinema has them in stock. 

As stated by snack food expert Marcus Smith, “Just like the Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle fiasco, you best get there early if you want to claim yours before they’re all sold out. 

“Theaters will begin selling them on Thursday, October 23, but select theaters that have CrunchyRoll Member Exclusive Early Screenings will most likely begin selling theirs even earlier: Wednesday October 22.”
