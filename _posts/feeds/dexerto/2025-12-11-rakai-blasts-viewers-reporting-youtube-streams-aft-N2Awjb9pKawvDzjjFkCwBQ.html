Streaming star RaKai hit out at viewers who had managed to get his YouTube stream taken offline after he started broadcasting over there because of his ban from Twitch. 

On December 4, RaKai was hit with a ban from Twitch for “illegal activities.” Initially, the ban message stated that the 18-year-old was to be banned for two years, but Twitch confirmed that it was only a visual bug. 

The exact moment that landed the streamer in hot water has been up for debate, with viewers pointing to two possible causes from recent streams. One of those included stealing flowers from Target despite claiming that he’d paid for them. That moment became a joke at The Streamer Awards, with host QTCinderella saying, “What’s the coolest thing you’ve ever stolen?” 

RaKai insulted QT during his December 9 stream, which he’d switched to YouTube for. However, the temporary switch to YouTube has come with a number of hurdles. 

RaKai’s YouTube hit with spam reports 

During his December 10 stream, RaKai’s chat became “locked” as he claimed he was being spam-reported by viewers. 

“Stop reporting my stream, please, bro! Please, stop reporting my stream. Please!” he pleaded. 

The Twitch streamer even took to X (formerly Twitter) to shed some light on what was going on. “ITS PEOPLE REPORTING ME AND SAYING THAT IM IM 13 so it’s taking my chat down,” he added. 

He eventually also went live on Instagram, hitting out viewers who had been reporting him. “Please, stop doing that. This shit is not funny at all. I’m trying to stream for my people,” he said. “Right now, I’ve got YouTube reps working on it right now, stop doing that bro. 

“There’s no way I get into your emotions that much. F*ck you. I’ll find out where you live and beat the f*ck out of you. Stop f*cking playing with me, yo. Real s*it.”

There have been claims that RaKai will be banned on Twitch until early January, but it remains to be seen if he’ll be able to stream on YouTube without interruption during that time.
