Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase desperately offered an Arc Raiders player season tickets to defib him after being downed, but to no avail.

Arc Raiders is undoubtedly the game of the moment, after launching in 2025 to critical acclaim and maintaining an impressive player count into the New Year. Its popularity has seen it cross over into more mainstream circles, too, after South Park referenced it in its big Thanksgiving episode.

Even NFL players are catching wind of the extraction shooter, as triple crown winner Ja’Marr Chase has been streaming the game on his Twitch channel.

In a recent fight that went sideways, Chase pulled out all the stops to try and save his precious loot, offering his attacker free tickets to try and persuade them not to finish the job.

Ja’Marr Chase offers Arc Raiders player season ticket to revive him

In a clip that’s been doing the rounds on social media, Chase and his teammate are mercilessly gunned down by a squad of rival raiders. But in Arc Raiders, losing all your health knocks you to the ground, giving the other team the choice of killing you and taking your loot, or using a Defibrillator and calling a truce.

With his loot on the line, the Bengals star tried to sweeten the pot and make the second option more appealing.

“Somebody defib me bro, I’ll give you season ticket holder bro, come on,” he announced to his attackers in voice chat.

His attackers were either not micced up or flat-out not interested, because they quickly moved in for the kill despite his pleas.

“Defib me bro, I can give tickets for the whole season bro, wait!” he shouted while the enemy raider brutally knocked him out and sent him back to the lobby.

Chase took the setback well, though, erupting in a fit of laughter despite losing his loot.

Hopefully, the guns and materials were worth missing out on a Bengals season ticket for. Maybe they were Steelers fans anyway.
