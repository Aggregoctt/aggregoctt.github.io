With Jordan rebelling against Godolkin, Gen V Season 2 is about to kick into a higher gear – and things could get worse for Marie in Episode 4.

The first season of Gen V ended with Marie, Emma, Jordan, and Andre. Now, Andre is dead (the show sweetly pays tribute to Chance Pordomo), and the others are all back at Godolkin under Vought’s new supe-supremacist regime.

A lot happened in the opening three episodes, but here’s the basics: Cate (seemingly) feels bad for everything she did in Season 1, Starlight asked Marie to investigate Project Odessa (which is Marie), and Godolkin has a mysterious new dean, Cipher.

With The Boys Season 5 on the horizon, Gen V Season 2 could set up the franchise’s endgame – so here’s exactly when Episode 4 comes out.

Gen V Season 2 Episode 4 will drop on Prime Video at 12am PT/3am ET on September 24.

This is the standard release time for Amazon shows (much like when Netflix releases films and series), and unlike Alien: Earth, it means people outside the US don’t need to stay up to the early hours or risk stuff getting spoiled when they wake up in the morning.

How many episodes of Gen V Season 2 are there?

There are eight episodes of Gen V Season 2. The first three episodes were released on September 17, and the rest will be released individually and weekly thereafter.

What happened in Gen V Season 2 Episode 3?

Gen V Season 2 Episode 3 ended with Jordan confessing to nearly killing Cate, rather than agreeing to frame ‘Starlighters’ for the incident and use language like “transtastic.”

Before that, it was revealed that Marie wasn’t like other babies injected with Compound V. She was conceived in a clinic… and Cipher delivered her. Marie still wants to find her sister, Annabeth, and Sam is struggling to reconcile his actions in the first season.

In the other opening episodes, Cipher hired Andre’s father Polarity, unaware that he plans to figure out exactly what happened to his son and the truth behind Project Odessa. There are still a lot of questions to answer, so make sure you tune in for Episode 4.
