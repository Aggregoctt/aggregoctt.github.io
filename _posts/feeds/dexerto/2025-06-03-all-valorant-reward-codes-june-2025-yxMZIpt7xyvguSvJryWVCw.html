How to redeem Valorant Codes

There are two ways to redeem codes for Valorant: either through the official online Riot Games website or in-game. Let’s break down how to redeem your codes through both methods:

In-game

If you’d rather redeem your code in-game, follow these steps:

  1. Launch Valorant and log in
  2. Click the VP icon (top right)
  3. Choose “Prepaid Cards and Codes”
  4. Type in your code exactly
  5. Hit “Submit” and enjoy your loot!

Online

If you feel more comfortable redeeming your code through the Riot Games website, follow these steps:

  1. Visit redeem.riotgames.com
  2. Log in to your Riot account.
  3. Enter the code
  4. Hit “Redeem” and enjoy!
