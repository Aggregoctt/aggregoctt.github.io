A GTA 6 YouTuber and TikToker is being blasted after traveling to Rockstar North in Scotland and harassing devs about the long-awaited game.

Gamers across the internet are anxiously waiting for more details on GTA 6 ahead of its scheduled May 26, 2026 release date.

So far, Rockstar has only given fans a couple of trailers, character bios, and some screenshots of VI, with the rest of the information gathered coming from leaks.

With a game as massive as GTA 6, many players have been growing impatient, despite calls from the CEO of Rockstar’s parent company, Take-Two, urging fans that it’ll be “worth waiting for.”

On September 29, amid rumors of a second possible delay, YouTuber ‘backonboulevard’ traveled all the way to Rockstar North in Edinburgh, Scotland with one goal: find out the truth from employees themselves.

YouTuber flies to Rockstar North to demand answers on GTA 6

In a video posted to social media, backonboulevard revealed that he flew all the way to Scotland because he was “sick and tired of waiting for answers.”

Clips showed the creator walking up to supposed devs and asking “when’s the delay” and “where’s the next trailer?”

Although no one in the video responded to the YouTuber, he claims to have received some answers. He claims that trailer 3 and a delay announcement will be coming on October 28th, but nothing has been confirmed just yet.

Backonboulevard’s antics have been blasted by users who defended the devs.

“If you’re waiting outside of somebody’s workplace to harass employees over a video game give your head a wobble,” slammed fellow content creator GameRoll.

“This guy should be arrested for harassment,” another commented.

“Just let them do their jobs, bro,” blasted another.

“Such an unnecessary act and childish behavior,” remarked someone else.

Until GTA 6 releases on May 26, 2026, fans are just going to need to be patient, but it’s easy to understand just why so many are going to such insane lengths for details.
