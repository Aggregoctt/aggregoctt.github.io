Thanks to Dispatch’s more narrative-driven gameplay, we’re met with tons of extremely talented (and familiar) voice actors, set on bringing the heroes, villains, and those in between to life.

Dispatch hasn’t wasted any time in cementing itself as one of the great narrative-driven adventures, despite releasing in episodes and only currently featuring a few romance options. Most of this is down to the stellar story and extremely talented voice actors behind each character.

So, if you’re playing through the game and swear you’ve heard a character’s voice from somewhere else, here’s the full voice cast and where you may know them from.
