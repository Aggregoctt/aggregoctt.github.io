Kai Cenat has responded after Drake took a swipe at him and posted a close-up shot of the Twitch streamer’s forehead on Instagram.

Over the last few years, Kai Cenat has been pretty friendly with a number of different celebrities and musicians. The likes of Kai Cenat, Druski, and Nicki Minaj have made multiple appearances on his stream. 

Kai has also been meant to stream with Drake on occasion, too. The ‘God’s Plan’ rapper had appeared on Kai’s stream via phone call before, and has been a regular member of his Twitch chat, but an on-screen appearance has been lacking. 

The pair have continued to be pretty friendly, even despite Drake taking shots at Kai from time to time. And after the AMP star called his newest ‘ICEMAN’ episode a “waste of time,” Drake took another dig.

Kai Cenat’s hariline posted by Drake

The Canadian rapper took to Instagram and posted a pretty close-up shot of Kai’s forehead amongst a photo dump from his concert in Paris. 

Naturally, it got onto Kai’s radar. “Why would he post this?! Drake! Where you get this from?! The news gotta be slow today,” the Twitch superstar said before taking a moment. 

“I don’t beef with Drake, but why is he posting my s*it like this?! Come on, it’s a tech!” Kai continued, referring to a technical foul in basketball, which would see Drake ejected from a game. 

‘Drizzy’ didn’t post anything else about Kai following that photo, and while some fans found it funny, others have questions for him.

“Is Kai Cenat banned from Toronto?” one asked. “Why is Kai catching a stray?” asked another. “Why you dissing Kai, bro?” quizzed another.
