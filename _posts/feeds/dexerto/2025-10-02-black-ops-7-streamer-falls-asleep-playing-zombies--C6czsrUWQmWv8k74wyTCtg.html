Content creators got the opportunity to play Black Ops 7 early at the CoD Next event, but BigCartiier couldn’t keep his eyes open during the Zombies demo.

In a post that has been doing the rounds on social media, the streamer was testing the new Ashes of the Damned map inspired by BO2’s transit. Everything was going smoothly until he got swarmed by a horde of zombies looking to tear him apart.

For most players, this would be enough to get the heart pounding and adrenaline kicking in. But for BigCartiier, it was a cue to take a quick nap.

In the clip, he can be seen slowly drifting off while the undead hack away at his health until he’s knocked to the ground. Luckily, his teammate is on hand to pick him back up, and he manages to wake himself up to get back into the action before any harm is done.

BigCartiier promises Black Ops 7 didn’t put him to sleep

As you’d expect, many CoD fans responded to the clip, joking that Black Ops 7 had sent him to sleep. However, the streamer replied to clarify that his drifting off was nothing to do with the game and he was just sleep-deprived.

“No i was deadass tired… I feel that zombies could be really fun … MP was amazing imo… and Warzone just needs better maps,” he said in a follow-up. “I gave tons of feedback and criticism but me falling asleep had NOTHING to do with the game being bad… I had 3 hours of sleep if that.”

So, if you’re a Zombies fan worried that this means that this year’s version isn’t fun, you can relax — it wasn’t the game that sent him off into dreamland.
