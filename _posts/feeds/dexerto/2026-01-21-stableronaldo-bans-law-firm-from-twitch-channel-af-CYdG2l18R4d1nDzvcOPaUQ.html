Twitch streamer StableRonaldo banned a law firm from his channel after viewers found them making clips of damage done to the ex-FaZe house. However, he’s not sold on it. 

At the end of December, a number of FaZe Clan’s biggest names took to social media to say that they had all left the organization. Everything under the FaZe umbrella, not including the esports division for Counter-Strike and Call of Duty, disappeared in a flash. 

This also spelled the end for the house that the likes of StableRonaldo and Lacy had been living in, with the creators confirming that they’d be moving out in the near future. Images of the FaZe house have started playing a key role in Ronaldo’s stream, with him regularly using them as a screensaver before he starts and playing them during different moments. 

However, during his January 20, viewers claimed that a local law firm had been keeping an eye on him to monitor any damage done to the house. 

StableRonaldo finds law firm watching his Twitch stream

The account, which bears the name of the Copete Law, P.C. firm, had clipped a moment where the streamer flung something into a wall. This was flagged to him shortly after it happened, with Ron being baffled by things. 

He found that the account had been following him for less than 30 minutes and had not sent a single message in chat. 

“Brother, did you not think my viewers would find you? Is this the guy who comes to the house?” he asked before banning the account from watching or making clips.

“Is this real?” he asked, before making a call to see if anyone else recognized the law firm. 

“I don’t know if I’m getting trolled by my chat. It says clipped by Copete Law Firm, they haven’t typed a single time in my chat, and every clip of them is just damages to the house. Do you think they’re actually watching me?” 

Copete Law, P.C. does return results for a real law firm based in Santa Ana, California, which specialises in real estate litigation. 

“Real estate litigation can be complex and intimidating, especially if you are not a legal expert. In Santa Ana and Downey, navigating these conflicts requires a skilled and knowledgeable real estate attorney who can protect your rights and find a fair resolution,” their website says.

It remains to be seen if the FaZe boys will be hit with a lawsuit or if it was, in fact, Ronaldo’s viewers just having fun.
