David Corenswet’s Superman is coming to save the day in Fortnite, but players who can’t wait to become the Man of Steel can get hold of his skin early.

Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 3 has arrived and it’s once again inspired by superheroes. Characters with unique powers can be found all around the map, and the new Battle Pass is full of suped-up skins to unlock.

The face of the new season is Superman, who features the same suit and likeness of David Corenswet from James Gunn’s upcoming movie. He’s due to arrive later in the update, but eager fans can get him early.

How to unlock Superman early in Fortnite

You can get Superman ahead of time by purchasing the Battle Pass Bundle for Chapter 6 Season 3. This costs 2,800 V-Bucks and contains the regular Battle Pass, as well as the new Man of Steel outfit that unlocks instantly.

To find it, simply head to the store and click ‘Battle Pass’ under the Fortnite Crew and Passes tab. Then, you’ll be given the choice of buying the normal BP for 1,000 V-Bucks or the bundle.

If you don’t want to splash the extra cash, you can pick up the regular Battle Pass and wait for Superman to arrive on July 11, 2025. There is a catch, though, as you’ll have to complete a series of challenges to unlock the skin.

So, if you want to fly around Utopia City as The Last Son of Krypton, but don’t want to grind out challenges, then the Battle Pass Bundle is the option for you. However, you can save yourself some money if you don’t mind waiting a little while.

Unfortunately, there’s no free way to get the Superman skin, as you’ll need to buy one of these two options to have any hope of unlocking it.
