What are Solo Hunters codes used for?

For the most part, Solo Hunters codes reward players with free Gems, which act as the backbone of character progression. Gems are mainly used at the Summoning Powers booth in the central hub, positioned opposite the Merchant, where you roll for Runes that enhance your abilities and increase overall damage. A single summon generally costs 100 Gems, while five pulls cost 450 Gems at a reduced rate.

Gems also power other progression systems, including gacha-style rolls for stronger items and premium class rerolls. Occasionally, codes may directly grant Clan Rerolls or Stat Reset items, giving you more control over your build without spending additional resources.
