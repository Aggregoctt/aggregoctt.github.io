Following the beta, Treyarch have promised major changes to skill-based matchmaking and aim assist when Black Ops 7 launches.

SBMM has been one of the hottest topics in Call of Duty for many years, with plenty of fans believing that it’s too punishing for higher-skill players. In the Black Ops 7 beta, the devs finally listened and implemented a playlist where the feature was drastically reduced, filling lobbies with a greater mix of abilities.

The test was clearly successful, as they’ve now confirmed that this will be the new standard in public matches when the game arrives in November.

Treyarch confirms lower SBMM will be standard in Black Ops 7

“At launch, Open Matchmaking with minimal skill consideration will be the default for Black Ops 7 Multiplayer,” they said in an October 9 blog post. “Simply put, imagine the matchmaking experience of Open Moshpit from the Beta, but as the standard in Black Ops 7 on day one.”

“Our team feels strongly about providing players with a more varied experience, and the Beta proved to be a great opportunity to test this approach. We’ll be sharing more details as we get closer to launch!”

The same post also addressed some other issues that emerged during the beta, the first of which is aim assist, which some players felt was too strong and made it trickier for MNK users. Although they didn’t go into detail, they confirmed that changes are on the way.

“It’s a priority of ours to have a fair and competitive balance across all input devices at launch, and we’ll be sharing more about changes coming to aim assist on controller ahead of launch,” said the blog.

The other big talking point was disbanding lobbies, which means that you’re automatically matched against new opponents in every match. Fans have been calling for the feature to be removed for many years, and Black Ops 7 is doing exactly that.

“We’re focused on keeping players together from match to match more often,” the devs added. “Today, we’re excited to announce we’ll have persistent lobbies at launch for Black Ops 7.”

This will all no doubt be music to the community’s ears, as these are all major changes that have been demanded for some time now. We’ll have to wait until November 14 to see how it all comes together, but players will be delighted to see their feedback being taken on board.
