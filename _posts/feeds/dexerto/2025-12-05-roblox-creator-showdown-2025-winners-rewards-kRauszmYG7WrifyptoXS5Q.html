Roblox’s Creator Showdown is complete, bringing an epic battle between eight streamers with only one team coming out as the winner. Here’s how it went down.

Sure, Roblox might be a fun host of all your favorite games like Blox Fruit, The Forge, Steal a Brainrot, or Uma Racing, but there’s definitely a skill in being a master of multiple adventures on the platform. Well, that’s exactly what the Roblox Creator Showdown aims to highlight.

Not only does it pit four teams of well-known creators together, but it also grants viewers tons of rewards, especially if they guessed the winner correctly. So, here’s all you need to know about the Showdown, and most importantly, who won and what you’ll get for predicting the winners.

What is the Roblox Creator Showdown?

The Roblox Creator Showdown is an event hosted by Roblox that has eight creators competing live for a reward. The event is livestreamed on Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, and Roblox’s The Block so players can enjoy the competition and vote on the games that they want to see the streamers play.

Viewers also get the chance to give the Streamers extra points and predict the winners for special rewards.

How does the tournament work?

The tournament has four teams of two creators who compete in five different Roblox games, earning points as they play or win matches, depending on the game.

After the game, viewers get the chance to vote on the best play, granting the creators an added point.

The last game is a wild card round, which means the creators and the viewers have no idea what game they’ll be playing. This is voted on by the viewers, and all points are doubled for this round.

To win, the team needs to get the highest total points after all five games.

In 2025, the Roblox Creator Showdown took place on Thursday, December 4 at 9:50 pm GMT, it ended on Friday, December 5 at 1:30 am GMT, so 1:50 PST until 5:30 pm PST on December 4 for those in that timezone.
