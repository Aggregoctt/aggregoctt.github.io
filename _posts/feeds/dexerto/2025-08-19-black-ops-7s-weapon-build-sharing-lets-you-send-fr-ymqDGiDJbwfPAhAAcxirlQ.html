Loadouts have become a huge part of Call of Duty, with everyone looking to use the best of the best. Black Ops 7 makes that much easier by introducing loadout sharing for the first time. 

The next Call of Duty, and its November 14 release date, have finally been revealed, with the devs showing off lots of what you can expect across the co-op campaign, multiplayer, and Zombies. 

Alongside introducing updates to progression, Omnimovement, and Perks, it’s also confirmed Weapon Build Sharing. 

If you’ve been in a lobby with your friends, slowly reading out each attachment you use one at a time, this is going to be the feature for you. 

Weapon Build Sharing arrives in Black Ops 7

New with Black Ops 7, Weapon Build Sharing lets you send your loadout attachments directly to a friend, who’ll be able to use them straight away. 

You can also share and find codes online, so you can use your favorite pro player or content creator’s loadout with ease.

This is similar to crosshair codes like you’ll see in Valorant, CS2, and Marvel Rivals, letting you simply put in the code and have the best crosshairs (attachments in this case) ready to go. 

This comes alongside the return of Weapon Prestige, where you can unlock exclusive camos and attachments by reaching the max level with a weapon, then choosing to Prestige it. 

This feature should also be introduced to Warzone once it integrates with Black Ops 7, which we expect to arrive with the Season 1 update, a few weeks after launch. We’ll know more about the battle royale side of things closer to the time.
