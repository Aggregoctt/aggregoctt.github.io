In Elden Ring Nightreign, you’ll have a chance to customize all eight classes with skins.

After all, you’re marching through shadowed halls or standing victorious after slaying a Nightlord, so your look should match your legend. Skins let you personalize your chosen hero and flex your dedication in both fashion and battle. From default garb to endgame exclusives, there’s something for everyone.

This guide will walk you through all the available skins and how to unlock them once you’ve found the Fitting Room.

All skins in Elden Ring Nightreign

Wylder

The first three Wylder skins are available in the Fitting Mirror since the start of the game, while the last two become available after beating the last boss.

  • Default: Available at start
  • Dawn: Can be purchased for 14000 Murk
  • Darkness: Can be purchased for 35000 Murk
  • Abysswalker: Can be purchased for 7500 Murk
  • Lion Knight: Can be purchased for 7500 Murk

How to Get More Murk

Getting new outfits means racking up Murk. Here’s how to do it:

  • Complete Expeditions: Your main source of Murk. The more bosses you beat and nights you survive, the more Murk you earn, up to 1,000+ for tough runs.
  • Sell Relics: Sell your extra relics at the Relic Rites altar in Roundtable Hold. Rare ones bring in more Murk.
