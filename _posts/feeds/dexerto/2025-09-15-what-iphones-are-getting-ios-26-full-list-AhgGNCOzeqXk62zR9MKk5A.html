Apple’s latest iPhone update, iOS 26, features a new “Liquid Glass” design alongside a new naming scheme. Here’s what devices will receive the massive update.

The Cupertino, California-based company revealed iOS 26 back in June 2025 alongside its new naming scheme and a design upgrade.

Available alongside the release of the iPhone Air and the new iPhone 17 devices, Apple’s new OS features its more transparent ‘Liquid Glass’ design upgrade.

But, what iPhones will receive the new iOS 26 update? Here’s everything we know.

When does iOS 26 release?

According to Apple, iOS 26 will fully release on Monday, September 15, 2025. The exact time is unknown, but the company often pushes their updates around 1pm EST.
