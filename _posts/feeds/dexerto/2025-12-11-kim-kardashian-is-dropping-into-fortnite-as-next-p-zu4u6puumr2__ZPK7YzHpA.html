Fortnite has just revealed a collab with superstar Kim Kardashian, detailing her Icon Series skin as well as additional cosmetics arriving in the game soon.

It hasn’t even been two weeks since Chapter 7, Season 1 kicked off, but the collabs have already gotten wild. First, we got a surprise Danny Phantom crossover, and soon after, two new Icon Series skins, Playboi Carti and CouRage, are set to drop imminently.

And now, the next big name joining Fortnite’s huge lineup is Kim Kardashian. Yes, you read that right. 

It’s Kim, the TV star and businesswoman many know from Keeping Up with the Kardashians. Epic has just unveiled the collab, and now we’ve got a real first look.

Fortnite is bringing Kim Kardashian to the game

Epic dropped the teaser for the crossover on December 10, 2025, showcasing Kim with her different roles in real life. It’s not until closer to the end that we see the star playing Fortnite on her phone and even dubbing a Victory Royale.

And by the end of it, her Fortnite skin was revealed. According to well-known leakers, the Kim Kardashian skin is going to include different styles, with multiple colors on her bodysuit. 

It’s currently unknown if the one with her hair down and jacket is also an additional style or a separate skin, but this version will also be available.

As for other cosmetics, the crossover will also include a private jet back glider, a smartphone with a ring light back bling, and a bag pickaxe.

Also, her iconic 2014 Paper Magazine reference will be there too as an emote. For the uninitiated, this is the cover that featured her balancing a champagne glass on her backside while wearing a black dress.

The emote will let you do the exact same as Kim. Instead of regular booze, though, it looks like the shield potion from Fortnite. Considering that Chapter 7, Season 1 is Hollywood-themed, this is a fitting collab.

While the exact pricing is currently unknown, the Kim Kardashian collab is set to drop on December 13, 2025, in the Item Shop.
