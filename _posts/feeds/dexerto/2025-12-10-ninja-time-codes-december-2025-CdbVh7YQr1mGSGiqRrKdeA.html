How to redeem codes

If you’re playing the game for the first time, you’ll first need to pick a village as your starting point as well as a clan before getting the free rewards. After selecting your village, follow the steps mentioned below to redeem a code:

  • In the start menu, select the ‘Spins‘ option.
  • Click on the gift icon in the top-right corner of the screen.
  • Enter the code in the empty box that appears and click on ‘Redeem.’

If you’re already inside the game and not in the starting menu, you can access the codes option by clicking on ‘Menu’ in the top-left corner, followed by selecting ‘Extra Menu.‘ Lastly, click on ‘Codes‘ from the new window that appears and enter the code to claim your reward.

What are Ninja Time codes used for?

In Ninja Time, the build of your character dictates how you perform on the battlefield. Family, Clan, and Element are the three key attributes you need to build around. Each of these can be obtained through the spinning mechanic, which is in turn obtained from codes. Keep in mind that the overall power of these traits depends on their rarity, so the more you spin, the higher your chances of getting a high-rarity trait.

Note: You are guaranteed a Legendary+ roll every 101 Spins.
