Twitch streamer Bisscute was taken to the emergency room after cutting two tendons in her hand during a live Twitch stream when a katana slipped from its sheath and fell toward the floor.

The incident happened while Bisscute was showing her chat several items she planned to use to decorate the back wall of her streaming setup. Among the decorations was a katana in a red sheath, which suddenly dropped while she was holding it.

In a brief moment caught on a now deleted Twitch stream, Bisscute attempted to grab the falling sword by the blade.

In the roughly 20-second clip circulating online, she can be seen reaching for the katana, yelping in pain, and immediately leaving the room while cradling her hand. The stream ended shortly after, leaving viewers unsure of the severity of the injury.

Bisscute later updated fans through her Discord server, confirming she had gone to the ER and suffered serious damage to her fingers.

“Update I cut two tendons and I need operation. Probably next week. Right now I’m waiting on the cast and some blood work. I feel fine so no worries,” she wrote.

She clarified that the injury affected her middle and ring fingers, with doctors determining that surgery would be required to repair the severed tendons. Despite the severity of the injury, she reassured her community that she was stable and in good condition while awaiting further treatment.
