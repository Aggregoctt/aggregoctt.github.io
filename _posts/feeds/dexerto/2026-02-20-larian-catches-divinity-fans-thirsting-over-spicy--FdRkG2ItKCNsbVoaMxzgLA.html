Larian Studios has made a hilarious discovery about what the most-watched moments of the Divinity reveal trailer are – and it’s exactly what you think. 

After the success of Baldur’s Gate 3, Larian revealed their next project, Divinity, at The Game Awards 2025. While the trailer didn’t show off any actual gameplay, it gave fans a good look at this new version of Rivellon, as well as the RPG’s potential plot. 

But it appears that the parts of the trailer that really caught fans’ attention didn’t have anything to do with the world or story. Instead, it turns out the most re-watched parts are the spicy ones. Naturally. 

The most-watched parts of the Divinity trailer are the spicy scenes

Larion’s director of publishing, Michael Douse, shared a post on X, joking that the team had some “very important findings.” This came with an image that appears to be a screenshot of an internal converstion with a fellow dev.

“Never noticed our 2 most replayed moments were the sexy times in the xcal trailer,” it reads.

You can see this for yourself by heading over to the Divinity trailer on The Game Awards YouTube channel. Thanks to the platform’s feature that shows the most viewed parts of the video, you’ll notice a spike around the 3:12 and 3:38 mark, which happens to be when a few of the townfolks are enjoying themselves.
