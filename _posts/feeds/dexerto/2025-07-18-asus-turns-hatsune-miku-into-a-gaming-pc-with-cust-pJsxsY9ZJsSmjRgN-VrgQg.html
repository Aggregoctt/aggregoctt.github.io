PC gamers can now build the ultimate Hatsune Miku-themed rig thanks to Asus ROG’s new gaming lineup, allowing fans to really show off their support for the virtual idol.

Hatsune Miku is more than just a virtual singer – she’s a global icon. Since debuting in 2007, Miku has evolved into a cultural phenomenon, starring in rhythm games, headlining live hologram concerts, and collaborating with brands around the world. 

She’s appeared in titles like Fortnite, Persona 4: Dancing All Night, and the Project DIVA series, and her image has been featured on everything from Magic: The Gathering to McDonald’s collabs. Now, she’s turning her attention to PC gaming.

Hatsune Miku gaming PC revealed

Asus ROG has now revealed Hatsune Miku-themed PC hardware, allowing fans to build a complete setup based around the twin-tailed icon. 

The vibrant collection includes a custom motherboard, graphics card, PC case, AIO cooler, power supply, SSD enclosure, and a high-end monitor, all of which feature artwork of the iconic virtual idol.

Asus has yet to reveal a release date or pricing details, but you can check out the full lineup by visiting the official website.

It’s also important to note that this is Asus’ second major Miku collaboration. Earlier in 2025, the brand released a TUF Gaming peripheral set, which included a headset, keyboard, mouse, and mouse pad, which, as you can guess, were also themed around the global icon.

So, whether you’re a hardcore Vocaloid fan or are looking for a new eye-catching build, the new ROG x Hatsune Miku PC collab will have you dripped out in some luxury Miku merch.
