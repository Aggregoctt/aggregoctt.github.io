Featuring similar gameplay to the classic Vampire Survivors and Risk of Rain, Megabonk is by no means an easy game to succeed in, especially if you’re starting off with a character like CL4NK. However, while tough to begin with, Clank is easily one of the most OP fighters, with the right build.

While it’s been seeing huge success on Twitch and in its player count, Megabonk is far from a forgiving game. In order to survive, you have to choose the right character, perfect your build, and sometimes just get a little lucky.

CL4NK (otherwise known as Clank) is one of Megabonk’s hardest fighters, especially in the early game. However, once you perfect your build and get through the first few minutes, you’ll quickly find one of the most overpowered characters in the game. So, here’s how to perfect that build.

Best Megabonk CL4NK build

The best Megabonk Clank build consists of maximizing his critical hit chance and utilizing his ranged weapons.

He’s got the following passives, weapons, strengths, and weaknesses:

  • Weapon: Revolver
  • Passive: Crit Happens – +1% Crit Chance per level.
  • Strengths: Ranged and great Crit scaling.
  • Weaknesses: Low HP, slow movement.

How to unlock

You can unlock Clank by defeating the final boss in under 6 and a half minutes and then purchasing the Revolver weapon.

This will unlock him as a playable character and let you start working on the build above.

How to play CL4NK

So, once you’ve sorted the best build, you’ll want to consider how to best play him. After all, he’s not particularly fast, and he’s not really that tanky. So, range is a vital feature. Always stay away from the enemy, at all times.

Play it safe early on, and then as you get more powerful, you can rely on your items and tomes to keep you safe.

On top of this, don’t worry about upgrading his crit chance; he already has this with his passive, so crit damage is your best friend here.

Lastly, lifesteal and regeneration are things you should always be looking out for. Sure, later in the game he’s overpowered, but don’t mistake that for strength. If people get near you, it’ll quickly spell game over.
