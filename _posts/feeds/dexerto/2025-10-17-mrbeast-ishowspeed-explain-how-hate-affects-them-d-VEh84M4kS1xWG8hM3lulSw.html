YouTube stars IShowSpeed and MrBeast have revealed how they deal with hate while having the success that they have. 

No matter how successful you are online, there is always going to be someone who disagrees and tries to critique your content. This is especially prevalent the more successful you become, with critics waiting for a downfall. 

As two of the most successful creators around, MrBeast and IShowSpeed were quizzed on this during their appearance at Joy Festival in Saudi Arabia. 

“It got to me, to a point, early in my career,” Speed said about hateful comments. “As for growing on the internet and streaming for the past five years, I’ve gotten better at it. You could do the best thing in the world, and somebody will still nitpick and say anything bad about that.”
