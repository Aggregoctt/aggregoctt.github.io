Clive Barker’s Hellraiser: Revival is set to deliver a brutal single-player experience that fans won’t want to miss. Here’s everything we know so far, including platforms, story, trailer, and editions.

The Hellraiser franchise is finally making its video game debut. Clive Barker’s Hellraiser: Revival is a new single-player survival horror title from Saber Interactive and Boss Team Games, bringing the twisted world of Cenobites to PC and current-gen consoles.

It’s a brand-new story crafted with direct input from Clive Barker himself, designed to drag players into a terrifying realm of pain, puzzles, and punishment.

Doug Bradley also returns as Pinhead for the first time in nearly two decades, so here’s everything we currently know about the grizzly survival horror game.

No official release date has been announced yet for Hellraiser: Revival, but fans can wishlist it now on PC and console store pages.

Platforms

Hellraiser: Revival is confirmed for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC via Steam.

There’s no mention of support for last-gen consoles, which suggests the game is being built specifically for current-gen hardware.

Trailers

On July 22, uploaded the Hellraiser: Revival announcement trailer, giving players over a minute of grizzly footage. Warning: the video isn’t for the faint of heart and includes some Doom-esque takedowns, fleshy enemies, brutal body horror, and of course, plenty of blood.

There’s even full frontal nudity thrown in for good measure, so you may want to reconsider watching the trailer in public.

Gameplay

In Hellraiser: Revival, players harness the power of the Genesis Configuration, a mysterious puzzle box that unlocks dark, supernatural abilities. You’ll use it alongside conventional weapons to survive relentless battles against twisted cultists, grotesque creatures, and demonic forces.

As the story unfolds, expect to confront disturbing choices that blur the line between salvation and damnation, with every decision pulling you deeper into Clive Barker’s world of pain and pleasure.

Hellraiser: Revival story

Hellraiser: Revival is a single-player, story-driven survival horror game. The story follows Aidan, a man who must descend into a nightmarish realm to rescue his girlfriend after opening a cursed artifact known as the Genesis Configuration. Along the way, he faces Cenobites, cultists, and horrors torn from hell itself.

Editions

Three editions of Hellraiser: Revival are available to pre-order right now, with each one including a light-up replica of the Hellraiser puzzle box, and a selection of officially licensed collectibles created exclusively for the game’s release.
