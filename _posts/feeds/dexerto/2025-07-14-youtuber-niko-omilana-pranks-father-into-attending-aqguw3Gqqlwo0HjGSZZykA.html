YouTuber Niko Omilana pranked a father into attending a movie premiere about his own life.

Back in 2023, Omilana met a man named Tudor on the street who only had 300 subscribers and gave him a shout-out, boosting his subs to 40,000.

After this, Tudor revealed he wanted to become a YouTuber because of his son, who wanted one of the platform’s famous ‘Play’ buttons for reaching 100,000 subs.

Tudor’s son is battling bone cancer, and once Tudor hit the milestone, Omilana met up with him to set up an epic prank.

YouTuber pulls off epic prank to celebrate dad’s life

The two came up with a plan involving sneaking into a movie theater for a premiere to surprise him with his own movie.

Amazingly, Omilana had everyone in the theater in on the prank, which was all for the 100,000 subscriber YouTube Play Button award.

Eventually, Tudor sat down to watch the film and broke down in tears when he realized the movie was about him and included a segment where his son asked for a YouTube Play Button, to which he started grinding to reach the goal.

Following the film, Tudor was presented with the Play Button by Omilana himself in an emotional moment that had the YouTuber’s family as overwhelmed as he was.

Since being posted on Omilana’s channel, the video has gone viral, amassing over 3M views. Tudor replied, praising Niko.

“Guys, this video is the most beautiful thing that happened to me in my life, I had the privilege of meeting this super man Niko, who for me and my family is a dream come true for all the things he has done for us, at this time when I write this comment I have the impression that I am dreaming, I want to thank Nikoand all the people who made this video possible,” he said.

“I thank you from the bottom of my heart for subscribing and supporting. With much love and respect.”
