New weapons & Fallout crossover ---

Following Warzone’s integration with Black Ops 7 in December, CoD’s battle royale gets even more content with the Season 1 Reloaded update.

This is the mid-season update, bringing more weapons, Operators, events, and meta-changing buffs and nerfs that’ll keep the game feeling fresh in the run-up to Season 2.

Here’s everything arriving in the Warzone Season 1 Reloaded update, including the start date, new weapons, and everything we know about the Fallout crossover.

Warzone Season 1 Reloaded is expected to begin on Thursday, January 8, 2026. 

The updates usually drop at 9 am PT / 12 pm ET / 5 pm GMT, but we’ll let you know once the time has been confirmed.

New weapons & attachments

Two new weapons arrive in Warzone Season 1 Reloaded:

  • Hawker HX Sniper Rifle
  • Sturmwolf 45 SMG

These weapons will be unlocked through mid-season events, with one likely to be unlockable on the day of the update.

Three more attachments are also still to arrive, unlocked through Season 1 Weekly Challenges:

  • Coda 9 Adaptive Discharge Mod
  • M8A1 Full Auto Conversion
  • MCP-25 Contrabloom Laser

Win streak rewards

Win streak rewards are arriving in Warzone Season 1 Reloaded, letting you earn exclusive weapon camos for winning multiple games in a row.

Redacted anomalies

Anomalies will appear at redacted POIs in the mid-season, but we don’t know much else about this.

Fallout crossover

Amazon’s Fallout TV show is crossing over with Warzone and Black Ops 7 in Season 1 Reloaded. From the teaser, it looks like Lucy (Ella Purnell), the Ghoul (Walton Goggins), and Maximus (Aaron Moten) will arrive as playable Operators.

We’d expect some Fallout cosmetics, like the skins, to arrive as bundles in the store, alongside an in-game event pass with both free and paid items.
