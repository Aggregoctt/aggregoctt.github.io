It: Welcome to Derry expands the world of the two movies directed by Andy Muschietti, with Bill Skarsgard returning as Pennywise the clown. Here’s when you can watch Episode 1.

The HBO series is set in 1962, examines how the creature that calls itself Pennywise has terrorized Derry for decades, and will look at how the clown came to be in the first place. It features actors like Jovan Adepo, Chris Chalk, Taylour Paige, and Dexter star James Remar.

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 1 kicks things off when a new class of kids discover that something is hunting them, and we wrote in our review that it “is definitely worth your time thanks to how it widens the story of the titular town (and clown), alongside the brutal uncompromising violence it inflicts on the entire cast.”

Season 1 sees the kids discover what It is, while also exploring some key moments that were only hinted at in Stephen King’s original novel. But here’s what time you can watch It: Welcome to Derry Episode 1.

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 1 arrives on October 26

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 1 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Sunday, October 26. Audiences not in the US will either have to stay up late to start It: Welcome to Derry, or have to catch up the next day and try to avoid spoilers. There are plenty of twists packed into the season, so horror fans will have to be vigilant!

How many episodes are there in It: Welcome to Derry Season 1 ?

There are eight episodes in Season 1, and it’s going to include some key characters to set up the It movies, including the Hanlon family — who’s future descendant Mike will be in the Loser’s Club.

There’s also Dick Hallorann (Chris Chalk) who horror fans will know best from two other King stories, The Shining and Doctor Sleep.
