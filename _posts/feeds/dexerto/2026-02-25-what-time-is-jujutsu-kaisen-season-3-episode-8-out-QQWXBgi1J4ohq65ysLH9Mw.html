Following a one week hiatus, Jujutsu Kaisen is back, so here’s when and where to watch Episode 8 of Season 3, plus a recap of Episode 7.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 is focussed on the Culling Arc, a story that features strange new characters, and deadly new curses.

The series took a break last week, though chief animation director Hiromi Niwa believes that’s a good thing, writing on social media: “Having a recap episode right when Itadori and the others fully join the Culling Game is helpful, since it lets us review everyone’s goals and feelings.”

But now JJK is back for the run-in to the Season 3 finale, so here’s all Episode 8 details, plus a synopsis of Episode 7 so you’re fully caught up.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 8 drops on Crunchyroll on February 26, 2026, at 9am PT / 12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (February 17)
  • 6am New Zealand (February 17)

The new episode is titled ‘Tokyo No. 1 Colony, Part 2’.

What happened in Episode 7?

Episode 7 featured weird dreams, new rules involving trading points, separation in the Tokyo Colony, and conflicting information about where Higuruma can be found.

In what appears to be a dream, Kenjaku tells Sasaki that her neighbourhood has been chosen for a fight to the death, but says she may leave before the Culling Game starts. Sasaki wakes up and sees a vision of Yuji, whom she knows from the Occult Phenomenon Research Club.

As for Yuji himself, together with Megumi and Panda, he tells Hakari and Kiara their story, and Hakari agrees to help them stop the Culling Game.

A kogane appears with news of Hajime Kashimo’s addition to the rules, which allows them to search for players enough points to trade points and leave the Culling Game.

Hakari and Panda head to Tokyo Colony No. 2 in search of Kashimo and Angel, while Fushiguro and Yuji go to Tokyo Colony No. 1 to look for Higuruma.

The group immediately become separated in the Colony, and Yuji battles powerful sorcerers, then encounters a teenager who claims to know where Higuruma is.

Megumi meets a woman who also says she knows where Higuruma can be found, but their guides give different locations.
