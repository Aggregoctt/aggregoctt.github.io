Hype for Battlefield 6 is starting to reach fever pitch, with people excited to see DICE get back to their roots and release a game that can once again compete with Call of Duty in the military shooter genre.

Feedback from Battlefield Labs has been mostly positive, with people who have had the chance to play an early version of the latest BF title being excited for the final release.

And, while the game’s reveal trailer has been a hit, people who visited Battlefield 6’s official website noticed something strange: The legal disclaimers in the description of each version noted the actual release date.

Not only is Battlefield 6 coming in 2025 according to this leak, it’s only a few months away.

Battlefield 6 coming soon according to EA leak

It’s been clear for a while now that Battlefield 6’s development is pretty far along. Considering they were letting people test an Alpha version that was well-polished, an imminent release date was expected.

And, though previous reporting placed the release window in the 2025 fiscal year (April 2025-April 2026), most people assumed it’d be in the later part of that window, perhaps releasing in early 2026.

However, according to EA’s very own site, it’s coming a lot sooner: October 10, 2025.

While it’s been pulled now, many people screenshotted the info before it got pulled to save the date. It’s related to a pre-order promo offer and, if the end date is October 10, then it’s pretty safe to assume the game will be out by then.

It is, however, important to take this with a grain of salt. Seeing as it wasn’t meant to be posted yet, there’s a chance the release date has been moved back in the time since the page was originally drafted.
