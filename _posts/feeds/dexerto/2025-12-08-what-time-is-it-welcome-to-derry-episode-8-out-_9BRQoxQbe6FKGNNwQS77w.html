It: Welcome to Derry Episode 8 has some big shoes to fill after the devastating seventh episode finally saw the Black Spot fire unfold with some heartbreaking consequences for the kids. So here’s when It: Welcome to Derry Episode 8 is out. Major spoilers ahead!

Episode 7 mainly focused on the racist mob burning down the Black Spot with everyone trapped inside. In a heartbreaking moment, Rich (Arian S. Cartaya) convinces Marge (Matilda Lawler) that there’s enough room for them both to escape the flames in an old Coke fridge, but when she climbs in she realizes he’s lying to her.

He saves her by lying on top of the fridge to keep it shut, and they tell each other they love her in a true tearjerker moment. This is all without mentioning how Pennywise (Bill Skarsgard) munches on Mr. Kersh (Larry Day) before Mrs. Kersh (Madeleine Stowe) confirms that she was the one who tipped off the mob so she could see her father again.

Although Pennywise intends to go to sleep after the Black Spot fire, the military incinerate the meteor chunk which causes him to wake up almost immediately — and we’re left on a cliffhanger where he attacks Will Hanlon (Blake Cameron James) while he’s home alone. So if you’re desperate to find out what happens, here’s what time It: Welcome to Derry Episode 8 is out.

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 8 will start streaming on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Sunday, December 14. 

Horror TV fans that aren’t based in the US will have to stay up quite late (or wait until after work) to watch It: Welcome to Derry Episode 8. For some countries like the UK, it might be easier to squeeze in the final episode of the series in before going to work — because there will be spoilers everywhere about how the season ends.

How many episodes are there in It: Welcome to Derry Season 1?

There are eight episodes in It: Welcome to Derry Season 1, meaning fans only have one more episode left to go until the season wraps up, and we leave the 1965 timeline behind. While HBO hasn’t officially renewed it for Season 2, Muschietti has already revealed his three-season plan for Derry and how the show is going back in time to explore Pennywise further.

“The second season will be in 1935. At the end of Season 1, we are hinting at the reason why we are going to tell the story in two more seasons and backwards,” he explained to Deadline. Audiences also got a glimpse of the 1908 timeline at the start of Episode 7, as the scene shows see Bob Gray performing as Pennywise before It lures him away into the woods.
