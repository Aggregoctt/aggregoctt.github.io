YouTuber Marques Brownlee has given his seal of approval to Apple’s AirPods Pro 3, calling them “so damn good.” 

Apple’s AirPods have gone through a few generations now, with each new release improving on the sound quality and battery life of the previous one.

On September 9, the tech behemoth announced that they’ve got an AirPods Pro 3 in the works. The next evolution in the AirPods is set to have an eight-hour battery life, be able to translate other languages in real-time, improved noise cancelling, and even has a heart rate sensor too.

Marques Brownlee, otherwise known as MKBHD, reviewed the AirPods Pro 3 shortly after the announcement, and he’s given his seal of approval to them.

Marques Brownlee loves Apple’s AirPods Pro 3: Review

The tech YouTuber was impressed by quite a few things, including the new design and comfort of the AirPods Pro 3. 

“I’m not normally an earbuds person, and I’m certainly not typically a wear my headphones all day every day type of person, but with all the improvements they’ve stacked up in this third generation, I get it. I totally get it,” he said. “For the walking around, everyday headphones, these are incredible.

“Overall, better fit, better sound, better ANC, better battery life, all for the same price? I get it. People who wear AirPods all day are going to love these things. People who sometimes wear AirPods are also going to love these things.”

The only knock Marques had for them is that he simply prefers over-the-ear headphones, like the AirPods Max, for his everyday use. 

“If you want one pair of headphones to do everything, or if you’re on the fence, these really don’t have any weaknesses if you’re an iPhone user,” he concluded. “Well played Apple, well played.
