Black Ops 7 is crossing over with the Amazon Prime series Fallout in Season 1 Reloaded, bringing skins and even Deathclaws to CoD, and players can’t get enough of it.

Crossovers were a hot topic going into Black Ops 7, with many fans feeling that previous games went too far with wacky Beavis and Butt-Head skins. The devs assured the community that they want to stick to cosmetics that make sense for the BO series this time around.

With Season 1 Reloaded, the first big collaboration has arrived, with a Fallout event that brings Lucy, Maximus, and The Ghoul skins, as well as a Warzone LTM and even Deathclaws to Zombies.

This isn’t the first time that Bethesda’s iconic RPG series has appeared in CoD, as there was a previous crossover back in MW3 before the Prime show first aired. These skins weren’t exactly well-received by fans, but it appears the wrong has been righted this time around.

CoD players are loving new Fallout crossover

Since the Fallout event was officially revealed ahead of the mid-season update, fans have been raving about the quality of the skins and other cosmetics up for grabs.

“The only bundle I’ll ever buy in BO7,” said one player on Reddit, while another added: “Dope event pass. Ghoul operator and Power Armor Maximus look fire, solid free vault dweller too. Locked in for tomorrow.”

“Is this the best done collab any game has done for Fallout,” wrote a player on X, with another saying: “This blows MW3’s Fallout collab out of the water LMAO.”

Zombies players are also itching to jump in, as they’re getting a new Project RADS LTM that brings Feral Gouls and Deathclaws to Ashes of the Damned and Astra Malorum.

“Okay this is f**king awesome but why does it have to be limited?” asked a fan on Reddit.

“Okay this collab looks really good. I wasn’t expecting a deathclaw mode for Zombies,” added another.
