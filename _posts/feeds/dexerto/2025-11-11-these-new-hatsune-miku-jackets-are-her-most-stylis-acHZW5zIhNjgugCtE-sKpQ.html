Hatsune Miku just got another major style upgrade, giving virtual idols fans the chance to deck themselves out in their very own Miku-themed jackets.

Japan’s virtual pop star has teamed up with Japanese streetwear label Karakuridama for two new reversible sukajan jackets (satin souvenir jackets) that are sure to turn heads.

Announced on Crypton Future Media’s official Piapro Blog, the collab features original artwork from longtime Miku illustrator iXima. The two designs, the Roaring Reversible Sukajan and the Cherry Blossom Reversible Sukajan, showcase some seriously stylish designs.

Miku jackets are the ultimate drip for fans

First up is the Cherry Blossom jacket, which pairs silver satin sleeves with a black body embroidered with Miku holding a guitar beneath a golden crescent moon as cherry blossoms fall along the sleeves.

Meanwhile, the Roaring jacket turns up the volume with teal sleeves, swirling soundwave motifs, and red accents that make it pure main character energy. Both are fully reversible, offering a clean, minimalist look on the inside with Miku’s signature “01” logo.

Each jacket is made from glossy polyester satin with dense, full-thread embroidery across the back and sleeves, giving them a premium finish worthy of the pop princess herself.

Pre-orders began on November 10, with delivery expected by the end of December. If you wish to purchase a jacket, you’ll need to cough up ¥32,870, which is about $215 USD, and availability varies by region, so be sure to check the official store listing for current shipping options.
