Kai Cenat just announced Mafiathon 3 is happening and it’s coming sooner than you might think. Here’s everything we know so far.

Kai Cenat broke out in large part thanks to his first-ever Mafiathon stream. The 2023 marathon broadcast shattered records as the Kai Mafia turned out in droves to support their favorite streamer, making him the most-subscribed personality on Twitch.

At the tail end of 2024, Kai ran it back. After briefly losing the Twitch sub record to VTuber IronMouse, he reclaimed the throne in December that year, ending the 30-day stream with 728,535 subscriptions.

To this day, the record remains his, but he’s continuing the tradition and keeping Mafiathon as an annual event. That’s right, Mafiathon 3 has been announced, and we’ve got the early details here.

Do we know when Mafiathon 3 will start?

There’s currently no exact start date for Mafiathon 3. What we do know for certain, however, is that the next time Kai Cenat goes live on Twitch, it’ll be for the marathon stream.

“The next time you see me stream is the first day of Mafiathon 3,” Kai told viewers on August 18. “See y’all soon.”

This is exactly what happened last year, and it took a few weeks before the event got underway. Stay tuned for updates though as the streaming superstar is sure to announce the official date sooner than later.

What to expect from Mafiathon 3

“I’ve put my everything into this right here,” Kai said after the big announcement.

As always with any Kai Cenat stream, fans are never quite sure what might happen next. One moment could see the streamer grinding through a difficult game, another might turn to a studio tour with Snoop Dogg. Anything is possible.

Expect plenty of fun surprises and hilarious moments from start to finish, though, as Kai’s team no doubt has tons prepared to keep fans entertained.

It’s safe to expect a few weeks of non-stop entertainment, too, as both previous Mafiathons have run for 30 days each.

Who will appear throughout Mafiathon 3?

The usuals like Druski and Fanum are more than likely to appear at one point or another as Kai Cenat streams again for weeks on end. But as for other celebrity appearances, nothing has yet been teased.

From basketball legends to the world’s most popular musicians, nothing is off the table. Expect to see some cultural icons throughout the run once again, as has become expected from the huge Mafiathon events.

Rest assured, we’ll update you here with any further details as we get closer to the stream.
