The DM-10 dominated Warzone Season 4 Reloaded but was nerfed in a small update on July 8, allowing another fast-killing Marksman Rifle to take over the meta.

The TR2 is actually the fastest-killing gun in the game. Despite being semi-automatic, it will kill faster than any other meta weapon beyond 50 meters. It’s no slouch at close range either, killing faster than the Grau, Cypher 091, and AMES 85 at all ranges.

Although it had a faster fire rate and was easier to use than the DM-10, it lost out simply because it didn’t quite have as much raw power. That changed on July 8, with the DM-10 no longer being the powerhouse it was, allowing the TR2 to enter the meta.

On top of its fantastic long-range TTK, it has low recoil, high bullet velocity, and a fast reload, so if you found success with the DM-10 or any other semi-automatic meta, it should absolutely be your go-to in Season 4 Reloaded.

FaZe Swagg highlighted the TR2 in a July 9 video, calling it “100% the best gun in Warzone right now.” In a Top 250 Ranked Resurgence lobby, Swagg used it alongside the LC10 SMG, dropping 15 kills and winning the match.

If you’d prefer to use an automatic weapon in Season 4 Reloaded, then it’s hard to go wrong with the GPR 91 and Grau conversion for the SWAT 5.56, as these Assault Rifles are both extremely dominant and easier to use.
