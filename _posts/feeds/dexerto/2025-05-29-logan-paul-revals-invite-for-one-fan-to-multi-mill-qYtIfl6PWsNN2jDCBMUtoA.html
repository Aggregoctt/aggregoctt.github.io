Logan Paul has revealed that he’ll be inviting one of his fans to his wedding with Nina Adgal, and it’s actually pretty simple to get involved. 

Over the years, YouTubers have used their relationships and, ultimately, weddings for content and Logan Paul hasn’t been shy about showing off his relationship with Nina Adgal. 

However, things got nasty during the build-up to his fight with Dillon Danis. The former UFC fighter took countless swipes at Adgal’s previous relationship and threatened to drop “bombshell” content. Logan addressed that during his Paul American series, calling it “traumatic” for him and his fiancee.

The pair, however, have since had a child together and are gearing up for a lavish wedding. A wedding that one fan will get an invite to – and they can even bring a plus one. Seriously.

Logan Paul will invite one fan to multi-million dollar wedding

Logan and Nina put the idea forward during Logan’s May 28 vlog as the pair talked abut spending multi-millions of dollars on the ceremony. “It’s affecting my net worth, but my god, it is going to be incredible,” Logan said. 

“I think we should invite one lucky viewer to our wedding, I’m not kidding. Someone who watches this video and comments,” he added. “Our favorite comment,” Nina added. 

“It can be anything, like literally anything, and then we’re going to invite one of you guys to our wedding,” Logan concluded.

Viewers have already started laying out their pitches for why they should get the invite. Many of them have leaned on how dedicated of a fan they’ve been to Logan over the years.

It wouldn’t be the first time that the Pauls have used a wedding for content. Logan’s brother Jake and Tana Mongeau got ‘married’ back in 2019, selling a live stream of the ceremony to viewers for $50.
