Silent Hill f star Konatsu Kato has stepped into the fashion spotlight, headlining a new limited-edition PlayStation clothing collaboration with Japanese streetwear label WIND AND SEA.

The collection blends gaming iconography with street-ready design, featuring hoodies, jerseys, pants, and outerwear built around PlayStation’s iconic buttons and logo. Unlike the 30th anniversary-themed apparel, the latest drip has gone for a more futuristic look with Kato at the helm of the latest collab.

While the WIND AND SEA x PlayStation collab doesn’t provide any scares (we hope, as we don’t know the prices yet!), it neatly caps off Kato’s breakout year, taking her from surviving Hinako’s nightmare to modelling official PlayStation gear.

New PlayStation drip modelled by Silent Hill f actress

It’s been a busy year for 26-year-old actress, as she famously played Silent Hill f’s protagonist Hinako Shimizu, a role she previously said was so intense it left her feeling like her sanity was slipping. Despite the demanding nature of the job, Kato’s performance earned her Best Performance at the Horror Game Awards 2025.

She later rented and bought a PS5 to relive her nightmares as Hinako, with her Silent Hill f livestreams going viral thanks to her comical reactions. Many fans also affectionately dubbed the game’s newly released difficulty mode “Kato mode“, with many joking that the easier difficulty was specifically released so that irl Hinako could beat Silent Hill f.

The fashion drop is a big moment for Kato and lands amid PlayStation’s wider push into clothing and footwear, including its limited-edition sneaker collaboration with Reebok for the brand’s 30th anniversary.
