LEGO has officially announced two new Disney sets centered on Winnie the Pooh and Piglet, set to arrive in March.

The releases expand LEGO’s Disney lineup, with a larger display focused on Pooh himself and a smaller build dedicated to Piglet. Both sets feature opening elements and interior scenes inspired by the Hundred Acre Wood.

The announcement was shared on January 29, 2026, and includes pricing, piece counts, and the core play features included in each build.

Winnie the Pooh and Piglet sets detailed

The main set, Winnie the Pooh, includes 1,399 pieces and carries a $149.99 price tag. The model centers on Pooh with his signature Hunny Pot, which opens to reveal small interior rooms inside the build.

Piglet’s Birthday Fun is positioned as a smaller standalone option, featuring 544 pieces and priced at $39.99. The set includes a brick-built Piglet figure, a birthday cake build, and multiple interior scenes concealed inside the cake, including Pooh’s house and the Hundred Acre Woods.

Both sets are scheduled to launch in March as part of LEGO’s Disney range, with the two builds revealed together for the upcoming release window.
