Here are details of when and where to watch Episode 7 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3, as well as a refresher on what happened in Episode 6.

Season 3 of Jujutsu Kaisen is providing fans with an animated adaptation of the Culling Arc, featuring the introduction of both new characters and new curses.

The season was initially packaged as a movie called Jujutsu Kaisen: Execution, which combined Episodes 1 and 2, and released in cinemas.

Now, fans of Greg Akutami’s manga are seeing what happens next, with Episodes 1-6 already available, and Episode 7 dropping this week, in advance of Episode 8, which is being delayed so the show can take a week off.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 7 drops on Crunchyroll on February 12, 2026, at 9am PT / 12pm ET.

For other international territories, you can tune in at the following times:

  • 2pm Brazil
  • 5pm UK
  • 6pm Central European Time
  • 10.30pm India Standard Time
  • 4am Australia (February 13)
  • 6am New Zealand (February 13)

The new episode is titled ‘Tokyo No. 1 Colony, Part 1’.

What happened in Episode 6?

Episode 6 of Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 sees Megumi and Panda infiltrate the fight club, and Hakari and Yuji continue their conflict before agreeing to work together. It ends with a new Culling Game rule.

Following the introduction of the JJK Fight Club, Megumi and Panda successfully infiltrate the society’s compound, where Kiara alerts Hakari of their presence.

They argue and debate with Kiara, who refuses to believe anything they say, most notably their claims that Gojo has been sealed.

After more back-and-forth – and following the summoning of Megumi’s rabbit shikigami – they realize that Kiara’s technique is based on the Southern Cross constellation. Megumi uses his Ten Shadows to defeat Kiara, and they finally get their point across.

Hakari and Yuji burst forth mid-fight, with Hakari hitting Yuji, and Yuji letting him do so. Kiara explains that they are simply there to ask for help, but Hakari claims he needs to see more fight from Yuji.

Yuji refuses to punch back, claiming to be a “cog” in the machine, existing to exorcise curses, and willing to do anything to achieve that end.

Hakari eventually agrees to work with them, just as a Kogane appears before the group with news of a new Culling Game rule that allows sorcerers to view the status of each other.

The player who created that rule then claims it was better in the old days, complains about not being able to find strong opponents, and expresses a desire to fight Sukuna.
