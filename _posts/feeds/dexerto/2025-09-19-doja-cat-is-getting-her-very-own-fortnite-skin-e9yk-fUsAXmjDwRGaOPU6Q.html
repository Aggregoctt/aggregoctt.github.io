Doja Cat has just opened up about her getting a skin in Fortnite and here’s everything you need to know about it.

Fortnite boasts a massive list of collaborations, not just with popular video game franchises or movies. Throughout the years, they’ve also brought real people into the game, including streamers, athletes, celebrities, and many others.

So far, Chapter 6 has been absolutely stacked with some of the most familiar faces. After all, we’ve seen skins for Clix, Sabrina Carpenter, and Kai Cenat. And clearly, Epic isn’t stopping there. 

Doja Cat, already known in Fortnite for her emotes based on her songs, has now revealed that she’ll be getting her very own skin.

Doja Cat is set to get her own Fortnite skin

The info that she’s getting her own skin was mentioned when she was live on Instagram. During the stream, she admitted that she was “excited” because she loves Fortnite. 

The celebrity then dropped the big news about getting her own skin, saying that she’s “one of the few to get a skin who actually plays it.”

She seems to be nodding to the stars already in the Icon Series. Most of them may not actually play Fortnite, but Doja Cat does.

For the uninitiated, Doja Cat is also a familiar face in the game’s community. Not only does she play the game, but players also know her for “keeping it real,” sharing the same frustrations as others in terms of certain weapons that were available in the loot pool.

At the time of writing, it’s currently unknown when her skin will be released. Though so far, several leakers have said that it’s “coming soon.”​

Usually, Epic tends to drop hints or tease Icon Series skins before they release. Once these have been dropped, rest assured, it’s only a matter of time. Until then, fans will just have to be patient. We’ll be sure to keep this section updated once there’s more information.

Cosmetics & prices

Looking at previous Icon Series skins, it’s likely that she’ll be getting her own bundle, including a back bling, pickaxe, emote, and other additional cosmetics to complement the skin. Chances are, the skin may have additional styles as well.

It’s speculated that the bundle may cost around 2,800 V-Bucks, since this is quite standard for Icon skins. Though, do keep this information with a grain of salt for now until there’s confirmation from Epic.
