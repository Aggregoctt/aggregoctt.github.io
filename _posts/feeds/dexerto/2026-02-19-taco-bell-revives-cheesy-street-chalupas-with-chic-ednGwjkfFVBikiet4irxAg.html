Taco Bell has added a new twist to one of its most recognizable flavor combos with the launch of Chicken Bacon Ranch Cheesy Street Chalupas, available for a limited time starting February 19.

The new menu item marks the return of Cheesy Street Chalupas, now featuring slow-roasted chicken, crispy bacon, and avocado ranch wrapped in toasted cheddar shells. Taco Bell is also pairing the launch with a matching Chicken Bacon Ranch Nacho Fries option, expanding the rollout across two formats.

Taco Bell says the launch continues its Street Chalupa line, which has returned in multiple flavor versions since its debut. The brand described the release as another iteration of its street-style format built around familiar flavor combinations.

Chicken bacon ranch joins Taco Bell’s street-style lineup

The Chicken Bacon Ranch Street Chalupas come as a two-pack and include slow-roasted chicken, bacon, pico de gallo, shredded cheddar cheese, and avocado ranch sauce inside toasted cheddar Street Chalupa shells.

Alongside the chalupas, Taco Bell is also offering Chicken Bacon Ranch Nacho Fries, which feature fries topped with chicken, bacon, nacho cheese sauce, avocado ranch, and pico de gallo.

The new menu items are available individually or as part of the $9 Discovery Luxe Box, which includes a pair of Chicken Bacon Ranch Street Chalupas, a Cheesy Bean and Rice Burrito, chips with nacho cheese sauce, and a medium fountain drink.

New Taco Bell Rewards members can also get a free Chicken Bacon Ranch Street Chalupa after signing up between February 19 and March 18. And, a separate Grubhub promotion offers discounts on qualifying orders that include Chicken Bacon Ranch Nacho Fries.
