Manni Perez, the rumored actress behind Lucia in Grand Theft Auto 6, has disappeared from the internet, but it might not be leading to a new trailer.

For years, Grand Theft Auto fans have been trying to find out who is playing the main characters in GTA 6. There have been a few nar misses with regards to Jason, but Lucia’s actress appears to have been nailed down.

Manni L Perez was highlighted as the actress behind the GTA 6 protagonist pretty early on, and she has spoken about working on big projects that she can’t name. 

Fans have, naturally, swarmed Perez’s social channels, looking for any hints about the highly anticipated game. However, amid hopes of more news from Rockstar, she has dropped off the internet. 

Manni Perez deletes Instagram account

GTA fans noticed that Perez’s Instagram disappeared on July 11 and quickly questioned if that was to do with upcoming news from Rockstar or “harassment” from fans. 

However, X user CYBER BOI – who has kept on top of GTA 6 news – noted that her account posted “scammy stories” before it was deleted.

“1. Load of scammy stories went up first, so she probs got hacked. 2. If she did want her privacy, it’s not a big deal. It’s perfectly normal to get bored of social media, she has no obligation to have social media,” he posted. 

As noted, fans have been hoping for some updates on GTA 6, and Perez’s silence has led to some speculation that something is coming. Though, that’s probably a bit more wishful thinking than anything else, especially if she’s just protecting herself from scams.

The actress has grown a massive following since being linked to the Grand Theft Auto series. This is always the case, and will likely spike again when things are confirmed.
