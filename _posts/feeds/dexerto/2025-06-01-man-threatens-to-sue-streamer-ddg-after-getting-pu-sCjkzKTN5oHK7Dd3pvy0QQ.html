Footage of a man getting hit by DDG’s brother and others after shouting “doo doo garbage” at him has gone viral on social media. In the aftermath of the brawl, he’s threatening to sue DDG and his crew.

Twitch streamer and rapper DDG has been in hot water as of late due to an ongoing controversy related to Halle Bailey, with her claiming he physically abused her.

These allegations have resulted in the streamer coming under fire across social media, with him being dubbed “doo doo garbage” by those who are angry with him.

However, when someone who goes by Lil Jay called him out in-person and used the name, he was immediately swarmed by DDG’s crew. He’s threatening to take legal action as a result.

Lil Jay threatens legal action against DDG’s crew

The altercation took place at Dreamcon, with Lil Jay calling DDG “doo doo garbage” directly to his face multiple times before things escalated.

It resulted in a bare-knuckle brawl, one that saw Jay get thrown to the floor by DuB, DDG’s brother, before event security came in and broke things up.

Jay was subsequently booted from Dreamcon and made a TikTok in response. He plans to threaten legal action against DDG, his brother, and his crew.

“I gotta take legal action against him, his brother, somebody out here. That’s unacceptable for real, they broke my chain. I’m banned from the event,” he claimed.

Jay said he though that doo doo garbage was “all jokes” and that he wouldn’t take nearly as much offense to it as he did. He feels as if their actions were unwarranted and claims he was assaulted.

DDG, meanwhile, claimed that the last person who called him doo doo garbage “lost some Mario coins” after insulting him, discouraging others from walking up to him and saying the phrase.

DuB has also responded, saying this: “You cannot sue nobody for trying to fight me. You’s a b*tch. You tried to fight me and you’s a b*tch and it didn’t go the way that your brain thought it was gonna go.”

He claims that he’d “tear his ass up in court” if Lil Jay tried to take legal action.
