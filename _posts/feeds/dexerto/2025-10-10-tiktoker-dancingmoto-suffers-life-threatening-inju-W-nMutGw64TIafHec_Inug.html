Instagram and TikTok influencer Dancing Moto has been left with “extensive, life-threatening injuries” after a crash with the Georgia State Patrol. 

On October 9, clips of Dancing Moto being chased by the Georgia State Patrol went viral on social media. One of the videos, an almost minute-long clip, showed the Instagram influencer being chased by police while riding a motorcycle. 

The chase occurred as Dancing Moto allegedly fled a speeding spot on the I-85 interstate outside of Atlanta. After a short while, the Georgia State Patrol ultimately hit the motorcyclist, sending him tumbling off his motorbike and onto the ground.

While down there, one of the police cars drove over him, pinning him down. Dancing Moto yelled in pain as the car stayed on top of him. Naturally, a number of social media users were concerned for his welfare.

DancingMoto addresses viral crash video

The clip, according to his own Instagram account, had come from an incident that happened a few days earlier, with the influencer being left in hospital by it.

“Our brother Dancingmoto was involved in a devastating motorcycle accident a few days ago, one that left him in critical condition after being struck and ran over, which caused severe injuries. He’s now fighting for his life in what can only be described as his darkest hour,” someone posted from his account. 

“The crash left him with extensive, life-threatening injuries, and his condition remains uncertain as doctors work around the clock to help him pull through. Those who know DancingMoto know the kind of man he is, strong, passionate, and full of life. He’s the one who always shows up for others, bringing light, laughter, and heart wherever he goes.”

A number of photos of Dancing Moto in hospital were used in the post, but his identity has, to this point, remained hidden. Emojis have been used to cover us his face, but there are visible marks on his arms and neck.

Comments on the Instagram post, which has also been used to launch a GoFundMe have been very split, however.
