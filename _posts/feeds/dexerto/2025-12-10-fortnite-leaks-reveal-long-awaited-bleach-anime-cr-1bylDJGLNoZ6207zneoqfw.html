Leaks have revealed yet another long-requested crossover coming to Fortnite, and this time it’s classic anime Bleach coming to the island.

Crossovers and collaborations have become a major part of what makes Fortnite special, and seemingly nothing is off the table when it comes to skins. In the last 12 months alone, we’ve seen horror Icons like Ghost Face join the roster, and even internet memes like Skibidi Toilet.

But some of the most popular releases over the years have been the anime collabs, from Attack on Titan and Naruto to Jujutsu Kaisen and Dragon Ball. Now, it appears that Epic Games are going back to the well, as leaks have revealed that a Bleach crossover is in the works.

When is Bleach coming to Fortnite?

The Bleach crossover is set to arrive in “early 2026” during Chapter 7 Season 1, according to a leak from trusted dataminer ‘SamLeakss’ that was shared by ‘Hypex’ and Shiina.’ Although they specify a date, Season 1 is scheduled to end on March 4, 2026, so it will likely arrive in January or February.

Details are pretty scarce right now on what will be included, but they did confirm that the song Number One – Bankai will arrive as a Jam Track.

There will also be “at least one skin,” but it’s not entirely clear who will be given the full Fortnite treatment.

Based on previous anime crossovers, it’s safe to assume that the protagonist Ichigo Kurosaki will be included. However, there are tons of characters that fans would love to see, so it remains to be seen if fellow Soul Reapers Rukia Kuchiki or Renji Abarai will also appear.

Of course, this is all based on leaks now and nothing has been officially confirmed, so we’ll be sure to update this page with more details when Epic Games makes an announcement.
