Fortnite will be adding famous Cartoon Dads as skins, including Hank Hill, Bob Belcher, and Cleveland Brown as part of a collab with Fox.

Fortnite’s crossovers have truly come a long way when compared to the game’s early days. This year alone, we’ve gotten new skins from Adventure Time, Mortal Kombat, and even well-known artist Sabrina Carpenter.

Of course, Epic isn’t stopping anytime soon when it comes to bringing more characters from other franchises to the game’s Battle Royale island.

As a way to celebrate Father’s Day, the game is set to collaborate with Fox by bringing three famous dad skins from the most popular animated shows from the network. Soon, you’ll be able to play as Hank Hill from King of the Hill, Bob Belcher from Bob’s Burgers, and Cleveland Brown from Family Guy.

Based on information shared by leakers, these three skins will be released on June 13, 2025, at 8 PM ET.

Epic has previously teased this collab by sharing a picture of Hank Hill’s shirt and jeans up close, writing, “Did we get it right?”. With that in mind, there’ll likely be another teaser or trailer soon, given that they’ve already confirmed this crossover.

Hank Hill, Bob Belcher, Cleveland Brown bundle prices & details

Much like other collabs, all these characters will come with their own set of additional cosmetics, such as back blings, pickaxes, and emotes.

Though they’re not available in the Item Shop yet as of now, leaks have already revealed the pricing of these skins, which are listed below:

Bob Belcher

  • Outfit: Bob Belcher (1,500 VB)
  • Pickaxe: Bob’s Spatula (500 VB)
  • Backbling: Flying Burger (400 VB)
  • Emote: Bob’s Happy Dance (400 VB)
  • Emote: Burger of the Day (400VB)
  • Bundle: (3,200 V-Bucks)

Cleveland Brown

  • Outfit: Cleveland Brown (1,500 VB)
  • Backbling: Cleveland’s Mailbag (200 VB)
  • Pickaxe: Mailbox (800 VB)
  • Emote: Mailman’s Stroll (400 VB)
  • Bundle: (2,900 V-Bucks)

Hank Hill Bundle

  • Outfit: Hank Hill (1,500 VB)
  • Pickaxe: Sirloin’d Tongs (800 VB)
  • Backbling: Strickland Propane Tank (200 VB)
  • Emote: Alley Hangout (500 VB)
  • Wrap: Gas Gauge (500 VB)
  • Bundle: (3,500 V-Bucks)

That’s everything we know so far about the upcoming collab with King of the Hill, Bob’s Burgers, as well as Family Guy. You’ll be able to purchase these skins separately. However, if you’re looking into collecting all their cosmetics, you can also opt to get the whole bundle.
