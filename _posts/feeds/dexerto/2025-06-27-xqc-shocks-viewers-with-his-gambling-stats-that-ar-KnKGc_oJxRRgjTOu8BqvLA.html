Streaming star Felix ‘xQc’ Lengyel has revealed an update to his all-time gambling statistics, and the money he’s wagered is now in the billions. 

Gambling while on stream has long been a controversial topic between streamers. Some are totally against it and were pleased when Twitch decided to ban the streaming of slots and casino games back in 2022.

Others, however, are all for it and have set up shop on other sites, like Kick, that do allow gambling streams. xQc falls into that second category, and while he has promised to quit the gambling broadcasts in the past, they still make up part of his content. 

The Canadian did take a bit of a break from the casino games. Yet, they’ve returned in late June during the Kick position of his streams. 

xQc reveals Stake wagers are over $3 billion

Lengyel was back gambling during his June 26 stream and decided to show off some of the statistics behind his wagers. “Jesus Christ, man,” the former Overwatch star said as they popped up on screen. 

According to the stats, xQc has placed over 1.3 million bets with his money wagered over $3.5 billion. Billion, with a b. The number of lost bets? 1.2 million, while he’s only hit on around 128,000 wagers. 

Those are his all-time stats since opening that specific account in early 2021, so almost five years’ worth of gambling. However, it doesn’t show the amount of money won and lost over that time.

The streamer touched on the actual losses back in November, telling Logan Paul and Mike Maljak that he believed it was over $100 million.

“Okay, the loss is over $100 million, right. I’m a diligent gambler, it’s gambling so the pays are really high, okay? But I’m dumb, okay, so yeah, I gamble a lot,” he said.
