Peacemaker is back with a Season 2 that dips into the multiverse, acts as a spiritual sequel to James Gunn’s Suicide Squad, and once again features a hard rocking soundtrack.

Christopher Smith returns to TV via a Peacemaker Season 2 that “tells a touching tale which lacks the potentially world-ending stakes of its predecessor, but makes up for that with an emotionally charged story that acts as the DCU’s answer to Sliding Doors.”

That story is spun off from multiple sources, meaning these are the movies and shows to watch in advance of Season 2, including this summer’s Superman.

While much like Season 1, it’s filled with rock music from the past, so the following is a guide to every tune that plays on the soundtrack, starting with Episode 1 (which James Gunn revealed early here).

Every song on the Peacemaker soundtrack

First up, it’s the all-important opening credits song. Peacemaker Season 1 kicked off with the cast dancing to Wig Wam’s ‘Do You Wanna Taste It.’

Season 2 features a similar dance sequence, but the song is different, with John Cena and co busting a move to the somewhat less memorable ‘Oh Lord’ by Foxy Shazam.

Here’s every other song that appears in Season 2, including details of the episode, song title, and artist.

Episode 1: ‘The Ties That Bind’

  • ‘Don’t You Ever Leave Me’ by Hanoi Rocks
  • ‘Guestlist’ by Hardcore Superstar
  • ‘Three Way Ride’ by Wildstreet
  • ‘Dreamer’ by Foxy Shazam
  • ‘Blue Jeans’ by Silvertide
  • ‘Outrage’ by Sister Sin
