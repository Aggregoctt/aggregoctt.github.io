YouTube star Airrack ended up being trespassed from Universal Studios after bamboozling thousands of fans into believing that Cristiano Ronaldo was there. However, it was just him in a mask. 

Over the past few years, Airrack has become one of YouTube’s biggest pranksters. The YouTuber, who has over 17 million subscribers on the platform, has regularly made videos with massive creators by hiding in their houses, pretending to arrest them, and sneaking into public spots.

Some of the videos have, however, landed him in hot water. There was plenty of backlash around his fake swatting prank on Twitch stars Maya and FaZe Adapt, especially as it’s such a controversial topic. 

In his latest video, however, he ended up being kicked out of Universal Studios for pretending to be Cristiano Ronaldo. 

Airrack kicked out of Mall dressed as Cristiano Ronaldo

The YouTube star donned a mask of Ronaldo’s face as he entered Universal’s CityWalk, and was quickly spotted by fans as he headed into Ben and Jerry’s. From there, things only escalated. 

Universal’s own security team were called into action as the crowd became “insane,” giving Airrack an escort.  After taking refuge from the crowd in a Starbucks, he was asked to leave by security, but ignored them for a little longer. 

“With security threatening to call the cops, we were forced to leave,” he said, telling a friend that they were trespassed from Universal over the stunt. “It unfortunately worked so well that we never made it into the park,” Airrack added.

The YouTuber also bamboozled a whole host of people at a mall, but did have some people question if it was him, with kids calling out his name as the fake Ronaldo exited a store.

“I heard its Airrack,” one passerby shouted. “You don’t know who Airrack is? Look him up on YouTube.”

Airrack has also planned a second attempt at things, this time posing as Lionel Messi. So who knows what’ll happen.
