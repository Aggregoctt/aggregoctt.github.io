Genshin Impact has just announced its collab with Cold Stone Creamery, which will feature exclusive merch and special flavors.

As one of the most popular gacha titles out there, Genshin Impact hasn’t been shying away from collaborating with different brands to promote its game and characters.

Just last month, the game teamed up with KFC to offer fans pint-sized figures with combo meal orders. At one point, it also collaborated with Pizza Hut, offering redeemable codes with certain menu items.

And it doesn’t stop there: ice cream chain Cold Stone Creamery is next, as announced on Genshin’s official social media.

Genshin Impact set to launch limited merch from Cold Stone collab

The Genshin Impact x Cold Stone Creamery collaboration will run from September 19 to October 19, 2025, bringing two special flavors: Strawberry Cheesecake and Blueberry Yogurt.

During this limited-time period, fans can grab “exclusive freebies” featuring Charlotte and Clorinde by purchasing special collaboration sets at Cold Stone. 

Merchandise from this collab includes reusable cups, sleeves, bags, stickers, photo cards, and scratch cards.

Overall, the promo of this collab comes in three set tiers. The Chill Set is the cheapest one, priced at the equivalent of about $5. This lets you snag two servings of the collaboration flavors along with extras like stickers, a cup & sleeve, and scratch cards.

Meanwhile, the most expensive tier requires spending above a certain amount to bring home the full lineup of merchandise.

Additionally, as seen in the official post, some of the merch variants will be given randomly, depending on stock at each store.

There’s a catch, however, as currently, this collab will only be available in Indonesia, and it’s unclear whether or not it will make it to the West. We’ll be sure to update you if this changes.
