K-Pop Demon Hunters is breaking records all over the place, and now the movie is selling out cinemas through a series of special singalong screenings.

K-Pop Demon Hunters hit Netflix on June 20, 2025, and has dominated cultural conversations ever since.

Songs from the animated musical fantasy are topping the charts, it quickly became the most watched animated movie in Netflix history, and Demon Hunters is now the streamer’s second most watched movie of all-time.

The celebrate the film’s success, Alamo Drafthouse has laid on a series of singalong screenings and parties in August, though you’ll need to be fast if you want to attend, as there are already reports of screenings selling out.

How to get tickets for the K-Pop Demon Hunters singalongs

On August 23 and August 24, the Alamo Drafthouse chain will host K-Pop Demon Hunters singalongs in 23 locations all over the US, with lyrics onscreen so you can join in with with Mira, Rumi, and Zoey.

Tickets are available at the Drafthouse website, and selling out fast according to Fandango.

If you want a more hardcore experience, the Alamo is also hosting full-on parties in Austin, San Antonio, Los Angeles, New York, Bay Area, Raleigh, Denver, Chicago, Boston, Naples, Dallas, Twin Cities, and Omaha.

These events will feature a live host, an immersive photo op, and official movie-branded props including:

  • Sodapop Lip Balm
  • Huntrix or Saja Boys fandom buttons
  • Rumi, Mira, or Zoey Ramyeon Cups
  • Greeting Cards – Derpy & Sussie or Rujinu
  • Huntrix VIP badge
  • Glowstick
  • Fit Check Sticker
  • Photocards

Tickets to the movie parties are available here, and are also selling fast.
