YouTubers and viewers have thrown their support behind Nate The Lawyer as the creator underwent a grueling brain surgery to remove a tumor.

On November 16, YouTuber Nate The Lawyer revealed he was going to need emergency surgery to remove a “massive” tumor in his skull.

According to Nate, he was out with his family when he suddenly fainted and required medical attention.

“They found this huge mass in my head,” he revealed. “This mass was pushing on my brain, causing a lot of neurological issues. I couldn’t sleep. I was passing out. I was forgetful. I was confused about a lot of things.”

Nate explained that after speaking to the neurologist, if he didn’t get it taken care of, the tumor would become life-threatening.

Scared, the YouTuber thanked his audience and the platform for changing his life, prompting many in the community to send their best wishes and prayers to Nate, who accepted them with open arms.

“I am scared, but we get to test if prayer works,” he said in one of his final updates before surgery. “I bet it does. There are no atheists in foxholes. This is one hell of a foxhole.”

On November 20, Nate broke his silence on social media, exclaiming “prayer work!” to the relief of fans and his fellow content creators.

UncivilLaw, who had been receiving updates from Nate’s family, revealed that 90-95% of the mass in his skull was able to be removed.

“Short-term recovery is optimal. Nate is recovering nicely in the hospital. Discharge tomorrow possible. Should be back to normal quickly,” he added.

One day later, Nate was up and tweeting again, thanking everyone for checking in.

“60% Survival Rate for 5 years,” he revealed. “Thank you, everyone. This is gonna be one hell of a Thanksgiving.”

Furthermore, Nate stated that because his hair had to be cut for the surgery, he would be rocking a mohawk.

It’s not clear when Nate will return to making content, but fans and influencers alike continue to pull for him as he heals and recovers in the weeks ahead.
