Streaming star IShowSpeed has shown off the $300,000 tour bus he and his team are using for their 35-day Speed Does America streaming tour, and it’s pretty slick.

Over the last few years, IShowSpeed has gone on some pretty big streaming tours. He went to South East Asia to watch Cristiano Ronaldo play, he’s traveled to the Middle East to see him too, and has been all over Europe and China meeting fans.

Speed’s tours have, at times, gotten a little wild. He’s had to pay tens of thousands of dollars in damages to rental cars and needed police to get him out of some incredibly crowded areas.

His newest tour, however, will see him travel across 25 different states in America over 35 days. And, instead of using rental cars or planes, he’s doing it all with a pretty slick tour bus. 

IShowSpeed tour of American tour bus

The bus features a wrap on the outside of Speed’s face and a Speed Does America decal, complete with eagles and the red, white, and blue. He’s all in on the USA theme. 

On top of that, inside, the bus has a dedicated space for him to stream from. This’ll be where he sits during the downtime between states.

Again, it is complete with his logo, the number seven – his all-too familiar nod to Ronaldo – as well as nods to One Piece. Oh, and he’s got a custom shower that has his name and logo decal on the front of it.

Given that he is going to be live 24/7, the bus is full of cameras. So, there are no angles missed when he’s streaming or sleeping.

Speed has promised a few “surprises” along the way, including those nighttime parts of the stream. So, fans will have to tune in to see what he has got up his sleeve.
