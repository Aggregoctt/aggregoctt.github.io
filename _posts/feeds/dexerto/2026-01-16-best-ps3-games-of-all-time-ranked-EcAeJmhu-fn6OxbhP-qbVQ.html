When the PlayStation 3 launched in 2006, it ushered in a new era of cinematic, story-driven gaming. With powerful hardware, online play becoming standard, and Blu-ray storage opening the door to massive worlds, the PS3 generation was where games began to feel truly blockbuster in scale.

This was the console that delivered generation-defining adventures like Uncharted 2, The Last of Us, and Metal Gear Solid 4. It introduced new icons through Demon’s Souls, Infamous, and LittleBigPlanet, while multi-platform giants like Skyrim, Mass Effect 2, GTA V, and BioShock Infinite pushed storytelling, player choice, and open-world design further than ever before. Competitive shooters, sports titles, and online multiplayer also came into their own, with Call of Duty, Battlefield, FIFA, and NBA 2K becoming everyday staples.

From emotional narrative journeys to genre-shaping action and RPG epics, the PS3 library represents one of gaming’s most transformative generations.

With that in mind, here’s our ranking of the best PS3 games.
