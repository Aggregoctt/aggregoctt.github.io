Black Ops 6’s Ballerina event is live, and to complete it, you’ll need to rack up some High Table Coins. Here’s the fastest way to get them all. 

For years, Call of Duty fans have dreamed about a John Wick collaboration. Keanu Reeves’ now-iconic action hero is basically made for the Call of Duty world, and he’s already appeared in Fortnite before. 

Instead of Wick being added, the devs have gone a different way. They’ve focused on Ballerina, the new installment in the Wick universe starring Ana de Armas, and brought her character into Black Ops 6. 

On top of being able to buy the Ballerina skin, there is also a themed event in multiplayer and Warzone. Like some of the pop culture crossover events that we’ve seen already, you have to go out and collect tokens so that you can unlock some of the rewards on offer.

Warzone is fastest way to get Ballerina event coins

To get all of the rewards on offer – including the Essex Model 07 Marksman Rifle, two rounds of double XP, and legendary GobbleGum – you’ll need 160 High Table Coins. 

The fastest way to get your hands on these is through Warzone, well, Resurgence specifically. TikToker MagicDonuts117 pointed out that if you play Resurgence, focus solely on looting, you can come out with around 22 coins per game. 

If you repeat that for around two hours, you’ll have enough to claim them all, finishing the event in no time at all. 

Obviously, not everyone can sit down and grind away for one evening, but it’s clearly a repeatable method that you can go back to at different times.

Plus, the event runs until June 20, so you’ve got plenty of time to get stuck into things. 

There is an additional reward on offer for those who have the BlackCell battle pass. That is the Decora Tenebris Nunchuck Blueprint. That doesn’t cost any more event coins than you already need, thank you.
