It might have been released back in 2020, but that hasn’t stopped Cyberpunk 2077 from dropping a brand new major update, adding new quests, cars, and the ‘You Play with Fire’ reported crime task.

If you never thought you’d hear the words: ‘Cyberpunk 2077 has a new major update‘ then you wouldn’t be alone. But, here we are, there’s new content for the five-year-old game, and it’s completely rejuvenated the playerbase, with many diving in to grab the new cars and run riot in the streets of Night City.

However, to get one of those cars, you’ll need to complete a certain quest, and that quest (naturally) isn’t readily available. That quest is ‘You Play with Fire’ reported crime, and we’ve got all you need, from how to find it to how to complete it, and of course, what you get for your time (Hint: It’s a car).

How to find and start ‘You Play with Fire’ reported crime

To start the ‘You Play with Fire…’ questline, you’ll need to head over to Charter Hill in Westbrook. Look for an H-shaped building on the east side of Night City, or just check the map above for exactly where to go.

Once there, look for the armored trucks and open fire. Take down all the enemies in that area and you’ll eventually see a yellow ‘!’ marker on your map.

Go there and look for the body with the readable: ‘Archived Conversation: Zeitgeist and Jorge‘ and pick it up. Once you do, you’ll have the quest.

How to complete ‘You Play with Fire’

Thankfully, once you have it, the quest is pretty easy. All you need to do is look for the quest marker and head to Zeitgeist’s den. Follow the waypoint until you get to a fence and look to the right of it for a small gap by the door.

Head inside, take out anyone who stands in your way, and look for a crate on the desk. It’s quite a small den (poor Zeitgeist), so you shouldn’t struggle to find it.

Inside is a list of tasks, which will complete the quest… yes, it’s that simple.

Rewards

Outside of the weapons and money you get from killing the enemies and looting, you’ll get the brand new Yaiba ASM-R250 Muramasa motorbike. 

Don’t worry if you failed (my instructions can only take you so far), as you can still buy the new motorbike in the Autofixer.
