How to redeem codes

Claiming codes in Jujutsu Beatdown requires an extra verification step before you can redeem anything. Before entering a code, you’ll need to follow the official @VIVEJova and @VIVEAvanti account on Roblox and keep your Roblox username ready, as the game asks you to confirm it during the process.

Once that requirement is complete, you can proceed with the redemption steps below to successfully claim your rewards:

  • Click the star icon in the top-left corner of the screen, or press M on your keyboard.
  • Enter your Roblox username in the text box and click ‘VERIFY‘ to proceed.
  • If you successfully verify your Roblox username, reopen the codes menu by clicking the star icon or pressing M again.
  • Enter a working code in the text box.
  • Click ‘REDEEM‘ to claim the rewards.


Note – The code redemption system previously required following the developers on X for verification, but it has now shifted to Roblox-based verification. If you had already verified using your X account earlier, you do not need to follow the developers on Roblox again to access the codes menu.
