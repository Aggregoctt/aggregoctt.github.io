YouTuber and streamer iShowSpeed has announced a new international project called Speed Does Africa, confirming plans to travel across the continent in a multi-country tour later this month.

Speed revealed the tour on December 21, outlining an ambitious schedule that will see him visit 20 African countries over the span of 28 days. According to the announcement graphic shared with fans, the tour is set to begin on December 29 and will be streamed live for viewers around the world.

The Africa tour marks another major milestone in Speed’s rapid rise as one of the most-watched creators in live streaming. Known for his high-energy broadcasts, IRL streams, and viral moments, Speed has increasingly leaned into travel-based content over the past year, taking his streams beyond the United States and into international settings.

When is Speed Does Africa?

Speed confirmed the global start times for the first stream, with broadcasts scheduled to begin at 7am EST, 1pm CET, and 9am BRT on December 29. Viewers will be able to watch the tour live on his official YouTube and Twitch channels, which were both linked in the announcement.

What countries will iShowSpeed visit?

The countries listed as part of the Speed Does Africa itinerary include:

  • Algeria
  • Angola
  • Benin
  • Botswana
  • Egypt
  • Eswatini
  • Ethiopia
  • Ghana
  • Ivory Coast
  • Kenya
  • Liberia
  • Morocco
  • Mozambique
  • Namibia
  • Nigeria
  • Rwanda
  • Senegal
  • South Africa
  • Zambia
  • Zimbabwe

No specific order or day-by-day breakdown for the stops has been shared yet.
