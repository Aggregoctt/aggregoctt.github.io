Solo Leveling: ARISE OVERDRIVE is giving fans the chance to step into Sung Jinwoo’s shoes like never before. 

Announced at the Xbox Games Showcase earlier this year, the action RPG is now preparing for launch on PC this November, with a brand new demo set to debut at TwitchCon San Diego.

Solo Leveling: ARISE OVERDRIVE is a premium adaptation of the worldwide hit anime, built as a high-quality action experience for PC and console players. Every main hunter, from Sung Jinwoo to the series’ iconic allies and enemies, has been recreated in detail.

If you’re a fan of the original story, you will recognize the combat variety too, with players able to switch between weapon types and abilities to unlock entirely new playstyles.

For the first time ever, players will also get to experience Jinwoo’s transformation into the Monarch of Shadows, use signature skills in real time, and explore a combat system that rewards precise timing with parries and high-level combos.

Beyond the story campaign, the game features boss raids and four-player co-op, letting hunters join forces to take down some of the most dangerous foes in the Solo Leveling universe.

TwitchCon showcase and launch details

For an early look, Netmarble will showcase Solo Leveling: ARISE OVERDRIVE at TwitchCon 2025 in San Diego. Attendees will be able to try a brand new demo, win exclusive merchandise through booth activities, and even catch streamers going live directly from the show floor.

With its fast-paced action, intricate combat design, and faithfully recreated characters, Solo Leveling: ARISE OVERDRIVE is positioning itself as one of the most anticipated RPGs of the year. It is set to release on PC via Steam and Xbox PC on November 17, 2025, and is available to preorder now. Console launch is set for 2026.
