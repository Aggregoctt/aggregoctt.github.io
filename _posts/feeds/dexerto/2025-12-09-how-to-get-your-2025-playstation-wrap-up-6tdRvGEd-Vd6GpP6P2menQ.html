PlayStation has launched a feature that allows gamers to review their PS5 and PS4 gameplay from the previous year. Here’s how to get it.

Social media platforms like Spotify, YouTube, and Twitch have all made their 2025 recaps available for fans to check out, but now Sony has joined the fold.

On Tuesday, December 9, PlayStation’s 2025 Wrap-Up became available for PS5 and PS4 fans to check out all the games they played, how many trophies they earned, and how many hours it took to get there.

How to see your PlayStation Wrap-Up 2025

The PlayStation Wrap-Up will continue to be available until January 8, 2026, and unlike others, Sony will continue to update the results until New Year’s Eve.

Here’s how to access it:

  • Go to the 2025 Wrap-Up website
  • Sign in to your PlayStation account

Players who check out their 2025 Wrap-Up will receive a unique glass-themed avatar to use on their account.

Sony does note, however, that PlayStation 5 users who have not consented to the collection of “full data” on their console will not be able to access their Wrap-Up.

Also, users must have a PlayStation account in their region, be of adult age or over, and have played at least 10 hours of games between January 1, 2025, and December 31, 2025.
