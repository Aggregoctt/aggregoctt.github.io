Here’s your full list of the Emmy 2025 winners, including Adolescence, The Studio, The Pitt, and Stephen Colbert.

The 2025 Emmy awards were handed out last night at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, with comedian Nate Bargatze hosting.

Adolescence was the bit winner of the night, with the Netflix drama scooping six awards, including best limited or anthology series.

Meanwhile, The Studio dominated in the comedy categories, taking home best comedy series, as well as best actor for Seth Rogen. Here’s the list in full…
