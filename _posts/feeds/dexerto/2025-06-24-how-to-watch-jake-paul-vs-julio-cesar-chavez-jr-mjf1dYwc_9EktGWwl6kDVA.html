Jake Paul is gearing up for yet another fight against a major combat sports powerhouse in Julio Cesar Chavez Jr. Here’s everything you need to know to tune into Jake’s 13th boxing match.

Jake Paul has come a long way from his days as a Disney actor. The YouTuber is on a mission to become a bonafide boxing champion, and he’s touched gloves with a few famous names in the space over the years since his first bout in 2018.

From Anderson Silva to the great Mike Tyson, Paul is now setting his sights on Julio Cesar Chavez Jr., who held the WBC middleweight title from 2011 – 2012.

However, Chavez Jr. wasn’t his first choice of opponents. Paul was initially slated to fight Saul ‘Canelo’ Alvarez in a weekend bout on Cinco De Mayo, but it ended up getting canned after Alvarez took a four-fight deal with Riyadh Season.

When is Jake Paul vs Julio Cesar Chavez Jr.?

Jake Paul and Chavez Jr.’s fight is scheduled for Saturday, June 28 at 8:00 PM PT / 11:00 PM ET. The event as a whole starts at 5:00 PM PT / 8:00 PM ET.

The bout is being held at the Anaheim Convention Center in Anaheim, California, which played host to the massive YouTuber convention VidCon just a week prior.

Where to watch Jake Paul vs Julio Cesar Chavez Jr.

You can tune in to Jake Paul’s fight against Julio Cesar Chavez Jr. exclusively on DAZN pay-per-view for $59.99.

However, you’ll also have to sign up for a subscription to the combat sports streaming platform, which costs $19.99 per month. It’s worth noting that the site offers a free, 7-day trial for those who aren’t interested in paying a monthly fee to watch other bouts, though.

Of course, tickets for in-person seats at the Anaheim Convention Center are still available, with prices ranging from $38 – $50. That didn’t stop a law firm from spending $1 million on an ‘Owner’s Experience’ ticket, which comes with perks like a private box inside the corral for two, plus four front-row ringside seats and more.

Jake Paul vs Julio Cesar Chavez Jr. undercard

A full list of fighters on the undercard is as follows:

  • Gilberto ‘Zurdo’ Ramirez vs Yuniel Dorticos – Cruiserweight for the WBA and WBO titles
  • Holly Holm vs Yolanda Vega – Lightweight
  • Floyd Schofield vs Tevin Farmer – Lightweight
  • Avious Griffin vs Julian Rodriguez – Welterweight
  • Raul “Cugar” Curiel vs Victor Ezequiel Rodriguez – Welterweight
  • Naomy Valle vs Ashley Felix – Light flyweight
