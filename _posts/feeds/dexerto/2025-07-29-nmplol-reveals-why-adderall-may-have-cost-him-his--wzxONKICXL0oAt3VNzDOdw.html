Twitch star nmplol believes his use of Adderall may have “cost him” his marriage to fellow streamer Malena Tudi. 

Back in September 2024, Nick ‘nmplol’ Polom and his spouse, Malena Tudi, revealed they had ended their relationship and claimed they had “different goals” for the future. 

Malena filed for divorce from the OTK co-founder in January 2025, and Polom responded just a few weeks later. Allegations of cheating and financial abuse surfaced shortly after, but have been denied by the Twitch streamer.

Nmplol hasn’t spoken about the situation much since the divorce was filed, but during the inaugural episode of his Punching Down Podcast with Sodapoppin, he revealed what he believes had an impact on the relationship.

Nmplol says Adderall is the “worst thing ever”

In the show, Sodapoppin and nmplol talked about how many streamers, including themselves, “abused the living sh*t out of adderall” during the Among Us era.

“No, I truly think Adderall is probably one of the worst things that ever [existed],” nmplol said. “I think it may have cost me my marriage, to keep it one hundred percent.”

He added: “People who use Adderall, their personality changes. I stay away from that [now].”

This podcast comes after years of Nmplol talking about starting a podcast with Sodapoppin, but leaving fans up in limbo when it came to actually going through with it.

“It only took eight short years,” he joked in the video description.
