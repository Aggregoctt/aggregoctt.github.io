There’s something quietly satisfying about gliding over Springfield in Fortnite. The colors are too bright, the roofs too square, the lawns suspiciously perfect, and then you see it — that pink house. The one you’ve seen a thousand times before while eating cereal on Saturday mornings.

Now it’s sitting right there in Fortnite, waiting to be looted. The Simpsons crossover hasn’t just added weapons and a new map, it’s built a pocket of nostalgia you can actually walk through. And yes, you can open the Simpsons’ front door, raid their kitchen, and steal Homer’s stash of healing items.

How to find the Simpsons house in Fortnite

To find Homer’s house in Fortnite, fly toward Evergreen Terrace on the west side of the map. Here, look for a two-story pink house with an orange roof, a pink car in the driveway, and a big tree out front.

You can land on the roof or balcony if you want an early chest, or walk through the front door and head straight for the TV room. There’s usually loot near the couch, another chest by the kitchen, and a hidden one tucked up in Bart’s room.

Marge is here, and Flanders’ not too far if you want to find all the NPCs in the Fortnite Simpsons season.

Once you’re done rummaging through Springfield’s most famous living room, move east toward the Nuclear Power Plant for stronger loot or stop by Springfield Slurpworks for quick shields.
