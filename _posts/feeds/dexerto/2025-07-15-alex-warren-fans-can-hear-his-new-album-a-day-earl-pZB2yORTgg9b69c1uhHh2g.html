Chipotle is partnering with singer-songwriter Alex Warren to host a global listening party for his upcoming album You’ll Be Alright, Kid on Thursday, July 17.

From 5 to 8 pm local time, Chipotle restaurants across the U.S., Canada, the UK, and France will play the album ahead of its official release on July 18.

Warren, whose single ‘Ordinary’ has spent six weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot 100, is also offering fans a chance to win a signed vinyl. Anyone who orders the Alex Warren Bowl through the Chipotle app or website during the listening window and uses promo code ALEX will be entered into the giveaway.

“Teaming up with Chipotle to help introduce my album to the world will allow my fans to meet up with each other and have a first listen while enjoying one of my go-to meals,” Warren said in a statement.

Alex Warren partners with Chipotle for custom bowl

The Alex Warren Bowl, which first launched in April, includes white rice, a double portion of Chipotle Honey Chicken, fresh tomato salsa, roasted-chili corn salsa, and sour cream. It’s available for a limited time through Chipotle’s digital platforms.

Anyone who orders the Alex Warren Bowl through the Chipotle app or website during the listening window and uses promo code ALEX will be entered into a giveaway for a signed copy of Alex’s new album.

Warren co-founded the TikTok viral Hype House back in 2019, and gained a massive following through collaborative videos featuring the rest of the group. He met his wife, Kouvr, through the group, and the duo has taken over the app together as Alex worked towards building his music career.
