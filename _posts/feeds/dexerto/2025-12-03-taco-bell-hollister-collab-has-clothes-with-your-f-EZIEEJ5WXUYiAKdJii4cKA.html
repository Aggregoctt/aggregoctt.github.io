Taco Bell and Hollister have teamed up for a new fashion collection that will let you celebrate your favorite sauce packets while out and about in public. 

Fast food restaurants have long collaborated with other brands to bring different things to their fans. This could be a toy with a value meal, a new set of drink cups, or even new flavors of sauces. Everything has been pretty much covered over the years. 

Some stores have even started putting their logos on different items of clothing. Chick-fil-A, McDonald’s, In-N-Out Burger, and Pizza Hut have all had clothing ranges in one form or another, to name just a few examples.

However, Taco Bell has taken things up to a notch. Instead of putting out their own produced merchandise, they’ve teamed up with Hollister – and some of the hoodies are pretty interesting. 

Taco Bell x Hollister merch out for December

That’s right, as of December 1, you can pick up Taco Bell-branded Hollister clothing. As of writing, there are a number of different items up for sale, including three t-shirts, set of socks, a hoodie, and a zip-up jacket. 

Each item has Taco Bell’s slogan, Live Mas, on it somewhere, but one hoodie design that you can purchase has different sauce packets stitched into the front. 

This means that you can rock the Diablo, hot, fire, and mild sauces everywhere you go. 

The pieces of merch on offer differ from country to country. In the United Kingdom, there are only six different types of items to buy, with sets of jeans and sweatpants being kept for the United States. 

It is a limited-edition collection, so once everything is gone, you’ll have missed out – possibly for good.
