Final Sentence is swapping trigger fingers for WPM with its unique spin on the battle royale formula, replacing bullets with words. Here’s everything we know so far about this fast-paced typing showdown.

Developed by Button Mash, Final Sentence drops players into tense multiplayer matches where accuracy is the only weapon that matters. Type fast, hit your word targets, and survive against dozens of others in real-time. Miss too many words, and you’re out.

Final Sentence release window

Final Sentence doesn’t have a confirmed release date yet, but according to its Steam page, it’s targeting a Q4 2025 launch window. Players can wishlist the game now to get notified when early access or public tests begin.

Additionally, a free demo was released on October 10, 2025.

What platforms will Final Sentence be on?

So far, Final Sentence has only been announced for PC via Steam. There’s no word on console versions yet, though given its fast-paced typing mechanics, the experience seems tailor-made for keyboard users.

What’s it about?

Unlike most typing games, Final Sentence turns the simple act of hitting the right keys into a full-blown fight for survival. Up to 100 players enter the same online match, each racing to out-type the others while avoiding elimination. Every mistake brings you closer to defeat, and only one player can be left standing.

Gameplay details

The core loop revolves around typing accuracy and speed. Players compete in real-time matches that test their focus and reflexes under pressure. There are ranked modes, detailed stats, and customizable lobbies for private games with friends.
