Destiny Rising is proving to be another immensely popular Destiny adventure, but given its place as a multiplayer game, there are often times when the servers go down, stopping you from playing.

Whether you’re trying to pull the best character on the game’s gacha, grabbing codes for some free rewards, or taking part in the many events dotted around the adventure, Destiny Rising is never short of things to do.

However, hunting for that Cat or taking down the enemy isn’t that easy when the servers are down, especially if you’re trying to plan a play session with your friends. So, if your game isn’t working or you just want to check to see if you can play before you all dive in, here’s the current server status for Destiny Rising.

Are the Destiny Rising servers down?

At the time of writing, the Destiny Rising servers are down for maintenance.

This was reported by the Destiny Rising X page, in which they explained the servers were being “brought offline for scheduled maintenance.”

Also explained in the post was that they “anticipate the downtime to last up to 4 hours,” meaning we have a fair amount of time to wait until it’s back and running.

That means, players can expect to see Destiny Rising back and running at the following times:

  • PDT: 6 am
  • ET: 9 am
  • BST: 2 pm
  • JST: 10 pm

How to check Destiny Rising servers

One of the easiest ways to check the servers is to bookmark this page, as we’ll be updating it whenever it goes back online or goes offline.

Alternatively, it’s worth keeping an eye out for the Destiny Rising X page, as they regularly share updates on scheduled maintenance.
