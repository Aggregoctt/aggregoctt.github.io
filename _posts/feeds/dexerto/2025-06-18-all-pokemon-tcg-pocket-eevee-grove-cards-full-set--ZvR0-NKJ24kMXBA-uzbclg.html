The second most iconic creature in the franchise is getting a Pokemon TCG Pocket expansion focused on itself and its evolutions: the adorable Eevee! Learn all about the Eevee Grove set and its release date.

The Pokemon TCG Pocket’s meta recently changed when the Extradimensional Crisis expansion was added to the game. And as is customary, this is about to happen again once the Eevee Grove kicks in with over 100 new cards.

Similar to the Mythical Island and Triumphant Light sets, this one will be small. However, it might become one of the most popular as it will feature Eevee, Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Glaceon, Leafeon, and Sylveon ex, full-art, and secret rare cards.

Here are all of the cards that will release on June 26, 2025, with the Eevee Grove expansion.
