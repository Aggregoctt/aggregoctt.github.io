What are Monster Ghoul codes used for?

In Monster Ghoul, codes mainly reward Yen and RC Cells, both of which are essential to progression on either side of the conflict. Yen acts as the primary currency, letting players purchase weapons, abilities, kagunes, quinques, and cosmetic items from in-game shops.

RC Cells play a more specialized role, especially for Ghouls. They’re required to form and upgrade kagunes and are also tied to regeneration, making them vital during combat. CCG members can also use RC-related resources for certain quinques.
