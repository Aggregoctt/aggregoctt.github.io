MrBeast has spent a lot of his career giving to others, but, for his latest video, he’s deciding to spend a fortune on his significant other by taking her out on a series of dates. The most expensive destination is Disneyland, with him renting out the whole park for just the two of them.

The YouTuber has put on productions worth literal tens of millions of dollars and regularly gives away more money than most people will ever see in their lives. MrBeast has a lot of money to work with, and the high stakes behind his videos are a huge part of why he has over 400 million subs.

However, he’s chosen to give his fiancé a special set of dates this time around, including a dinner served by Gordon Ramsay, renting out a private island, reserving the biggest indoor theme park in America, and more.

In total, he spent almost a million dollars on dates in this video, one of which was him trying to hook his friend up with Madison Beer.

MrBeast proposed to Thea Booysen, his now-fiancé, on Christmas day in 2024, and, though the two have yet to tie the knot officially, he’s not pulling any stops when it comes to organizing dates.

He got Gordon Ramsay to put together a full meal with several courses, complete with live music. And, believe it or not, it was $10k and one of the cheaper dates he put together.

This was followed by renting out a private island for $50k and an entire theme park for $100k, both of which were relatively cheap in comparison to the crown jewel of his video.

He also spent $250k setting up his friend Nolan with Madison Beer, renting out a Lambo and securing private seats at an MLB game just to try and get him a second date with her and take her to Disneyland.

This ended with MrBeast renting out Disneyland for him and his friends, taking a trip through the entire park and skipping all the lines for the low, low price of $500,000.

And, though this was surely an expensive video to make, it’s not even close to being the priciest thing this YouTuber is working on. Beast Games Season 2 is already putting up $15 million in prize money, not even including how much it’ll cost to film.
