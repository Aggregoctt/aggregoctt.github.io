Streamer Jynxzi has officially beaten his first Geometry Dash demon level, clearing the iconic Clubstep after 3,620 attempts.

The creator, best known for his Rainbow Six Siege and Clash Royale streams, has been playing Geometry Dash more frequently on stream in recent weeks as viewers pushed him toward more difficult levels.

Jynxzi first started experimenting with Geometry Dash several weeks ago but began taking the game more seriously after receiving guidance from Zoink, one of the top-ranked Geometry Dash players, on November 3. Since then, his streams have featured steady practice runs, repeated checkpoints, and increasing attempts at tougher sections of Clubstep.

Clubstep is the first official demon level in Geometry Dash’s main map pack, but it’s far from the easiest demon in the game. Many players begin with simpler community-created demon levels before attempting Clubstep.

A major milestone for the streamer

Completing Clubstep as a first demon is generally considered a challenging milestone for beginners, due to its tight timing windows, memorization-heavy UFO sequences, and late-level ship controls that punish even small mistakes.

Jynxzi beat the level on November 22, celebrating the clear on stream as viewers spammed the chat with disbelief that he pulled it off in under a month of serious practice. While he hasn’t announced whether he plans to continue pushing into harder demons, he did announce it as his “first demon,” hinting that more may be in store. Clearing Clubstep puts him on the path toward the game’s wider difficulty ladder, where thousands of user-created demon levels rank far above it.

His improvement over the past two weeks has also become a recurring highlight for his viewers, who’ve watched him progress from basic levels to tackling one of Geometry Dash’s classic difficulty benchmarks. And they won’t soon forget his heartbreaking choke at 96% followed by his victory shortly after.
