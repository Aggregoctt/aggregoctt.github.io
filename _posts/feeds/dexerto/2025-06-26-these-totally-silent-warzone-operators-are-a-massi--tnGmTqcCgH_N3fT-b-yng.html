Most players know that using Warzone Operator skins with dark colors and a low profile offers an advantage, but you might not have known that some characters are completely silent, so they don’t give your position away.

There’s almost always a ‘meta’ skin in Warzone that players who want every advantage available will use. It was Roze in the original Verdansk, the Gaia ‘Groot’ skin during MW2 and MW3, and in Black Ops 6‘s lifecycle, it’s the T-800 Terminator with its dark colours and almost see-through skeletal design.

However, you might actually have skins in your locker that offer this different yet equally powerful advantage.

Silent Warzone skins keep you hidden

Most Operators have voice lines where they’ll announce what they’re doing or make a post-fight quip, but nearby enemies can actually hear some of these.

When grenades land near them, they spot a mine, or are reloading, they’ll shout something that nearby players can hear.

This can be avoided by using Operators like Velikan, John Black Ops, and Michael Myers, who are completely silent and will never give your position away.

It was highlighted in a Reddit thread from June 25, where the OP said, “I’ve gotten kills on people just because [their] Operator reacted to a Semtex while they were hiding.”

“Honestly this sh*t is so op,” said another player. “It’s always funny when I hear ‘reloading’ [or] ‘changing mags.'”

You can also use Black Noir, R0-Z3, Silent Bob, Vortex, Goliath, and the CDL Operators, as they also won’t make any noise during gameplay.

While it won’t save your life in every situation, if you’re looking for every competitive advantage, there’s no reason not to use these silent Operators – especially those with dark outfits like John Black Ops.

On the other side, it’s also worth listening out for these audio cues. But don’t rely on them, as other players might be using one of the skins listed above.
