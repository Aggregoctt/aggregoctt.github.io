Borderlands 4 has finally arrived, which can mean only one thing: it’s time for a brand new set of SHiFT codes to grant you free Keys, skins, and so much more.

SHiFT codes are nothing new in the world of Borderlands. Tiny Tina’s Wonderland, Borderlands 3, and all the games that came before featured tons of handy codes to offer you free loot, including unique (and extremely powerful) weapons, ammo, and so much more.

That’s no different from Borderlands 4, which also features these handy SHiFT codes. However, they’re limited, meaning you’ll need to redeem them before they expire. So, here’s every SHiFT code available right now and how to redeem them.

Active SHiFT codes in Borderlands 4

As of September 11, there is only one active SHiFT code in Borderlands 4, to mark the release of the game.

Be sure to bookmark this page and check back soon, as we’ll share details of any new codes as soon as they become available.

How to redeem codes

There are two different ways to redeem codes in Borderlands 4. Simply click on the one you want to use and follow the instructions below:

  • In Game
  • Through the Website

In Game

  • Load up Borderlands 4.
  • Head to the main page and click Social.
  • Head over to the SHiFT tab.
  • Paste or type your code into the space.
  • Click Submit.
  • Grab your rewards from your Mail.

Through the website

  • Navigate to the SHiFT code website.
  • Create an account or log in with an existing one.
  • Verify your details on your profile page.
  • Head to the Rewards Page and select the platform you’re playing Borderlands 4 on.
  • Copy and paste the code you want to redeem and press Check.
  • Choose the game you want to claim the code in, so pick Borderlands 4.
  • Head into the game and grab the rewards from your Mail.

All expired codes

Currently, there are no expired SHiFT codes for Borderlands 4. However, when there are, they’ll be moved here, to save you from redeeming codes that won’t work.

What are Borderlands 4 SHiFT codes used for?

Just the same as all the games that came before it, SHiFT codes in Borderlands 4 let you redeem free rewards in the game, including Golden Keys, Diamond Keys, and, of course, skins for your weapons and more.

Expect ammo, guns, and new designs to appear occasionally, offering Harlowe, Amon, Vax, and Rafa a brand new toy to unleash on those bandits.
