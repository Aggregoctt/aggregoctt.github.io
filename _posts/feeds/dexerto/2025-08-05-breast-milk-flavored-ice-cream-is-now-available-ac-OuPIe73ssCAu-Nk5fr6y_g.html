You can now buy breast milk-flavored ice cream across the United States, thanks to a new collab that’s going viral for obvious reasons.

Parenting brand Frida has teamed up with New York-based small-batch ice cream company OddFellows to launch a limited-edition breast milk-flavored pint, available for online purchase with nationwide shipping.

To nip this in the bud right away, it’s not made from actual human breast milk. But it’s “just like mom used to make,” according to the brands, and contains some of the same ingredients.

Frida first launched the flavor earlier this year, and after going viral, it’s back for a wider release. Online orders are open now, with each pint costing $12.99 (minimum order: two pints). The desert is shipped in insulated packaging with dry ice and will go anywhere in the US aside from Alaska and Hawaii.

The scoop is also being served in person at OddFellows’ Dumbo pop-up in Brooklyn from August 5–10.

Breast milk ice cream tastes “close to the real deal”

You’re probably wondering what this tastes like. Frida describes the ice cream as “sweet, a little salty, smooth, with hints of honey and sprinkles of colostrum,” topped off with a yellow tint to match the theme.

“We think our recipe tastes pretty close to the real deal,” Frida’s website states.

The idea came from a wave of celeb moms who’ve tasted their own breast milk on social media, including Kourtney Kardashian.

The fascination with breast milk came to a head in 2021 when nursing Activision-Blizzard staff revealed refrigerators at work needed to be padlocked because other employees would steal their breast milk.

And while this might be the weirdest ice cream you’ve heard of lately, it’s not the only one. Earlier this year, KFC turned its famous gravy into Gravy Gelato, continuing the 2025 trend of food brands going all-in on novelty flavors.

Whether it’s a nostalgic nod or a viral stunt, the internet’s certainly eating it up.
