Grow a Garden is Roblox’s latest hit, allowing players the chance to cultivate crops, make tons of money, and participate in the game’s latest events to get free rewards, like the Honey Sprinkler.

Despite its popularity, Grow a Garden leaves a lot for players to figure out as they perfect their gardens and learn which crops make the most money. Throughout your green-thumbed adventure, you’ll find various events, all of which offer different rewards.

The latest is the Bizzy Bee Event, which allows players to buy Honey Sprinklers, one of only two event-exclusive Sprinklers available in the game. So, here’s what they do, how to get them, and of course, when they’ll disappear from the shop.

Grow a Garden Honey Sprinklers uses

Honey Sprinklers will apply the Honey Glazed Mutation to your Fruits and Flowers in its surrounding area, and is part of the Bizzy Bee Event.

The Honey Glazed Mutation will offer you a 5x Sheckles Multiplier and will have a yellow fog surrounding the item, which will also be dripping in yellow liquid.

How to get Honey Sprinklers

To get Honey Sprinklers in Grow A Garden, you’ll need to spend 30 Honey in the Bizzy Bee Event’s Honey Shop. Alternatively, if you want to spend Robux, you can purchase it anytime for 199.

This is best done by depositing at least 10kg of Pollinated Mutated plants into the Honey Combpressor. After three minutes, you’ll get 10 Honey.

When a Bee Swarm occurs (every 60 minutes), bees will begin to pollinate your plants, turning them golden. Hand these to the Honey Combpressor and you’ll soon get enough Honey to purchase the Sprinklers.

How long will they be in Grow a Garden?

You’ll have to be quick, however, as the Honey Sprinklers will only be in Grow a Garden until June 7, which marks the end of the Bizzy Bee Event.

As such, this means you’ll need to save up plenty of Honey before the event expires and grab as many Sprinklers as you can.
