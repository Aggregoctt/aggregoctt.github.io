As the year wraps up, platforms across tech and social media are rolling out personalized recap experiences, breaking down how users spent their time over the past 12 months.

From Spotify showing your music streaming habits to YouTube revealing your viewing history, year-end “wraps” have become a familiar part of December.

Uber is the latest company to join that wave with the release of YOUBER 2025, its annual year in review for riders and Uber Eats users. The recap began rolling out on Monday, December 15, but may take some time to become available.

The recap highlights rides, routes, food orders, and usage patterns that shaped each user’s year, based on data collected throughout 2025.

How to see your 2025 Uber recap

To access YOUBER 2025, open the Uber app and tap the “YOUBER 2025” banner displayed at the top of the home screen. From there, users can view a series of shareable cards breaking down their personalized stats.

The recap includes details such as overall Uber rating, total rides taken, destinations visited, preferred ride upgrades, and Uber Eats ordering habits.

Food-related insights include the number of restaurants ordered from, favorite items, and most ordered categories.

Each user is also assigned one of 14 personality types based on how they use Uber and Uber Eats. These include categories like Planning Prodigy, Do Gooder, Rise & Shiner, and The Big Deal, which reflect patterns such as scheduling habits, ratings, early morning rides, or deal-seeking behavior.

Alongside individual recaps, Uber shared several global highlights from its 2025 data. The longest ride of the year stretched nearly 700 miles from Austin, Texas, to Pensacola, Florida, and took around 11 hours to complete.

Uber Eats’ largest order of 2025 was a Chinese food delivery with more than 180 items. The company also reported delivering over 150,000 live crickets through Uber Eats, primarily as pet food.
