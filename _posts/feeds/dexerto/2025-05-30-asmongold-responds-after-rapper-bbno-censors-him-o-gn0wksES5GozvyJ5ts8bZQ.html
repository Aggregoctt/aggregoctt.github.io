Asmongold has responded after rapper bbno$ censored him out of a music video he filmed with various members of OTK.

Founded back in 2020 by Asmongold, Mizkif, and others, the Twitch collective One True King – also known as just OTK – has collaborated with rapper bbno& through a variety of IRL and just chatting streams on the Amazon-owned platform.

Asmongold revealed he left OTK back in February 2025 due to “org changes” and after backlash to his comments about the people of Palestine.

On Sunday, May 29, bbno$ revealed a music video for his song ‘Mary Poppins’ that features various members of OTK, which quickly went viral on social media due to him placing a lizard face to block Asmon out of the video.

“At the time of recording, I was unaware of certain individuals’ ethical beliefs. As a result, I’ve chosen to censor those whose values I do not support,” the rapper added at the beginning of the TikTok video.

Alongside the now-former OTK member, you can find Mizkif, nmplol, Cyr, WillNeff, Emiru, and other Twitch streamers.

Asmongold responds to bbno$ censoring him

“I don’t expect anybody to carry my banner, I don’t expect anybody to defend me,” he said on stream. “For me, the only thing that I care about is doing the right thing. If that’s not it, and people disagree with me… that’s fair. Honestly, I felt bad about this. I felt like I inconvenienced them.

“I don’t have any negative ill will towards him at all. I was going to message him and apologize. I didn’t know if they were going to upload it or not.”

In a follow-up reply to a post on X, Asmon joked: “Very generous of them to give me more teeth in post production, never had someone care like that before.”
