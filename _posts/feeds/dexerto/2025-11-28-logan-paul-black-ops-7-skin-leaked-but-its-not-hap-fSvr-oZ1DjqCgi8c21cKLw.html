A Logan Paul-themed bundle for Call of Duty Black Ops 7 has been ‘leaked’, but it actually isn’t launching.  

Call of Duty collabing with celebrities isn’t an entirely new thing. They first collaborated with Snoop Dogg back in Call of Duty: Ghosts, and have continued to ramp things up as time has gone on. 

The celebrity bundles have, however, become a source of contention from players. Many have complained about a lack of realistic skins making their way into the game. It was something Battlefield 6 even parodied before launching earlier this year. 

For Black Ops 7, the Call of Duty developers even canceled a number of “big brand” collabs. Treyarch’s Senior Director of Production, Yale Miller told CharlieIntel that they’d be aiming for things that “make sense for Black Ops 7” instead. 

No, Logan Paul’s Black Ops 7 skin isn’t happening

Well, on November 27, social media claims surfaced, suggesting that Logan Paul would getting a skin in the new Call of Duty. 

“Looks like Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 might be getting a Logan Paul operator bundle, according to new datamined files discovered this week,” X users VelixGaming said, attaching an image of a Logan-themed skin. 

However, a number of reputable CoD leakers have downplayed that claim. “Where’s this come from? I’ve not found a single thing that points to this?” CoDWarfareForum responded. 

Other leakers, including RealityUK and HeyImAlaix were quizzed on if they’d heard anything and had a more forceful responses. “Bait,” RealityUK said. 

HeyImAlaix delivered a simple “no,” when asked if the crossover was true and if they’d heard anything about it. 

Logan’s brother, Jake, was used in some of the advertising for Black Ops 7. That led to fears from fans that he might have a skin in-game at some point in the future. 

That hasn’t been mentioned since before the launch, so who knows. But, Logan seemingly isn’t getting one.
