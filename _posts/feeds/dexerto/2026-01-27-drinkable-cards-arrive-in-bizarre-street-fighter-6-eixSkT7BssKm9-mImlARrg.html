An energy drink maker has revealed a set of drinkable cards that Street Fighter fans can enjoy without the need to crack open a can or bottle.

Japanese snack and beverage company UHA Mikakuto has teamed up with Capcom to release drinkable cards featuring artwork from Street Fighter 6.

Unlike traditional promo cards or collectibles, the card itself is the drink, and each one is designed to be snapped open with one hand and consumed directly.

This turns the act of drinking an energy drink into something closer to pulling a card from a booster pack rather than cracking open a can, and it’s just as cool as it is bizarre.

Drinks maker creates drinkable cards featuring Street Fighter art

According to the official press release, the drinkable cards were designed for moments where stopping to slam an energy drink could prove difficult.

“When playing games, taking an exam, in a meeting, or driving a car, in situations that require concentration, many people face the challenge of refueling, which equates to a break in concentration,” reads the translated post.

“Energy Arts addresses this issue by thoroughly pursuing the goal of ‘an effortless way to replenish energy.’ Simply pick it up, snap it open with one hand, and suck it up. The series of actions of ‘opening, holding, drinking, putting it away’ has been eliminated as much as possible.”

UHA Mikakuto confirmed the product is part of its new energy drink line and went on sale on January 23, 2026, in Japan.

Despite the cards being marketed as single-use, the Street Fighter 6 collaboration includes all 24 character designs from the game, including popular characters like Chun-Li and Akuma.
