Minecraft and Cool Shirtz have teamed up for a fresh apparel collection that brings the game into real-life fashion. The collaboration blends Minecraft’s familiar visuals with Cool Shirtz’s creative approach to fashion and turns in-game motifs into awesome pieces that work as everyday streetwear.

Minecraft has been one of the biggest games in the world since its release back in 2009. Its block-based worlds have shaped online culture for more than a decade, and it doesn’t always stop at video games. The company regularly collaborates with streetwear brands to bring the recognizable design to clothing, bags, and more.

This new collection with Cool Shirtz captures that shared spirit, featuring over 70 pieces that span clothing, accessories, and home items, ranging from small embroidered symbols to large standout prints. Fans can pick up everything from simple short-sleeve shirts to premium outerwear, and some items can be bagged for free.

How to get Minecraft x Cool Shirtz collection

To get the items, head to the Minecraft x Cool Shirtz store and locate your favorite item.

It’s important to note that the drop is exclusive to the store, which means this is the only place to buy the collaboration.

Fans have the chance to bag the following items:

  • Cherry Blossom Hoodie
  • Wither Denim Jacket
  • Iron Golem Denim Jeans
  • Iron Golem Denim Jacket
  • Villager Necklaxe

At the time of writing, the pieces are selling out fast, with the Enderman hoodie, Wither Ring, and hats selling out extremely fast, so it’s important to grab your favorite item as soon as possible.

How to get free Enderman Hoodie

Those gutted about the selling out of the Enderman Hoodie could be lucky enough to bag it for free thanks to a Minecraft giveaway. Here’s how to enter.

  1. Fill out the form to enter.
  2. Uncover the name of the armadillo featured in @coolshirtz’ Minecraft world post on X. (Sock)
  3. Refer your friends.

There are thousands of players all looking to bag the same item, so it’s worth trying just in case you’re feeling lucky.
