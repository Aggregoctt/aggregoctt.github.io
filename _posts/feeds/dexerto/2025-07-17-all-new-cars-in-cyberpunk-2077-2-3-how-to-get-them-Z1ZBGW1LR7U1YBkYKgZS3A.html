Cyberpunk 2077 2.3 is the next big update, bringing new quests, improved photo mode, bug fixes, and new vehicles to unlock.

Despite the game releasing in 2020 and The Witcher 4 now in development, CD Projekt Red is still keeping Cyberpunk 2077 updated with new patches. Update 2.3, which arrives on July 17, brings four new vehicles, but unlocking them isn’t as simple as spending credits.

Here are all the new vehicles in the update and how to unlock them.

Yaiba ARV-Q340 Semimaru

The Yaiba ARV-Q340 Semimaru sports coupe was actually shown off in the Kickdown comic series, and finally arrives in Cyberpunk with the 2.3 update through a new side job.

How to unlock

To unlock it, you’ll need to complete The Hunt and The Beast in Me quests, which will trigger the new side job. Plus, “An additional side job unlocks the CrystalCoat feature for it.”

Rayfield Caliburn “Mordred”

The Rayfield Caliburn “Mordred” variant is the first armored sports car in Cyberpunk 2077 and can only be unlocked through a new side job.

How to unlock

To start this side job, complete The Beast in Me: Badlands, The Beast in Me: Santo Domingo, Transmission, and Search and Destroy.

Yaiba ASM-R250 Muramasa

The first customizable motorcycle in Cyberpunk 2077, the Yaiba ASM-R250 Muramasa, is unlocked by completing a new side job.

How to unlock

To unlock this, you’ll need to have brought at least one Yaiba vehicle, purchase at least three vehicles through the Autofixer, and have completed Reported Crime: You Play with Fire. The devs did say that if you fail the side job, you can still buy it through the Autofixer.

Chevillon Legatus 450 Aquila

The first ever weaponized truck, the Chevillon Legatus 450 Aquila, doesn’t have an associated side quest and can instead be bought from the Autofixer.

The truck is the only non-customizable vehicle added in 2.3, but the devs explained that it’s connected to its lore, which you can learn more about in-game.
