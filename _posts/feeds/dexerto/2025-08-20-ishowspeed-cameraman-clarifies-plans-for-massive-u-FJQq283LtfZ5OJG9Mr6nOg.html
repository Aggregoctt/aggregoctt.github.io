IShowSpeed’s cameraman Slipz has revealed more details about his upcoming tour across the United States, including the fact that it’s going to be non-stop for 35 days. 

Over the last few years, IShowSpeed has been traveling the world, meeting fans and ticking off some crazy side quests. The streaming stars has played in charity football matches, raced in the Gumball 3000, and even danced with robotic cars in China. He keeps upping the ante with each new livestreaming tour, basically. 

On August 19, Speed, who has currently been on a bit of a break from streaming, revealed that his next streaming tour would take place across the United States. 

The YouTube and Twitch streamer wants to visit 25 states in 35 days, promising a “non-stop” stream for fans. 

IShowSpeed’s cameraman reveals more US tour details

MrBeast has already stated that he has “something fun” in mind for when Speed visits North Carolina, but now his cameraman, Slipz, has revealed more. 

“12hrs+ of IRL every day, then desktop and sleep on the bus,” he said on X (formerly Twitter) in response to rumors about Speed switching to desktop streaming as soon as he crosses into a new state.

He also confirmed rumors about Speed and his team having a tour bus for their trip. Previously, they’ve flown from country to country during European tours. So, a tour bus is pretty new. 

Speed’s European streams, in particular, have gotten pretty wild. The YouTuber has had to ditch plans in certain cities before because of the sheer number of fans that have turned up to meet him. 

His last tour of the US didn’t quite reach those levels, but with half a day of in-real-life streams being promised, who knows what could happen.
