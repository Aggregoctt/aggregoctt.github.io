Popular YouTuber IShowSpeed obliterated fitness influencer Ashton Hall in a series of races after Hall promised to “embarrass” him.

IShowSpeed is one of the most athletic YouTubers out there, with the 20-year-old often showing off his ability to backflip, score goals in soccer, and, as his name implies, run really fast.

In fact, Speed came close to besting Olympic runner Noah Lyles in a 50-yard dash – something the YouTuber claims made “everyone” afraid to race him.

Enter Ashton Hall, a former NFL hopeful turned fitness influencer who went viral after unveiling his absurd morning routine.

On June 11, Hall showed up on Speed’s stream to race him… and it wasn’t even close.

IShowSpeed obliterates Ashton Hall in races

On the way to the race, Hall had some fighting words for Speed, promising that he’d not only smoke the streamer, but he’d “embarrass him.”

“Your name IShowSpeed. This is gonna be bad for you, literally. I’m about to smoke him and talk crazy. I promise you.”

When Hall showed up on Speed’s stream, the YouTuber instantly started mocking him, joking that his muscles were “for show.”

“You’re about to get dusted,” Speed warned.

“I told you to back out,” Hall shot back, seemingly not knowing exactly who he was up against.

When the two eventually raced, it was a complete wash, with Speed winning easily as Hall face-planted on the ground, leaving his YouTube rival to win without much of a challenge.

Hall demanded a rematch and in round two, the fitness guru was able to stay on his feet. But he was no match for Speed, who pulled ahead and even put Hall on blast for calling him out.

If that wasn’t enough, Speed then proceeded to beat Hall for a third and fourth time, proving once and for all that he was faster.

Amazingly, Speed could be getting a huge upgrade to his quickness in the near future. In May, Jamaican sprinting star Usain Bolt offered to coach the content creator and improve his skills.
