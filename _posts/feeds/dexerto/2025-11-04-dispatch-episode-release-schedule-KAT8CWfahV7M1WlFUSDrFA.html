When Dispatch initially released, it didn’t drop the entire game in one go, instead offering weekly episodes. While this means players have longer to enjoy the game’s story, it also means many are dying for the next release to find out what happens to their beloved heroes.

Telling the tale of retired superhero Robert Robertson (played by Aaron Paul), who gets a job as a dispatcher who manages a team of ex-villains and heroes (featuring Critical Role voice actors), Dispatch is pretty much a superhero video game version of The Office. It’s a workplace comedy with tough choices, an absolutely stellar cast of voice actors, and tons of hilarious moments.

However, you can’t complete all of the game in one go (unless you’re super patient) as the developers have split Dispatch into episodes, with each releasing weekly. Here’s all you need to know so you’re among the first to dive into the madness of the Superhero world.

When is the next Dispatch episode?

The next two Dispatch episodes will drop on November 5 and will feature episodes five and six at 9 am PST / 12 pm EST / 5 pm GMT.

The fifth episode is called Team Building, and the sixth is called Moving Parts, so fans can certainly theorize what’s going to happen next. You can watch the preview trailer below.

Dispatch episode schedule

As we’ve seen with the previous episodes, we’ll be getting two a week, which began on October 22, when the game officially came out. Nevertheless, the schedule is below:

  • Episode 1 & 2: October 22, 2025
  • Episodes 3 & 4: October 29, 2024
  • Episodes 5 & 6: November 5, 2025
  • Episodes 7 & 8: November 12, 2025

Altogether, there are eight episodes in Dispatch, so if you’re waiting for them to all release, then you’ll be able to dive in on November 12.
