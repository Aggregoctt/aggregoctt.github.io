From Climax to Siren’s Kiss, here’s your guide to the new K-dramas and Korean variety shows hitting Netflix, Viki, Disney Plus, and more in March 2026. 

It’s never been a better time to be a fan of Korean content. Squid Game Season 3, Weak Hero Class 2, and When Life Gives You Tangerines were among the standout shows of 2025 – and that’s before diving into the world of reality TV.

From Physical: 100 to The Devil’s Plan and Single’s Inferno, viewers are spoiled for choice, and 2026’s TV show release calendar shows there’s plenty more where that came from.

All streaming services have something unique to offer, with many K-dramas making their debut next month. So, here’s a rundown of all the new Korean releases to mark in your calendar.

K-dramas to watch this month on streaming – Our picks

Siren’s Kiss – Prime Video

  • Release date: March 2, 2026
  • Regions: Everywhere
  • How long is it? 12 episodes

As the tagline warns, “If you love her, you die.” Siren’s Kiss is a romance thriller following an insurance fraud investigator who begins probing a string of mysterious deaths linked to a captivating auctioneer. Suspecting she may be a victim, he poses as her lover, only to uncover darker secrets beneath the surface. Squid Game’s Wi Ha-joon and Marry My Husband’s Park Min-young lead the cast in what promises to be a tense, twist-filled drama.

Shining – Netflix

  • Release date: March 6, 2026
  • Regions: Everywhere
  • How long is it? 10 episodes

If you’re in need of some heartwarming romance in between all the death and deception, Shining is for you. This romantic melodrama follows a subway driver living firmly in the present, whose quiet routine is shaken when he reunites with his first love years later. Park Jin-young and Kim Min-ju lead this tender story about finding light in unexpected places.

Climax – Viki

  • Release date: March 16, 2026
  • Regions: Everywhere
  • How long is it? 10 episodes

You had us at Ju Ji-hoon and Ha Ji-won. These two acting powerhouses are starring as a man who becomes a star prosecutor overnight and an actress who helps him reach these heights. Climax is set to be a cutthroat thriller centering on two people who will stop at nothing to achieve success – even if it means crossing each other.
