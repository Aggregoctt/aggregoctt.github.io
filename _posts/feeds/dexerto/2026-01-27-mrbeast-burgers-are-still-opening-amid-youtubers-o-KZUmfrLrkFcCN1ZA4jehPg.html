American YouTuber Jimmy Donaldson, known online as MrBeast, has opened a MrBeast Burger location in Sydney, marking the brand’s latest international expansion.

The new outlet has launched at Chatswood Westfield on the city’s North Shore, bringing the viral food brand to Australian customers for the first time.

MrBeast Burger operates as a virtual restaurant franchise, with meals prepared inside existing kitchens rather than standalone locations. The pop-up style locations typically remain open for a limited time before moving to new areas.

The Australian opening comes while MrBeast remains in an ongoing legal dispute with Virtual Dining Concepts, the company that helped launch the MrBeast Burger brand in the United States.

Sydney menu prices draw attention online

A photo of the Chatswood menu was posted to Reddit, where users focused on the listed prices for several of the chain’s best-known items.

The Beast Style burger is priced at $20.90, while the Crispy Chicken Tender sandwich costs $19.90. The Nashville Hot Chicken Tender is also listed at $20.90.

Combo meals for either the Beast Style Burger or Chicken Sandwich are priced at $27.90, which appear to include chips and a drink.

MrBeast currently has more than 460 million subscribers on YouTube, making him the most followed creator on the platform.
