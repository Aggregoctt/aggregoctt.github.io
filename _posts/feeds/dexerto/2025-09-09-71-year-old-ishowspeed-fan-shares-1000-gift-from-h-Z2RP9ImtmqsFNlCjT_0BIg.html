A 71-year-old fan of IShowSpeed has responded after the streaming star gifted her $1,000 on stream, revealing she used it to help someone in a car crash.

IShowSpeed has gone on massive streaming tours over the last few years, meeting fans all across the globe. Some of the interactions have been pretty sweet, while others have seen the police and his own security have to intervene for safety reasons.

The YouTube and Twitch streamer has embarked on another streaming tour too, visiting 25 states across America in just over a month.

During his pitstop in Detroit, Speed was introduced to a 71-year-old grandmother, Denise, who turned out to be a massive fan of his. The streamer even invited her to join him for breakfast and gifted her $1000 to do with however she pleased.

IShowSpeed fan reveals she gave money to woman in need

Well, the grandmother has since revealed that she used the money Speed gave her to help someone in need after witnessing a car crash on her journey home. 

“On my way home, I was just behind a car accident, and there was a lady sitting on the pavement next to her smashed-up car crying,” she posted on Facebook. Denise noted that the lady feared she would be arrested by the police for driving without insurance due to having a “rough time.”

“Speed had also given me a few hundred dollars. Always pay it forward. I shared the money with her!” Denise added.

She was also full of praise for Speed too. “What a wonderful and amazing guy, so genuine and generous, I just was blown away,” she said after meeting him. 

On top of the $1000, Speed also promised Denise a “free vacation” to Rome, as that was her dream destination. She was completely shocked by that offer, just because she had made his morning. 

“You made my morning. I never met nobody like you. You actually watch my streams at 71!” he said. “Denise, thank you, seriously.”
