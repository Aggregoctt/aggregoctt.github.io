Social media superstar iShowSpeed has just announced that he’s going to take a streaming break for “a very good reason.”

iShowSpeed isn’t just known for his gaming and collab streams, but he has also done plenty of IRL broadcasts that have reeled in a ton of fans due to how entertaining he is. 

Among his popular content are his travel streams, with the latest one taking place in Europe. Despite some hurdles faced during the tour, things have been an absolute blast, as he managed to hit an incredible milestone following his Twitch comeback.

While Speed now has many new fans looking forward to watching more of his content, they’ll have to be patient for the time being. The creator has just announced that he’s taking a little break from streaming, but teased something big to come.

iShowSpeed announces streaming break but promises big return

iShowSpeed revealed his plans to take a break in a recent stream, claiming it’s “for a good reason.” He didn’t expand on the details but did specify that the streaming break would last two weeks.

This means fans can expect the next Speed livestream during the week of August 4, assuming no delays.

“I’m taking a two-week break for a very good reason. When I come back, it’s gonna be huge. Just wait and see. I promise it’s worth it,” he assured fans.

He also added that “Summer ain’t over” and that he’ll be back in Europe. However, for now, as he put in his own words, “that’s it.” 

The streamer also thanked everyone who supported him on the tour, saying it’s been a “W.” Now, while he didn’t outright confirm plans for the return, that hasn’t stopped fans from speculating.
