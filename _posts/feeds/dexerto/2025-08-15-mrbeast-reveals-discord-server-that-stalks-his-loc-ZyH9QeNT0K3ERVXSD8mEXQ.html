YouTube star Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson has revealed that there is a Discord channel that is full of photos of his location and follows his every move. 

As he’s become the biggest YouTube channel out there, MrBeast has had to deal with plenty of people trying to pitch him ideas. Some have done it over the internet, while others have flown to his hometown and tried to meet him in person.

The YouTube megastar has had private security in place for such situations, with them having to stop fans in their tracks to see what they’re up to.

However, he has now revealed that there is a Discord server out there that tracks his every move based on photos that viewers have submitted of him. Seriously. 

MrBeast Discord actually stalks his location

“You know the craziest thing that happened? I go to a Target, super late, to just get a toothbrush and as I’m checking out a kid runs in and he’s like ‘holy s*it, its MrBeast’ and he’s like ‘there’s this Discord I’m in that tracks your location,” he told Adin Ross and xQc.

MrBeast noted that whenever a photo of him is posted online, this Discord “auto updates” and people “try to figure out” where he is based off that.

“He’s like ‘I got a notification that said you were in this Target because someone posted a photo on Instagram of it and you’re here!’ and I was like ‘what the f*ck!’” 

The YouTuber revealed that has now forced him to create a “four minute rule” which means that whenever a photo is taken of him, he tries to leave within four minutes because of the trackers.

Both Adin and xQc were blown away by the story, telling MrBeast that is “insane” behavior from those tracking him.

“That is awful,” xQc added.
