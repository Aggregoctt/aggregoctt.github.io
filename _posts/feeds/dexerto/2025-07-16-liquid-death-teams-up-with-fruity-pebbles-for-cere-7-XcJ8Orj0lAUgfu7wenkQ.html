Liquid Death is back with another bizarre beverage, and this time, they’re taking breakfast to a whole new level.

The canned water brand has partnered with Fruity Pebbles to launch a new sparkling water that tastes just like the iconic cereal and milk combo. In fact, Liquid Death says fans won’t be able to tell the difference between ‘Cereal Criminal’ and the popular breakfast.

“These cans of flavored sparkling water taste just like Fruity Pebbles and milk and will murder your thirst with soda-level flavor,” the Amazon listing states.

Liquid Death ups the ante with Fruity Pebbles water

The limited-edition flavor has already begun popping up at select retailers and will be available on Amazon starting Friday, July 18.

This latest drop comes nearly one year after Liquid Death teamed with Van Leeuwen for a Hot Fudge Sundae water, a surprisingly solid hit that showed dessert-flavored hydration can work.

Unconventional pairings are all the rage lately. Just recently, Oreo and Coca-Cola dropped crossover flavors that included Oreo Coca-Cola Zero and Coca-Cola-flavored Oreo cookies.

Plus, earlier this summer, Cinnamon Toast Crunch revealed a new bacon-flavored breakfast cereal.

Liquid Death has a history of pushing boundaries beyond just taste. Their past collabs include the Pit Diaper – a wearable adult diaper made in partnership with Depend for concertgoers who don’t want to miss a beat.

Now with cereal milk water on shelves, it’s clear: no flavor is too weird when it comes to Liquid Death.
