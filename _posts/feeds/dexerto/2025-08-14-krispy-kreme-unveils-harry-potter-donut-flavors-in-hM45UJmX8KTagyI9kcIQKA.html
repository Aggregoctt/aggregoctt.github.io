Krispy Kreme is bringing customers into the Wizarding World with five special Harry Potter-themed donuts and a latte, but only for a short time.

Harry Potter is a franchise that has remained popular for over two decades, and with HBO creating a new TV show for the beloved books, the HP hype is bigger than ever.

Krispy Kreme is cashing in on the excitement with a special, limited-time collaboration with the series that Potterheads won’t want to miss.

What flavors are in the Krispy Kreme x Harry Potter collaboration?

The Harry Potter x Krispy Kreme collab features five new donuts, each with a lavish, savory flavor profile unique to each House — and the Sorting Hat, of course.

Each donut bears an edible decoration of their house symbol, as well as other crafty designs to compliment the theme of each faction at Hogwarts.

Gryffindor Donut

Topped with red icing and Biscoff cookie crumble, the Gryffindor house donut is filled with “luscious cookie butter flavored Kreme.” If you’re a fan of Biscoff, this is the donut for you!

Slytherin Donut

The Slytherin donut is a regular glazed donut topped with chocolate icing and a green buttercream swirl. Slytherins can sink their teeth into the crunchy, chocolate cookie sugar blend atop the chocolate frosting for an extra-decadent experience.

Hufflepuff Donut

Hufflepuffs, rejoice! This tasty treat is filled with brown butter toffee flavored custard and topped with yellow icing, chocolate drizzle and a crunchy cookie topping. All it needs is a Butterbeer to wash it down.

Ravenclaw Donut

The Ravenclaw house donut offers a refreshing, fruity alternative to its siblings on the menu with blueberry-flavored icing and blue sprinkles — the perfect study snack.

Sorting Hat Donut

The Sorting Hat has also gotten its very own donut in this Krispy Kreme collaboration. What makes it special is that each Sorting Hat donut is filled with a mystery House color, effectively ‘sorting’ you into a house when you bite into it.

Topped with chocolate frosting, gold stars and shimmer sugar, the Sorting Hat will help you make a decision if you can’t choose which house flavor to gobble down.

Golden Snitch Latte

Alongside these five donuts is the Golden Snitch Latte. Infused with a caramel-toffee flavor, it’s topped with whipped cream, Biscoff cookie crumble, and golden shimmer sugar.

How long will Harry Potter donuts be at Krispy Kreme?

Krispy Kreme’s Harry Potter collaboration will last from August 18 through September 14, just in time to usher in spooky season. What’s more, they’re available in shop, drive thru and online for pickup and delivery.
