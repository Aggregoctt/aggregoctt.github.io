What are Jujutsu Zero codes used for?

Jujutsu Zero codes are mainly used to earn Lumens, clan rolls, and XP boosts, all of which directly impact progression. Lumens are spent on upgrades and progression systems, while clan rolls give you more chances to land stronger clans. XP boosts help speed up leveling, making it easier to keep up as combat difficulty increases.

This is especially useful before domain-heavy fights, where weak clans or under-leveled setups get punished almost immediately.
