TikToker and Instagram star Big John has shared a health update revealing he has lost more than 40 lbs, or 20kg, while working with his doctor to manage his weight.

The content creator, who uploads videos under BoshTV and Boshtokk on Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok, is best known for food and comedy-related content, often featuring his son, professional boxer Johnny Fisher.

In an Instagram reel shared with his followers, John Fisher said he had just come from an appointment with his doctor in Essex, explaining that his doctor is actively helping with and monitoring his weight loss.

Big John said he had lost more than 20kg, or 3.2 stone, and plans to continue losing more.

In the video, Big John said his GP had been closely tracking his progress as part of the process. “He’s helping me with my weight loss. He’s monitoring my weight, and now I’ve lost over 20kg, 3.2 stone down,” he said in the clip.

He added that his goal is to continue losing weight, saying: “Onwards and upwards, here’s to the next 3.2 stone. Let’s have it, they want me to lose weight.”

John didn’t say if he plans to lose additional weight beyond what was stated in the video, or what regimen he had been following.

Fisher has been sharing photos of his changing body on Instagram and Twitter, often using AI to show him with a much slimmer figure. He still, though, enjoys a sizable order from his local Chinese takeaway, and looks to find his favorite foods while touring the world, which he is currently doing in Australia.
