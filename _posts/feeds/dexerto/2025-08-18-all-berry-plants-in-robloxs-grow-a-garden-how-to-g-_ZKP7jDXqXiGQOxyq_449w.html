Grow a Garden’s latest event makes you submit various plant types to grow a massive beanstalk in the middle of the map. But what will you need when he asks for Berry plants?

The popular Roblox game has players planting various types of flowers and foods so you can sell them, sometimes for tons of money, but the new event changes things up from last week’s cooking adventure.

With the Beanstalk event, a new NPC named Jack asks players for a certain type of plant to grow a beanstalk in the middle of the server. The requested type changes each hour, and the list of available plants is massive.

Here’s all you need to know about Berry plants in Grow a Garden.

All Berry Plants in Grow a Garden

There are currently 9 different Berry Plants we’ve uncovered in Grow a Garden. Each will be accepted by Jack. As we find more, we’ll add to the table below, so check back soon.

What are Berry plants?

Grow a Garden’s Berry Plants are exactly what they sound like – they’re berries that grow on a plant. All berries that you come across in Grow a Garden will be part of the berry plant category that the NPC is asking for, but they have different rarities and are worth different points.

The aim is to get the most points, so that the beanstalk will grow and give you rewards as you climb the plant up to the gentle giant merchant at the top.

How to get Berry plants in Grow a Garden

Getting Berry plants in Grow a Garden during the Beanstalk event is the same as if you were playing without the event. So make sure you have some money, head over to Sam at the seed shop, and buy anything list above that is in stock.

However, Cranberry, White Mulberry, and Celestiberry are currently unavailable in the game as they were limited to previous events in Grow a Garden.
