Twitch streamer and FaZe member StableRonaldo revealed that he hasn’t been streaming often due to people coming to the FaZe house with weapons and threatening him.

StableRonaldo is a former Fortnite pro and popular Twitch streamer with over 4 million followers. He was inducted into FaZe Clan in 2022 during the org’s viral ‘reboot,’ and has remained as a staple member ever since.

Internet fame isn’t all it’s cracked up to be, though. Like many star streamers and influencers, Ronaldo has faced his fair share of parasocial behavior and even threats from those who know of his online presence.

StableRonaldo explains security fears after armed threats at FaZe house

In fact, it’s because of this that he hasn’t been streaming as often. During a September 2025 broadcast, Ronaldo revealed that people have been showing up to the FaZe House with weapons looking for him, specifically.

“About every other night, around 1 AM, 2AM, midnight — about twice a week, people would come to the house super late, saying they were looking for me. They had weapons. They had guns,” he told his viewers.

He went on to explain that he has hired a private security team for himself, and has even contacted local authorities with photos of the trespassers.

“It wasn’t kids, it was like older people,” he continued. “By the way, we have photos of them. The police know, etc. I also have 24/7 security on me, privately, for the next couple months, minimum. We have more security at the house.

“It kinda just spooked me. I don’t know how to talk about this, because I don’t want to give it attention, but I also don’t want to lie to you guys… I was just very scared. I was scared to IRL stream.”

Ronaldo isn’t the only streamer who’s run into such a scary situation. Plenty of broadcasters have feared for their safety due to similar ordeals, such as Amouranth, whose home was broken into by a trio of armed thieves in March 2025 while she and her husband were inside.
