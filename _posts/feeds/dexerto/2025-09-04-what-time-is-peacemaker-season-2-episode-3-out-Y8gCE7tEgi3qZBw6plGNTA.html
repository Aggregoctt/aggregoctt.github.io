Peacemaker Season 2 is about to get a lot crazier, with Episode 3 set to pick up with Christopher Smith back in the alternate dimension – and there could be another familiar face.

Last week’s Peacemaker was pretty wild, all things considered. It started with Chris and Vigilante cutting up and disposing alt-Peacemaker’s body, before Chris and Harcourt had an awkward, upsetting chat at a party, and it ended with him returning to his dead alternate self’s home.

However, he may not like everything he finds in the other dimension. The new episode is titled ‘Another Rick Up My Sleeve’, and several fans have predicted that Rick Flag Jr could show up; what if he’s the “jarhead” that the other Harcourt is dating?

Either way, James Gunn has highlighted Episode 3 as a personal favorite – so make sure you know when it drops.

Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 2 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Thursday, September 4.

Unfortunately, if you’re not based in or around the US, you’ll probably have to stay up late or watch it as soon as you wake up (if not, you’ll need to mute everything on your social media platforms). You can find out what time it’s airing in other time zones below:

How many episodes of Peacemaker Season 2 are there?

There are eight episodes in Peacemaker Season 2.

One episode is being released per week, with Gunn telling his social media followers that “the anticipation and conversation around dropping an episode week to week is part of the fun.”

What happened in Peacemaker Season 2 Episode 2?

After accidentally killing the Peacemaker from the alternate dimension, Season 2 Episode 2 starts with Chris and Vigilante dismembering and disposing of the body.

Later in the episode, the 11th Street Kids reunite for a party on Harcourt’s rooftop. They reminisce, drink a lot of beer, and Chris has a chat with Harcourt that quickly turns bitter (by no fault of his own). So, he gets drunk, goes home, and returns to the alternate dimension – where he texts the other Harcourt.

Meanwhile, Langston Fleury leads a raid on Chris’ home to find his Quantum Unfolding Chamber, but they’re attacked by Eagly, who leaves them all bruised, cut up, and unconscious.
