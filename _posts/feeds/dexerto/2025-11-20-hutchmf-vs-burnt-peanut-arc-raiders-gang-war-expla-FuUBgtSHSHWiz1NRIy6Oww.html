A gang war has erupted in Arc Raiders, and some of the biggest streamers around are involved in the battle. But what exactly is going on?

If you’ve been on social media in the last few days, you’ve probably seen a feud break out between HutchMF and The Burnt Peanut in Arc Raiders. The two are assembling teams of streamers, trash-talking, and fighting for control of Speranza.

So, here’s a breakdown of what’s going on, who’s involved, and how you can pick a side in the Battle of the Bunguraiders.

How did Arc Raiders Battle of the Bunguraiders begin?

It all began when HutchMF declared war on Burnt Peanut, after he was mercifully killed in Arc Raiders by a player, despite his pleas for mercy. Before he was executed, his attacker said, “For the Bungaltors,” in reference to Burnt Peanut’s nickname for himself: The Bungaltor.

Hutch shared the clip on X, announcing: “THIS MEANS WAR!!”

The next day, Burnt Peanut responded, posting: “If it’s war with the bungulators you want, then it’s war you’ll get.”

Now, both streamers are rallying their troops and looking for support from fellow creators and their communities.

Meanwhile, xQc is also involved, although he is currently staying more neutral, and there’s a mutual agreement in place with HutchMF’s team not to attack each other. Check out the video below to see exactly how the teams took shape.

Map

As part of the battle, the Arc Raiders map has been divided into different realms controlled by different streamers.

The northern areas, named The Realm of Bungulators and Mythic Lands, belong to The Burnt Peanut and his crew, while most of the south is owned by HutchMF.

When does HutchMF vs Burnt Peanut

All of this is set to come to a head on Saturday, November 22, 2025, when the teams are due to meet on the Spaceport map for the final showdown. The two have agreed on a time of 5pm PT / 7pm CT / 8pm ET / 1am GMT, when the fireworks will begin.

Between now and then, don’t be surprised to see even more familiar faces join the war effort, and keep an eye out for anyone wearing each team’s colors while playing Arc Raiders.

Only time will tell who’s going to come out on top, and we’ll be keeping this page updated with all the latest from the frontlines in the Battle of the Bunguraiders.
