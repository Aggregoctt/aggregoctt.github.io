Fortnite fans spotted new leaks hinting at a surprising KPop Demon Hunters crossover, with claims it could be arriving soon.

Fortnite has built its identity on collaborations. From Marvel to anime hits, Epic pulled in global icons. Animated crossovers always hit hardest with players. Naruto, Dragon Ball, and Jujutsu Kaisen dropped with skins, emotes, and challenges.

Epic has always timed collabs around peak hype, whether a show was airing or an album was topping charts. Fans got something recognizable, shareable, and instantly trending. This is how Fortnite built momentum. The game kept itself relevant by attaching to what’s hot.

Fortnite tipped for KPop Demon Hunters collaboration from insider leak

Leaks shared by Hypex and Shiina now point at Demon Hunters. A Korean music and animation crossover like Demon Hunters fits exactly with how Epic has handled past collabs.

The leaks come from a “reliable source,” who revealed Fortnite was gearing up for a Demon Hunters collaboration. Leakers said, “The collab is coming THIS SEASON, expect more info in the upcoming week/weeks!”

Nothing official dropped from Epic yet, but the leak followed the same pattern as past collabs that turned out real.

The leak didn’t outline exact bundles, but that didn’t stop players from speculating. A soda pop emote, tiger or bird back blings, maybe themed pickaxes.

Fans pointed to Demon Hunters’ music videos, saying their flashy choreography could translate into in-game emotes. Others guessed at three character skins based on the main lineup.

Reactions came fast. One player said, “3 skins, tiger & bird backbling & 5 emotes please & thank you!” Another wrote, “WE HAVE WON.” A third demanded, “Better have a Soda Pop emote.” The hype spread across Reddit and X. Even without confirmation, fans were already setting aside V-Bucks just in case.

The next Fortnite update is coming on the third week of September, so it’s likely dataminers will have access to more data shortly.
