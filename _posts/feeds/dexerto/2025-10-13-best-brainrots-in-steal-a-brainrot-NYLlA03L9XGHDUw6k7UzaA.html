Roblox Steal a Brainrot’s roster has grown into a surreal playground of memes, mash-ups, and creatures that look like they escaped an art-school fever dream — so knowing which of the 150+ brainrots are best is essential to get started.

Some are glorious, while others are barely held together by pixels and hope. Below is the updated Steal a Brainrots tier list based on design, rarity, and how much pure joy they bring to the screen in 2025.

Steal a Brainrot tier list

Our tiers explained

  • S Tier: The rarest and most expensive Brainrots with top money-per-second output and standout designs.
  • A Tier: High-value Brainrots that mix strong MPS with great rarity, just below S-tier exclusives.
  • B Tier: Mid-range options offering decent MPS for their cost, reliable but common.
  • C Tier: Low-value Brainrots collected more for novelty than performance.
  • D Tier: Cheap starters with minimal MPS and little long-term value.
