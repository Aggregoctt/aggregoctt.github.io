Arby’s is giving steak lovers a new way to enjoy their favorite cut on the go. The fast-food chain announced the launch of its new Steak Nuggets, a “first-of-its-kind” menu item that combines bite-sized convenience with premium steak flavor.

According to Arby’s, the nuggets are made from hand-cut pieces of tender steak seasoned with garlic and pepper for a bold, smoky taste. The brand described the new product as offering “a whole new way to nugget.”

“Who doesn’t love tender and savory steak?” said Arby’s Chief Marketing Officer Jeff Baker. “But, until now, it’s been inconvenient to eat, particularly on the go. That’s why we created this new innovative product. Two of America’s favorite foods – nuggets and steak – combined to create Arby’s Steak Nuggets. No knife needed, fork optional.”

Three ways to order

Customers can try the new Steak Nuggets in several variations:

  • As a 5 or 9-piece entree with Hickory BBQ sauce
  • In a Steak Nugget Sandwich featuring Havarti cheese, crispy onions, tangy pickles, and mayo on a toasted bun
  • A Steak Nugget Bowl served over creamy white cheddar mac and cheese, topped with crispy onions

Arby’s has not yet confirmed how long the Steak Nuggets will remain on the menu, but the chain says they offer “a whole new way to enjoy steak” in a quick-service format.

The Steak Nuggets are also positioned as a high-protein alternative to traditional fast-food items, giving customers an option that fits both a quick meal and a more balanced diet.
