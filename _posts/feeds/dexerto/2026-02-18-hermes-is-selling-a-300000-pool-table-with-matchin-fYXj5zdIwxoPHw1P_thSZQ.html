Hermès is selling a pool table for $296,700, easily the most expensive way to sink an eight ball.

The French luxury house, best known for its Birkin bags and silk scarves, has listed an American-style pool table for just shy of $300,000. At $296,700, it costs more than some houses.

The 8-foot table is made in France and features lacquered, curved legs and rails, a cabinet and pockets wrapped in Regate taurillon leather. The bed cloth is a 90% wool and 10% nylon blend.

Even the accessories lean into the luxury angle. The table comes with four cue sticks made from maple wood with leather wraps and polished hornbeam shafts. You can buy a spare stick for $5,100 if you need it.

There is also a bakelite rack finished with lacquered paint and leather, plus billiard chalk covered in leather and a branded Hermès cover.

Hermès’ most expensive home items

Hermès describes the table as having an archetypal shape, with matte leather set against glossy lacquer for what it calls a strong and timeless presence. In practical terms, it means your trick shots come with matching leather stitching and polished finishes.

The price tag is high, but it fits into the brand’s wider home collection. Hermès also sells a backgammon board for $27,900, a chess and checkers games table for $130,400, a lamp priced at $21,700 and a foldable chair listed at $16,000.

Founded in 1837 as a harness workshop in Paris, Hermès built its reputation on leather craftsmanship before expanding into fashion and homeware. The pool table is the latest example of the brand turning everyday objects into high-end design pieces.

For anyone with a spare $296,700 and a large enough room, it is one way to make sure game night never looks ordinary.
