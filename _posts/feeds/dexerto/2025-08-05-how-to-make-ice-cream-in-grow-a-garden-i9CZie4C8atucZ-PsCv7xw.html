Grow a Garden’s cooking event has been a huge hit for the already massive Roblox adventure. During this time players can cook up some delicious meals, like Ice Cream.

Cooking and gardening kind of go hand in hand, especially in Grow a Garden, where the majority of your plants are ingredients. As such, it makes sense to see Grow a Garden adding in a cooking event, and it makes even more sense to see it so popular.

So, if you’re enjoying the event and want to whip up an Ice Cream, here’s all you need to know, including its ingredients, and no, there’s no milk involved…somehow.

How to make Ice Cream in Grow a Garden

Thankfully, once you know the ingredients, cooking an Ice Cream in Grow a Garden isn’t that tricky; all you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Go to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat in the middle of the world.
  • When he tells you to cook Ice Cream, you can continue.
  • Gather the ingredients above and put them into the cauldron next to Chris.
  • Press the big green Cook button.

If you’ve used the right ingredients, Chris will offer you a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth using the best meals you can.
