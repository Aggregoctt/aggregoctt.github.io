Streaming star IShowSpeed has become one of IMDB’s biggest names now too, overtaking Leonardo DiCaprio and other big actors in the process. 

Over the years, a number of content creators have stepped out of their YouTube and Twitch spheres and jumped into acting. This has seen a few appear on TV shows, in movies, and even in commercials as well. 

Getting into TV and movies will, naturally, land you on IMDB. However, IShowSpeed has taken a more unconventional route to being mentioned on the TV and movies database. 

On October 21, his fans listed his iconic Early Stream broadcast a new show on there. It quickly leaped to become the highest-rated piece of content on IMDB, outpacing the likes of Breaking Bad, Game of Thrones, and The Sopranos. 

IShowSpeed jumps into IMDB’s top 5 most popular celebs

Fans have followed that up by listing a number of his other popular broadcasts too, even filling his IMDB profile with different streamer awards that he’s won over the years. 

Well, that has seen him rise to become the fourth most popular celebrity on IMDB, leapfrogging Leonardo DiCaprio, Glen Powell, Jeremy Allen White, and Keira Knightley. 

Speed does sit behind Charlie Hunnam – who is all over the internet for his role as Ed Gein -, Rebecca Ferguson, and McKenna Grace. So, he’s in good company, even if it might be short-lived. 

As noted, his ‘Early Stream’ being listed kick-started the attention for him, but that has since been removed from IMDB. A number of Speed’s collaborations with Kai Cenat, The Sidemen, and WWE have replaced it, and his music videos are also mentioned on there as well. 

It remains to be seen if the YouTube streamer will continue to have such popularity on IMDB. But, for now at least, he is making waves.
