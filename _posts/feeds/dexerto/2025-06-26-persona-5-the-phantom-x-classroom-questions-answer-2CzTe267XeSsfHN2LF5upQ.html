As it goes with most Persona games, you’ll have to answer a few tricky classroom questions, and Persona 5: The Phantom X is no different.

When you’re not in intense battles, pulling on the game’s banner, redeeming codes, or just grabbing as many characters as you can, you’re likely sitting in school. Look, no one said being a hero meant you could give up on your education.

Persona 5: The Phantom X carries on from the traditional Persona format, bringing some unreasonably tricky classroom questions for you to answer. So, if you’re not one for studying, here’s the cheat sheet, and whether you’ll get any rewards.

Persona 5: The Phantom X classroom answers

Below are all the current questions and answers we’ve uncovered in Person 5: The Phantom X. When we go back to school (shudder) and get more questions, we’ll be updating this table, so be sure to check back soon.

If you’re looking for a specific question, then type it into the question filter and it’ll bring up the answer for you.

Do you get any rewards for the classroom questions?

Unfortunately, whether you studied or cheated, you don’t get any rewards for answering the classroom questions correctly.

Though you do get the praise of your teacher, which should be enough to keep you going (it’s not, I know we all want free items).

It’s worth noting that, since there aren’t any rewards and the questions are pretty tricky, you can simply skip past the feature with no repercussions. Well, aside from not seeing your teacher proud of you.
