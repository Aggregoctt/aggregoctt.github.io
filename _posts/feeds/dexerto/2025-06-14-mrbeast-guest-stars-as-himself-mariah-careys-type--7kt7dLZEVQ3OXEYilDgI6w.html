Mariah Carey is back, and she’s brought an unexpected guest with her upon the release of her song ‘Type Dangerous’. MrBeast showed up at the end, starring as the most “dangerous” man of all.

The song itself released a week before the music video, garnering over a million views on YouTube ahead of the official music video. However, from the lyrics of the song, no one would have expected MrBeast to show up.

He’s been working on his physique for the past few years to get himself in better shape, and its paid off. MrBeast didn’t look too out of place in a music video about “dangerous” boys.

MrBeast guest stars in Mariah Carey video as “Mr. Beast”

The core storyline of the video and song is that Mariah Carey “likes ’em dangerous”, with each part of the video having a “Mr” something. Whether it be a player, dancer, or dealer, the lyrics go through a list of men she had a flirtatious rivalry with.

And, at the very end, the final “Mr” was MrBeast himself. Arguably the most famous Mr in the world, he has a brief cameo with the same sort of editing that the other men in the video got, framing him as “her type”.

However, she shrugged him off, saying “I don’t know him,” resulting in MrBeast exploding into a cloud of money mere seconds after he shows up on screen.

With the release of this video, we now know where the viral images of “Hood MrBeast” look came from.
