Netflix’s Physical: Asia is the latest gruelling reality competition series from the Physical: 100 team, and these are all the players in the cast who are battling it out.

The spinoff show includes teams from Japan, Korea, Thailand, Indonesia, Turkey, Mongolia, Philippines, and Australia.

Physical: Asia pivots to focus on teams from across the continent as world-class athletes take on bicep-bursting battles to prove which is the strongest country in the competition. If that wasn’t enough, they’re also competing for ₩1 billion Korean won, which is roughly $700,000.

The roster includes previous Physical: 100 players, as well as professional boxer Manny Pacquiao. So you know who’s who, here’s every contestant in Netflix’s Physical: Asia.
