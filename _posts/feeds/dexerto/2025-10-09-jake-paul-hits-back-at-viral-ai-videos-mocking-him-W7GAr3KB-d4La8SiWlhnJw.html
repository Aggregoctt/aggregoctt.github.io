Jake Paul has mocked some of the super viral AI-generated videos that people have been making with him as the main character, claiming he will “sue everyone.”

Back on September 30, OpenAI finally released their Sora app to the public, alongside the Sora 2 video generator. While the app, which works a little bit like TikTok, is still invite-only, thousands of AI-generated videos have been going viral on the back of it. 

People have been putting themselves and historical figures inside WWE, famous TV shows, and even popular video games. Jake Paul has, also, been a regular target for some Sora users. 

There have been AI-generated clips of the YouTuber-turned-boxer doing makeup routines, fighting in hospitals, and even complaining about the results of his fights. 

Jake Paul mocks viral AI videos of himself

Well, on October 8, the ‘Problem Child’ decided to hit back at some of the AI clips of himself by openly mocking them. 

“I’ve had it with the AI stuff. It’s affecting my relationships and businesses. People are hiting me up saying ‘yo, did you say this? What did you do this for?’” he said on Instagram while applying makeup to his face.

“It’s really affecting things and, honestly, its like people need to get a life. It’s kind of pissing me off, I’m going to be suing everybody that is continuing to spread these false narratives of me doing shit that I would literally never ever do! So, be ready for lawsuits.”

A number of viewers found the funny side of Jake’s post. “Bro doesn’t even know what makeup he’s putting on,” one said. “Bro, I’m crying.”
