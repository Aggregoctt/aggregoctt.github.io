GTA Online is getting a new update in the form of Money Fronts, which will allow you to own some new businesses and vehicles. Here’s what you need to know.

The Grand Theft Auto community is desperately awaiting the release of GTA 6. However, that wait will go on until May 26, 2026, seeing as Rockstar Games has delayed the game for a few more months. 

GTA Online, however, is still chock-full of new updates and content. In the last few months, Rockstar has released Bottom Dollar Bounties, Agents of Sabotage, and Oscar Guzmann Flies Again.

On June 17, they will release another in the form of Money Fronts. The new update will allow players to purchase some iconic Los Santos businesses for their money laundering operations, as well as new vehicles. 

All new vehicles in GTA Online Money Fronts

New vehicles are always the most exciting part of new updates. This has been evident in the last few updates with the release of expensive police-themed cars and motorbikes.

There will be another added to the mix in Money Fronts in the form of the Western Police Bike and the Summer Highway Patrol Outfit to match.

Four other cars have been announced for the new update, including the Karin Everon RS, Woodlander SUVs, the Declasse Tampa GT, and Överflöd Suzume. The Suzume will be exclusive to GTA+ Members at first, however. 

No prices have yet been revealed for these new cars, but just make sure your Maze Bank account is topped up.

All new businesses in GTA Online Money Fronts

As noted, the businesses that you’ll be able to buy will be instantly recognizable to anyone who played the GTA V story. 

The Hands On Car Wash in Strawberry, Smoke on the Water dispensary, and Higgins Helitours will all be purchasable in the new update.

Smoke on the Water dispensary and Higgins Helitours will boost earnings on your Weed Farm and Air Freight Cargo, while Hands On Car Wash will be a passive boost to your criminal empire as a whole.

Again, no prices for these businesses have been mentioned, but it’s safe to assume they’ll cost around $1,000,000 each – if they’re subject to upgrades.

We don’t have to long to wait, though, as the new update drops on June 17.
