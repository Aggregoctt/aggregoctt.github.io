After the second episode delivered a grisly cliffhanger, horror fans are understandably keen to find out what time It: Welcome to Derry Episode 3 is out.

The show is expanding the mythology behind the creature that calls itself Pennywise, and the second episode even hinted that the show could tie into another classic Stephen King movie. That’s without mentioning the horrific sequence where Ronnie (Amanda Christine) has to relive her own birth…

After bringing the Loser’s Club to life in two movies, director Andy Muschietti is determined to widen the world of King’s original novel by exploring the other times that Pennywise has tormented the town.

The end of the second episode saw the military dig up an old 1930s-era car filled with skeletons, providing audiences with a new mystery that will likely set up Season 2. For those desperate to find out what’s going on, here’s what time It: Welcome to Derry Episode 3 is out.

It: Welcome to Derry Episode 3 will be available to stream on HBO Max at 6pm PT/9pm ET on Sunday, November 9. Yes, unfortunately fans will have to wait longer for the third episode because the streamer dropped Episode 2 a few days early to celebrate Halloween.

Audiences that don’t live in the US will need to stay up late to watch It: Welcome to Derry Episode 3, or they’ll have to catch up the next day and try to avoid spoilers. Since the next episode will likely answer some questions about the car of skeletons, fans will need to be vigilant about dodging spoilers on social media.

Here’s what time it airs at in other timezones:

  • 10pm Brazil
  • 2am UK
  • 3am Central European Summer Time
  • 4am Eastern European Summer Time
  • 6.30am India
  • 11am Australia
  • 1pm New Zealand

UK audiences will be able to stream It: Welcome to Derry Episode 3 on Sky Max and NowTV live, and there will be a repeat showing at 9pm on Monday, November 10.

How many episodes are there in It: Welcome to Derry Season 1 ?

There are eight episodes in It: Welcome to Derry Season 1, and if it’s successful enough then the series will roll back the clock for its sophomore season.

Muschietti explained the show’s three season plan in an interview with Deadline, saying: “The second season will be in 1935. At the end of Season 1, we are hinting at the reason why we are going to tell the story in two more seasons and backwards.”
