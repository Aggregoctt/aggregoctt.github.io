Grow a Garden‘s cooking event is here, bringing tons of exciting rewards to the Roblox gardening adventure. However, you’ll have to guess the ingredients for meals like the Sandwich, unless you find the recipe.

Whether it’s celebrating Summer, Bees, or the 4th of July, Grow a Garden isn’t a stranger to a good event, and the Cooking event is no different. Spanning from August 2 to August 16, players can bring the brilliantly named Chris P. Bacon his craved meal and bag tons of exciting rewards to help them make the most money possible.

However, it’s not that easy when you don’t have the ingredients. So, if Chris is craving a Sandwich and you don’t want to waste any of your hard-grown crops, here’s all you need to know about how to make a Sandwich in Grow a Garden.

Sandwich recipe

No matter the rarity of your dish, the cook time will be five minutes.

How to make a Sandwich in Grow a Garden

Once you have the ingredients, making a Sandwich in Grow a Garden isn’t that tricky; all you need to do is follow the steps below:

  • Go to Chris P. Bacon, the pig in a chef’s hat.
  • Head over to the steel pot.
  • Select your ingredients in your inventory and place them inside.
  • Press the big green Cook button.

If you’ve used the right ingredients and satisfied Chris’ craving, he’ll offer you a reward. The level of reward depends on the rarity of the dish you create, so it’s worth using the best meals you can. You can check out all the available rewards in our guide.

It’s worth noting, you can cook a Sandwich at any time in Grow a Garden, but save it for when Chris is craving it before you offer the dish.
