What happened in Season 2 Episode 6?

The Pitt Season 2 Episode 6 deals with the immediate aftermath of Louie Cloverfield’s collapse, and despite Dr. Robby and Dr. Langdon’s efforts, he dies early in the hour. The loss hits the department hard, even though the Fourth of July shift continues without pause.

The episode opens with Robby and Langdon attempting to stabilize Louie following the emergency at the end of Season 2 Episode 5. CPR and resuscitation efforts fail, and he is pronounced dead. 

His passing affects several members of staff who have treated him multiple times over the years. Emma assists Dana with post-mortem care, marking another difficult learning moment during her first major shift.

Elsewhere, the ER remains overwhelmed due to the nearby hospital closure, with diverted patients continuing to flood in. Robby and Al-Hashimi continue to clash over treatment decisions, particularly involving a patient from prison who’s presenting with signs of severe malnutrition. 

Although tensions between Langdon and Robby aren’t fully resolved, a touching moment sees Langdon making amends to Dana, apologizing to her for his actions. “You can check me off your list, kid. We’re good,” she replies. 

By the end of the episode, the department has little time to process Louie’s death as new emergencies arrive, reinforcing the real-time structure of the season.

In Episode 7, the pressure is set to escalate further. Dana will be treating a sexual assault victim, Dr. Jack Abbot is returning to assist with an injured officer, and Mohan will be trying to prevent an uninsured patient from being discharged prematurely.
