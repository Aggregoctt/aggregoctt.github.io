Looking to date the cast of Critical Role or perhaps Joel from The Last of Us has caught your eye? Well, in Date Everything, you can do just that, though they are in the form of furniture. Don’t worry, though; this furniture is unexpectedly attractive.

Date Everything has you putting on some stylish Dateviators and romancing all the items around your house. Naturally, given that the game was created by voice actors, all those 100 unique characters are brought to life by different renowned voice actors.

Think Matthew Mercer, Felicia Day, Ben Starr, Ray Chase, Robbie Daymond, Steve Blum, and really anyone you’ve likely heard in your favorite video game.

So, whether you’re looking to know all 100 characters, their voice actors, or how to unlock them, we’ve got all you need.
