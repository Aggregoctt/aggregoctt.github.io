Asmongold is officially 2025’s most-watched streamer in Q2 after deciding to multistream on Twitch and Kick.

In June 2025, Asmongold, along with many other creators, opted to join Kick while co-streaming on other platforms. That decision has paid dividends, as it’s pushed him over the edge to become the top streamer.

According to stats from StreamsCharts, Asmongold was the second most-watched streamer on Twitch from April 1 to June 30, with 26.41M hours watched on the platform.

He trailed only behind League of Legends co-caster Marc ‘Caedrel’ Lamont, who had a whopping 27.87 hours watched while live for over 100 hours more than Asmongold at 669H.

However, it was Asmongold’s decision to multi-stream on Kick that allowed him to topple Caedrel.

Multi-streams push Asmongold ahead as 2025’s most-watched streamer

When factoring in both Kick and Twitch, despite the fact the stats only account for a single month of June when Asmongold started streaming on the Stake-owned platform, he reigns supreme.

His Kick broadcasts pushed him to 28.58M hours watched in Q2 2025. This puts him firmly ahead of North American rivals Hasan, Jynxzi, Kai Cenat Caseoh, and IShowSpeed.

Elsewhere, Twitch and YouTube streamer Gaules had 27.41M hours watched, good enough for third place overall behind Asmongold and Caedrel.

We’ll have to see what Q3 2025 looks like in a few months and if Asmongold can pull away even further with even more time spent streaming on multiple websites simultaneously.

The decision to multistream has already had a big impact on the streamer’s revenue after he made $36,910 in his first two broadcasts.
