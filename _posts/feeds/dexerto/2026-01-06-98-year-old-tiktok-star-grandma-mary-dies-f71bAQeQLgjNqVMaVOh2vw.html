Grandma Mary, a 98-year-old grandmother who shot to fame on TikTok with her positivity in her granddaughter’s videos, has passed away. 

The 98-year-old’s passing was announced by her granddaughter, TikToker itsPyxie, on January 2. The TikToker told her more than 1.4 million followers that Mary had passed away on New Year’s Eve. 

“On New Year’s Eve, we said goodbye to Grandma. She peacefully passed away in her sleep. How lucky we are to have experienced her and her love. She will always be with us,” she said alongside a compilation of her grandmother’s signature “good morning” message. 

The TikToker had regularly included her grandmother in videos, but slowed down towards the end of 2025. Fans regularly quizzed itsPyxie on her grandmother, with the TikToker updating fans as recently as December 19, saying Mary was just being “quieter” and that’s why she hadn’t appeared as much. 

TikTok viewers pay tribute to Grandma Mary

Upon announcing her passing, viewers have paid tribute to the 98-year-old, thanking the TikToker for sharing her grandmother’s “happiness and light” with them over the years. 

“Silent watcher here. I’m so sorry for your loss. She was an amazing person,” one said. “The TikTok community has been so blessed to have gotten to know her. You’re one lucky granddaughter. I will hold her memory dear to my heart,” another said.

“How privileged we were to have know such a joyful woman,” commented another. 

“What a blessing she has been to not only your family, but all of us here on TikTok who have enjoyed watching your videos! Thank you for sharing her with us. Rest in peace Grandma Mary, you are loved and missed!” replied another. 

Some viewers have called Grandma Mary the “best Grandma ever,” and thanked the TikToker for allowing them to feel like family too. 

“Thank you for sharing her sweet soul with us,” commented another fan.
