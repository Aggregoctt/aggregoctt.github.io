Leaks have revealed that a South Park crossover is coming to Fortnite, complete with character skins and a Stick of Truth Mythic weapon that lets you control the storm.

One of the many reasons that Fortnite has managed to maintain its popularity over so many years is the sheer number of popular franchises it can collaborate with. In Chapter 7 alone, we’ve seen the likes of Harry Potter, Pulp Fiction, and even the anime Bleach arrive on the island.

But Epic Games isn’t slowing down any time soon, as leaks have all but confirmed that a South Park crossover is in the works and coming very soon.

Fortnite x South Park crossover coming soon

This collab has actually been rumored for some time, with dataminers claiming in late 2025 that a South Park mini pass could arrive during Winterfest. This turned out to be false, but leaks have since confirmed that while there won’t be a mini pass, there’s plenty more to look forward to.

Back in December, HYPEX shared a post revealing that “multiple” characters will appear as skins, with the likes of Cartman, Kyle, Stan, and Kenny being the most likely. They will reportedly be piloting mechs similar to Morty from Rick and Morty, to account for their smaller size compared to other Fortnite characters.

The event will also bring a Respawn Token item, allowing players to rejoin the action after a death. These will also be granted to all players temporarily during the “Everyone Kills Kenny” Rift Anomaly, in a hilarious nod to one of the show’s seemingly immortal main characters.

Possibly the most interesting part of the crossover, though, is the Stick of Truth Mythic, which a new leak claims will be able to “modify” the storm. It’s not entirely clear how this will work or how much control the user will have, but this could be extremely effective towards the end of matches.

There is currently no official release date for the Fortnite South Park crossover, but the next update is scheduled for January 9, so there’s every chance it could be then. If not, it could be later in the month, but we’ll be sure to keep this page updated with all the latest details.
