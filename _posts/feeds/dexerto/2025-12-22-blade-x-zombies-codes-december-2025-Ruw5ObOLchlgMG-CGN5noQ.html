What are Blade X Zombies codes used for?

Blade Zombies codes mostly give Crystals, which are required for awakening weapons and pushing them past basic upgrade limits.

These awakenings are what turn anime-style blades into real wave-clearing tools once enemies start scaling hard. Codes can also give coins, which cover the everyday upgrades like boosting weapon stats and keeping your build stable between awakenings.
