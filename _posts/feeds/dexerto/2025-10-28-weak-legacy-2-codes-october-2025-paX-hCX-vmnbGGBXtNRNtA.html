How to redeem codes

Redeeming codes in this Roblox game is quite easy. Just follow these simple steps:

  1. Open Weak Legacy 2 in Roblox.
  2. Tap on the Menu button in the bottom left corner or press M on your keyboard.
  3. Select the purple gift icon – the title ‘CODES’ will appear when you do.
  4. On the blank rectangle, put the code you want to use.
  5. Tap ‘Redeem‘.

Remember that codes must be entered exactly as displayed; otherwise, an ‘invalid’ message will appear.

Is there a level requirement to redeem codes?

No, unlike other Roblox games, you don’t have to reach a particular level in order to be able to redeem codes in Weak Legacy 2.
