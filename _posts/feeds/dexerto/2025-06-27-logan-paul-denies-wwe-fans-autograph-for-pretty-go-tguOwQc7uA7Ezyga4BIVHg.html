Logan Paul rejected a WWE fan’s request for an autograph on a ‘rookie’ trading card of his, but he had pretty good reason for doing so.

It’s been a few years since Logan Paul first got involved with the WWE, as his first match happened all the way back in 2022.

Since then, ‘The Maverick’ has become a big star in the professional wrestling world, been involved in a number of high-profile matches, and thrown himself all the way. However, he has only just recently gotten his rookie trading card for his WWE career.

It came during his tag team match with John Cena, with Logan promoting the limited-edition card at the end of his June 11 vlog. However, one fan claimed to have gotten their hands on it already. 

Logan Paul calls out fake WWE card of him

During his appearance at Fanatics Fest, Logan was asked to sign the supposed rookie card by a fan in the crowd, but the YouTube superstar was cautious. 

“I don’t think this is real. How did you get this?” he asked the fan. “How could you have already gotten it? It was only out three days ago. Nah, they didn’t deliver yet. This is a fake!” Logan added, handing the card back.

“This is a fake, there is no way you could have gotten this card. How?! How could you have gotten this card that fast? You’re lying, you’re lying. That’s a fake.”

The imPaulsive host addressed the situation on X (formerly Twitter) too, confirming that it was a bogus card.

“Confirmed with Topps that this was a fake,” he posted. “It would’ve been the very first “Logan Paul Rookie Card” that I autographed & I didn’t want that moment to happen with a counterfeit.”

With Logan being such a big card collector, he’ll likely have one of the early prints of his own card before anyone else.
