December is always a time for reflection across social platforms, and Snapchat is no exception.

Following the likes of Spotify Wrapped and YouTube Recap, Snapchat has officially rolled out its 2025 Snapchat Recap, giving users a personalized look back at how they connected throughout the year.

The Recap arrives as a short, individualized video highlighting a Snapchatter’s Memories, Snaps, Stories, and Chats from across 2025.

Available now, Snapchat Recap offers a snapshot of how users communicated and expressed themselves on the app, with this year’s data pointing to friendship, creativity, and connection as defining themes.

How to see your Snapchat Recap for 2025

Snapchat Recap for 2025 began rolling out on Monday, December 15, but it may take time for some users to get access. Here’s how to see if yours is available:

  • Open Snapchat on your phone
  • Go to the camera, then tap on memories

Your 2025 Snapchat Recap video will be available to watch at the top of the page.

Talking on Snapchat climbed throughout the year, with users globally spending nearly 1.7 billion minutes per day on voice notes, voice calls, and video chats. That figure represents an increase of nearly 30% year over year. In the U.S. alone, Snapchatters sent more than 5 billion voice notes in 2025, marking nearly 10% growth compared to last year.

Visual communication continued to gain momentum in 2025. Chat Reactions increased by 44% over the year, with the heart emoji emerging as the most-used reaction on the platform. Sticker use also grew, with more than 3 million additional users relying on stickers to communicate emotion and context in chats.

Pop culture trends made their way directly into conversations. Bitmoji-inspired hairstyles such as slicked-back buns, broccoli hair, and curtain bangs ranked among the most requested looks of the year. The 6/7 Bitmoji sticker also set a new record, becoming the most-used sticker since its launch.

Group chats remained central to Snapchat activity. The number of users sending messages in group chats grew by more than 5% in 2025, and some Snapchatters sent over 8,880 messages to their most active group chat during the year.
