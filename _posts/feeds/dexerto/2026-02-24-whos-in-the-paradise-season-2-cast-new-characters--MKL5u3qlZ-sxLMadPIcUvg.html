New characters in Hulu series explained ---

The Paradise Season 2 cast has some fresh additions, as the story kicks off by introducing a handful of new characters into Hulu’s post-apocalyptic thriller.

The streamer dropped the first three episodes of Paradise Season 2 at once to ease fans back into the dangerous world outside of the bunker and beyond. However, the show changes things up by telling a completely new story that helps fill in the time after the day the world ended.

The binge-worthy first season ended on a cliffhanger where Xavier Collins (Sterling K. Brown) leaves the safety of the bunker to go and look for his wife Teri (Enuka Okuma) after hearing her voice on a radio transmission from the outside world.

Instead of picking up with that dangling plot thread straight away, the show follows one of the new members of the Paradise Season 2 cast: Shailene Woodley. Yes, the Big Little Lies star is now playing a post-apocalyptic survivor. But here’s who else is in the series.

Who’s in the Paradise Season 2 cast? Shailene Woodley, Thomas Doherty, and more

Shailene Woodley joins the Paradise Season 2 cast as Annie, a medical student dropout who becomes a tour guide at Graceland, the home of Elvis Presley. She’s working there when the disaster first unfolds, and she shelters inside the property for over 2 years.

A whole band of bikers led by Link (Thomas Doherty) then make their way to Graceland, asking Annie to help them rebuild the US. So with that in mind, here’s all the cast of Paradise Season 2:

  • Sterling K. Brown as Special Agent Xavier Collins
  • Julianne Nicholson as Samantha “Sinatra” Redmond
  • Shailene Woodley as Annie
  • Sarah Shahi as Dr. Gabriela Torabi
  • James Marsden as President Cal Bradford
  • Nicole Brydon Bloom as Agent Jane Driscoll
  • Charlie Evans as Jeremy Bradford
  • Enuka Okuma as Teri Collins
  • Thomas Doherty as Link, the leader of a biker gang
  • Timothy Omundson as Chef
  • Michael McGrady as Geiger
  • Patrick Fischler as a Professor
  • Aliyah Mastin as Presley Collins
  • Percy Daggs IV as James Collins
  • Krys Marshall as Agent Nicole Robinson
