Skibidi Toilet creator DaFuqBoom has allegedly lost creative control of the series to Invisible Narratives, according to claims circulating in the community and messages shared by content creator EliteBlueGuy.

The company, led by CEO Adam Goodman, is also accused of taking control of the YouTube channel that publishes new episodes. DaFuqBoom, real name Alexey Gerasimov, is the creator behind the viral Garry’s Mod web series Skibidi Toilet that has generated hundreds of millions of views since 2023.

Gerasimov signed a deal with Invisible Narratives in 2024 to develop official Skibidi Toilet projects, including a planned film. Fans began raising concerns in November 2025 after Discord messages attributed to Gerasimov suggested he was no longer involved in key decisions related to the show.

In these alleged messages, Gerasimov writes that he feels “completely isolated from process,” saying he has not received updates or concept materials for the film.

In screenshots shared by EliteBlueGuy, he wrote, “I have not seen any previsions, were not involved in discussing any details. Just nothing,” and later added, “Something imprecise and blurry was described on the phone about it, I seen bits of script, and that’s it.”

Screenshots claim creator expresses concern over contract and payment

Alongside the creative-control claims, additional screenshots appear to show Gerasimov expressing financial stress. In one exchange, he appears to write, “I can’t. I’m almost broke, dude,” when told he could retire. In another message, he states, “Nothing, I’m just not being paid out anything” when asked why he was struggling financially.

Other messages depict Gerasimov saying he feels “scammed, lied to, paranoid,” and that the situation is “the closest people in business and around that affect the most.” In one February 2025 exchange, he wrote that he was “just this close to crash out,” referencing feeling overwhelmed by ongoing issues.

#bringboomback movement trends on X

EliteBlueGuy has continued posting claims that Invisible Narratives has removed Skibidi Toilet creators from Discord servers, pressured community members, and taken over the YouTube upload pipeline.

He claimed, “[Invisible] was also the one who striked Garrys mod.,” and later said, “They are suppressing the fandom protest and are already using pressure tactics.”

TheGamer reported that some messages attributed to Gerasimov were later deleted at his request, with one screenshot showing him asking a friend to remove earlier posts after reportedly speaking with the company.

In another message shared by EliteBlueGuy, he referenced speaking with “Michael,” though the creator later clarified that Michael Bay is not directly involved and that Invisible Narratives is run by Adam Goodman.

As of now, Invisible Narratives has not publicly commented on the allegations. Gerasimov has not posted any statement on his public channels addressing the circulating screenshots. The #bringboomback hashtag continues trending in the Skibidi Toilet community as fans call for clarity on the creator’s status and involvement.
