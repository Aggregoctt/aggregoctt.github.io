We’ve all been burned before by overhyped, neon-lit, near-future action games that promise the world and deliver a pixelated puddle.

But Mindseye actually looks like it’s got the goods. Directed by former GTA mastermind Leslie Benzies and set in a sandy sci-fi city full of AI drama and neural mystery, it stars Jacob Diaz, ex-soldier and current memory mess.

So what’s the plan once you hit the mean streets of Redrock on June 10, 2025? Here’s a peak breakdown of what to expect at launch and beyond.

Full Mindseye Roadmap for 2025

Launch content

At launch, Mindseye is all about single-player free roam. You’ll get access to a variety of missions and modes, including:

  • Combat missions like Honor Amongst Thieves and Friendly Fire
  • Horde Mode with Destruction Site Shootout
  • Races including Quick Silva, Tickets Please, and more
  • Checkpoint races such as Craz-Eye Taxi and Crosstown Traffic
  • Drone races including Garden Party and Chase the Sun

Premium players get a bonus Horde Mode mission called Attack the Block along with the Exotic Pack, which includes skins for a drone, a vehicle, and Jacob’s vest.

Summer 2025

The first update comes in the Summer 2025, though the date remains a mystery.

  • Special collaboration content
  • New missions
  • Community updates, possibly tied into the Everywhere platform

Fall 2025

The second update comes in the Fall of 2025, though the date remains a mystery.

  • Multiplayer makes its debut
  • New single-player game modes
  • Additional missions to expand the story

Winter 2025

The third update comes in the Winter of 2025, though the date remains a mystery.

  • Another round of special collaboration content
  • Free roam updates to keep the world feeling fresh
  • New missions including Major Adventure and Starstream Universe

Each season also brings extra missions and new packs, so there’s always something new to jump into even after the main story ends.
