A mom has gone viral on social media after watching CaseOh during active labor, claiming it helped ease her anxiety.

Content creator CaseOh is known for his funny, high-energy, and delightfully chaotic streams. Look no further than the wild time he had in his Goob Lagoon virtual park for proof.

But even with all that chaos in mind, some fans actually find him to be a surprisingly calming Twitch streamer.

That was the case for one woman, who shared that she watched CaseOh’s stream during one of the biggest moments of her life: while giving birth.

Mom claims watching CaseOh’s stream took her anxiety away during labor

On October 22, 2025, a user named ‘Ravenlayton_’ posted a picture of herself in a hospital bed on X, saying she might be “the first person ever to watch CaseOh during active labor.”

She explained that while getting an epidural needle placed, she watched CaseOh to help calm her nerves. Amusingly enough, the streamer’s antics indeed helped keep her distracted.

In her own words, she mentioned: “And safe to say, it worked. Thank you, CaseOh.” Since then, the post has gone viral, with many people congratulating her and hoping everything’s going well.

“Imagine telling [your] kid you literally watched CaseOh while you birthed them, that’s actually so awesome,” commented one fan.

Meanwhile, another said: “This is actually a really smart way to distract yourself from the pain of labor, omg.”

Even CaseOh’s mom showed up in the comments, writing: “Hope everything went well and baby is perfect.”

Some users asked if she was the same viewer who once requested a “special message” from CaseOh to play during labor. She clarified that it wasn’t her, but admitted that the clip “would’ve come in handy.”

For those unaware, the clip shows CaseOh repeatedly yelling “PUSH!”, acting as if he’s right there in the delivery room with the viewer who requested it.

The cherry on top? He even went all out and clapped his hands, congratulating them as if they’d just successfully given birth.
