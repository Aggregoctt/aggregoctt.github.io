YouTube star Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson has cleared up viral claims that Kai Cenat wouldn’t join him on stream because of Adin Ross’s presence for his Team Water charity.

Over the last few years, MrBeast has been using his online success to start a number of charitable ventures. He’s used his MrBeast Philanthropy channel to fund life-changing surgeries and free meals, but that’s not all.

Donaldson has also launched a number of efforts with crowdfunding. Team Trees, Team Seas, and Team Water have seen him team up with Mark Rober and other creators to bring in millions to plant trees, clean up the seas, and deliver clean water to those in need. 

Team Water is his latest venture, and MrBeast has even started streaming on Kick to bring more eyes to it. He’s joined Adin Ross and xQc on a few broadcasts already with the pair and Kick donating millions to him. 

MrBeast responds to claim Kai Cenat wouldn’t join stream

However, with Adin’s involvement, there have been claims that MrBeast has been unable to tap Kai Cenat as a potential partner, given the AMP star’s ongoing fallout with Adin. 

During a stream, Jimmy told xQx and Adin that he’d invited streamers to join his stream. One initially said yes but changed their mind when informed that Adin would be involved. Social media, naturally, pointed the finger at Kai. However, MrBeast has cleared that up.

“I never said it was Kai, and to be clear it’s not Kai. Let’s chill with this whole Kai hate boner thing,” he posted on X (formerly Twitter). 

Kai has already been named as a key part of Team Water, and should be involved before too long anyway. 

MrBeast has previously joined him on stream too, and that may happen again here.
