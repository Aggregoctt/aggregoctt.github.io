Squid Game has made it into the streaming Top 10 of 2025 so far, but the might of Netflix has been no match for the dominance of Bluey on Disney+.

Nielsen has released its 2025 viewing figures for streaming, and acquired shows like Grey’s Anatomy, NCIS, and Family Guy continue to post huge numbers.

Bluey sits at the top of the tree, as it usually does, with the animated kid’s show wracking up over 25 million minutes viewed.

Squid Game is the only original show to make the Top 10, pulling in more than 15 billion minutes viewed, in spite of the fact that Season 2 dropped days prior to the six month period concerned, and Season 3 arrived just before the end.

Top 10 most streamed shows of 2025 so far

Here’s the list – as per The Hollywood Reporter – which adds up all minutes viewed between January 1, 2025 and June 30, 2025.

  • Bluey (154 episodes) – 25.059 billion
  • Greys Anatomy (449 episodes) – 22.551 billion
  • NCIS (488 episodes) – 19.117 billion
  • Family Guy (450 episodes) – 19.072 billion
  • Bob’s Burgers (286 episodes) – 17.105 billion
  • Spongebob Squarepants (323 episodes) – 16.682 billion
  • Big Bang Theory (281 episodes) – 16.618 billion
  • The Rookie (126 episodes) – 15.643 billion
  • Squid Game (22 episodes) – 15.074 billion
  • Law & Order: SVU (575 episodes) – 14.524 billion

Biggest ‘Originals’ of the year, including Squid Game and Reacher

The above list combines acquired and original shows, while the following are just the originals, which have far fewer episodes, making it tougher to build up those views.

Netflix dominates, taking three of the top four slots via Squid Game, The Night Agent, and Ginny and Georgia, while Prime Video title Reacher comes in at number two.

Once again, these numbers are minutes viewed:

  • Squid Game (22 episodes) – 15.074 billion
  • Reacher (24 episodes) – 13.313 billion
  • The Night Agent (20 episodes) – 12.219 billion
  • Ginny & Georgia (30 episodes) – 10.201 billion
  • Severance (19 episodes) – 9.275 billion
  • 1923 (15 episodes) – 8.536 billion
  • The Pitt (15 episodes) – 8.227 billion
  • The Handmaid’s Tale (66 episodes) – 8.165 billion
  • You (53 episodes) – 8.097 billion
  • Landman (10 episodes) – 7.826 billion
