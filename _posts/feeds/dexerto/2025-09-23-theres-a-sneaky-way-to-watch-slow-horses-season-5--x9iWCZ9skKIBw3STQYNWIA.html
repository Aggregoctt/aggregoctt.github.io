Get ready for more spies, screw-ups, and Jackson Lamb stinkers, as Slow Horses Season 5 is nearly here. If you want to watch the action, there’s a handful of ways to stream the show for free. 

Not only is Slow Horses one of the best Apple TV+ series, but it also breaks free from television’s worst trend: making fans wait years for new episodes. Sure, good shows take time, but we’ve had five whole seasons of Slough House shenanigans in the gap between Stranger Things Seasons 4 and 5.

What’s even more amazing is that the spy thriller never misses, and with a 100% Rotten Tomatoes score, Season 5 is no different. This time, the story centers on a mystery that unfolds when, somehow, some way, Roddy Ho (Christopher Chung) gets a girlfriend. 

Lamb (Gary Oldman) is suitably suspicious, as are the rest of the gang. If you want to tune in, you’ll need an Apple TV+ subscription. 

How to watch Slow Horses Season 5 for free 

The good news is that, unlike Netflix, Apple TV+ offers new and eligible users a free trial. You can get seven-days for free if you sign up directly, which will cover you for the first couple of episodes. 

But if you’ve recently bought a new Apple device such as an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple TV from the tech company or an authorized seller, you can get a free trial that ranges from three months to an entire year.

All you need to do is sign into the Apple TV app using the account associated with your recent purchase and the offer will automatically appear. You’ll need to claim it within 90 days of your purchase. 

New users can also get a free trial of the streaming service by signing up for the Apple One bundle, which includes Apple Music, Apple Arcade, and other services. 

If you’re a T-Mobile customer in the US on an Experience Beyond or Experience More plan, you may not know that you could be eligible for a six-month free trial. All you need to do is login to your T-Mobile account and, once verified, you’ll be redirected to create your Apple TV+ account. 

It even works if you already have an account with the streamer. Once the Apple TV+ On Us promo is redeemed, your paid subscription will pause and the free version will take over. You can find out more about how to redeem this deal here. 

Best Buy also offers a two-month free Apple TV+ trial for qualifying purchases made, which you can learn more about here. 

If you love TV but can’t break the bank, it’s well worth keeping an eye out for deals, as Apple TV+ is home to some of the best shows on the market. 

Severance, The Morning Show, Foundation, Ted Lasso, Silo, Presumed Innocent, and, of course, Slow Horses are just a few of the titles the streamer has to offer.
