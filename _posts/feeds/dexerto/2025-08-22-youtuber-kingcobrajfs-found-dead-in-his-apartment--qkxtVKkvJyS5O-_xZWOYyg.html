KingCobra‘s YouTube following was a lot bigger than his 116k YouTube subscriber count would have you believe. The lore around this YouTuber goes far beyond the scope of just his channel, as his escapades over the years earned him a level of notoriety that had people studying his life and making full-length documentaries about it.

He would also livestream, often very drunk, and talk back and forth with his viewers. Some people were there to hate on him, while others were there to genuinely support Josh.

After a few days of silence, KingCobraJFS’s father uploaded and promptly deleted a video from his channel speaking about the death of his son.

KingCobraJFS found unresponsive by his father

Rumors of Josh’s death began circulating in the hours before the video was uploaded, with his father going live to put a stop to them and say what really happened.

His father confirmed his death. He had planned to take Josh to the doctor and check on his health, but he was found unresponsive.

And, while doctors believe KingCobra died in his sleep rather than it being an attempt at his own life, Josh’s father was still very emotional when recounting how his son was treated on the internet and otherwise.

“I got on today to delete all of his social media… because ‘f**k you’ is how I’m feeling. But I realized that would not be fair to his fans, and it’s certainly nothing he would want.
