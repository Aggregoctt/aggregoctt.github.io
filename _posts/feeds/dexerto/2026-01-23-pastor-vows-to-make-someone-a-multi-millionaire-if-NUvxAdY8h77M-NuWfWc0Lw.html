A Pastor has gone viral on social media after asking one of his congregants to donate $10,000 in order to become a millionaire, as the video has resurfaced. 

Donating to a charitable cause is something that everyone aims to do at some point. It might only be a small donation, but it can help in a big way for someone else. 

In the case of Pastor Dr Todd Hall, he was asking for a pretty big donation in the form of $10,000. The catch? Whoever was able to seed that massive bundle of cash would receive even more. 

He made that promise back in July at the ADR Prophetic Conference 2025. However, the clip has resurfaced in the early part of 2026, becoming a viral talking point on social media, with some uploads getting well over 3 million views.

Pastor asks for $10k in 90 seconds in viral video

Hall called the money the “hard part” of his “request,” adding that the last time he’d made the request was “probably 20 years” ago. 

“The Lord said, I want to make a multi, multi-millionaire out of someone but they have to sow $10,000. He said and they have to do that within 90 seconds,” he said, with a QR code flashing up on the screen behind him. 

“If you’re gonna do this, get it now. Write the cheque. If you do this, God said I’m going make you a multi, multi-millionaire. I wish it was me! Money does not have me, at all. 

“I control it, I’m like Katt Williams, he said ‘the reason why I have a lot of it is I give 10% away on a regular basis because it belongs to God. But there’s a person in here. You sow that within 90 seconds, it should be less than that, God said I’m going to show you my face.” 

Timestamp of 3:29:28

As noted, the moment was originally broadcast back in July, but has resurfaced online. Many have pointed to a skit from comedian Druski about pastors asking for money, saying it bears similarities. 

“This is the exact thing Druski was talking about and y’all were bashing him,” one commented. “Druski video wasn’t even skit, it was an exact reenactment,” added another. 

While no one owned up to donating $10,000, Hall’s 90-second time limit came and went with him still asking for the money.
