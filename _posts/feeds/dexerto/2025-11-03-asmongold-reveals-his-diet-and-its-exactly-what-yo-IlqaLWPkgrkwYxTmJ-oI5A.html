Asmongold detailed his current diet during a recent Twitch stream, revealing his daily eating and drinking habits. It’s all about what you’d expect.

Although Asmongold is one of the biggest streamers on the planet today and would therefore certainly be making a pretty penny, the internet celeb hasn’t used his wealth to change his living conditions.

In fact, the state of his house has become a meme in the industry, and his well-being is often called into question, too, given his obsession with soda and disdain for water.

Now, Asmon has hopped on stream and peeled back the curtain on what fuels him for a day of social commentary on Twitch. The details will not shock you.

Asmongold’s diet involves at least three sodas a day

When the topic was broached during his November 1 Twitch stream, Asmon took some time out from his usual chatter to break down his typical day of eating and drinking.

For some context, he doesn’t buy groceries. You won’t find fresh produce in his kitchen. Instead, he ventures out or has delivery drivers drop off goods throughout the day.

On a typical streaming day, Asmon claimed to eat one proper meal, with plenty of snacks along the way. Breakfast consists of beef jerky, a protein bar, along with a mix of candy and the first soft drink of the day.

That’s enough to power him through a typical 6-8 hour stream before more beef jerky, more candy, and another soda are administered.

For dinner, it’s a toss-up between a number of local fast-food spots, be it Chipotle, Taco Bell, Wendy’s, or any given pizza place. That’s his one proper meal, more than likely washed down with another soda, but the day’s not done yet.

Later in the evening he’ll snack on more beef jerky and candy, but it’s here where he enjoys some ice cream “as a reward for being good.”

After that, if he’s still not quite satiated, Asmon explained how he’ll head to McDonald’s at 1AM, but not for any food, just for another soft drink.

“I’ve been doing this for three or four years now,” Asmon said on stream, adding that he “honestly [feels] pretty good” all things considered.

“My goal is to eat so much, so many unhealthy things, that I will fundamentally change my body’s chemistry and transcend being a human being. I will evolve.”

For any concern Asmon might be missing a few essentials, rest assured, he does drink at least one small cup of water “before bed.”
