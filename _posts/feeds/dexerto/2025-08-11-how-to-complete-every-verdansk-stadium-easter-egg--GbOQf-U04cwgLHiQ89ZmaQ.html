Warzone’s Verdansk Stadium is finally open thanks to the Season 5 update, and there are Easter eggs to complete for permanent rewards.

Back when Stadium first opened in Modern Warfare Season 5, it had a keycard Easter egg that unlocked a secret room containing the exclusive Enigma Blueprint for the AMAX. Now that the Stadium has opened up again in Black Ops 6 Season 5, that Easter egg’s back alongside a couple of new ones.

Here’s how to complete all three Stadium Easter eggs in Warzone Season 5.

How to open LAB-2 room in Stadium

  1. Head to the Overlook in Downtown.
  2. Go to the roof and look for the small construction room on the south-east corner. 
  3. Find the LAB-2 Card on the table next to a blueprint of Stadium. 
  4. Go to Stadium.
  5. Head inside, find the LAB-2 portacabin, then use your keycard on the door. 

There isn’t much of a reward for completing this Easter egg other than some high-tier loot, but it does appear to tease another map coming to the game (more on that below).

How to complete Stadium keycard Easter egg 

  1. Get something to make notes with, e.g, a pen and paper, a Google Doc, or Discord.
  2. Load into Warzone and head inside Stadium. 
  3. Search for two of the three keycards, which can be found on shelves, the floor, or on dead enemies:
    • P2 – 16: Parking Garage
    • CL – 19: Concourse
    • EL – 21: Executive Level
  4. Take a keycard to the locked room on the level where you found the keycard. 
  5. Interact with the computer, then write down the code that appears on the screen. This is different every match, so you can’t just copy someone else.
  6. It’s helpful to name the symbols that appear, e.g:
    • House 
    • Nose
    • T
  7. You’ll end up with a code such as: 23H48NNT
  8. Find one more keycard, then take it to the room on the level you found it. 
  9. You’ll end up with another code such as: 2354866T
  10. Compare the codes and you’ll realise that H = 5 and N = 6. 
  11. Head to the boardroom on the executive level. 
  12. The only number we don’t know is T, so instead of finding another keycard, you can simply use trial and error on the numbers that haven’t been used in the sequence yet. 
  13. Once you’ve found the correct final number, head inside the room and collect the CR-56 AMAX Blueprint from the table. 

Not only do you get the Enigma CR-56 AMAX Blueprint, but a map on the wall shows a small section of Vondel, potentially hinting at the Resurgence map’s return in Season 5 Reloaded or Season 6.

How to complete Electrical Box Easter egg

  1. Search the Stadium for Electrical Toolboxes.
  2. Once you’ve found one, look for the broken electrical boxes scattered around Stadium (broken ones are open and sparking with electricity).
  3. Approach them and hold the interact button to repair them. 
  4. The game will tell you once you’ve repaired enough, then head to the top floor of the broken building on the east side of Stadium. 
  5. Interact with the terminal, then head to the area underneath the missile, which has just opened up, then collect the SWAT 5.56 Blueprint. 

Completing this Easter egg unlocks this new room with tons of orange crates and the Phantom Alloy Blueprint for the Grau, aka the SWAT 5.56.
