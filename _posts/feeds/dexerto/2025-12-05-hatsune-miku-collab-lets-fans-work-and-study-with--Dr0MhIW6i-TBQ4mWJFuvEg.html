Hatsune Miku fans will soon be able to work and study alongside the virtual idol thanks to a new collaboration, transforming even the dullest work environment into something more vibrant.

The collab arrives through gogh: Focus with Your Avatar, a virtual study hangout game where players build cute rooms, customise avatars, and quietly focus together while chill lo-fi beats play in the background.

However, with Miku’s arrival, cozy study sessions are about to get a whole lot bluer and a whole lot more vibrant. Instead of staring at a plain desk setup, players will be able to sit beside their own Miku and complete their work beside the world-famous virtual star.

Hatsune Miku will soon help you study

The Study with Miku DLC adds two official Hatsune Miku outfits, a themed room style, collaboration items, and a pile of extra decorations so fans can build their own personal Miku study nook.

It’s not quite as dedicated as building your very own 2-meter-tall Hatsune Miku gaming PC, but the game promises to make study and work sessions more fun.

A matching music pack is also on the way for anyone who wants to blast through their workload while listening to Miku-themed lo-fi beats while you tackle tasks. It also lands during a massive streak of Miku collaborations, following an ASUS gaming PC, a Fortnite skin, branded streetwear, and even an official car collab.
