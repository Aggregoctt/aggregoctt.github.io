One Piece Chapter 1151 sets the stage for the long-anticipated clash between Luffy and Imu, so here’s what you need to know.

Being deep in the Final Saga, the Elbaf Arc is bound to have some monumental plot points and decisive fights. So far, the arc has already introduced a number of important characters, including Scopper Gaban, Prince Loki, and some of the Holy Knights.

The storyline also revealed the World Government’s nefarious plan to kidnap Elbaf’s children to use them as hostages against the giants. However, when the Holy Knights fail to accomplish the task in time, their supreme leader takes over.

In Chapter 1149, Imu took over Gunko’s body and mind just when she was remembering her past. In the next chapter, Imu used a secret technique to turn the giant warriors into demons to form a fearsome demon army. What happens next will be seen in One Piece Chapter 1151.

One Piece Chapter 1151 spoilers

Titled ‘I Get It Now!!!’, One Piece Chapter 1151 is only 15 pages long. The cover page features Yamato at a sumo tournament in Hakumai. Urashima’s brother, Sukune the Yokozuna, is thrown off the ring by his opponent.

The chapter starts right where the last one left off. As Imu raises a demon army, Killingham returns to his hybrid form. This form looks like a centaur, with the upper body of a human and the lower body of a Qilin. His human form has long lower lashes and sharp teeth. He orders his dream monsters with a horn. 

Imu declares to continue their rampage until they destroy Elbaf’s ‘heart’.

A flashback from some minutes ago starts. After Gunko strikes Gaban, he’s saved by Chopper’s ‘Guard Point’. They both fall from the Sun World into the Underworld. Luffy suddenly senses Chopper’s presence and also that he’s with someone else.

After the group finds Chopper and Gaban, they give first aid to the injured man and learn his real identity as the left-hand man of Gol D. Roger. When Hajruddin says he was the third strongest man in Roger’s crew (after Roger and Rayleigh), Gaban clarifies that he was the second strongest.

Chopper explains everything happening in Sun World. Just as the group prepares to go back above, they feel Imu’s frightening Conqueror’s Haki (from the scene in Chapter 1150). But while everyone is worried, Loki is laughing. Chapter 1151 ends with Loki offering Luffy and the group his aid to defeat Imu.
