A Silent Hill 1 remake has just been announced at Konami’s Press Start showcase, where the game was confirmed to be in development.

On June 12, 2025, Konami finally revealed that the long-awaited Silent Hill remake is being worked on. While the release date and gameplay have yet to be announced, we now know that Bloober Team, the devs behind the excellent Silent Hill 2 remake, are working alongside Konami to develop the game.

The short 25-second trailer features the iconic music from the first game, which plays eerily in the background while fog sweeps across the screen. You can see the full trailer below:

The original Silent Hill was released in 1999 for the PS1 and follows protagonist Harry Mason as he searches for his missing daughter in the fictional American town of Silent Hill. Players must make their way through the fog-filled town, solving puzzles and fighting off the creepy creatures that stalk the streets.
