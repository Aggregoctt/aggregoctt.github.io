Timing is everything in Splitgate 2, so you’ll need to make sure you’ve got the highest FPS possible to improve your aim and reaction times.

The open beta is here, giving you a taste of the portal FPS action ahead of its full release. Portals offer up a ton of room for strategy, but you’ll need to be fast on your feet, and having low FPS or stuttering issues can make the game feel impossible.

To help you out, here are the best Splitgate 2 graphics settings to make sure the game is running as smoothly as possible.

Best Splitgate 2 beta PC graphics settings

General

It’s important you have VSync off, as it will cause input lag, so you’re much better off capping your FPS limit to your monitor’s refresh rate.

FOV is down to personal preference, but having it set to 103 will let you see more of your surroundings and increase your spatial awareness. Some games, like Call of Duty, let you crank your FOV all the way up to 120, which can look stretched and affect performance, but 103 (the max) is a happy middle ground.

Scalability

Splitgate 2 looks good even on low settings, but there are some settings you’ll want to turn up. It’s worth setting View Distance Quality to High, as it’ll make it easier to see far-off enemies.

Texture Quality isn’t too taxing, so Medium is a nice spot to keep the game looking good. Finally, you’ll actually want to turn Portal Frame Rate and Portal Quality up to High, as this means it’ll be easier to see and shoot enemies through Portals – a huge part of Splitgate 2.

Super Resolution

NVIDIA DLSS will boost your framerate, so you’ll want to have that turned on with the Resolution set to Quality. However, if your PC is still finding the game difficult to run, then you can turn it down to Balanced or Performance.

Frame Generation

Frame Generation gives you more FPS at the cost of input lag, which isn’t a worthwhile tradeoff. Even if you’re not getting as many frames as you’d like, it’s still better to leave this off.

Low Latency

To fight input lag, you should turn the NVIDIA Reflex Low Latency Method on to Boost. This will make sure the game reacts to your inputs as soon as you press them.

Splitgate 2 PC system requirements

You could experience stuttering, low FPS, and other issues if you’re using out-of-date graphics drivers, so you should make sure you’re using the latest drivers. Here’s how to update them:

  1. Install the NVIDIA App through NVIDIA.com and then launch it.
  2. Go to the ‘Drivers’ tab.
  3. Hit the ‘Download‘ button in the top right corner.
  4. Wait for the download to finish, and then install the drivers. It’s worth restarting your PC afterward.

  2. Select AMD Radeon Settings.
  3. Click the Home icon.
  4. Head to ‘New Updates’ on the bottom left.
  5. Click ‘check for update.’
  6. If an update is available, follow the steps.
