Kai Cenat, Druski, and Kevin Hart have revealed that they’re working on a movie that is actually based on a Twitch stream gone wrong.

Over the last few years, Kai Cenat has linked up with a fair number of celebrities on his Twitch streams, but Kevin Hart and Druski have been two of the most popular.

The comedians have made multiple appearances on Kai’s streams, both normally and during planned segments for his Mafiathons, providing plenty of laughs for viewers.

Naturally, some fans have called for them to link up in a more traditional way by making a movie or TV series together. And, well, it turns out that those fans might just be in luck. 

Livestream from Hell teaser by Kevin Hart, Kai Cenat & Druski

Hart and Druski appeared on the September 29 part of Kai’s Mafiathon 3, revealing a teaser trailer for a movie that they’re working on – Livestream from Hell. 

It is based on the three linking up for a stream before everything starts going wrong. Things get broken in Hart’s house, a person dies, blood starts appearing around the three of them, and the police have to get involved, too. 

“I believe that they got next,” Hart said, suggesting that Kai and Druski would be movie stars. “We put something together. We’re not done yet, but we want to show the world what we’ve been working on.”

Kai echoed that, saying the project isn’t done yet. “It’s a dope ass thing. I’m excited to partner with my f**king guys,” Hart added. “It ain’t about me, it’s about y’all!” 

With the movie not being completed, there is no release date or mention of where it might be broadcast. There is only the mention of “coming soon” towards the end of the short teaser. 

Hart’s ‘Hartbeat Productions’ is behind it, and they’ve had movies published across Netflix, Roku, and Peacock. So, it could pop up anywhere.
