Roblox is looking a little green right now, with tons of players all dressing up in the same outfit. If you’re not clued up, then this entire ‘war’ may be a little confusing.

For those who have jumped into Blox Fruit, Grow a Garden, Jump Stars, or Doors, you may have noticed quite a few similarities. Namely, almost everyone is in the same outfit. Now, if you’re not clued up on either Grow a Garden or Steal a Brainrot, then the entire shift is pretty confusing.

So, here’s all you need to know, from what’s going on to how you can take part in this upcoming ‘war’ between two of the biggest Roblox games out there.

Why is everyone green in Roblox?

Currently, there’s a ‘war’ going on between Jandel, the owner of Grow a Garden, and Sammy, the owner of Steal a Brainrot. Both are aiming to have the largest number of players during their next admin abuse, which is the event that happens just before an update: “whoever’s admin abuses has more players, wins.”

So, on Saturday, August 23, at 3 PM EST, players are going to flood into Grow a Garden or Steal a Brainrot with the aim of having the biggest player count.

As explained by Jandel on X, “for every 1m players more than Steal a Brainrot – I will donate $5k to Team Water,” so it’s also a massive charity event as well.

To show their support, players are equipping different skins, with the Grow a Garden one being Green Bean.

How to get Green Bean outfit in Roblox

If you want to support Grow a Garden, then you can also get the Green Bean outfit for free. To do this, follow the steps below:

  • Head into Grow a Garden.
  • Join a new server.
  • Head to the bottom right of the map.
  • Speak to the Grean Bean and press ‘I want to join the cause‘
  • Equip your outfit.

With that, you’ll get a new free outfit and will be supporting Grow a Garden in the upcoming war between the two games.

Does Steal a Brainrot have an outfit?

Unfortunately no. Steal a Brainrot hasn’t added a specific outfit for players to show their support just yet.

This means you’ll have to just log into the game during the scheduled Admin Abuse if you want Sammy to win the war.
