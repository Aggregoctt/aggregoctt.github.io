NBA 2K26 Twitch Drops have started going live, and there are some pretty nice rewards for you to get your hands on.

When it comes to getting free rewards, NBA 2K has always had pretty of different options for those players who don’t want to shell out on VC. Locker Codes are the best way – even if they can be limited in numbers, there is the daily spin wheel, and in-game objectives in MyCareer. 

There are, of course, ways to get rewards from outside of the game too. We’ve already mentioned Locker Codes – they are regularly posted on social media by the 2K devs – but there are also Twitch Drops. 

You can get some pretty nice rewards, especially in the early days of an NBA 2K launch, just by watching your favorite creator.

All active NBA 2K26 Twitch Drops rewards

Right now, to celebrate the launch of NBA 2K26, there are a handful of helpful Twitch Drops for you to claim too. 

All you have to do is watch a 2K-partnered streamer who has drops turned on for a certain amount of time and you’ll get the reward added to your Twitch Inventory. These rewards, as of September 11, are: 

  • 45 Minutes: MyTEAM 1 Hour 2XP Coin, MyCAREER 1 Hour 2XP Coin
  • 2 Hours: MyTEAM ‘26 Series 1 Deluxe Pack (Unauctionable), MyCAREER 1 Hour 2XP Coin
  • 4 Hours: MyTEAM 92+ OVR Free Agent Pack (5 Games), MyCAREER Skill Boosts – 5x All Categories

How to claim NBA 2K26 Twitch Drops

To get started on earning your rewards, you’ll have to head over to the 2K website and log in with your Twitch account. Connecting your 2K and Twitch profiles will make you eligible for rewards.

Once you’ve done that, kick back and relax. 

  1. Head over to the NBA 2K26 website to connect your Twitch & 2K accounts 
  2. Find a 2K-partnered streamer on Twitch with drops enabled
  3. Sit back and watch their stream for a few hours
  4. Check your Twitch Inventory for the rewards
  5. Enjoy your rewards in-game!

Twitch Drops will be rarer than Locker Codes and free VC drops, but they will be useful throughout the year. Plus, they’ll change as we progress through the in-game seasons too. 

So, make sure you’re going back to Twitch whenever new rewards pop up!
