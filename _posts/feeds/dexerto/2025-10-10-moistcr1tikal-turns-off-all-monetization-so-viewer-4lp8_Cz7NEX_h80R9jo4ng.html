Popular YouTuber and Twitch streamer MoistCr1TiKaL has turned off every form of monetization possible and is urging viewers to hold onto their money or donate to charity.

Viewers donating large amounts of money to streamers has always been a controversial topic, and often makes headlines. Earlier this month, a streamer was murdered by a viewer who had sent her nearly $70,000.

While some big names like Asmongold and Tectone have urged streamers to outright ban viewers who send outrageous amounts, Charles ‘MoistCr1TiKaL’ White Jr isn’t even letting it go that far. Instead, he’s making it nearly impossible for fans to spend their money on him.

In a YouTube video uploaded on October 9, the legendary YouTube star explained that he disabled every form of monetization possible, including super chats and channel memberships.

MoistCr1TiKaL urges viewers to donate to charity instead of giving him money

According to Charles, in addition to turning off forms of YouTube monetization on his channel, he even raised the minimum Bit donation on Twitch to an amount so drastically high, he hopes no one will ever be able to spend that much.

“Once you opt into the Partner program or whatever, you can’t turn off bits or subs,” he explained.

“For subs, I can’t get rid of that. If I could, I would. I would say toodle-oo, give it a strong slap on the ass, tell it good game and goodbye. But from what I can tell, there are a lot of hoops to jump through in order to accomplish that,” he said. “I just want to strongly urge you, do not waste your money subbing to me on Twitch.”

At one point, Asmongold, streaming exclusively on a new account that wasn’t Partnered so viewers couldn’t get any ads. Eventually, Twitch grew frustrated because they were losing money given the size of his channel and he was forced to monetize it.

According to Moist, he would just rather have viewers hold onto their money “more than ever, with how everything’s been going,” he urged. “I think it is crucial you get your finances in a f**king Full Nelson and you just don’t let those puppies fly willy-nilly, donating clams to a goofball like me.”

However, for viewers who have money burning a hole in their pocket, he’s pleading for them to donate to a charity instead.

“There are so many good charities doing extremely good work that could be benefited greatly from your contribution…That would be a significantly better use of that money than donating it to me.”

MoistCr1TiKaL concluded by saying that while he openly discouraged donating for a while, it was time to pull the plug on the option completely.

“I feel like this is long overdue, it’s something I should have done a while ago, but I had convinced myself I could deliver something of value to people that were paying,” he added. “I just don’t think there’s any value in giving money during a stream to get my attention, so that way I talk about it for a second. That’s just not a good use of your money.”

We’ll have to see if this starts a trend across the streaming space, with more creators opting to do the same.
