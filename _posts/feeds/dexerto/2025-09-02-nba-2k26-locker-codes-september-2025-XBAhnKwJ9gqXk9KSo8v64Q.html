NBA 2K26 is here, and that means Locker Codes are back to help you with MyTeam, MyCareer, and in the City with MyPlayer.

There are some solid ways to make a fair bit of VC in the series already: You can grind out MyCareer, win games online, and even see the daily rewards. However, Locker Codes are usually the best way to get a massive one-time hit. 

Locker Codes can also get you some exclusive rewards, including skill boosts, badges, packs, and XP. They drop at random times throughout the season, and the start of a new 2K cycle means fresh codes — so here’s everything available right now and how to redeem them.

Active Locker Codes in NBA 2K26 

With NBA 2K26 being in early access, there are only two working codes as of September 2, 2025. Unless stated otherwise, codes are typically active for a week or so. 

Make sure to bookmark this page and check back soon, as we’ll have the latest updates on the newest rewards for you here as they’re released.

How to redeem codes

As ever, redeeming a Locker Code is simple in NBA 2K26: Just like last year, you’ll have to head into MyTeam and find the ‘Market’ tab. Here’s a rundown of what to do:

  1. Once in-game, head into MyTeam
  2. Find the Market tab
  3. Scroll down to ‘Locker Codes’ and type in your code
  4. Enjoy your rewards!

Don’t worry, these codes are not case sensitive, so you can enter them in lower case or upper case — it won’t matter. Some codes in the future might be case sensitive, but 2K will announce those as and when. 

You can also use the NBA 2K mobile app to redeem your Locker Codes. It’s an even simpler process on there, seeing as you just press the Locker Codes option on the main screen and type it in.

Just make sure that if you’re using this option, you have selected NBA 2K26 as the game and you’re not trying to redeem a 2K25 Locker Code.

Are there any expired Locker Codes?

As noted, we’re only early into the NBA 2K26 cycle, so we don’t have any expired codes just yet. Any codes that we do add to the table below going forward won’t be usable, so don’t try and sneak in a free 10,000 VC code months after it expires.

You can redeem as many codes as you like at any one time, but remember, they may be limited quantities, so prioritize those first! You wouldn’t want to miss out on a diamond Kevin Durant or Cam Thomas. 

Locker Codes will pop up in different places too – we’ve seen them revealed during NBA games before, live on TV, but they regularly drop in-game on 2KTV and on social media as well. So, keep your eye out.
