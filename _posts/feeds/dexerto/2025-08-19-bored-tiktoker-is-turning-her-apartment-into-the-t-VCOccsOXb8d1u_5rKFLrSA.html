A TikToker is going viral as she has been renovating her apartment to look like the Titanic, and has even filled it with models of the ship. Why? Well, she’s “bored”.

When it comes to the Titanic, everyone has some sort of fascination. Be it re-watching every documentary on the subject, owning thousands of copies of the DVD and video versions of the movie, or even trying to visit the shipwreck on something like the Titan submarine. 

However, one TikToker has taken things to a different level. The aptly named titanicapartment, or Sarah, who is based in New York, has been renovating her apartment for months to make it look like the submerised supership. Why, you ask? Well, because she got bored. 

The TikToker, who has uploaded well over 100 videos of the renovation, claimed it is also an “art project” that she is living in. 

TikToker is making apartment look like the Titanic

Sarah has done everything from painting the doors to resemble those on the ship, adding portholes for windows, and decking it with plenty of replicas of the ship.

She has also added blue flooring and blue cloth to really give the feeling that you’re underwater and living in a shipwreck.  

Some viewers have questioned the TikToker on how she manages to live in it every day, and she admitted she is fine. Her dog, however, is supposedly “pissed” by all of the changes. 

Other viewers couldn’t help but joke about the whole thing. “You never really know what other people are doing in their own homes,” one said. 

“I sign a one-year lease and don’t even hang a single picture or painting,” another added. “Props if this is a basement apartment that occasionally floods,” another joked. 

Some viewers have claimed that it is a “dangerous” idea because of the potential fire hazard. However, the TikToker isn’t too worried. “It’s been up since April. We’re good,” she replied to one.
