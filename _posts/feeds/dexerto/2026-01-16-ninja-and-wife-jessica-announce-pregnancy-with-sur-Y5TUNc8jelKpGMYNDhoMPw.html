Twitch star Tyler ‘Ninja’ Blevins and his wife, Jessica, are leaving fans gushing after revealing the gender of their baby in an unexpected Instagram post.

Ninja is going to be a dad! The 34-year-old streamer broke the happy news on January 16, 2026, along with the gender of his and wife Jessica’s upcoming baby.

The couple decided to film the reveal for their fans, using a white cake that was baked for them by a friend. They went with the trendy ‘wine glass cake’ method, where they took two wine glasses and shoved them inside the cake without looking.

After raising their glasses to toast, the two finally took a peek at the color of the cake and instantly started celebrating. Ninja and Jessica will be parents to a baby girl — something the famous Fortnite icon said he wasn’t expecting.

“I was so sure it was gonna be a boy!” he exclaimed, excitedly dubbing himself a “girl dad.”

Fans and fellow creators congratulate Ninja & Jessica after pregnancy reveal

Their video has already racked up 40,000 likes in just one hour, with fellow content creators like CouRage, LazarBeam, and more sending their well-wishes to the happy couple.

“Absolutely awesome, congratulations!” LazarBeam wrote.

“So excited for you both!! Such amazing news!!” CouRage said.

“Let’s go!” YouTube star Roman Atwood commented.

Even the Detroit Tigers got in on the celebration, writing in a comment, “CONGRATULATIONS!”

Ninja and Jessica are the latest internet-famous couple to go through a big change in their relationship after Markiplier got hitched to long-term girlfriend Amy Nelson in a private ceremony last year.

And in September 2025, the Sidemen’s Zerkaa tied the knot with wife Freya in an announcement that went viral within their fanbase and beyond.
