Former Nintendo employees believe the company “will find” the people responsible for leaking Switch 2 consoles early.

During a video uploaded on May 27, former Nintendo employees Kit and Krysta sat down to discuss the viral Target photo that has been making its way around social media. 

The picture in question shows Switch 2 consoles in an unlocked trolley, seemingly ready for distribution. Since then, numerous images have begun to surface online, with some fans boasting that they have received the portable console from retailers, despite it being locked behind a day-one patch.  

Well, according to the pair, Nintendo will likely find those responsible for the leaks.

Former Nintendo employees warn Switch 2 leakers

“I recall something like this happened with the original Switch. “We were within a week or so of the system coming out, and there was big video of some guy who had it…, and it was like code red to find out what happened,” said Kit.

“The interesting thing, though, is [Nintendo] can get pretty precise, with like finding where this is, who this is, and what store it came from. It was actually really surprising for me…Like again, these things can get scanned and identified.” 

Kit mentioned that there are so many ways Nintendo can locate the leakers, which will ultimately enable them to “find who it is” even if the leaks have already gotten out.
