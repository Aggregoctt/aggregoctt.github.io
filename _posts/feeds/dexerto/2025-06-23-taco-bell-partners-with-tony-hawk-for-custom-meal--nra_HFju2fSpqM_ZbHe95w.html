Taco Bell has teamed up with Tony Hawk to give him his own Luxe Craving Box in the company’s latest celebrity collaboration.

When it comes to skateboarding, Tony Hawk is by far the most recognizable name in the sport. This is largely thanks to his iconic line of video games, Tony Hawk Pro Skater.

The latest release in the series, Tony Hawk Pro Skater 3+4, comes out on July 10, 2025, and is a remake of games that were originally released on the PlayStation 1 and 2.

To celebrate the release, Taco Bell has partnered with Tony Hawk for a custom meal of his choice and a chance to get in-game items.

Taco Ball reveals Tony Hawk Luxe Craving Box

The box will be available through Taco Bell’s mobile app and website, and is a variation of the existing Luxe Craving Box, as the brand asked him to “share his take” on the offering.

Here’s everything included in the Tony Hawk Luxe Craving Box:

  • Cheese quesadilla
  • Crunchy taco
  • Chips and nacho cheese sauce
  • Medium Lipton iced tea

With the meal, the taco-focused restaurant is offering in-game items for the upcoming Pro Skater 3+4 remake once it releases on July 10.

Here are all the Taco Bell exclusive items that will be available:

  • July 11-17: Salsa verde skateboard
  • July 18-24: Taco Bell hoodie
  • July 25-31: Taco Bell sweatpants
  • Aug. 1-7: Verde grip tape
  • Aug. 8-13: Taco Bell puffer vest

On top of that, Taco Bell rewards members will be able to have a chance to earn one of 500 limited-edition skateboard decks on June 24 at 2 pm PT. Additionally, the company has 50,000 codes available for fans to check out the pre-release demo.
