Milky Way is giving caramel fans exactly what they have been asking for with its upcoming caramel-filled candy bar.

The Mars-owned candy brand is launching a new Milky Way All Caramel bar, a nougat-free version that puts the brand’s signature caramel front and center.

The new bar is set to hit shelves nationwide beginning in January 2026, according to an announcement shared with PEOPLE.

The launch marks a notable return to a format Milky Way fans have repeatedly called for online, years after a similar caramel-only bar was discontinued.

What is Milky Way All Caramel?

Milky Way All Caramel is described as a bar “filled with all of the Milky Way caramel that consumers know and love with none of the nougat.” The candy will be available in two sizes: a 1.55 oz single bar and a 3.11 oz shareable option.

While the name is new, the concept is not. In 2010, Milky Way released a similar product under the name Milky Way Simply Caramel. That version was later discontinued in 2023.
