From Severance to Andor, the 2025 Emmys is stacked – so, get yourself ready for the ceremony with our list of all the nominations.

Up until this month with Superman and Fantastic Four, you could argue it’s been a quiet year for movies (Sinners and 28 Years Later excluded, obviously).

The same can’t be said for television. 2025 has already played host to some of the best TV shows of the decade so far, whether it’s the conclusion to one of the galaxy far, far away’s greatest stories, Squid Game Season 3, or Netflix’s record-breaking Adolescence.

This September, the 77th Primetime Emmy Awards will be one of the most competitive ceremonies in recent memory – and these are all the actors and shows vying for a prize.
