Fortnite has teased that the KPop Demon Hunters collab isn’t over just yet. Here’s everything we know about what’s next.

The Battle Royale title has been on an absolute collaboration spree lately, and it’s safe to say Kpop Demon Hunters was easily one of the standouts.

When it first dropped, it wasn’t just a simple skin release either. We got the Huntrix trio as playable outfits, a dedicated mode, themed weapons, and even items pulled straight from the movie.

Ever since then, fans have been asking for more, and it looks like Epic might finally be answering those prayers. The company recently posted a new teaser on social media, all but confirming that another wave of KPop Demon Hunters content is on the way.

When will KPop Demon Hunters Wave 2 release in Fortnite?

Epic hasn’t revealed the release date just yet, but they did drop a pretty telling hint by posting “영원히 깨질 수 없는”, a lyric reference to Golden. That alone confirms the crossover isn’t done just yet.

The only info we have right now about the date is from leakers. According to HYPEX, it’s expected to arrive this week, likely with an upcoming update.

How to get Fortnite KPop Demon Hunters Wave 2 skins

We already have Rumi, Mira, and Zoey from the first drop. So, who’s next? Leakers suggest that the spotlight for the second wave will be on Jinu, and possibly, the other Saja Boys. As with the first wave, these items are expected to be available through the Item Shop.

The lineup will also be reportedly joined by Derpy, the blue supernatural tiger from the show. Instead of a skin or back bling, though, he’ll be a Sidekick that’ll follow you around.

And, of course, seeing that Epic gave a nod to Golden from their hint, it’s likely that we’ll be getting that song as an emote. According to leaks, we may be getting an emote for How It’s Done, too.

While pricing hasn’t been announced, the first wave’s bundle was priced at 3,500 V-Bucks, with individual skins available for 1,500 V-Bucks each.

Alongside new cosmetics, players can also expect the KPop Demon Hunters Mythics to return to the loot pool.

Do keep in mind that these info are from leaks, so you’ll want to take it with a grain of salt for now until Epic officially releases more details. Though we’ll be sure to keep you updated when that happens.
