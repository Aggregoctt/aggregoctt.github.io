Voice actors bring games to life, and there are some pretty recognizable voices telling the story of Borderlands 4.

Borderlands games may have their own unique style, gameplay, and complete chaos, but the story is where it truly shines, and that’s no different in Borderlands 4. Naturally, that story is best told using some fantastic villains, heroes, and of course, the adorable Claptrap.

Those characters are brought to life by their voice actors, and it’s likely you’ll recognise some of the names as you play. So, if you’re scratching your head, wondering where you’ve heard Amon, Rafa, Harlowe, Vex, or even The Time Keeper before, then we’ve got you covered.
