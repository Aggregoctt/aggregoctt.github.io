Houses are finally in World of Warcraft, giving you the chance to design and create your very own homes when you’re not exploring the world — but you’ll need to obtain decor to truly make your hearth a home.

The World of Warcraft community has long called for houses, and now they’re finally here, letting those who pre-order the Midnight expansion the opportunity to decorate their homes early to represent their style, or just make it as cozy as possible.

However, decor can be expensive, which is where the Decor Treasure Hunt quest comes in, offering you a chance to earn some free items and gold. The only issue is that all the decor is hidden in treasure chests buried around Founders Point. So, here’s how to find them.

How to start Decor Treasure Hunt

To start the Decor Treasure Hunt in World of Warcraft, you’ll need to speak to The Last Architect, who’s found in the middle of Founders Point in the Town Square.

Head over to him, chat with him, and he’ll give you a riddle. It’s up to you to solve this puzzle and head to the correct location. Then all you need to do is dig the chest up and reap the rewards.

Decor Treasure Hunt riddle answers

The Last Architect will offer you a different riddle every day, and they’re all random depending on the player. So, when you get your riddle, type it into the box below, and you’ll find the location and reward. Alternatively, search by the reward.

When you have your location, install the TomTom addon if you don’t have it already (you can do this while playing), type /reload, and then type in the following command into the search bar. This will place a map ping of where to go:

  • /way [insert coordinates]
