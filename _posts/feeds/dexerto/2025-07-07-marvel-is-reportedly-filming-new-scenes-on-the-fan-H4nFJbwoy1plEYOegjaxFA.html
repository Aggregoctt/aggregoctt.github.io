With Fantastic Four: First Steps about to his screens worldwide, ‘Marvel’s First Family’ are rumored to be shooting new scenes on their version of Earth.

The Fantastic Four: First Steps is set to hit screens worldwide on July 25, 2025, when fans will get a first looks at Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards/Mister Fantastic, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm/Invisible Woman, Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm/Thing, and Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm/Human Torch.

We know that the film will feature the Shalla-Bal iteration of Silver Surfer, played by Julia Garner. While Ralph Ineson is the biggest of bad of them all in his role as Galactus.

First Steps is also something new for the Marvel Cinematic Universe, playing out in a different Earth to the one we live on and the previous MCU movies have taken place. According to a new report, this won’t be the last we see of the F4’s version of the planet.

New Fantastic Four scenes rumored to feature a baseball game

According to Entertainment Weekly, Fantastic Four: First Steps “begins on a parallel world in the Marvel multiverse where history has unfolded differently.

“In our timeline, the ’50s and ’60s were full of artists and thinkers who imagined utopian futures full of miracle inventions like flying cars and robot friends. In this world, Reed Richards exists to invent them.”

The trailers and footage released thus far suggest that retro-infused present-day. And movie scooper DanielRPK suggests the setting will ongoing, posting on his X account: “They are currently filming scenes taking place on F4’s Earth like a baseball game.”

With First Steps out in less than three weeks, it’s too late for last-minute reshoots, so these are likely to be scenes for Avengers: Doomsday, which the Russo brothers are currently making on location in the UK.

That films features old Avengers, the New Avengers, various X-Men, Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom, and the Fantastic Four. So if that rumor proves to be true, expect to see some multiverse shenanigans to pull all the many, many comic book characters together, potentially on the Fantastic Four’s version of Planet Earth.

Fantastic Four: First Steps is out on July 25, 2025, while Avengers: Doomsday is set to hits screens on December 18, 2026.
