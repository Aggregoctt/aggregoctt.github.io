Pokemon fans who are planning to pick up Pokopia early have been given the opportunity to bag themselves an adorable free gift if they preorder from a specific store.

Pokopia is the next Pokemon spinoff heading to Nintendo Switch, launching on March 14, 2026. Unlike the mainline series, Pokopia leans into cozy exploration and lighthearted creativity rather than gym battles and league progression.

It’s set on a handcrafted island filled with Pokemon that live, wander, and interact with the environment, and players are encouraged to decorate spaces, complete relaxed requests, and uncover small stories across the island at their own pace, a lot like Animal Crossing.

As more details surface ahead of launch, one perk has started to catch attention. Those planning to lock in a preorder might want to double-check where they are buying from to grab a free gift.

Preordering Pokopia comes with a Ditto surprise

If you preorder Pokopia directly from the Pokemon Center, you will receive a free Ditto Sitting Cuties Plush at no additional cost. It is a physical bonus that is easy to miss if you are not actively looking for it.

The plush features Ditto in its signature relaxed sitting pose, styled in the soft and minimal Sitting Cuties line, making it compact, great to display on your shelf, and of course, instantly recognizable thanks to Ditto’s absolutely adorable face. It also originally costs $9.99, saving a fair chunk of money if you get it for free.

That’s not all, there’s also a digital extra tied to preordering Pokopia. Players who buy the game early can claim a free in-game Ditto-themed rug through the Mystery Gift feature.

The rug can be placed as decoration within the island and is available to redeem until January 31, 2027, so there is no rush on day one, but it is worth remembering before the window closes.
