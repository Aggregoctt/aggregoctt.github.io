Kick streamer Konvy was shot at while live streaming from a car in New York, with video from the broadcast capturing the moments surrounding the incident.

Footage circulating on social media shows Konvy and his crew driving through the city during the early morning hours when gunfire is heard. The stream was live at the time, and the clip has since spread widely across X and other platforms.

According to a News 12 The Bronx report, a 23-year-old man was grazed by a bullet on the left side of his head during a shooting in the Longwood section of the Bronx. The incident occurred shortly before 11 p.m. at 1050 Prospect Avenue.

Police said the victim was taken to Lincoln Hospital and is expected to recover. No arrests have been announced.

While Konvy was initially believed to be the victim, multiple reports and social media posts indicate that fellow streamer ItsJJx was the person injured. At this time, there is no confirmation from police publicly identifying the victim by name, and authorities have not stated whether the shooting is directly connected to the live stream.

Streamer Adin Ross, who was also live during the shooting, publicly stated that the incident was real and said he helped arrange contact with NYPD following the shooting. Police have not released further details, including a suspect description or motive.
