YouTube megastar Jimmy ‘MrBeast’ Donaldson has offered IShowSpeed his craziest race challenge yet, as he wants him to go up against a Cheetah. Seriously. 

Over the last year or so, IShowSpeed has been showing off his, well, speed in different foot races. The YouTube streaming star has taken on a few different athletes, including the fastest man on the planet Noah Lyles. 

That race with the Olympic gold medalist was set up by MrBeast, as the YouTube megastar put $100k up for grabs. Lyles defeated Speed by a few steps and stated that he wouldn’t give the streamer a rematch. That, naturally, left him a bit annoyed. 

Speed has tried to set up races with other athletes too, most notably NFL superstar Tyreek Hill. The Miami Dolphins wide receiver has said he’s up for the race, but they’ve been unable to make it happen.

MrBeast offers IShowSpeed race against real-life Cheetah

While his nickname might be Cheetah, MrBeast has had the idea to put Speed up against the actual animal. Seriously. 

“@IshowSpeedsui if you ever want to race a cheetah let me know,” Donaldson posted on X (formerly Twitter) on July 6. 

It came after the YouTuber uploaded a video in which he pitted a number of different animals against humans in athletic competitions. The Cheetah raced three different cars, including a Formula E car. While it beat the first two, the endangered animal was defeated by the race car.

Speed has embarked on another European tour stream, so MrBeast might be waiting for a little bit for a response. 

The streamer hasn’t been afraid of any race challenges put his way to this point, so may well give it a go at some point. Plus, it might get him closer to that foot race with Hill at long last.
