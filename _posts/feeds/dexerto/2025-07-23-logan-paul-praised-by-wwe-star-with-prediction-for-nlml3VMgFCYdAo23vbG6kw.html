Logan Paul has been tipped to become one of WWE’s good guys by a top star, even though he isn’t too sure himself. 

After Logan Paul first entered the world of WWE, it didn’t take long for the crowd to turn on him. The YouTube superstar is one of the company’s top bad guys, and he embraces that with his trash talk and in-ring moves. 

Making the switch from being a bad guy to a fan-favorite is something that pretty much everyone goes through at some point or another. 

As it stands, it is something that probably won’t happen anytime soon for Logan, but he has been tipped to make it happen down the line by a current top star. 

Drew McIntyre tips Logan Paul for big WWE career change

During his chat with former WWE champion Drew McIntyre on imPaulsive, Logan received some big advice from the Scottish superstar about his future in the wrestling game. 

“Everything has been a slam dunk so far. Just keep doing what you’re doing in the present, but also, where is this going to get me?” McIntyre told Logan. 

“Eventually, people don’t believe it and can laugh, but eventually you’re going to turn baby face (good guy). There is a fine line between love and hate, and, eventually, you’re going to get wildly cheered down the line.”

The Scotsman told the YouTuber that his “slam dunks” are only going to help towards that and to not be surprised when fans start showing him love. 

The pair are linking up as a tag team for WWE’s SummerSlam event, where they’ll take on Randy Orton and singer Jelly Roll.
