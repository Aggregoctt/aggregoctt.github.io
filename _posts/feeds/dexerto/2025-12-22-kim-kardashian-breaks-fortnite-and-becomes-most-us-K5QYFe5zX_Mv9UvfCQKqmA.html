The Kim Kardashian skin has rapidly become one of Fortnite’s biggest cosmetic releases ever, dominating Chapter 7 Season 1 just weeks after its release.

According to Fortnite.gg, the Icon Series Kim Kardashian outfit is currently the most used skin of the season. Around 2.9 million players have equipped it, which reperesents 5.13% of the active community, putting it ahead of every other cosmetic.

Despite only launching on December 13, 2025, the skin has also climbed to 70th place in the top 100 most-used skins of all time. Usage data shows the cosmetic outperforming a number of established favorites, including Backflash (2.53%), Cat Holloway (2.52%), Chani (2.44%), and Surf Witch (2.01%).

Kim Kardashian Fortnite skin is the most used in Chapter 7

On December 21, Epic Games also revealed that players have already played 37 million matches using the Kim Kardashian skin. It’s a crazy figure and one that is just as surprising as when the devs revealed the Ned Flanders NPC had gone rogue with over 33k eliminations.

Kim’s rise to Fortnite stardom is no mean feat either, as her bundle was released in one of its busiest cosmetic periods of the year, with Winterfest handing out free cosmetics daily and major drops like Snow Miku and the Harry Potter skins arriving in the Item Shop.

As of writing, the Kim Kardashian bundle is currently discounted to 3,000 V-Bucks (5,900 V-Bucks), and includes multiple cosmetics like her iconic latex outfit, private Jet glider, and an emote that allows characters to pull off her viral “Break the Internet” pose.
