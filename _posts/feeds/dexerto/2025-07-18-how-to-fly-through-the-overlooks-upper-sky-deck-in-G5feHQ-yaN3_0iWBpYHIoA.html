Warzone and Black Ops 6’s CODtoons event has started, bringing plenty of American Dad-themed rewards to unlock.

You can complete the challenges in either Black Ops 6, Warzone, or Zombies, but some are much easier than others. To unlock the TR2 CQB Auto attachment, you have to “fly through the Overlook’s Upper Sky Deck” in Warzone.

While easy to complete, you’ll struggle if you don’t know where to go, so here’s how to complete the challenge.

Where is the Overlook in Warzone?

The Overlook is the huge building in Verdansk’s Downtown surrounded by cranes. It was added in the Season 4 update, replacing the SKN Comms Tower.

Here, you’ll be able to open the 10 loot caches and fly through the Upper Sky Deck to complete the CODToons challenges.

How to fly through Overlook Upper Sky Deck

Simply load into Verdansk, look for the large gap about three-quarters of the way up the Overlook (you can’t miss it), then fly through it.

You can fly through it at the start of the game, but don’t worry if you miss it, as you can use a helicopter or the nearby cranes to get high ground and fly through later in the game.

Casual mode is the best place to get this done, as the bots won’t be able to shoot you out of the sky, but pretty much any mode is a safe bet.

Doing so will unlock the TR2 CQB Auto Conversion, and completing all seven CODtoons challenges will grant you the Speed Lines camo.
