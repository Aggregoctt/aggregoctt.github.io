Clash Royale’s Arena 7 can seem tough to beat. However, the best decks for Royale Arena will help you get through this level with a breeze.

By the time you get to Arena 7, also known as the Royal Arena in Clash Royale, you’ll likely stumble upon more experienced enemies who already have a solid grasp of the game. 

Most of the time, not only do they already understand basic counter-pushes and elixir management, but their deck synergies will also be a lot better. Hence, it can be challenging to push through this level, especially if you’re unsure what kind of deck you should run.

You might win a few matches, but then bounce back to where you started. If you want to be able to jump to the next level quickly, we’ve compiled the best decks for Arena 7 you can use below.

Best Arena 7 decks

Arena 7 introduces some powerful cards like Royal Giant, Prince, and Dark Prince, among others, so you’ll start seeing more variety in decks. 

The best part? With more cards unlocked, you’ve got way more options to experiment with compared to the early Arenas. Below, you’ll find three solid builds, each designed to suit a different playstyle.

Hog-Valkyrie-Arrows deck

Deck composition:

  • Valkyrie
  • Hog Rider
  • Skeleton Army
  • Inferno Tower
  • Archers
  • Mega Minion
  • Goblin Barrel
  • Zap

With only an average cost of 3.5 Elixir, this fast cycle, control, and chip deck lets you utilize the Hog Rider and Goblin Barrel as dual win conditions. The main idea of this deck is to apply constant pressure by chipping away at your enemy’s towers instead of aiming for one massive push.

As the primary win condition, the Hog Rider acts as your main tower damage dealer, with Valkyrie or Zap being used to support it. Meanwhile, Goblin Barrel is used interchangeably if your Hog gets countered. So, if one gets shut down, you still have the other to chip away at the damage.

When it comes to defense, you’ll want to save the Inferno Tower to lure huge tanks. Then, depending on what the enemy is running, have your Archers or Mega Minion deal with air troops, or place the Skeleton Army as an emergency defense.

Just remember to defend cheaply, then use Hog or Barrel to counterpush. As long as you play your cards right, you should have their towers down in no time.

Royal Giant-Mini P.E.K.K.A.-Minions deck

Deck composition:

  • Royale Giant
  • Mini PEKKA
  • Minions
  • Fireball
  • Musketeer
  • Tombstone
  • Valkyrie
  • Spear Goblins

This balanced control and beatdown deck is built around the Royal Giant, which acts as your main win condition. With an average Elixir cost of 3.8, it’s slightly heavier than the previous deck but offers strong defense and excellent counterpush potential.

The way this works is simple: have the Royal Giant apply steady tower pressure, and use the Musketeer, Minions, or the Fireball to support it.

You don’t need to panic if the enemy tries to run tanks and Prince against you, as you can utilize the Tombstone as a cheap distraction. Add Valkyrie to the mix, and you’ll be able to get rid of their swarms and support troops.

For chip damage and faster cycling, use Spear Goblins. They keep pressure on the opponent while helping you get back to your Royal Giant pushes.

One thing to watch out for is enemy defensive buildings. Since your main win condition is Royal Giant, ideally, you’ll want to play patiently and use your spells wisely to open up opportunities.

What is the best deck in Arena 7 in Clash Royale?

Deck composition:

  • PEKKA
  • Zap
  • Mini PEKKA
  • Goblin Barrel
  • Witch
  • Arrows
  • Baby Dragon
  • Musketeer

As a deck that has solid control and counterpush, this PEKKA-centered deck is the best one to use in Arena 7. 

Its 4.2 average Elixir cost makes it heavier than fast-cycle decks, but the tradeoff is rewarding. With careful management and smart defenses, you’ll be able to turn the tide with massive counterattacks.

The main strategy with this is to defend first with PEKKA, Mini PEKKA, Witch, and Baby Dragon. Then, as soon as you have the opportunity, build counterpushes behind PEKKA using the Musketeer with any other additional troops and spells.

Utilize the Goblin Barrel to bait spells and chip away at the enemy’s towers. Additionally, you can rely on Zap and Arrows to clear swarms while protecting your push. 

Just make sure to be mindful with your Elixir and play smart, given that this deck can be tough to cycle fast if you overcommit.
