Bobbi Althoff has launched a new monthly podcast, ‘Not This Again,’ with the first episode featuring a reunion interview with Drake. The appearance follows the pair’s 2023 fallout that led to their first podcast episode being deleted.

In August 2023, Bobbi interviewed Drake for her now-defunct podcast ‘The Really Good Podcast.’ Shortly after, the episode was removed from YouTube, and they both unfollowed each other on social media, fueling speculation.

In July 2025, Bobbi ended The Really Good Podcast and stepped back from regular uploads before announcing her new project earlier this year.

‘Not This Again’ debuted on September 2, 2025, with Drake as its first guest. The two are lying in bed – just like their original interview – and they talk about what happened back in 2023.

Drake and Bobbi finally explain podcast drama

During the episode, Bobbi admitted that the fallout between them stemmed from a message she’d sent during Drake’s Los Angeles concert in 2023, where she jokingly wrote “f**k you” while he was on stage. According to her, she was blocked soon after, and then she deleted the interview.

“And then that was the end of our friendship right there,” she said. “I just deleted it immediately. And people were like, “Why didn’t you archive it?” I was like, “I don’t ever want to see that again.”

The two went on to talk about how Bobbi was going through things at the time, including a divorce from her ex-husband Cory, and how she may have taken stress from that situation out on the Canadian rapper.

It may have taken a couple of years, but it’s clear Drake and Bobbi are friends again.
