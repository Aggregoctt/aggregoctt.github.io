Hollow Knight: Silksong officially launched at 10 am EST on September 4, immediately drawing massive attention from players worldwide.

The release overwhelmed digital storefronts, with Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo eShop all experiencing crashes shortly after launch. Even with the disruption, the game passed 100,000 concurrent players within its first 30 minutes.

At the same time, Silksong has become the most-watched game on Twitch. As of writing, it sits at more than 351,000 live viewers, putting it ahead of all other categories on the platform.

Silksong builds momentum on Twitch

Alongside its massive impact on Steam, Silksong’s Twitch debut has also set it apart, with streamers across the platform showcasing the sequel to hundreds of thousands of viewers at once.

Twitch star Caedrel is among those playing the game to thousands of viewers, and he quickly discovered aspects of the game that he considers “f**kin cool.”

The combination of record-breaking player counts and surging Twitch viewership highlights Silksong’s arrival as one of the biggest launches of 2025.
