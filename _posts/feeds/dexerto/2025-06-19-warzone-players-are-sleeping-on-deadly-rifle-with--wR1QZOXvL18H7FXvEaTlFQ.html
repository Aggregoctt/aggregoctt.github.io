This criminally underrated Warzone rifle outguns the game’s best weapons with an insane TTK, but no one is using it.

More often than not, the Warzone meta is dominated by just a handful of weapons. These tend to be Assault Rifles and SMGs, thanks to how easy they are to use and how versatile they are, covering you at multiple different ranges.

But there are plenty of other options in different categories that fly under the radar, despite having superior stats in many cases.

The DM-10 Marksman Rifle is perhaps the best example of this, as it has one of the best long-range TTKs around, capable of putting popular picks like the Krig C to shame.

Underrated DM-10 is one of Warzone’s best guns

CoD expert WhosImmortal highlighted the DM-10 in his June 18 video, where he compared the best long-range guns in Warzone. He called it one of the “fastest-killing options in the entire game,” explaining that it “melts” through players in the right hands.

With this in mind, we headed over to TrueGameData to see exactly how it stacks up, and the results were impressive.

It kills in just 600ms up to 46 meters, head and shoulders above any of the other meta weapons right now. The likes of the Kilo 141 and Krig C could only manage 640 and 658ms, respectively, and their damage dropped off much earlier.

Apart from a brief window between 50 and 55 meters where the Krig is superior, the DM-10 kills faster at all ranges. What’s most impressive, though, is that it still manages a TTK of 877ms at 80 meters, which can only be bettered by one-shot Sniper Rifles like the HDR.

However, these impressive figures do come with a slight drawback, as the rifle has a semi-automatic fire rate. This means you’ll need to have a rapid trigger finger and be extremely accurate to get the best out of it, which raises the skill gap.
