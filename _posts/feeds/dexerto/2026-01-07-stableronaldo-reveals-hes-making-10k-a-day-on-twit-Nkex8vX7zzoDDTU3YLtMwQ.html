Twitch streamer StableRonaldo has revealed how much money he is making per day on stream in early January, claiming he is averaging around $10,000 a day – but says this figure is actually low.

On his December 6 stream, StableRonaldo broke down his daily revenue on Twitch after averaging roughly 40,000 viewers per stream at the start of the month. He shared exact earnings for each day between January 1 and January 6, giving viewers a rare look at his streaming income.

According to the figures shown on stream, StableRonaldo earned $8,743 on January 1, followed by $9,888 on January 2 and $7,621 on January 3. His highest earning days came shortly after, with $12,469 on January 4 and $14,968 on January 5, before dropping to $7,120 on January 6.

How much StableRonaldo makes on Twitch

Combined, the six days added up to $60,809 in revenue, putting his average daily earnings just over $10,000. What’s more, based on his hours streamed, this works out to $1447 per hour.

Despite this, the streamer stressed that January has been a weak month compared to what he typically sees at the end of the year, due to lower ad revenue.

“If it was December,” he explained, “we would have already made hundreds of thousands already,” pointing to seasonal ad spending as the main difference.

Twitch creators often see fluctuations in revenue throughout the year, with advertisers typically spending more in November and December around Black Friday, Christmas, and end-of-year campaigns. January is generally a slower month across the platform as ad budgets reset.
