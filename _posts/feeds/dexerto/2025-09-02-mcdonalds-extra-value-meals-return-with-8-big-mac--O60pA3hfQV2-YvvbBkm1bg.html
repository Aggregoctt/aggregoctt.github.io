McDonald’s is bringing Extra Value Meals back to its menus across the United States starting September 8, giving customers new ways to save on some of the chain’s most popular items.

Back in August, McDonald’s revealed plans to bring back Extra Value Meals to give customers savings across the US. With franchisees agreeing to price combos at 15% below cost, the fast food giant is offering financial support for those stores.

To celebrate the return, breakfast fans can grab a $5 Sausage McMuffin with Egg meal that includes Hash Browns and a small coffee. Later in the day, customers can order an $8 Big Mac meal with medium fries and a medium drink.

Extra Value Meals lineup

The new rollout includes eight meal options across breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Each Extra Value Meal saves customers about 15 percent compared to buying items separately. The lineup features:

  • Sausage McMuffin with Egg
  • Sausage Egg & Cheese McGriddles
  • Egg McMuffin
  • Bacon, Egg, and Cheese Biscuit
  • Big Mac
  • 10-piece Chicken McNuggets
  • Quarter Pounder burgers (Original, Bacon, or Deluxe)
  • McCrispy Sandwiches (Original, Deluxe, or Spicy Deluxe)
