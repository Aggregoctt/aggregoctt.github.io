Resident Evil 4 fans with $3,600 burning a hole in their pocket can now get their hands on an ultra-realistic Ashley Graham statue, and it features silicone skin and real hair.

Ashley Graham may spend most of her time in Resident Evil 4 as a bit of an annoying escort mission, but she’s still one of the only real people Leon meets on his journey and was improved a great deal in the remake.

The 2023 version of Capcom’s survival horror classic was met with critical acclaim and now, Infinity Studio is letting everyone can get their hands on the President’s daughter.

Resident Evil 4 Ashley statue is super lifelike

Ashley’s statue comes with meticulously styled hair, crafted from milk fiber, while her skin uses platinum-grade silicone. She’s dressed in a custom fabric outfit designed to closely match her in-game look.

Plus, the base is detailed with candles and lanterns fitted with built-in LED lighting, recreating the game’s atmosphere in figure form.

The LED lighting is powered by two AAA batteries and two LR44 batteries.

The statue itself stands at an impressive 33 inches high and is 14 inches wide, making it quite an impressive work of art for any Resident Evil fan willing to dish out $3,600 for it.

Orders are going to be shipped out in early 2027, so fans will need to wait around a year to get their very own Ashley.

This isn’t the only time RE fans have been able to buy an absurdly large statue. In 2022, Capcom and PureArts joined to produce a 3-foot statue for Resident Evil Village’s Lady Dimitrescu, and it was listed at $1,500.
