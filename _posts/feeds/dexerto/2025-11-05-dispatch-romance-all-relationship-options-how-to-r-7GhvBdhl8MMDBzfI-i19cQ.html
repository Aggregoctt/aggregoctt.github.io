Dispatch puts you in the shoes of Robert Robertson (played by Aaron Paul), a retired superhero who’s now working as a dispatcher, managing a team of ex-villains and heroes to protect the planet. Naturally, during all the chaos, there’s always a little time for romance.

When it comes to narrative-heavy games like Dispatch, romance is always one of the first things on players’ minds. After all, you get to learn so much about the individual characters that it’s hard not to fall for them.

Luckily, there is plenty of romance in Dispatch, but just like real life, you have to be savvy and say the right things at the right time. So, here’s who you can romance, and more importantly, how you can make them fall in love with you – if only it were this easy irl.

Who can you romance?

Currently, you can romance two characters in Dispatch:

  • Blonde Blazer
  • Invisigal

Blonde Blazer was a romance option from episode one, where (spoilers) you’re able to kiss her. However, Invisigal was a romance option introduced much later on in the game, in episode four, so given we still have more episodes coming, there’s a chance more love is on its way. If it is, we’ll update this article.

How to romance Blonde Blazer

Thankfully, romancing Blonde Blazer is pretty simple, as you’re kind of led through that angle. All you need to do is be kind, kiss her when you’re given the option, and flirt as much as possible.

You’ll want to choose the following options (spoilers ahead)

  • Episode 1: “I like helping people.“
  • Episode 1: Kiss her in the Billboard scene.
  • Episode 2: “I’ll take your lead” in the conference room.
  • Episode 2: “Gotta get home to my dog” when meeting Phenomaman.
  • Episode 3: “I’m sorry, Blazer.” When you hear about her breakup.
  • Episode 3: “I like it.” When cutting a member of the team.
  • Episode 4: “Incredible” when she asks you how she looks.
  • Episode 4: Choose dinner with Blazer

The most important choice here (though you should choose all of these) is the dinner with Blazer, which places you on your first date and seals another kiss.

How to romance Invisigal

After Invisigal has a romantic dream about Robert, a romance begins between the two. This only begins in episode four, so it’s later down the line.

The only thing you currently need to choose is the following:

  • Episode 4: Choose a movie with Invisigal.

Currently, it’s not clear whether choosing the dinner or the movie hard-locks you into either romance, but it’s assumed the other won’t be too pleased you ditched them for someone else, so you’ll want to choose carefully.
