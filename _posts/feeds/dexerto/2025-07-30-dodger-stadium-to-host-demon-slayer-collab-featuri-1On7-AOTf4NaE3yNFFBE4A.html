Demon Slayer is teaming up with Dodger Stadium this August with a special collab event to celebrate Infinity Castle’s release. 

Dodger Stadium is set to host a unique crossover event on August 15, 2025, when the Los Angeles Dodgers face the San Diego Padres. The Demon Slayer collab will include exclusive themed activities celebrating the hit anime series. 

Meanwhile, fans in attendance will receive a limited-edition Dodgers x Demon Slayer hat, featuring Tanjiro Kamado’s iconic green and black checkered pattern alongside the team’s logo. 

Demon Slayer heads to Dodger Stadium for special collab

A ceremonial first pitch is also planned, and the evening will conclude with a drone show inspired by the upcoming Infinity Castle film, which is set to release in theaters across North America on September 12, 2025.

This isn’t the Dodgers’ first anime collaboration. Earlier this summer, the team hosted One Piece Night in partnership with Toei Animation, giving away co-branded straw hats to the first 40,000 fans and closing the night with a drone display featuring characters from the series. 

Dodger Stadium also recently held hololive Night, which included a drone show, a dance segment with VTuber talent like Ninomae Ina’nis and IRyS, and special collectible merchandise. Both events drew large crowds, proving that the Los Angeles Dodgers are committed to collaborating with popular anime in their games.
