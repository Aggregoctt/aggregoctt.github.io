FBC: Firebreak Twitch Drops enable players to get the free Overseer Armor Set, so here’s how you can unlock it. 

Remedy Entertainment is celebrating the release of FBC: Firebreak by giving players the chance to claim a free armor set. The Overseer Armor kits your character out in a purple jumpsuit, so you’ll easily stand out when fighting your fleshy foes. 

So, if you’re looking to play FBC: Firebreak when it launches on June 1 and want to add to your in-game drip, here’s how you can get your free Twitch Drop and how long it’ll be available for. 

Twitch drop event duration 

The FBC: Firebreak Twitch Drops event will begin on June 17, 2025, at 3AM PDT/11AM BST and run until July 8th, 2025, at 3AM PDT/11AM BST. 

How to get FBC: Firebreak Twitch Drops

To claim FBC: Firebeak Twitch Drops, you need to watch a stream in the FBC: Firebreak category that states “Drops enabled” for a total of three hours. Once you have done this, you’ll be rewarded with in-game items. 

You can find a detailed step-by-step guide below:

  • Boot up FBC: Firebreak.
  • Go to “Settings” and select the “Gameplay” option.
  • Head over to “Connect Twitch Account” or select “Connect Twitch Account” and scan the QR code on screen.
  • Link your Twitch account and FBC: Firebreak account together.
  • Watch eligible streams for three hours.
  • Claim your rewards from your Twitch Inventory. 
  • Open up FBC: Firebreak to get your rewards. 

Rewards

The Overseer Armor Set (Threshold Revision) is the only reward available. Unlike the regular armor, the Overseer kits your character out in a vibrant purple jumpsuit, as shown in the image below:

It’s important to note that FBC: Firebreak rewards will be available until July 8th, 2025, at 3AM PDT/11AM BST. Be sure to claim your reward as quickly as possible to avoid missing out.
