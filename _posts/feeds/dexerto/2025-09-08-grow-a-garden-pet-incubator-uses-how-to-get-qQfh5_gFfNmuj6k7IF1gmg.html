Pet Incubators are a vital feature in Grow a Garden, especially if you want to level up your adorable new pet in this popular Roblox adventure. But you’ll need to know how to get one and how they work first.

Added in the Grow a Garden Fairy event, which has players offering plants to a wishing well, pet incubators are a fantastic (and extrememly popular) new feature, especially if you want to increase your pets’ EXP.

However, nothing in Grow a Garden is ever easy, and you’re not told how to get hold of the Pet Incubator. So, here’s all you need to know from what it does, how to get it, and, of course, how to use the new machine.

What does the Pet Incubator do?

Unlike an Egg incubator, which speeds up the hatching process, a Pet Incubator will increase your pet’s EXP gain by 3x.

If you put your pet into the machine, power it with crops, and continue on their adventure, your pet will level up much faster than usual.

Increasing the level of your Pets in Grow a Garden is a vital part of the mid to late game mechanics. You’ll need to mutate them, perfect their passive abilities, and ensure your Pet turns the game into an idle machine. That’s what makes the Pet Incubator so important.

How to get

To get the Pet Incubator, you’ll need to reach level two with Raphael, the seller dealing in Pet Eggs.

To do this, you’ll need to feed Raphael cooked food, which will increase his friendship. The higher the rarity of your meal, the quicker they’ll level up, so be sure to make the rarest food you possibly can.

Once you reach level two with Raphael, you’ll be given a free Pet Incubator, meaning you don’t need to spend a penny to unlock this feature, as long as you have the food to level him up.

How to use

So, once you have the Pet Incubator and know what it does, it’s a matter of using it. To do so, you’ll want to place your Pet Incubator in your farm, like you would a piece of equipment. Then, place your chosen Pet inside and begin feeding it crops.

As you do that, your pet will begin levelling up. When it’s at the level you want, take it out. It’s as simple as that.
