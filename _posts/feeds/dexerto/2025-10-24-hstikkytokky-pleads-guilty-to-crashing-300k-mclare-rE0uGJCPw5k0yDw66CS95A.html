TikTok star HSTikkyTokky has pleaded guilty to dangerous driving after crashing a $300K McLaren in 2024 and spending a whole year on the run from the police.

On October 24, 24-year-old Harrison Sullivan, better known by his influencer name of HSTikkyTokky, appeared in court where he pleaded guilty to dangerous driving and driving without insurance.

A year earlier, Sullivan, who also streamed on Kick, crashed his $300,000 McLaren 720S and fled the vehicle and the scene, eventually going to Dubai.

Viral clips showed the mangled supercar with fellow UK influencer General G, a friend of HSTikkyTokky, lying on the ground nearby. The McLaren was pointed toward the curb with smoke billowing from it.

HSTikkyTokky held in custody until sentencing

Months later, in November, HSTikkyTokky became wanted by Surrey police after skipping a court appearance to compete in a Misfits boxing match in Qatar that ultimately didn’t take place.

After a year-long manhunt, the TikToker was finally apprehended on October 10 and held without bail.

In addition to the dangerous driving and driving without insurance charges, Sullivan was also charged with using a mobile phone while driving, but his lawyer got the charge dropped after successfully arguing that General G was the one filming.

According to the BBC, while at the Staines Magistrates’ Court, HSTikkyTokky only spoke twice to confirm personal information and thank the judge. He also blew a kiss to his mother as he was removed from the courtroom.

He will be held in custody until sentencing on November 14.
