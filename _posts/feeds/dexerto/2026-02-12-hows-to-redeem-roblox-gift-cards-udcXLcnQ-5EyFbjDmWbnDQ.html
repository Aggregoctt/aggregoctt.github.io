Roblox offers various gift cards for players to buy, granting Robux, premium memberships, and exclusive items as a reward for purchasing. However, redeeming them isn’t always easy.

Whether you’re looking to dive into Blox Fruit, Bee Swarm Simulator, Neighbors, or your favorite adventure, Roblox always has a place for its premium currency and paid features.

If you want to unlock the game’s premium features, you need Robux. This is often brought through gift cards.

However, once you’ve bought the gift card, you then have to figure out how to redeem it. So, here’s all you need to know, and what free rewards you can get for buying that gift card.

How to redeem a gift card in Roblox

It’s worth noting that you need to redeem a gift card on your PC or through a browser. You cannot redeem a Roblox gift card on your console or through the mobile app.

  • Head to the website.
  • Log in or create an account.
  • Find the code or pin on your gift card and enter it into the box.
  • Click Redeem.

Once you’ve done that, you’ll get a success message, and the credit you brought will be added to your account for you to spend as you wish. The free items you’ll get below will also be added to your account automatically.
