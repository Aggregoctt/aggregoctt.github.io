Logan Paul exposed Jake Paul for moving the goalposts on his long-awaited fight with KSI, claiming his Prime Hydration business partner still wants it.

For years, it seemed like Jake Paul and KSI were on a collision course to meet in the boxing ring. KSI had a win over Logan Paul on his record, while Jake had beaten KSI’s brother, Deji, as well. 

The longtime rivals even agreed to make their superfight happen at Wembley back in 2022, but in the over three years since then, they’ve not come close. Jake has focused on fighting ‘real’ boxers, while KSI has stepped away from fighting and is more of a promoter now. 

It is still a fight that some fans want to see, including Logan. The imPaulsive host even offered them the chance to fight at his recent wedding. 

Jake Paul questions Logan over KSI fight

Well, with Jake preparing to take on Gervonta ‘Tank’ Davis in an eye-catching exhibition, Logan once again hit back at his brother for not fighting KSI.

“Why doesn’t KSI want to fight me? What’s the deal?” Jake asked Logan while streaming with Adin Ross. 

“Bro, stop. We’ve gone over this a thousand times. He does want to fight you at 190lbs, and you don’t want to fight him at 190,” Logan fired back. Jake suggested he would fight his rival at 192lbs, before joking about compromising at 191.25lbs.

The clip of the chat has racked up over 2 million views on X (formerly Twitter) alone, with plenty of viewers mocking Jake over it.

“Imagine ducking your biggest bag fight,” one said. “Holy s*it, 2.5 pound difference, this is ducking to the extreme,” another added. “You’ll go down to 195 to fight tank but you won’t go down to 190 to fight KSI,” another chimed in. 

The ‘Problem Child’ did reveal that his fight with ‘Tank’ will land him around $40 million, which is some going for an exhibition bout.
