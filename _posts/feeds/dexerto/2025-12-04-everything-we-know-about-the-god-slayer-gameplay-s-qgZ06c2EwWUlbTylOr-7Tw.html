The God Slayer is an upcoming open-world RPG that blends Avatar: The Last Airbender-style elemental abilities with flashy martial arts moves, and you can check out the gameplay, story, and trailer right now.

On December 4, Pathea Games, the developers behind cozy game My Time at Portia, released a seven-minute gameplay video for The God Slayer. The Eastern-inspired steampunk game aims to excite martial arts fans with its unique abilities, which give players the ability to take down deadly enemies and even deadlier bosses.

So, whether you’re currently waiting for the next Where Winds Meet update or just a fan of wuxia-style martial arts, then our handy hub has everything you need to know about The God Slayer and what you can expect when it launches.

Platforms

The God Slayer is coming to PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S.

No, there is currently no confirmed release date. The God Slayer’s Steam store page lists the launch timing as “to be announced.”

Story

The God Slayer takes place in a Steampunk world where gods designed humans to cultivate special energy that would return to the heavens after death. However, when some humans learned to harness this energy as elemental power and became Elemancers, the Celestials struck back with devastating force.

You play as Cheng, an Elemancer who seeks revenge on the gods for the death of his family. It’s up to you to the Celestial gods and “rise to be the God Slayer.”

Gameplay

As shown in the seven-minute gameplay trailer, players can use elemental abilities to take down their enemies in flashy martial arts-inspired combat. Cheng uses his fiery fists to pummel soldiers, soaks groups of enemies with water before freezing them, rips slabs of earth from the ground to hurl as projectiles, and even raises stone walls mid-fight to block attacks.

While unarmed combat takes centre stage, players will also be able to wield weapons dropped from defeated foes, including a massive hammer that can send enemies flying.

Progression also revolves around mastering qi, unlocking skills from scrolls and elemental masters, and strengthening Cheng enough to challenge the godly Celestials.
