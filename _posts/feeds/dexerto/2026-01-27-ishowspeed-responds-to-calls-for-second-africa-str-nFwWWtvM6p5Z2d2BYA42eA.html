IShowSpeed has revealed that he will return for a second tour of Africa, teasing a “season two” as his current tour comes to a close. 

Over the last few years, IShowSpeed’s streams have been less about FIFA, Fortnite, and reacting to YouTube videos, and more focused on travelling the globe. Speed has ticked off streaming tours of Europe, North America, South America, Asia, and Australia. 

His long-awaited tour of Africa kicked off at the start of 2026 too, with the YouTube star finally surpassing the 50 million subscriber mark along the way. He’s visited more than 20 countries too, but that means that he has missed out on some big ones during his trip. 

With the streaming tour coming to an end, fans have been begging Speed to extend his trip so that he can tick off another set of countries. However, while he’s not doing it right now, he has good news for those fans. 

IShowSpeed confirms he’ll return for second Africa tour

During his January 26 stream, Speed revealed that this tour of Africa has only been “season one,” and that he is already plotting a second tour in the future. 

“We have traveled to 20 countries on the continent of Africa. Like, that’s insane. In 28 days, that’s such a fast time,” he said. “Tomorrow will basically be the ending of part one of the Africa season. 

“This season is ending, but that doesn’t mean that there won’t be a season two. There will be a season two of Africa, chat.”

During the early part of his tour, Speed said that he’s focused more on travel content so that fans can see things they may never get to experience for themselves. 

“A raw, true experience of me being at that certain location. Whatever experience it is, I’m your test dummy,” he said.
