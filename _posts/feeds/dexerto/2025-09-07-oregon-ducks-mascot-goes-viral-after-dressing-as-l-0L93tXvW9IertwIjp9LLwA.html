The Oregon Duck has gone viral once again, this time by dressing up as the internet-famous Labubu doll.

Labubu dolls, created by Hong Kong artist Kasing Lung, have exploded in popularity online, with collectors paying hundreds of dollars for the tiny, toothy figures. The wide-eyed characters have become a status symbol on social media, and have recently been the target of a South Park episode.

Meanwhile, the Oregon Duck has made a habit of stealing the spotlight this season. For the team’s opening game against Montana State, the beloved mascot appeared in a full Hannah Montana getup, complete with wig and outfit, instantly earning attention from fans both in the stadium and online.

Labubu x Oregon Duck mascot

For Week 2, the Duck leveled up his antics, appearing in an elaborate Labubu costume that included a full-body suit, hood, and the doll’s signature jagged teeth. The mascot debuted the look on X (formerly Twitter), where clips of the appearance quickly racked up millions of views.

The Oregon Ducks went on to make headlines on the field as well, crushing Oklahoma State in a 69–3 blowout, but for many fans, it was the mascot’s viral cosplay that stole the show.

The Duck hasn’t revealed what he’ll wear for Week 3, but his recent streak has fans keeping a close eye on his next move.
