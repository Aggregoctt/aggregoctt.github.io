Nickelodeon star spotted with Billie Eilish after dating rumors ---

As the ‘Hit Me Hard and Soft’ tour lands in Europe, romance rumors between Billie Eilish and Nat Wolff have gone viral online. But who is the former Nickelodeon star?

23-year-old Eilish is three albums in and taking the world by storm. Her most recent tour started in the US in September 2024, and isn’t expected to end until November 2025. Straight after winning an Oscar for the hit Barbie movie song ‘What Was I Made For?’, it’s no mean feat.

Even so, the star is now embroiled in her own dating rumor scandal. If Tom Cruise and Ana de Armas weren’t enough, Eilish has recently been linked to Nat Wolff after photos emerged of the pair in Venice, Italy.

You might have heard the name before, but who exactly is Nat Wolff, and what was he best known for on Nickelodeon?

Who is Nat Wolff?

If you’re a young millennial or older Gen Z, you’ll likely know 30-year-old Nat Wolff best for his days on Nickelodeon show The Naked Brothers Band. These days he’s a musician, touring with Billie Eilish as an opening act since 2024.

The Naked Brothers Band ran on Nickelodeon from 2007-2009, with Nat starring alongside younger brother Alex Wolff. The mockumentary-style show followed them as they rehearse, perform, and live “extraordinary teen lives,” and was created by their mother, Polly Draper.

After that, fans saw Wolff in the likes of The Fault in Our Stars, Paper Towns, and the Netflix Death Note adaptation. You can also spot him in Eilish’s music video for ‘Chihiro.’

Wolff released music as a duo with his brother as recently as 2023, revealing they are currently working on a third album as of March 8, 2024.

In the late 2000s, he dated fellow Nickelodeon star Miranda Cosgrove, then dated Margaret Qualley and Grace Van Patten in the years after. He’s also been previously romantically linked to Ashley Benson and Bella Thorne.

Nat Wolff Billie Eilish dating rumors go viral after kiss snap

The pair are currently in Italy for the European leg of Eilish’s ‘Hit me Hard and Soft’ tour, snapped kissing on a balcony on June 8, 2025. Originally published by DeuxMoi, the photographs have since gone viral.

The pair supposedly met at an Oscars party in 2024, seen attending Coachella together with Alex Wolff in April of the same year. The music duo began opening for Eilish on the tour in September 2024. Alex Wolff can be seen telling Eilish in a Vogue ‘Off The Cuff’ video, “Your family is literally the greatest thing that’s ever happened to me.”

Dating rumors first began in March 2025, with Eilish and Nat seen leaving the iHeart Music Video awards together. A Page Six source shut this down, stating they are “nothing more” than “close friends,” adding, “Billie is affectionate with all of her friends.” However, further sources then said the pair were being “very affectionate” when out bar-hopping on March 25, 2025.

In an interview with Vogue in 2024, Eilish stated, “I wish no one knew anything about my sexuality or anything about my dating life. Ever, ever, ever. And I hope that they never will again. And I’m never talking about my sexuality ever again. And I’m never talking about who I’m dating ever again.”

She previously dated The Neighbourhood frontman Jesse Rutherford from 2022 to 2023, and was romantically linked to Brandon Adams, Matthew Tyler Vorce, and Quenlin Blackwell beforehand.
