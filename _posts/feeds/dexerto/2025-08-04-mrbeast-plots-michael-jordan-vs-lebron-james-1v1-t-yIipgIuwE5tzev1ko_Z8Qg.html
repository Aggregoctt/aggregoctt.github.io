MrBeast is eyeing one of his wildest video concepts yet—pitting LeBron James against Michael Jordan in a 1v1 to settle the NBA’s GOAT debate once and for all.

Speaking with Adin Ross, the YouTube megastar floated the fantasy matchup during a casual conversation, drawing inspiration from one of his own most viral uploads.

“One of my most viewed videos last year was when I had Cristiano Ronaldo play a random fan one-on-one for a million dollars,” he said.

“So I was randomly thinking the other day, imagine getting LeBron and Michael Jordan to go 1v1, but we handicap LeBron in some way.”

The internet reacts to MrBeast’s Lebron vs Jordan video

The idea instantly caught fire online. Despite Jordan being 62 and LeBron still going strong at 40, fans couldn’t resist imagining how it might play out—especially with a MrBeast-style twist to level the playing field.

“Never ever happening, but if it did, it would break the internet,” one user wrote.

“This would be the most boring video ever, Michael Jordan would crush LeBron so hard it wouldn’t even be enjoyable,” another said.

“It’s crazy to think that if Jordan was in his prime, LeBron wouldn’t even get a shot up,” a third chimed in.

Adin Ross was all-in on the pitch, calling it “the craziest video ever” and saying, “If anyone does it, it’s you.”

The LeBron vs Jordan debate has raged for decades, with MJ holding six rings and a perfect Finals record, while LeBron boasts unmatched longevity, all-time scoring dominance, and four titles of his own. But a head-to-head showdown—no matter how unrealistic—would be an internet-breaking event.

Even MrBeast himself doesn’t think it’s a doable challenge, claiming the two basketball icons would “never do it” despite thinking of ways to make the 1v1 a reality.

While the odds of either NBA legend accepting are slim, maybe MrBeast could convince them to team up for a Space Jam-style adventure in his upcoming big-budget animated project.
