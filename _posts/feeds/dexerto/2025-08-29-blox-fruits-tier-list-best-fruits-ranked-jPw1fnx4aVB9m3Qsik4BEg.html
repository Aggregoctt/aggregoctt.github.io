Blox Fruits is one of Roblox’s biggest hits, and at the core of its gameplay are the powerful fruits that can completely change how you fight and explore. Not all fruits work the same way, either, as some dominate PvP with insane combos, while others excel in PvE grinding or mobility.

That’s why, depending on whether you’re grinding for mastery, tackling raids, or fighting other pirates, choosing the right one can be the difference between sailing smoothly and getting wiped in battle.

With each update shifting the balance, knowing which fruits are the best of the best is key to staying ahead, so read on for a full Blox Fruits tier list, detailing how each fruit shapes up in the current meta.

Best fruits in Blox Fruits

Our tiers explained

  • S: The absolute best. The most used in the current meta.
  • A: Very strong and flexible.
  • B: Situational. Strong but can be outclassed.
  • C: Weak or too niche in the meta.

While some Fruits may be suited slightly better to PVP or PVE over the other, our rankings above consider the general placement for them overall.

In PvP, high-mobility and combo-heavy fruits excel, while those that focus on defense for grinding are better in PvE. In the sections below, where relevant, we’ve detailed when a Fruit is slightly better in PvE or PvP.

S-Tier fruits

Kitsune

Kitsune quickly cemented itself as one of the best fruits in Blox Fruits because it balances raw power, mobility, and combo potential, making it lethal in PvP and efficient in PvE.

Its wide-ranging AoE moves hit hard with little end-lag, while invisibility and fast combos make it a nightmare for opponents to track.

If you’re looking to dominate the battlefield with fluid, high-impact attacks and game-winning speed, Kitsune is a top-tier choice across the board.

Dough

Dough is extremely useful in the current meta thanks to its unmatched combo potential and oppressive crowd control. With long stuns, reliable grabs, and heavy-hitting abilities, it can lock opponents in place while dishing out massive damage, making it a nightmare to face in PvP.

This fruit is also a powerhouse in PvE due to its AoE coverage and sustained DPS, letting players shred through bosses and raids with ease.

Dragon

Even though Dragon is one of the most explosive fruits in Blox Fruits, it is ideal for players who want to rule the game with sheer force, as it has huge burst damage and a wide AoE that can quickly overwhelm anyone caught in its path.

When transformed, Dragon becomes even scarier, with boosted speed, damage, and combo potential to terrifying levels that make it one of the best fruits for grinding in PvE. It does, though, take a bit more skill to master compared to Dough or Kitsune, and it’s not as good in PvP as it can be easily countered.

Buddha

Despite updates and new additions, Buddha has cemented its place as one of the ultimate best fruits in Blox Fruits. Its massive hitbox, boosted range, and defensive power make it ideal for farming mobs, bosses, and raids without breaking a sweat.

While it lacks the flashy combos of Kitsune or Dragon, Buddha’s consistency and tankiness keep it firmly as an S-tier for PvE. In PvP, it’s more situational, but in the right setup, it can still catch opponents off guard.

Shadow

Shadow has relentless chip damage and a control-heavy playstyle. Its lingering effects and AoE abilities make it perfect for pressuring opponents, forcing mistakes, and setting up devastating combos.

With its transformation boosting both speed and damage, Shadow thrives in PvP while still holding its own in PvE.

Spirit

The key to Spirit is its versatility, as it offers a mix of offense and support. This fruit can summon spirits that attack, heal, or grant speed. This means it provides players with steady damage and utility, while its abilities allow them to pressure enemies from a safe distance.

This balance makes Spirit good at both PvP and PvE, as it has damage, utility, and crowd control.

Venom

Venom is one of the most potent fruits in Blox Fruits, as it excels at wearing opponents down with its powerful poison damage and massive AoE control.

The transformation takes Venom to another level, unlocking even more damage and pressure with a monstrous form that dominates both PvP and PvE.

Leopard

As expected, Leopard is one of the fastest and most aggressive fruits, built around raw speed and burst damage. Its ranged attacks are incredibly fast and hit hard, plus its hybrid fighting style lets players dive in and out of combat with ease.

The transformation gives Leopard insane mobility and lethal combos that can shred through PvP duels and raid bosses alike. Though in PvE, it’s just considered mediocre for grinding, as its hitboxes are inconsistent and hard to hit.

A-Tier fruits

Light

Light is a good choice that offers players unmatched mobility and quick, spammable attacks. Its speed makes it great for traveling and grinding, while its piercing moves allow players to stand their ground.

Though it doesn’t hit as hard as top-tier powerhouses, Light shines in PvE for farming and remains a strong PvP option in the right hands.

Dark

Dark is a skilled fruit to use as it gives the player the ability to create, become, and manipulate darkness to stun, pull, and attack. It offers strong stuns and combos that make it a solid choice for PvP.

Its grab and crowd-control abilities let players lock opponents down and set up devastating follow-ups with swords or guns. However, it does have a rather small AoE and range, which leaves it behind other fruits in PvE.

Ice

Ice is a fan-favorite thanks to its versatile abilities and utility for Bounty Hunting and grinding. It is powerful, with high crowd control, and an easy-to-use combo setup.

Its fast, low-cooldown abilities can freeze enemies in place, making it perfect for chaining sword or gun attacks in PvP. It also performs well in PvE with solid AoE and reliable stuns.

Magma

Magma is one of the best options for players who want raw damage without overcomplicating their kit. Its huge AoE and burn effects make grinding and raid clears feel effortless, as enemies simply melt under the pressure of its abilities.

Despite not being flashy or quick, Magma still makes up for it with consistency. Its zoning tools and punishing damage keep it relevant in PvP, but where it really shines is in PvE farming.

Lightning

Lightning, previously known as Rumble, has become a strong pick in the meta thanks to its high damage, speed, and reliable stuns.

It offers plenty of combo potential, AoE, and movement ability that make it dangerous in PvP. Plus, it is one of the best fruits for grinding in PvE due to its Elemental Reflex, high damage, large AoE, and stuns.

The awakened form takes it even further, adding stronger AoE and faster casts that can shred through raids.

Gravity

Gravity is all about control, giving players the tools to pull enemies in and dominate space on the battlefield. Its abilities make it tricky for opponents to escape, opening the door for clean combos and heavy punishments in PvP.

It requires a little bit of mastery, but it is worth it, as it is one of the most powerful and abusable fruits in PvP due to its high damage output, expansive AoE capabilities, long stuns, and range. That same mastery requirement, though, makes it a less appealing choice for PvE – at least in the First Sea.

Spider

Spider, formerly known as String, is a versatile fruit that shines through its multi-target moves and large hitboxes. Its stun-heavy attacks give players plenty of room to chain sword or gun attacks, making it a dangerous pick in PvP when awakened.

Spider’s range and utility give it a solid spot in the meta, especially in group fights where its control tools can disrupt multiple opponents.

Portal

Portal, previously known as Door, is one of the most utility-packed fruits in Blox Fruits, offering unmatched mobility and evasiveness. Its teleporting abilities make it easy to reposition, dodge, or chase down opponents, giving players a huge edge in PvP – especially if they pair it with swords.

Despite its strength, though, Portal is a close-range fruit, so it not only requires skill and practice to exploit its full potential, but it also falls short in PvE.

Blizzard

Blizzard is a strong fruit that thrives on PvE thanks to its wide AoE damage and flight ability, which allows the player to deal infinite damage.

The versatility of Blizzard lies in its mix of stuns, high damage output, and high combo potential. Its icy storms and ranged attacks make it great for zoning enemies in PvP while also farming mobs and bosses with ease.

Sound

Sound is considered a reliable all-rounder. Its moves hit in wide arcs with strong knockback, giving players solid zoning tools and a straightforward kit that’s easy to pick up in PvP.

While it doesn’t deal insane pressure, Sound’s range and utility let it control space effectively. It’s also handy in PvE for clearing mobs, though it lacks the raw farming power of other higher-ranking fruits.

T-Rex

T-Rex is built around raw power and disruptive attacks. This makes it good for farming and excellent in PvP thanks to its move sets, which have in-built combos.

Its moves hit hard and cover wide areas, making it a threat in raids and boss fights where sustained damage really matters. However, T-Rex can be tricky to use as it requires high mastery, so it is better to save it for the mid to late game.

Yeti

Yeti currently sits as the third most expensive fruit in Blox Fruits and is one of the most sought-after due to its incredibly high power.

This particular fruit is excellent for PvP, grinding, and carrying raids when transformed. Moreover, it offers great team support due to the ice platforms it can create. However, like most Mythical fruits, it requires high mastery.

Gas

Gas is a tricky fruit that leans on its zoning and damage-over-time effects. Its poison-like clouds can lock down space and chip away at enemies, making it useful for keeping pressure in both PvP and PvE.

It has extremely high AoE, damage, and combos, which is why it is considered one of the best tank and anti-teamers of the game.

While it has solid utility and can be frustrating to fight against, Gas struggles to keep up at higher levels of play.

B-Tier fruits

Control

Control is a unique fruit in Blox Fruits as it is good for almost every stat build, making it good in PvP due to its versatility, high damage output, and combo potential. It does require skilled players, as its moves can be tricky to land.

It’s flashy and powerful when mastered, but inconsistent compared to meta staples, making it bad for PvE.

Phoenix

Phoenix is as powerful as it is expensive. However, it is a good fruit to keep an eye on, as it allows players to hover and move through the air while unleashing large, burning AoE moves.

In PvP, it is very annoying to go up against because of its healing, flying, and stunning capabilities. And while regular Phoenix is good for grinding in PvE, its awakened form is great at evasion and survival, making it a top pick for Sea events and raids.

Quake

Quake is known for its large hitboxes, drastic screen shake, and good stun power. Its wide-range shockwaves make it effective for controlling groups of enemies in PvE and catching opponents off guard in PvP.

The downside is that its moves have a considerable amount of knockback, but it’s still a fun and powerful fruit to try out.

Mammoth

Mammoth is a tanky Beast-type fruit that leans on raw durability and heavy-hitting moves. Its transformation gives a big stat boost, and because it doesn’t require a full meter, it can stay like that as long as the player wants.

In PvP, Mammoth offers huge hitboxes and solid defense, while its constant damage and decent AoE attacks make it great for PvE’s Sea events and raids. It’s a fun, versatile, bruiser-style option, but its lack of speed and mobility keeps it in the B-tier.

Sand

Sand’s use is very situational. In PvP, its unawakened version is awful. However, its awakened form excels in this mode due to its move hitboxes, damage, speed, stun, and versatility. In PvE, it can handle grinding thanks to its area damage.

In general, Sand’s moves can feel sluggish, and without awakening, it falls behind quickly.

Diamond

Diamond is a defensive-leaning fruit in Blox Fruits that gives players extra durability and a bit of crowd control. Most of its moves have large hitboxes and deal huge damage, making it good for PvP. However, keep in mind that it can only use attacks while transformed.

The problem with Diamond is that recent nerfs left it outclassed by sturdier fruits like Buddha or more disruptive picks like Dark.

Rubber

Rubber is a fun but situational fruit in Blox Fruits, mainly used for countering guns and grinding thanks to its long-ranged attacks. It does, however, require a great deal of accuracy.

This fruit is better for grinding than most regular ones, but it doesn’t offer the efficiency needed for fast grinding in PvE and struggles to keep pace in high-level PvP.

Love

Don’t let its design and name fool you, Love is a powerful fruit great for bounty hunting, as it has abilities that can apply debuffs to its enemies. Plus, it can summon an ally and attack while hovering in the air without consuming energy (unlike Phoenix).

It excels at PvP, PvE grinding, and raids thanks to its huge range, decent damage, and fast combos, though it lacks consistency.

Creation

Creation, previously known as Barrier, is a utility-heavy fruit with a unique playstyle of customizable builds for all types of activities.

It is very powerful in PvP due to its extremely large hitboxes, long stuns, and good defensive abilities. It can still work for grinding in PvE because of its low mastery requirements, and is a solid support in raids and sea exploration.

Smoke

Smoke is one of the more beginner-friendly fruits in Blox Fruits, giving players simple AoE moves that make early grinding and low-level PvP feel easy.

It has multiple stunning attacks and deals impressive damage when compared to other common fruits. However, once you progress into the later seas, Smoke quickly falls off.

Flame

Flame is a classic starter fruit in Blox Fruits, known for its simple, AoE-heavy kit that makes early grinding smooth and accessible.

In PvP, it is extremely powerful – mainly when awakened – as it benefits from long stuns, huge hitboxes, and high damage. Its abilities are easy to use and can push enemies away, making it good for PvE grinding and raids, too.

Still, Flame struggles to keep up with raw power or the utility.

C-tier fruits

Eagle

Eagle, previously known as Falcon, focuses on flight and mobility – as expected. Its transformation grants wings for fast travel and some aerial attacks, making it decent for moving around the map or staying away from enemy fire in PvP.

Its stun moves, large AoE, and combo potential make it a good alternative for Buddha in PvE. However, keep in mind that it falls short when facing runners or fast fruits in general.

Rocket

Rocket’s main appeal is that it allows the user to shoot out rocket missiles, dealing explosive damage, while also being able to fly.

Even though it is the cheapest fruit in the game, its decent AoE and attacks are strong enough to make it useful for grinding in PvE, and potentially PvP.

Blade

Blade, previously known as Chop, has instinct-breaking moves, good damage, decent mobility, can stun enemies, and gives the player immunity to all types of sword attacks, making it a good choice for PvP. It can also work for grinding in all seas in PvE, especially for inexperienced players.

Its moves are straightforward, dealing moderate damage with some utility, but they lack the range and power of higher-tier fruits, leaving it outclassed.

Spring

Spring is a quirky fruit built around fast, bouncing attacks and mobility tricks. While it can catch opponents off guard with sudden movement, its damage and hitboxes are too limited to make a real impact in PvP.

In PvE, Spring doesn’t fare much better, since its single-target focus makes grinding inefficient compared to AoE-heavy fruits like Magma or Buddha.

Bomb

Bomb is one of the more straightforward fruits, with explosive AoE moves that make early-game grinding simple and fun. It’s easy to use, and its big blasts can clear groups of enemies quickly at lower levels.

The problem is that its damage and utility fall behind compared to other choices. While decent for beginners, it’s outclassed in both PvP and PvE, leaving it very well placed as a C-tier pick.

Spike

Spike is a very basic fruit that relies on the thorns in its body to deal damage to opponents in close range. Its attacks don’t offer much range or AoE, making it weak for grinding in PvE.

In general, its lack of mobility and combo potential make it bad for PvP, unless you use it in the early stages, where inexperienced players can benefit from its low mastery requirement.

Ghost

Ghost, previously known as Revive, has the ability to give players a second chance in battle, reviving them once after being defeated. Its kit also includes spooky crowd-control moves that can disrupt enemies in PvP.

While the revive mechanic is fun and occasionally clutch, Ghost’s overall damage is lacking compared to top picks. It’s situationally useful but inconsistent, keeping it in the lower C-tier range in the current meta.

Pain

Pain, previously known as Paw, deals solid chip damage with its projectiles and can push enemies back to create space.

It is one of the weakest fruits due to its poor grinding ability and lack of elemental reflex. However, its long-distance playstyle makes it alright for poking in PvP and for safe farming in PvE.

Spin

As the name says, Spin can spin around extremely rapidly, turning the air into attacks that can stun and damage opponents.

It is considered one of the weakest fruits in the game, with ranged capabilities and almost no mobility, making it bad in both PVP and PvE grinding.
