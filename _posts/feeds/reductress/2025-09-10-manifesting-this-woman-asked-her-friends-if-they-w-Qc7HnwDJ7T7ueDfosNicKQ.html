In a story that demonstrates the true power of manifestation, 27-year-old Kate Robby asked her friends if they were mad at her so many times that they did, in fact, become mad at her.

You create your reality!

“I knew it,” Kate told reporters. “I knew they hated me. I just had to get them to admit it.”

Yes! Speak your thoughts into existence!

However, when reached for questioning, Kate’s friends said they weren’t so much “admitting it” as they were slowly becoming angry with her due to her persistent questioning on the matter.

“Kate’s great, it’s super hard to be mad at her,” Kate’s friend, Gianna, told reporters. “Unless she’s asking you if you’re mad at her, in which case she becomes the most annoying person on the planet.”

See? Words have power!

Unfortunately, as much as Kate’s friends tried to explain this to her, she just couldn’t seem to grasp the concept.

“I was wondering if they were mad at me and they are,” she said. “What’s so confusing about that?”

She continued, saying, “If you live as if something’s already true, it’s only a matter of time before it actually does become true.”

That’s the Law of Assumption at work!

“I’ve just always kind of been able to speak my thoughts into existence,” Kate explained to reporters. “Like when I asked my dad if he wished I’d never been born. Which, after 10 hours of asking, he, in fact, did. Or when I badgered my roommate about if she had any regrets about us living together. It was hard to hear her through her closed bedroom door, but I’m pretty sure she said, ‘I do now!’ So, yeah. I just have a sense about these sorts of things.”

As of press time, Kate had asked the reporters if they thought she was being annoying about the whole thing so many times that they eventually were forced to admit that, yes, she was.
