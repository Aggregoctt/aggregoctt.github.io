Want to connect with 1,000+ founders, investors, and tech leaders gathering in Boston for TechCrunch All Stage on July 15?

Apply by June 10 to host a Side Event during “TC All Stage Week” (July 13-19) — and put your brand front and center.

Whether you’re planning a workshop, cocktail hour, product demo, or panel — you’re in control. Make your mark. Build buzz. Apply here before the deadline.

Why host a Side Event?

Side Events extend the action beyond the main event — and give your brand the spotlight. As a host, you’ll receive:

  • Visibility to the full TechCrunch audience
  • Featured on the official agenda, Side Events page, and mobile app
  • Highlighted in attendee emails and articles
  • Custom discount code for you and your team

Know before you apply

You handle the logistics, we’ll help spread the word. It’s free to be listed, just make sure your event is:

  • In-person and Boston-based
  • Held July 13-19 (only post-5 p.m. ET events allowed on July 15)
  • 18+ only (21+ if there’s alcohol)
