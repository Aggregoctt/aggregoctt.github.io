Google on Thursday said it has begun rolling out its Veo 3 video generation model to Gemini users in more than 159 countries.

Video generation via the new model is available only to paying subscribers of Google’s AI Pro plan, and is capped at 3 videos per day.

Veo 3, which Google showed off in May, lets users to generate videos up to 8 seconds long using text prompts.
