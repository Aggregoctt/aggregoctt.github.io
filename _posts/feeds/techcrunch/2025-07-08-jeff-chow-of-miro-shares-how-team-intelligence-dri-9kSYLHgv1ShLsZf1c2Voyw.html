AI can help your team move faster — but only if your team knows how to move together.

Join Jeff Chow, Chief Product and Technology Officer at Miro, for a roundtable sessions at TechCrunch All Stage on July 15 in Boston’s SoWa Power Station, where he’ll break down how product-led innovation can supercharge collaborative problem-solving. This session, titled “Building Team Intelligence: How Product-Led Innovation Transforms Collaborative Problem-Solving,” and is built for founders, product leaders, and startup teams looking to work smarter in the age of AI.

From startups to scaleups, how your team thinks together may be more important than what they build. Chow will share frameworks that empower teams to align across disciplines, speed up ideation cycles, and balance AI-powered efficiency with human judgment.

From Miro to MIT — leadership built on insight and impact

With 25+ years of experience building high-growth organizations and customer-first digital products, Jeff Chow has been at the helm of transformational teams at companies like Google, TripAdvisor, InVision, and now Miro. His career spans founding startups, leading C-suite turnarounds, and scaling collaboration platforms used by millions.

What sets Jeff apart? He doesn’t just build products — he builds teams that know how to solve problems together. Expect real-world takeaways on:

  • How to create inclusive product design that works from the intern to the CPO
  • Where AI implementation makes the biggest impact — and where humans must lead
  • Building a team culture that encourages strategic, cross-functional problem-solving
  • Elevating product decisions by combining data, insight, and feedback loops

Whether you’re scaling your first product or refining your team’s decision-making, this session will show you how product-led strategy can unlock smarter collaboration.
