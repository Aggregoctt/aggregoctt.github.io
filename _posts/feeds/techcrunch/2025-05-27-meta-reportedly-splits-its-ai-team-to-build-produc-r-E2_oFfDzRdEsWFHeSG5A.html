Meta is dividing its AI department into two new teams, according to a report from Axios. Now, the company’s AI personnel will be assigned to an AI products team, or an AGI Foundations unit. No jobs appear to have been cut.

The AI products team will focus on consumer-facing efforts like the AI features within Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, as well as Meta’s new standalone AI app.

The AGI Foundations unit will focus on more of the bigger picture efforts, like improving its Llama models.
