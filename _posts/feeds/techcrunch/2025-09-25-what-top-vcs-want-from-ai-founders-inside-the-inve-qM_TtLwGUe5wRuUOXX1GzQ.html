Who’s speaking

  • Jon McNeill, CEO and co-founder, DVx Ventures — Jon has launched 12 companies through his venture studio, built and scaled businesses delivering multifold returns, and led operations at Tesla and Lyft during periods of dramatic growth.
  • Aileen Lee, founder and managing partner, Cowboy Ventures — Aileen invests in early-stage enterprise and consumer software companies like Dollar Shave Club, Drata Security, Guild, and Ironclad. She is the person who coined the term “unicorn,” co-founded All Raise, and is a longtime voice for women in tech.
  • Steve Jang, founder and managing partner, Kindred Ventures — Steve is a Midas List investor in AI, decentralized systems, infrastructure, and frontier tech. His portfolio includes early bets in Uber, Coinbase, Perplexity, Fal, and PlayAI.

What you’ll learn

This session goes beyond pitch decks to reveal what investors are watching. Expect insights on:

  • Signals investors look for before writing the check.
  • How to distinguish yourself in a crowded landscape.
