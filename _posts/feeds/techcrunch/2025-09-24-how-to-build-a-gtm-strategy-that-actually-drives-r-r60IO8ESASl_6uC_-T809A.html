Lessons from leaders who’ve done it at scale

Max Altschuler, Alison Wagonfeld, and Marc Manara bring a rare combination of startup grit, enterprise scale, and AI-native insight to the go-to-market conversation. Alison leads global marketing across Google Cloud Platform and Workspace as CMO of Google Cloud, blending brand and performance at massive scale.

And Marc leads the global Startups, VC, and Private Equity team at OpenAI, drawing from experience at AWS, his own startup journey, and now the front lines of AI’s impact on GTM strategy. Together, they offer a 360-degree view of what it takes to build and scale a go-to-market engine that actually works.
