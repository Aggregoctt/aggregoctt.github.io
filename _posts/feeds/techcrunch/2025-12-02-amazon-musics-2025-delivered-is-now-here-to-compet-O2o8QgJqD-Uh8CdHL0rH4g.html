Amazon Music announced on Tuesday the launch of 2025 Delivered, the platform’s take on Spotify’s popular Wrapped experience. The feature offers users a personalized annual summary of their music listening habits.

With 2025 Delivered, users will see a recap of their listening trends, showcasing their top artists, favorite songs, and preferred genres, along with stats for audiobooks and podcasts. 

Amazon Music’s year-end recap stands out with a feature specifically designed for users with an Alexa-enabled device. These users can view the title of the most frequently requested song they’ve asked Alexa to play throughout the year.

This year, Amazon Music introduced new badges for listeners to show off. For example, the “Trendsetter” badge is awarded to “early adopters” of trending albums, while the “Headliner” badge recognizes fans who rank in the top percentage of an artist’s listeners, the company explains. 

Like its competitors, Amazon Music offers listeners animated shareable cards that highlight their unique music stats, making it easy to share with friends and followers on social media. 

The new 2025 cards are designed with a “music festival” theme, personalized for each listener. For instance, a listener named Katie receives her own “Katie Fest,” which includes a lineup featuring Bad Bunny, Charli XCX, Chappell Roan, and others.

2025 Delivered is available in the Amazon Music app in the U.S., the U.K., Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Japan, Mexico, Brazil, India, Canada, and Australia. To be eligible, Amazon Music users need at least a few hours of listening history.
