Former NBA champion, fintech entrepreneur, and AI founder Tristan Thompson is bringing a different kind of game to TechCrunch Disrupt 2025 in October at San Francisco’s Moscone West — one that blends athletic grit, entrepreneurial instinct, and cutting-edge AI.

After more than a decade lighting up the NBA, Thompson has shifted focus from the hardwood to the innovation arena. His latest venture, TracyAI, applies advanced artificial intelligence to sports analytics — turning real-time data into predictive insights that empower athletes, teams, and fans alike.

Unlocking the next era of sports and tech

At Disrupt, he’ll unpack how he’s using AI to reimagine performance, strategy, and fan engagement, and why athletes are increasingly shaping the startup ecosystem.

But Thompson’s tech story doesn’t stop there. He currently holds four C-suite roles across the Web3 and FinTech landscape. From chief digital equity officer at World Mobile, driving affordable community-owned internet access, to chief advisory officer at AxonDAO, an AI-powered medical research platform inspired by his brother’s epilepsy journey. He’s also the co-founder of Basketball Fun, an immersive digital experience redefining how fans connect to the game.

As an outspoken advocate for innovation and inclusion, Thompson is part of a new wave of athlete-entrepreneurs using their platforms for impact. His projects have been featured by Forbes, CNBC Crypto World, and CoinDesk, and he recently took the stage at Bitcoin 2025 to discuss athlete-driven innovation in decentralized tech.

“Sports taught me discipline, but tech taught me scale,” Thompson says. “When you merge those worlds, the potential is unlimited.”
