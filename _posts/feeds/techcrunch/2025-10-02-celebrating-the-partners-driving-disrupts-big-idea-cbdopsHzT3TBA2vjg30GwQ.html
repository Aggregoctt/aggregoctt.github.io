TechCrunch Disrupt 2025 wouldn’t be possible without the incredible support of our sponsors, who bring world-class expertise, bold innovation, and community energy to every corner of the conference.

Spotlight sessions you won’t want to miss

Our sponsors are shaping the future of industries — and you can catch them live at Disrupt:

Monday October 27

Space Stage

Aerospace Startup Showcase: Solving the Hardest Problems in Space

8:35 a.m. – 9:40 a.m.
Presented by The Aerospace Corporation

AI is transforming countless industries, and space is no exception. The Aerospace Corporation is hosting a pitch-off at TechCrunch Disrupt to showcase startups working on innovative AI solutions to the hardest problems in space exploration, orbital intelligence, and infrastructure.

Builders Stage

Flying into the Future: AI Is Fueling Aviation

10:10 a.m. – 10:30 a.m.
Presented by Epic Aircraft

Epic Aircraft is shaking up aviation with AI, from design to certification to customer support. Fresh off Federal Aviation Administration approval of its E1000 AX, the Oregon startup shows how automation can speed safety, scale, and performance. Don’t miss how startup culture is redefining the future of flight.

AI at the Edge: Startups Powering the Future of Space

11:00 a.m. – 11:20 a.m.
Presented by The Aerospace Corporation

Space is no longer just rockets and satellites — it’s data and decisions, done fast. AI now powers autonomy, resilient compute, and onboard intelligence, reshaping how missions run at scale. The Aerospace Corporation and innovators reveal how early-stage breakthroughs are building the next space economy.

Tuesday October 28

Builders Stage

Designing Products for the AI Age

10:10 a.m. – 10:30 a.m.
Presented by WARP

AI is changing how we work and the tools we depend on. Join Yuhki Yamashita (chief product officer, Figma), Zach Lloyd (founder and CEO, Warp), and Andrew Reed (partner, Sequoia) for a conversation on how product strategy is evolving in the AI era and what it takes to design great AI experiences

AI Stage

From Web Pages to Autonomous Agents: A Conversation on Linking Today’s Web to Tomorrow’s Data Layer

11:40 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.
Presented by Bright Data

Bright Data CEO Or Lenchner shares how today’s web underpins tomorrow’s AI-powered internet. Discover how to future-proof your stack so chatbots and agents can reason with live data — while tackling scale, speed, and regulatory challenges.
