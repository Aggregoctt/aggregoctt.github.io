Lessons in scaling and innovation

As the co-founder of Robinhood, Bhatt helped redefine investing for over 20 million users worldwide, turning finance into a platform accessible to a generation of new investors. His journey at Robinhood, where he also served as co-CEO and board director, taught him invaluable lessons about scaling a business, navigating regulatory complexity, and innovating under pressure.

Today, Bhatt is taking those lessons to Aetherflux, an ambitious aerospace venture pushing the boundaries of clean energy with space-based solar power. In this fireside chat, he’ll discuss why he’s tackling one of the hardest engineering challenges in history, how his founder experience informs decisions in radically different industries, and what it takes to operate at the intersection of deep tech and space.
