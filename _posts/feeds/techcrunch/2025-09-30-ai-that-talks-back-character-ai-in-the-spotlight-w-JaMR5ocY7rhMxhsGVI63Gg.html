Karandeep Anand: Building the future of AI and video for millions

As CEO of Character.AI, Anand guides the company’s long-term strategy and expansion into new modalities such as video generation, monetization initiatives, and strategic partnerships. The platform now reaches 20 million monthly active users worldwide.

Previously, Anand served as president and chief product officer of Brex, leading the launch of its AI-driven Brex Assistant and spend management platform, Empower. Prior to Brex, he was vice president and head of business products at Meta, where he connected over 3 billion people and served 200 million businesses through Ads, Messenger, Instagram, Workplace, and WhatsApp. Anand also spent 15 years at Microsoft, leading product management for Azure Cloud and developer platforms.
