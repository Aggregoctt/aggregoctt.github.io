Check Out All 100+ New Items in Version 3.0 Animal Crossing: New Horizons Update (Datamine)

The long awaited Version 3.0 Update for Animal Crossing: New Horizons (ACNH) has finally released adding plenty of brand new items including furniture, clothing, and more to the game!

For Animal Crossing fans who want a quick peek at everything new the Version 3.0 Update has to offer, without waiting for the items to appear in shops or scanning amiibo, we’re providing a handy catalog in this article of visuals for every new item found in the update.

In this early edition, all types of items and variants are included for simplicity, so you can view them ASAP. We will steadily update and improve this article soon to improve it with item names, type categorization, and variant organization!

Remember that an article of this nature obviously contains potential spoilers and you may wish to experience the new content of your own discovery on your own time as many Animal Crossing players do. Read on at your own risk only if you don’t mind ‘spoiling’ new content for yourself.

Personally, we would actually recommend waiting to enjoy the content yourself organically as it’s typically the best way to enjoy Animal Crossing. Consider this provided as a resource to fulfill the demand from those who do want to see everything — not a recommendation on our part.

For a brief bit of commentary, unless we’ve made some grave errors, it would appear that the Version 3.0 update’s volume of new item content is on the relatively small side compared to what we saw with Version 2.0 – although that was a truly massive release. This does not appear to be on the same scale, which could be disappointing, depending on your pre-release expectations.

Note: This early first attempt to publish the images as quickly as possible may contain some errors in regard to old items miscategorized as new or some new items missing entirely. Pre-release information also indicated that some existing items may have new variations/colors, which should appear in this list if true. If you notice any mistakes, please give us a heads up in the comments.
