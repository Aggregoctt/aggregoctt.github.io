Which Splatoon & Zelda Amiibo Unlock What in Animal Crossing: New Horizons Version 3.0

The Animal Crossing: New Horizons Version 3.0 Update launches on January 16th, bringing new Splatoon and The Legend of Zelda content, but which amiibo unlocks what? We’ve got the scoop!

The official Japanese website for amiibo from Nintendo has been updated as of today with specific details on amiibo compatibility in the new update, so let’s break down all the new information below.

The Legend of Zelda Amiibo Compatibility List

The entire set of themed collaboration items (furniture and clothing) for The Legend of Zelda unlocks with the scanning of any amiibo figure from the dedicated The Legend of Zelda series, or any Zelda themed amiibo figures from the Super Smash Bros line.

Additionally, there are two Zelda-inspired villagers introduced in Version 3.0 called Tulin and Mineru. For villagers, the available The Legend of Zelda amiibo figures have been roughly split in half — one half will help you invite Tulin to your island, while the other half can invite Mineru.

Splatoon Amiibo Compatibility List

The entire set of themed collaboration items (furniture and clothing) for Splatoon unlock with the scanning of any amiibo figure from the dedicated Splatoon series, or Inkling from the Super Smash Bros line.

Additionally, there are two Splatoon-inspired villagers returning in Version 3.0 named Cece and Viché. For villagers, the Splatoon amiibo figure collection has been split in about half — one half will help you invite Cece to your island, while the other half can invite Viché.

And as a special bonus, if you own the special Big Man amiibo figure, he can actually invite both villagers choosing between them each time! Specifically, the Japanese explanation machine translates to “You can choose whichever you prefer and call it up each time you load it.”
