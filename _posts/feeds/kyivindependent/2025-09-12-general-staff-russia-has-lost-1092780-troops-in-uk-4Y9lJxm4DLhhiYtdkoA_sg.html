Russia has lost 1,092,780 troops in Ukraine since the beginning of its full-scale invasion on Feb. 24, 2022, the General Staff of Ukraine's Armed Forces reported on Sept. 12.

The number includes 890 casualties that Russian forces suffered over the past day.
