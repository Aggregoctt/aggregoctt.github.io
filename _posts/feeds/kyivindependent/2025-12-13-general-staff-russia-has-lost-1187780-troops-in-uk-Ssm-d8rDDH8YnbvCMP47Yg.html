Russia has lost around 1,187,780 troops in Ukraine since the beginning of its full-scale invasion on Feb. 24, 2022, the General Staff of Ukraine's Armed Forces reported on Dec. 13.

The number includes 1,300 casualties that Russian forces suffered over the past day.
