Russia has lost around 1,248,560 troops in Ukraine since the beginning of its full-scale invasion on Feb. 24, 2022, the General Staff of Ukraine's Armed Forces reported on Feb. 10.

The number includes 980 casualties that Russian forces suffered over the past day.
