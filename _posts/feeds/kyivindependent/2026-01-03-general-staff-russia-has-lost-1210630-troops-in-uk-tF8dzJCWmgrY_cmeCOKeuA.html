Russia has lost around 1,210,630 troops in Ukraine since the beginning of its full-scale invasion on Feb. 24, 2022, Ukraine's General Staff reported on Jan. 3.

The number includes 750 casualties that Russian forces suffered over the past day, the General Staff said.
