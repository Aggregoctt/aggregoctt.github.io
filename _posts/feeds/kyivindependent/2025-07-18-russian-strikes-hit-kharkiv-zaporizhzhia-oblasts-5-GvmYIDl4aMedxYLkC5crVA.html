Russian forces carried out a series of overnight attacks on Ukraine on July 18, injuring at least five people, local officials reported.

Four people were wounded in a strike on Chuhuiv in Kharkiv Oblast, Mayor Halyna Minayeva reported. All four have received medical treatment.

Minayeva said Russian forces targeted a residential neighborhood, damaging homes, a doctor's office, and an educational institution.

Emergency crews responded to the scene to assess and repair the damage.
