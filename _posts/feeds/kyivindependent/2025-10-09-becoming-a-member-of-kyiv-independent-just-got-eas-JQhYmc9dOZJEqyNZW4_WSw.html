The Kyiv Independent team has finally introduced a long-requested feature — members can now pay for monthly or annual memberships using PayPal.

We’ve also expanded our payment features and now there is no need to pay for conversion fees when paying in EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD.

This makes supporting our journalism easier, more flexible, and more accessible to readers around the world.

Our system now automatically detects a user’s location and displays the most relevant currency by default. Readers can also manually select any of the supported currencies during checkout.
