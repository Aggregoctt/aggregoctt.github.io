Make no mistake: This is great news for all language learners!

Explain My Answer—our popular in-lesson feedback feature available to subscribers—is now free for all Duolingo learners.

Here's how it works, and how you can try it out today.

Explain My Answer provides personalized tips

Explain My Answer started as a Duolingo Max feature that gave subscribers detailed explanations about grammar and vocabulary. It was a big hit with learners—and for good reason! Mistakes might be necessary for learning, but knowing the why behind rules and exceptions helps you build confidence and improve for next time.

Now, we're making Explain My Answer available to everyone! 

How Explain My Answer works

Explain My Answer crafts personalized feedback that takes into account the error you made and what about the pattern or rule can be challenging for learners like you. 

For example, grammatical gender—a system of dividing nouns (including objects!) into categories like “masculine” and “feminine”—can be really challenging for English speakers.

Explain My Answer takes this into account and tailors its explanations especially for learners who don't have grammatical gender rules in their language. (Hear that, English speakers? 🙏)

Whether you're just starting out or can brag about your triple-digit Duolingo Score, Explain My Answer delivers smart guidance that gives you:

  • More control: Choose whether you want feedback on a mistake.
  • Faster feedback: See—and understand—the correct form in the moment so you can more easily remember it later.
  • Deeper understanding: Feel confident in your grammar and vocabulary skills!

Available now—for free!

So next time you get an answer—right or wrong—give Explain My Answer a tap. 

Explain My Answer is available for most learners in our courses teaching Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, and Korean… and this is just the start! We're working hard to bring it to more courses in the coming months.

It’s another step closer to achieving our mission: to develop the best education in the world and make it universally available. 💚
