Whether you're shopping for a language lover, a streak keeper, or someone who just really likes hugging small green owls, we've got you covered. The Duolingo Store has gifts that are funny, functional, and packed with mascot energy. 

This year, we're spotlighting plushies, study sidekicks, and one very musical surprise that's guaranteed to earn you best-gift-giver status. Bonus: All of our plushies ship FREE through 2025 (US only) 🎁

Let’s unwrap it all, shall we?

Duolingo Plushies

Classic, buff, and anime. Soft? Yes. Supportive? Always. Judgy? Only when you skip a lesson.

Duo Slippers

Back by extremely popular demand! Our Duo Slippers are plush, cozy, and slightly threatening in the best way. Perfect for studying, snacking, or sprinting to your phone when you forgot your lesson.

Learning Companions

For the learner in your life (which might be you), the Learning Companion Notebook and Stick With It Kit make studying feel actually fun. With sleek pages, goal trackers, and plenty of streak flame stickers, they’re designed to keep motivation high and streaks going strong.

Duolingo x Loog Piano

And now for the showstopper you might’ve missed this year: the Duolingo x Loog Piano. It’s sleek, portable, and designed to pair perfectly with Duolingo’s Music course. With built-in speakers, MIDI compatibility, and that signature Loog design, it’s more than just a keyboard, it’s an invite to explore a whole new kind of learning.

Duolingo Blind Boxes

Our Duo Blind Boxes are back for their final run: collectible, adorable, and disappearing soon. If you’ve been waiting to complete your set, now’s the time to grab one, unbox the mystery, and hope for your favorite feathered friend.

But wait, there’s more!

No matter who you’re shopping for, the Duolingo Store has something that says "I believe in your streak." Pair a plushie with a notebook. Add a Stick With It Kit. Grab a blind box just because. And if you're really feeling generous, that Loog Piano is hard to beat.

Gifts that are smart, funny, and a little bit judgmental? Yeah, that sounds like us.
