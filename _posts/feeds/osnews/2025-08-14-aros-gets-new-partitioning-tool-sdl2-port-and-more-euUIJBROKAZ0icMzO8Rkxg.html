We’re not done with the AROS news quite yet, as Andrzej Subocz published another update with the latest news from the AROS community. Some of it we already covered, but a lot it we haven’t. Work has also begun on porting SDL2 to AROS, replacing the aging SDL 1.2 version currently available.
