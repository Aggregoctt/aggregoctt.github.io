Anker just announced three new chargers at CES 2026, and surprisingly, one of them is already on sale ahead of its launch on January 20th launch. Anker’s latest 45W Nano Charger, the company’s first Nano Charger to sport a built-in display, is available to preorder from Anker’s online storefront in one of several colors for $29.99 ($10 off) when you use promo code WS24D5XT3DV9 at checkout.

Anker’s latest single-port charger delivers up to 45W of power, which should be enough to charge an iPhone 17 Pro to 50 percent capacity in as little as 20 minutes. What truly sets it apart, though, is its front-facing “smart display,” which shows the charge level, power flow, and temperature of the connected device. It can also recognize and identify specific iPhone models dating back to the iPhone 15 — as well as iPad Pro models from 2020 onward — and adjust charging based on power needs. For example, it’ll deliver maximum speeds when a phone is nearly dead, then drop to 15W once a device is above 80 percent.

Additionally, Anker’s new Nano Charger features a “Care Mode” that can lower charging temperatures overnight with a simple double-tap. The charger’s folding prongs can also rotate 180 degrees, making it easier to plug in at different angles or in tight spaces. Just bear in mind that, while it does work with non-Apple devices, the built-in display likely won’t provide as much information.
