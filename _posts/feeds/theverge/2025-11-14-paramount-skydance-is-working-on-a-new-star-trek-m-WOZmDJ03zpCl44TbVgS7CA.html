Paramount Skydance head David Ellison made it very clear that he was interested in restarting the studio’s Star Trek movie franchise with some new blood, and it seems like that plan is moving forward.

Deadline reports that Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves co-directors Jonathan Goldstein and John Francis Daley have signed on to an all-new Star Trek feature that will be “a completely new take” on the IP with no direct connections to any past series or films. There’s no word yet on a production timeline, story details, or which actors Paramount Skydance is eyeing to star.
