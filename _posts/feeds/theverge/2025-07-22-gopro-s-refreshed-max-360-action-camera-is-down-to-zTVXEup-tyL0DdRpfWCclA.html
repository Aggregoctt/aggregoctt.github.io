Earlier this year, GoPro introduced a refreshed version of its Max 360-degree action camera, and right now that camera is down to an all-time low of $249.99 ($120 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and GoPro. The newest version comes with a long-lasting Enduro battery and an upgraded mounting system, making it compatible with all standard tripod mounts. Your purchase also includes two protective lens caps, a mounting buckle, and a microfiber bag to easily transport everything.

The original GoPro Max was released all the way back in 2019, so a refreshed version has been a long time coming. Its 5.6K spherical camera makes capturing 360-degree video incredibly easy — just press record, and the camera handles the rest. You don’t need to worry about framing your subject or missing the moment. While spherical videos look particularly immersive during action sports, it also adds a unique perspective to travel videos, vlogging, and everyday adventures. The Max also provides the flexibility to record standard video, photos, and time lapse footage with a single lens.

GoPro Max owners can take advantage of GoPro’s updated Quik App, a mobile editing app that makes it easy to edit 360-degree video. The app supports features like AI-powered object tracking, keyframing, and the ability to make edits to single clips. If you purchase the discounted GoPro Max through GoPro’s website, you’ll also get a free one-year premium subscription, a $49.99 value. The service offers unlimited cloud storage, damaged camera replacement, and discounts on GoPro accessories.
