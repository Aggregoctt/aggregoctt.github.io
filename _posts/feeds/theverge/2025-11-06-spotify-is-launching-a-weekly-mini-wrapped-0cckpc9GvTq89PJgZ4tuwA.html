Spotify has introduced a new “listening stats” feature that gives you a weekly overview of your recent music streaming habits. It essentially acts like a mini version of Spotify’s annual Wrapped review, showing you which artists and songs you’ve listened to the most each week, and making curated playlists inspired by those results. Listening stats will be available to revisit for four weeks.

Like Wrapped, the feature allows users to share their results to platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp, and also provides a “special highlight that captures what makes your listening unique,” according to Spotify, such as new artist discoveries, fan moments, and milestones. Listening stats are available to free and premium users in more than 60 regions globally, and can be accessed from the menu that opens when you tap on your profile image.

This follows a similar move from Apple, which debuted a monthly version of Apple Music’s annual Replay feature last year and later baked it into the app experience with iOS 26.
