Halloween parties are fun. The preparation and cleanup? Not so much. Luckily for those looking to make life a little easier next week, Eufy’s X10 Pro Omni is available from Amazon for $499.99 ($300 off) when you clip the on-page coupon, which matches the all-time low we last saw during Amazon’s October Prime Day event. If you prefer a different retailer, can also pick up the robovac directly from Eufy for the same price when you use promo code WS24T2351 at checkout.

Even with the arrival of newer models from Dyson, iRobot, and others, the X10 Pro Omni remains our favorite midrange robot vacuum / mop hybrid. The robovac’s impressive dual oscillating brushes performed exceptionally well in our tests, even scrubbing away dried stains with its 1kg of downward pressure. Its built-in water reservoir also lets it clean for longer intervals without returning to its dock, though, when it does need to head back, its all-in-one dock can take care of everything on your behalf. It can auto-empty its own bin, refill its own water tank, and even dry its own mop pads, helping keep things odor-free.

On the vacuuming front, the X10 Pro Omni excels at vacuuming both carpets and tile, with 8,000Pa suction and enough power to reliably dig up dirt and debris. Its AI-powered obstacle detection is excellent, too, and in our testing, the robovac did a nice job navigating around fake dog turds and cables without an issue. Plus, the X10 Pro Omni pairs with a well-designed, easy-to-use app that uses lidar-based mapping for fast, accurate navigation; it also offers plenty of customization options, including room-specific, zone, and custom cleaning modes.
