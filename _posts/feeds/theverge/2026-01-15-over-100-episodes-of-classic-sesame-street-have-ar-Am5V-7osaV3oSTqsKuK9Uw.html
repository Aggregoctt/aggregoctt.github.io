YouTube is now home to the “largest digital library of Sesame Street content” with the arrival of over a hundred older, “classic” episodes of the show, as first announced last year.

YouTube’s collection of Sesame Street episodes spans the series’ history, as the announcement for the streaming launch mentions:

“More than 100 full Sesame Street episodes from previous seasons are now available on YouTube and the YouTube Kids app for the first time. This includes episodes from recent seasons as well as some of the most beloved moments from Sesame Street history that fans have been waiting for.”

That includes the show’s first episode from 1969 and highlights like the Mister Rogers episode and Big Bird’s big Snuffleupagus reveal, which you can now stream on the Sesame Street Classics YouTube channel. The YouTube deal is just the latest new platform for the series. Starting last year, you can now visit Netflix to stream new episodes of the show’s latest season, which is also airing on PBS and PBS Kids.
