At long last, Amazon’s October Prime Day event has begun — and as expected, it’s packed with a slew of notable Apple deals. If you don’t want to wait until Black Friday or Cyber Monday to score a discount, the current promo is a great opportunity to save, as Amazon is offering steep discounts on a variety of last-gen Apple devices. For example, refurbished second-gen AirPods Pro have dropped to a new record low price of $139.99, which is well over $100 off their original price. Meanwhile, the Apple Watch Series 10 is down to $279, matching the low price we saw during July’s four-day Prime Day.

It’s not just older devices on sale, either. Amazon is also offering discounts on accessories for the new iPhone Air, as well as current-gen gadgets like the latest iPad Mini. To make sorting through the deluge of deals a little easier, we’ve rounded up all the best Apple discounts available, along with links to competing retailers who happen to be matching Amazon’s pricing. You may not need to sign up for a Prime membership just to save a few bucks.
