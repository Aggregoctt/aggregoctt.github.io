Is a smartwatch better than a smartphone? Not which one is more important or which would you rather have, but simply which one is better? This is the kind of question you really never need to ask — but we’re here to ask it. Why? Because Google has had a terrific year of gadget launches, from Pixel phones to Pixel Watches. For the first time ever, the Pixel lineup is a formidable competitor in every category in which it competes. But only one Pixel can be the best Pixel.

On this episode of The Vergecast, The Verge’s Allison Johnson and Victoria Song join David to try and define the official Pixel ranking. There are six products available: the Pixel phones, the Pixel A-series phones, the Pixel Fold, the Pixel Watch, the Pixel Buds, and the Pixel Tablet. With the help of a new tool called (for now) the Rank-o-meter, the three hosts set out to put the six products in their correct order.

Ranking them requires considering everything from their price to their competition to their overall immeasurable awesomeness. There is no simple conversion chart, but ultimately, our three hosts agreed on a ranking.
