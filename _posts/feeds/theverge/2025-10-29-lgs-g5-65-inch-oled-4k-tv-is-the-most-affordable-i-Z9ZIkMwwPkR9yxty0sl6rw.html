If you want one of the best home theater experiences LG can provide, the 65-inch LG G5 OLED is down to a new record low price. Regularly $3,399.99, you can buy the TV for around $1,996.99 at Amazon and directly from LG. Other configurations are also on sale, with the 77-inch going for around $3,456.99 (about $1,000 off) at Amazon and LG. If you purchase the TVs from LG, you’ll also get free professional wall mounting or stand set up with a free matching TV stand through November 16th.

While it didn’t win this year’s “TV Shootout” award for flawless picture quality, the LG G5 OLED still delivers an impressive viewing experience. It features the same stunning OLED picture quality as the popular C5 line — complete with support for Dolby Vision — but takes things a step further with LG’s “Brightness Booster Ultimate” technology. As a result, it’s noticeably brighter.

The G5 additionally boasts LG’s more powerful Alpha 11 Gen 2 processor, which improves image processing and upscaling, along with a higher maximum 165Hz refresh rate. It also includes four HDMI 2.1 ports along with AMD FreeSync Premium and NVIDIA G-Sync compatibility for responsive gaming. Rounding things out, the TV supports Dolby Atmos audio, comes with LG’s wand-like Magic Remote, and offers access to a wide range of streaming apps — making it an excellent choice for both gamers and home theater enthusiasts alike.
