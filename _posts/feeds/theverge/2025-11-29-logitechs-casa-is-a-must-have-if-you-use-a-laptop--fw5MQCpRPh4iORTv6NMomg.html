If you’re someone who’s constantly on the move, yet you want the tools to help you feel comfortable anywhere you post up to do work, I have a recommendation: Logitech’s Casa. It’s a compact (and sleek) kit of accessories that can give you a more ergonomically-friendly laptop setup, and it’s $149.99 ($30 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and from Logitech during Black Friday.

That might seem pricey, but the Casa is a one-stop shop for busy laptop owners. It includes a stand that props up your laptop so that its screen is more comfortable to look at. It also includes a wireless Bluetooth keyboard and standalone trackpad, both of which work great on Windows 11 and macOS. It might sound silly, but using the Casa boosts my productivity when I need to set up a mobile office in a hotel room, or even at a cafe. My favorite feature is that the trackpad and keyboard fit into dedicated slots within the Casa, so they won’t rumble around your bag when you need to pack everything up.
