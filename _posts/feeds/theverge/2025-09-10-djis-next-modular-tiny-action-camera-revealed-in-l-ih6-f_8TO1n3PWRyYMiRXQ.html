There’s been no official announcement from DJI yet, but leaked images appear to confirm a follow-up to the company’s modular and compact Action 2 camera that debuted in 2021, as spotted by Notebookcheck. The new DJI Osmo Nano takes a similar approach with accessories like a screen that attach using magnets, but the camera itself appears to be much smaller than the Action 2, pill-shaped, and closer in size to the Insta360 Go Ultra announced last month.

Details are scarce about the capabilities of the DJI Osmo Nano camera, but YouTuber The Product Village briefly shared a clip of its packaging (the video was reuploaded to X by reliable leaker Igor Bogdanov) revealing it will come with 128GB of built-in storage and will potentially be released in several different bundles with various accessories.

Yesterday, leaker Roland Quandt also shared what appear to be official product shots of the DJI Osmo Nano revealing a camera module that lacks a screen of its own and instead relies on an additional module for live previews or reviewing footage.
