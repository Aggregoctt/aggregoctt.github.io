If you have a mountain of holiday shopping to tackle over the next few weeks, Walmart is offering a great deal on Walmart Plus. New subscribers can snag an annual Walmart Plus membership for half off through December 2nd. The deal knocks the yearly price down to $49 ($49 off), which will automatically renew at the regular price next year if you decide to continue your subscription.

Walmart Plus offers a number of enticing perks, including free delivery on orders over $35, free shipping with no order minimum, an ad-supported membership to either Paramount Plus or Peacock, and more. Walmart Plus members also get access to limited-time offers, like five free months of Apple Music. And if you’re on the hunt for early Black Friday deals, Walmart Plus members can get early access to Black Friday discounts five hours before the general public.

Speaking of which, Walmart is planning to roll out its Black Friday deals in waves, beginning online at 7PM ET on Thursday, November 13th, for Walmart Plus subscribers. For everybody else, the early deals will kick off on November 14th at 12AM ET, and will run both in-store and online through November 16th. There will be a second wave of deals before the event officially kicks off online and in-store at 12AM ET on November 28th and lasts through November 30th.

If you do a lot of shopping at the retailer, grabbing this discount is a great way to save during the holidays and throughout the year. Just remember to set a reminder for this time next year, so you can assess whether you want to continue paying for a membership before it automatically renews at full price.
