Logitech is launching a digital stylus for Apple Vision Pro that makes it easier to write, draw, and manipulate 3D designs. The Logitech Muse is essentially a pen-shaped Vision Pro controller that can be used like a paintbrush or pencil when navigating apps, making annotations, or creating art, providing a more natural experience in mixed reality environments compared to just using your hands.

Logitech announced the Muse in June, but now it’s available to preorder for $129.95, with shipments starting from October 22nd. Features include real-time haptic feedback, a pressure-sensitive tip for use on surfaces, and a force-sensing button that allows users to adjust line thickness while drawing in the air.

It’s the same price as the MX Ink, a visually near-identical stylus that Logitech released for Meta Quest headsets last year. Aside from sporting different button designs, the most notable difference is that Logitech’s MX Ink pen can be charged via USB-C or its included Inkwell Charging Dock, whereas the Muse is USB-C only and doesn’t provide a dock at all. The latter could make the Vision Pro accessory prone to getting lost, as Apple Pencil users can probably attest.
