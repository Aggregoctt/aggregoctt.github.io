Amazon is the retailer to beat when it comes to offering deals to compete with Amazon’s October Prime Day event. Walmart has proven to be a contender this year, matching the prices on several discounts and marking down many other tech products that we think you’ll like. Its sale event, simply called “Walmart Deals,” runs through Sunday, October 12, giving you plenty of time to save — no membership required. There are great deals below on gaming products, 4K TVs, laptops, even compact electric tire pumps, and more.

Whether you’re trying to shop less at Amazon or you just want to see what other retailers are offering in the run-up to the holidays, we’ve rounded up the best competing Prime Day deals you can currently grab at Walmart. We’re on the hunt this week for noteworthy deals to add to this post.
