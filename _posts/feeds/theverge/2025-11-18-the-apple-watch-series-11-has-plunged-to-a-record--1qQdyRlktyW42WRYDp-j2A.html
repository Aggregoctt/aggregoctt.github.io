The Apple Watch Series 11 has plunged to a record low price

The latest Apple Watch SE impressed us with all of its upgrades this year, but the Apple Watch Series 11 is still the better option if you’re an iPhone owner who requires more advanced health notifications. Thankfully it, like the SE, is now down to a new record-low price, starting at $349.99 ($50 off) at Amazon and Walmart for the 40mm, GPS-enabled version.

Unlike the SE 3, Apple’s entry-level wearable, the Apple Watch Series 11 offers features like EKG readings, blood oxygen tracking, and the FDA-cleared hypertension notifications. Its 24-hour battery life makes it far more useful for sleep tracking, and it won’t drain nearly as quickly if you opt for the new 5G-enabled cellular model. It also features a larger display with thinner bezels, which is especially helpful for older users or anyone who doesn’t want to strain their eyes.

The Apple Watch Series 11 comes loaded with other helpful features. Like the Apple Watch SE 3, it includes sleep-apnea notifications, a wrist-temperature sensor, fall detection, and ovulation tracking. It also supports wrist-flick and double-tap gestures, so you can quickly clear notifications or answer phone calls hands-free without tapping the screen.

Some more ways to save

  • You can buy the Onn 4K Pro Streaming Device for $44.73 ($5 off) at Walmart, which is a rare discount. Along with supporting Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, the 4K streaming device comes with a remote that includes a customizable shortcut button and a built-in remote finder. It also doubles as a smart speaker with Google Assistant and gives you access to more than 800 free live TV channels, along with all the major streaming services like Netflix.
  • The Coway Airmega AP-1512HH is down to $142.99 ($97 off) at Amazon and directly from Coway, which is its best price in a year and $20 shy of its record low price. The small air purifier offers a three-stage filtration system, including a true HEPA filter, designed to get rid of pollutants and even reduce smells. It also displays real-time air quality with a brightly colored LED, so you always know how clean your air is.
  • The wired second-generation Ring Indoor Cam Security Camera is on sale for $24.99 ($25 off) at Amazon and Best Buy. The 1080p camera includes a handy cover that physically blocks the lens and disables audio for added privacy. It also delivers all the essentials — color night vision, two-way audio, and motion detection — and comes with an adjustable mount so you can easily tweak the camera’s view.
