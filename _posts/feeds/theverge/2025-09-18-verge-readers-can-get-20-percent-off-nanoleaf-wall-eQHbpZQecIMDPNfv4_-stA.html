If you want a fun way to add light and color to your space, Nanoleaf’s wall lights are a great option. Ahead of Prime Big Deal Days, Nanoleaf is offering 20 percent off select products exclusively for Verge readers with the promo code THEVERGE20OFF through September 20th. For instance, you can save 20 percent off Nanoleaf’s Blocks Squares Smarter Kit, dropping the price down to $144 ($36 off). This is currently the best price available on this model.

The Blocks Squares Smarter Kit features six large square light panels with edge-to-edge illumination. You can arrange the modular panels however you want on your wall, and they can be controlled through mobile and desktop apps, voice control, or with the included remote. Each individual square delivers 320 lumens of light and can be mounted via double-sided tape, which is also included with your purchase. They also feature a color temperature range of 1200K–6500K, and these lights can be mixed with other Nanoleaf lights, including the Light Pegboard Smarter Kit, which is currently down to $107.97 (about $27 off).

Additionally, you can take 20 percent off a two-pack of Nanoleaf’s Blocks Shelves Add-On Kit, taking the price down to $24 ($5.99 off) by using the same code as above at Nanoleaf’s site. These stainless steel shelves give you a place to display your favorite handheld gadgets. Each shelf is about 9 inches wide and features a weight limit of about three pounds.
