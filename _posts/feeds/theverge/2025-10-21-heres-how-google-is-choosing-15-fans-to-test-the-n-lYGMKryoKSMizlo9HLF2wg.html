3. How to Enter

3.1 Entrants must follow the link to the Google form sent to them via email and submit their submission during the Contest Submission Period.

3.2 Entry into this Contest will give entrants a chance to become 1 of 15 nominees to be put forward to the next round of selection to become a member of the Pixel Trusted Tester program. The Trusted Tester program is an opportunity to provide feedback and help shape a Pixel phone currently in development. If successful in this Contest, Google will contact winners for a second round of questions with the Trusted Tester Team, who will select the final 15 device testers.

3.3 A panel of Google judges will determine who receives 1 of 15 nominations to be put forward for consideration to become a Trusted Tester.

3.4 Entrant answers to the following questions in the Google form will constitute their submission (“Submission”):

3.4.1 How many Pixel devices have you ever owned?

3.4.2 As a Pixel Superfan, please give us feedback on your current Pixel device. What would you improve and why?

3.4.3 If you had a magic wand, what feature would you add to your device and why?
