A Humble Choice membership comes with much more alongside the games listed above. Each month, PC players will get to enjoy a curated selection of PC games that they can choose to keep or skip for the month. With a membership you'll also get to enjoy up to 20% off select games on the Humble Store and 5% of your membership supports a charity each month. October's charity is the WDC, Whale and Dolphin Conservation, which is dedicated to (as its name says) the conservation of whales and dolphins.

It's also worth noting that if you find the membership isn't for you, you can cancel at anytime. If you find you enjoy it, though, Humble has plenty to offer alongside its Choice monthly drops, including many different bundles. At the moment, the excellent Remedy Games 30th Anniversary Bundle (which includes 10 games for as low as $27) is still running for another day, which is one well worth jumping on while it's still available.
