August has arrived, and it brings with it a new batch of PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC games for your enjoyment. This month sees the release of Mafia: The Old Country, this year's Madden, Metal Gear Solid Delta, and even a few games that don't start with the letter M. Read on to see release dates for all the biggest games and expansions coming out soon (or soonish). Let's have a look.

If you're someone who likes to preorder your games, you can click the links on the platform of your choice to make sure it arrives on launch day.
