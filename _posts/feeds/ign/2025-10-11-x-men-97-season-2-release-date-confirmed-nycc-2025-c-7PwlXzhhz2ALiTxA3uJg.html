While we've know that X-Men '97, the hit Disney+ Marvel animated series, would be getting at least two more seasons, the news out of New York Comic Con today is that Season 2 now has a release date: Summer 2026.

The team behind the show also promised that Season 2 will "tee up Season 3 in a massive way."

X-Men '97 proved to be an instant success for Marvel, bringing back the beloved animated adventures of Marvel's merry mutants from the 1990s. In his 9/10 review of Season 1, IGN's Jesse Schedeen said "X-Men '97 isn't just a fitting continuation of X-Men: The Animated Series, but one of the best adaptations ever of this iconic Marvel franchise."

A trailer for Season 2 was also shown at New York Comic Con, which included the return of X-Men baddie Apocalypse.

Also announced at the panel: Season 2 of Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man will hit in the Fall of 2026, Daredevil: Born Again Season 2 is coming in March of 2026, Wonder Man arrives on January 27, and Vision Quest is now being called the conclusion to a trilogy that started with WandaVision and continued with Agatha All Along. That one is coming on a TBD date in 2026.
