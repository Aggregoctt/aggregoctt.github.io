TL;DR – Best Kindle Accessory Prime Day Deals

Deals on everything from straps and PopSockets to stands and stickers for the Kindle are happening now; many of these products have been trending, going viral on TikTok and Instagram. Straps, PopSockets, and stands can do wonders to improve your experience with the Kindle, while stickers add a personal touch and some character to the device.

Strap and PopSocket Kindle Deals

While all Kindle models are lightweight and easy to hold, you can still experience some strain or cramping when gripping them for too long. Straps and PopSockets make it much easier to clutch the Kindle in your hand. Luckily, a range of options are on sale for Prime Day, and all of my top picks easily attach to the Kindle using a strong adhesive, a unique MagSafe adapter, or a slip-on silicone design.

Kindle Stand Deals

If you don’t want to deal with holding the Kindle, there are tons of tablet stands discounted for Prime Day. Whether you’re looking for something to place next to a bed or couch, attach to a headboard or a desk, or plop right on your lap, there’s a stand to suit just about anyone's taste. All of these stands are easy to adjust and require little effort to set up. I’m a massive fan of the TabHolderix Tablet Pillow Stand; it even has food and drink storage for when you’re feeling snackish and don’t want to derail a binge read.

Kindle Sticker Deals

Let’s be honest, Kindles are pretty boring devices on their own. An easy and inexpensive way to jazz them up is with stickers. Plenty of bookish stickers are on sale for Prime Day. I’ve found some fun options to suit any reader, and all of them are vinyl for better durability. Each pack comes with 50+ stickers, so you can also add them to water bottles, laptops, phones, and more.
