gamescom 2025 is nearly here, and IGN is once again the official partner for gamescom studio in Germany! We'll be covering all the big news out of gamescom, and this guide will provide you with everything you need to know to watch gamescom, Xbox, and IGN's biggest livestreams, including when the biggest events - like Opening Night Live - start, a list of places you can watch them with us, what you can expect to see, and so much more.

While you wait for this year's show to begin, feel free to look back at the biggest announcements from gamescom 2024 to get an idea of what to expect!

When Is gamescom 2025?

gamescom 2025 runs from August 19 through August 24 in Cologne, Germany.

What to Expect at gamescom 2025

IGN will livestream Opening Night Live, Xbox's two streams, the gamescom Award Show, and four days of IGN's in-studio coverage with exclusive interviews, trailers, and hands-on demos from games you won't want to miss.

While we can't reveal everything that will be featured on our livestreams, there have been some teases as to what fans can expect from gamescom, including a new look at Resident Evil Requiem and the reveal of Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 during Opening Night Live alongside Lords of the Fallen II, the opening cinematic of World of Warcraft: Midnight, and much more.

Hollow Knight: Silksong will also be playable at the Xbox booth on PC and the ROG Xbox Ally. Will we finally get a release date during ONL? We don't have long to find out!

Speaking of Xbox, they will have a big presence at the show, and the team has already announced it will be featuring the ROG Xbox Ally handhelds, Grounded 2, Keeper, Call of Duty: Black Ops 7, Ninja Gaiden 4, Overwatch 2, The Outer Worlds 2, and more during Xbox's official gamescom broadcasts.

As for IGN, we are looking forward to sharing exclusives for Borderlands 4, Marvel’s Deadpool VR, Anno 117: Pax Romana, Invincible VS, STALKER, Infinity Nikki, and so many more!
