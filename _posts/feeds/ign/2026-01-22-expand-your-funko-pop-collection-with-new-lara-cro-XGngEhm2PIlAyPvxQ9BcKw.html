Like many other Funko Pop figures, these miniature Laras come to just under four inches tall (3.75 inches, to be exact), so they won't take up a whole lot of space in your collection. If you're a big Tomb Raider fan, or all of the recent announcements have you excited for more adventures with Lara, these certainly seem worth picking up.

These aren't the only new Funko Pop figures dropping soon, either. If you're in the shopping mood for more, there's also a few The Last of Us Funko figures based on the characters from the TV show, available to preorder right now and releasing in February. If you're hoping to add Joel, Ellie, Tommy, and a clicker to your shelves, now is a great time to get an order in for those.

Outside of these Funko figures, there's already quite a few items for those with a love of collectibles to look forward to this year. In the realm of action figures, preorders are live for the very cool looking Metroid Prime 4: Beyond CHOGOKIN Samus Aran figure at the IGN Store.
