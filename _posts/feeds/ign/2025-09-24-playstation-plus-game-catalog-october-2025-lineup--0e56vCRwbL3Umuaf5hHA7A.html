Sony has announced the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog October 2025 lineup as part of today's State of Play broadcast.

Next month's games list is headlined by Remedy's Alan Wake 2, the long-awaited sequel to its spooky survival adventure which launched to critical acclaim back in 2023.

Other games set to join the subscription next month include indie darling Cocoon and comedy sequel Goat Simulator 3. All three titles arrive on October 7.

But that's not all! The Last of Us Part 2 will be joining the PlayStation Plus Catalog, including its Remastered version for those playing on PlayStation 5.

The PlayStation Plus Classics Catalog will also be getting some additional titles before the end of this year, with Tekken 3, Soulcalibur 3 and Tomb Raider Anniversary all set to arrive 2026 is out.
