Many of the popular Star Wars LEGO sets over the last few years have been display models. Whether it's ships, helmets, or individual characters, the shift to a more adult audience has been obvious. This Star Wars Logo build is a representation of that shift in its purest form. This isn't something you can play with when you're done putting it together, it's just a straight up logo that is entirely meant to be displayed. It's perhaps the closest thing to a Star Wars-themed LEGO Art set currently available and is one of the most affordable 18+ builds you'll find within the franchise.

At 700 pieces, it can probably be put together in a single afternoon. Many of these pieces are the greebling details that go into the surface of each letter. Something as uniform as a logo has the potential to be incredibly boring to put together, so it's these tiny little pieces that make the build entertaining the full way though. And while this set doesn't come with any minifigures, there is a surprise Easter Egg hidden in the "T" for you to discover as you put it together.
