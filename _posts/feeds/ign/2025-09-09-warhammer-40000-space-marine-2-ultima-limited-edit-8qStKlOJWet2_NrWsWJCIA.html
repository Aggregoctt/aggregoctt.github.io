Alongside the main Space Marine 2 game and a very cool white and gold steelbook that features Lieutenant Titus on the front and a Tyranid on the back, the Ultima Limited Edition also comes with exclusive artwork, Season Pass 1 and 2 (which comes with a wide variety of decals, colors, armor pieces, and champion skins) and the Macragge's Chosen DLC. It's truly the full package for Space Marine fans to pick up.

We found a lot to love in Space Marine 2 when it was released last year. IGN's Chris Reed said in his Space Marine 2 review that it's "a terrific third-person shooter with a compelling story, loads of weapons that are a blast to use, and a healthy variety of enemies to use them on. It once again borrows many of its ideas from the Gears of War series, but they’re good ideas, and Space Marine 2 does a fine job of making them its own."

With Space Marine 3 officially in development over at Saber Interactive, there's truly no better time to jump back into this world and savor everything available in it.
