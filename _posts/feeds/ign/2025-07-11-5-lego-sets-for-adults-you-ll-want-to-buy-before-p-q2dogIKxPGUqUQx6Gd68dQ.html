LEGO: these building sets are not just for kids anymore. Over the past few years, LEGO has been releasing more and more LEGO sets for adults. Some of these are complicated builds that would pose a challenge for kids. Others are nostalgia-fueled sets like the Atari 2600. In any case, there are a bunch of LEGO sets on sale for Prime Day 2025. And since today is the last day of the sale, I thought I’d pick out the five sets for adults to buy before the prices go back up. LEGO is definitely one of those things you'll want to buy before Prime Day ends this year.
