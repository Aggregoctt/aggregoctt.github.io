IGN's Hollow Knight map is here! Our interactive map tracks essential locations across Hallownest, including Grubs, Soul Totems, and Benches, so you always know where to go on your journey throughout the game's various tunnels and caverns.

Hollow Knight Interactive Map

The available map filters for our Hollow Knight interactive map include:

  • Points of Interest, including Soul Totems, Lifeblood Cocoons, and Whispering Roots.
  • Locations, such as Benches, Hot Springs, Stag Stations, and Vendors.
  • Collectibles, including Grubs, Charm Notches, Hallownest Seals, and Mask Shards.
  • Items, including Geo Deposits, Pale Ore, and Rancid Eggs.
  • Equipment, such as Charms, Abilities, and Spells.
  • Enemies, including Bosses, Grimm Troupe, and Warrior's Dream.
  • Quests, which includes Quest Items and Trials.
  • Other noteable map markers, such as Achievements and Shade Gates.
