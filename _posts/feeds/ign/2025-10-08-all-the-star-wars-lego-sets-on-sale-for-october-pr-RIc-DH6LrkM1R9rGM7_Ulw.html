We’re on the second (and final) day of Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days, aka October Prime Day. That means time is running out to pick up any of the on-sale items you might have your eye on. If it’s Star Wars LEGO sets you want, then you’re well served in this sale. There are plenty of options from pretty much every point in the Star Wars timeline, most of which are on sale for 30% off. We take a look below, or you can widen the scope and check out all the LEGO sets on sale for October Prime Day.

Star Wars LEGO Sets on Sale

If you’re into Ahsoka, you can pick up a big discount on the Ghost and Phantom II set, which lets you build two ships, one big and one small. Unlike many Star Wars LEGO sets, these ships aren’t all gray on the outside. They’re white, with colorful accents in gray, red, yellow, and green. They look terrific. It also comes with four minifigures and the droid Chopper. Additionally, there’s a set based on Ahsoka Tano’s Duel on Peridea that’s also on sale.

Fans of The Mandalorian can snag a deal on Grogu with Hover Pram. This set has you build Baby Yoda himself, along with the floating carriage he travels around in. These two can be displayed with Grogu in the vehicle or outside of it. It also comes with a buildable Sorgan frog, a shifter knob, and cookies for Grogu to hold. Another Mandalorian set on sale is the Spider Tank, which comes with minifigures of Mando, Bo-Katan Kryze, and a tiny Grogu.

Skeleton Crew fans can pick up the Onyx Cinder Spaceship on sale. Anyone who watched the original trilogy can get a discount on a scene depicting the boarding of the Tantive IV, which started the whole series. Speaking of early in the timeline, a set of six BrickHeadz figures from the Phantom Menace are also on sale. These stylized characters include ar Jar Binks, Anakin Skywalker, Queen Amidala, Captain Panaka, Qui-Gon Jinn and Darth Maul, all with accessories. For the adult age group, there's also a great looking Droideka, the rolling combat droids from the prequel trilogy.
