Deus Ex Remastered is on the way, and it’s already got a release date of February 5, 2026.

Sony and Aspyr Media announced a refresh for the original Deus Ex video game during its September 2025 State of Play today. It comes with a trailer, teasing a re-release for a classic that is only a few months away from launch.

Deus Ex Remastered is coming to PlayStation 5. Its full platform list is unclear.

While we wait for updates on how exactly Deus Ex is different from the original, you can check out everything announced at the September 2025 State of Play.
