Nintendo has announced a Switch 2 Edition of Super Mario Bros. Wonder, which sees the oddball side-scrolling platformer gain additional levels, power-ups and a fresh mini-game mode.

Officially titled Super Mario Bros. Wonder: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park, the fresh version of the game will launch in spring 2026.

The latter half of that title, Meetup in Bellabel Park, looks set to offer a plaza full of multiplayer mini-games that task players with coin collecting, playing 'tag', and much more.

As for additions to the main Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Nintendo promised further details would arrive at a later date — though one quick clip featured a new Bellabel Flower power-up. Mysterious.

Even stranger, Nintendo will launch a toy version of Wonder's chatty flowers. The talking object will also launch in spring 2026, price unknown.

For more, check out everything announced at the Nintendo Direct September 2025.
