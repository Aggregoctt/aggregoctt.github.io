The Lenovo T210 carrying case is designed to be carried around as-is, although you could probably stuff this into an even bigger bag if you needed to. It features grab handles, a messenger bag strap, and - when you're traveling on the airplane - a luggage bag strap that can slip onto luggage handles making it a cinch to tote around as a carry-on bag. For $12, there's not much more you can ask for.
