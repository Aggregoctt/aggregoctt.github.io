Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days, as you can imagine, have plenty of deals on the retailers own hardware products.

None is more popular, though, than the Fire TV Stick, which offers a cheap way to get a bevvy of apps onto your TV, smart or otherwise. Here’s the full lineup on sale right now.

These Fire TV Stick Deals Are Great For Accessing Your Favorite Streaming Content

While it’s true that many TVs have streaming services built in, and others will use their video game consoles to access the likes of Netflix, the fact that the Fire TV Stick HD starts from just $17.99 in today’s deals makes it ideal for a second TV.

Plug it into an HDMI, connect a power cable, and you’re good to go with the likes of Prime Video (obviously), Netflix, Disney Plus, and much, much more.

As the name suggests, this model caps out at 1080p, but the Fire TV Stick 4K ramps things up with higher resolution, with support for Dolby Vision, HDR 10+, and Dolby Atmos audio. It’s available for $24.99, a 50% price drop.

Then there’s the Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max, which amps up the standard model with double the storage (16GB versus 8GB), faster Wi-Fi 6E connectivity, and a faster processor for more quickly opening and switching between apps. It’s $39.99, a 33% drop from the MSRP.

The Fire TV 4K models can run Xbox Game Pass Ultimate cloud streaming tech to play games without a console, and as we covered yesterday, Amazon has a bundle that includes a month of the subscription service (now worth $30, lest we forget), an Xbox controller, and the Fire TV Stick 4K (non Max).

You can save 40% on the bundle, bringing the $149.97 package down to $90.18.
