Magic: The Gathering’s Universes Beyond lineup of fresh sets based on third-party properties is showing no signs of slowing down, but before Marvel’s Spider-Man and Avatar: The Last Airbender, Final Fantasy has stolen the show.

The set is the most popular in Magic’s long history, and has led to prices skyrocketing for collector’s items like the Travelling Chocobo range and characters like Sephiroth.

These are the priciest Final Fantasy cards you can buy right now, thanks to data from TCGPlayer.

The Most Valuable MTG Final Fantasy Cards Right Now

Vivi Ornitier (Borderless) - $104.48

Final Fantasy 9 mage Vivi Ornitier (Borderless) has been a particularly popular card in this set, but don’t pay the $500 he’s up for right now.

Tifa Lockhart (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $150.76

Magic: The Gathering Tifa Lockhart

Borderless - Surge Foil

Fan-favorite Tifa Lockhart (Borderless - Surge Foil) is down to around $150, with a really nice green backdrop.

Clive, Ifrit’s Dominant (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $186.08

Magic: The Gathering Clive, Ifrit’s Dominant

Borderless - Surge Foil

It’s going for over $400, but the market value of Final Fantasy 16 protagonist Clive, Ifrit’s Dominant (Borderless - Surge Foil), and the flip-side’s Ifrit, Warden of Inferno, is under $200.

Kefka, Court Mage (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $218.69

Magic: The Gathering Kefka, Court Mage

Borderless - Surge Foil

Kefka, Court Mage (Borderless - Surge Foil) puts the clown front-and-center, while offering a transformation into Kefka, Ruler of Ruin on the other side.

Lightning, Army of One (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $318.71

Magic: The Gathering Lightning, Army of One

Borderless - Surge Foil

Lightning, Army of One (Borderless - Surge Foil) was a super popular card at the set’s launch, and this is far and away the best-looking version of a fantastic aggro card.

Yuna, Hope of Spira (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $371.99

Magic: The Gathering Yuna, Hope of Spira

Borderless - Surge Foil

Yuna, Hope of Spira (Borderless - Surge Foil) is one of the best-looking surge foil treatments in the set, giving the legendary summoner a card befitting of her status in Final Fantasy X.

Cloud, Midgar Mercenary (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $485.36

Magic: The Gathering Cloud, Midgar Mercenary

Borderless - Surge Foil

Cloud, Midgar Mercenary (Borderless - Surge Foil) can run you almost $500, but its his rival that takes top spot for Final Fantasy 7 characters.

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER (Borderless - Surge Foil) - $890.93

Magic: The Gathering Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER

Borderless - Surge Foil

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER (Borderless - Surge Foil) offers gorgeous artwork of one of gaming’s most legendary bad guys, and it flips over for the One-Winged Angel side of the character, too.

Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Neon Ink) - $1779.29 to $2,159.62

These Borderless, Neon Ink treatments of Final Fantasy’s most iconic bird creature are fetching huge prices right now.

Here’s how the prices shake out:

  • Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Neon Ink Blue) - $2,159.62
  • Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Neon Ink Green) - $2,028.27
  • Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Neon Ink Pink) - $1,824.18
  • Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Neon Ink Yellow) - $1,779.29

Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Japan Exclusive) - $4,836.47

Magic: The Gathering Travelling Chocobo

Borderless - Japan Exclusive

The Travelling Chocobo (Borderless - Japan Exclusive) is the most expensive card of any 2025 Magic: The Gathering set, racking up a total of around $5,000. It’s still fluctuating regularly, but it’s ahead by a long shot.
