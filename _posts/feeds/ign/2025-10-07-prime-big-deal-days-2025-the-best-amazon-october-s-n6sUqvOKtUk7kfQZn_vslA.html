Prime Big Deal Days is a two-day event, which means you only have 48 hours total to take advantage of these savings. Some of these discounts won't last throughout the entire sale window, especially if it's a Lightning Deal, so don't hesitate if you see something you like. Prime Big Deal Days vs. Black Friday Deals

Although Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days has a lot of good deals, we still expect Black Friday to have more discounts on just about everything in 2025. With that in mind, more discounts on everything doesn't necessarily mean better discounts. There are plenty of price cuts in the October Prime Day sale that may actually be more expensive next month. The rising costs in the U.S. could potentially have an impact on what deals are available between now and Black Friday, but it's difficult to know just how big that impact will be.

If you're wondering if it's worth shopping now or waiting until Black Friday to buy, it really just depends on what it is you're looking for. Some items, like major appliances, won't get big price drops until November. But if you're looking for a cool LEGO set or a decent TV, now is a great time to grab one at a discount. Black Friday is still over a month away, but these deals are available right now. If something you want is on sale at a great price, just buy it—there's no guarantee of a better discount later.
