New York Comic-Con 2025 is right around the corner, and Mondo is betting big on Daredevil for this year's crop of Marvel exclusives. IGN can exclusively reveal both of Mondo's Daredevil-themed NYCC exclusives, including the latest addition to the Spider-Man: The Animated Series line of 1:6 scale figures and a classic Daredevil poster.

Check out the slideshow gallery below to see both the figure and the poster:

While previous Spider-Man figures from Mondo have focused on the mainstay heroes and villains of the classic '90s animated series, this latest release is veering a bit further afield. This Daredevil figure is based on the character's guest appearances in episodes like "Framed" and "The Man Without Fear." As you can see, the figure includes distinctive black shading that makes this version of the character so visually unique.

This Daredevil figure clocks in at 12 inches tall with a full range of articulation. The figure includes various accessories like an unmasked head sculpt, billy club weapons, and alternate hands. The figure is priced at $225.

The Daredevil poster, meanwhile, hearkens all the way back to Ol' Hornhead's very first appearance in 1964's Daredevil #1. Mondo's latest Marvel poster reproduces the original cover art by Jack Kirby and Bill Everett.

This poster is a limited edition screenprint on French Index Off-White paper and is priced at $80. Note that it'll only be sold at NYCC and will not be available on Mondo's website.

NYCC 2025 runs from Thursday, October 9 through Sunday, October 12. Stay tuned to IGN for plenty more coverage from the show.
