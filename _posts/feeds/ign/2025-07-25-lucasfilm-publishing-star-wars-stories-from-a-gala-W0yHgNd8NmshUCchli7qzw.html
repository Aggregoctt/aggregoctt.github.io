Star Wars: Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away, Lucasfilm Publishing's big SDCC 2025 panel, has arrived and has given a glimpse into the future of Star Wars books and comics, including The High Republic - The Finale, Hyperspace Stories: General Grievous, and the absolutely adorable You're My Little Grogu board book.

There were a lot of exciting announcements and reveals, and we're here to break them all down right here so you don't miss a thing!

Industrial Light & Magic: 50 Years of Innovation

Industrial Light & Magic: 50 Years of Innovation is set to be released in Early 2026 by Ian Failes and is a "spectacular celebration" of the award-winning visual effects house behind Star Wars, Indiana Jones, E.T. Terminator 2, Jurassic Park, and more.

The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte

The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte

On January 13, 2026, Kristin Baver's The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte will be released, the official behind-the-scenes companion to the Disney+ series. Fans will be able to check out production art, character and vehicle designs, planets, storyboards, and more of the production of the series set 100 years before The Phantom Menace.

Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: Bad Batch #2: Rogue Agents

Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: Bad Batch #2: Rogue Agents

The Bad Batch returns in December 2025 in Michael Moreci's Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: Bad Batch #2: Rogue Agents. In this tale, we follow Clone Force 99 as they track down a Separatist scientist and his "dangerous invention," who went missing during the Clone Wars. Since nothing can be too easy, our crew quickly learns they aren't the only ones on the scientist's trail.

Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: Tides of Terror

Kit Fisto takes center stage in Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: Tides of Terror on July 30, 2025. From author George Mann, this new adventure follows the Jedi Master as he "explores a distant ocean planet and fends off the fierce beasts that swim in its depths."

Fisto won't be headed to this planet called Tordus alone, however, as Mace Windu, Shaak Ti, and Fisto's Padawan Nahdar Vebb will be along for the ride. Their journey takes them to a controversial underwater research facility that was built to explore the ruins of an ancient civilization, and danger strikes when the station begins flooding.

Joining Mann will be the penciler/inker duo Luis Morocho and Le Beau Underwood.

Star Wars: Tales From the Nightlands 1-3

Cavan Scott's Star Wars: Tales From the Nightlands will debut on September 5, 2025, and tells a tale of Anakin Skywalker and his Twi'lek friend Daesha. This story is set on Ryloth and explores the Nightlands, which is a realm where Twi'lek spirits retire after death. On nights when the "veil is thinnest, you can speak with the shades who reside there."

Anakin in on Ryloth to help Daesha summon a relative for a final goodbye, but their quest to make contact with the Nightlands turns dangerous as The Nightlander approaches, who is a "Star Wars boogeyman that seeks to possess a living host and bring chaos to the galaxy!"

Leter issues look to follow some other fan favorite characters as well!

Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: General Grievous

Early 2026 will see the arrival of Michael Moreci's Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories: General Grievous, and this story follows the beloved villain as he "stalks a Jedi Knight and his battalion of clone troopers on the jungle planet of Katou."

Count Dooku sent Grievous to Katou and the mission is a way to prove his worth to his Sith masters, but the Jedi Knight, Cardiff Baye, and his clone troopers won't be an easy target and may require a bit more stealth than usual.

Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures - The Training Sessions HC

Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, and Nubs are the stars of Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures - The Training Sessions HC, which will be released on December 9, 2025. In this story, our trio have to race Wookiees to climb the tallest trees on Kashyyyk, Nubs and Qort have to rescue a Republic ship from disaster, and the younglings have to go on a scavenger hunt in a "stinky swamp full of monsters."

Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures - The Training Sessions HC comes from writers Dave Scheidt and Daniel José Older alongside artist Andy Duggan.

Star Wars: The High Republic - The Finale

The "end of the beginning" of Star Wars: The High Republic starts on July 30, 2025, from author Cavan Scott and artist Marika Cresta. Star Wars:

The High Republic - The Finale chronicles the aftermath of the final battle between the Jedi and the Nihil, where "sacrifices have been made." After the dust settles, friends and allies "gather for one last celebration, a decision is made, and a fate is sealed."

It was also teased that there will be guest stars from across The High Republic era and that "all roads lead to Dalna."

Star Wars: You're My Little Grogu

For our youngest fans of that galaxy far, far away, Star Wars: You're My Little Grogu is an adorable, rhyming board book that features some of our favorite characters from The Mandalorian, including Grogu, Ahsoka Tano, Luke Skywalker, Bo-Katan, and Din Djarin. As for the story, it "celebrates a parents' love for their child" and is a "wonderful read for traveling the Galaxy or any time."

Star Wars; You're My Little Grogu is the latest entry in the best-selling 'You're My Little' series and will be released in April 2026. Grace Baranowski is the author and Kaley McCabe is serving as the artist.
