This Day in Gaming 🎂

In retro news, I'm throwing a big birthday biscuit to Nintendogs, Nintendo's adorable pet simulator that had me training puppies on my DS. Initially, you could get it in three different breeds: Dachshund, Chihuahua or Lab. I went with the latter, and, last I checked, 'Chewbarka' is still living his best life on my cartridge.

This one wasn't just a game, it was a cultural phenomenon. Suddenly, my school bus was full of kids whistling at their dual screens, teaching tricks, and proudly showing off their digital pooches. It made the DS feel like something magical; a gadget that blurred the line between toy and companion. I remember how its voice recognition felt like voodoo at the time, even if Chewie obeyed commands as often as my real-life cat did.
