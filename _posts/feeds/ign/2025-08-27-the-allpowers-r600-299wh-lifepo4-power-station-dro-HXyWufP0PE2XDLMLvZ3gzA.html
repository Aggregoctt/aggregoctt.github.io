The Allpowers R600 is a portable 299WH power station for off-grid use, whether it be as in-home battery backup or your sole source of power during your outdoor adventures. It has two AC outlets capable of 600W (1,200W surge) each, which is double the capacity of an equivalent Ecoflow River power station. It even features a pure sine wave inverter to protect sensitive electronics. The R600 also includes two USB Type-C ports with 100W output, two USB Type-A ports with 18W output, a cigarette lighter with 120W output, and an inductive wireless charger with 15W output.

The 299Whr battery isn't big enough to replace a gas generator, but it can still power mobile elecronics (including your laptop) for several hours to days. It's also much lighter, weighing in at about 16 pounds. The R600 uses LiFePO4 cells instead of NCM batteries you'd find in some other budget power stations. LiFePO4 cells have inherently safer chemistry and have a much higher cycle lifespan than NCM cells. They also have lower calendar aging.

The R600 can be used as a UPS battery backup, which means it will automatically swap to battery power when there is a power outage. The shutoff delay is less than 10ms, which is small enough for even sensitive electronics like PCs to remain on the entire time. The R600 also features smart functionality via an app on your smartphone, which will allow you to check on its status, download updates, and even power on or off outlets remotely. This Allpowers power station comes with a 2 year warranty as well as a 90-day free return window.
