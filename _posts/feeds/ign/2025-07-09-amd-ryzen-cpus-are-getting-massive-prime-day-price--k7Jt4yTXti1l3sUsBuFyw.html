Over the last few years, AMD CPUs have quickly made an impressive push in the market, providing better and faster performance than their Intel counterparts in many instances. Since building a new PC or upgrading your current one can be quite expensive, it's important to save every dollar you can by picking up components on sale throughout the year.

Amazon Prime Day sales are live all week, and there are numerous deals on AMD CPUs. Many of these discounts include AMD's 2024 CPU lineup of the Ryzen 9000 Series, which are some of the best gaming CPUs on the market. If you're planning to upgrade your CPU anytime soon, do not miss your chance to potentially save hundreds of dollars with these CPU deals for Prime Day.

AMD CPUs on Sale for Prime Day 2025

There are a lot of amazing deals here, especially if you want to build a gaming PC that will last for years to come. The Ryzen 9 9950X for $434 sits as the best deal available currently, saving you over $200 off this extremely powerful processor. The 9950X packs in 16 cores and 32 threads, making it the ultimate CPU for creative workloads, video editing, and more.

A great option for PCs built around gaming is the Ryzen 7 9700X. This 2024 processor includes 8 cores and 16 threads, with an increase to 40MB of cache from the previous model. Additionally, if you're looking for the cheapest Zen 5 processor, you can score the Ryzen 5 9600X for $156. This CPU has outstanding performance for its price point, with 6 cores and 12 threads available.

Finally, if you're still on Zen 4 and wish to skip out on upgrading your motherboard to Zen 5, the Ryzen 7 5800XT is on sale for $141, and the Ryzen 7 7800X3D is $341. You can still get great performance on modern games with both of these, and as we all know, almost anything is better than upgrading your motherboard.
