The Baseus international charger has a total of six ports: a retractable USB Type-C cable (70W), a USB Type-C port (60W ), two USB Type-A ports (60W and 5W), one three-prong AC outlet, and one two-prong AC outlet. The AC outlets can deliver up to 1,200W of power. The charger also includes four different receptacle plugs (Type C/A/G/I) for use in over 200 countries across Europe, Asia, Australia, and the United States, so you don't have to bring an extra plug adapter.

The 70W USB Type-C power output allows it to fast charge an Apple iPhone 17 (40W-60W), iPhone 16 (30W), and Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra (45W), or pretty much any smartphone with a USB Type-C port. It's also powerful enough to charge the Nintendo Switch or Switch 2, Steam Deck, or Asus ROG Ally gaming handhelds at their maximum rate. You'll also be able to play while charging at the same time.

Other features include GaN technology, which allows it to be smaller than typical chargers (it measures only 3"x2"x2") and a built-in 27" USB PD 3.1 cable in case you forget to bring your own.
