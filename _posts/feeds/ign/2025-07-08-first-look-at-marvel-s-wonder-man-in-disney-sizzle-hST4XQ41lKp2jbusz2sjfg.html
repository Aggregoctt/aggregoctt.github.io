Disney+ just revealed footage from its late 2025 slate, including new and returning shows like Marvel’s Wonder Man, the King of the Hill reboot, the highly-anticipated small screen debut of the Alien franchise Alien: Earth, and the new true crime adaptation The Twisted Tale of Amanda Knox, among many others.

The MCU show is probably our most anticipated, of course, with, honestly, most of the rest of the new projects falling not far behind. In the Wonder Man clips, fans get a taste of Candyman’s Yahya Abdul-Mateen II as Simon Williams, an actor and stunt performer with powers who auditions for the lead role in a superhero TV series. Very meta, of course.

Though the sneak peek is only 60 seconds, we do get some fast-paced and exciting footage from Wonder Man that also highlights legendary actor Ben Kingsley in a supporting role.

He’ll be playing Trevor Slattery, a failed actor who was previously abducted by the Ten Rings organization and went on to become immortal warlord Xu Wenwu’s court jester. We see them both converse and we also see what looks like an audition setting, as well as a tense moment on-set.

Outside of Wonder Man, that Alien: Earth footage is sure to get folks even more hyped up for the show. Fans get a glimpse of the space vessel that crash lands on Earth that sets the entire story into motion, as well as Sydney Chandler as the first robot-human hybrid and the story’s protagonist.

Additionally, fans can expect the slate to also include new series Chad Powers with Glen Powell and Ryan Murphy’s legal drama All’s Fair, as well as season 2 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians and season 5 of Only Murders in the Building — all featured in the exciting sizzle.
