There's never been a better time to invest in a Sonos home audio setup. Sonos is offering a pre-matched Dolby Atmos surround set consisting of the Sonos Beam Gen 2 soundbar and two Era 100 speakers on sale for $727 (normally $937). But there's actually a way for you to build out the exact same setup but well under $500.

For October Prime Day, Amazon is offering 35% off coupon that works on hundreds of items at Amazon Resale (formerly Warehouse). This is Amazon's official channel for used products. The coupon happens to work on "Like New" condition Sonos speakers, including the Beam Gen 2 soundbar and the Era 100 smart speaker.
