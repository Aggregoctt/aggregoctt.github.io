Magic: The Gathering’s first set of the year, Innistrad Remastered, marks the fourth such remaster Wizards of the Coast has released over the years.

Incorporating cards from Shadows over Innistrad, Innistrad Midnight Hunt, Innistrad Crimson Vow, and Innistrad itself, it’s a set packed with shlocky horror and vampiric vibes.

Thanks to data from the team at TCGPlayer, we’ve got a full rundown of the priciest cards from the set, but as you’ll see, one certainly runs away with things.

The most valuable Innistrad Remastered cards

Edgar Markov (Retro Frame) - $16.28
