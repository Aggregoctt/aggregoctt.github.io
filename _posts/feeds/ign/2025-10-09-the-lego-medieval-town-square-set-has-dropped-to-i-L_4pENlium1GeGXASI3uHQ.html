It's not like this set has never been discounted before, but this is by far the lowest price we've ever seen it on Amazon and it just so happens to be during October Prime Day. It's still an expensive set, but when you factor in how many pieces this build includes it becomes obvious why this is such a good deal. The LEGO Medieval Town Square set is made up of 3,305 pieces and eight minifigures. It even comes with a little cat, squirrel, and a goat. Pretty much everything you need for a successful plastic brick village.

The build itself is split up into two different buildings. There's a house with a little guard tower included as well as a cluster of what looks to be three residential buildings. There's a ton of other little details in between those two things that make a rather lively scene once it's all put together. At over 3,000 pieces, this isn't a set that you'll likely be finishing in one sitting, but it is likely one you'll enjoy every time you sit down to build more of it. And once you're done putting it together you can choose to display it and move the characters around any time you feel like switching it up.
