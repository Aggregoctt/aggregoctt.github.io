Heat: Pedal to the Metal is currently on sale for $47.99 for Prime members as part of Amazon's October Prime Day event. This is a 36% discount from its list price of $74.99, but what's more important is it's a new low price for the game. Outside of Prime-exclusive sale events like this, the game's lowest price was $49.99 according to price tracker camelcamelcamel, so Prime members can save a couple of extra bucks with this exclusive discount.

The nice thing about Heat is it can be played either solo or with a group of friends. As you play, you'll jump into races where you'll have to push your vehicle as fast as it can go without overheating. We were very big fans of it in our review, giving it a glowing 9/10 score.

Reviewer Charlie Theel said it's, "a vivid and delightful racing board game that surpasses the accomplishments of all that came before it. It’s an inspired title in both product and design, offering a range of options while presenting a dynamic racing system that is extremely impressive."
