Jakks Pacific has brought a stacked lineup of collectibles to San Diego Comic-Con, covering franchises like The Simpsons, Nintendo, DC x Sonic, and Dog Man. IGN has an exclusive first look at the entire lineup being revealed at Jakks Pacific's SDCC panel.

Check out the slideshow gallery below for a closer look at all these new figures, along with pricing and release date information. Note that, due to the ongoing global tariff situation, prices are subject to change.

The Simpsons fans have plenty of new figures to look forward to in both the 2.5-inch and 5-inch collections. For the former, Jakks Pacific is offering a Simpson Sedan bundle that includes the iconic pink car and the entire Simpson clan. For the latter, expect more Treehouse of Horror-themed releases, including Fly Boy Bart and King Homer. There's even a Costco-exclusive Treehouse of Horror Countdown Calendar, featuring 31 different 1.5-inch figurines to be unboxed over the course of October. And don't forget the previously revealed Funzo animatronic doll.

On the DC x Sonic front, Jakks Pacific is using the license as an opportunity to release some very cool 5-inch Justice League figures based on the DC x Sonic the Hedgehog comic. Each figure comes packaged with a member of the Sonic family dressed in a matching superhero outfit.

Also worth highlighting is a Gamestop-exclusive Shadow Ice Cream Plush, a companion to last year's Comic-Con-exclusive Sonic Ice Cream Plush.

For more SDCC collectibles, check out Hasbro's Marvel Legends Series Spider-Man Electronic Expressive Mask.
