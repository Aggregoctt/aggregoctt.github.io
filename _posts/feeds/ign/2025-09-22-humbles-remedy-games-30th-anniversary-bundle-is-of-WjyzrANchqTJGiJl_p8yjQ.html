Paying just $27 nets you all of these games, but you can choose to pay a little bit extra to support the Humble community and a charity, too, which is Save the Children for this bundle. You can choose between higher amounts of $32, $37, and $42.

If you're not looking to drop that much on the bundle, though, paying at least $12 gets you eight games (Quantum Break, Control Ultimate Edition, Alan Wake Remastered, Alan Wake Collector's Edition, Max Payne, Max Payne 2: The Fall of Max Payne, Death Rally, and Alan Wake's American Nightmare), while paying at least $5 sets you up with four games (Max Payne, Max Payne 2: The Fall of Max Payne, Death Rally, and Alan Wake's American Nightmare). It's also worth noting that the coupon for FBC: Firebreak is for the Humble store and it does not stack with Humble Store sales.

This bundle is set to run for another 18 days, so there's plenty of time to grab it and add it to your PC library.
