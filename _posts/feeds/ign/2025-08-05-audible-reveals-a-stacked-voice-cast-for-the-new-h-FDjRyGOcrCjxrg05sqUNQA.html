There are already multiple versions of the Harry Potter books available on Audible. There's the original audiobooks narrated by Jim Dale (which actually earned him a Grammy) as well as another set narrated by Stephen Fry. Now Audible is at it again with a third iteration of the audio series, this time with a full voice cast for each character.

The first round of cast announcements includes Hugh Laurie as Dumbleore, who is now set to play a new version of the Hogwarts Headmaster before John Lithgow stars in the upcoming TV series. The first audiobook in the new series is set to arrive on November 4, 2025 and you can already preorder it from Amazon or Audible directly. Release dates for the other six books have not yet been revealed.
