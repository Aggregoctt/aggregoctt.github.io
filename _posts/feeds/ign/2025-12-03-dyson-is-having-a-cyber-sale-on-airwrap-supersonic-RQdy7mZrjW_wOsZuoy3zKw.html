The Supersonic is a hair dryer on steroids. This is another product that can retail for as much as $650 but is on sale for $199.99 with code. Dyson Fans and Heaters

Dyson's fans and heaters have their fan blades positioned internally, making these a safer and still effective option for anyone with toddlers or kids. They are also air purifiers that remove up to 99.97% of allergens and pollutants as small as 0.3 microns from your home (basically the same rating as HEPA). Dyson cooling only fan models start at $150, while the cooling + heat models start at $240. These prices are more affordable than retail options which cost up to $1,000.

Dyson Vacuums

The vacuum cleaners are what propelled Dyson into becoming a household brand name. Several different options are discounted on eBay, including uprights, canisters, and hand vacs. They boast powerful suction, bagless disposal, and HEPA-type filtration. The hand vac models are especially popular because they include an attachment that converts them into a stick vac for versatility. I've owned one of these for well over a decade and it still works well. I've had to replace some parts over the years, but the nice thing about Dyson is that you can easily find inexpensive aftermarket parts.
