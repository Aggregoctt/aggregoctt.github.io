Audible’s Big Starter Deal Is Back

This deal gives you three months at a reduced price, after which it’ll renew at £8.99 per month, but you can cancel beforehand.

What’s neat about Audible is that each month you earn a ‘Credit’ which you can use to add a book to your library permanently, even if you cancel your subscription.

With that in mind, you’re getting three audiobooks for less than £3. Given a single one of those books would set you back a tenner or more for a physical copy, that’s quite the saving.

You’re also getting member-only deals while you’re subscribed, and you also have access to a whole library of other books to listen to with no listening limits - you just won’t have that access if your membership lapses.

The offer runs until December 16, and you can only redeem it once per account. It also won’t work if you’re an existing Audible subscriber, sadly.

Still, if you’re looking to check out books like Dan Brown’s The Secret of Secrets, Richard Osman’s The Impossible Fortune: Thursday Murder Club, and Lisa Jewell’s Don’t Let Him In, then there’s never been a better time.
