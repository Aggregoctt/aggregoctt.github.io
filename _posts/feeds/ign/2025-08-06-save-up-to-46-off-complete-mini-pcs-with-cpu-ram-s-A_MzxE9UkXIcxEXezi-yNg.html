Acemagic Micro PCs for Gaming

These Mini PCs are equipped with newer AMD Ryzen CPUs that have onboard GPUs that are quite capable of playing even modern day games. The Ryzen 7 6800H and Ryzen 9 6900HX feature the Radeon 680M iGPU and the Ryzen 7 8745HS and 7840HS have the Radeon 780M iGPU, which is newer and offers a bit better performance. Both are excellent for gaming as far as onboard solutions go. In the past, iGPU gaming performance was just a meme, but not so anymore. They're perfectly capable of running games like Fortnite and Minecraft at 1080p, but they can even run more demanding games like Red Dead Redemption 2, Apex Legends, or even Cyberpunk at a reduced 720p/1080p resolution in FSR performance mode and maintain a respectable 30fps frame rate. Sure, you'll get better performance when you throw in a discrete card, but then you're paying at least double or triple the price.

Acemagic Micro PCs for Productivity and Streaming

If you don't intend to use your mini PC for gaming, you can find deals for as low as $123 for a very capable model. These mini PCs have processors ranging from the Intel N150, which you'll find in entry level Chromebooks and home NAS servers, to the much powerful AMD Ryzen 7 5875U CPU, which was commonly found in laptops (although it's a few generations old now). These are great options for everyday productivity tasks like word processing, browsing, and up to 4K @ 60fps video streaming. They're not great for gaming because the integrated graphics can't really handle gaming at resolutions of 1080p and up. For that, you'd have to look at the other models above.

These mini PCs include plenty of connectivity options. All the ones on thist list include USB 3.0 port and USB 2.0 ports for your peripherals or for extra storage, HDMI and DisplayPorts for video, RJ45 etheernet port, and audio jacks. They're also equipped with WiFi and Bluetooth.
