Here it is — our first peek at Cody Rhodes as Street Fighter's Guile.

Admittedly, the image really doesn't show us much — actually, a less chartiable position is that it doesn't show us anything! — but we do, at least, have it confirmed that Rhodes will be rocking Guile's signature flat-top hairdo and a whole buncha muscles. You can check it out below:

Cody Rhodes is, of course, a famed professional wrestler. He's in his second reign as WWE Champion, as well as the present King of the Ring, and is, of course, no stranger to video games having played himself in a raft of WWE titles. He also joined Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 as a playable operative alongside Rhea Ripley and Rey Mysterio, and most recently appeared as a bartender in The Naked Gun.

Street Fighter is a feature film based on the popular Capcom fighting game, and it's expected to release on March 20 next year. So far key roles announced include Rapper Curtis Jackson, aka 50 Cent, as boxer Balrog, Aquaman star Jason Momoa as Blanka, Noah Centineo as Ken, another WWE superstar Roman Reigns as Akuma, Andrew Koji as Ryu, Callina Liang as Chun-Li, and villain specialist David Dastmalchian as M. Bison.

It feels as though we're in the best of times when it comes video game adaptations, not least due to stellar projects like The Super Mario Bros. Movie, Sonic the Hedgehog, and TV series based on The Last of Us and Fallout.
