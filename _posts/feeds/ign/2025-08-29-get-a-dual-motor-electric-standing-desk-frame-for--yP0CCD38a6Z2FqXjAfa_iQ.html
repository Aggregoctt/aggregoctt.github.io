Normally desk frames at this price point only include a single motor. However the Topsky frame features dual motors. Two motors generally have a longer lifespan because each motor doesn't need to work as hard and the weight on the motor is distributed more evenly. They're also generally quieter.

The Topsky frame supports up to 225 pounds. The crossbar can be extended by up to 5 feet and will accomodate desktop surfaces up to about 6 feet long and 32" deep. The height range is between 27.5" to 47.2 inches and features an anti-collison sensor. The control panel has simple button controls with three memory presets and a digital height display.

This frame does not include a tabletop

Instead of opting for a cheap tabletop with a seam down the middle and particle board core, you can instead get yourself a much nicer desktop surface. Some popular suggestions are the veneered countertop surfaces at Ikea, solid wood desktops at Home Depot, or your local Facebook marketplace. The frame simply screws into the desktop, which means it's universally compatible with pretty much any tabletop that fits the dimensions and weight limit.
