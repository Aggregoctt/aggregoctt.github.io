While these models of AirPods 4 don't offer noise-canceling features like their sibling model with ANC, they still offer excellent sound quality thanks to the H2 chip. On top of that, they also feature Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking, which make sounds feel like they're coming from all around you.

The AirPods 4 also have quite a tough build that's IP54 dust, sweat, and water resistant. So if you're someone who frequently works out or if you get caught in the rain, you don't have to worry too much about these earbuds being damaged. They've got a nice battery life as well that can last up to five hours on a single charge or, if you use the case, you can stretch it up to 30 hours.

This deal is just one of many early deals to cross our radar ahead of Prime Big Deal Days, and there's bound to be plenty more when the sale starts up tomorrow.
