AirPods Pro 2 vs AirPods 4 with ANC: Which Should You Buy?

If you can stretch to the Pro model, it’s worth it. The AirPods Pro 2 offer better sound, more effective noise canceling, and adjustable in-ear tips for a more secure fit and proper audio seal.

Featuring Apple’s H2 chip, a refined driver and amp setup, and in-ear passive isolation, they remain the top pick for iPhone users. You’ll also get useful features like Adaptive Transparency Mode and Conversation Mode, plus a MagSafe case and USB-C charging.

The AirPods 4 with ANC are more comfortable for some due to their open-ear design, but that also means more sound leakage and less isolation, despite sharing the same H2 chip.

With the price gap now just $20, the Pro model easily justifies the jump unless you strongly prefer the open fit.
