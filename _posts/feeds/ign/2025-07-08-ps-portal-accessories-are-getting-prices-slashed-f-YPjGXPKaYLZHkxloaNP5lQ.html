TL;DR: Top PS Portal Accessory Deals

The PlayStation Portal isn’t as portable as the name suggests, as it requires a strong Wi-Fi connection in order to be playable. At least a PlayStation 5 is no longer required to play games; a recent update to the PS Portal allows for cloud gaming when using a PlayStation Plus subscription. That extra functionality makes PS Portal accessories more necessary than ever.

While it’s hard to deny just how brilliant the crisp 8-inch LCD and responsive DualSense-style controls are on the PS Portal, an accidental drop or too much pressure when stored in a bag could spell the end of the pricey handheld. The easiest solution to the problem is grabbing a protective case and a screen protector. That ensures the remote player stays out of harm's way when you’re playing on the go — or just storing it. Luckily, plenty are on sale for Prime Day.

A hard shell case won’t do much good when you’re actually playing games on the PS Portal. You’ll want a great gaming headset to keep distractions at bay while you play. The PS Portal lacks Bluetooth support, which means the only way to listen to action using a headset is through PlayStation Link or a wired 3.5 mm aux. Unfortunately, none of Sony’s few PlayStation-Link-supported options have great discounts for Prime Day, making wired headsets your best option for savings.

For something a little bit more portable, top-tier gaming earbuds are the way to go. A PlayStation Portal dock serves two purposes: a place to store the remote player when it isn’t in use and a charging station. Sure, you could just use a basic USB-C charging block to top up the device, but docks from FASTSNAIL and LVVIL provide a more secure spot to charge quickly while also adding a bit more visual flair with RGB lighting. The PlayStation Portal has a surprisingly great battery life, lasting 7 to 9 hours before needing to recharge. However, you probably won’t always be right next to a wall outlet to charge it up, making a portable charger a handy accessory to have on hand.

Not only does it offer up to 45W PD — more than PS Portal is capable of — but it comes with a built-in USB-C cable; that’s one less thing to worry about when traveling with a PS Portal.

For an even cheaper option, INIU has a 10000mAh power bank on sale for less than $20. It still offers up to 45W power delivery and comes in an ultra-compact form factor. Unfortunately, it doesn’t have a built-in USB-C cable, so be sure to remember a cord when topping up the PS Portal on the go.
