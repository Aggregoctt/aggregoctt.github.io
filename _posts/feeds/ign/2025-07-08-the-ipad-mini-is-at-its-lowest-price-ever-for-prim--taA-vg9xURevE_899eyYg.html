Should You Get a Kindle Instead of the iPad Mini?

If you’re looking for a tablet that you’re going to use exclusively for reading and nothing else, a Kindle is often the way to go. Besides the Colorsoft model, Kindles feature e-ink, monochrome displays that are easier on the eyes, perform better in direct sunlight, and help to extend the battery life for weeks. However, their screens are far less luminous than the iPad mini, leading to major limitations. You’ll only get access to reading apps, while only some Kindles support note-taking; otherwise, they can’t do much more, so forget about checking emails, browsing the web, or streaming. The iPad mini, on the other hand, is a versatile, multitasking maven with a powerful processor.
