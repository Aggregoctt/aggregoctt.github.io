The iPad Mini is the smallest iPad in the stack, measuring 7.7" x 5.3" and only 0.23" thin. It weighs just 10 ounces. It's not that much bigger or heavier than an iPhone 16 Plus. The current generation model is equipped with an A17 Pro processor, which is better than the A16 Bionic found in the latest Apple iPad (2025). The iPad Mini has an 8.3" 2266x1488 display (326ppi) with P3 Wide Color and True Tone. Although the standard iPad screen is larger, it's not as sharp (264ppi) and doesn't support wide color gamut. Also, unlike the iPad, the iPad Mini is Apple Intelligence compatible.
