After months of rumors, leaks, and teases, the Google Pixel 10 series is finally here. Google's new flagship lineup consists of four options — Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, and 10 Pro Fold — with each offering specific upgrades over the last. If you're looking to pick up one of these freshly announced phones, you can do so right now at the Google Store or through retailers like Amazon and Best Buy.

The Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, and Pixel 10 Pro XL are all set to be released on August 28, but the Pixel 10 Pro Fold won't arrive until October 9 this year. If you're hoping to get the best possible preorder deal on the new phones, Amazon has one of the best offers for most people. We've also outlined all of the other promotions going on right now below.

The Google Pixel 10 Series Is Up for Preorder

The Pixel 10 starts at $799, while the 10 Pro and 10 Pro XL come in at $999 and $1,199, respectively. If you're looking to score a new foldable, you can grab the Pixel 10 Pro Fold starting at $1,799. Of course, be prepared to pay a higher premium if you need more storage space on your device of choice.

Where to Buy the New Unlocked Pixel 10 Phones

Unless you're planning to purchase some accessories or other items from the Google Store, I'd argue the Amazon or Best Buy credits are likely to be more versatile options. Either way you go, though, you're essentially getting a nice chunk off the cost of a future purchase at any of these retailers, making it more appealing to upgrade to a Pixel 10 series device before these deals end on September 4.

Here's a quick rundown of each of the major promotions going on for these new phones:

Google Store

If you buy the Pixel 10 directly from the Google Store between now and September 4, you can earn $100 in Google Store credit. If you want the Pixel 10 Pro or Pro XL, that credit increases to $200, while the Pixel 10 Pro Fold can score you a whopping $350. The Google Store will also accept trade-ins for additional credit.
