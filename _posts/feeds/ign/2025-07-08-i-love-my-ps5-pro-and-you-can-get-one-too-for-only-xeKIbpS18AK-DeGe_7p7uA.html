Why the PS5 Pro over the PS5 Slim, also discounted via a ‘used’ option? Sony dubs its improvements the “big three”.

There’s a larger GPU which offers more power than the base PS5 or Slim variant, as well as Advanced Ray-Tracing, which offers much speedier ray tracing.

Finally, PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) uses AI to upscale to higher resolutions with more detail.

The end result is that games can play at higher frame rates and resolutions, making fewer tradeoffs between the two.
