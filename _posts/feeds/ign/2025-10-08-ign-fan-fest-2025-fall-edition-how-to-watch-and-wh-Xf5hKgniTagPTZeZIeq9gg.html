With the leaves changing and spooky season among us, that means IGN Fan Fest 2025: Fall Edition is almost here!

The show will stream live on Wednesday, October 15 at 9am PT, and there will be dozens of exclusive game trailers, interviews with celebrities, and exclusive clips and reveals across games, movies, TV, and more. For all the details on how to tune into the event live, check out the info below:
