I expect the AirPods Pro 3 will see some sort of discount this Black Friday. That's simply an educated guess, through years of working these shopping events, and covering Apple deals day to day. Now what the actual discount will be, that's anybodies guess (perhaps $20-$30 off, who knows?).

For now, a $10 discount at launch is still likely good enough for most Apple users, especially if you want to pay yourself in time and avoid the stress of deal hunting in November.

But, at the same time, major retailers will almost certainly push the AirPods Pro 2 down to their lowest price yet. That’s good news if you’re a savvy, patient shopper, because the AirPods Pro 2 are still excellent earbuds. For reference, they dropped to $149 during Amazon’s last Prime Day, and Costco is running a similar deal this week as well.

With October Prime Day coming up on October 7-8, I wouldn’t be surprised to see a repeat of that deal again at Amazon. Plus, if we’re lucky, the price could dip even further, landing somewhere between $109 and $139 in a couple of weeks, or even on Black Friday itself. Let's wait and see.
