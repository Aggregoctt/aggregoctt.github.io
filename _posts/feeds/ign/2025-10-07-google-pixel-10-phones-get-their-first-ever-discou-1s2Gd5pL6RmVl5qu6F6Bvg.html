The latest Pixel phones have barely been available for a month now, so it's pretty surprising that Amazon is already offering discounts. The final phone in the lineup, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, doesn't even come out until Thursday this week. With the current discounts, the prices on both the standard Pixel 10 and Pixel 10 Pro are definitely worth considering for your next phone upgrade.

Pixel phones have always been on the more affordable side, so to see these discounts on the latest generation isn't too surprising. Especially when the Pixel 10 line didn't come with enough upgrades to make the phones much better than the Pixel 9.

Should you wait for a better deal?

Amazon has some surprisingly good deals on Android phones right now, but that doesn't mean there won't be better deals during Black Friday season this year. Considering we are still so close to the release date of these phones, I would expect that we'll probably see even lower prices next month when all of the phones go on sale. That's just a guess, but it's pretty in line with what I've seen happen with Google Pixel phone prices over the last few years.

With that in mind, these discounts still make it a good time to upgrade if you need a phone right now. $799 for the Pro model is a great price and is how much it would cost to buy a new standard iPhone 17 on its own.
