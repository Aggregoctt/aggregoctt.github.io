After cutting cable, one of the first things you'll likely be searching for is a way to watch live sports. For NBA fans, there are plenty of options to catch games without a traditional cable plan, though you may need to get creative. NBA streaming rights are always changing hands, but currently, there are three main channels you'll need to access: ABC, ESPN, and TNT. However, starting this year through the 2035-36 season, the landscape will change once again, as NBC and Prime Video will exclusively air select games.

In the meantime, we've rounded up the best streaming services for you below to ensure you don't miss a single NBA game this season and the next. With the official NBA season kicking off today, you'll likely want to catch a few games at home as soon as possible.
