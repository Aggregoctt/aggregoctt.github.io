What’s New at Walt Disney World

As always, there are new seasonal things to see and do across Walt Disney World’s parks this fall and into the holiday season. The first is the EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival, which is available now and will run until November 22. It’s a culinary journey that includes 30 global marketplaces with food and drinks options from six continents.

There are returning favorites like The Fry Basket, Flavors From Fire, Canada, India, and Spain, and new selections like Gyozas of the Galaxy. And if you have dietary restrictions, no worries. There are plant-based and allergy-friendly options, so everyone can find something to enjoy.

There are also new seasonal fall decorations. Magic Kingdom in particular got a new look, with fall decor throughout Main Street, U.S.A, including Mickey Mouse–shaped pumpkins and scarecrows.

If you’re not ready for spooky season to end, you can enjoy attractions like The Haunted Mansion, The Twilight Zone Tower of Terror, and Pirates of the Caribbean. Speaking of which, there’s a new Pirates of the Caribbean–themed tavern called The Beak and Barrel.

That’s not the only new attraction. There’s also a nighttime parade called Disney Starlight: Dream the Night Away on non-party nights during the fall. And Zootopia: Better Zoogether!, a 4D show that opens November 7.

And now that the holiday season is officially here, Christmas events start ramping up very soon. Mickey’s Very Merry Christmas Party begins on November 7 and runs on select nights until December 21. It includes a Christmas parade, fireworks show, stage show, and Disney characters wearing festive costumes.

Disney Jollywood Nights begin on November 8 and run until December 22. It’s a five-hour event at Disney’s Hollywood Studios that includes a holiday show with music and dancing, a Nightmare Before Christmas sing-along, and special event merch. These Christmas events require separate tickets, so make sure to visit this site to see which dates are available.
