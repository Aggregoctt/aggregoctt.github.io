Doomsday Diner is a new post-apocalyptic first-person shooter in which you also manage a diner. Check it out and you might find that, like peanut butter and chocolate, it's two unique tastes that taste great together. It's in development for PC.

Featuring a Borderlands-ish art style and a tone that clearly doesn't take itself too seriously, Doomsday Diner is from developer Space Rock Games and will have you trying to upgrade your guns and your diner and keep it in business while raiders walk through the doors and try to put you out of business – literally and figuratively. Take a look at the announcement trailer above and the first gameplay screenshots in the gallery below.

Your guests will want hot dogs, coffee, and plenty of much stranger orders than that, so you'll need to keep one arm free to serve food while the other hangs onto your gun, ready to serve up some hot lead if needed.

Wishlist Doomsday Diner on Steam if you're interested.
