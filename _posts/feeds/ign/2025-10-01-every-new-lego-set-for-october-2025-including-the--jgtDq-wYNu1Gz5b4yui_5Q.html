October is here, bringing with it the spookiest of holidays, as well as some spooky LEGO sets. But also some Christmas sets. And some Star Wars sets. There aren’t a ton of new LEGO releases this month, but there are some remarkable ones, including a retro gaming handheld, a pricy space weapon, and more. Here’s every LEGO set coming in October 2025.

If you're not into the act of blurb reading, you can find all the new sets in the catalog above.

A slew of holiday-themed LEGO sets is out this month, including a gingerbread house, a family Christmas tree, an up-scaled Santa minifigure, and a pair of snowman ornaments. But the coolest one in my book is probably the Holiday Express Train, which is big enough to put around the base of your Christmas tree. It also comes with a tiny train that's the first-ever 3D printed LEGO piece.
