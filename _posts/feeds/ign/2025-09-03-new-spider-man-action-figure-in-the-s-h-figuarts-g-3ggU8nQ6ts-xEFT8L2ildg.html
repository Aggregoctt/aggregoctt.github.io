This Spidey action figure stands about six inches tall and comes with a variety of extras so you can customize him however you like when he claims a place among your collectibles. These additional pieces include four pairs of interchangeable hands, an interchangeable head piece, a spider web to pose him swinging, his Web Shield, effect parts (like one that shows off his Spider-Sense), and a Special Move effect card that showcases his Ultimate Web Throw from the Marvel vs. Capcom game, which you can see below.

Again, it's worth it to get a preorder in now as figures like these can sell out quite fast. And if you're hoping to pick up Cyclops as well for your collection, the good news is he's also still available right now. His release date also pushes into next year, on January 30, but you at least won't have to wait as long to have him in your hands.

Spidey and Cyclops are not the only Marvel figures worth a look right now, though. Hasbro has a few Marvel figures of its own that are set to be released this fall, including figures of Juggernaut and Deadpool. If you're curious to see what else they have planned in the world of Marvel, it's worth having a look at the figures they revealed at SDCC this year, too.
