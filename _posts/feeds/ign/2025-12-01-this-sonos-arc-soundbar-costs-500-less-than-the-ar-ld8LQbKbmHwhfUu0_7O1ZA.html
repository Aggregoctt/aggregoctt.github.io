Sonos doesn't discount its popular speakers very often, so it's a good idea to jump on a decent sale when you see one. The Sonos Arc was discontinued last year to make way for its successor, the Arc Ultra. It's no longer available new straight from Sonos, but you can pick up a certified refurbished model for just $399 during Cyber Monday. That's nearly $500 less than its original retail price of $900, and a better deal than the Arc Ultra, currently on sale for $879.

Sonos refurbished products go through rigorous testing, come with all accessories, manuals, and documentation, and are reshipped in "pristine" packaging. Most importantly, Sonos includes the same 1-year warranty as buying new. I like when retailers offer the same warranty as new because that means they have faith in the reliability of their refurbs.
