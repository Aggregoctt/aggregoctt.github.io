Recent years have seen gamers shift more and more to buying video games digitally. According to Newzoo via GamesIndustry.biz, more than 95% of game revenue was digital in 2024. This has given rise to websites like G2A.COM, which operates as a third-party marketplace with a diversity of digital entertainment offerings below MSRP and has shared details about how they offer safety and security to users. They’re also currently offering discount codes for 12% off a range of listings to encourage you to try it out for yourself, and you can find those codes on this IGN Rewards page.

If this is your first time hearing of G2A.COM, it’s a purely digital marketplace where sellers can list game keys, DLCs, in-game items, gift cards, subscriptions, or software. There are listings for lots of different platforms, including Steam, GOG, Epic Games Store, PlayStation Network, Xbox Live, and Nintendo eShop. The site has grown exponentially since being founded in 2010, with more than 35 million people across 180 countries purchasing more than 135 million items.

G2A.COM is not an online store and does not sell any digital items itself, it’s an intermediary platform between buyers and sellers. It only works with verified sellers and uses an anti–money laundering (AML) policy as part of that process. Sellers and their listings are thoroughly reviewed, and transactions are monitored with systems designed to detect and prevent fraud.

These fraud prevention systems are continuously updated as new technology is introduced with the goal of ensuring smooth and seamless transactions. That resulted in G2A.COM being awarded the American CNP Award for Best Merchant Team of the Year in Anti-fraud and Cybersecurity, alongside companies like Microsoft and Barclays Bank.

Of course, that also means your payment methods need to be protected and secured. According to PayPal, 77% of consumers would abandon their shopping carts if their preferred payment method wasn’t an option. So G2A.COM offers a wide range of payment options, both local and global methods, so you can choose what you’re most comfortable with. And G2A.COM is in compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), the framework for any company that stores, possesses, or transmits cardholder data. Once you’ve bought something, delivery is nearly instantaneous. You provide an email address during checkout, your key is sent to that address, and then you just need to redeem it on whatever platform you bought it for.

But before you get to the point of payment, you have to find something you want to buy. Listings are grouped together by item, so you first click on what you’re interested in, then see all the sellers you can currently buy it from. For each seller, you can see how many sales they’ve previously made and the level of positive feedback they’ve received, which can help you be confident they’re legit.

There are listings for thousands of titles, which you can search for directly or filter by platform or genre. That includes new releases like Borderlands 4 and Silent Hill f. With so many available titles, you might be wondering where all those game keys come from.

Some are from game developers and publishers, primarily indies, who upload keys directly to the marketplace through the G2A.COM Direct program so they can control listings themselves. Others are from wholesalers and distributors whose businesses involve buying keys in bulk for reduced prices, then reselling them at a slight markup while keeping them below the normal MSRP for one key. It’s why you can find things cheaper than you can elsewhere, and you can read more about this on the site’s “how we operate” page.

Prices are already low, but there are ways to save even more with the loyalty program G2A Plus. The free version enables users to collect Plus Points from purchases, which can be used to reduce a future order price. G2A Plus Premium has that same benefit, while also offering lower prices, regular bonuses and rewards, and post-buy premium assistance.
