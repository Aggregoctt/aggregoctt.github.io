Great news! Amazon’s three-for-two deal on vinyl is back for Amazon’s Big Deal Days, and a Prime membership isn’t required to enjoy savings. A similar sale ran during Prime Day back in July, and I’m happy to report Amazon has brought it back for what is essentially October Prime Day. That means if you buy two select vinyl records, you can get a third one completely free. And there are a ton of albums to choose from. Chances are good you’ll find at least three (but probably more) to add to your collection.

It’s not only vinyl that’s part of this deal; it’s three-for-two on all physical media. Whether you’re looking to grab books, DVDs, 4K Blu-rays, CDs, and more, there’s a seemingly endless selection to choose from. Best of all, you can mix and match between different media. So if you want to buy two vinyls and a book, you still get the third item free. However, the lowest-priced item is what’s discounted, meaning it’s best to find three similarly priced products.

Buy Two, Get 1 Free Vinyl

I’ve scrolled through the pages of vinyl that are a part of the sale and curated a list of my personal favorites. And let me tell you, it was extremely difficult to narrow down my selection. There really are that many great, iconic vinyl records that are a part of this deal. Of course, everyone has their own preferences, so be sure to venture beyond my picks and check out the full vinyl sale.

Along with a biblical reunion tour, Oasis celebrated the 30th anniversary of their most commercially successful album this year. While the new special edition of the album that just came out isn’t a part of the deal, (What’s the Story) Morning Glory? Remastered is. With it comes the remastered versions of the original tapes by Ian Cooper in London's Metropolis Studios, along with bonus content that includes demos and B-sides for digital download. If you’ve been itching to hear classics like Don’t Look Back in Anger, Wonderwall, and Champagne Supernova in analog, jump on this deal.

I could talk about Oasis all day, but there are plenty of other albums that are part of this sale, including the no-skips The Stranger from Billy Joel. This 30th Anniversary Legacy Edition features an 180-gram vinyl, delivering a wide soundstage that brings hits like Vienna and She’s Always Woman to life. The classic Beatles’ Abbey Road, an album that should be a part of everyone’s collection, is also included in the deal. If you want to take things back even further, the best-selling jazz album of all time (and one of the greatest), Kind of Blue by Miles Davis, which inspired Joni Mitchell, Pink Floyd, and countless others, is discounted.

For something more modern, Noah Kahan’s two-vinyl Stick Season and Gracie Abrams' Secret of Us Deluxe with transparent records can be chosen in this buy two, get one free sale. Mac Miller might be gone far too soon, but his NPR Tiny Desk Concert was one of the best of all time, and it lives on in an awesome Blue colored Vinyl. Or maybe you want to get in formation and grab Beyoncé’s iconic Lemonade?

Now, that’s just scratching the surface of the records available. This Amazon sale has some truly epic vinyls to choose from, and each one delivers that classic analog experience that digital just can’t replicate. Nothing is better than throwing a vinyl on a great record player, lowering the needle, and hearing that rich, detailed audio.
