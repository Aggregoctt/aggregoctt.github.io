Superman Comic Deals

If you're popping out to see Superman this weekend and fancy reading what director James Gunn considers to be his main inspiration for the film, then it's time to pick up All-Star Superman. It's down to $9.29 in the sale, or $27.90 for the Deluxe Edition.

You can pick up a couple more comics along with it and save a decent chunk of change. For example, you can grab another big inspiration for an upcoming DCU film, Supergirl, in Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow, which is also included in the "3 for 2" sale. That's alongside Kingdom Come, Superman: Birthright, Superman: For All Seasons, Batman: The Long Halloween, and plenty more as well.

It's not the only thing in the sale right now, either. Plenty of Marvel comics also available. Ultimate Spider-Man Vol. 2: The Paper (collecting issues #7–12) is available at $17.01, and it’s eligible for Amazon’s 3-for-2 Prime Day sale, meaning you can mix and match it with other comics, DVDs, books, and more.

That means if you pick up three eligible items across books, comics, or even vinyl, the cheapest one is free. It’s a perfect excuse to also grab Ultimate Invasion ($19.39) or Ultimate X-Men Vol. 1 ($17.01)—both of which are included in the same deal.
