There are few better ways to spend a long weekend than a movie marathon. If you have a lot of time on your hands over Labor Day weekend or are looking to plan a fun and relaxing group activity to share with friends, you can never go wrong with watching hours upon hours of films in a row.

Though there are plenty of different types of movie marathons you can watch, some of the best ones are done one franchise at a time. To help you plan your next movie marathon, we've gathered together 17 of the best franchises to binge below as well as total runtimes for all of the movies combined.

The Conjuring universe is surprisingly large, and if you’re just entering its orbit for the first time, it can be a pretty confusing timeline. There are a total of nine movies in the franchise so far, including The Conjuring, Annabelle, and The Nun films. The Conjuring: Last Rites is set to release next week, on September 5, 2025, making now a good time to do some homework.

If you include all nine movies and the five shorts in this horror movie marathon, you can potentially watch all of the Conjuring movies in just under 14 and a half hours. You can realistically catch up on the whole story, including The Nun 2, in just a weekend.

There are surprisingly few really good dinosaur movies out there, so if you’re in the mood for a dinosaur movie marathon, the Jurassic Park films are your best option. There are a total of six feature-length movies in the franchise, as well as a couple of short films and an animated Netflix series. The latest movie in the franchise, Jurassic World Rebirth, is now available to watch at home as well. The two existing trilogies are split into the Jurassic Park movies and the Jurassic World movies, but they all exist on the same timeline.

If you’re planning on watching all of the movie at once, the combined runtimes are just a little over 12 hours if you count Rebirth. You can see our guide on the Jurassic Park movies in order for more info and where to watch the films.

The Mission: Impossible franchise first began a TV series back in 1996, but most folks likely know the name thanks to the Tom Cruise versions that came to the big screen. There have been a total of eight movies in the franchise so far with the latest film, The Final Reckoning, releasing early in 2025. If you're looking for action, outrageous stunts, and espionage, this is a great franchise to binge all at once.

If you plan on watching all of the Mission: Impossible movies in order, you're looking at about 18 hours of total runtime. That includes The Final Reckoning, which is now available to buy digitally. You can stream the rest of these movies with a Paramount+ subscription.

Before Marvel movies became the MCU, the X-Men franchise reigned supreme. Now counting Deadpool & Wolverine amongst its ranks, there are 13 movies currently in the timeline. The chronological order of the franchise gets a bit wonky when you start factoring in time jumps and the Deadpool movies, but you can also just follow the Wolverine timeline for a shorter binge.

If you remove the Deadpool movies from the list, you can watch all of the X-Men movies in just over 22 hours. Though you’ll likely want to include Deadpool, because it’s Deadpool.

With the arrival of Fast X back in 2023, there are now officially 13 Fast and Furious movies. Nobody could have guessed that something that started as a series of racing movies would become a neverending franchise that goes to space, but here we are. If you’re looking for an action-fueled movie marathon that is all about family, the Fast and Furious films should be right up your alley.

Watching all of the films together takes almost 23 and half hours. Take a look at our guide to the Fast and furious movies in order for more info. The next film in the franchise, titled Fast X: Part 2, won't arrive until 2027 and is said to feature the return of Paul Walker's character somehow.

One of the most classic movie marathons is the original LotR trilogy. The epic fantasy story, based on J.R.R Tolkien’s Lord of the Rings books, has become a timeless classic. You can also include all three of the Hobbit movies, Amazon’s Rings of Power TV series, and the War of Rohirrim if you want to binge the whole franchise. But we recommend just sticking to the two trilogies.

All six of the feature-length films take about 19 and a half hours to watch when you combine runtimes. Add in two full seasons of Rings of Power episodes for another nearly 18 hours of content. Take a look at our guide to the Lord of the Rings in order for more info or see our guide to Lord of the Rings Blu-ray sets.

Star Wars is another one of those franchises that has no end in sight. With The Acolyte ending this summer and a huge amount of confirmed upcoming Star Wars projects, the series has been growing exponentially. There have been a total of 11 Star Wars movies released so far with more on the way. And if you factor in animated movies, episodes of The Clone Wars, The Mandalorian, and more, the timeline gets even longer.

If you want to do a movie marathon with just the live-action films, you’re looking at a total runtime of just over 25 hours. See our guide to all Star Wars movies and shows in order for more information about the timeline or dive into the Star Wars Legends books for extra lore.

Out of all of the pirate movies out there, the Pirates of the Caribbean movies are the only ones to become a true franchise. There are a total of five feature-length films in the series, all of which feature Johnny Depp’s roguish Jack Sparrow. We are still waiting on a release date for Pirates of the Caribbean 6, but in the meantime, you can still marathon all of the movies in one day (and check out the new LEGO set based on the series).

Watching all five of the Pirates of the Caribbean movies in a row will take just a little over 12 hours.

With Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes out just last year, there's never been a better time for a classic sci-fi movie binge. The rest of Planet of the Apes movies – now totaling 10 films in the franchise – are absolutely worth your time. Although the newest films are not connected to the original series, and there are technically three Planet of the Apes timelines, it’s still a fun movie marathon filled with our ape overlords.

While we don’t have the exact time it would take you to get through all of the Planet of the Apes movies, the sheer volume of films would realistically put you within a comfortable two-day binge zone. You can also just stream the new prequel films and finish in a single day.

Indiana Jones is a movie franchise that spans over four decades, but only has five total movies in the series. With the latest film Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny out back in 2023 and the new Indiana Jones game, it’s a great time to dive back in. If you’re planning on doing a Harrison Ford movie marathon, Indiana Jones is an excellent starting point as well.

You can potentially watch all of the Indiana Jones movies in one day, with a total combined runtime of just over 10 hours for all five films.

With Transformers One now available to stream, it’s once again a good time to dive into a Transformers movie marathon. There are now a total of eight feature-length films in the franchise, and all of them offer the action, explosions, and humor you can expect from Michael Bay. Transformer: Rise of the Beasts actually takes place earlier on in the timeline, so you watch it first and then move on to the original film shortly after.

If you want to watch all of the Transformers movies from start to finish, it will take you about 18 and half hours to do so. This one is perfect for filling two days of a weekend with some sweet Transformers action.

Although the Fantastic Beast series went a bit off the rails at the end, the Harry Potter movies in general are still one of the best movie marathons you can have. Filled with magic, wonder, and Weasleys, the Harry Potter universe is solidified as a classic in many adults who grew up reading the books. With the upcoming Harry Potter TV series in the works, you can still binge the entire franchise before a new version changes how you see it.

If you want to exclude the Fantastic Beasts movies from your streaming marathon, you can get through all eight of the Harry Potter movies in just under 20 hours. Adding in the prequel films will add another 6 hours and 49 minutes to that total.

With the addition of Alien: Romulus, the Alien franchise now stands at nine movies total. There are six in the mainline series of films, two Predator crossovers, and two prequels. With the addition of the Alien: Earth TV series airing on Hulu, this is the perfect sci-fi franchise to marathon.

If you're looking specifically for a space-horror movie marathon, the Alien films are a great place to start. It will take you just a little over 17 hours to watch every Alien movie in order, and about 14 hours if you remove the Predator crossovers.

The Scream franchise is currently enjoying a surge in popularity after Scream 5 in 2022, followed by Scream 6 in 2023, and Scream 7 coming in 2026. If you're looking for a horror movie marathon to watch in October that doesn't take itself too seriously, watching all the Scream movies in order is a great way to do so.

If you want to watch all six of the Ghostface movies in marathon form, it will take you a little over 11 and half hours to do so. Here's where you can watch all of the Scream movies online.

James Bond is one of the longest-running movie franchises in existence. Because of this, there are a total of 26 movies played by seven different James Bond actors. Allthough you can certainly try to watch every James Bond movie in order of release, it would be easier to pick a single Bond and watch all of the movies from that era. For the sake of this article, we'd suggest just marathoning the Daniel Craig Bond movies to start with.

If you did want to watch all 26 Bond films in a row, it would take you over 54 hours to do so. If you decide on a Daniel Craig Bond marathon, then you're looking at a more reasonable 7 hours and 40 minutes.

Now that The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes is available to stream and a new Hunger Games book is already being adapted into film, it's a great time to go back and watch every Hunger Games movie in order. The most recent film is a prequel, so once you've watched it, you can move onto the next films in the franchise without jumping around the timeline.

If you're hoping to watch all five Hunger Games films in one weekend, you're looking at just over 11 and a half hours. The new prequel is the longest film in the series, coming in at 2 hours and 38 minutes alone. We don't yet have a runtime for the upcoming Sunrise on the Reaping movie.

If you're in the mood for something a bit more lighthearted, the Shrek franchise is just what you need. There are a total of six Shrek movies available, which includes two Puss in Boots spin-offs. You could choose to only watch the four films starring the ogre himself or watch all of the films. I'd recommend the latter because The Last Wish is absolutely delightful. There's also a Shrek 5 in the works, which is supposed to come out in 2026.
