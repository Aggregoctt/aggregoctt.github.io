We are well into the first day of October Prime Day and, to be honest, there aren't as many TV deals as I would have liked to see. Fortunately, the deals that are available are pretty good. For example, this is the first time a brand new, current-year 77" Samsung OLED TV has dropped to $1,299 from a reputable vendor. Most the the deals are from Amazon, however there are some deals from other retailers that are offering counter Prime Day sales that Amazon hasn't price matched. Despite the fact that these are all "high end" OLED TVs, there are a good number of options here from budget to best.

Samsung S90F 4K QD OLED Smart TV (2025 Model)

The S84F is the most affordable TV in Samsung's 2025 OLED lineup. It uses a W-OLED panel, which is a small step down from the quantum dot OLED panels you'll find on higher end Samsung models like the S90. The primary difference between those two panel types is brightness: the QD OLED is brighter so it works better in bright rooms. Despite it's low price, the Samsung S84F still has proper gaming features like a 120Hz native panel with HDMI 2.1 ports, VRR, and ALLM.
