The 37th Annual GLAAD Media Awards nominations have been announced, with popular shows, movies, games, comics, and more among the categories, totaling almost 300 nominees.

Stranger Things, The Last of Us, Severance, Sandman, and Doctor Who are some of the nominees for Outstanding Drama Series, while Pluribus, Heated Rivalry, I Love LA, and The Hunting Wives are up for Outstanding New TV Series. Outstanding Comedy Series includes Abbott Elementary, Hacks, The Righteous Gemstones, and Hazbin Hotel.

Blue Moon, Christy, I Know What You Did Last Summer, and Downton Abbey: The Grand Finale are a handful of nominees for Outstanding Film – Wide Theatrical Release.

Hades II, Assassin's Creed Shadows, Ambrosia Sky: Act One, and Old Skies are among the nominees for Outstanding Video Game.
