Razer sells the Kraken Kitty V2 Gengar Edition for $139.99 — which is already $40 more than the standard colorways of the headset — but prices on eBay have already pushed well beyond that as sellers seem to be noticing the increasing interest.

Prices on eBay for the Kraken Kitty V2 Gengar Edition are currently ranging from around $165 - $190, with some listings even occasionally reaching over the $200 mark. So, if you're in the market for this collab, you'll have to decide whether you want to pay a noticeable premium or wait in hopes that Razer will restock soon.

In the meantime, Razer has plenty of other Pokémon-themed products, as well as a variety of collabs featuring other popular properties like Hello Kitty and Minecraft. And if you don't mind skipping out on the purple colorway, you can actually score some of the company's wireless headsets, such as the Barracuda X, for less than the cost of this special Gengar version of the wired Kraken Kitty V2. We loved the Barracuda X in our review, so it's an easy one to recommend in the same price range.

But hey, no one's going to blame you if you hold out for the cuter one. It's Gengar. We get it.
