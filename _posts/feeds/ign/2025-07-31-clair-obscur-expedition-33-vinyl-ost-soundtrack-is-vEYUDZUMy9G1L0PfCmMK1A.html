After being an instant Game of the Year contender following its May 2025 launch, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 felt like the quickest title ever to have its soundtrack become a “please release it on vinyl and take money” type request.

After all, as we mentioned in our IGN review of Expedition 33, it is quite the “evocative soundtrack”.

Across multiple retailers, including the IGN Store, the Clair Obscur vinyl is finally a reality that’s able to preorder right now in several varieties, including a CD version as well.

Specifically, the vinyl soundtrack is available in two versions: the 6LP (LP meaning ‘Long Play’ for vinyl newcomers) with 63 specially selected tracks, sequenced by Clair Obscur’s genius composer Lorien Testard, and the 2LP/Double LP versions with 20 tracks.

Each iteration of the vinyl comes with its own tracklist in the product description for you to check, but each will at least come with the game’s most iconic tracks like Une vie à t'aimer, In Lumière's Name, and Lost Voice.

Pricing is where things can get a little bit tricky, since the cost of each vinyl and CD SKU for the Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 soundtrack varies depending on the retailer and exclusive editions. No matter which one you buy, though, all have the same expected estimated release date of February 2026.
