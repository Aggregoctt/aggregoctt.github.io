In Grow a Garden's Beanstalk Event, you help grow beanstalks by bringing Jack the specific plant type he asks for. Once your lobby has contributed 900 points, the beanstalk will grow and you're able to climb it to collect rewards, such as Sprout Seed Packs and Sprout Crates, and meet the giant at the top.

IGN has put together a list of guides to help you figure out the plant types in Grow a Garden, so you know exactly what to give Jack to grow the beanstalk.

Once you've got some plants in your inventory, interact with Jack in the center of the lobby and select "Feed all my plants to the beanstalk" to give them to him. If you'd rather submit one plant, click on it in the hotbar so that you're holding it and select "Feed this to the beanstalk." Once the beanstalk reaches 900 points, it will grow.
