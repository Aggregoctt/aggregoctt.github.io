This version of the LEGO Imperial Star Destroyer is the perfect mix of playset for the kids and a display model for adults. It's comprised of 1,555 pieces, so it could take a few sessions to finish. Throwing on your favorite Star Wars movie while you lock in and sit down to build this sounds like a great night in to me. You can open it up to see a mini-scale version of the ship's bridge where Darth Vader looms over Imperial troops and gives orders. The detail on the inside is pretty intricate for such a small space, and helps capture the aesthetic of its on-screen counterpart well.

The set stands over 6in. tall, is 18in. long, and 11in. deep. It isn't the largest LEGO iteration of the ship we've seen, but it's still a great option if you're limited on shelf space. Seven minifigures come with it, including Darth Vader, Commander Praji, an Imperial Gunner, an Imperial Navy Trooper, and a special 25th Anniversary edition of Cal Kestis from the Star Wars Jedi games. He comes with his own display stand, while the ship itself unfortunately does not.
