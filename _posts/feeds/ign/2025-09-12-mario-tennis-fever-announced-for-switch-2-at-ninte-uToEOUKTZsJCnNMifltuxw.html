Nintendo has announced Mario Tennis Fever for Switch 2, due out in 2026.

This latest version of Nintendo's tennis series lets you slide and use new defensive maneuvers. New equipment includes Fever Rackets. You can keep your rally going to fill up your gauge then unleash a Fever Shot, which changes depending on your racket. There are 30 rackets in the game, each with their own abilities, and there are 38 playable characters — the most in series history.

As shown off during the Nintendo Direct, there's even an Adventure mode where you play as the babies. The debut trailer is below:

Mario Tennis Fever launches on Switch 2 on February 12, 2026. For more, check out everything announced at the Nintendo Direct September 2025.
