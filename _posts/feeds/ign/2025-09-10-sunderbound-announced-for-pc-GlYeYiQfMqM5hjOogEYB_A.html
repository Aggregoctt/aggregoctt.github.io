Warm up your old-school top-down twin-stick shooter skills with SunderBound, a two-player co-op shooter set in a crumbling mega-tower. It's in development at Kipi Interactive for PC.

As its developers describe it, SunderBound is "a co-op top-down shooter with roguelite progression, set in a crumbling mega-tower. Survive floor by floor with a partner you trust, or an AI companion if you’re climbing solo. Every room’s a test. Every upgrade’s earned. And the only way out is up." Sounds pretty old-school bullet-hell to us, in a very good way. Watch the announcement trailer above and the first gameplay screenshots in the gallery below.
