The 2025 Steam Autumn Sale is now live, running now through October 6 at 10 AM PT, and it is packed with excellent discounts.

Big-name PC hits from this year sit alongside older favorites, while recent console-to-PC ports from publishers like PlayStation have also joined the lineup.

To save you the infinite scroll, we’ve pulled together the standout deals, plus a few direct links to trusted Steam key retailers like Best Buy, Fanatical, and Green Man Gaming, where you might score an even better price.

Let's start off with one of my favorite games of the year: Doom: The Dark Ages. The latest entry is an absolute delight and down to $46.89 at Steam, and $39.89 at Fanatical, an absolute tremendous drop from its $69.99 MSRP.

TDA feels like Doom going back to its roots with a gameplay style that heavily leans into its classic Doom straithing gameplay, with the added edge of its moment to moment shield counters giving it that extra bit of intensity. I love this game, and can't wait for whatever iD is cooking up for its DLC.

Other Xbox Game Studios titles like The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered and Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, are also included in the Autumn sale, and now is the best time to jump in and check out the excellent The Order of Giants DLC.

Looking at the other side of the dugouts, one of the other best deals in the sale is on a PlayStation console exclusive, but now on PC: Stellar Blade. It's down to just $47.99 at Steam, or $42.59 at Fanatical. That's a huge deal on one of the best games from last year. Not only that, but it's also Steam Deck Verified!

Speaking of Steam Deck Verified, there's more than enough indie darlings to tuck into during the sale as well. That includes Hades, R.E.P.O., Blue Prince and more. Hades is down to just $6.24, and well worth playing if you haven't checked it out yet, especially with the equally as excellent Hades 2 that's recently released into 1.0 (we also scored it a 10/10).

There's plenty of other PlayStation hits on sale right now as well, including Spider-Man 2 and Spider-Man Remastered, God of War and its sequel Raganarok, Helldivers 2, and plenty more (RIP your Steam wallet).

But honestly, that's just the tip of the ice berg, be sure to dig into the sale and rip open your wishlist to see what's been discounted (because it's a lot!). And, final reminder, remember to cross check those deals to ensure you're getting the best price possible - save every penny you can!
