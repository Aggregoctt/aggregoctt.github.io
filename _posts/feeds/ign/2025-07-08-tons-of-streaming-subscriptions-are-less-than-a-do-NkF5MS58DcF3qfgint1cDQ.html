Prime Day is one of the best times to save on just about anything, but one thing I know I spend way too much on is streaming subscriptions. I typically expect the best streaming deals to drop around Cyber Monday, but Amazon has managed to pull through with solid discounts on a spread of Prime Video's Channel Add-Ons. These discounts are available exclusively for Prime members and are only live until July 11.

Tons of Streaming Add-Ons Are Discounted for Prime Members

These Add-Ons feature the full library of what you’d get on the streaming service’s own site or app but accessed through Prime Video. If you’re like me, it’s actually nice to have all your subscriptions in one spot. You get to see everything you’re paying to watch without bouncing around websites.

Most of these Prime Day deals offer pretty much the same thing: your selected streaming subscription for $0.99/month for two months. The one exception is Apple TV+, which is 50% off, down to $4.99 from $9.99, but with the same two-month limit. All of the deals are live until July 11, the final day of Amazon's sale event.

I’m personally going to pick up Crunchyroll to catch the summer anime season, specifically Dan Da Dan Season 2 and Gachiakuta. I’d also highly recommend checking out Britbox. It’s where you’ll find classic British comedy shows like Would I Lie to You as well as The IT Crowd since it was removed from Netflix.
