The 12 Across the Spider-Verse minifigures means you have a chance to get a wide variety of different Spideys, including:

  • Miles Morales (Spider-Man)
  • Miguel O’Hara (Spider-Man 2099)
  • Gwen Stacy (Spider Gwen)
  • Hobie Brown (Spider-Punk)
  • Pavitr Prabhakar (Spider-Man India)
  • Miles G. Morales (Prowler)
  • Peter B. Parker (Spider-Man) and May “Mayday” Parker
  • Margo Kess (Spider-Byte)
  • Peter Parker (Werewolf Spider-Man)
  • Patrick O’Hara (Web-Slinger)
  • Petra Parker (Cyborg Spider-Woman)
  • Charlotte Webber (Sun-Spider)

Each character also features a collector's leaflet and at least one accessory, which is a fun little bonus.

It's worth keeping in mind that there's always a possibility of getting doubles with a blind box like this, but if you don't mind, it's a fun way to pick up some new Spidey minifigures.

If you're looking for more Spider-Man items from LEGO outside of these minifigures, you're in luck this month as the Iron Spider-Man Bust recently dropped from the company as well.
