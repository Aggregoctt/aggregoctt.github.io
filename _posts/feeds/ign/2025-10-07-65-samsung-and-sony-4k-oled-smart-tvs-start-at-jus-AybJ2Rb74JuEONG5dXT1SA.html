The Sony Bravia 8 is a 2024 TV that uses a standard W-OLED panel, so image quality is comparable to the Samsung S85D and LG B4 TVs. The Bravia 8 supports HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision video formats and Dolby Atmos and DTS audio formats. This is a good TV to pair with the PS5 console since it has a native 120Hz panel, HDMI 1.4 ports, VRR, and ALLM.

The Bravia 8 uses "Acoustic Surface Audio" technology, which essentially places the speakers right behind the screen, thus removing any unncessary bezel space and giving the impression that the sound is coming directly from the video itself. Finally, the Bravia 8 uses the Google TV smart interface, which I find it to be preferable to Samsung's proprietary Tizen interface. You can pair your TV with your Android phone so that you can use it as a remote.
