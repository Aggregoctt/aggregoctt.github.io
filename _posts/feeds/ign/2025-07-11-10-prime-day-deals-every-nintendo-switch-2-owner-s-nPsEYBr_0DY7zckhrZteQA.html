Amazon’s Prime Day deals are heading the way of the Dodo in the coming hours, and that means you don't have much time left to make your purchases. If you’re a Switch 2 owner, still play your Switch 1, or anything else, then good news - there are a whole host of deals on games, storage options, and so much more. Here are the best ones we’ve found.

Switch Game Prime Day Deals

Amazon has an array of games discounted, including retro collections new releases. The big one is probably the Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 1, which has been reduced by almost 60% and includes iconic entries in the stealth action series that you can take with you on the go.

For under $20, you can get the Yu-Gi-Oh! Early Days Collection, Rune Factory 5, Balatro Special Edition, and Story of Seasons: A Wonderful Life.

If you’re looking for first-party titles, you’ll find those discounted at Best Buy instead, including Super Mario Odyssey, New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe, Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Splatoon 3 and more.

OG Switch Storage

There are a bunch of microSD cards on sale for your Switch 1, including some slick ones with Sonic branding. With the Prime Day savings, you can get 1TB for $69.99 (a saving of 40%), 512GB for $29.99 (30% off) or 256GB for $19.99 (17% off).

Switch 2 Storage

Ironically, though, these Sonic offerings aren’t fast enough for Switch 2. For the new system, you’ll need a microSD Express card, which are harder to find and not discounted.
