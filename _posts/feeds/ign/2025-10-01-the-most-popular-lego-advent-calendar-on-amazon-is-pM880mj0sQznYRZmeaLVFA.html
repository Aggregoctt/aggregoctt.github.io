Although the LEGO Advent calendars were only recently released in September, many of them have actually been discounted for a while now. The 16% discount on the LEGO Minecraft calendar is the lowest we've seen the price go so far, and don't expect it to drop any further than that on Amazon or elsewhere. With Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days sale coming up, I expect that a lot of shoppers will jump to buy this set at this price next week as they are browsing for holiday gifts. And because this is the most popular LEGO set on Amazon overall right now before that sale even starts, it's possible it could go out of stock well before Black Friday this year.

More LEGO Advent Calendars on Sale

If you're looking to grab a different Advent calendar at a discount, the good news is that they are all on sale right now. The second most popular version is the LEGO City Advent Calendar, which is based on LEGO's own line of the same name (rather than attached to a specific IP). It's also a much cheaper option at just $25. After that, the Star Wars calendar and Harry Potter Calendars have the same discount as the Minecraft one. Here's a quick breakdown of how many pieces you'll get per set at those prices:

  • LEGO Star Wars Advent Calendar - 263 Pieces
  • LEGO Disney Frozen Advent Calendar - 231 Pieces
  • LEGO Harry Potter Advent Calendar - 278 Pieces
  • LEGO City Advent Calendar - 186 Pieces
  • LEGO Friends Advent Calendar - 237 Pieces
