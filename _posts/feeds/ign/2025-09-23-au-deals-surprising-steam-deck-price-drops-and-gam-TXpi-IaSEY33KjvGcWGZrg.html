This Day in Gaming 🎂

In retro news, I’m letting my Rapidash unleash Flare Blitz on 21 birthday candles for Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen. Hard to believe two decades have passed since I first slotted this Gen III remake of Red and Blue into my GBA and went on a spree of catching unsuspecting Kanto critters for, let’s be honest, glorified cockfighting. My standout memories come from the Sevii Islands, where Double Battles awaited and legendaries like Lugia and Ho-Oh turned the heat up even further. Glorious times.

Meanwhile, Pikmin 4 deserves more love than it gets. It began as a side project in Shigeru Miyamoto's notebook years ago, and its quirky ecosystem of tiny gardeners never stopped charming me.

Exciting Bargains for Xbox

The Quarry, meanwhile, is basically a playable horror flick from Supermassive, and it is fun watching A-ish-list actors deliver B-movie dialogue that somehow works.
  • Resident Evil 4 (-46%) - A$32.50 Capcom’s legendary survival horror remade. Leon’s Spanish village trip is gorier, tenser, and slicker than ever. A masterclass in pacing, upgrades, and hair physics.
  • Assassin's Creed Valhalla (-56%) - A$43.70 Raid, pillage, and settle as Eivor. Ubisoft’s Viking saga leans into RPG systems, vast open-world exploration, and meaty DLC expansions. Still one of the most content-stuffed Assassin’s Creed entries.
  • Crash Bandicoot 4: It's About Time (-43%) - A$57.20 A faithful yet fresh sequel decades later. Classic platforming chaos returns with new masks, multiple playable characters, and enough challenging jumps to make even veterans sweat.
