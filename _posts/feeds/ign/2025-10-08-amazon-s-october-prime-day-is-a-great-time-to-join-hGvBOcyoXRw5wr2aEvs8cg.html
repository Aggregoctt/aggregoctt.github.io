There’s No Better Time To Sign Up To Audible

This deal gives you three months at a reduced price, after which it’ll renew at $14.95 per month (unless you cancel beforehand).

For those three months, you’ll be able to listen to a huge library of books across all genres, including top reads like Freida McFadden’s The Housemaid, Dan Brown’s The Secret of Secrets, Richard Osman’s The Impossible Fortune: Thursday Murder Club, and Lisa Jewell’s Don’t Let Him In.

You’ll also get a credit each month that you can use to add an audiobook to your library permanently, meaning once your subscription lapses, you’ll still have access to it. This can be used on any book, so at the very least you’re getting three audiobooks of your choice for $3.

You’re also eligible for member-only deals while you’re subscribed, and there are no listening limits so you can keep enjoying as many books as you want until your trial expires (unless you want to pay to continue, of course).

The offer runs until December 16, and you can only redeem it once per account. It also won’t work if you’re an existing Audible subscriber, sadly.
