Amazon's Prime Day 2025 sale doesn't officially begin until tomorrow, but there are already dozens of deals to take advantage of before the sale fully kicks off. You can get all kinds of LEGO sets on sale, and some of the more popular LEGO Star Wars sets are all 20% off right now. All you have to do to qualify for these prices is be a Prime member, and if you're not already all you have to do is sign up for a free trial.

LEGO Star Wars Sets Are on Sale Ahead of Prime Day

Except for the sweet C-3PO set, all of these are under $100. They make great gifts and look great on bookshelves.

LEGO Star Wars Darth Vader Bust

This Darth Vader buildable bust is a pretty faithful recreation of its on-screen counterpart. Coming in at 834 pieces, it's a deceptively beefy build and will probably take around two hours to finish in one sitting. It stands over 8in. high and comes with a sweet display plaque.

LEGO Star Wars C-3PO Buildable Figure

This is one of the most intricately detailed LEGO Star Wars models we've had in awhile. It stands over 15in. tall and is made up of 1,138, making one of the biggest Star Wars sets you can get. The level of detail is impressive, from his discolored leg to his pose, and would look great with the R2-D2 set coming up next.

LEGO Star Wars R2-D2

I've had this on my LEGO want list since it first released, and now might be the perfect time. It's not the first or only to-scale R2-D2 LEGO set we've had, but it's far, far and away the best. It's comprised of 1,050 pieces, is 9in. tall, and comes with a Darth Malak minifigure for some reason.
