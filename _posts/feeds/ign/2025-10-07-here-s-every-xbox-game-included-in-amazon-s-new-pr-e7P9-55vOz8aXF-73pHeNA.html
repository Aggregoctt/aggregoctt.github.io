It’s been a strange year for Xbox, it would be fair to say. Despite some absolutely fantastic releases (and more to come), the recent Xbox Game Pass Ultimate price hike has many considering just, you know, buying their games.

Thankfully, Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days are offering a bunch of discounts, including price drops of over 70%. Here are our favorites so far.

Xbox Prime Day Deals

We have to start with Sid Meier’s Civilization 7, which is seeing a staggering 81% discount, bringing it down to just $13.25. I’m still playing it regularly, and if you’re wondering about how it handles on console, fear not - it plays brilliantly on a gamepad.

One of the best games of 2024, Metaphor: ReFantazio, is down to just $15.96. That’s a 77% discount for the huge RPG, and elsewhere in SEGA’s lineup, you can snap up Sonic x Shadow Generations and Sonic Frontiers for $20.99 and $7.95, respectively.

There’s also a whopping 67% off of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chorbobyl, with the horror shooter down to just $19.96 less than a year from launch, while this year’s Assassin’s Creed: Shadows is just $44.99 after a 36% discount.
