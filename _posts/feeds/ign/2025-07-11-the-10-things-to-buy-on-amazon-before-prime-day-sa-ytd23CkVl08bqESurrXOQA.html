It's the last day of the longest Prime Day ever and some of the best deals are already gone. If you haven't had the chance to do any shopping yet, you haven't missed your chance entirely. Luckily there are still plenty of great discounts around to hop on before the sale ends.

This year has been a pretty tumultuous year for the prices of things, so the deals we've seen during this week have been a welcome change of pace. It's unclear if Black Friday will be better than Prime Day this year due to the rising cost of goods, which means right now is potentially the best time of the year to make some major purchases.

To help direct you to what deals are actually worth your time, I've gathered up a list of all of the things worth buying before the sale ends. Prime Day deals at Amazon will end at midnight PST tonight, so you still a little bit of time left to save before the prices go back up.
