Although these are genuine official Nintendo Switch Joy-Con 2 controllers, they are imported, most likely from Hong Kong, China, or Japan. However, it doesn't really matter because Switch hardware is region unlocked, meaning you can use the Joy-Con 2 in the US with any locally purchased Switch 2 without any problems. Keep in mind that imported hardware does not get a US-based Nintendo warranty, however AliExpress does include a 15-day free returns window.

The original Joy-Con is compatible with the Switch 2, but the newer Joy-Con 2 features several upgrades that are worth saving up for. The biggest difference is that the Joy-Con 2 is physically larger, with bigger buttons and a matte texture for a better grip and improved ergonomics. It also magnetically attaches to the Switch 2 console for a more secure hold. Functionally, the new Joy-Con is updated with "HD Rumble 2" for better tactile feedback, an optical sensor so that you can use the controller like a mouse, and a C button to access the GameChat feature.

If you're looking to add more controllers for local multiplayer action, or if you simply want a backup pair of Joy-Con, then you should probably consider this deal. Recently the retail price of the Joy-Con 2 went up from $95 to $100 as a result of tariffs. Although some retailers still sell it for $95, that will likely change soon. This deal brings the price to below even the retail price at launch, but it won't last forever.

If you don't yet own a Switch 2 and are still looking to buy one, AliExpress has a deal on that too.
