Live-Action Lilo & Stitch 4K Steelbook Bonus Features

Alongside more traditional bonus features like bloopers and deleted scenes, the Lilo & Stitch 4K steelbook comes with some fun extras, including something called 'Scenes with Stitch' where the titular alien talks about his favorite scenes in the movie. You can see the full list of bonus features below:
