If our recent coverage of the Yu-Gi-Oh! 2025 Mega Pack Set (that’s a mouthful) had you interested in checking out the long-running card game for the first time, or returning after a lengthy hiatus, then good news.

Best Buy is offering a host of Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG products, including booster boxes and more at a significant discount right now, with the highlight being 24 packs of Duelist’s Advance selling for $44.99.

The Best Buy Yu-Gi-Oh! Deals Are Great For Collectors

That box of 24 packs is reduced by 50%, meaning the $3.75 packs are now just $1.79. That’s a great way to kickstart your collection, or add a bulk load of new cards to your existing one.

In fact, these packs would have cost $4.49 individually at MSRP, so you’re getting a bulk discount and then some.

Speaking of cheap packs, singular sleeved boosters of Duelist’s Advance are down to $1.99, so you can really fill out the set. Finally (for Duelist’s Advance), the box that includes four packs is just $7.99 now - a drop of 56%.

Other sets are on offer, too. Sleeved boosters for Alliance Insight are also down to $1.99, and you can grab a booster box of Justice Hunters at a 28% discount.

That makes each pack from that particular set $2.71, when they would usually have been $7.50.

There’s a $5 saving (that’s 17%) off of the Quarter Century Stampede box which includes a series of smaller packs that are all foil,

Finally, the discount on the Retro Pack 2 is a little lower (you’re only saving $3) but it got a limited release many, many years ago. It’s yours for $14.99, down from $17.99.
