Xbox is once again hosting a Tokyo Game Show broadcast in 2025, and this time it's happening today, September 25, at 3am PT/6am ET/11am BST/7pm JST. While we don't know exactly what will be featured in the show, Microsoft did share that it will "feature titles from our creative teams, alongside exciting updates from our partners in Japan, across Asia and around the world."

No matter what they announce, we will be right here covering all the biggest moments live, so we encourage you to keep checking back to ensure you don't miss any of the reveals, trailers, gameplay, and more.

007: First Light Casts Eternals' Gemma Chan as Dr. Selina Dan

007: First Light not only was featured during the stream with quick looks at gameplay, but we were also introduced to MI6's Head of Tactical Simulation, Gemma Chan's Dr. Selina Tan. For those unfamiliar, Chan has previously been seen in Eternals and Raya and the Last Dragon.

Gungrave Gore Blood Heat Lets You Dualwield Guns and Use a Massive One As Well

Gunegrave Gore Blood Heat is a remake of 2022's Gungrave Gore and its "newly added systems preserve the legacy while delivering a modern and more explosive feel." These updates will be felt in level design, motion, visuals, player interactions, combat system, and more.

And yes, you'll be playing as Beyond the Grave, who was once known as Brandon Heat, and will have full access to his dual Cerberus pistols and the gigantic Death Hauler that can be used as a weapon and a shield.
