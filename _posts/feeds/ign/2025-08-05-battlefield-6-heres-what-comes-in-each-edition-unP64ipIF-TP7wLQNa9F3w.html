Battlefield 6 is set to release for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC on October 10. It promises more of the large-scale warfare the series is known for, with infantry units fighting on the ground and vehicles covering large distances both on the ground and in the air. There’s also a focus on environmental destruction and four character classes to choose from.
