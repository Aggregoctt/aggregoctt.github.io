In retro news, I'm lighting up 21 candles for a cake baked for Donkey Konga, the GameCube's delightfully odd peripheral thumper. The gist: smack, slap, or clap against a small replica drum to recreate pop hits, classical tunes, and a few cheeky Nintendo tracks. If you owned four of said bongos (like I did, because I go all in), Donkey Konga could take over any living room with its approachable and communal rhythm gaming. It's such a shame this never turned into an absolute sequel 'splosion like Guitar Hero. I would have bought a bunch more of 'em.
