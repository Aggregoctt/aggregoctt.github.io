LEGO has just announced what looks to be the ultimate set for Stranger Things fans, a $300 version of The Creel House stuffed full of Easter eggs from each of the show's five seasons.

The 2,593-piece set is described by LEGO as its first-ever transforming house design, allowing you to build and rebuild the mansion into its varying states, and relive various iconic moments from the series.

Could this include unrevealed secrets from the upcoming Stranger Things finale, set to air on Netflix and in theaters on December 31? Curiously, the set will only be able to pre-order from New Year's Eve at 5pm Pacific time, and become available to actually purchase once the show's grandfather clock has ticked over to New Year's Day 2026.

For now, we know the set will include all manner of props (Max's cassette tape! Henry's mind flayer sketch!), vehicles ( Steve's car! Will's bike! The WSQK radio station van!) and a generous 13 minifigures. Indeed, you'll be able to fill The Creel House with all your favorite characters: Eleven, Will, Mike, Lucas, Dustin, Holly, Steve, Nancy, Robin, Jonathan, Max, Mr. Whatsit and Vecna.

As an early bonus for fans, purchases made until January 7, 2026 will also include a Stranger Things: WSQK Radio Station set as a Gift with Purchase, which will add both Joyce and Hopper minifigures as well, while stocks last.

Two other characters (not included in the set) rather excited about all of this are Stranger Things creators Ross and Matt Duffer, who had the following to say: "We grew up obsessed with the LEGO brand, so seeing Stranger Things rebuilt in bricks is honestly surreal. With the show heading into its final chapter - and hitting the 10-year mark - bringing the Creel House to life this way feels like the perfect celebration of the world and the fans who made all of this possible."

Stranger Things' climactic final season has now begun airing, with its first four episodes available to watch via Netflix (which briefly crashed due to the show's enormous popularity). "It takes a minute, but the Duffers soon find their groove," IGN wrote in its Stranger Things Season 5 Vol. 1 review, "confidently moving their epic campaign towards its conclusion."

If you're already up to date, join us as we recap and theorize over the series' biggest burning questions ahead of its final episodes arriving (and yes, if you're a video game fan, you may not be alone in spotting a fun little error). And finally, as a reminder, Stranger Things' next three episodes will debut via Netflix on December 25, before the feature-length series finale arrives on December 31.
