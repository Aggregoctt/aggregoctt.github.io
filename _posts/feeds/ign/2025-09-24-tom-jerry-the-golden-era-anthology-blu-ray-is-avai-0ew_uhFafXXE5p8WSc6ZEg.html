According to the press release, this set, which is part of the Warner Archive Collection, comes with five discs that feature all 114 shorts remastered in 1080p resolution. There's a sixth disc included as well that features over three hours of bonus features, including two new featurettes: Lady of the House: The Story of Mammy Two Shoes and Animal Hijinks: The Friends and Foes of Tom.

Fans will also get to enjoy 20 audio commentaries, and this collector's edition even comes with a 28-page booklet that's filled with artwork and essays. If you're a longtime fan of the Tom and Jerry cartoons, this is a collector's set that seems well worth adding to a collection.
