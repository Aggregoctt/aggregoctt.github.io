It’s a new month, a new year, and a terrific time to be a LEGO fan. New set releases really petered out in November, and especially in December, when literally zero new sets came out. Thankfully, they’re back in force for January. We have a new Back to the Future set that looks great and is priced at a modest $27.99. We have a new batch of excellent (and not crazy-expensive) Star Wars sets, plus a pair of Stranger Things sets, one of which, unfortunately, has already sold out. Let’s dive in.

LEGO Minifigures Animal Series 28

LEGO Architecture Paris – City of Love

Paris is a gorgeous city, and this LEGO Architecture set pays homage in a worthy way. All the major sites are included, from the Eiffel Tower and Notre-Dame cathedral to the Arc de Triomphe and the Louvre. It’s designed so you can hang the completed set on your wall, or set it on a shelf for display.
