The Baseus wall charger is very compact thanks to its use of gallium nitride (GaN) instead of the more common silicon for its semiconductor material. This makes the charger smaller, faster, and more energy-efficient than a traditional charger. The plug can also fold out of the way so that it doesn't get damaged during travel.

There are a total of three ports: two USB Type-C ports capable of 65W of power output each, and a USB Type-A port capable of 60W. Note that the maximum charging output is 65W. That means although all three ports add up to a total of 190W, only a maximum of 65W will be delivered across all ports if more than one is used simultaneously. This throttling is actually a good thing; charging at 190W would generate a ton of heat and you'd need a much bigger charger to handle it.

65W output is higher than what I see in most chargers at this price point and is capable enough to charge most portable electronics out there at their fastest rate. This includes the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, most gaming handheld PCs like the Steam Deck and Asus ROG Ally, the Apple iPhone 16, the Samsung Galaxy S25, and more. For a charger this small, you'd be hard pressed to find a higher output. If you do need more power, Baseus does also have a 100W variant, but it is bigger and you only get one charger for the same price.
