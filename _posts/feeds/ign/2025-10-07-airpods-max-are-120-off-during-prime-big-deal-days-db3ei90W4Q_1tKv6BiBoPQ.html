The Apple AirPods Max have just about everything you need from a great pair of headphones. Most importantly, they sound amazing and offer some of the best-in-class noise cancellation around. With the use of spatial audio and head tracking, the AirPods Max manage even deeper immersion and a more personalized listening experience.

The latest AirPods Max also introduces a few new color options. However, the headphones still rock the same premium build with a telescopic headband, aluminum earcups, and a steel frame ready to take on some wear and tear.

If you’re deep in Apple’s ecosystem and want to upgrade your current headphones, the AirPods Max are hard to beat. Chances are good you won’t see savings like these for a bit. Be sure to grab this deal while you still can.
