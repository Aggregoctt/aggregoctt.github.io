Reading is one of my favorite hobbies. I can easily burn through three or four books each month, largely thanks to my Kindle Unlimited subscription making it so easy to do so. Having an endless source of books I can download onto my phone or Kindle instantly is an incredible thing and I love everything about it... but there's still that part of me that yearns for actual pages to flip. The sensory experience of reading an actual book from my shelf has always held a feeling of magic to it that ebooks just can't capture.

It's because of this that I've learned to start looking at reading books and collecting books as two separate hobbies. The convenience of digital books lets me to read anything I want at any time while also allowing me to be more selective about which novels get added to my personal library. And being more selective about which physical books I actually buy means I can focus on saving as much money on each purchase as humanly possible.

If you're looking to find discounts on popular box sets, special editions, or even just new releases, there are quite a few times throughout the year when discounts are plentiful. Here's when I recommend doing your shopping:

Online retailers are fairly consistent when it comes to discounts on books during major sales events. This could either be in the form of a direct discount or some sort of "buy one, get one" sale. These discounts can usually be found at all of the places that sell books online, but some sales are exclusive to certain retailers.

Prime Big Deal Days (October)

The next big sale where we expect to see deals on books is Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days event. This is essentially an October Prime Day sale in that many of the discounts are exclusive to those with a Prime Membership. Amazon has offered up a sneak peak of the sale and already stated that there will be discounts on physical books this year. We're already seeing some early discounts trickle in, including a price drop on the Hunger Games five-book box set.

If you're someone who likes to buy hardcovers of newer releases, there are a few different times I've seen discounts become available on these.

Preordering Before the Release Date

Surprisingly, one of the best times to buy a new hardcover at a discount is before it even comes out. In 2025 some of the biggest new releases (Onyx Storm and Sunrise on the Reaping) were priced well below MSRP throughout a large chunk of their preorder window. If you know you are going to want a copy immediately after the book comes out, it's definitely worth checking for deals ahead of release day. Many times these same discounts will also persist even after release, like what we saw with Dan Brown's new book this year.

During "Buy One, Get One" Promotions

Another great time to find deals on new releases is whenever a major retailer has some sort of "buy one, get one" sale. Both Amazon and Target offer these promotions fairly regularly and there is usually at least one popular new release included to entice people to buy. This does mean that you'll need to buy more than one book to get the discount, but many times this is a good chance to grab a full collection for cheap. One example from earlier this year was when Amazon had all of the Fourth Wing books wrapped up in one sale pretty close to the release of Onyx Storm.
