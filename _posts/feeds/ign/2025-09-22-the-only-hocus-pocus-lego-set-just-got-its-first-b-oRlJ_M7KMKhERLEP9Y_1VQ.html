LEGO sets get discounts at third-party sites like Amazon and Target fairly frequently, making them one of the best places to buy LEGO in general. However, the more popular LEGO Ideas sets don't usually receive the same level of discounts as other themes available. This is especially true of sets that are one-of-kind, like this LEGO Hocus Pocus build. This isn't a theme that's likely to receive any more sets any time soon, so if you're a fan of the movie this is potentially the only build you'll ever get for it. And to add to that, this is the lowest price we've ever seen on the set and likely the lowest price we'll see before LEGO officially retires it for good.

The set itself depicts the Sanderson cottage in all its glory. You build the full cottage, inside and out, as well as a front gate complete with headstones and a gnarly tree. The cottage includes a roof on a hinge that lets you to open it up and take a look inside. There are also a total of six minifigures included that you can place anywhere in the house or the surrounding property (depending on how you choose to display it). In terms of minifigures, you'll get all of the sanderson figures as well as a few additional characters. The full list includes: Winifred Sanderson, Sarah Sanderson, Mary Sanderson, Max Dennison, Allyson Watts, and Thackery Binx in cat form.

Will there ever be more LEGO Hocus Pocus sets?

The original Hocus Pocus movie came out all the way back in 1993, but it wasn't until the release of the sequel almost 20 years later that the series got its first LEGO set. So the likelihood of LEGO releasing any further sets related to cult classic Halloween movies seems pretty unlikely. That being said, the release of another movie could potentially be cause for further collaboration between the plastic brick company and Disney. Sarah Jessica Parker recently made comments that there have been some conversations about a Hocus Pocus 3. If that third film in the series does manage to become a reality, it's possible that LEGO may be inclined to release more sets alongside that new movie.
