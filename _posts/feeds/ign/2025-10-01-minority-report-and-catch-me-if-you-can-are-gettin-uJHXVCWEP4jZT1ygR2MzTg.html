This year has already offered up plenty of exciting 4Ks and Blu-rays for collectors to add to their libraries, but it's time to clear out a little extra space as some classics are set to get shiny new steelbooks. Steven Spielberg's Minority Report and Catch Me If You Can are getting the 4K steelbook treatment this year (and releasing as standard 4Ks, for those who aren't looking to pick up the steelbook version).

Both steelbooks are currently listed for $36.99, while the standard 4Ks are available to preorder for $30.99, with release dates of December 9. That's only a couple months away, so you won't have to wait too long for a rewatch. Head to the links below to secure a copy for yourself before they sell out.
