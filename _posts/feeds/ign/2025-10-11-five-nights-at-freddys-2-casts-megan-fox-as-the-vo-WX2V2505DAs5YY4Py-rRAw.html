Transformers and TMNT franchise vet Megan Fox has joined the cast of Five Nights at Freddy’s 2, Blumhouse announced at their New York Comic Con panel on Friday.

Blumhouse also unveiled the movie’s new poster, which can be seen below.

Fox will voice the role of Toy Chica, who is described in the IGN guide for the Five Nights at Freddy's 2 game as being “a yellow chicken with an orange beak and feet, with a bib saying 'Lets Party!' and pink hot pants.

She has blue eyes, however when she leaves the stage they turn black, and her beak disappears for unknown reasons. When she appears in the Right Air Vent, put on the mask to make her go away.”

Megan Fox joins a supporting cast that includes MatPat (Five Nights at Freddy’s, Transformers: Titans Return) as Toy Bonnie, and Kellen Goff (Five Nights at Freddy's (The Games), Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man), who has voiced other Freddys in the past but will voice Toy Freddy in the sequel.

Josh Hutcherson, Matthew Lillard, Elizabeth Lail, Piper Rubio, Skeet Ulrich, Wayne Knight, Mckenna Grace, and Teo Briones star.

Five Nights at Freddy’s 2, once again directed by Emma Tammi, opens in theaters on December 5th.
