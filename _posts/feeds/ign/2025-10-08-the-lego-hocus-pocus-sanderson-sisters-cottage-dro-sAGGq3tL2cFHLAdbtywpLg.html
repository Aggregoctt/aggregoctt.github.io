For Hocus Pocus fans, especially those who love revisiting the film during this time of year, this looks like a delightful Lego set to piece together. It comes with 2,316 pieces that create The Sanderson Sister's cottage from the film, complete with fun little details on the inside including the museum gift shop, a cauldron, and the Book of Spells, to name a few. It's also recommended for those aged 18 and up, making it a LEGO set angled towards adults. However, a younger fan can piece it together if an adult gives them a hand.

It even comes with six minifigures: Winifred, Sarah, and Mary Sanderson, of course, along with Max and Dani Dennison, Allison Watts, and a little Thackery Binx cat figure. They also come with fun accessories so you can recreate scenes from the movie, from Dani’s little Halloween pumpkin bucket for trick-or-treating to the witches broom, mop, and vacuum cleaner that they fly on.
