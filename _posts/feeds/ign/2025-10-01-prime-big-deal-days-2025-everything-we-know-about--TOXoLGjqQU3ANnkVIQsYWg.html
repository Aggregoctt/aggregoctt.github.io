The next major sale kicks off next week. Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days event is set to begin on October 7 and run through October 8 this year with discounts exclusively available to Prime members. This is the fourth year in a row that Amazon has decided to have an October Prime Day and we here on the IGN Deals team have covered every single one. Although we don't yet have all of the details about what deals to expect this year, we've done this enough times to make some educated guesses.

If you're planning to do some shopping before November this year, Amazon's two-day fall sale is certainly a good time to do so. But you shouldn't expect that everything you are hoping to buy will be getting a discount. Prime Big Deal Days will definitely include some of the best deals of the year on many popular items, but Black Friday is still when we expect to see the biggest spread of discounts from every retailer.

Amazon has released a sneak peek of some of the discounts that will be available during the sale, but the list doesn't include actual prices. Based on this sneak peek, as well as our coverage of the sale from last year, we've outlined what discounts we expect to see in 2025 below. You can also jump to Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days page right now to check out early deals.

Black Friday has consistently been the best time to find deals on just about everything. However, that trend has started to shift in recent years as the actual start date for Black Friday deals has shifted closer and closer to October. If you're wondering whether or not it's worth shopping now vs. during the November sales season, it's become a bit more complicated than just "yes, of course." Early sales like Prime Big Deal Days will often have discounts that are better than Black Friday, so the trick is knowing what prices are good and what prices are worth waiting on.
