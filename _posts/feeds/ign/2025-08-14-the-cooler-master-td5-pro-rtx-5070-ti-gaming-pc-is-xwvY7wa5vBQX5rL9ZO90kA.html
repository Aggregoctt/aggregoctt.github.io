The Cooler Master TD5 Pro is equipped with an Intel Core Ultra 7 265KF processor, GeForce RTX 5070 Ti graphics card, 32GB of DDR5-6000MHz RAM, and a 2TB SSD. The system is housed in Cooler Master's MasterBox TD500 Mesh V2 case with a total of four 120mm airflow fans in addition to the three 120mm fans that are part of the 360mm Cooler Master MasterLiquid 360L Core ARGB liquid cooling system. The motherboard uses the new Intel Z890 chipset with both WiFi and Bluetooth built-in. It's all powered by a Cooler Master MWE 750W 80Plus Gold fully modular power supply.

The Intel Core Ultra 7 265KF CPU has a max turbo frequency of 5.5GHz with 20 cores and a 36MB L2 cache. This is a "K" series CPU, meaning it has a slightly higher clock speed and it is unlocked for better overclocking potential. On the Intel side, this is pretty much as good as you're going to get with gaming performance. The Ultra 9 CPU has more cores, which won't matter in the vast majority of games, and is an unnecessary upgrade. It's paired with 32GB of DDR5-6000MHz RAM. Savvy system builders might recognize 6000MHz as the optimal speed for DDR5 nowadays, and you don't see it very often in most prebuilt PCs, including those from Alienware, HP, or Lenovo.

The RTX 5070 Ti offers up plenty of power for gaming at 4K resolution

Among the new Blackwell releases, the GeForce RTX 5070 Ti is probably our favorite model. It is definitely the most affordable graphics card that I'd recommend if you want to game at 4K resolutions at consistent frame rates of 60fps and beyond. Compared to the previous generation cards, it's roughly comparable to an RTX 4080 Super but with the added benefit of DLSS 4 and multi-frame generation.
