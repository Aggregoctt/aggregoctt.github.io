LEGO and Star Wars go way back. There are a ton of LEGO Star Wars sets that have been released over the years and more new ones just keep coming month after month. The sheer volume of output from this collaboration means that some of the coolest LEGO builds ever created are LEGO Star Wars. It also means that there is almost always some sort of sale available.

With Labor Day weekend just around the corner, Target has unleashed a flurry of LEGO discounts with Star Wars sets getting some of the best price cuts. If you've been looking to add to your collection, I definitely recommend you check out these deals before they're gone. This LEGO sale will only last through Saturday, August 30.

The Best LEGO Star Wars Deals at Target Today

Target's LEGO sale is a flat 20% off select sets, but there are some discounts featured here that actually go even further than that. One such sale is on the Darth Vader Helmet, which has dropped to the lowest price I've ever seen. This is a build specifically designed for adults and is meant to be displayed. It's a recreation of Darth Vader's iconic helmet and is made up of a total of 834 pieces. This set was released alongside a bunch of other display sets back in 2022, and this discount finally brings it down to a more reasonable price for 2025.

Another notable deal on this set is the Tie Fighter & X-Wing Mash-up set. This is a 1,063-piece bundle set that primarily consists of two of the most iconic and recognizable ships in Star Wars history: the TIE Fighter that Imperial pilots use, and the X-Wing, the signature starfighter of the Rebellion. This set went on sale at the LEGO Store earlier this year for a slightly better price and was incredibly popular. It has since completely sold out at the LEGO Store and is low in stock at Amazon, so it's definitely worth picking up at this price.

The last deal I want to highlight is the Droideka Build and Display model. This is another 18+ set that is designed to be built and put on your desk or shelf. At only 583 pieces, it's a fairly quick build you should be able to complete in a couple of hours or less. What separates it from some of the other display models is that you can actually roll it up into a ball shape or unfurl it into attack mode. At $41.59, it's about $10 cheaper than the lowest price we've ever seen on Amazon.
