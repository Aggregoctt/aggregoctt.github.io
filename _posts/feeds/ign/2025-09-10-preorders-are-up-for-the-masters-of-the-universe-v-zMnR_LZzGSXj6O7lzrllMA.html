This new Castle Grayskull playset is considered "vintage" as it is inspired by a prototype designed by Mark Taylor, creator of the original He-Man and Masters of the Universe toys. It comes with a Stygian Moat mat that surrounds the castle, which you can see in more detail below, along with a special edition Space Suit figure. The castle has plenty of cool features as well, including the Jawbridge that opens and closes, an elevator, trap door, and more.

This playset comes with a bunch of accessories to use as well, including a sheet of stickers so you can add little details around the castle, a flag, ladder, four weapons and two racks to hold them, and more. If you're a Masters of the Universe fan, this Vintage Castle Grayskull playset seems like an excellent addition to a collectible collection.

If you're looking for even more collectibles to pick up for your collection right now, there are quite a few worth keeping on your radar. This is especially true if you're a Marvel fan, as S.H.Figuarts and TAMASHII NATIONS have new Spider-Man and Cyclops action figures up for preorder right now - with both set to be released next year - and Hasbro is dropping a new Juggernaut and Deadpool figure this year. To see what else the latter has up their sleeve, have a look at their reveals from SDCC 2025.
