It took over a month, but Amazon's listings for the Nintendo Switch 2 have finally changed. The pages went up way later than most other retailers, a couple of weeks after the console released, but they didn't have buy buttons. They still don't have buy buttons, but they do now have a "Request invite" button, which hints that the Switch 2 may finally be coming to Amazon. You can visit the listing and submit your request now.

Ongoing Prime Day Deals

The Switch 2 listing change happened to take place at the start of Amazon Prime Day 2025. Is it related? Likely not. But if you're in the market for accessories and games, now's a great time to stock up. For those who'd like to avoid the Switch 2 price tag, used editions of the original Switch OLED are currently on sale for $208, while Best Buy has tons of discounts on Switch games.
