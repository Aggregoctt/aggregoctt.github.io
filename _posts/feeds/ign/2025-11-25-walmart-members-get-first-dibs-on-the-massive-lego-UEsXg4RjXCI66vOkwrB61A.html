The LEGO Star Wars Ultimate Collector Series (UCS) Millennium Falcon was the largest LEGO Star Wars set up until a few months ago with the release of the $1,000 9,023-piece Death Star. With an imposive 7,541-piece brick count, it boasts 2,346 more pieces than the previous UCS Millennium Falcon which was released back in 2007. This behemoth measures 8" high, 33" long, and 22" wide and weighs 29 pounds. It weighs twice as much and has nearly 3,000 more bricks than the LEGO Death Star that appeared in Marvel's Spider-Man: Homecoming. I own this set, and between myself and my son, it took about three weeks to build. We enjoyed every minute of it. It's certainly the crown jewel of my LEGO collection.

Although I would never suggest purchasing LEGOs as an investment, it is true that LEGO Star Wars Ultimate Collector Series sets have among the best resale value across all LEGO franchises. Once LEGO discontinues a UCS set, the value skyrockets. The Millennium Falcon isn't discontinued just yet, but it has been in production for quite a while, and if the previous Millennium Falcon is anything to go by, this is going to be one of the most coveted Star Wars sets in existence.
