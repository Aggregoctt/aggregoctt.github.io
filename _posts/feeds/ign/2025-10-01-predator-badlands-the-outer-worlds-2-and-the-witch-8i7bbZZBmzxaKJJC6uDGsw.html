IGN Fan Fest is returning this October with a special Fall Edition of the event. Fans will be able to watch celebrity interviews, exclusive gameplay, trailers, and deep dives across movies, TV, games, and more during the one-day event on October 15.

There will be more than 80 exclusive reveals in the main show beginning at 10am PT / 1pm ET / 6pm BST, with an additional 25 trailers, clips, and interviews in the special Countdown Show at 9am PT / 12pm ET / 5pm BST.

We'll have special celebrity appearances from:

  • Keanu Reeves
  • Emma Stone
  • Aziz Ansari
  • Jesse Plemmons
  • Dan Trachtenberg
  • Andy and Barbara Muschietti
  • And more!

Check out some of the big-name titles that will be coming IGN Fan Fest: Fall Edition 2025 below. And be sure to check out the live show on October 15 for announcements of some brand-new games and more!
