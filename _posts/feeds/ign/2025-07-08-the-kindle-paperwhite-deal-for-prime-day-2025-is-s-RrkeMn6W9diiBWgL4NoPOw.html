Like I mentioned above, this is the lowest price we've ever seen for the newest Paperwhite. The last time this Kindle was discounted this heavily was during Black Friday last year, but this price is even lower than that. As the latest model, it comes with a larger 7-inch display. Compared to the standard Kindle, it's bigger, faster, and an overall better reading experience. My wife has the regular Kindle and whenever I've used it I've always been dissatisfied with the smaller screen. If you have enough money to spring for the more expensive Paperwhite during this sale, I recommend doing so.

In addition to the e-reader itself, you can also opt in to a 3-month free trial of Kindle Unlimited with your purchase. You don't have to try it out, but it is an overall great service if you're someone who reads a lot of books. I personally read 3-5 books (usually LitRPG) each month and they are almost always included with my monthly subscription.

You should probably also get a cover

While you can certainly use any Kindle without a cover, I do recommend you get one. All of the newer models feature an automatic sleep mode that only activates if you have a compatible cover to close when you're done reading. The official case works great if you want to pick that one up, but there are a few other Paperwhite covers I'd recommend as well. I've featured all of these above.
