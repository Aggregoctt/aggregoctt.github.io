Magic: The Gathering has a busy 2026 planned, but one set remained a mystery up until today - a crossover with Nickelodeon.

Not only is that set now revealed to star none other than the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, but preorders are live. Here’s everything you need to know, from Play Boosters to a Commander Deck.

Preorders In A Half Shell

As you can probably imagine, there are more products coming than you can shake a Bo Staff at launching on March 6, with pre-release a week prior from February 27 to March 5.

The foundation, as expected, is Play Boosters, and Amazon has a box for $209.70 right now. If that price changes you’ll be refunded the difference, but history tells us that a box of thirty packs will start to get cheaper shortly after launch (just look at Spider-Man).

You can also grab the now-customary booster bundle, which includes a promo card, a series of nine Play Boosters, a storage box and a spindown life counter. That’s $69.99 right now.

Less clear pricing-wise are Collector Boosters. As with any set, these are where you’ll find the high-value cards, and Amazon doesn’t have a price on these yet, nor for a box of them. Expect them to be expensive, though, with an MSRP of $37.99 each.

New this time is a Pizza Bundle, which includes 9 Play Boosters, 1 Collector Booster, 25 non-foil Pizza lands (yes, really), five foil Pizza lands, 2 foil promo cards, and a spindown life counter. Amazon has this for $99.99.

Draft Night, a new boxed product making its debut in January’s Lorwyn, is also included. It has a bunch of packs (twelve in total) to play sealed draft, and a Collector Booster for the winner to take home. It’s $119.99 at Amazon.

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Is Getting A Commander Deck

Finally, Commander players can expect a return to precon decks… but just one.

After being absent since Edge of Eternities, there’s a new precon coming, which is a five-color deck called Turtle Power!

Wizards has the decklist live now, but the big focus is on teamwork, so you can have a pair of Heroes in a Half Shell to be your Commanders. It’s currently up for preorder at $69.99, but expect it to climb.
