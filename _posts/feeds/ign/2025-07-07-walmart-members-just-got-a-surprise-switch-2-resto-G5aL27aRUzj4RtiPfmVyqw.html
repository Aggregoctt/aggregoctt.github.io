Amazon Prime Day officially kicks off tomorrow, but Walmart is starting the party with new deals only Walmart+ members can access before tomorrow. In addition to those early deals, Walmart is also dropping something Amazon has yet to have any of: Switch 2 stock. The latest Nintendo console has been particularly hard to find since it first came out back in June and Amazon has yet to offer it to customers at all.

It's unclear how long the Switch 2 console bundle will be available, but only Walmart+ members can currently get access to what is in stock.
