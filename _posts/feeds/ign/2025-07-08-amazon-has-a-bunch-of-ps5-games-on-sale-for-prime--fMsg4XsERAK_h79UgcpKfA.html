PlayStation 5 has amassed a huge library since its launch in 2020, and whether you’re looking for early launch titles or recent hits, there’s a good chance Amazon’s Prime Day is offering discounts on something worth playing.

The retailer has discounted popular games like Alan Wake II Deluxe Edition, the Steelbook version of Horizon Forbidden West, and more recent releases like Assassin’s Creed: Shadows.

Save on Some Fantastic PS5 Games for Prime Day

When we say there’s something for everyone, we truly do mean it. Assassin’s Creed: Shadows only launched this year, and is down to $47.49.

Given the incredible scale of Ubisoft’s latest open-world epic, there’s every chance you could keep playing it for months like an everlasting gobstopper of a game.

Sticking with 2025 releases, co-op adventure Split Fiction is one of my personal favorites of the year so far, and it’s absolutely packed with memorable moments.

And, thanks to the Friend Pass, you only need one copy (reduced to $35) to be able to play the entire thing with a buddy.

Superhero fans can swing through NYC as Spidey and Miles Morales with Insomniac’s Marvel’s Spider-Man 2, which is a great showcase for the PS5 Pro if you happen to have one handy, while fans of another Disney franchise can explore the open world areas of Star Wars Outlaws for under $30, too.

Final Fantasy is well-represented, too, with both Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade and its sequel Final Fantasy VII Rebirth are down to $29.99 and $39.99, respectively, while the formerly PS5 exclusive Final Fantasy XVI is down to $24.79.

Horizon Forbidden West’s Special Edition is $39.99 with a steelbook case, while the survival horror masterpiece Alan Wake II Deluxe Edition is $44.99 and includes Alan Wake Remastered and all the DLC.

Elsewhere, Black Myth: Wukong has $5 off, Dragon Ball Sparking! Zero is down to $39.99, while Souls titles Demon’s Souls and Elden Ring are both under $30.

We’d also be remiss not to mention Farming Simulator 25, which is down to $29.99. If you’ve been curious about it, now could be the perfect time to check it out since that’s a 50% discount.

Swap crops for clotheslines with the latest entry in the WWE gaming pantheon, WWE 2K25. It’s reduced by more than 40%, despite only launching a few months ago, meaning you can get it for just $39.99.

You want more? Phew, OK, how about Suikoden I & II HD Remastered for $29.97, which includes two iconic RPGs at 40% off?
