This Batman Anniversary 4K Collection Is 61% Off

The 10 movies in this set include some excellent comic adaptations, like The Killing Joke and the iconic Year One, as well as Gotham By Gaslight and Hush.

My favorite of the bunch has to be Batman: Mask of the Phantasm. This feature-length episode of the animated series packs everything you could want into a relatively short runtime.

There’s Conroy’s Batman, genuine emotional challenges for him to overcome, a surprising new threat, and Mark Hamill’s Joker.

Don’t skip Assault on Arkham, either. It’s just as much a story about the Suicide Squad as it is Batman, and it’s a really fun hour and change.

If you’re looking for live-action Batman adventures, Amazon also has a whole host of other options on sale.

The Batman 4K Film Collection includes the Keaton adventures, Batman Forever, and Batman & Robin, and it’s just $32.99, while the Dark Knight Trilogy is under $30 for the Blu-Ray version (albeit not in 4K).

Robert Pattinson’s recent entry, The Batman, is 63% off in 4K. I’ll take any excuse to watch it again, especially at $12.49, and if Mask of the Phantasm has you intrigued, the Complete Animated Series is $28.99 on Blu-Ray (it’s included in this set, too).
