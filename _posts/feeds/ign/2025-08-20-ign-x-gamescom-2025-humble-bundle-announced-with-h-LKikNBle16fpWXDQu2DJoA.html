IGN is partnering with gamescom to present a brand-new Humble Bundle that, for at least $18, will get you a collection of seven games worth $214, including Homeworld 3, Bomb Rush Cyberfunk, and more. Furthermore, your purchase will go a long way in helping DonorsChoose, a charity that helps teachers get the supplies they need to help their students succeed.

The IGN x gamescom 2025 Humble Bundle is available now right here and will be available for purchase until September 10 at 6pm PT/9pm ET. The full bundle, which once again can be yours for at least $18, includes Bomb Rush Cyberfunk, Homeworld 3, Antonblast, Victory Heat Rally, Lost Eidolons, Pyrene, and The Expanse: A Telltale Series.

While $18 will get you all of these games, $12 will still allow you to add Lost Eidolons, Pyrene, and The Expanse: A Telltale Series to your gaming library.

We here at IGN are fans of a lot of these games, including Homeworld 3, which has a single-player campaign and multiplayer that we called great for its epic space battle and intense close-quarters combat.

The Expanse: A Telltale Series is well-worth a journey for those invested in the series, as it is a "solid addition to the Telltale roster that accurately captures its source material."
