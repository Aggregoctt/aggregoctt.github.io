I’ll just cut to the chase: Pretty much every Studio Ghibli steelbook is on sale right now. Most of the discounts knock around 40% off the special edition Blu-rays. Despite having a smaller discount at only 18% off, Grave of the Fireflies is at its lowest price yet. It may be the least cheerful of the bunch (we even named the saddest anime of all time), but it’s definitely a worthy addition to the shelf of any Studio Ghibli fan.

Every Studio Ghibli Steelbook Is on Sale at Amazon

The Boy and the Heron and Grave of the Fireflies, the two most recent releases, both feature full art covers that wrap around the back of each steelbook. The Boy and the Heron is also the only movie with a release in 4K UHD; the rest stick to Blu-ray. (Here’s our explainer on the difference between those things, in case you were curious.)

The rest of the Studio Ghibli steelbooks, which were created in collaboration with GKIDS, feature a similar design: one flat background color tailored to the palette of the movie with a specific character or scene in the center. This consistency makes the collection especially beautiful lined up on your shelf. I personally have Howl’s Moving Castle, and think now’s a good time as any to give it some companions. I'm looking at you, Porco.

Each Blu-ray Comes Loaded With Bonus Features

One thing you’re guaranteed across the board? A solid set of bonus features that highlight, in particular, the storyboards and concept art that went into each film. Here’s what’s included in the Grave of the Fireflies steelbook, for reference:

  • Feature Length Storyboards
  • Deleted Scene Storyboards
  • Interview with Director Isao Takahata
  • Interview with Roger Ebert
  • Promotional Video
  • Image Galleries
  • Teasers and Trailers

You can check out what comes with your favorite Studio Ghibli movie by scrolling down the Amazon listing. I’ve highlighted deals that are particularly notable, given the frequency with which these get discounts, but you can also use tools like camelcamelcamel to check individual price histories.

What’s Next for Studio Ghibli

While most of the Studio Ghibli movies are streaming on HBO Max, Grave of the Fireflies is now available on Netflix. It also happens to be next up in the lineup of Studio Ghibli Fest, which brings the studio’s movies back to theaters one weekend at a time.
