Save on Titan Evo Gaming Chairs at Secretlab

Should you buy a Gaming Chair on Prime Day?

If you're on the fence about springing for a gaming chair on Prime Day, it's worth noting that prices are probably going to go up. This is especially true for certain gaming accessory brands as the tariffs has started to kick in. With the higher tariffs poised to unpause on Chinese goods in August, brands like Secretlab that do most of their manufacturing in China will potentially have to raise prices. This means that July is probably going to be a better time to buy than Black Friday this year.
