Relying on your TV’s speakers might be the easiest way to play on Xbox Series X or S, but you’re leaving a lot on the table by not using a proper gaming headset. The right headset brings out subtle details in sound design, with tuned drivers delivering sharper audio and accurate positional cues. Story-driven titles like Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 come alive when you can fully appreciate the soundtrack, while competitive shooters such as Call of Duty: Warzone give you a clear edge when you can pick up on footsteps or distant gunfire. Regular earbuds or TV speakers simply can’t compete.

With so many headsets on the market, the challenge is finding the one that fits your setup, playstyle, and budget. Some offer premium soundscapes and advanced features, while others keep things simple and affordable without sacrificing the essentials. The good news is that you don’t need to spend a fortune to score a great Xbox headset – there are solid options at every price point that will transform your gaming experience.

TL;DR: These Are Our Picks for the Best Xbox Series X/S Headsets

There are straightforward picks like the official Xbox headset, which delivers dependable sound and a clean, minimalist look without costing a fortune. For players chasing every competitive edge, the new Razer BlackShark V3 Pro is worth stretching the budget for, packing premium features into a slick design. On the other end of the spectrum, the Turtle Beach Stealth 500 is a cracking choice if you want strong performance at a wallet-friendly price. And for those who prefer earbuds with the flexibility to double as everyday music gear, the SteelSeries Arctis GameBuds are an easy recommendation.

Whichever way you lean, make sure you grab the Xbox-specific version of the headset. Microsoft’s consoles use their own wireless standard for accessories, which means non-Xbox models won’t connect wirelessly even if they look identical. Many of the top headsets are sold as multiplatform gear, but this guide points you directly to the proper Xbox editions so you don’t end up with the wrong one.

Picking the right headset for your Xbox Series X or S can take your gaming to the next level, giving you crisp sound, clear chat, and comfort for marathon sessions. First, set your budget – it shapes the options you’ll be looking at. Comfort is key: padded ear cushions, an adjustable headband, and a lightweight build make long gaming sessions far more enjoyable.

Sound quality comes next. Look for clear audio with solid bass so you can catch every footstep or distant gunshot. Some headsets add features like surround sound or spatial audio for an even more immersive experience.

Connectivity is another factor. Bluetooth headsets are versatile, but wired options usually offer more reliability. If you play multiplayer often, a quality mic with noise-canceling tech is essential for smooth communication. Higher-end models may also include perks like programmable buttons, customizable EQ, or companion software.

Do you need specific headphones for the Xbox Series X?

Both wired and wireless headsets work, including Bluetooth models. Just make sure your headset is compatible with Xbox, which uses a proprietary wireless tech for some devices.

Does the Xbox Series X have a headphone jack?

No, but you can plug a 3.5mm headset into a paired Xbox controller.

Can I use AirPods with an Xbox?

Not directly. You can connect AirPods via your TV’s Bluetooth or through the Xbox app on a phone.

When do Xbox headsets go on sale?

Best deals usually coincide with Amazon Prime Day (July and October) or Black Friday in November, when consoles and accessories often get big discounts.
