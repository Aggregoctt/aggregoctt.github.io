Some of the best Funko Pop! toys are those that lend the series' trademark cutesy aesthetic to characters that are anything but. That's why Funko and FX's Alien: Earth are basically a match made in collecting heaven. Who doesn't want a pint-sized version of the Xenomorph and other terrifying critters from the FX series?

IGN can exclusively reveal the first images and details for Funko's first wave of Alien Earth Pop! figures, two of which are available to preorder at Amazon. Get a closer look in the slideshow gallery below:

This wave of figures includes two creatures and one human character. Or, rather, the human-turned-synthetic known as Wendy. Here are the prices and official descriptions for each Funko Pop! toy:

POP Super: Alien (2025) – POP! Super Xenomorph

An extraterrestrial threat is scoping out your Alien: Earth collection! Poised to attack, this POP! Super Xenomorph is ready to strike and prove its might as the apex predator in your POP! Television lineup. Vinyl figure is approximately 5.8 in (14.7 cm) tall.

  • Preorder on Amazon
  • MSRP: $24.99

POP TV: Alien (2025) – POP! Wendy

An extraterrestrial threat is scoping out your Alien: Earth collection! Face this dangerous adversary alongside POP! Wendy when she joins your POP! Television lineup. Vinyl figure is approximately 4 in (10.2 cm) tall

POP TV: Alien (2025) – POP! Sheep with the Eye

An extraterrestrial threat is scoping out your Alien: Earth collection! Experiment with a new discovery when you make POP! Sheep with the Eye the next addition to your POP! Television lineup. Vinyl figure is approximately 3.3 in (8.4 cm) tall.

Alien: Earth is currently airing weekly on FX and Hulu. IGN's Clint Gage gave the series a 9 in his Alien: Earth review, writing, "In this, the far flung future of the year 2025 when all that’s on offer is familiar IP, you have to appreciate the ones that do it right. Much like Andor’s second season from earlier in the year, Alien: Earth matches an apex of late ’70s cinema in look and feel, while blowing out the world to create a new context with which to view the franchise – even if one of the central story points might take a step too close to the absurd. Simultaneously providing fans with the eggs (Easter and Xenomorph) they expect, and shedding concerns of fitting into continuity, Noah Hawley has made an amazing piece of science fiction on the strength of solid production and creature design, an incredible cast, and needle drops that make me want a cigarette."
